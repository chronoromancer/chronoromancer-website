Steam edition: optional usage statistics and diagnostics

This notice covers optional usage statistics and diagnostics in the Chronoromancer Steam edition. The Steam edition is planned and has not yet been publicly released.

Your choice

Steam data sharing is off by default. No optional usage or diagnostic reports are sent until you enable sharing in Setup. Declining or switching sharing off does not restrict game content. You can change this choice at any time in Setup. The choice and random diagnostic reference are stored separately from game saves on this device; importing a save does not opt you in.

Switching sharing off clears unsent reports and stops future reporting. A request already transmitted cannot be recalled. Switching off does not itself delete reports already received; you may contact us to request deletion as explained below.

What is sent when sharing is enabled

  • Random diagnostic, session and report references, plus report event and sequence information. These references are generated by the game, not derived from your Steam account, identity or hardware.
  • Game and Ren'Py engine versions, operating-system family, selected game language and renderer type.
  • Limited progress and resource counters, including game day and story progress, wealth/crystals, combat and dungeon totals, location visits, worker recruitment and training totals, selected minigame/activity totals, and playtime totals.
  • Error categories when an error is reported. The Steam diagnostic report does not send a full traceback or save-file contents.

Steam reports do not send player names, Steam account identifiers or credentials, hardware identifiers or hardware-model inventories, redeem codes, adult-content preferences, or save files. Reports grouped by a random diagnostic reference are pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous.

Purpose, recipients and connection data

Chronoromancer Studio uses these optional reports to improve the game, understand feature use and investigate bugs and customer-support problems. This reporting does not submit Steam leaderboard scores. Reports are sent over encrypted HTTPS to our private reporting database hosted by Supabase. They are not sold, shared for advertising or made public.

Our hosting and network providers necessarily process connection data, including an IP address, to deliver and secure HTTPS requests and may retain operational or security logs under their own policies. We do not intentionally store your IP address in the game telemetry database. Valve separately processes Steam account, purchase and platform data under its own privacy policy.

Retention and privacy requests

Routine game reports are automatically deleted by a daily cleanup once they are more than 28 days old. Relevant extracts preserved for a support case or security investigation may be retained longer while needed to resolve the case or document its findings. Aggregate statistics may be retained longer when they no longer identify or single out a particular installation, session or player.

Where applicable under law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or portability, object to processing, or complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact chronoromancerstudio@gmail.com. If available, provide the diagnostic reference shown while sharing is enabled and the approximate report time so we can locate the records. A reference is not required to contact us; if we cannot reasonably locate or verify records, we will explain what further information is needed.
