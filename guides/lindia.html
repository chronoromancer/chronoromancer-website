Lindia is a gifted but anxious wizard's apprentice. A forbidden transformation spell turned her master into a frog, and another accidental spell transformed Spectra's lost cat into Whiskers. Her route is about learning to use power without treating herself as a disaster waiting to happen.

Recruiting Lindia

Lindia is the fixed miniboss on Dungeon Floor 7. Her first encounter has 235 Energy, targets your Energy, and runs for five narrative rounds.

Defeating her immediately calls activate_worker(12). Recruitment is silent, automatic, and unconditional; it works even if you have not purchased the brothel yet. Once the Rose & Thorn is available, Lindia is already in its worker roster.

Floor 7 battle advice

The encounter rewards calm, observant, or empathetic answers. Choose actions supported by your strongest relevant stat; unlocked skill choices are normally excellent.

Round Strong normal choice Excellent skill choice
1 Talk to her (Charm) or read her ward (Intelligence) Protective Ward (Mystic)
2 Predict the wild magic (Perception) or name her panic (Charm) Tactical Awareness (Intelligence)
3 Offer to help find her master (Charm) Illusion Weaving (Mystic)
4 Use responsibility (Charm), weather it (Stamina), or close in (Ability) Exploit Weakness (Intelligence)
5 Tell her amends are possible (Charm) Empathy (Perception)

Avoid snatching her spellbook in Round 3. It is the battle's explicit trap and doubles the counterattack multiplier.

After victory, an optional Beast Mode branch requires Beast 25 and Erotic Experience 300. Its deeper branch requires Erotic Experience 600. Recruitment does not depend on either branch.

Where to Find Her

Lindia only appears at relationship locations after Worker 12 is active.

Location Hours Days
Brothel Bar 04:00-08:00 Every day
Brothel Training Room 09:00-18:00 Every day

The current schedule is broader than the original fan page: her main progression content is in the Brothel Training Room, not only the early-morning bar slot.

If she is missing, first confirm that Floor 7 was cleared and that she appears as an active Rose & Thorn worker.

Sunset Walk with Lindia

This is a repeatable resort heart-to-heart. It is available through the resort's Sunset Walk activity when Lindia is vacationing and the resort activity requirements are met. It has a 57-point maximum route and uses the resort's once-per-day sunset-walk cooldown for Lindia.

See the Sirena Isle Resort Guide for vacation scheduling, presence chances, activity hours, and resource costs.

Sirena Isle Resort Content

Lindia is a fully supported resort worker. In addition to the normal worker-vacation effects, she has:

  • Eat with Lindia at the Resort Buffet: base +3, with a best +5 choice bonus.
  • Relax with Lindia at the Beach: base +3, with a best +5 choice bonus.
  • Spa Night with Lindia: base +3, with a best +5 choice bonus.
  • Sunset Walk with Lindia: repeatable heart-to-heart.
  • Tide Index: Lindia's solo ambient Beach plot, 08:00-16:00.

She also has resort combination scenes with Foxy, Bellavia, Elara, Shadow, Raven, Jade, Kasumi, Willow, Gem, A.R.I.A., and Vespa. The appropriate workers must be vacationing together and present at the correct resort location and time.

Recommended Route

  1. Clear Dungeon Floor 7 and defeat Lindia.
  2. Confirm Worker 12 is active, then meet her in the Training Room at 09:00-18:00.
  3. Talk four times per day; at Known, add up to two Books per day.
  4. Complete Margin Notes at Known.
  5. At Acquaintance, complete Controlled Magic. Bring Mystic 40 for its special answer if convenient.
  6. At Friend, complete The Master Question, Spell Control Notes, and the one-time Bazaar Date.
  7. At Close Friend, complete Close Enough; Empathy provides its special answer.
  8. At Crush, trigger Apprentice No More in the Training Room.
  9. Use Sirena Isle activities and combination scenes for additional content and relationship gains.
  10. At In Love, use Intimate Bonding daily when you want to level the Elemental Stone.

Troubleshooting

Lindia is not at the Bar

  • She must have been defeated on Floor 7.
  • Her Bar schedule is only 04:00-08:00.
  • Most of her route is instead at the Training Room from 09:00-18:00.

Her next plot will not trigger

  • Enter the Brothel Training Room during 09:00-18:00.
  • Make sure Lindia is an active worker.
  • Complete the preceding plot.
  • Reach the required relationship stage: Known, Friend, or Crush.
  • If several automatic plots qualify at once, leave and re-enter after the current event finishes.

Spell Control Notes is missing

You need Friend relationship, Mystic 35, the Training Room schedule, and an uncompleted lindia_spell_control_notes_completed flag.

Close Enough is missing

You need Close Friend and must already have completed Controlled Magic.

Intimate Bonding is missing

You need In Love, the Elemental Stone in key items, the Training Room schedule, and must not already have bonded with Lindia that day.

Lindia是一位天赋出众、却容易焦虑的巫师学徒。一道禁忌变形法术把她的导师变成了青蛙，另一次意外施法又把Spectra走失的猫变成了Whiskers。她的故事讲述了如何学会驾驭力量，同时不再把自己视为一场随时可能发生的灾难。

招募Lindia

Lindia是 地牢第7层 的固定小Boss。初次遭遇时她拥有235点精力，会攻击你的精力，战斗共进行五轮叙事回合。

击败她后，游戏会立即调用activate_worker(12)。招募过程没有提示、自动完成且不附带任何其他条件；即使你尚未买下青楼，也同样有效。玫瑰与荆棘开放后，Lindia已经在员工名单之中。

第7层战斗建议

这场遭遇会奖励冷静、善于观察或富有同理心的回答。请选择有自己较强相关属性支持的行动；已解锁的技能选项通常都非常优秀。

回合 优秀的普通选项 极佳的技能选项
1 与她交谈（魅力），或解读她的结界（智力） 防护结界（神秘）
2 预判失控魔法（感知），或指出她正在恐慌（魅力） 战术意识（智力）
3 主动帮她寻找导师（魅力） 幻象编织（神秘）
4 诉诸责任感（魅力）、硬扛过去（耐力），或贴身逼近（能力） 利用弱点（智力）
5 告诉她仍能弥补过错（魅力） 共情（感知）

第3回合不要选择 抢走她的法术书。这是本场战斗明确设置的陷阱，会让反击倍率翻倍。

胜利后会出现一条可选的野兽模式分支，需要兽性25、情趣经验300；更深一层的分支则需要情趣经验600。无论是否进入这些分支，都不会影响招募。

推荐路线

  1. 通过地牢第7层并击败Lindia。
  2. 确认12号员工已激活，然后在09:00-18:00前往训练室见她。
  3. 每天交谈四次；达到已知后，每天最多再送两本书。
  4. 达到已知后完成 页边笔记。
  5. 达到相识后完成 可控的魔法。方便的话，将神秘提升到40以选择特殊回答。
  6. 达到朋友后，完成 导师之问、法术控制笔记 和一次性的 集市约会。
  7. 达到亲密朋友后，完成 足够亲近；共情可解锁其中的特殊回答。
  8. 达到迷恋后，在训练室触发 不再是学徒。
  9. 利用塞壬岛活动与组合剧情，体验更多内容并获得更多好感度。
  10. 达到恋爱中后，如果想提升元素之石，就每天使用一次 亲密羁绊。

故障排查

酒吧里没有Lindia

  • 必须先在第7层击败她。
  • 她在酒吧的活动时间只有04:00-08:00。
  • 她的大部分个人路线位于训练室，时段为09:00-18:00。

下一段剧情没有触发

  • 在09:00-18:00进入青楼训练室。
  • 确认Lindia是已激活的员工。
  • 完成上一段剧情。
  • 达到所需关系阶段：已知、朋友或迷恋。
  • 如果同时有多个自动剧情满足条件，请在当前事件结束后离开，再重新进入。

找不到法术控制笔记

你需要达到朋友关系、拥有35点神秘、处于训练室活动时段，并且lindia_spell_control_notes_completed标记尚未完成。

找不到足够亲近

你需要达到亲密朋友，并且已经完成 可控的魔法。

找不到亲密羁绊

你需要达到恋爱中、关键物品中拥有元素之石、处于训练室活动时段，并且当天尚未与Lindia进行过羁绊。
