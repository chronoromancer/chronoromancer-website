Overview

Sabrina is the 21-year-old self-made illusionist and trick designer who runs the Magic Shop in Downtown — a hardcore skeptic and debunker who builds her reputation on figuring out how every trick is done. She dresses like a witch (purple cape, jagged-hem dress, witch hat) because the brand demands it and because she genuinely loves the aesthetic. Behind her shop are professional-grade workshops where she designs original illusions sold to acts worldwide.

Her romance arc is the one where the rationalist falls for actual magic. The hardcore skeptic gradually finds herself unable to debunk you — and the climax is her moving in to her shop (literally just admitting she's been sleeping in the back the whole time) and asking you to show her what real magic looks like.

She's a devoted Pencill and Listener (in-game band) superfan, has two intimate videos plus a "Fooled" plot scene, four dates (Comic Con, Fair, Stargazing, Boardwalk Sunset), and a special Couch Corner location that opens at Romantic stage when she stops pretending she lives somewhere else.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Mystic 35, Creativity 40, Ability 30 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Arcane Knowledge skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Show Me Real Magic (base 80), all 3 favorite stats met, Arcane Knowledge learned: (80 + 3) × 2 = 166 points per session — plus possible +1s.

Min-max tip: Mystic 35 is her unusual favorite stat. Most NPCs use Charm/Strength/Stamina. Arcane Knowledge skill is critical — it doubles points AND unlocks special-choice paths in H2H 2.

The Plot Chain — The Skeptic Falls

Sabrina's plot chain is one of the most narratively layered in the present era. Eight auto-triggering plots track her transformation from skeptical debunker to vulnerable believer.

"The Witch of Downtown" (Intro)

  • Auto-triggers: Magic Shop, on first entry
  • Brief introduction to Sabrina and her shop
  • Reward: +1 Sabrina

"The Debunker" — Known

  • Auto-triggers: Magic Shop, Known stage+
  • She demonstrates her debunker philosophy — explains how every trick works
  • Three-choice response (caring/funny/flirty)
  • Reward: Choice bonus only (+2 to +4 Sabrina)

"The Street Witch" — Acquaintance + Street Location

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown City Street, 11 AM – 4 PM, Acquaintance+
  • Her first public street performance — you encounter her in the wild
  • Rewards: +8 Sabrina + choice bonus

"The Best Classroom" — Friend + Street Location

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown City Street, 11 AM – 4 PM, Friend+
  • Second street performance — she explains her belief that street performing is the best way to learn the craft
  • Rewards: +8 Sabrina + choice bonus

"Behind the Curtain" — Close Friend

  • Auto-triggers: Magic Shop, Close Friend+
  • She finally invites you behind the workshop curtain — the actual workshop where she designs illusions
  • Rewards: +5 Sabrina + choice bonus
  • Outcome: Unlocks player access to her workshop area

"You Keep Coming Back" — Crush + Street Location

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown City Street, 11 AM – 4 PM, Crush+
  • Third street performance — she notices you've been showing up to all of these
  • Rewards: +10 Sabrina + choice bonus

"The Impossible Trick" — Crush

  • Auto-triggers: Magic Shop, Crush+
  • She shows you a trick she invented that even she can't fully explain in retrospect — it's hers and she's proud, but uncertain
  • Reward: +50 Sabrina (largest single auto-plot reward in her arc)
  • Triggers the dedicated "Fooled" video animation

"I Live Here" — Romantic

  • Auto-triggers: Magic Shop, Romantic stage, 9 PM – 11 PM
  • She admits she actually sleeps on the couch in the shop — has been the whole time
  • Multiple choice branches (caring/funny/flirty + watch + kiss-ask scenarios)
  • Reward: +40 Sabrina + multiple choice bonuses
  • Outcome: Unlocks the Couch Corner location — late-night content opens up

Total auto-plot relationship rewards: +1+0+8+8+5+10+50+40 = +122 base across the chain, before choice bonuses or skill multipliers. Her plot chain is the most rewarding in the present era.

Why these matter: Sabrina has no apartment visit branching conversation — H2H 2 is her equivalent. The Arcane Knowledge / Mystic Seduction special choices reward you for bringing the right skills to the table.

  • The plot chain is dense — eight auto-triggering plots totaling +122 base relationship. Visit Magic Shop AND Downtown street regularly to catch them all.
  • The Comic Con date references her band fandom (Pencill and Listener). It's one of the more character-flavored dates in the game.
  • Don't miss the I Live Here plot at Romantic — it unlocks the entire Couch Corner location and all late-game content. Visit the shop 9-11 PM at Romantic to trigger it.
  • Intimate Talk on the couch has unlimited daily limit — pure dialogue content for the couch persona. Worth using even though it's 0 base.
  • Two intimate videos at 1,000 and 1,200 points — there's a 200-point gap between them. Plan accordingly.
  • Most late-game actions are Patreon paywalled — Late Night Unwind, Intimate Night, Couch Cuddle, Show Me Real Magic, Boardwalk Date. Free demo players have limited late-game Sabrina access.
  • Four dates available — Comic Con, Fair, Stargazing, and Boardwalk Sunset. Don't skip any.
  • Show Me Real Magic at Intimate is +80 base — the single highest-reward action in her arc. Stack stats and skill before running it.

概述

Sabrina 是一位 21 岁、白手起家的幻术师兼机关设计师，在市中心经营魔法商店。她是坚定的怀疑论者和揭秘者，靠弄清每项把戏 如何 完成来建立声誉。她穿着女巫装（紫色披风、锯齿裙摆连衣裙和女巫帽）既是品牌需要，也是因为真心喜爱这种美学。商店后方设有专业工坊，她在那里设计原创幻术，并卖给世界各地的表演者。

她的恋爱线讲述的是 理性主义者爱上真正魔法 的故事。这个坚定的怀疑论者逐渐发现自己无法揭穿你；高潮则是她 搬进自己的商店（其实只是承认自己一直睡在后面），并请你向她展示真正的魔法。

她是游戏内乐队 Pencill 和 Listener 的狂热粉丝，拥有 两段亲密视频、一段“Fooled”剧情场景、4 场约会（漫展、游乐园、观星、木板路日落），以及在浪漫感阶段开放的特殊 沙发角落 地点——那时她终于不再假装自己住在别处。

加成计算（准确顺序）

好感度公式按以下顺序运行：

  1. 从行动的 基础点数 开始
  2. 每达到一项偏好属性便+1（神秘 35、创造力 40、能力 30——最高 +3）
  3. 玩家拥有 奥术知识 技能时 ×2
  4. 玩家拥有 “1 Ring” 关键物品时 +1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 +1
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时 +1
  7. 根据魅力值判定，有概率 +1（上限 50%，持有华丽吊坠时为 60%）

计算示例： 向我展示真正魔法（基础 80），3 项偏好属性全部达标，并学会奥术知识： (80 + 3) × 2 = 166 points 每次——另有可能获得数个 +1。

极限优化提示： 神秘 35 是她少见的偏好属性；大多数 NPC 偏好魅力／力量／耐力。奥术知识至关重要——既让点数翻倍，也解锁 H2H 2 的特殊路线。

剧情链——怀疑论者沦陷

Sabrina的剧情链是现代叙事层次最丰富的路线之一。8 段自动触发剧情记录了她从怀疑一切的揭秘者变为脆弱信徒的过程。

  • 奖励： Sabrina +8＋选项加成

  • 地点： 魔法商店沙发角落（浪漫感以上）

  • 奖励： 基础 +60＋每回合选项点数

H2H 2——“向我展示真正魔法”

  • 地点： 魔法商店（亲密阶段）
  • 费用： 90 分钟，−30 精力。Patreon 付费内容。
  • 3+ 回合： 她反常地提出请求——承认自己一生都在证明魔法不存在，却无法解释为何选择这栋建筑→拉上帘幕，请你展示真正魔法→魔法开始，气氛转变

  • 漫展约会会提及她喜爱的乐队（Pencill 和 Listener），角色特色鲜明。

  • 两段亲密视频分别需 1,000 和 1,200 点，中间相差 200 点，请提前规划。
  • 大多数后期行动为 Patreon 付费内容——深夜放松、亲密之夜、沙发依偎、向我展示真正魔法和木板路约会。免费试玩版的后期 Sabrina 内容有限。
  • 共有 4 场约会——漫展、游乐园、观星、木板路日落，请勿遗漏。
  • 亲密阶段的向我展示真正魔法基础 +80——是路线中单次奖励最高的行动。进行前请配置好属性和技能。
