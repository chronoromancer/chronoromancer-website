Overview

Sabrina’s Magic Shop route combines daytime shop visits, Downtown street events and later couch visits. Her workshop and couch each need their own access event.

Where to find them

Visit the Downtown Magic Shop 09:00–23:00 before Romantic (800). From Romantic onward, she leaves the shop portrait at 21:00 and appears at the couch 21:00–24:00. At Romantic, revisit the main shop 21:00–23:00 for the couch-access event even though its ordinary portrait has moved. Then use the couch travel action after 21:00.

Route and relationship milestones

The Workshop opens through the Close Friend (200) shop event. Her three street performances occur on Downtown City Street 11:00–16:00 at Acquaintance (50), Friend (100) and Crush (400). These are separate visits from her shop portrait.

Magic Practice needs Known (25) and Communication 50, takes 30 minutes and gives Ability +1 plus 8 direct affection once. Dates each complete once. The Voucher prize date is separate and consumes one Voucher; the Boardwalk date additionally needs Diane’s construction and the content access named in its notice.

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Late Night Unwind is a one-time couch H2H at Romantic (800). Show Me Real Magic is a different one-time H2H at Intimate (1600), started from the main shop. Their affection comes from conversation choices. The two couch videos require 1000 and 1200 ordinary affection, respectively; each costs 45 minutes and 40 Energy, gives 40 base affection and has a separate daily use.

The shop’s separate location lesson teaches Sleight of Hand: Ability 16, 09:00–18:00, $250, 180 minutes, 30 Energy and 40 Calm. It does not teach Arcane Knowledge. The Illusionist job belongs to the work system.

Optional reference: NPC intimacy gallery

This character has three current gallery rewards at 100, 250 and 500 total NPC intimacy experience. Open the character details, then Intimacy details, and select an unlocked thumbnail. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience, worker Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate; viewing a reward adds no experience.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Mystic 35; Creativity 40; Ability 30

Favorite skill: Arcane Knowledge

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

After Romantic, main-shop Stargazing starts 19:00–21:00 in practice; its raw date window continues later, but Sabrina has moved. The couch gate and ordinary affection thresholds must both be met. Neither couch video requires either H2H completed first.

概览

Sabrina 的神秘珍奇商店路线结合白天店内探访、市中心街道事件，以及后期沙发区相聚。工坊与沙发区分别有自己的开放事件。

在哪里找到

浪漫感（800）以前，可在 09:00–23:00 拜访市中心神秘珍奇商店。达到浪漫感后，她会在 21:00 离开普通店内肖像，改在沙发区 21:00–24:00 出现。此时仍需于 21:00–23:00 回到店内触发沙发区开放事件，即使普通肖像已经移走；之后再于 21:00 后使用前往沙发区的行动。

路线与关系节点

工坊由亲密朋友（200）的店内事件开放。三项街头表演事件在市中心城市街道 11:00–16:00 触发，分别要求相识（50）、朋友（100）与迷恋（400）；不能用反复点击店内肖像代替。

魔术练习要求已知（25）与沟通 50，耗时 30 分钟，一次性给予能力 +1 和 8 直接好感度。各项约会分别完成一次；兑换券奖品约会是独立活动，会消耗 1 兑换券。木板路约会还要求 Diane 已完成建设，以及进入提示所列的内容权限。

夜间沙发谈心在浪漫感（800）开放，为一次性内容。另一段展示真实魔法的谈心要求亲密（1600），从普通店内肖像开始；两者好感度由对话选择决定。两项沙发影像行动分别要求普通好感度 1000 与 1200，各耗时 45 分钟、消耗 40 精力，增加 40 基础好感度，并分别计算每天一次的次数。

店铺独立的地点课程教授巧手：能力 16、09:00–18:00，消耗 $250、180 分钟、30 精力与 40 镇定，不会教授奥术知识。幻术师工作属于工作系统。

可选查阅：NPC 亲密画廊

该角色目前有三项画廊奖励，分别要求累计 NPC 亲密经验 100、250 与 500。打开角色详情，再进入亲密详情，点击已解锁缩略图查看。普通好感度、玩家情欲经验、员工经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；查看奖励不会增加经验。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

神秘 35; 创造力 40; 能力 30

偏好技能：奥术知识

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

达到浪漫感后，店内观星约会实际在 19:00–21:00 开始；虽然约会时段写得更晚，但她届时已移动到沙发区。沙发入口与普通好感度门槛都要满足。两项沙发影像不额外要求先完成谈心。
