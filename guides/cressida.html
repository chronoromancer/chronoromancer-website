Overview

Cressida’s route begins at the Bureaucracy Automation Center in the Future Fringe Zone. It combines ten personal scenes, three H2Hs, two dates, two skill lessons and a one-time Intelligence reward. A later conversation can also improve the army’s Propaganda stat.

Where to find them

Before Authority to Refuse, find her at Bureaucracy Automation Center every day, 08:00–21:59. Afterwards she remains there on odd-numbered days and visits Operations Center on even-numbered days, also 08:00–21:59.

Her personal H2Hs, dates and Read the Fine Print action are offered at the Bureaucracy Center. On even days at Operations, her menu instead offers Talk, Information Brokering and Economic Warfare. Automatic scenes keep the locations listed below.

Route and relationship milestones

Return to the Center after completing each prerequisite. The early scenes need their affection stage; the later Accountability Layer and Authority to Refuse also need Story 34. The last ordinary personal scene is at Operations on an even-numbered day.

Talk takes 15 minutes and gives 1 base affection, four times daily; its allowance is shared across both locations. Read the Fine Print gives Intelligence +1 and 8 direct affection once, at Friend (100) with Perception 35.

Information Brokering can be learned at either workplace; Economic Warfare is taught at Operations after her schedule changes. Both require the supporter access shown by the lock and disappear once learned. Their Intelligence or Commerce requirement is separate from the relationship stage.

Her favorite stats are Intelligence, Communication, Perception. The learned Negotiation skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. H2Hs and automatic personal scenes complete once; ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • She has disappeared from the Center: after Authority to Refuse, use the Center on odd days and Operations on even days.
  • No H2H or date at Operations: those personal actions remain at the Center, so return on an odd day.
  • A late automatic scene is missing: check Story 34 and the evening start window, not just affection.
  • The Exception Becomes Policy starts at Operations on even days and requires No Approval Required.
  • A Promise Worth Signing needs In Love (3,000), the preceding personal conclusion and the last H2H.
  • The one-time fine-print reward gives Intelligence; buying a skill lesson is a different action.

概览

Cressida的路线从未来时代边缘区的官僚自动化中心开始，包含十场个人事件、三次谈心、两场约会、两种技能课程，以及一次智力奖励。较后的对话还可提高军队的宣传数值。

去哪里找

完成“拒绝权”前，每天08:00–21:59在官僚自动化中心见她。之后改为单数日在中心、双数日在作战中心，两处均为08:00–21:59。

个人谈心、约会和“阅读细则”仍从官僚自动化中心的头像选择。双数日在作战中心时，菜单提供交谈、情报交易和经济战。自动事件则保留在下表指定地点。

路线与关系进展

完成前置后，再次进入中心。前期事件主要检查好感；后期“问责层”和“拒绝权”还需要完成主线34。最后一场普通个人事件在双数日的作战中心触发。

交谈每次15分钟、1基础好感，每天4次，两处地点共用次数。朋友（100）、感知35时，可完成一次“阅读细则”，获得智力+1及8直接好感。

情报交易在两处工作地点都能学；经济战则在改变出没安排后，于作战中心教授。两项都需要锁形提示所示的支持者内容权限，学会后不再显示。智力或商业门槛，与好感阶段是不同条件。

两场约会不能代替主线34或个人谈心。

她偏好的属性为智力、沟通、感知。学会谈判可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。谈心与个人自动事件只完成一次；普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 中心里找不到她：完成“拒绝权”后，单数日去中心，双数日去作战中心。
  • 作战中心没有谈心或约会：这些个人行动留在官僚自动化中心，要单数日再去。
  • 后期自动事件没出现：检查主线34及夜间开始时段，不要只看好感。
  • “例外成为政策”在双数日的作战中心开始，并要求“无需批准”。
  • “值得签下的承诺”需要恋爱中（3000）、前一段个人路线及最后一次谈心。
  • “阅读细则”的一次性奖励是智力，和付费学习技能是不同的行动。
