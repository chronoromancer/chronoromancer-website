Overview

Cressida Vale is the Director of Civic Continuity at the Bureaucracy Automation Center, the senior human signature inside VOID's automated government. Her route, The Human Signature, asks one question: when a machine has already chosen the outcome, what does a human signature actually authorize? Her ending is not resignation. It is authorship.

Unlike most Future Era routes, her story begins with no story-beat gate: her first plot triggers on your first daytime visit to the Bureaucracy Automation Center. The full VOID reveal, her alliance, and her finale wait for Story Beat 034.

Route Arc

  1. A citizen is trapped in a form loop; Cressida cuts through it and notices the only person reading consequences instead of instructions.
  2. She demonstrates a lawful override and argues that a human signature creates accountability.
  3. She constructs a continuity exception for a resident the system deleted.
  4. The 47-step process sends Cressida herself to an AI therapist for issuing too many exceptions.
  5. A polite denial notice hides an execution timestamp that predates her review.
  6. She finds authenticated approvals bearing her biometric signature on cases she never saw.
  7. Ghost connects her evidence to VOID: her office is a human-accountability layer, and her mercies trained the system.
  8. She builds a hidden appeal interval between automated decision and physical enforcement — her first deliberate sabotage.
  9. She rejects a simple escape into TD and negotiates an alliance on her own terms.
  10. At the TD Operations Center she countersigns her first Resistance corridor and returns to her desk as deliberate cover.

Heart-to-Hearts

Each H2H contains five rounds and fifteen possible standard responses across caring, funny, and flirty tones. Special skill responses appear where defined.

After the Last Applicant

  • Stage: Friend+
  • Requires Proof of Residence
  • Time window: 19:00–22:00
  • Time: 75 minutes
  • Special choice skill: Empathy

Cressida explains the first case she failed to save and why leaving the desk would have been easier for her and worse for everyone still in the queue.

Unsigned

  • Stage: Close Friend+
  • Requires Signed in Advance and completion of After the Last Applicant
  • Time: 90 minutes
  • Special choice: Intelligence 40+

She shows the player the resignation she has rewritten for years, and admits she no longer knows whether her presence was restraint or permission.

No Approval Required

  • Stage: Crush+
  • Requires Authority to Refuse and completion of Unsigned
  • Time: 90 minutes
  • Special choice skill: Empathy

This is the first-kiss sequence. Cressida separates her decision about the Resistance from her desire for the player, asks clearly, and closes the distance herself. Completing it unlocks the finale.

Route Tips

  • Her route can begin very early: just visit the Bureaucracy Automation Center during the day.
  • Plots 6, 7, and 9 only trigger in the evening (18:00–22:00).
  • Plots 7 and 9 wait for Story Beat 034; finish the main story to that point before expecting the VOID reveal.
  • After Authority to Refuse, she splits her week: odd days at the Center, even days at TD Operations. Both offer Talk and Information Brokering.
  • The finale triggers at the TD Operations Center on an even day, not at the Bureaucracy Center.
  • Negotiation doubles normal relationship gains through her special-skill preference.

Writing Voice

Cressida is precise, economical, dry, and fully self-possessed. She uses administrative language as wit, threat, seduction, and eventually self-indictment. Flirtation is unmistakably adult: she notices desire, names it, and protects consent. Vulnerability arrives as exact admissions, never melodrama.
