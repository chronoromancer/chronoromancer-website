Spoiler note: This guide explains how the Investment System works — the math, the mechanics, the buffs, and the strategy. It avoids spoiling Portia or Jade's H2H scenes, character beats, or the Broker job's narrative flavor.

What the Investment System Is

The Investment System is your late-game passive-income engine. You buy into businesses ranging from a $50 Coffee Shop to a $5,000,000 Space Mining operation — 50 investments total, each upgradable through 5 levels. Once owned, every investment generates daily income that accumulates while you play. Walk in, click Collect, walk out richer.

Unlike work jobs (which trade time for money), investments scale without consuming your day. A late-game portfolio can pay tens of thousands per real-world play session with zero active input from you.

How to Find It

The Investment Planning office is in the Present Era → Downtown → Office Building → Investment Planning room.

  1. Travel to the Office Building in Present Downtown (open 9 AM - 5 PM)
  2. From the office lobby, use the Go to Investment Planning action to enter the planning room
  3. Inside, you'll see three actions: Investment Planning, Broker (work shift), and Return to Office Lobby

Both the office building and the planning room are gated to 9 AM – 5 PM. Outside those hours, you can't reach the room.

The Three Actions in the Room

Investment Planning (the portfolio screen)

  • Time cost: 0 minutes (free to open)
  • Hours: 9 AM - 5 PM
  • Opens the portfolio management screen — buy, upgrade, and collect

This is where you actually manage your investments. Opening the screen is free; only purchases/upgrades cost money.

Broker (the office work shift)

  • Time cost: 60 min, -30 energy
  • Hours: 9 AM - 5 PM
  • Stat requirement: Commerce 30+
  • Pay formula: random((Charm + Communication), (Commerce + Intelligence)) + Luck
  • Skill bonus: Market Analysis ×2.0
  • Stat growth: 8% chance per shift to gain +1 Commerce

Broker is the highest-ceiling proficiency-tier work job in the game. Four stats feed the formula, plus Luck. With Market Analysis (×2.0 multiplier) it scales massively. At Commerce/Charm/Communication/Intelligence all 50 plus 30 Luck, expect roughly $260–460 per shift with the skill — better than any single-stat career.

Return to Office Lobby

Travel action back to the office hub. 0 cost.

How Investments Work

The Daily Income Formula

For each investment you own:

Daily Income = (Total Cumulative Cost Invested in this slot) ÷ 50

Every dollar you sink in pays itself back in 50 days flat. Past that, it's pure profit.

Example — Coffee Shop:

Level Cost This Level Cumulative Base Daily
1 $50 $50 ~$1
2 $75 $125 ~$2.50
3 $110 $235 ~$4.70
4 $165 $400 ~$8
5 (MAX) $250 $650 ~$13

So a fully maxed Coffee Shop costs $650 total and pays ~$13/day forever. Same math scales to the multi-million-dollar investments — Space Mining maxed totals about $15,066,335 and pays ~$301,326/day base.

Risk Tiers — Variance, Not Average

Each investment has a risk tier that controls daily variance. The average payout is the same — it's the spread that differs.

Risk Min Variance Max Variance
Low 80% 110%
Medium 60% 140%
High 0% 200%

A High-risk investment can pay $0 today and 2× tomorrow. A Low-risk one is a steady drip. Over many days they average out near base, but Low-risk wins for predictable cash flow and High-risk wins for jackpot moments.

Earnings Accumulation

Earnings calculate only when you visit the screen. The system checks how many days have passed since your last visit, runs the daily calculation for each of those days (with risk variance applied each day), and adds the total to your pending collection pile.

You don't have to "log in" daily to collect. Skip a week, come back, and you'll get 7 days of earnings at once. There's no cap and no decay.

The Cash-Out Button

Inside the Investment Planning screen, the COLLECT: $X,XXX button shows your pending total. Click it to transfer the full pending amount to your wealth and reset the counter to zero. You can collect any time — there's no penalty for letting it accumulate, and no penalty for collecting often.

The Four Multipliers (They All Stack)

Four separate bonuses stack to boost your investment income. Get all four for max returns.

1. Investment Savvy (skill) — 15% off all costs

Every investment cost — purchases and upgrades — is reduced by 15%. On a $1,000,000 Banking investment, that's $150,000 saved on Level 1 alone. Across a fully built late-game portfolio, this skill saves you millions.

How to learn it: There are two paths.

Path 1 — Portia's Villa apartment H2H (the original source):

  • Choose the "Discuss Investment Savvy" special choice in Round 1

  • Requires you to NOT already have the skill

  • The Round 1 → 47 → 48 path ends with the skill being added

    To unlock the Villa visits in the first place:

    • 600+ relationship points with Portia (around Romantic stage)
    • Must have completed her prior "New Place" H2H ($300,000, requires 300 rel points + completed Mall Shopping H2H)
    • Visit between 7 PM - 11 PM at the Apartment Complex Downtown
    • Daily limit of 3 villa visits, so you have room to retry the conversation

Path 2 — Arestes (Master Thief, Thieves Guild Hidden Chamber):

  • Known+ relationship with Arestes
  • Intelligence 19+
  • 120 min, -50 energy, $500
  • The "Learn Investment Savvy" action appears in the Hidden Chamber once you qualify
  • Available 24/7 (Arestes is always at the Hidden Chamber)

Bonus synergy: Investment Savvy is also Portia's character special skill — having it doubles all relationship points earned with her going forward. So learning it (from either source) accelerates the rest of her romance arc.

2. Market Analysis (skill) — +20% to minimum variance

Boosts the minimum end of your daily variance roll by 20% (multiplicative on the floor). On Low-risk investments, the floor goes from 80% to 96%. On Medium, from 60% to 72%. On High-risk, 0% × 1.2 = still 0% (you can still get $0 days on High-risk even with this skill — it doesn't help bad days disappear, just makes mediocre days better).

Usage outside investments: Also gives ×2.0 income on the Broker work shift, and is Valerie's character special skill (doubles her relationship points).

How to learn it:

  • Jade (Brothel Master Bedroom) → 90 min, -40 energy, $300, Crush+ relationship with Jade, daily limit 20
  • Stat requirement: Commerce 17

3. Commerce stat — Commerce ÷ 1000 multiplier

Your Commerce stat applies a small multiplier: 1.0 + (Commerce / 1000).

  • Commerce 50 = 1.05× (+5% income)
  • Commerce 100 = 1.10× (+10%)
  • Commerce 150 = 1.15× (+15%)

Modest individually, but it stacks with the others and grows naturally if you're running the Broker shift (8% Commerce growth per shift). Commerce also gates the Broker job (30+) and Market Analysis (17+), so building it up serves multiple purposes.

4. Buffer Optimization (Patreon Master) — +5% flat

The Patreon Master perk applies a flat +5% multiplier to every day's earnings via the buffer_optimization_level flag. Same +5% applies across the brothel and proficiency career income too.

Strategy by Phase

Early Game (Day 1–20)

  • Buy the cheapest Low-risk investments first: Coffee Shop ($50) → Barbershop ($80) → Laundromat ($100)
  • Don't worry about upgrading yet — get more horses in the race
  • Daily income ramps to ~$5–10/day in this phase
  • Don't bother with High-risk yet — variance will burn you when cash is tight

Mid Game (Day 20–50)

  • Max all Low-risk investments to Level 5
  • Add Medium-risk to cover gaps (Delivery Co, Food Truck, Bookstore)
  • Priority: get to Portia at Villa stage before sinking serious cash. The 15% Investment Savvy discount on a $50,000 Private Island purchase = $7,500 saved per level. The earlier you have the skill, the more it saves.
  • Daily income: $50–150/day
  • Start running Broker shifts once Commerce hits 30 — feeds the wealth into more investments

Late Game (Day 50+)

  • Push into the big-ticket investments ($10k–$1M+)
  • Max Banking ($300k Level 1, Low-risk) — it's the biggest reliable Low-risk in the game
  • Consider High-risk for the upside (Space Mining maxed = ~$300k/day base, average)
  • Daily income: $500–5,000+ in mid-late, $20,000–100,000+ in true endgame
  • Combined with maxed Brothel + Broker shifts, money stops being a constraint

Strategy Notes

  • Earnings only calculate when you visit. Coming back after 30 real-world days gives you 30 days of payout in one cash-out — there's no "must check in daily" pressure.
  • No collection penalty, no urgency. You can let pending earnings sit forever or collect every visit. Whatever fits your playstyle.
  • Investment Savvy is the single highest-ROI skill in the game for wealth. 15% off forever, on a portfolio that will eventually total millions, is worth dropping a romance arc on Portia even if she's not your type. Some players prioritize Portia early purely for this.
  • High-risk averages the same payout as Low-risk over time. If you can stomach $0 days, the math works out. If you need predictable income (e.g. you have brothel maid wages that auto-deduct), stick with Low-risk for your foundation and add High-risk on top.
  • The Broker shift is the best work job in the game — but only if you build for it. Charm + Communication + Commerce + Intelligence all feeding the formula means you can't dump-stat anything. A balanced build pays off massively here.
  • Sleeping passes 8 hours. Sleep, wake up, collect. Sleep, wake up, collect. Investments accrue while you do other things.
  • Keep an eye on the COLLECT button color. It's grey when there's nothing to collect, green when there is. If it's green and big, walk into the office.

投资系统是什么

投资系统 是游戏后期获取被动收入的核心机制。你可以投资从 售价$50的咖啡店 到 售价$5,000,000的太空采矿业务 等各种产业——总计50项投资，每项均可升级至5级。买下后，每项投资都会产生每日收入，并在游戏过程中持续累积。走进去，点击收取，出来时就会更富有。

工作需要用时间换钱，而投资则能在 不占用一天时间 的情况下扩大收益。游戏后期的投资组合无需你主动操作，每次现实中的游玩期间便可带来数万美元的收入。

如何找到投资系统

投资规划办公室位于 现代时代 → 市中心 → 办公楼 → 投资规划 房间。

  1. 前往现代时代市中心的办公楼（上午9点至下午5点开放）
  2. 在办公楼大厅使用 前往投资规划 行动，进入规划室
  3. 进入后会看到三个行动：投资规划、经纪人（工作班次）和 返回办公楼大厅

办公楼和规划室都只在 上午9点至下午5点 开放。其他时段无法进入该房间。

房间内的三个行动

投资规划（投资组合界面）

  • 耗时： 0分钟（可免费打开）
  • 开放时间： 上午9点至下午5点
  • 打开投资组合管理界面，可购买、升级和收取收益

你需要在这里实际管理投资。打开界面免费，只有购买和升级会花钱。

经纪人（办公室工作班次）

  • 耗时： 60分钟，-30能量
  • 开放时间： 上午9点至下午5点
  • 属性要求： 商业30+
  • 报酬公式： random((Charm + Communication), (Commerce + Intelligence)) + Luck
  • 技能加成： 市场分析×2.0
  • 属性成长： 每次工作有8%几率获得+1商业

经纪人是 游戏中收入上限最高的熟练度级工作。公式会同时使用四项属性，并额外计入幸运。拥有市场分析（×2.0倍率）后，收入会大幅提升。当商业、魅力、沟通和智力均为50，幸运为30时，配合该技能，每班预计可赚约$260–460，优于任何只依赖单一属性的职业。

返回办公楼大厅

返回办公楼枢纽的移动行动，消耗为0。

投资的运作方式

每日收入公式

你拥有的每项投资均按以下公式计算：

每日收入＝（该栏位累计投入总成本）÷ 50

投入的每一美元都会在 整整50天 后回本。此后便是纯利润。

示例——咖啡店：

等级 本级成本 累计成本 基础每日收入
1 $50 $50 约$1
2 $75 $125 约$2.50
3 $110 $235 约$4.70
4 $165 $400 约$8
5（最高） $250 $650 约$13

因此，将咖啡店升满总计花费$650，此后将永久获得约$13/天。数百万美元级别的投资也使用同一套公式——太空采矿升满约需$15,066,335，基础每日收入约为$301,326。

风险等级——影响波动而非平均值

每项投资都有一个风险等级，用于控制每日收入的波动幅度。各风险等级的 平均 收益相同，区别只在于波动范围。

风险 最低波动值 最高波动值
低 80% 110%
中 60% 140%
高 0% 200%

高风险投资可能 今天收入$0，明天收入2×。低风险投资则会稳定地产生收益。经过许多天后，两者都会趋近基础平均值；低风险适合追求可预测的现金流，高风险则能带来中大奖般的瞬间。

收益累积

收益 只会在你打开该界面时计算。系统会检查距离上次访问经过了多少天，对其中每一天分别计算所有投资的收益（每天单独应用风险波动），再将总额加入待收取金额。

你不必每天“登录”收取。 即使离开一周，回来时也会一次获得7天的收益。累积金额没有上限，也不会衰减。

提现按钮

投资规划界面内的 收取：$X,XXX 按钮会显示待收取总额。点击即可将全部待收取金额转入财富，并把计数器归零。你随时都可以收取——让收益继续累积或频繁收取都没有惩罚。

四种倍率（可全部叠加）

共有四种不同的加成可以叠加，提高投资收入。集齐全部四种便可获得最大收益。

1. 投资智慧（技能）——所有成本降低15%

每项投资的购买和升级成本都会降低15%。以一项$1,000,000的银行业投资为例，仅1级就能节省$150,000。将游戏后期的投资组合全部建成后，此技能能为你节省 数百万美元。

学习方式： 共有两条途径。

途径1——Portia的别墅公寓谈心（原始来源）：

  • 在第1轮选择特殊选项 “讨论投资智慧”

  • 要求尚未拥有该技能

  • 依次经过第1轮→47→48，最后会获得该技能

    首先解锁别墅拜访的条件：

    • 与Portia的好感度达到 600+（约为浪漫感阶段）
    • 必须已完成她此前的“新住处”谈心（花费$300,000；要求300好感度，并完成商场购物谈心）
    • 在 晚上7点至11点 前往 市中心公寓楼
    • 别墅每天最多拜访3次，因此可以重新尝试该对话

途径2——Arestes（盗贼大师，盗贼公会隐藏密室）：

  • 与Arestes达到 已知+ 关系
  • 智力19+
  • 120分钟、-50能量、$500
  • 满足条件后，隐藏密室内会出现“学习投资智慧”行动
  • 24/7全天开放（Arestes始终位于隐藏密室）

额外协同： 投资智慧也是 Portia的角色专属技能；拥有该技能后，今后与她获得的 所有好感度翻倍。因此，无论从哪种途径学会，都能加快她后续的恋爱剧情。

2. 市场分析（技能）——最低波动值提高20%

将每日收益波动的最低值提高20%（对下限进行乘法运算）。低风险投资的下限会从80%变为96%，中风险从60%变为72%。高风险则是0%×1.2，结果仍为0%（即使拥有该技能，高风险投资依然可能出现收入$0的一天；它不会消除最糟糕的日子，只会改善普通偏低的收益）。

投资以外的用途： 经纪人工作收入也会获得 ×2.0 加成；此外，它还是 Valerie的角色专属技能（使与她获得的好感度翻倍）。

学习方式：

  • Jade（青楼主卧）→90分钟、-40能量、$300、与Jade达到 迷恋+关系，每日上限20次
  • 属性要求：商业17

3. 商业属性——商业÷1000倍率

商业属性会提供一项小幅倍率：1.0 + (Commerce / 1000)。

  • 商业50＝1.05×（收入+5%）
  • 商业100＝1.10×（+10%）
  • 商业150＝1.15×（+15%）

单独来看幅度不大，但它可以与其他加成叠加，并会在进行经纪人工作时自然成长（每班有8%几率提升商业）。商业还决定能否从事经纪人工作（30+）和学习市场分析（17+），所以提升它能同时满足多个用途。

4. 缓冲优化（Patreon大师）——固定+5%

Patreon大师 特权会通过buffer_optimization_level标记，为每日收益提供固定+5%倍率。同样的+5%也会应用于青楼和熟练度职业收入。

分阶段策略

游戏前期（第1–20天）

  • 优先购买最便宜的低风险投资：咖啡店（$50） → 理发店（$80） → 自助洗衣店（$100）
  • 暂时不用急着升级——先让更多项目开始产生收益
  • 此阶段每日收入可逐渐提高到约$5–10
  • 暂时不要碰高风险投资——资金紧张时，波动会让你吃不消

游戏中期（第20–50天）

  • 将所有低风险投资升至5级
  • 加入中风险投资填补空缺（配送公司、餐车、书店）
  • 优先让Portia的剧情推进至别墅阶段，再投入大笔资金。 投资智慧的15%折扣用在$50,000的私人岛屿购买上，每级可节省$7,500。越早获得该技能，节省的钱就越多。
  • 每日收入：$50–150
  • 商业达到30后开始进行经纪人工作，将赚来的财富继续投入更多项目

游戏后期（第50天以后）

  • 进军高价投资（$10k–$1M+）
  • 将银行业升满（$300k，1级，低风险）——这是游戏中规模最大且可靠的低风险投资
  • 可以考虑高风险项目以追求更高上限（太空采矿升满后，平均基础收入约为$300k/天）
  • 中后期每日收入约$500–5,000+，真正的大后期则可达$20,000–100,000+
  • 再配合升满的青楼和经纪人工作，金钱将不再构成限制

策略要点

  • 收益只在你访问时计算。 即使现实中30天后才回来，也会在一次提现中获得30天的收益，不存在“必须每天查看”的压力。
  • 收取没有惩罚，也无需着急。 待收取收益可以永远留在那里，也可以每次访问时都拿走，完全取决于你的玩法。
  • 投资智慧是游戏中对财富回报率最高的单项技能。 它能永久降低15%成本，而最终投资组合的总额将达到数百万。即使Portia并非你喜欢的类型，为此推进她的恋爱剧情也很值得。有些玩家纯粹为了这个技能而优先发展Portia。
  • 长期来看，高风险与低风险投资的平均收益相同。 如果你能接受收入$0的日子，数学上的结果没有问题。如果你需要可预测的收入（例如青楼女仆工资会自动扣除），就先用低风险投资打好基础，再把高风险投资叠加在上面。
  • 经纪人是游戏中最好的工作——但前提是围绕它培养属性。魅力、沟通、商业和智力都会参与计算，因此不能舍弃任何一项属性。均衡发展会在这里获得巨大回报。
  • 睡觉会推进8小时。 睡觉、醒来、收取；再睡觉、再醒来、再收取。你进行其他活动时，投资仍会累积收益。
  • 留意收取按钮的颜色。 没有可收取收益时为灰色，有收益时为绿色。如果它变绿且金额很大，就去一趟办公室吧。
