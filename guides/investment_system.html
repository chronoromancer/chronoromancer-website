Start a portfolio

Visit Present → Downtown → Office Building → Investment Planning, then choose Investment Planning between 09:00 and 16:59. Opening the portfolio and buying holdings take no in-game time. It contains 50 businesses, each with five investment levels and a separate company buyout.

  1. Choose a business you can afford. The tile quotes the next level; buy levels 1–5 in order. Each price is an additional payment.
  2. Let an in-game day pass and reopen the portfolio. New earnings enter COLLECT; press it to transfer them to Wealth. Pending money does not automatically buy more holdings.
  3. At Level 5, the tile becomes BUYOUT. Confirm the quoted purchase to become the owner. The tile then says ACQUIRED and the company counter rises.

There is no portfolio sell or downgrade action. Keep enough spendable money for your other plans; Holdings is an asset-value display, not a cash balance.

Investment levels and company ownership

Let S be the sum of a company’s five undiscounted prices. Its buyout value is 15 × S; after acquisition, the income calculation values the holding at 16 × S. Merely reaching Level 5 does not count as company ownership.

Investment Savvy discounts both level purchases and buyouts by 15%. Prices round to the nearest dollar, with halves rounded up. Nominal holdings, buyout valuations and income bases still use the original prices, so the discount does not weaken future income.

Daily returns and risk

A holding’s daily base is its cumulative nominal stake, plus its buyout value if acquired, divided by 50 and rounded half up. For each elapsed day, every funded business independently rolls a multiplier within its risk range. Each payout rounds separately; their sum then gains raw Commerce ÷ 1, 000 as a percentage multiplier. Commerce 50 means +5%, and 100 means +10%. An active supporter bonus benefit adds a further 5% after that rounding.

Market Analysis raises the lower bound by 20%. It does not raise every payout by 20%, and cannot improve High risk’s zero floor. High-risk days can pay zero, but do not subtract invested principal. These averages exclude whole-dollar rounding and portfolio bonuses.

When earnings accumulate

Only in-game days count. Closing the game for real-world days does not earn income. Reopening the portfolio calculates each unprocessed day once; reopening on the same day does not repeat those earnings. Collection transfers all positive pending earnings and clears that amount.

The calculation uses your holdings, Commerce and skills when you open the portfolio. It does not reconstruct historical portfolios. Entry settles pending days before you buy new levels there, so check the portfolio regularly when changing your build. Pending money has no interest, expiry or storage cap in this system.

A buyout’s nominal additional cost is roughly 50 days of its added base income. Risk, discounts, Commerce, supporter bonus and rounding change the result; this is a comparison tool, not a promised repayment date.

Useful skills and the Broker job

Investment Savvy requires Intelligence 19; its teachers include Portia and Arestes. Market Analysis requires Commerce 17 and is taught by Jade. Their personal lessons have their own relationship and payment conditions.

Broker is a separate job at Investment Planning: Commerce 30+, 09:00–16:59, 60 minutes and 30 Energy per shift, with an 8% chance for +1 Commerce. Its base wage rolls between modified Charm + Communication and modified Commerce + Intelligence, swapping the endpoints if necessary, then adds modified Luck. Market Analysis doubles that amount before separate job benefits. It does not double portfolio income.

If company progress stalls, check that the tile says ACQUIRED, not simply Owned Lv 5. If Wealth did not rise, open the portfolio after a day change and use COLLECT. For another income source, see the Rose & Thorn operations guide and its calculation reference.

开始投资

前往现代 → 市中心 → 办公楼 → 投资规划，在09:00–16:59选择投资规划。打开投资界面、购买投资等级或收购公司均不消耗游戏时间。共有50家企业，每家有5级投资，投满后还可另行买断。

  1. 点击付得起的企业。格子显示的是下一级价格，必须按1–5级依次购买；各级价格都要单独支付。
  2. 度过一个游戏日后重新打开界面，收益会进入收取栏。点击后才计入财富，待收收益不会自动复投。
  3. 达到5级后，格子改为买断。确认付款才算收购成功；此后显示已收购，公司计数随之增加。

投资界面没有卖出或降级操作。请给其他目标留足现金；「持有资产」显示资产估值，并不是可直接花费的财富。

投资等级与公司收购

设某企业五级的原价总和为S。买断估值为15 × S，收购后用于计算收入的资产价值变为16 × S。仅投到5级不算拥有公司。

投资眼光使各级投资和买断费用均降低15%，金额四舍五入，恰好半元时向上取整。持有资产、公司估值及收益基数仍按原价计算，因此省下的费用不会削弱收益。

每日收益与风险

每项投资的每日基础收益，等于已买等级的原价累计额，加上已收购公司的买断估值，再除以50，四舍五入且半元向上。每过一个游戏日，各企业分别在自己的风险区间内抽取倍率，单项收益先取整，再汇总并乘以1 + 基础商业÷1,000。商业50相当于+5%，100相当于+10%。有效的支持者加成权益会在这次取整后再加5%。

市场分析提高的是收益倍率的下限，增幅20%，并不是每次收入都增加20%。高风险下限为0，技能无法提高它。高风险某天可能完全没有收益，但不会倒扣投资本金。表内平均值未计入逐项取整和组合加成。

结算时间与收取

只计算游戏内经过的天数，现实中关游戏几天不会产生收入。重新进入投资界面时，每个尚未结算的游戏日只计算一次；同一天反复打开不会重复获利。收取会把全部正数待收款转入财富，并清空待收金额。

结算采用打开界面时的投资、商业和技能状态，不会还原过去每天的组合。进入时会先结算，再让你购买新等级；准备改变投资或加成时，宜定期回来查看。待收款本身没有利息、到期日或储存上限。

按未折扣的基础值比较，买断的额外费用约等于新增基础日收益的50倍。风险、折扣、商业、支持者加成及取整都会影响实际回收时间，不能把它当作保证回本日期。

相关技能与经纪人工作

投资眼光需要智力19，可向Portia或Arestes学习；市场分析需要商业17，由Jade教授。各人的授课还需满足自己的关系与费用条件。

经纪人是投资规划地点的独立工作：商业30+，开放时间09:00–16:59，每班消耗60分钟、30精力，有8%概率增加1点商业。基础工资在「修正后的魅力＋沟通」与「修正后的商业＋智力」之间随机抽取，必要时互换上下限，再加修正后的幸运。市场分析使该金额翻倍，之后另计工作相关加成；它不会使投资组合收入翻倍。

公司进度没增加时，先确认格子是「已收购」，而非仅有5级。财富没增加时，经过一个游戏日后重新打开界面并点击收取。其他经营收入可参阅玫瑰与荆棘经营指南和数值计算参考。
