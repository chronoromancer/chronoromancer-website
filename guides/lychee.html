Overview

Lychee travels between six morning stops before recruitment. Build affection on that route, then invite her to Rose & Thorn; afterwards her portrait moves to the Maid Quarters.

Route and relationship milestones

Own Rose & Thorn and reach Crush (400) for her to accept the 10-minute invitation. The button may appear earlier, but an early invitation is declined and does not recruit her. Acceptance has no hiring fee and unlocks her maid role.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

The four gift actions belong to her traveling menu. After joining, look in the Maid Quarters from 14:00–18:00 for Talk, the Charm lesson and Maid Service. All copies of an ordinary action share their daily count.

Maid Service needs Intimate (1600), takes 30 minutes and is a repeatable H2H with a 15-point base conversation reward. Beauty & Presentation is the separate one-time Charm increase at Known (25) and Communication 45.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Attractiveness 50; Charm 45; Creativity 35

Favorite skill: Fashion

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Morning locations change every hour; a long gift can finish after she leaves. Follow the next stop rather than waiting at the old one.
  • After acceptance, stop following the old morning route and use her afternoon Maid Quarters hours.

路线与关系节点

拥有玫瑰与荆棘，并达到迷恋（400）后，她才会接受耗时 10 分钟的邀请。按钮可能较早出现，但提前邀请会被拒绝，不会招募。接受时没有雇佣费，并开放她的女仆职能。

四项送礼行动属于招募前的巡回菜单。加入后请在女仆宿舍 14:00–18:00 探访，使用交谈、魅力课程及女仆服务。同一普通行动在不同地点共用每日次数。

女仆服务要求亲密（1600），耗时 30 分钟，是可重复的谈心，对话基础奖励为 15 好感度。另一项一次性魅力课程要求已知（25）与沟通 45。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。

吸引力 50; 魅力 45; 创造力 35

偏好技能：时尚

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择还可能在事件完成基础奖励之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

  • 上午地点每小时更换，送礼可能结束在她离开之后。请前往下一站，不必等在旧地点。
  • 接受邀请后，请改用女仆宿舍的下午时段，不再沿旧上午路线寻找。
