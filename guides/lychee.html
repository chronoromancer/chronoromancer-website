Overview

Lychee is the 22-year-old fashionable maid of the Earth Element — a beautiful pink-haired, pink-eyed beauty trained by Lady Serenade, a former courtesan who taught her the Art of Aesthetic Service. Born beautiful and always defined by appearance, Lychee learned from Lady Serenade that true Earth mastery means creating beauty in ALL things, not just oneself. She's fashion-obsessed, flirty, materialistic but genuinely sweet, self-aware about being "shallow," and her catchphrase is "Ooh, that's so pretty!"

Her recruitment is the most traditional romance arc of the five maids — no boss fight, no faction grind, no plot trigger. You meet her wandering between cleaning jobs across the medieval districts and build a relationship with her like any other NPC: daily Talk + gifts (chocolate / flowers / perfume / jewelry, with jewelry as her favorite). Once you reach Crush stage, the "Invite to Brothel" action becomes available, and she joyfully accepts.

She covers the 2 PM – 6 PM brothel cleaning shift (the third of five — afternoon slot, hands off from Mandarin at 2 PM and to Lime at 6 PM).

Where to Find Her — Pre-Recruitment

Lychee is the only maid who wanders multiple pre-recruitment locations on a strict hourly rotation. Each location has a 1-hour window, and the rotation moves through six different medieval locations every morning:

Time Location
7 AM – 8 AM Caravan Rest Stop
8 AM – 9 AM Castle Courtyard
9 AM – 10 AM Hovel District (Demesne)
10 AM – 11 AM Scriptorium Lobby
11 AM – 12 PM Peasant Huts
12 PM – 1 PM Merchants Quarter

After 1 PM she's done for the day. The next morning she starts the rotation over.

Why so spread out? Lychee's a contract cleaning worker — she rotates between jobs across the medieval districts. She's never permanent at any one location, which is why she dreams of "working somewhere really elegant" with "beautiful things everywhere."

Brothel Maids' Quarters

How to Recruit Her — The Crush Threshold

Lychee's recruitment is traditional romance gameplay. No plot scenario, no boss fight, no faction rep — just Talk + gifts until Crush stage, then invite her.

Trigger Conditions

  • Required: Brothel must be owned (the "Invite to Brothel" action requires the brothel_owned flag)
  • Required: Crush stage with Lychee (relationship_points ≥ Crush threshold)

The Method

  1. Find Lychee on her hourly rotation across 6 medieval locations
  2. Spam Talk (+2 base, daily 30) and gifts (Jewelry +10, Perfume +8, Flowers +6, Chocolate +5 — daily 10 each!)
  3. Push her relationship to Crush stage
  4. Use the "Invite to Brothel" action (10 min, daily 3, free to use)
  5. She accepts joyfully

What Happens on Invite (Below Crush)

If you try to invite her before Crush, she politely declines:

  • "That's really sweet of you to ask, but I'm happy with my work right now. We should be better friends first!"
  • "I appreciate the offer, but I like doing my own thing! Maybe ask me again when I know you better?"

What Happens on Invite (At Crush+)

She accepts immediately:

  • "You know what? You've been so sweet and generous... I'd love to work somewhere that has beautiful things!"
  • "After all these lovely gifts and nice conversations, how could I say no? You've made me so happy!"

The "Invite to Brothel" action triggers acquire_maid(3) — sets maid_3_acquired flag, increases the Maids' Quarters level by 1, and shifts her schedule to the brothel from then on.

Why this is the easiest recruitment: Mandarin needs a Greater Demon kill, Violet needs Bards Guild rep 50, Lime needs the Wizard Initiation Test, Rain needs the Researching skill in a Back Alleys encounter. Lychee just needs gifts and time. If you have a healthy stockpile of jewelry/perfume and 30 daily talk + 40 daily gift actions, she goes from Stranger to Crush very fast.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Attractiveness 50, Charm 45, Creativity 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Fashion skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Jewelry gift (base 10), all 3 favorite stats met, Fashion learned: (10 + 3) × 2 = 26 points per gift — at daily limit 10, that's +260/day from jewelry alone.

Min-max insight: Lychee's favorite stats are Attractiveness 50 + Charm 45 + Creativity 35. The Attractiveness 50 threshold is the most demanding — push it through training and equipment. Stack all three plus Fashion skill before recruiting her, and she'll hit Crush in a few in-game days.

Voice & Personality Highlights

Lychee's dialogue is constantly sweet, fashion-obsessed, and self-aware about being shallow:

  • Universal: Comments on appearance/clothing/colors/sparkle of everything; uses "Ooh," "so pretty," "sparkly," "gorgeous" constantly
  • Lady Serenade references: "Lady Serenade taught me that Earth Element is about making the material world beautiful! I love that philosophy!"
  • Element philosophy: Earth = beauty, material harmony, grounded aesthetics. Beauty isn't shallow — it lifts spirits.
  • Compliment fishing: "Do you think my hair looks okay today? I tried a new style this morning!"
  • Material desires: "I saw the most beautiful jewelry in the market — all gold and sparkly!"
  • Self-aware shallow: "That sounds really complicated — I usually just focus on making things look nice!" "I don't really understand all that, but you seem very smart and well-dressed!"
  • Prophecy awareness (sweet/romantic): "The old prophecy was real! Five maidens serving together under one special person! How romantic!"

Sister Dynamics (post other maids acquired)

  • Violet: "Such theatrical STYLE! I love how she makes everything look dramatic and pretty!"
  • Mandarin: "Intimidating but those MUSCLES are impressive! She needs better fashion advice though."
  • Lime: "Mysterious and otherworldly — great aesthetic! But she needs more COLOR in her wardrobe!"

She approaches the maid sisterhood like a friend group — she's the one bringing color theory and aesthetic balance, and she's vocal about how each sister's "look" could be improved.

Recommended Progression Path

Phase 1: Find Her on the Rotation

  • Visit any of her 6 pre-recruitment locations during her hourly window
  • Best targets: any of the morning locations on your usual route
  • Daily Talk + Gifts (jewelry priority — +10 base, daily 10)

Phase 2: Stranger → Acknowledged → Known → Acquaintance

  • Spam Talk (+2 × 30/day = +60/day) and Jewelry (+10 × 10/day = +100/day)
  • This is the fastest stage progression in the game
  • Stockpile jewelry from the Crafts & Jewelry Stall and other merchants

Phase 3: Friend → Close Friend → Crush

  • Continue daily Talk + Jewelry
  • Mix in Perfume and Flowers if jewelry runs low
  • At Crush stage, the "Invite to Brothel" action unlocks and she accepts

Phase 4: Brothel Phase — Stranger → Acknowledged → Known

  • After invite, her schedule shifts to Brothel Maids' Quarters 2-6 PM
  • She comes in at Stranger relationship stage from the brothel's perspective (her relationship resets — but she carries memory of you)
  • Daily Talk (+2, daily 30) — gifts no longer available
  • Push Communication to 45 for Beauty & Presentation

Phase 5: Known → Acquaintance → Friend

  • Beauty & Presentation at Comm 45: +1 Charm permanent, +10 rel
  • Cross-maid plots fire as you recruit more maids:
    • Lime + Lychee Elements (Acquaintance+ both)
    • Three Maidens First Meeting (Mandarin/Lychee/Rain all join)
    • Lime + Lychee Elemental Sisters (Friend+ both)

Phase 6: Friend → Close Friend → Crush

  • Continue daily Talk
  • Push favorite stats: Attractiveness 50, Charm 45, Creativity 35
  • Learn Fashion skill for permanent point doubling
  • Maids Compete Bedroom plot fires at Friend+ with all three (Mandarin/Lychee/Lime, Master Bedroom)

Phase 7: Intimate (Maid Service Unlocks)

  • Maid Service H2H unlocks at Intimate stage (+15 base, daily 3)
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Whispers of Six plot fires at Close Friend+ with all five maids

Phase 8: Endgame

  • Run Maid Service daily (+15 × 3 = +45 base/day)
  • Equip her with Lychee's Frost Crystal epic item (Kupie Love Training reward) — +30% Cleaning, +3 Cleaning all maids, +20% Variance — for cleaning powerhouse + team boost

Equipment — Lychee's Signature Item

Lychee's Frost Crystal (Epic Tier)

  • Source: Kupie Love Training achievement
  • Maid: Lychee only
  • Effects: +30% Cleaning (one), +3 Cleaning all maids, +20% Cleaning Variance
  • One of the most balanced epic items — boosts her own cleaning by 30%, gives every other maid +3 flat cleaning, and reduces team-wide cleaning randomness
  • The +3 cleaning all maids is brothel-wide value — every maid benefits

Recommended Stack

  • Slot 1 (L1+): Personal Polish Cloth ($350, +4 cleaning) early; replace with Lychee's Frost Crystal at L4
  • Slot 2 (L4+): Bonded Brush (+15% Cleaning) for max single-maid output, or Aromatic Incense (+5% Brothel Reputation) for thematic fashion-aesthetic vibe

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Lychee is the easiest maid to recruit if you have gold and gifts. No combat, no faction grind, no special skills required — just spam Talk + Jewelry until Crush.
  • Jewelry is her favorite gift (+10 base, daily 10) — stockpile from the Crafts & Jewelry Stall before starting the grind. With Fashion skill + 3 favorite stats, jewelry gives +26/gift × 10 = +260/day.
  • Her hourly rotation across 6 locations is unique — plan a route through one or two of her morning windows. The Castle Courtyard (8-9 AM), Scriptorium Lobby (10-11 AM), and Merchants Quarter (12-1 PM) tend to be on common routes.
  • The Invite to Brothel action requires Crush stage — try it earlier and she politely declines. Save the action for when you've crossed the Crush threshold.
  • Brothel must be owned for the Invite action to appear — the action is gated by the brothel_owned flag.
  • Talk's 30/day daily limit applies in both phases — same fastest stage-progression grind in the game (matches Violet/Mandarin/Lime/Rain).
  • Beauty & Presentation gives free +1 Charm permanent — Comm 45 gate, Charm 45 is also her favorite stat. Don't miss it.
  • Fashion skill doubles all her points — keep it learned. Thematically perfect for Earth Element.
  • Her favorite Attractiveness 50 threshold is high — push your Attractiveness through Cologne buffs and equipment before grinding gifts to maximize bonus stacking.
  • Lychee's Frost Crystal epic item is balanced excellence — single-maid 30% boost + team-wide +3 + 20% variance. Pursue Kupie Love Training for it.
  • No animations, no dates — Lychee's content is purely brothel-based after recruitment: Talk, Beauty & Presentation, Maid Service H2H.
  • All 6 images are interchangeable — her sparkle stays constant. The transformation is in dialogue, not art.
  • She's neutral-aligned — fits her sweet, materialistic-but-kind personality. No alignment shifts.

概述

Lychee 是22岁的 土元素 时尚女仆。她有一头粉色长发和一双粉色眼睛，美貌出众，师从昔日的交际花 Serenade夫人，学习审美服务之道。Lychee天生美丽，也始终被他人以外貌定义；Serenade夫人让她明白，真正的土元素造诣，在于为万事万物创造美，而不只是打扮自己。她痴迷时尚、善于调情、物质至上，却又真诚甜美；她知道自己有些“肤浅”，也常把 “哇，好漂亮！” 挂在嘴边。

她的招募方式是五名女仆中 最传统的恋爱路线——不必挑战首领，不需要派系声望，也没有特殊剧情触发条件。你会在中世纪各城区之间奔波做清洁工作的她相遇，并像培养普通NPC一样 与她发展关系：每天交谈并赠送礼物（巧克力／鲜花／香水／珠宝，其中珠宝是她的最爱）。达到 迷恋阶段 后，“邀请到青楼” 行动便会出现，她也会欣然接受。

她负责青楼 下午2点至6点 的清洁轮班（五个班次中的第三班，也就是下午班；下午2点接替Mandarin，下午6点交班给Lime）。

在哪里找到她——招募前

Lychee是 唯一会在招募前往返多个地点的女仆。她每天早上都会严格按照小时轮换；每个地点的停留窗口为1小时，整条路线会经过六个不同的中世纪地点：

时间 地点

上午8点至9点 城堡庭院
上午9点至10点 棚户区（国王领地）
上午10点至11点 抄写室大厅
上午11点至中午12点 农民小屋
中午12点至下午1点 商人区

下午1点后，她当天的工作便结束了。第二天早上，她会重新从第一个地点开始轮班。

为什么分布得这么散？ Lychee是一名承接零工的清洁工，会在中世纪各城区的不同雇主之间轮转。这正是她为何梦想着“在一个真正高雅的地方工作”，那里还要“到处都是漂亮东西”。

如何招募她——迷恋门槛

Lychee采用的是 传统恋爱玩法。你不必经历剧情遭遇、首领战或派系声望考验——只需不断交谈、赠送礼物，直至达到迷恋阶段，然后邀请她。

触发条件

  • 要求： 必须已经拥有青楼（“邀请到青楼”行动需要brothel_owned标记）
  • 要求： 与Lychee达到 迷恋阶段（relationship_points ≥ Crush threshold）

招募方法

  1. 按照每小时轮换日程，在6个中世纪地点之一找到Lychee
  2. 大量使用 交谈（基础+2，每日上限30次）并赠送礼物（珠宝+10、香水+8、鲜花+6、巧克力+5——每种每天都能送10次！）
  3. 将关系提升至 迷恋阶段
  4. 使用 “邀请到青楼” 行动（10分钟，每日上限3次，免费）
  5. 她会欣然接受

未达到迷恋时邀请

如果在达到迷恋阶段前邀请她，她会礼貌拒绝：

  • “你愿意邀请我真的很贴心，不过我现在对自己的工作挺满意的。我们还是先成为更好的朋友吧！”
  • “谢谢你的邀请，不过我喜欢按自己的方式做事！也许等我们更熟悉一些，你可以再问问我？”

达到迷恋以上时邀请

她会立刻接受：

  • “你知道吗？你一直对我这么体贴，还这么慷慨……我很愿意去一个满是漂亮东西的地方工作！”
  • “收到这么多可爱的礼物，又和你聊了那么多开心的事，我怎么可能拒绝？你让我好幸福！”

“邀请到青楼”行动会触发acquire_maid(3)——设置maid_3_acquired标记，使女仆宿舍等级提升1级，并从此将她的日程转移到青楼。

为什么这是最容易完成的招募： Mandarin要求击败高阶恶魔，Violet要求吟游诗人公会声望达到50，Lime要求通过巫师入门试炼，Rain则要求在后巷遭遇中拥有研究技能。Lychee只需要礼物和时间。 如果你囤积了大量珠宝／香水，又能每天进行30次交谈和40次赠礼，她很快便会从陌生人升至迷恋。

招募后行动（青楼女仆宿舍）

招募Lychee后，她会改为提供标准的女仆行动组合：

行动 耗时 阶段 每日上限 点数

招募后无法继续送礼。 她加入青楼后，所有赠礼行动都会消失。此后的感情培养会转为完全依靠交谈推进（仍为基础+2，每日上限30次）。

交谈

这是她的日常行动，会触发Lychee的三类对话系统（一般闲聊／尚未找到其他女仆／已经找到其他女仆）。对话内容包括在漂亮场所工作的感受、按颜色整理物品、闪亮美学、时尚观察，以及她对其他女仆的看法。

美丽与展现 （一次性）

这是她唯一能永久提高属性的行动。要求达到 已知阶段 且 沟通达到45以上。奖励：永久+1魅力，好感度+10。

“Serenade夫人教我，土元素关乎让物质世界变得美丽！当你带着爱安排漂亮的东西，让空间闪闪发亮时，你其实是在创造幸福！美并不肤浅——它能振奋每个人的精神，让生活值得继续！”

女仆服务 （交心谈话，亲密阶段）

她的亲密交心谈话，达到亲密阶段后可以进行，每日上限3次。完成后会标记附加内容图库条目lc_h_1。

加成计算（精确顺序）

好感度会按照以下顺序计算：

  1. 从行动的 基础点数 开始
  2. 每满足一项偏好属性便 +1（吸引力50、魅力45、创造力35——最多+3）
  3. 玩家拥有 时尚 技能时 ×2（她的特殊／偏好技能）
  4. 玩家拥有 “1 Ring” 关键物品时 +1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 +1
  6. 缓冲区优化等级生效时 +1
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，成功时 +1（上限50%；拥有华丽挂坠时为60%）

计算示例： 赠送珠宝（基础10），满足全部3项偏好属性，并学会时尚： (10 + 3) × 2 = 26 points，每件礼物均按此计算——每日上限10件，仅靠珠宝每天就能获得260点。

极限优化提示： Lychee的偏好属性是 吸引力50+魅力45+创造力35。其中吸引力50的门槛最高，应通过训练和装备加以提升。招募她之前先叠满三项属性并学会时尚，她只需几个游戏日便能达到迷恋。

说话风格与性格亮点

Lychee的对话总是 甜美可爱、痴迷时尚，而且清楚自己有些肤浅：

  • 通用特点： 评论万事万物的外表／服装／颜色／闪亮程度；经常使用 “哇”、“好漂亮”、“闪闪发光”、“美极了”
  • 提及Serenade夫人： “Serenade夫人教我，土元素关乎让物质世界变得美丽！我喜欢这个理念！”
  • 元素理念： 土代表美、物质和谐与脚踏实地的审美。美并不肤浅——它能振奋人心。
  • 讨要称赞： “你觉得我今天的头发还好吗？我早上试了个新发型！”
  • 物质欲望： “我在市场看见了最美的珠宝，全是金色又闪亮！”
  • 知道自己肤浅： “听起来好复杂——我通常只关心怎么把东西弄得漂亮！” “那些我不太懂，不过你看起来既聪明，衣着品味又好！”
  • 意识到预言（甜蜜／浪漫）： “古老的预言是真的！五位少女一同侍奉某个特别的人！多浪漫呀！”

推荐推进路线

阶段1：按照轮换日程找到她

  • 在6个招募前地点之一，于对应的1小时窗口内找到她
  • 最方便的选择，是挑选平时路线会经过的任一清晨地点
  • 每日交谈并赠送礼物（优先珠宝——基础+10，每日上限10件）

阶段2：陌生人→已遇见→已知→相识

  • 大量使用交谈（+2×每天30次＝每天+60）并赠送珠宝（+10×每天10件＝每天+100）
  • 这是游戏中关系阶段提升最快的路线
  • 从工艺品与珠宝摊位及其他商人处囤积珠宝

阶段3：朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 继续每日交谈并赠送珠宝
  • 珠宝不足时，混合赠送香水和鲜花
  • 达到 迷恋阶段 后，“邀请到青楼” 行动便会解锁，她也会接受

阶段4：青楼阶段——陌生人→已遇见→已知

  • 接受邀请后，她的日程会转移到青楼女仆宿舍，时间为下午2点至6点
  • 从青楼角度看，她会以陌生人关系阶段加入（关系会重置，但她仍记得你）
  • 每日交谈（+2，每日上限30次）——无法再送礼
  • 将 沟通提升至45，以便进行美丽与展现

阶段5：已知→相识→朋友

  • 沟通达到45后进行 美丽与展现：永久+1魅力，好感度+10
  • 招募更多女仆后，会触发联动剧情：
    • 元素对照（Lime与Lychee均达到相识以上）
    • 三位元素少女的初次会面（Mandarin、Lychee和Rain全部加入）
    • 元素姐妹（Lime与Lychee均达到朋友以上）

阶段6：朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 继续每日交谈
  • 提升偏好属性：吸引力50、魅力45、创造力35
  • 学会 时尚 技能，使点数永久翻倍
  • Mandarin、Lychee和Lime均达到朋友以上时，会在主卧室触发 主人的卧室

阶段7：亲密（解锁女仆服务）

  • 达到亲密阶段后，解锁 女仆服务交心谈话（基础+15，每日上限3次）
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 五名女仆均达到亲密朋友以上时，会触发 六之低语

阶段8：终局

  • 每天进行女仆服务（+15×3＝每天基础+45）
  • 为她装备史诗物品 Lychee的霜冻水晶（Kupie爱之训练奖励）——清洁+30%、全体女仆清洁+3、清洁浮动+20%；既能让她成为清洁主力，也能强化整支队伍

装备——Lychee的专属物品

Lychee的霜冻水晶（史诗级）

  • 来源： Kupie爱之训练成就
  • 适用女仆： 仅限Lychee
  • 效果： 单人清洁+30%、全体女仆清洁+3、清洁浮动+20%
  • 这是最均衡的史诗物品之一——使她自身的清洁提高30%，为其他每名女仆提供固定+3清洁，并降低整支队伍清洁结果的随机性
  • 全体女仆清洁+3 会影响整个青楼——每名女仆都能从中受益

推荐搭配

  • 栏位1（L1+）： 前期使用个人擦亮布（$350，清洁+4）；L4时替换为 Lychee的霜冻水晶
  • 栏位2（L4+）： 追求单人最高产出时使用 绑定画笔（清洁+15%）；若想搭配符合时尚审美主题的装备，则使用 芳香熏香（青楼声望+5%）

技巧与最优玩法

  • 只要拥有金币和礼物，Lychee就是最容易招募的女仆。 不必战斗，不用刷派系，也不需要特殊技能——只需不断交谈并赠送珠宝，直至达到迷恋。
  • 珠宝是她最喜欢的礼物（基础+10，每日上限10件）——开始培养前，应先从工艺品与珠宝摊位囤货。拥有时尚技能并满足3项偏好属性时，每件珠宝可提供 +26×10件＝每天+260。
  • 她每小时在6个地点之间轮换，是独一无二的日程——规划路线时，可挑选一两个清晨时间窗口。城堡庭院（上午8点至9点）、抄写室大厅（上午10点至11点）和商人区（中午12点至下午1点）通常都位于常用路线附近。
  • “邀请到青楼”要求达到迷恋阶段——提前尝试只会得到她的礼貌拒绝。跨过迷恋门槛后再使用该行动。
  • 青楼必须已经归你所有，“邀请到青楼”才会出现——该行动受brothel_owned标记限制。
  • 交谈每日30次的上限在两个阶段都会生效——与Violet／Mandarin／Lime／Rain相同，这是游戏中最快的阶段推进方式。
  • 美丽与展现会永久免费+1魅力——要求沟通45，而魅力45也是她的一项偏好属性，千万不要错过。
  • 时尚技能会使她获得的全部点数翻倍——务必学会并保留。这个技能也与土元素的主题完美契合。
  • 她偏好的吸引力50门槛很高——开始大量赠礼前，应通过古龙水增益和装备提高吸引力，以充分叠加奖励。
  • Lychee的霜冻水晶是一件表现全面的优秀史诗装备——自身提高30%、团队固定+3、浮动+20%。应推进Kupie爱之训练来取得它。
  • 没有动画，也没有约会——招募后，Lychee的内容完全围绕青楼展开：交谈、美丽与展现、女仆服务交心谈话。
  • 全部6张立绘会交替使用——她始终光彩照人。真正发生变化的是对话，而不是美术。
  • 她属于中立阵营——符合她甜美、物质至上却心地善良的性格。不会发生阵营变化。
