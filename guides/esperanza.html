Overview

Esperanza offers repeatable stat training, a skill lesson and personal conversations. Her daytime ballroom actions, odd-day evening visits and late remote visits are separate schedules.

Where to find Esperanza

Find her at Medieval → Kings Demesne → Grand Ballroom 12:00–17:00 daily, or Noble Quarter 18:00–21:00 on odd days. The late Visit Esperanza and Seduce Esperanza options are remote Noble Quarter actions 21:00–24:00, rather than extensions of the odd-day portrait schedule.

Route and relationship milestones

Dancing Toward opens at Acquaintance(50); Last Dance at Close Friend(200). The Bazaar date is offered from the ballroom, so its practical start window is 14:00–17:00. Sunset Walk is offered during the Noble Quarter appearance,18:00–21:00 on odd days.

Her brothel-related visits are separate optional events after Elara’s refinement introduction, with the relevant workers recruited and available. They do not replace Esperanza’s own conversations or skill requirements.

Activities and rewards

Dance Training is the repeatable stat activity: each safe completion gives Charm,Ability and Stamina+1 each. It costs 120 minutes,$180 and 75 Energy, once per day. It does not teach Dance Mastery; Dance Together requires that skill separately.

The Romantic skill lesson requires Crush(400),Charm 20,$400,200 minutes,40 Energy and 40 Calm. Grace and Elegance gives Charm+1 once at Known(25) with Communication 65 and 30 minutes.

The later remote visit needs Romantic(800),150 minutes and 80 Energy. The separate video action needs 1,000 relationship points,60 minutes,40 Energy and the corresponding additional-content access. Their listed starting hours are daily, even though the earlier Noble Quarter portrait is odd-day only.

Missing actions and useful checks

If an afternoon date is missing, check the ballroom’s 17:00 departure time. For Sunset Walk, check odd day and Close Friend. For a late visit, use the Noble Quarter location action after 21:00.

Do not repeat Dance Training beyond its safe daily allowance expecting another free stat increase. The Romantic lesson disappears once the skill is learned.

概览

Esperanza提供可重复属性训练、技能课程与个人谈心。白天舞厅动作、单数日傍晚行程和深夜远程拜访各自计算时间。

出现地点与时间

在中世纪 → 国王领地 → 大舞厅，每天12:00—17:00可找到她；单数日18:00—21:00则去贵族区。深夜拜访与引诱选项是贵族区21:00—24:00的独立地点动作，不是单数日头像行程的延长。

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

「你舞向的目标」要求相识（50），「夜晚的最后一支舞」要求亲密朋友（200）。集市约会从舞厅提供，因此实际开始时段为14:00—17:00；日落散步则随贵族区头像出现，只在单数日18:00—21:00可用。

与青楼有关的拜访属于另一路可选事件，需要先完成Elara的相应礼仪引介，相关员工也须已招募且能够参与。它们不能代替Esperanza自己的谈心或技能门槛。

行动与奖励

舞蹈训练是可重复的属性活动：每次安全完成，魅力、能力、耐力各+1。花费120分钟、财富180、精力75，每天一次；它不会教会舞蹈精通，一起跳舞所需的该技能要另外学习。

浪漫技能课程要求迷恋（400）、魅力20、财富400、200分钟、精力40与镇定40，实际技能名为「浪漫」。已知（25）且沟通65时，还可花30分钟进行一次「优雅与风度」，永久增加魅力1。

深夜远程拜访要求浪漫感（800），耗150分钟、精力80；另一项视频互动要求关系点数1,000，耗60分钟、精力40，并有相应追加内容权限。它们的开始时段是每天，不受较早贵族区头像只在单数日出现的限制。

动作缺失时的检查

下午约会缺失时，检查是否已经到舞厅17:00离场时间。日落散步要检查单数日与亲密朋友条件。深夜拜访则在21:00后使用贵族区地点动作。

舞蹈训练超过每日安全额度后，不应期待再获得一份属性提升；浪漫课程学会技能后会消失。
