Overview

Riki’s relationship route opens after her main-story recruitment. It includes ten automatic scenes, three H2Hs, two optional dates, an Archery Focus lesson and a one-time Perception exercise and two later private activities. Her ordinary relationship and Army unit progress are separate.

Where to find them

After Story 32, carry $20,000 and visit Robot Bar, 18:00–23:59, for Story 33. Completing the recruitment costs $20,000 and 30 minutes. Riki then moves to Barracks, all day, every day, where her personal menu is available.

Complete Story 34 at Operations Center before starting her personal sequence. Several later scenes use Armory, Hyperloop Transit Nexus, the Multi-Level Automated Parking Complex and Operations rather than her regular Barracks portrait.

Later main-story and shared Resistance scenes can give additional Riki affection. These rewards do not remove the personal scene order or count as NPC intimacy experience.

Her favorite stats are Perception, Rogue, Ability. The learned Archery Focus skill improves her relationship bonus.

Resistance Clout gifts

After recruiting Riki to your army, reach 110 Resistance Clout. From Riki’s portrait at Barracks during their available hours, choose “Outside the Terms”. Complete this one-time scene to receive Boss Hunter Emblem. It takes no in-game time and checks Clout without spending it.

After recruiting Riki to your army, reach 30 Resistance Clout and complete “Chosen Position”. From Riki’s portrait at Barracks during their available hours, choose “A Little More Reach”. Complete this one-time scene to receive Horizon Relay. It takes no in-game time and checks Clout without spending it.

The reward enters Army Equipment; equip it on the intended hero in Barracks. Effects and alternatives: TD Army.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Only her story appearance is available: finish Story 33, then use Barracks.
  • Recruitment will not start: check Story 32, the Robot Bar evening hours and $20,000 available money.
  • The first personal scene requires Story 34, even after you have already recruited her.
  • A higher stage does not replace an unfinished location visit. The Armory Clause is at Armory, Shared Sightline at Hyperloop Transit Nexus, and A Clean Target at the Multi-Level Automated Parking Complex.
  • Range Estimation requires The Armory Clause; it is not granted automatically when that scene finishes.
  • The dates are optional. For Chosen Position, complete the last H2H after The Renewal Window.
  • The two private activities need Romantic (800) and Chosen Position. Completing the earlier Crush-stage story does not remove the stage requirement.

概览

Riki的关系路线在主线招募后开放，包含十场自动事件、三次谈心、两场可选约会、弓术专注课程，一次感知练习，以及后续两种私人活动。普通关系与军队单位成长分别计算。

去哪里找

完成主线32后，准备$20,000，于18:00–23:59前往机器人酒吧触发主线33。完成招募会消耗$20,000及30分钟，之后Riki搬到兵营，全天可见，个人菜单也在这里。

先在作战中心完成主线34，再开始个人事件链。后续部分事件在军械库、超环交通枢纽、多层自动停车楼和作战中心触发，不在她平时的兵营头像处。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。谈心与个人自动事件只完成一次；普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

反抗军声望赠礼

取得Riki的军队单位后，反抗军声望达到110，在Riki可用的时段，从兵营的头像选择「合约之外」。完成这段一次性事件，可取得「首领猎手徽章」。行动不耗游戏内时间，只检查声望，不会扣除声望。

取得Riki的军队单位后，反抗军声望达到30，并完成「选择的位置」，在Riki可用的时段，从兵营的头像选择「再远一点」。完成这段一次性事件，可取得「地平线中继器」。行动不耗游戏内时间，只检查声望，不会扣除声望。

奖励进入军队装备，之后还要在兵营为目标英雄装备。效果与选择详见塔防军队与训练。

卡住时检查什么

  • 只有主线见面内容：先完成主线33，再去兵营。
  • 招募没有开始：检查主线32、机器人酒吧夜间时段，以及可用金钱是否达到$20,000。
  • 即使已经招募，第一场个人事件仍需要完成主线34。
  • 好感更高不能代替地点回访：“军械库条款”在军械库，“共享视线”在超环交通枢纽，“干净的目标”在多层自动停车楼。
  • “距离估算”需要“军械库条款”，完成事件不会自动领取练习奖励。
  • 两场约会都是可选；“选择的位置”需要在“续约期限”后完成最后一次谈心。
  • 两种私人活动需要浪漫感（800）和“选择的位置”，完成先前迷恋阶段的故事，仍不能代替这个好感要求。
