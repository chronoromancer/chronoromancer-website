Overview

Muramasa is an independent instructor with daytime training, evening meetings and a later brothel Training Room branch. Her romantic route, combat lessons and apprentice-room progression are separate paths that can be developed alongside one another.

Where to find Muramasa

Meet her at the Knight’s Training Grounds daily 08:00–18:00, then at Traveler’s Inn Tavern 20:00–23:00. After the Training Room introduction, she also appears there 18:00–20:00. She does not become a numbered brothel worker.

Route and relationship milestones

The Empty Ring requires you to own the brothel and have its Training Room built. Prepare that room before trying to continue her early relationship route.

Finish her introduction and The Empty Ring at the grounds. With both Muramasa and Kent Known (25), visit the Inn 20:00–23:00 for their arrangement. This opens Listen Before Moving at Acquaintance (50). At Friend (100), complete the healer encounter with Amelia and Kent, then The Measure of Mercy.

At Close Friend (200), select A Blade at Rest at the Inn. Complete the dinner date and that scene, then take The Road With No Job from the grounds 08:00–15:00. At Crush (400), Between Heartbeats follows the road date. A Place in the Silence at Romantic (800) opens her two separate private activities.

For the room branch, visit the built Training Room 18:00–20:00 after The Empty Ring, at Acknowledged (10). A Wider Ring later requires Close Friend, the room introduction and A Blade at Rest; it starts the room’s base service level and apprentice progression.

Activities and rewards

Talk gives 1 base point, five safe uses shared across her three locations. Share Wine at the Inn gives 7 base points for 30 minutes and one Wine, once daily. Sense Mastery grants Perception +1 once at Known and Communication 45.

Katana Training grants Perception +1 and Ability +1 each time, plus 1 base relationship point, up to three safe sessions daily. All three cost 225 minutes, $450, 120 Energy and 30 Calm before modifiers. An additional session grants no training stats and can lower relationship.

Stormslash teaches the named sword skill once. Blademaster instead unlocks a combat path: Close Friend (200), Fighters Guild Clout 200, 300 minutes, 150 Energy and 100 Calm. After acquisition, progress the path through combat rather than repeating the lesson.

If you own Sword Stone and reach In Love (3000), Intimate Bonding adds one stone level for 60 minutes, once daily shared between the grounds and unlocked Training Room. The action requires the existing stone; it does not grant it. Her evening animation and longer remote room visit have separate daily allowances; the latter starts as late as 23:59.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

The two dates and the three later story actions must be selected from her portrait; re-entering a room does not replace the named action. Training Room progression is separate from the romantic-room unlock. Keep enough Energy and Calm for Blademaster’s unusually costly lesson.

概览

Muramasa是独立教官，白天提供训练，晚上可以会面，后续还有青楼训练室分支。感情路线、战斗教学与学徒房间进展各自独立，可以并行培养。

出现地点与时间

每天08:00—18:00可在骑士训练场见她，20:00—23:00在旅人旅店酒馆。训练室初见完成后，她也会于18:00—20:00在那里出现；她不会成为青楼普通员工。

路线与关系进展

空荡的演武圈还要求已拥有青楼并建成训练室，推进早期关系路线前请先准备这个房间。

先在训练场完成初见和空荡的演武圈。Muramasa与Kent都达到已知（25）后，20:00—23:00到旅店推进安排事件，再于相识（50）开放先听再动。达到朋友（100），与Amelia、Kent完成诊疗所共同事件后，继续仁慈的尺度。

亲密朋友（200）时，在旅店选择刀锋暂歇。晚餐约会与该事件都完成后，08:00—15:00从训练场开始没有任务的旅途。达到迷恋（400），旅途约会后开放心跳之间；浪漫感（800）的沉默中的位置则会开放两项独立的私人活动。

房间分支需要先建成训练室，完成空荡的演武圈，关系达到已遇见（10），再于18:00—20:00进入。后续更广的演武圈要求亲密朋友、训练室初见及刀锋暂歇，完成后开始房间的基础服务等级与学徒进展。

行动与奖励

交谈每次获得1点基础关系，三处地点共用每日5次安全额度。分享美酒消耗30分钟和一瓶葡萄酒，获得7点基础关系，每日一次。感官精通要求已知、沟通45，一次性给予感知+1。

武士刀训练每次增加感知1点、能力1点，以及1点基础关系，每日安全额度3次。三次合计消耗225分钟、450财富、120精力、30镇定，之后再受修正影响。额外训练不会增加训练属性，还可能降低关系。

风暴斩是一次性的指定剑技教学。剑术大师则开放战斗路线，要求亲密朋友（200）、战士公会声望200，消耗300分钟、150精力和100镇定。取得路线后，靠战斗推进，不必重复教学。

持有剑石并达到恋爱中（3000）后，亲密羁绊可花60分钟增加一级灵魂石，训练场与已开放的训练室共用每日一次额度。这项动作不会直接赠送剑石。晚间动画与较长的远程房间拜访各有独立每日额度，后者最晚可在23:59开始。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

两项约会和三项后期故事动作需要主动从头像选择，单纯重新进入房间不能代替。训练室进展与感情房间解锁互相独立；剑术大师教学消耗很高，请事先准备足够精力与镇定。
