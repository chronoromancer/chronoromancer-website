Muramasa is a blind katana master who reads a fight through sound, air movement, scent, balance, and footwork. Her route combines a complete relationship progression with unusually valuable permanent stat growth and physical-combat progression.

Where to Find Her

Muramasa follows the same schedule every day.

Location Hours Available content
Knights Training Grounds 08:00-18:00 Introduction, The Empty Ring, Talk, Sense Mastery, Katana Training, Blademaster, Sword Stone bond
Brothel Training Room 18:00-20:00 Training Room contract, Talk, and the later A Wider Ring action plot
Travelers Inn 20:00-23:00 Talk, Wine, relationship plots, heart-to-hearts, and dinner
Travelers Inn, upstairs room 20:00-24:00 Repeatable private evening after completing A Place in the Silence

The ranges use an exclusive end hour: arrive before 18:00 for the Training Grounds, before 20:00 for the Brothel Training Room, and before 23:00 to meet Muramasa in the public Inn room. Her unlocked private-room action is remote and remains available until 24:00.

No recruitment flag is required. You can meet Muramasa as soon as you can reach the Knights Training Grounds during her schedule.

Unlocking Blademaster

The Blademaster action is at the Knights Training Grounds.

Requirement Value
Muramasa relationship Close Friend+ (200 points)
Fighters Guild clout 200+
Existing path state Blademaster not yet acquired (blademaster_points < 0)
Time 300 min
Variable-stat cost 150 Energy, 100 Calm
Money cost None

The action launches The Blademaster Path, awards a base +30 Muramasa relationship, and sets Blademaster points to 0. Caring adds +5, Flirty +4, and Funny +3, so the best total is +35.

Current clout progression warning

The current 25 Fighters Guild quests award 184 Fighters Guild clout in total, but Muramasa requires 200. Source search finds the quest board as the ordinary gameplay award path, while an external reward/code handler can add further Fighters Guild clout.

Therefore, on an ordinary fresh save, the present quest chain alone cannot satisfy Muramasa's 200-clout gate. Additional imported/reward clout or a future balance/content change is required. This is a current progression gap, not a missing relationship requirement.

The existing Fighters Guild Guide lists every quest and its reward.

How the Blademaster Path Works

Blademaster is a combat path, not a normal skill in the player's skills list.

Once acquired:

  • Basic Attack and Power Strike gain Blademaster path points when used in combat.
  • Outside the Infinity Dungeon, each qualifying action normally gives 1 point.
  • In the Infinity Dungeon, each action gains the base point plus a random depth bonus from 1 through the current Infinity floor, then applicable affix/specialist multipliers.
  • Blademaster rank multiplies the final damage of Basic Attack and Power Strike.
  • Quick Attack and Stormslash belong to the Assassin path instead; they do not gain Blademaster points or its rank multiplier.

Ranks continue beyond 20. Each additional rank adds 0.15x, while the required point span grows by 1.5x.

Efficient path leveling

  • Use Basic Attack when conserving Energy; it gains the same base path point as Power Strike.
  • Use Power Strike when you also need its higher damage.
  • Infinity Dungeon depth dramatically accelerates point gain, but only if you can survive and keep taking turns.
  • Fast enemies that die in one action give fewer total opportunities than durable enemies, although deliberately prolonging unsafe battles is rarely worth the risk.

Sword Stone Bond

Muramasa does not give you the Sword Stone. It is one of the rare key-item stones obtainable through the Mining game. Once you own it, its level adds flat damage to Basic Attack and Quick Attack, a different action pairing from the Blademaster path.

At In Love, Muramasa gains Intimate Bonding at the Knights Training Grounds.

Requirement Value
Relationship In Love (3,000 points)
Required item Sword Stone
Time 60 min
Reward Sword Stone level +1
Limit Once per game day with Muramasa

The Stone is not consumed. The action hides for the rest of the day after use.

This creates complementary physical bonuses:

  • Sword Stone level: flat damage to Attack and Quick Attack.
  • Blademaster rank: multiplicative damage to Attack and Power Strike.

Basic Attack benefits from both.

Recommended Route

  1. Meet Muramasa at the Knights Training Grounds from 08:00-18:00; choose Kind in her introduction.
  2. Use up to five regular talks per day across all of her locations. At Acknowledged, return to the Training Grounds from 08:00-18:00 to complete The Empty Ring.
  3. Use Katana Training from Acknowledged onward for permanent Perception and Ability. After The Empty Ring, visit the Brothel Training Room from 18:00-20:00 for Muramasa's one-time scene.
  4. At Known with Communication 45, take Sense Mastery. Raise Kent to Known as well.
  5. After The Empty Ring, enter the Travelers Inn from 20:00-23:00 with both Muramasa and Kent at Known for An Arrangement at the Inn.
  6. Complete Listen Before Moving at the Training Grounds, then Dinner Without Casualties at the Inn after reaching Friend.
  7. With Amelia and Kent ready, trigger The Old Healer's Oath at the Healer's Hut, then complete The Measure of Mercy at the Inn.
  8. At Close Friend, complete A Blade at Rest. You may then return to the Brothel Training Room from 18:00-20:00 and complete A Wider Ring to establish Muramasa's apprenticeship agreement.
  9. Complete The Road With No Job; at Crush, complete Between Heartbeats.
  10. At Romantic, complete A Place in the Silence. This unlocks Visit Muramasa's Room at the Inn from 20:00-24:00.
  11. Complete the Fighters Guild quest chain, while remembering its current 184-clout total still leaves you 16 short of the separate Blademaster gate.
  12. At Close Friend and 200 Fighters clout, rest to at least 150 Energy and 100 Calm, then take Blademaster training.
  13. Level the path with Attack and Power Strike; use Infinity Dungeon floors for faster gains.
  14. Obtain the Sword Stone through Mining and, at In Love, bond with Muramasa daily to level it.

Troubleshooting

Muramasa is missing

Check the time and location:

  • Training Grounds: 08:00-18:00
  • Brothel Training Room: 18:00-20:00
  • Travelers Inn: 20:00-23:00

She has no recruitment gate.

Sense Mastery is missing

You need Known relationship, Communication 45, and the Knights Training Grounds schedule. It disappears permanently after completion.

The Empty Ring will not trigger

Complete Muramasa's introduction, reach Acknowledged, and enter the Knights Training Grounds from 08:00-18:00. The plot triggers automatically and cannot repeat after completion.

The Training Room plot will not trigger

Complete The Empty Ring, make sure Muramasa is at least Acknowledged, and enter the Brothel Training Room from 18:00-20:00. The plot triggers automatically once.

A Wider Ring is missing

First complete Muramasa at the Training Room and A Blade at Rest. Muramasa must still be Close Friend or higher. Return while she is present in the Brothel Training Room from 18:00-20:00; A Wider Ring appears as a one-time action rather than triggering automatically.

The Inn plot will not trigger

Complete The Empty Ring first. Both Muramasa and Kent must then be Known, and you must enter the Travelers Inn from 20:00-23:00.

The Old Healer's Oath will not trigger

Complete Amelia's introduction and the Inn arrangement, meet all three relationship gates, visit the Healer's Hut from 08:00-19:00, and make sure Muramasa and Kent are not marked as vacationing.

Visit Muramasa's Room is missing

Complete Between Heartbeats and then A Place in the Silence, reach Romantic, and return to the Travelers Inn from 20:00-24:00. The action is remote, so it can appear even after Muramasa's public 20:00-23:00 schedule ends. It disappears for the rest of the day after one use.

Blademaster is missing

The action needs Close Friend, 200 Fighters Guild clout, and an unacquired Blademaster path. The current ordinary Fighters quest chain stops at 184 total clout, so the relationship stage alone is not enough.

Intimate Bonding is missing

You need In Love, the Sword Stone in key items, the Training Grounds schedule, and must not have used Muramasa's bond already that day.

Current Content Boundary

Muramasa currently has three heart-to-hearts, two personal dates, a complete romantic plot chain, Share Wine, the optional A Wider Ring professional branch, and the repeatable private inn encounter described above. She has no resort activity or brothel-worker route. Her Training Room master agreement keeps her independent. Blademaster and the In Love Sword Stone bond remain separate mechanical endpoints rather than prerequisites for her romance route.

当前声望进度警告

当前25项战士公会任务总共只奖励 184点战士公会声望，但Muramasa要求达到 200点。检查源代码后可以确认，任务板是普通游戏流程中的声望奖励途径，而外部奖励／代码处理程序可以进一步增加战士公会声望。

因此，在正常开始的新存档中，现有任务链本身不足以满足Muramasa的200点声望门槛。你还需要额外导入／奖励的声望，或等待未来的平衡调整／内容更新。这是当前的进度缺口，并非遗漏了某项关系要求。

现有的 战士公会指南 列出了每项任务及其奖励。

剑术大师路线的运作方式

剑术大师是一条 战斗路线，并非玩家skills列表中的普通技能。

取得后：

  • 在战斗中使用普通攻击和强力打击时，会获得剑术大师路线点数。
  • 在无限地下城之外，每次符合条件的行动通常会获得1点。
  • 在无限地下城中，每次行动会获得基础点数，并额外获得1至当前无限层数之间的随机深度加成，随后再应用词缀／专精者倍率。
  • 剑术大师等级会乘算提升普通攻击和强力打击的最终伤害。
  • 快速攻击和风暴斩属于刺客路线；它们既不会获得剑术大师点数，也不享受其等级倍率。

高效提升路线等级

  • 想节省精力时使用普通攻击；它获得的基础路线点数与强力打击相同。
  • 同时需要更高伤害时，使用强力打击。
  • 无限地下城的深度会显著加快点数获取，但前提是你能存活下来并不断获得行动回合。
  • 一击就会倒下的脆弱敌人提供的行动机会总数较少，但为了刷点而故意拖长危险战斗通常并不值得。

剑石羁绊

Muramasa 不会 把剑石送给你。剑石是采矿小游戏中可以获得的一种稀有关键物品灵魂石。拥有后，其等级会为 普通攻击和快速攻击 增加固定伤害——这套行动组合与剑术大师路线并不相同。

达到恋爱中阶段后，Muramasa会在骑士训练场解锁 亲密羁绊。

要求 数值
关系 恋爱中（3,000点）
所需物品 剑石
时间 60分钟
奖励 剑石等级+1
上限 与Muramasa的行动每个游戏日限一次

剑石不会被消耗。使用后，该行动会在当天剩余时间内隐藏。

由此形成两种互补的物理加成：

  • 剑石等级：为普通攻击和快速攻击增加固定伤害。
  • 剑术大师等级：乘算提升普通攻击和强力打击的伤害。

普通攻击可以同时受益于两者。

推荐路线

  1. 在08:00—18:00之间前往骑士训练场与Muramasa见面；在她的介绍剧情中选择友善回应。
  2. 尽早进行武士刀训练，永久提升感知和能力。
  3. 完成五次常规交谈；时间允许时，再跟她前往旅店进行五次交谈。
  4. 达到已知阶段且沟通达到45后，学习感官精通。
  5. 将Kent提升至已知阶段，然后在20:00—23:00之间进入旅人旅店，触发旅店里的约定。
  6. 在18:00—20:00之间前往青楼训练室，观看Muramasa的一次性场景。
  7. Muramasa达到朋友阶段，Amelia和Kent也满足条件后，在治疗师小屋触发老治疗师的誓言。
  8. 完成战士公会任务链，同时牢记当前任务总共只提供184点声望，距离剑术大师门槛仍差16点。
  9. 达到亲密朋友阶段并拥有200点战士公会声望后，将精力恢复至至少150、镇定恢复至至少100，再接受剑术大师训练。
  10. 使用普通攻击和强力打击提升路线等级；前往无限地下城楼层可以更快积累点数。
  11. 通过采矿获得剑石；达到恋爱中阶段后，每天与Muramasa建立羁绊以提升剑石等级。

疑难解答

找不到Muramasa

检查时间和地点：

  • 训练场：08:00—18:00
  • 青楼训练室：18:00—20:00
  • 旅人旅店：20:00—23:00

她没有招募门槛。

找不到感官精通

你需要达到已知关系阶段、沟通达到45，并在骑士训练场的日程时间内前往。完成后，该行动会永久消失。

旅店剧情没有触发

Muramasa和Kent都必须达到已知阶段，而且你必须在20:00—23:00之间进入旅人旅店。

老治疗师的誓言没有触发

完成Amelia的介绍剧情和旅店里的约定，满足三人的全部关系门槛，在08:00—19:00之间前往治疗师小屋，并确保Muramasa和Kent没有被标记为正在度假。

找不到剑术大师

该行动要求达到亲密朋友阶段、拥有200点战士公会声望，并且尚未取得剑术大师路线。当前普通战士公会任务链到184点总声望便会结束，因此只满足关系阶段还不够。

找不到亲密羁绊

你必须达到恋爱中阶段，在关键物品中持有剑石，于骑士训练场的日程时间内前往，并且当天尚未与Muramasa进行过羁绊行动。

当前内容边界

Muramasa目前没有已登记的交心谈话、约会、公寓拜访、度假村活动、最爱礼物行动或青楼员工路线。她的主要终点是剑术大师路线，以及恋爱中阶段的剑石羁绊。
