Overview

Raven becomes part of Rose & Thorn after the normal dungeon’s floor-20 challenge. Her content includes room sessions, a Tamer lesson, a repeatable private action and a resort continuation.

Where to find them

Complete the normal dungeon’s floor-1 and floor-20 challenges. The floor-20 victory recruits Raven and creates the business’s Dungeon room if needed.

With Raven active and home, find her at Brothel Dungeon, daily 18:00–23:00 and 00:00–03:00. There is a real 23:00–00:00 gap. This room is the brothel’s prison facility, separate from the combat dungeon.

Route and relationship milestones

Submit to Raven takes 20 minutes and gives 1 base affection, four positive uses per day. The room’s Commend Raven is another 20-minute interaction, once daily while she is present: +1 base affection and +1 Loyalty, capped at 100.

The six illustrated room sessions below unlock at their listed affection stages independently. They do not require finishing earlier sessions, and can be repeated while resources and availability allow. All take 30 minutes and need the indicated content access.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the room is available but Raven is absent, check the 23:00–00:00 gap and her vacation status.
  • The Tamer lesson needs Communication 20 and its own full Energy/Calm cost, even though the ordinary Submit action does not charge those resources.
  • Room-session affection is direct; player Erotic Experience, worker experience and Raven’s intimacy experience are separate rewards.
  • Watching a collected session in Extras does not grant its live rewards again.

概览

通过普通地下城第20层挑战后，Raven加入玫瑰与荆棘。她的内容包括房间训练、驯兽师课程、可重复的私人行动和度假后续。

去哪里找

完成普通地下城第1层和第20层挑战。第20层胜利后招募Raven，并在需要时建立青楼地牢房间。

在职且已结束度假时，她每天在地牢18:00–23:00及00:00–03:00出现，23:00–00:00之间确实不在。这里是青楼的地牢设施，与战斗地下城不同。

路线与关系进展

服从 Raven消耗20分钟、获得1基础好感，每天4次。她在场时，房间中的称赞 Raven另耗20分钟，每天一次，获得1基础好感及忠诚度+1，忠诚度上限100。

卡住时检查什么

  • 房间已开放却找不到Raven时，检查23:00–00:00的空档及度假状态。
  • 驯兽师课程需沟通20，并扣完整的精力与镇定；日常臣服行动不扣这些资源，不代表课程也免费。
  • 房间训练给予直接好感，玩家情趣经验、员工经验与Raven的亲密经验另行计算。
  • 在附加内容中回看已收藏训练，不会再次获得实际奖励。
