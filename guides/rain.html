Overview

Rain is the 25-year-old analytical maid of the Water Element — a deep-blue-haired researcher with intelligent blue eyes that observe and catalog everything around her. Trained by Doctor Aquinas, a scientist who taught her that Water Element means "flowing intelligently around obstacles," she approaches cleaning the way she approaches research: as systematic data collection, controlled experimentation, and continuous process optimization. She's clinical, precise, talks in percentages and statistical terminology, and her catchphrase is "According to my calculations..."

She's recruited via a search encounter in the Back Alleys — you find her methodically documenting urban decay patterns and waste management efficiency in neglected areas. Engaging her requires the Researching skill, the only key that unlocks the conversation paths that lead to her recruitment. Without it, she greets you politely and goes back to her work.

She covers the 10 PM – 2 AM brothel cleaning shift — the fifth and final maid, the late-night/overnight slot. She is the only maid with no pre-recruitment relationship phase — like Mandarin, she has no Talk action available before recruitment. The encounter itself is the entire courtship.

How to Recruit Her — The Back Alleys Search Encounter

Rain's recruitment is entirely contained within a single search encounter. No relationship grind, no faction rep, no boss fight — just trigger the encounter and pick the right conversation path.

Trigger Conditions

  • Location: Back Alleys (search encounter)
  • Required: Researching skill (otherwise the recruitment paths don't appear)
  • The encounter is a search-table draw — keep searching the Back Alleys until it triggers

Stage 1: Discovery

You navigate the shadowy back alleys and notice someone methodically working in a corner. A young woman with blue hair is carefully examining debris while systematically cleaning the area around her. She looks up, her analytical eyes meeting yours with curious interest:

"Oh, hello there. I'm Rain. I'm conducting environmental research on urban decay patterns and waste management efficiency in neglected areas."

She continues:

"It's rare to meet someone else in these forgotten corners of the city. Most people avoid places like this."

Stage 2: The Skill Gate

The conversation choices presented depend entirely on whether you have the Researching skill:

With Researching Skill (Recruitment Paths Available)

  • Ask about her research methodology → Recruits Rain
  • Discuss systematic data collection → Recruits Rain
  • Leave her to her work → Polite exit, no recruitment

Without Researching Skill (No Recruitment)

  • Ask what she's doing → Friendly conversation, no recruitment
  • Leave her to her work → Polite exit, no recruitment

Stage 3: Recruitment Dialogue (Path 1 — Research Methodology)

You ask about her research methodology and her eyes light up with genuine excitement:

"You understand research methodology? Finally, someone who appreciates systematic analysis!"

"I've been documenting contamination patterns, social infrastructure decay, and environmental restoration potential. Your understanding of proper research methods is... refreshing."

"You know what? I think I could learn a lot working with someone who values intellectual rigor. Would you be interested in offering me employment? I promise to bring the same analytical approach to any work environment."

She joins as Maid #5. acquire_maid(5) triggers — maid_5_acquired = True, Maids' Quarters level increases by 1.

"Rain gathers her research materials with systematic efficiency. 'I'll finish documenting this area, then report to your establishment. Thank you for recognizing the value of proper methodology.'"

Stage 3 (Alternate): Recruitment Dialogue (Path 2 — Data Collection)

You discuss systematic data collection:

"Exactly! Data collection without proper systematic approach is worthless. I'm using standardized sampling methods to ensure statistical validity."

"Most people don't understand the importance of consistent methodology. Your grasp of systematic analysis suggests we could work well together."

"I believe I could contribute meaningfully to an organization that values intellectual precision. May I join your establishment?"

She joins. "Excellent. I'll complete my current data set, then transition to your employment. This should prove most intellectually stimulating."

What Happens Without Researching Skill

She politely explains her work without offering employment:

"I'm studying urban environment patterns — how waste accumulates, what it tells us about social behavior, and how neglected areas might be systematically improved."

"It's fascinating work, though most people don't appreciate the methodical approach required for proper analysis. Perhaps we'll encounter each other again during your explorations."

The encounter ends. She remains in the Back Alleys search pool — you can re-trigger the encounter on a later visit. The encounter is NOT a one-time event if you fail to recruit; it remains available until you successfully complete it (with the Researching skill).

Why this is unique: Rain is the only maid recruited through a search encounter with a specific skill requirement. No combat, no relationship grind, no plot scenario, no faction rep. The Researching skill is the only key — without it, she's not recruitable, period. Acquire the Researching skill before grinding the Back Alleys for her encounter.

How to Get the Researching Skill

The Researching skill is taught at the Library (Present Era). Visit a librarian / book teacher there during their available hours, pay the gold cost, and learn the skill. Once you have Researching listed in your skills, the recruitment paths in Rain's encounter become available.

(For the full skill learning system, see the skills_glossary and skills_learning guides.)

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Intelligence 40, Perception 45, Technology 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Material Science skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Maid Service H2H (base 15), all 3 favorite stats met, Material Science learned: (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points per H2H — at daily limit 3, that's +108/day base.

Min-max insight: Rain's favorite stats include Technology 35 — the only maid who values Technology. Push Technology through the Future Era stat-boost actions and equipment to capture her +1 favorite-stat bonus. Her favorite Material Science skill is also taught at the Library (Present Era).

Voice & Personality Highlights

Rain's dialogue is clinical, precise, and full of statistical terminology:

  • Universal: Talks in percentages, correlations, sample sizes, control groups, standard deviations
  • Doctor Aquinas references: "Doctor Aquinas taught me that Water Element means intelligent flow around obstacles. Adaptation through analysis."
  • Element philosophy: Water = systematic adaptation, methodical erosion of obstacles, patience over brute force. "My methodology follows Water's principle: systematic progression, like erosion gradually shaping optimal pathways."
  • Specific stats: "My systematic approach to environmental maintenance has improved overall hygiene metrics by approximately 23%."
  • Documentation focus: "I document everything — temperature variations, humidity levels, supply usage rates, and efficiency metrics."
  • Player anomaly recognition: "Interesting anomaly — my predictive models fail around you. Statistical improbability suggests significance." — Rain is the maid who explicitly identifies the player as a statistical anomaly her models can't explain
  • Prophecy realization (data-driven): "I've documented references to five specialized practitioners in historical cleaning texts. Correlation: 94.3%." / "Five distinct Elements. Five specialized methodologies. Statistical probability of random occurrence: 0.002%. Conclusion: destiny."

Sister Dynamics (post other maids acquired)

  • Violet: "Violet's performance-based approach provides valuable data on presentation and theatrical effectiveness variables."
  • Mandarin: "Mandarin's security-focused methodology adds crucial protection parameters to our overall operational matrix."
  • Lychee: "Lychee's aesthetic optimization techniques produce measurable improvements in visual appeal and harmony metrics."
  • Lime: "Lime's mystical analysis methods offer alternative data interpretation frameworks that complement scientific approaches." — Rain feels closest to Lime, recognizing kinship in their shared observational nature

She approaches the maid sisterhood as complementary methodologies — five different research approaches creating optimal team performance. The cosmic prophecy is just "Statistical probability of random occurrence: 0.002%."

Cross-Maid Plot Integration

Rain participates in these cross-maid plots:

  • Lime + Rain Analysis (Acquaintance+ both) — Lime and Rain are the introvert sisters; Rain is the one Lime feels most understood by
  • Three Maidens First Meeting (Mandarin/Lychee/Rain all join) — Rain is one of the three featured here
  • Whispers of Six (Close Friend+ all five maids) — Rain's data analysis hints at the sixth Void Maid concept

She has the fewest cross-maid plots of the five — partly because her recruitment encounter is so self-contained, and partly because her analytical voice doesn't naturally generate the dramatic conflict that drives plots like Maids Compete Bedroom (which features Mandarin/Lychee/Lime, not Rain).

Tips & Optimal Play

  • The Researching skill is the gate — learn it at the Library before grinding the Back Alleys for her encounter.
  • Her encounter is NOT a one-time event — if you fail to recruit (no Researching skill, or you choose Leave), she remains in the search pool. Come back with the right skill.
  • No pre-recruitment relationship grind — like Mandarin, Rain has no Talk action before recruitment. The encounter is the entire pre-relationship phase.
  • Material Science (her favorite skill) is different from Researching (her recruitment skill) — they're two separate skills. Don't confuse them. Researching gets her in the door; Material Science doubles your relationship gain after.
  • Daily Talk is the workhorse — +2 base, 30/day limit, fastest stage-progression grind in the maid system (matches the other four maids).
  • Systematic Analysis gives free +1 Intelligence permanent — Comm 45 gate. Don't miss it; Intelligence 40 is also her favorite stat.
  • Her favorite Technology 35 threshold is uncommon — most NPCs don't value Technology. Push it through Future Era training and equipment if you're optimizing her bonuses.
  • Rain's Analytical Lens epic item is the apprentice multiplier — combined with Mentorship Manual, you get +55% Apprentice EXP on her late-night shift. Pursue Kupie Love Training for it especially if you're running an apprentice-focused brothel build.
  • Her clinical voice is canon — she talks in statistics and correlation coefficients. Players who expect maid banter sometimes find her cold. Lean into the data-driven aesthetic; her humor is in the numbers.
  • She's the maid who explicitly recognizes the prophecy — her dialogue includes "Statistical probability of random occurrence: 0.002%. Conclusion: destiny." She's the one who treats the prophecy as observed data rather than mystical belief.
  • Lime + Rain is the introvert sister bond — Acquaintance+ with both unlocks the Lime + Rain Analysis plot. They're the analytical observers of the maid team.
  • All 6 images are interchangeable — her clinical demeanor stays constant. The transformation is in dialogue, not art.
  • No animations, no dates — her content is pure dialogue and the H2H. The romance is the relationship grind plus her growing recognition that you are a statistical impossibility.
  • The 2 AM – 6 AM gap — Rain's shift ends at 2 AM, Violet starts at 6 AM. The 4-hour overnight gap is when no maid is cleaning. Plan your highest-mess events to not fall in that window if possible.

概述

Rain 是一位 25 岁的分析型女仆，代表水元素。她留着深蓝色头发，聪慧的蓝眼会观察并记录周遭一切。她的导师 Doctor Aquinas 教导她，水元素意味着“以智慧绕过障碍流动”。她对待清洁就如同研究：系统采集数据、进行对照实验，并持续优化流程。她冷静、精确，言谈充满百分比和统计术语，口头禅是*“根据我的计算……”*

她通过后巷的搜索邂逅招募。你会发现她正在有条不紊地记录城市衰败模式和荒废区域的废弃物管理效率。只有研究技能能开启通往招募的对话路线；没有该技能时，她只会礼貌问候，随后继续工作。

她负责青楼晚上 10 点至凌晨 2 点的清洁班次，是第 5 位也是最后一位女仆，覆盖深夜时段。她是唯一没有招募前关系阶段的女仆——与 Mandarin 类似，招募前无法与她交谈；这场邂逅就是完整的求职过程。

如何招募她——后巷搜索邂逅

Rain 的招募完全包含在一次搜索邂逅中。无需刷好感度、派系声望或战斗，只需触发邂逅并选择正确的对话路线。

触发条件

  • **地点：**后巷（搜索邂逅）
  • 必需： 研究技能（否则不会显示招募路线）
  • 该邂逅由搜索表随机抽取；反复搜索后巷，直至触发

阶段 1：发现

你穿过阴暗后巷，发现有人正有条不紊地在角落工作。一位蓝发年轻女子仔细检查碎屑，同时系统地清理周围。她抬起头，带着好奇兴趣用善于分析的眼睛与你对视：

“哦，你好。我是 Rain，正在研究荒废区域的城市衰败模式与废弃物管理效率。”

她接着说：

“在城市这些被遗忘的角落遇到别人很罕见。大多数人都会避开这种地方。”

阶段 2：技能门槛

出现的对话选项完全取决于你是否拥有研究技能：

拥有研究技能（可选择招募路线）

  • 询问她的研究方法→招募 Rain
  • 讨论系统化数据采集→招募 Rain
  • 让她继续工作→礼貌离开，不招募

没有研究技能（无法招募）

  • 询问她在做什么→友好交谈，不招募
  • 让她继续工作→礼貌离开，不招募

阶段 3：招募对话（路线 1——研究方法）

你询问她的研究方法，她的眼睛因由衷兴奋而亮起：

“你了解研究方法？终于有人懂得欣赏系统分析了！”

“我一直在记录污染模式、社会基础设施衰败以及环境修复潜力。你对正确研究方法的理解……令人耳目一新。”

“这样吧，我觉得与重视学术严谨的人共事能让我学到许多。你愿意雇用我吗？我保证会把同样的分析方式带到任何工作环境。”

**她以第 5 号女仆身份加入。**触发 acquire_maid(5)——maid_5_acquired = True，女仆宿舍等级提高 1。

“Rain 有条不紊地收好研究材料。‘我会先完成这里的记录，再到你的机构报到。感谢你认可正确方法的价值。’”

阶段 3（替代）：招募对话（路线 2——数据采集）

你与她讨论系统化数据采集：

“正是如此！没有正确系统方法的数据采集毫无价值。我使用标准化抽样方式来确保统计有效性。”

“大多数人不明白方法一致性有多重要。你对系统分析的掌握说明我们会合作愉快。”

“我相信自己能为重视学术精确性的组织作出实质贡献。可以让我加入你的机构吗？”

她加入了。“很好。我会完成当前数据集，再转入你的雇佣体系。这应该会带来极大的智力刺激。”

没有研究技能时会发生什么

她会礼貌解释自己的工作，但不会提出受雇：

“我在研究城市环境模式——废物如何积累、它反映了怎样的社会行为，以及如何系统改善荒废区域。”

“这项工作非常迷人，可惜多数人不懂正确分析所需的严谨方法。也许你以后探索时，我们还会再见。”

邂逅就此结束。她仍会留在后巷搜索池中，日后可以再次触发。招募失败时，该邂逅并非一次性事件；在你拥有研究技能并成功完成前都会保留。

独特之处：Rain 是唯一通过搜索邂逅且有特定技能要求的女仆。无需战斗、刷好感度、剧情场景或派系声望；研究技能是唯一钥匙，没有它就绝对无法招募。刷后巷邂逅前请先取得研究技能。

如何取得研究技能

研究技能由图书馆（现代）教授。在相关人员开放的时段造访，支付金币并学习。技能列表中出现研究后，Rain 邂逅的招募路线就会开放。

（完整技能学习系统见 skills_glossary 和 skills_learning 指南。）

招募前行动

**无。**Rain 不像 Violet（吟游诗人公会）或 Lime（法师塔）那样拥有招募前交谈行动。整个招募前阶段就是一场搜索邂逅：遇见她、拥有正确技能、招募她，仅此而已。

招募后行动（青楼女仆宿舍）

招募后，Rain 使用标准女仆行动组：

交谈

主要的每日行动，会触发 Rain 的三类对话系统（普通闲谈／尚未找到其他女仆／已找到其他女仆）。对白涵盖清洁效率分析、统计相关模式、系统方法、环境研究、优化原理，以及她逐渐意识到青楼是“某种事物的……汇聚点”。

系统分析（一次性）

她唯一提供永久属性提升的行动。需要已知阶段和沟通 45+。奖励：智力永久 +1、好感度 +10。

“Doctor Aquinas 教导我，水元素意味着以智慧绕过障碍流动。观察模式、采集数据、调整方法。关键在于：看似无法克服的问题，若从多个角度系统处理，往往会自行消解。耐心与分析胜过蛮力。”

女仆服务（H2H，亲密阶段）

她的亲密 H2H 谈话，在亲密阶段开放，每日上限 3，并会标记额外画廊条目 rn1_h_1。

好感度数值

行动 基础点数

加成计算（准确顺序）

好感度公式按以下顺序运行：

  1. 从行动的基础点数开始
  2. 每达到一项偏好属性便 +1（智力 40、感知 45、科技 35——最高 +3）
  3. 玩家拥有材料科学技能时 ×2
  4. 玩家拥有 **“1 Ring”**关键物品时 +1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 +1
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时 +1
  7. 根据魅力值判定，有概率 +1（上限 50%，持有华丽吊坠时为 60%）

**计算示例：**女仆服务 H2H（基础 15），3 项偏好属性全部达标，并学会材料科学： (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points 每次——每日上限 3 次，即每天基础 +108。

极限优化要点：Rain 的偏好属性包括科技 35，她是唯一重视科技的女仆。通过未来时代的属性提升行动和装备提高科技，以取得这项 +1 加成。她最喜欢的材料科学技能同样在图书馆（现代）教授。

语气与性格亮点

Rain 的对白冷静、精确，充满统计术语：

  • **通用：**谈论百分比、相关性、样本量、对照组和标准差
  • 提及 Doctor Aquinas：“Doctor Aquinas 教导我，水元素意味着以智慧绕过障碍流动。通过分析来适应。”
  • **元素哲学：**水＝系统适应、以有条不紊的侵蚀克服障碍，以耐心取代蛮力。“我的方法遵循水的原则：系统推进，就像侵蚀逐渐塑造出最优路径。”
  • 具体数据：“我对环境维护采取的系统方法，使整体卫生指标提高了约 23%。”
  • 重视记录：“我会记录一切——温度变化、湿度、物资使用率和效率指标。”
  • 察觉玩家异常：“有趣的异常——我的预测模型在你身边会失效。统计上的极低概率说明此事意义重大。”——Rain 是会明确指出玩家是其模型无法解释之统计异常的女仆
  • 以数据领悟预言：“我在历史清洁文献中记录到 5 位专业从业者的相关记载。相关性：94.3%。”／“5 种不同元素，5 套专门方法。随机发生的统计概率：0.002%。结论：命运。”

姐妹互动（取得其他女仆后）

  • Violet：“Violet 以表演为主的方法，为展示效果和戏剧成效变量提供了宝贵数据。”
  • Mandarin：“Mandarin 以安保为核心的方法，为整体运作矩阵加入了关键防护参数。”
  • Lychee：“Lychee 的美学优化技术使视觉吸引力与和谐指标产生了可测量的提升。”
  • Lime：“Lime 的神秘分析方法提供了替代数据解释框架，与科学方法相辅相成。”——Rain 与 Lime 最亲近，因为她在彼此善于观察的本性中感受到共鸣

她把女仆姐妹视为互补的方法体系——5 种研究方式共同创造最佳团队表现。宇宙预言对她而言只是*“随机发生的统计概率：0.002%。”*

跨女仆剧情联系

Rain 会参与以下跨女仆剧情：

  • Lime＋Rain 分析（两人均为相识+）——两位内向姐妹；Rain 是最能理解 Lime 的人
  • 三位少女初次会面（Mandarin／Lychee／Rain 全部加入）——Rain 是三位主角之一
  • 六者低语（5 位女仆均为亲密朋友+）——Rain 的数据分析暗示第 6 位虚空女仆的概念

她参与的跨女仆剧情是 5 人中最少的，一方面因为招募邂逅非常独立，另一方面是她的分析型语气不易产生推动“女仆卧室竞赛”之类剧情的戏剧冲突（该剧情包含 Mandarin／Lychee／Lime，不包含 Rain）。

推荐推进路线

阶段 1：学习研究技能

  • 造访图书馆（现代）
  • 支付金币学习研究技能
  • 这是强制条件；没有它就无法招募 Rain

阶段 2：触发后巷邂逅

  • 反复搜索后巷
  • Rain 邂逅只是多个随机搜索结果之一
  • 持续搜索，直至她出现

阶段 3：招募她

  • 拥有研究技能时，选择询问她的研究方法或讨论系统化数据采集
  • 两条路线都能成功招募
  • 她以第 5 号女仆身份加入，日程改为青楼晚上 10 点至凌晨 2 点

阶段 4：陌生人→已遇见→已知

  • 在她的深夜班次造访青楼女仆宿舍
  • 反复使用交谈（基础 +2，每日 30 次）
  • 将沟通提升至 45，以进行系统分析

阶段 5：已知→相识→朋友

  • 沟通 45 时进行系统分析：智力永久 +1、好感度 +10
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 招募更多女仆后触发跨女仆剧情：
    • Lime＋Rain 分析（两人均为相识+）
    • 三位少女初次会面（Mandarin／Lychee／Rain 全部加入）

阶段 6：朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 继续每日交谈
  • 将偏好属性提升至智力 40、感知 45、科技 35
  • 学习材料科学，令点数永久翻倍

阶段 7：亲密（解锁女仆服务）

  • 女仆服务 H2H 在亲密阶段解锁（基础 +15，每日 3 次）
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 5 位女仆均达到亲密朋友+时触发六者低语

阶段 8：后期

  • 每天进行女仆服务（+15×3＝每天基础 +45）
  • 为她装备史诗物品 Rain的分析镜片（Kupie 爱之训练奖励）——清洁 +20%、脏乱 −15%、学徒经验 +30%
  • 学徒经验加成会加速员工学徒训练；Rain 的史诗物品正是学徒系统倍率装备

装备——Rain 的标志物品

Rain的分析镜片（史诗级）

  • **来源：**Kupie 爱之训练成就
  • **女仆：**仅限 Rain
  • 效果： 单人清洁 +20%、脏乱 −15%、学徒经验 +30%
  • 学徒经验 +30% 是女仆史诗物品中的独特效果，会加速服务房间中的学徒进度
  • 脏乱 −15% 对整个青楼生效，其他女仆都能受益

推荐组合

  • **栏位 1（L1+）：**前期使用个人抛光布（$350，清洁 +4）；L4 时替换为 Rain的分析镜片
  • 栏位 2（L4+）： 导师手册（学徒经验 +25%，战士公会任务奖励）——与 Rain的分析镜片叠加后，她的班次获得学徒经验 +55%，大幅加快服务房间的学徒训练

提示与最优玩法

  • 研究技能是门槛——刷后巷邂逅前先在图书馆学习。
  • 她的邂逅并非一次性——若因没有研究技能或选择离开而招募失败，她仍会留在搜索池中。带上正确技能再回来。
  • 无需刷招募前好感度——与 Mandarin 类似，Rain 招募前没有交谈行动，邂逅就是完整的前置阶段。
  • 材料科学（她的最喜欢技能）不同于研究（她的招募技能）——两者不可混淆。研究让她加入；材料科学使加入后的好感度收益翻倍。
  • 每日交谈是主力——基础 +2，每日 30 次，是女仆系统中最快的阶段推进方式，与其他 4 位女仆相同。
  • 系统分析免费提供智力永久 +1——门槛为沟通 45；不要错过，因为智力 40 也是她的偏好属性。
  • 科技 35 的偏好门槛很少见——大多数 NPC 不重视科技。若要优化加成，请通过未来时代训练和装备提升。
  • Rain的分析镜片是学徒倍率装备——与导师手册结合后，她的深夜班获得学徒经验 +55%。若采用学徒型青楼配置，应优先完成 Kupie 爱之训练。
  • 她冷静的语气属于正式设定——她用统计和相关系数说话。若觉得她冷淡，请体会数据驱动风格；她的幽默藏在数字里。
  • 她会明确辨认出预言——对白包含*“随机发生的统计概率：0.002%。结论：命运。”*她把预言视为观察数据，而非神秘信仰。
  • Lime＋Rain 是内向姐妹组合——两人均达到相识+后解锁 Lime＋Rain 分析剧情，她们是女仆团队中的分析观察者。
  • 全部 6 张图像可交替使用——她冷静的举止始终不变；转变体现在对白，而非美术。
  • 没有动画，也没有约会——内容集中于对白和 H2H；恋爱线是关系培养，以及她逐渐意识到你是统计上不可能存在的异常。
  • 凌晨 2 点至 6 点的空档——Rain 凌晨 2 点下班，Violet 早上 6 点开工；这 4 小时无人清洁。尽量不要在该时段安排最容易制造脏乱的事件。
