Overview

Violet is one of the brothel’s maids. Her acquisition depends on Bards Guild Clout; ordinary affection and maid cleaning progression are separate systems.

Where to find Violet

Before acquisition, her Bards Guild portrait appears daily 22:00–24:00. After acquisition she moves to Maids Quarters, 06:00–10:00, and the ordinary guild portrait is removed.

Route and relationship milestones

At Bards Guild Clout 25, enter the guild for A Stage of Her Own. At Clout 50, The Standing Ovation acquires Violet and adds 50 direct relationship points, separately from that scene’s normal reward. These automatic events have no extra late-night restriction or ordinary affection requirement.

At Known (25) and Communication 45, Artistic Expression costs 30 minutes and grants Creativity +1 plus 10 direct relationship points once. At Intimate (1600), Maid Service is a repeatable 30-minute conversation.

Activities and rewards

Watch and Clean at the guild gives 1 base point for 25 minutes, five safe uses daily. Morning Talk gives 1 actual base point for 15 minutes, with 30 safe uses. These use different daily counters.

Maid Service’s ordinary response gives 15 base relationship points; Romantic adds 25 final points at conversation completion. Her cleaning level, forge equipment and Cleansing progression are covered in the Maid System guide.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

Missing actions and useful checks

Watch and Clean does not replace the Bards Guild Clout requirement. Check the guild’s performance progression, then visit her new morning room after acquisition. Her ordinary Talk can lower affection beyond its safe allowance even when the response is friendly.

概览

Violet是青楼女仆之一，取得她需要吟游诗人公会声望；普通好感与女仆清洁进展是独立系统。

出现地点与时间

取得前，她每天22:00—24:00在吟游诗人公会出现。取得后改为06:00—10:00在女仆宿舍，普通公会头像会移除。

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

吟游诗人公会声望25时，进入公会可触发属于她自己的舞台；声望50时，起立喝彩会取得Violet，并在事件通常奖励之外再直接增加50点关系。这两项自动事件不额外限制深夜，也不要求普通好感阶段。

已知（25）、沟通45时，艺术表达可花30分钟，一次性增加创造力1点和10点直接关系。达到亲密（1600）后，女仆服务是可重复的30分钟对话。

行动与奖励

公会的观看与清理消耗25分钟，给予1点基础关系，每日安全额度5次；早晨交谈消耗15分钟，实际给予1点基础关系，每日安全额度30次。两者分别计算。

女仆服务的普通回复给予15点基础关系，学会浪漫后，完成对话时再加25点最终关系。清洁等级、锻造装备与净化进展见女仆系统指南。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。

动作缺失时的检查

观看与清理不能代替公会声望。请推进公会演出进展，取得她后改在早晨到新房间拜访。普通交谈超过安全额度后仍会降低好感，即使回复仍然友善。
