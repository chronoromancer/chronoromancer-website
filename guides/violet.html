Overview

Violet is the 26-year-old theatrical maid of the Air Element — a purple-haired, eternally enthusiastic performer who treats every cleaning task as a stage performance. She's loud, dramatic, calls everyone "darling," and approaches mundane work with the seriousness of a one-woman opera. Trained for eight years by Madame Crescendo (a retired opera diva) in the Art of Expressive Service at her secret Performance Arts of Domestic Excellence academy.

She's recruited at the Bards Guild as Maid #1 (Air, 6 AM – 10 AM shift), and her arc is the most theatrically self-aware of the five. Her vulnerability beneath the dramatic flair: she's spent her entire life cleaning stages and has never once been allowed to perform on one. Your music gives her the first sense of being seen — and your brothel gives her the stage of her own she's always wanted.

Where to Find Her

Violet's locations work in two phases — before and after recruitment.

Pre-Recruitment: Bards Guild

  • Hours: 10 PM – 12 AM, every day
  • She works the late-night cleanup shift — after every show, when the hall is empty
  • Disappears from this location once recruited

Post-Recruitment: Brothel Maids' Quarters

  • Hours: 6 AM – 10 AM, every day
  • Her permanent home and shift after joining the brothel
  • Covers the early morning cleaning slot (first of the five maids)

Schedule note: Violet only appears at the Bards Guild during her late-night cleanup shift (10 PM – 12 AM). If you visit at other times, she's not there. After everyone else has left and the candles burn down, she sweeps and watches the night's recordings replay in her head.

The Plot

A blur of purple hair rushes toward you from the back of the hall, broom clattering to the floor. Violet introduces herself in pure theatrical fashion: she's been watching every single one of your performances from behind the curtains, memorized every song, hums them while she mops, and the other cleaners think she's lost her mind.

Her "your music makes me FEEL things" speech is the emotional core. She reveals her training: eight years under Madame Crescendo's "Art of Expressive Service" — a secret academy of "Performance Arts of Domestic Excellence" where the retired opera diva taught her to turn every mundane task into something magnificent.

She knows about your brothel. She wants to serve a master whose art moves her. She asks for the job.

Three-Choice Response

Each lead to the same outcome (recruitment), with slightly different flavor:

  • Caring: "Anyone who loves music that much belongs with me."
  • Funny: "You had me at 'lunatic clutching a broom.'"
  • Flirty: "A woman with your passion? I'd be a fool to say no."

After the Plot

Violet promises the Maids Quarters won't know what hit them, retrieves her fallen broom with a graceful flourish, sweeps into an exaggerated curtsy, and disappears backstage humming one of your songs.

The next morning she arrives at the brothel.

The Arrival Plot — "Violet's Arrival"

The morning after recruitment, Violet shows up at the Maids' Quarters and meets Elara — who is mid-supply-calculation when the door bursts open and Violet sweeps in broom held aloft like a scepter.

"GOOD MORNING, darlings! The Air Element has ARRIVED and this establishment will NEVER be the same!"

Elara is bewildered ("'Art of Expressive Service'? That's certainly a new one. And 'Madame Crescendo' sounds like a character from an opera.") and turns to you for confirmation that this is, indeed, your new maid.

Three-Choice Response

  • Polite: "Sorry for the short notice. Violet is incredibly talented, you'll see."
  • Funny: "Wait until you see her mop the floor. It's like watching ballet."
  • Flirty: "She swept me off my feet at the Bards Guild. Figuratively. Mostly."

Elara reluctantly accepts her and starts walking her through procedures. Violet immediately announces she doesn't follow procedures, she ELEVATES them, and within thirty seconds is critiquing the lighting and curtain arrangement of the Maids' Quarters with the eye of a director sizing up a new theater.

"She's been here thirty seconds and she's already redecorating. This is going to be interesting." — Elara

The Backstory Plot — "A Stage of Her Own"

This is Violet's most vulnerable moment. It triggers as a quiet, candlelit conversation in the empty Bards Guild — before her recruitment, when you find her not performing, not cleaning, just sitting on the edge of the empty stage humming softly to herself.

She invites you to sit with her. The hall feels different from down on the stage, looking out at vacant chairs.

What She Reveals

She's cleaned this stage every single night for longer than she can remember. She's never once stood on it and performed. Not once.

Madame Crescendo found her at age 12, sweeping a stage in a traveling theater troupe. The retired opera diva — "voice like thunder and honey mixed together" — took one look at her and said, "You have the Air in you, child."

Eight years of training in voice projection, physical theater, interpretive dance. The Performance Arts of Domestic Excellence. Violet still believes every sweep can be choreography, every folded sheet a curtain call.

But sometimes she wonders if Madame Crescendo taught her to love cleaning so she wouldn't notice that nobody ever asked her to do anything else.

Three-Choice Response

  • Caring: "You have more talent in your broom hand than half the performers I've seen here."
  • Funny: "Violet, I've seen your Tempest Twirl. That IS a performance. The mop is your instrument."
  • Flirty: "If nobody's asked you to perform, they haven't been paying attention. I have."

Her Confession

After your response, she opens up: she watches every performer who walks through the guild's doors. Memorizes their songs. Hums them while polishing the brass. But your music is different. When you play, she forgets she's holding a broom, forgets she's the cleaning girl, and she's right there with you.

The line that lands hardest:

"All I've ever wanted is a stage of my own. Not the spotlight. Not the crown. Just a little corner of the world where somebody actually SEES what I can do."

She catches herself getting "all SENTIMENTAL," plasters on a theatrical smile, thanks you for sitting with her, twirls the broom once, and heads toward the back hallway humming softly.

Why this matters: This is the emotional weight under the theatrical bombast. Violet isn't just a comic-relief character — she's spent her whole life backstage, and your brothel becomes the stage of her own she's always dreamed of. Her dramatic flair at the brothel reads differently after this scene.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Charm 40, Perception 45, Creativity 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Dramatic Arts skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Maid Service H2H (base 15), all 3 favorite stats met, Dramatic Arts learned: (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points per H2H — at daily limit 3, that's +108/day base.

Min-max insight: Talk's daily limit of 30 is by far the highest of any maid daily action. With Dramatic Arts + favorite stats stacked, Talk becomes a major point grind (+2 × bonuses × 30/day). Violet's Brothel Maids' Quarters phase is one of the fastest stage-progression grinds in the game.

Recommended Progression Path

Phase 1: Pre-Recruitment (Bards Guild)

  • Build Bards Guild faction reputation through performance and Sandra's questline
  • Visit Violet at the Bards Guild 10 PM – 12 AM for daily Watch and Clean (+1, daily 5)
  • Push faction rep to 50+

Phase 2: The Standing Ovation

  • At Bards Guild rep 50+, the Standing Ovation plot auto-triggers when you visit
  • Choose any response (all three lead to recruitment)
  • Violet is now Maid #1
  • The next morning, Violet's Arrival plot fires at the Brothel Maids' Quarters

Phase 3: Acknowledged → Known (Stage Push)

  • Visit her at the Brothel Maids' Quarters 6-10 AM daily
  • Spam Talk (+2 base, daily 30 — fastest stage grind in the maid system)
  • Push Communication to 45 for Artistic Expression

Phase 4: Known → Acquaintance → Friend

  • Run Artistic Expression at Comm 45: +1 Creativity permanent, +10 rel
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Cross-maid plots fire as you recruit more maids:
    • Violet + Mandarin Shift Change (during 10 AM transition)
    • Three Maidens First Meeting (when Mandarin/Lychee/Rain all join — Violet is referenced through her shift)

Phase 5: Friend → Close Friend → Crush

  • Continue Talk daily
  • Push favorite stats: Charm 40, Perception 45, Creativity 35
  • Learn Dramatic Arts skill for permanent point doubling

Phase 6: Intimate (Maid Service Unlocks)

  • Maid Service H2H unlocks at Intimate stage (+15 base, daily 3)
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Other cross-maid plots fire (Maids Compete Bedroom at Friend+ all three with Mandarin/Lychee/Lime, Whispers of Six at Close Friend+ all five)

Phase 7: Endgame

  • Run Maid Service daily (+15 × 3 = +45 base/day)
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Equip her with Violet's Stage Presence epic item (Kupie Love Training reward) — +25% Cleaning, +8% Reputation, +15% EXP — for the brothel-wide multiplier

Equipment — Violet's Signature Item

Violet's Stage Presence (Epic Tier)

  • Source: Kupie Love Training achievement
  • Maid: Violet only
  • Effects: +25% Cleaning, +8% Brothel Reputation, +15% Maid EXP
  • The reputation bonus is brothel-wide — every other character benefits from Violet wearing this
  • Worth pursuing the Kupie achievement for: this single item turns Violet from "cleans 4 hours of the day" into "boosts the entire brothel's reputation 8% daily"

Recommended Stack

  • Slot 1 (L1+): Sturdy Broom ($300, +3 cleaning) early; replace with Master Mop or Violet's Stage Presence later
  • Slot 2 (L4+): Cleaning Manual Vol. 2 (+20% EXP) for power-leveling, or Aromatic Incense (+5% reputation) for pure brothel boost

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Bards Guild rep 50 is the gate — start performing at the Bards Guild and pursuing Sandra's questline early if you want Violet first.
  • Watch and Clean has 5/day daily limit — use it during the wait for faction rep 50 to build pre-recruitment relationship.
  • Talk's 30/day daily limit is huge — fastest maid stage-progression grind in the game. With Dramatic Arts + 3 favorite stats, you can hit 100+ relationship/day.
  • Artistic Expression is free +1 Creativity — Comm 45 is a moderate gate. Don't miss it.
  • Dramatic Arts skill doubles all her points — keep it learned.
  • Her favorite stats are mid-tier (Charm 40, Perception 45, Creativity 35). Push Perception 45 last — it's the hardest threshold.
  • Violet's Stage Presence epic item is brothel-wide value — the +8% reputation alone makes this one of the highest-leverage maid items. Pursue Kupie Love Training achievements for it.
  • The Backstory plot ("A Stage of Her Own") fires at the Bards Guild empty hall — see it before the Standing Ovation if you can. The vulnerability lands harder before the recruitment.
  • No favorite gift system — maids don't accept gifts the way relationship NPCs do. Don't try to bring her flowers.
  • Voice projection comments mean nothing mechanically — but flavor-wise, she's been training her voice for 8 years and never gets to use it on a stage. The brothel becomes that stage.
  • All 6 images are interchangeable across all stages — Violet's vibe doesn't change as your relationship progresses. The transformation is in the dialogue, not the art.

概述

Violet 是26岁的 风元素 戏剧派女仆——她有一头紫发，永远热情洋溢，把每项清洁工作都当成舞台表演。她嗓门响亮、举止夸张，称每个人为“亲爱的”，还会把日常杂务演成一出郑重其事的单人歌剧。她曾在退役歌剧名伶 Crescendo夫人 开设的秘密“卓越家政表演艺术学院”中接受八年训练，学习 表现式服务艺术。

她会在吟游诗人公会被招募为1号女仆（风，早上6点–10点轮班），其故事弧在五位女仆中最具戏剧性的自我意识。浮夸举止下隐藏着她的脆弱：她一生都在清扫舞台，却 从未获准登台表演过哪怕一次。你的音乐第一次让她感到自己被人看见——而你的青楼则给了她梦寐以求的专属舞台。

剧情

一抹紫色身影从大厅后方飞奔而来，扫帚当啷落地。Violet以十足的戏剧腔调自我介绍：她一直躲在幕布后观看你的每一场演出，记住了每一首歌，还会边拖地边哼唱；其他清洁工都觉得她已经疯了。

她那段“你的音乐让我感受到一切”的演说是本段剧情的情感核心。她讲起自己的训练经历：在Crescendo夫人的“表现式服务艺术”课程中学习了八年——那是一所秘密的“卓越家政表演艺术学院”，这位退役歌剧名伶在那里教她把每项平凡工作都变得精彩绝伦。

她知道你经营着青楼，也想侍奉一位能以艺术打动她的主人。她主动求职。

三种回应选择

所有选项都会导向相同结果（成功招募），只有风格稍有不同：

  • 体贴： “如此热爱音乐的人就该和我在一起。”
  • 幽默： “听你说到‘抱着扫帚的疯子’时，我就已经决定要你了。”
  • 调情： “像你这样热情的女人？拒绝你才是傻瓜。”

剧情结束后

Violet保证会让女仆宿舍发生翻天覆地的变化，随后优雅地挥动手臂捡起掉落的扫帚，行了一个夸张的屈膝礼，哼着你的一首歌消失在后台。

第二天早上，她会来到青楼。

入职剧情——“Violet到来”

招募后的第二天早上，Violet来到女仆宿舍，与正在计算物资的Elara见面。大门猛地打开，Violet旋风般闯入，把扫帚高举得如同权杖。

“早上好，亲爱的各位！风元素已经驾到，这里从此将焕！然！一！新！”

Elara困惑不已（“‘表现式服务艺术’？这倒是个新鲜说法。还有‘Crescendo夫人’，听上去像歌剧里的角色。”），转头向你确认这确实是你的新女仆。

三种回应选择

  • 礼貌： “抱歉这么突然。Violet非常有才华，你会见识到的。”
  • 幽默： “等你看过她拖地就知道了，简直像在看芭蕾。”
  • 调情： “她在吟游诗人公会把我迷得神魂颠倒。比喻意义上。基本算是吧。”

Elara勉强接纳了她，开始介绍工作流程。Violet却立刻宣布自己不遵循流程，而是要将流程升华；不到三十秒，她便以导演审视新剧院的目光，开始点评女仆宿舍的灯光与帷幕布置。

“她来了才三十秒，就已经开始重新布置房间了。以后一定很有意思。”——Elara

背景剧情——“属于她自己的舞台”

这是Violet最脆弱的一刻。剧情会在招募 之前 于空荡而烛光摇曳的吟游诗人公会触发。你发现她没有表演，也没有打扫，只是坐在空舞台边缘轻声哼唱。

她邀请你坐在身旁。从舞台上望向一排排空座椅，整座大厅给人的感觉都不一样了。

她透露的往事

她每晚都清扫这座舞台，已经久到记不起究竟有多少年。她却从未站在台上演出过，一次也没有。

Violet 12岁时，Crescendo夫人在一个巡回剧团里发现了正在清扫舞台的她。这位退役歌剧名伶——“嗓音仿佛雷霆与蜂蜜交融”——只看了她一眼便说：“孩子，你体内蕴含着风。”

八年来，她学习声音投射、形体戏剧和诠释性舞蹈，也就是卓越家政表演艺术。Violet至今仍相信，每次挥扫都能成为舞蹈编排，每张折叠好的床单都能化作谢幕。

但她有时也会怀疑，Crescendo夫人教她 热爱 清洁，是不是为了让她意识不到——从来没有人请她做过其他事情。

三种回应选择

  • 体贴： “你握扫帚的那只手，比我在这里见过的一半表演者都更有才华。”
  • 幽默： “Violet，我见过你的暴风旋舞，那本身就是表演。拖把就是你的乐器。”
  • 调情： “如果从来没人请你表演，那只能说明他们没有认真看你。但我看到了。”

她的坦白

听完你的回应，她终于敞开心扉：她看过每个走进公会的表演者，记住他们的歌曲，再在擦亮铜器时轻轻哼唱。但 你的 音乐截然不同。你演奏时，她会忘记手里还握着扫帚，忘记自己只是清洁女工，并且感觉自己 就与你同在台上。

最触动人心的一句话是：

“我一直以来只想要一座属于自己的舞台。不是聚光灯，不是王冠，只是世界上的一个小角落，让某个人能真正看见我的本领。”

她猛然察觉自己变得“太多愁善感”，又挂上戏剧化的笑容，感谢你陪她坐了一会儿。她旋转一次扫帚，轻声哼唱着走向后方走廊。

这段剧情为何重要： 这正是夸张戏剧表象下的情感重量。Violet不仅是个负责搞笑的角色——她一生都待在后台，而你的青楼会成为她梦寐以求的专属舞台，让她终于被人看见。经历此场景后，她在青楼里的浮夸举止也会显出不同意义。

招募前行动——“观看与清扫”

加入青楼之前，Violet在深夜轮班期间只有 一项 吟游诗人公会行动可用。

观看与清扫

  • 地点： 吟游诗人公会
  • 时间： 晚上10点–午夜12点
  • 耗时： 25分钟
  • 每日上限： 5次
  • 奖励： 基础好感度+1
  • 触发特殊对话组 “bards_guild_cleaning”——Violet会谈及自己对深夜清扫轮班的热爱、观看演出、向表演者学习，也会经常提到Crescendo夫人

这项行动为何重要： 你可以在“起立喝彩”剧情触发 之前 提升与Violet的关系。将吟游诗人公会声望提升至50，在等待期间与她培养感情，随后便会触发招募剧情。

招募后行动（青楼女仆宿舍）

招募后，Violet的行动将转移至女仆宿舍。

交谈

主要的日常行动。触发Violet的三类对话系统（日常闲聊／尚未找到其他女仆／已找到其他女仆）——她会评论戏剧哲学、音乐、舞蹈、服装设计，以及你招募到的其他女仆。

艺术表达 （仅限一次）

她的行动中唯一能永久提升属性的一项。要求达到 已知阶段 且 沟通45以上。奖励：永久+1创造力、好感度+10。

“亲爱的，Crescendo夫人教过我，风元素本身就是表达！每个手势、每次呼吸、每个动作都在讲述一个故事！只要将灵魂倾注于自己所做之事——就连平凡的工作也会变得辉！煌！壮！丽！世界就是你的舞台，你必须演绎真实的自己！”

女仆服务 （H2H，亲密阶段）

她的亲密H2H谈心，达到亲密阶段后解锁，每日上限3次。会触发一段H2H谈心场景。

加成算法（准确计算顺序）

好感度按以下顺序计算：

  1. 以行动的 基础点数 开始
  2. 每项达标的偏好属性 +1（魅力40、感知45、创造力35——最多+3）
  3. 若玩家拥有她的特殊／偏好技能 戏剧艺术，则 × 2
  4. 若玩家拥有关键物品 “1枚戒指”，则 +1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 +1
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时 +1
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，成功则 +1（上限50%；持有华丽挂坠时为60%）

计算示例： 女仆服务H2H（基础15），3项偏好属性全部达标，并已学会戏剧艺术： (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points——H2H每日上限3次，也就是每天基础+108。

极限优化心得： “交谈”的 每日30次 上限远高于任何其他女仆的日常行动。叠加戏剧艺术与全部偏好属性后，“交谈”会成为主要的好感度刷取途径（+2 × 加成 × 每日30次）。Violet在青楼女仆宿舍阶段的关系升级速度位居游戏前列。

说话方式与性格亮点

Violet的对话 始终充满戏剧感：

  • 通用特点： 称每个人为“亲爱的”，大量使用戏剧／表演词汇，并以舞台指示般的语言描述自己的工作
  • 提及Crescendo夫人： “Crescendo夫人总说：‘风是表达，是运动，是表演本身的吐息！’我始终遵循这句话！”
  • 元素哲学： “风将与烈火之威共舞……”——她的元素哲学贯穿每次对话
  • 预言意识： “预言说五人终将聚首……如今我们全！都！到！齐！了！亲爱的，这多么富有戏剧性啊？”
  • 自我旁白： “我把每天都当成一场新剧首演，必须以崭新的活力和对卓越艺术的投入来迎接！”

姐妹互动（获得其他女仆后）

  • Mandarin： “好强大的气场！不过亲爱的，你的动作还需要更多风！采！”
  • Lychee： “审美很出色，只是有点……流于表面？艺术必须有深！度！”
  • Lime： “神秘氛围妙极了！但请大声一点，亲爱的，要用横膈膜发声！”
  • Rain： “方法体系真有意思，可热！情！在哪里？灵！魂！在哪里？”

她在艺术方面争强好胜，自视为女仆团队的“女主角”，只要你在场，就会编排精心设计的清洁演出。

推荐推进路线

阶段1：招募前（吟游诗人公会）

  • 通过表演与Sandra的任务线提升 吟游诗人公会阵营声望
  • 晚上10点–午夜12点前往吟游诗人公会找Violet，每天进行“观看与清扫”（+1，每日5次）
  • 将阵营声望提升至50以上

阶段2：起立喝彩

  • 吟游诗人公会声望达到50以上后，前去拜访便会自动触发 起立喝彩 剧情
  • 任意选择一种回应（三种都会成功招募）
  • Violet现在成为1号女仆
  • 第二天早上，Violet到来 剧情会在青楼女仆宿舍触发

阶段3：已遇见 → 已知（推进阶段）

  • 每天早上6点–10点前往青楼女仆宿舍找她
  • 反复使用 交谈（基础+2，每日30次——女仆系统中最快的阶段刷取行动）
  • 将 沟通提升至45，以解锁艺术表达

阶段4：已知 → 相识 → 朋友

  • 沟通45时进行 艺术表达：永久+1创造力，好感度+10
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 招募更多女仆后会触发女仆联动剧情：
    • Violet + Mandarin换班（上午10点交班期间）
    • 三位少女初次见面（Mandarin／Lychee／Rain全部加入后——剧情会提到Violet的轮班）

阶段5：朋友 → 亲密朋友 → 迷恋

  • 继续每日交谈
  • 提升偏好属性：魅力40、感知45、创造力35
  • 学会 戏剧艺术 技能，使好感度收益永久翻倍

阶段6：亲密（解锁女仆服务）

  • 女仆服务H2H 在亲密阶段解锁（基础+15，每日3次）
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 触发其他女仆联动剧情（三位女仆均达到朋友以上时，与Mandarin／Lychee／Lime触发“女仆卧室竞赛”；五位女仆均达到亲密朋友以上时，触发“六人的低语”）

阶段7：终局

  • 每日进行女仆服务（+15 × 3 = 每日基础+45）
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 为她装备史诗物品 Violet的舞台气场（Kupie爱之训练奖励）——+25%清洁、+8%声望、+15%经验，获得覆盖全青楼的加成

装备——Violet的标志物品

Violet的舞台气场（史诗级）

  • 来源： Kupie爱之训练成就
  • 女仆： 仅限Violet
  • 效果： +25%清洁、+8%青楼声望、+15%女仆经验
  • 声望加成为全青楼共享——Violet装备后，其他所有角色都能受益
  • 值得为此完成Kupie成就：这件物品能让Violet从“每天清洁4小时”变成“每天使整个青楼的声望提升8%”

推荐搭配

  • 槽位1（L1+）： 前期使用结实扫帚（$300、清洁+3）；后期换成大师拖把或Violet的舞台气场
  • 槽位2（L4+）： 若要快速升级，使用清洁手册第2卷（经验+20%）；若只想提升青楼，则使用芳香熏香（声望+5%）

提示与最优玩法

  • 门槛为吟游诗人公会声望50——若想最先招募Violet，请尽早在吟游诗人公会表演并推进Sandra的任务线。
  • “观看与清扫”每日上限5次——等待阵营声望达到50期间，利用它提升招募前关系。
  • “交谈”每日上限高达30次——这是游戏中推进女仆关系阶段最快的途径。叠加戏剧艺术和3项偏好属性后，每天可以获得100以上好感度。
  • 艺术表达可免费获得永久+1创造力——沟通45只是中等门槛，不要错过。
  • 戏剧艺术技能会让她的所有点数翻倍——务必保留该技能。
  • 她的偏好属性处于中等门槛（魅力40、感知45、创造力35）。最后提升感知至45——这是最难达到的一项。
  • Violet的舞台气场是能惠及全青楼的史诗物品——单是+8%声望就足以让它成为增益最高的女仆物品之一。请为此完成Kupie爱之训练成就。
  • 背景剧情（“属于她自己的舞台”）在吟游诗人公会空厅触发——尽可能在“起立喝彩”前观看。招募前了解她的脆弱，会使这段经历更具冲击力。
  • 没有偏好礼物系统——女仆不像普通关系NPC那样收礼。不要试着给她送花。
  • 声音投射相关评论不影响机制——但在剧情层面，她训练嗓音长达8年，却从来没有机会在舞台上施展。青楼最终成为了她的舞台。
  • 全部6张立绘会在所有阶段交替使用——Violet的整体气质不会随关系推进而改变。她的转变体现在对话而非画面中。
