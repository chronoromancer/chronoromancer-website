Overview

Shadow has a night-time discovery, a guild trail, recruitment, a movement skill, worker activities and Sirena Isle outings. Her ordinary affection, worker relationship and NPC intimacy experience are separate progress tracks; use the right activity for the reward you need.

Where to find them

First enter Back Alleys at 22:00–01:59 with Perception 45 while Shadow is not away on vacation. This one-time discovery makes her regular portraits available. Her later schedule changes with odd and even day numbers; midnight begins a new day.

Route and relationship milestones

Build Shadow to Known (25), then ask Arestes for initiation in Hidden Chamber with Rogue 35. Reaching that chamber also needs Lockpicking and Perception 60; Shadow need not stand beside Arestes. After initiation, follow Shadow’s Trail through these locations, in order: Whispering Woods → Tournament Grounds → Arcane Shop → Mountain Foothills → Archery Range → Castle Garden → Back Alleys. Each stop starts after the previous one.

The Arrangement starts automatically at the public guild with 120 guild Clout and recruits Shadow. You do not need to finish the personal Arestes route or a separate Vixen scene first. The recruitment supplies her room if needed. The Phantom Stone Raid is a later, separate guild event: recruited Shadow must be active, all three of Arestes/Keira/Shadow must be Romantic (800), and you need Thieves Gloves plus Dark Consolador while not already holding Phantom Stone.

Two additional shared scenes use other contacts: Shadow Arts Apprentice at the brothel Bar, 20:00–23:59, needs active Shadow and Kasumi, Shadow Known and both home from vacation. Lost Pet Situation at Central Plaza, 08:00–17:59, needs active Sophie, Sophie Acknowledged (10), Shadow Known (25), Hailey Known (25), and Shadow/Sophie home from vacation. Follow those guides for the surrounding routes.

Shadow Step is a skill for escaping ordinary non-boss encounters; its long lesson still begins from her guild portrait during her schedule. Her favorite stats are Ability, Perception and Rogue, and Fleet Footed improves the relationship bonus. Guild portraits do not offer a direct ordinary gift action.

At In Love (3,000), two crafted gifts open one-time training upgrades while Shadow is home from vacation: No Contingency at the guild uses a Void-Kissed Charm and needs Shadow Step plus The Arrangement; No Leverage in Back Alleys uses a Golden Pulse Bracelet and needs Night Bazaar. Each takes no additional time and permanently doubles the corresponding Cowgirl or Oral worker-training Experience reward. Each completed upgrade also adds 50% of her base worker income. Follow the Forge guide for crafting costs.

As a worker, Shadow uses the shared visit system: one Talk, Praise, Gift and lesson per visit. A lesson takes 60 minutes, 60 Energy and 40 Calm; one shared training level advances from 1 to 8, not a separate level for each category. Jewelry is her favorite ordinary session gift and Flowers her least favorite. Worker Experience, worker relationship, Loyalty and Stress are covered in the Brothel guide; these lessons do not automatically raise ordinary NPC affection. Fernando can also fit her clothing.

On a Sirena Isle vacation, use her island portrait for six daily activities: Talk (15 minutes, up to six/day), Buffet meal (11:00–15:59), Beach relaxation (08:00–15:59), Private Beach Time (10:00–17:59), Spa Night (19:00–22:59), and Sunset Walk (17:00–19:59). The last five take 60 minutes each and each has its own once-daily use. Her sunset H2H gives 5 kissing experience; Private Beach Time gives 25 missionary experience. Shared island encounters also depend on the other vacationers present; use the resort guide for combinations and access.

Her three NPC intimacy-gallery images unlock at 100, 250 and 500 NPC intimacy experience. Worker training and the island intimacy activities supply that experience; ordinary affection and player Erotic Experience cannot substitute for it.

What usually blocks the next step

  • No portraits anywhere: complete the Perception-45 alley discovery first. Vacation also removes her usual home schedule.
  • A midnight schedule is determined by the new day number. Do not carry the previous evening’s odd/even label past midnight.
  • Night Bazaar does not start after midnight, even when her even-day guild portrait is still present.
  • Shadow Step needs all 180 Energy and 100 Calm for a 350-minute lesson. Ability or affection alone cannot cover the resource cost.
  • Recruitment checks 120 guild Clout at the public guild; the later Phantom Stone raid additionally checks active Shadow and the three Romantic stages.
  • For a crafted upgrade, finish its specific date/skill prerequisite, carry the correct gift and bring Shadow home from vacation. The two gifts upgrade different lessons.

概览

Shadow的内容包括夜间发现、公会踪迹、加入青楼、移动技能、员工活动及塞壬岛出游。普通好感、员工关系和NPC亲密经验是不同进度，应按所需奖励选择行动。

路线与关系进展

先让Shadow达到已知（25），再以盗贼35向隐藏密室的Arestes申请入门。进入密室还需开锁及感知60，不要求Shadow同时站在Arestes旁边。入门后，按Shadow的踪迹顺序前往：低语森林→锦标赛场地→奥术商店→山麓→射箭场→城堡花园→后巷。每站都要求完成上一站。

“安排”在公会声望达到120后，于公会大厅自动触发，让Shadow加入；不要求先完成Arestes个人路线或另一场Vixen事件。需要时会同时安排她的房间。后续的“幻影之石突袭”是另一个公会事件：Shadow已加入并启用，Arestes／Keira／Shadow三人都达到浪漫感（800），持有盗贼手套及暗影慰藉者，且尚未持有幻影之石。

另外两场共同事件涉及其他联系人：青楼酒吧20:00–23:59的“潜影术学徒”，需要Shadow与Kasumi都启用、Shadow已知，且两人都已结束度假；中心广场08:00–17:59的“走失的鸣禽”，需要Sophie启用、Sophie已遇见（10）、Shadow已知（25）、Hailey已知（25），Shadow与Sophie都没有在度假。相关角色的其他步骤见各自指南。

影步用于从普通非首领遭遇中逃脱。这堂课虽然很长，仍需在她的公会出没时段，从头像开始。她偏好的属性为能力、感知和盗贼，步履轻盈可提高关系加成。公会头像没有直接送普通礼物的行动。

达到恋爱中（3000）且Shadow没有在度假时，可用两件锻造礼物完成一次性训练升级：公会的“没有后备方案”消耗虚吻护符，要求学会影步并完成“安排”；后巷的“无可利用”消耗金脉手镯，要求完成夜市约会。每项不额外耗时，并永久让对应女上位或口交员工训练的经验奖励翻倍；每完成一项，员工收入还增加其基础收入的50%。制作费用见锻造指南。

作为员工，Shadow使用共通探访系统：每次探访可交谈、称赞、送礼和上课各一次。训练消耗60分钟、60精力及40镇定，所有类别共用一个从1升到8的训练等级，不是每类各练一套。普通探访礼物中，她最喜欢珠宝，最不喜欢鲜花。员工经验、员工关系、忠诚度与压力规则见青楼指南；上课不会自动增加普通NPC好感。Fernando也能替她制作服装。

前往塞壬岛度假后，从岛上的头像进行六类日常行动：交谈（15分钟，每天最多6次）、自助餐用餐（11:00–15:59）、海滩放松（08:00–15:59）、海滩私密时光（10:00–17:59）、水疗之夜（19:00–22:59）、日落散步（17:00–19:59）。后五项各60分钟，各自每天一次。她的日落谈心给予5点接吻经验，海滩私密时光给予25点传教士位经验。岛上共同事件还看其他度假成员是否在场，组合与入岛方式见度假村指南。

她的三张NPC亲密画廊图片分别在NPC亲密经验100、250、500时解锁。员工训练及岛上亲密活动可提供这项经验，普通好感或玩家情趣经验不能代替。

下表时间均指开始行动的时间；例如，结束于23:00的时段，最晚可在22:59开始。基础好感会受关系加成影响；直接好感按所列数值增加。

卡住时检查什么

  • 哪里都没有头像：先完成感知45的后巷发现；度假也会让她暂时离开平日地点。
  • 午夜后的时段按新一天的单双数计算，不要沿用前一晚的日期。
  • 午夜后不能开始夜市约会，即使双数日公会里仍有她的头像。
  • 影步课程长350分钟，必须准备180精力和100镇定；能力或好感足够不能代替这些消耗。
  • 加入事件在公会大厅检查120公会声望；后来的幻影之石事件还检查Shadow启用及三人的浪漫感阶段。
  • 锻造礼物升级需要各自的约会／技能前置，带对礼物，并让Shadow结束度假回家；两件礼物提升的是不同课程。
