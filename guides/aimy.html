Overview

AIMy’s personal route mixes everyday conversation, a technical lesson, five personal scenes, three H2Hs and two dates. The later half depends on late main-story progress as well as affection; this guide gives the requirements without describing the outcomes.

Where to find them

Find her in AIMy’s Control inside the stronghold, all day, every day, once the main story opens the base beneath Cid’s clinic. Start her personal actions from the portrait in that room. Her first automatic personal scene needs Story 32 completed.

Her affection meter uses the ordinary relationship stages even though you already know her through the story. Talk is the dependable early activity: 15 minutes and 1 base affection, with up to 99 favorable uses per day.

Route and relationship milestones

Return to the control room after completing each prerequisite. Alternate the automatic scenes with the H2Hs below; an affection threshold does not replace an earlier conversation. The two dates require Story 94, while the final personal action requires Story 96.

Nano-Engineering needs Acquaintance (50) and Intelligence 28. Prepare 200 minutes, 80 Energy and 40 Calm; it has no money fee and disappears after you learn the skill.

The battle interface, automated defenses and stronghold buildings use separate Tower Defense controls. Talking to AIMy or finishing a personal scene does not buy a defense upgrade.

Her favorite stats are Technology, Perception, Creativity. The learned Data Mining skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. H2Hs and automatic personal scenes complete once; ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

Resistance Clout gifts

AIMy is a default army member. Reach 30 Resistance Clout. From AIMy’s portrait at AIMy's Control during their available hours, choose “More Than Statistics”. Complete this one-time scene to receive Training Data Chip. It takes no in-game time and checks Clout without spending it.

The reward enters Army Equipment; equip it on the intended hero in Barracks. Effects and alternatives: TD Army.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Only the early scenes appear: check the separate Story 94 gate on One More Test and both dates.
  • The last H2H requires In Love (3,000), Stay With Me and the second H2H. Romantic (800) is an earlier stage.
  • With You needs Story 96 and the final H2H; meeting the Story 94 date requirements is not sufficient.
  • If the current objective restricts access inside the stronghold, complete that objective before returning for the final action.
  • No Nano-Engineering lesson: check Intelligence 28, Acquaintance and whether you already know the skill.

概览

AIMy的个人路线包含日常交谈、技术课程、五场个人事件、三次谈心和两场约会。后半段除了好感，还需要推进较后的主线；下文只说明条件，不揭示事件结果。

去哪里找

主线开放Cid诊所下方的要塞后，到“AIMy 的控制室”找她，全天可见。个人行动从这个房间的头像开始，第一场自动个人事件还需要完成主线32。

虽然剧情中早已认识，她的好感仍按普通关系阶段计算。前期可用交谈稳定培养关系：每次15分钟、1基础好感，每天最多99次正常回应。

路线与关系进展

完成前置后，再次进入控制室。自动事件和下方谈心要交替推进，好感足够不能代替前一次对话。两场约会需要完成主线94，最后的个人行动则需要完成主线96。

达到恋爱中（3000）、完成“陪着我”“此后我想要的”及主线96后，从头像选择“与你相伴”。消耗120分钟、30精力，仅一次；当前主线目标也必须允许进入要塞内部。事件结果留在游戏中体验。

两场约会是独立出游，不能替代个人谈心。各完成一次，在表列开始时段内从控制室菜单选择。

反抗军声望赠礼

AIMy是默认军队成员。反抗军声望达到30，在AIMy可用的时段，从AIMy 的控制室的头像选择「不只是统计数字」。完成这段一次性事件，可取得「训练数据芯片」。行动不耗游戏内时间，只检查声望，不会扣除声望。

奖励进入军队装备，之后还要在兵营为目标英雄装备。效果与选择详见塔防军队与训练。

卡住时检查什么

  • 只出现前半段事件：检查“再做一次测试”和两场约会各自要求的主线94。
  • 最后一次谈心需要恋爱中（3000）、“陪着我”及第二次谈心；浪漫感（800）是更早的阶段。
  • “与你相伴”还要完成主线96和最后一次谈心，达到主线94的约会条件仍不够。
  • 若当前目标限制进入要塞内部，先完成该目标，再回来进行最后的行动。
  • 找不到纳米工程课程：检查智力28、相识阶段，以及是否已经学会。
