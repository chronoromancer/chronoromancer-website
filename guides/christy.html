Overview

Christy offers ingredient purchases, a one-time Creativity increase, relationship activities and a route to Mary Ann. Her market portrait is the starting point for most of these.

Where to find them

Farmers Market: 07:00–17:00. After Diane builds the Boardwalk, she also appears at Boardwalk from 17:00–20:00. The two Talk actions have separate normal limits of 6/day and 3/day.

Route and relationship milestones

Potato Education → Catching Up at the Market (Acquaintance (50)) → Zoo Date and Explore Market. Complete both dates, and Mary Ann’s Pavilion introduction, for the shared introduction route. The Main Story guide covers the later connected Objectives.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Optional reference: relationship bonuses

Meeting each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before the remaining relationship bonuses. The favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct one-time affection rewards are added as listed.

Charm 30; Stamina 30; Communication 25

Favorite skill: Animal Care

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Listed time and resources are base values; applicable skills, difficulty and active bonuses can change the shown cost or affection. Base affection, direct affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The Mary Ann introduction needs both Zoo Date and Explore Market; buying ingredients is not a substitute.
  • The two later private activities open at 1000 and 1500 affection, not at the same named stage. Wait for their actual buttons if your point total is hidden.
  • Bring one Voucher for the prize date.

概览

Christy 提供食材购买、一次性创造力提升、关系行动，以及联系 Mary Ann 的路线。大部分内容从农贸市场的肖像开始。

在哪里找到

农贸市场：07:00–17:00；Diane 建成木板路后，她也会在木板路于 17:00–20:00 出现。两处交谈的常规次数分别为 6次/日与 3次/日。

路线与关系节点

土豆种植课 → 集市上的叙旧 (相识 (50)) → 动物园约会与探索市场。完成这两项约会，并完成 Mary Ann 在蝴蝶馆的介绍后，可推进共同介绍路线。后续相连目标请查看主线指南。

常见卡点

  • Mary Ann 的介绍要求完成动物园约会与探索市场，购买食材不能代替。
  • 两项后期私人行动分别要求 1000 与 1500 好感度，并不对应同一个关系阶段。若界面隐藏分数，可根据实际按钮是否出现判断。
  • 奖品约会需携带 1 兑换券。
