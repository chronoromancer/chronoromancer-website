Overview

Christy is a 26-year-old cheerful country girl who runs a potato stand at the farmers market. Blonde braids, denim overalls, checkered shirt, and a southern drawl thick enough to spread on toast. Three years ago she showed up in the city with a beat-up truck full of seed potatoes and not a clue what she was doin'. Now she's one of the warmest faces in the Present Era and a central figure in the V0.91 main story arc.

Her romance is an easy, joyful one — no dramatic hooks, no twists, no hidden agenda. She likes you. She'd like you to know she likes you. And if you keep showin' up, she'll eventually say so herself.

Talk to Christy

Six per day at +1 each (×2 with Animal Care = +2) — easily the most farmable interaction in the Present Era.

Give Wine — The Fun Gift

Friend-level only, two per day. Try for a third and she'll remind you, in her own sugary way, that she works a farm, not a winery.

Potato Education — King's Quest Trigger

$50, 120 minutes, one-time. This is the action that unlocks the Medieval King's Quest food supply questline. Do it early — Christy is the starting node for one of the oldest side quests in the game.

H2H Conversations (2 Total)

1. Runnin the Market (h2h_christy_running_the_market)

  • Requires: Known stage, 45 min, −10 energy, daily limit 1
  • 4 rounds — Christy opens up about what the market means to her

Round 1 — How she got started. Four standard choices (+2 to +5). Special (Empathy skill): "Startin from scratch takes more grit than most folks realize. You should be proud." → +7, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — What she loves about the market. Rhythm, community, the Fletcher twins and Mr. Park. Four standard choices (+3 to +6). Top standard: "Right here might be startin to feel that way."

Round 3 — What scares her. One bad harvest, one big chain settin' up down the road. Four standard choices (+2 to +5). Special (Charm 40+): "You won't lose it, Christy. Folks like you don't fall easy." → +7.

Round 4 — What she hopes to build. Quiet pride, looking out at the stalls. Four standard choices (+4 to +7). Top: "I want what you want. To still be HERE, whatever that looks like." → +7.

Completing this H2H is required to unlock the second H2H.

2. What I've Been Feelin (h2h_christy_what_ive_been_feelin)

  • Requires: Close Friend stage, 60 min, −12 energy, h2h_christy_running_the_market completed
  • 5 rounds — Christy confesses her feelings and the H2H ends in a kiss

Round 1 — She admits she's been looking forward to your visits. Four standard choices (+5 to +8). Special (Empathy skill): "You don't gotta make yourself small about it, Christy. Say what you mean." → +9, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — She opens up about loneliness. The market's full of folks but she's been quietly lonely since she got there. Four standard choices (+5 to +9). Top: "You make MY days quieter too. Good-quieter."

Round 3 — Testing the waters. "When you come by the market, are you comin for the potatoes? Or are you comin for me?" Four standard choices (+7 to +9). Special (Charm 50+): "I come for YOU, Christy. The potatoes are an excuse to keep lookin at ya." → +10.

Round 4 — The moment right before. Her composure cracks, cheeks pink. "Would it be alright if I just did what I wanted to do instead of sayin it?" Four standard choices (+8 to +10). Top: "step a little closer, don't say a word" → +10.

Round 5 — The kiss. She closes the distance and kisses you. Three standard choices (+8 to +10). Top: "pull her in for another, longer kiss" → +10.

This is the emotional peak of her visible content before the video scenes.

Main Story Integration

Christy is central to story beats 53, 54, and 55 in the V0.91 main story arc.

  • Beat 52 — The Strawberry Errand — setup, she's referenced
  • Beat 53 — Ten Million Dollars — Synergy Corp makes an offer on the farmers market land
  • Beat 54 — The Transfer — the negotiation unfolds
  • Beat 55 — The Counter-Offer — with Diane from Town Hall, relationship rewards for both characters

This is a heavy arc. Christy builds everything from nothing and is about to watch it all slip away — unless the player steps in. Beat 53 in particular is one of her most emotional scenes in the game.

Progression Path — Recommended

  1. Stranger → Acknowledged: Talk to Christy 6×/day (+1 each, ×2 with Animal Care = +2). Trigger Potato Education ($50) early to open King's Quest.
  2. Known: Continue talking daily. Complete Relationship Wisdom one-time reward (Communication 33+ for +8 rel + 1 Creativity).
  3. Known+: Trigger Runnin the Market H2H for the first deep conversation.
  4. Acquaintance: Catching Up at the Market unlocks Zoo Date and Explore Market. Do both one-time dates.
  5. Friend: Unlock Wine gifts (+8 each, 2/day). Big daily boost.
  6. Close Friend: Trigger What I've Been Feelin H2H (requires first H2H done). The kiss scene.
  7. 1000+ points: Behind the Stand animated scene unlocks during market hours.
  8. 1500+ points: Roll in the Hay animated scene unlocks during market hours.

Writing Voice Notes

Christy's dialogue is defined by:

  • Heavy southern drawl. "Sugar," "darlin'," "honey," "y'all," "mister." Drops "g"s — "runnin'," "feelin'," "buyin'." Contractions everywhere.
  • "Bless your heart" / "Oh lord" / "sweet pea" — genuine country expressions, never ironic.
  • Warmth as a baseline. She's friendly even to strangers. Not performative — she genuinely likes people.
  • Self-deprecating humor. "That probably sounds silly comin' from a girl who sells root vegetables." Makes herself small but not sadly — it's just her rhythm.
  • Loneliness tucked underneath. She's not a sad character, but the second H2H reveals the quiet loneliness she keeps hidden from customers.
  • Physical grounding. Wipes potato dirt off her hands, leans against her stand, tucks hair behind her ear, picks up a potato and turns it in her hands. Her dialogue stays physically anchored to the market.
  • Playful directness when flirting. "Not a CHANCE, darlin'." "Don't you DARE finish that sentence." Capitals for emphasis in flirty moments.
  • She is going to make the cowgirl joke herself. She knows. She is insufferable about it. Lean in.

When writing Christy: sugary drawl + physical grounding at the stand + warmth-first, loneliness-underneath + self-deprecating humor + capital-letter emphasis when flustered or flirty.

Tips & Notes

  • Animal Care skill is essential — it doubles every interaction. Learn it early.
  • Wine is her best daily gift — +8 × 2/day = +16 with Animal Care = +32. Big daily boost.
  • She hates Book gifts — save those for Bella.
  • Flowers are her favorite gift category — standard flowers action exists if the flowers consumable is available.
  • Potato Education unlocks King's Quest — don't forget to do it, even if you're not romancing her.
  • Talk limit is 6/day — the highest in the game. Farm this aggressively.
  • Both dates require christy_market_catchup_completed — don't skip the catchup plot.
  • The second H2H requires the first H2H completed — sequential gate.
  • The 1500-point cowgirl scene is the payoff. She has been waiting on you specifically. She will tell you.
  • Main story beats 53–55 are heavy — save before each one.

概述

Christy 是一位26岁、性格开朗的乡村姑娘，在农贸市场经营一个土豆摊。她梳着金色辫子，身穿牛仔背带裤和格子衬衫，一口南方乡音浓得简直能抹在吐司上。三年前，她开着一辆破旧卡车来到城里，车上装满种薯，自己却完全不知道该怎么做。如今，她已成为现代最亲切的面孔之一，也是V0.91主线故事中的核心人物。

她的恋爱路线轻松而快乐——没有戏剧性的引子，没有反转，也没有隐藏目的。她喜欢你，也希望你知道她喜欢你。只要你不断来见她，她最终会亲口告诉你。

与Christy交谈

每天可进行六次，每次+1（有动物照料时×2，即+2）——这是现代最容易反复刷取的互动。

赠送葡萄酒——有趣的礼物

仅限朋友阶段以上，每天两次。如果想送第三瓶，她会用自己甜得发腻的方式提醒你：她经营的是 农场，可不是酿酒厂。

马铃薯教育——国王任务触发点

消耗$50和120分钟，仅限一次。该行动会解锁中世纪国王任务中的食品供应任务线。请尽早完成——Christy是游戏中最古老的支线任务之一的起始节点。

交心谈话（共2段）

1. 经营市场（h2h_christy_running_the_market）

  • 要求： 已知阶段、45分钟、精力−10、每日上限1次
  • 4轮对话——Christy会敞开心扉，谈谈市场对她而言意味着什么

第1轮——她如何起步。 四个标准选项（+2至+5）。特殊选项（同理心技能）：“白手起家需要的韧劲儿，可比大多数人想象的多。你应该为自己骄傲。” →+7，解锁变体。

第2轮——她喜欢市场的哪些地方。 这里的节奏、社群、Fletcher家的双胞胎和Park先生。四个标准选项（+3至+6）。最佳标准选项：“这里或许已经开始有家的感觉了。”

第3轮——她害怕什么。 一次歉收，或者一家大型连锁店开到这条路上。四个标准选项（+2至+5）。特殊选项（魅力40以上）：“你不会失去这里的，Christy。像你这样的人没那么容易倒下。” →+7。

第4轮——她希望建立什么。 她带着安静的自豪，看向一排排摊位。四个标准选项（+4至+7）。最佳选项：“我想要的和你一样。不管将来变成什么样，都要继续留在这里。” →+7。

必须完成这段交心谈话，才能解锁第二段。

2. 我一直有的感觉（h2h_christy_what_ive_been_feelin）

  • 要求： 亲密朋友阶段、60分钟、精力−12，且已完成h2h_christy_running_the_market
  • 5轮对话——Christy会坦白自己的感情，最后以一个吻结束交心谈话

第1轮——她承认自己一直期待你的来访。 四个标准选项（+5至+8）。特殊选项（同理心技能）：“你不用把自己的心意说得那么轻描淡写，Christy。想说什么，就直说吧。” →+9，解锁变体。

第2轮——她坦承自己的孤独。 市场里总是人来人往，可她自从来到这里后，内心一直悄悄感到孤单。四个标准选项（+5至+9）。最佳选项：“你也让我的日子安静了下来。是舒服的那种安静。”

第3轮——试探你的心意。 “你来市场找我的时候，到底是为了土豆，还是为了俺？”四个标准选项（+7至+9）。特殊选项（魅力50以上）：“我是来找你的，Christy。土豆只是让我能一直看着你的借口。” →+10。

第4轮——行动前的一刻。 她再也维持不住镇定，双颊泛红。“要是俺不再光说，直接做自己想做的事……你愿意吗？”四个标准选项（+8至+10）。最佳选项：“ 悄悄靠近一步，一句话也不说 ” →+10。

第5轮——那个吻。 她靠近你，吻了上来。三个标准选项（+8至+10）。最佳选项：“ 将她揽进怀里，再吻一次，这次更久 ” →+10。

这是视频场景之前，她现有内容中的情感最高点。

写作语气说明

Christy的对话风格由以下元素构成：

  • 浓重的美国南方口音。 “甜心”“亲爱的”“宝贝”“大伙儿”“先生”。词尾的“g”经常省略——例如“runnin'”“feelin'”“buyin'”。到处都是缩略语。
  • “愿上帝保佑你”／“老天爷啊”／“小甜豆”——都是真诚的乡村表达，从不带讽刺意味。
  • 以热情为底色。 她即使面对陌生人也很友好。这不是表演——她是真的喜欢与人相处。
  • 自嘲式幽默。 “这话从一个卖根茎蔬菜的姑娘嘴里说出来，大概挺傻的。”她总会把自己说得微不足道，却并不悲伤——这只是她的说话节奏。
  • 藏在底下的孤独。 她不是一个悲伤的角色，但第二段交心谈话揭示了她一直藏在顾客面前的那份静默孤独。
  • 以身体动作落地。 她会擦去手上的土豆泥，倚在摊位边，把头发别到耳后，拿起土豆在手里翻转。她的对话始终扎根于市场里的真实动作。
  • 调情时俏皮而直接。 “想都别想，亲爱的。”“你敢把那句话说完试试。”调情或慌乱时会用大写字母强调语气。
  • 她肯定会亲口开那个女牛仔玩笑。 她心里清楚，而且会故意烦个没完。顺着她来吧。

为Christy撰写内容时，应遵循：甜蜜的乡音+摊位旁扎实的身体动作+热情为先、孤独藏在底下+自嘲式幽默+慌乱或调情时用大写字母强调。

技巧与说明

  • 动物照料技能至关重要——它会让每次互动的收益翻倍，请尽早学会。
  • 葡萄酒是她最好的每日礼物——+8×每天2瓶=有动物照料时+16×2，即+32，是一项巨大的每日加成。
  • 她讨厌书籍礼物——把书留给Bella吧。
  • 鲜花是她最喜欢的礼物类别——如果持有可消耗的鲜花物品，就可以使用标准赠花行动。
  • 马铃薯教育会解锁国王任务——即使不打算攻略她，也别忘了完成。
  • 交谈上限是每天6次——全游戏最高，请积极刷取。
  • 两次约会都要求christy_market_catchup_completed——不要跳过叙旧剧情。
  • 第二段交心谈话要求先完成第一段——这是连续前置条件。
  • 1500点的女牛仔场景就是最终回报。 她一直在等的就是你，她也会亲口告诉你。
  • 主线故事节点53—55情节沉重——每个节点开始前都请保存游戏。
