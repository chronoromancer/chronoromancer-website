Overview

Blinky is a thousands-of-years-old wizard who involuntarily teleports through time and space — pop! — appearing in random places, never staying long enough for anyone to remember her. She's ageless, naive about basic human things like burned food and kissing, and speaks in confused bursts. She calls herself Blinky because of the blinking. Her staff holds fragments of every place she's ever been.

She arrives at the brothel during blinky_arrival and stays — the first place she's ever stopped blinking away from. The player's establishment becomes her first home.

She teaches the exclusive Blink skill and serves as a magical mentor alongside Sashina (though her teaching style is pure confused enthusiasm).

Appearance

Cute, wizard-hatted figure with a staff full of swirling memory-light. Deliberately lacking physical_description block in her character data — her visual identity is image-driven.

Unlock — Arrival Plot (blinky_arrival)

She teleports into the brothel in a flash of purple light, confused about when and where she is. "I was just at a place with floating rocks... or was that last week? Or next week?"

Three-Choice System — First Meeting Response

  • Polite ("Are you alright?") → She thanks the player sincerely; she usually blinks away before anyone asks
  • Normal ("Uh... yeah, I'm a person. Who are you?") → "I forget how to introduce myself — I don't meet persons often"
  • Flirty ("You're quite cute when you're confused") → She doesn't know what "cute" means but assumes it's good

The Offer

She proposes the Anchor Spell trade — let her stay, and she'll teach the player how to lock onto a place and always teleport back. Accept any of three responses (polite, casual, warm):

  • "That sounds like a fair trade. You can stay."
  • "Sure, why not? You seem harmless enough."
  • "I'd love to have you stay here, Blinky."

She vanishes in another flash of purple light mid-conversation to "find her room" — leaving the player realizing she apparently has a room now.

Starting stage after arrival: Acknowledged (not Stranger).

Blink Training Scene (blinky_blink_training)

Triggered by the Blink skill action. Blinky is ecstatic — "It's my favorite spell! Well, it's sort of why everyone calls me Blinky."

Three-Choice System

  • Kind ("Thank you for teaching me") → "I don't get to teach people very often. It's nice to share something I'm actually good at" (image 7, blushing)
  • Funny ("Anchor here in case I need to escape boring conversations?") → "That's exactly what I use it for sometimes!" (image 9, giggling)
  • Flirty ("With you as my teacher I'll master it in no time") → "O-oh! Let's focus on the magic part for now" (image 11, bright red fidgeting)

The Training

She guides the player through anchor-point meditation. "Feel the space around you. Now imagine this room as your safe place, your anchor point... When you need to return, just reach for that anchor and... Blink!" The player gains a permanent sense of their anchor point location. Skill added.

H2H Conversations (2 Total)

1. Where Do I Belong? (h2h_blinky_where_do_i_belong)

  • Unlock: Acquaintance stage, 60 min, daily limit 1
  • 5 rounds. Completion is tracked as h2h_blinky_where_do_i_belong in completed_h2h_conversations.

Blinky is unusually quiet, sitting on the floor clutching her staff. The crystals pulse in a slow, melancholy rhythm. She's processing something deep.

Round 1 — Finding her still. Standard +5-6. Special (Empathy): +8, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — Her staff holds memories of every place she's been. "A world with three suns. A forest that grew upside down. A city made entirely of singing glass. Hundreds of places." Variant tone: "Beautiful places. But I was never in any of them long enough to matter." Standard +5-7 across three choices.

Round 3 — Her vulnerability peaks. "Sometimes I wonder if I am even a real person. Maybe I am just... a thing that happens. Like wind. Or lightning." Standard +7-8. Special (Emotional Bonding): +10 — "You are not something that happens to places. You are someone who deserves a place of her own."

Round 4 — Hope. She asks if the player is the reason she stopped blinking away. +7-8 choices.

Round 5 — Peace and belonging. Her crystal glows warm and steady for the first time. She presses her staff against her heart: "Home is not a place you pass through. Home is where someone notices when you are quiet. Where someone sits with you when you are sad. You made me real." Single +25 closer.

Narrative importance: The emotional foundation H2H. Establishes that the player has actively ended Blinky's endless wandering. She stopped blinking because of the player.

2. Never Been Kissed (h2h_blinky_never_been_kissed) — FIRST KISS

  • Unlock: Close Friend stage, 60 min, daily limit 1
  • 5 rounds. Completion is tracked as h2h_blinky_never_been_kissed in completed_h2h_conversations.

She's hunched over the Grimoire of Emerald Weaving — a tome about how emotions shape magical energy. She's read that a kiss between two people who care for each other is the most powerful magical catalyst that exists, and she's never experienced one.

Round 1 — She's absorbed in the book, blushing. Standard +5-6. Special (Empathy): +8, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — She explains emotional magic — fear strengthens wards, joy amplifies healing. Then the kissing chapter. Standard +5-7. Variant: "And I realized I have never experienced that. Not once."

Round 3 — She admits she wants to try. "You are the first person who has stayed. Or maybe... I am the first me who has stayed." Standard +7-8. Special (Emotional Bonding): +10 — "You don't need a grimoire to tell you what you're feeling right now."

Round 4 — The moment before. Face close-up, anticipation. "I am nervous. My heart is doing the fast thing again." Standard +7-8 across three comforting responses.

Round 5 — The kiss. The crystals in the room flare, the air hums, her staff on the floor blazes emerald. "That was not just magic. That was... everything." Single +25 closer.

Mechanical significance: Her first romantic peak. Its canonical H2H completion gates later story progression and supports the push toward Romantic/Intimate stages.

Bazaar Date (bazaar_blinky)

  • Requirements: Known stage+, $75, 120 min, −40 energy, 9 AM – 2 PM
  • One-time — hidden after bazaar_blinky_completed

She's been reading books describing "a bazaar" — "a place full of smells" — and is baffled. "How can a place be full of smells?" The player offers to show her.

Three Decision Points

Point 1 — Bazaar arrival, she's overwhelmed and grabs the player's arm:

  • Caring: "Stay close. I will guide you through it all" → "I have never had a guide before"
  • Funny: "Nobody knows where they're going. They walk with confidence and hope" → "That is exactly what I do when I blink! Maybe I have been doing society correctly this whole time!"
  • Flirty: "Just hold on to me. I kind of like you this close" → "My face is doing the warm thing again. I think that means I like it too" (image 21, shy)

Point 2 — Food stall, Blinky discovers burned food:

  • Caring: "This is your day" → "Nobody has ever given me a whole day before"
  • Funny: "Humanity's greatest achievement is setting food on fire" → "Greater than the crystal bridge of the fourth realm?"
  • Flirty: Wipe sauce from her lip → She freezes: "Can you do that again later? For non-sauce reasons?" (image 21)

Point 3 — Trinket stall, she finds a blue crystal with rain-memory inside: Player buys it for her. "Nobody has ever given me a gift before. I have had things thrown AT me, but never given TO me."

  • Caring: "Some memories stay with you forever on their own" → "Maybe memories and people work the same way. The important ones do not leave"
  • Funny: "If anyone can jam a memory into a rock it's you" → "I accidentally stored a sunset inside a teacup once. The tea tasted like orange for a week"
  • Flirty: "I plan on giving you plenty more memories worth saving" → "My heart is doing the fast thing again" (image 21)

Closing

Back at the Arcane Room, she carefully places the crystal on her desk. "Today I learned about cinnamon, and burned food, and crystals with memories, and how persons go to places together on purpose just to have fun. That is called a 'date,' right? ... Is there a form I need to fill out?" bazaar_blinky_completed flag set.

Iconic line: "Cinnamon. Cinnamon cinnamon cinnamon."

Sunset Walk Date (sunset_walk_blinky)

  • Requirements: Close Friend stage+, 120 min, −30 energy, 4 PM – 7 PM
  • One-time — hidden after sunset_walk_blinky_completed
  • No money cost (free date)

Takes Blinky to see her first sunset. She has wandered thousands of years through time and space but has never stayed in one place long enough to watch a full sun set from start to finish. Follows the three-choice-point structure common to the game's dates.

Stage gate is Close Friend — later than the Bazaar Date's Known requirement. This is the emotional-peak date, after "Where Do I Belong?" has built the foundation.

Progression Path — Recommended

  1. Complete blinky_arrival → Blinky's Arcane Room unlocks, she starts at Acknowledged.
  2. Acknowledged: Learn Protective Ward (Mystic 14, 180 min). Talk 5×/day.
  3. Known: Learn Divination (Mystic 22, 200 min). Complete Wisdom of Moments one-time (+1 Mystic, Communication 50+). Bazaar Date (9 AM – 2 PM, $75) — one of the most endearing scenes in the game.
  4. Acquaintance: "Where Do I Belong?" H2H — foundational emotional beat.
  5. Close Friend: Learn Blink exclusive skill (Mystic 28+, 260 min). Sunset Walk Date (4-7 PM). "Never Been Kissed" H2H — first kiss.
  6. Romantic + Story Beat 043 + paid version: Intimate Moment video action.

Writing Voice Notes

Blinky's voice is one of the most distinct in the game:

  • "Um..." as punctuation. "Um... is this... where I am now?" "I have been to... um... a very long time." Hesitation baked into every thought.
  • "Persons" instead of "people." Her alien perspective on basic social categories. She has to think about what a person is.
  • Literal descriptions of concepts she doesn't have words for. "The warm brown one" (cinnamon). "The fast thing" (heartbeat). "The warm thing" (blushing). "Making her lips do the thing" (smiling).
  • Runs words together when excited. "Cinnamon cinnamon cinnamon." "Burned food burned food."
  • Matter-of-fact about absurdity. "I have been eating things raw for centuries." "One time I accidentally stored a sunset inside a teacup."
  • Describes people like she describes places. "The warm brown one" for cinnamon, "the nice person" for the player at first. Everything is a novel observation.
  • No contractions early on. Stilted, slightly archaic phrasing that softens as she warms up. "I do not" becomes "I don't" in more intimate moments.
  • Her vulnerability arrives through literal metaphors. "Maybe I am just... a thing that happens. Like wind. Or lightning." Never abstract — always concrete natural phenomena.
  • Zero social instincts. She asks if there's a form to fill out for dates. She doesn't know that "cute" is usually a compliment. She has to deduce emotions from physical symptoms.

When writing Blinky: "um..." hesitations, "persons" instead of "people," literal descriptions of concepts, no contractions, and her emotions always expressed as physical observations rather than feelings.

Tips & Notes

  • Cultural Adaptation is her Special Skill — learn it early to double all relationship points. Intelligence 14 skill, very low bar.
  • She's a 3-skill Mystic teacher (Protective Ward, Divination, Blink) — essential stop for any Mystic-build player.
  • Blink is exclusive — only Blinky teaches it anywhere in the game. Don't miss this.
  • H2H calm bonus is unique. Both her H2Hs restore +5 calm instead of draining it. Use Blinky's H2Hs to recharge between more demanding activities.
  • Talk daily limit 5 — highest-limit Talk in the game alongside a few others. Grind-viable with Cultural Adaptation for +2/Talk = +10/day passively.
  • Bazaar Date at Known is very early — do this ASAP. "Cinnamon cinnamon cinnamon" alone is worth the trip.
  • Sunset Walk is free ($0) — no money barrier for the second date.
  • Intimate Moment needs Story Beat 043 — the video action is completely hidden until the story flag is set. Don't worry about rel points gating; push the main story.
  • No gifts defined — don't waste chocolate/flowers/wine on her. Points come from Talk, skills, H2Hs, and dates only.
  • No apartment visit system — she has no 40-round late-night conversation. Her intimate progression lives entirely in the video action.

概述

Blinky 是一名活了数千年的巫师，会不由自主地穿越时空——啪！——突然出现在随机地点，又总是在任何人记住她之前离开。她不会衰老，对烤焦的食物、亲吻等人类常识十分懵懂，说话时总是一阵阵困惑。因为自己会不断“闪现”，她给自己取名Blinky。她的法杖保存着自己到过的每个地方的碎片。

她会在blinky_arrival期间来到青楼并留下——这是她生平第一次 没有闪现离开的地方。玩家的产业成为了她的第一个家。

她会教授 独有的闪现技能，也像Sashina一样担任魔法导师（不过她的教学风格完全是困惑又热情）。

解锁——到来剧情（blinky_arrival）

她在紫光一闪后传送进青楼，对自己身处何时何地十分困惑。“我刚才还在一个有漂浮岩石的地方……还是那是上周？或者下周？”

三选一系统——初次见面时的回应

  • 礼貌（“你还好吗？”）→她会真诚感谢玩家；通常她会在人们来得及询问前就闪现离开
  • 普通（“呃……对，我是个人。你是谁？”）→“我总是忘记怎么自我介绍——我不常遇到‘人’。”
  • 调情（“你困惑的时候还挺可爱的”）→她不知道“可爱”是什么意思，但觉得应该是好话

提议

她提出以 锚定咒 作交换——让她留在这里，她便教玩家如何锁定某个地点，并随时传送回来。下列三种回应（礼貌、随意、热情）任选其一即可接受：

  • “听起来很公平。你可以留下。”
  • “行啊，为什么不呢？你看起来挺无害的。”
  • “我很愿意让你留在这里，Blinky。”

她说到一半便在另一阵紫光中消失，要去“找她的房间”——只留下玩家意识到，她显然已经在这里有了房间。

  • 亲密时刻受故事进度限制： 只有拥有story_beat_043_completed标记时才会显示——必须推进主线故事。
  • 亲密时刻属于付费内容（requires_paid_version: True）。
  • 片刻的智慧 除了属性和点数外，还会奖励 巫师影响力。
  • 第七折叠 是将魔导书从6级提升到7级的一次性路线。它要求达到恋爱中、拥有付费访问权限，并且魔导书恰好为6级；设置blinky_grimoire_level_7_completed后隐藏。

Blinky教授的技能

防护结界（已遇见、神秘14以上、180分钟）

  • 标准技能：通过神秘屏障降低魔法伤害
  • 学会后隐藏
  • 最早可学的技能——已遇见阶段是游戏中的最低门槛

占卜（已知、神秘22以上、200分钟）

  • 标准技能：窥见可能发生的未来
  • 与Sashina的预知天赋在主题上相呼应——Blinky教授实际技能，Sashina则体现相关设定

闪现（亲密朋友、神秘28以上、260分钟）——独有

  • 触发blinky_blink_training剧情场景
  • 通过神秘的空间折叠进行短距离传送
  • 唯一来源——整个游戏中只有Blinky教授这项技能
  • 锚点机制：锁定一个地点，之后随时传送回来

片刻的智慧（已知、沟通50以上、一次性）

  • 永久神秘+1
  • 好感度+5、巫师影响力+1
  • 标记：blinky_wisdom_of_moments_completed
  • 她关于存在与时间的哲学

闪现训练场景（blinky_blink_training）

通过闪现技能行动触发。Blinky欣喜若狂——“这是我最喜欢的法术！嗯，大家叫我Blinky，多少也是因为这个。”

三选一系统

  • 友善（“谢谢你教我”）→“我很少有机会教别人。能分享一件自己真正擅长的事，感觉真好。”（图像7，脸红）
  • 风趣（“在这里设置锚点，好让我逃离无聊的对话？”）→“我有时候就是这么用的！”（图像9，咯咯笑）
  • 调情（“有你当老师，我很快就能掌握”）→“哦、哦！我们先专心学魔法部分吧。”（图像11，满脸通红、局促不安）

训练

她引导玩家进行锚点冥想。“感受你周围的空间。现在，把这个房间想象成你的安全之处、你的锚点……需要回来时，只要伸手触及那个锚点，然后……闪现！”玩家由此获得对自己锚点位置的永久感知，并习得技能。

谈心对话（共2段）

1. 我属于哪里？（h2h_blinky_where_do_i_belong）

  • 解锁： 相识阶段、60分钟、每日上限1次
  • 5轮。 完成状态以completed_h2h_conversations中的h2h_blinky_where_do_i_belong为准。

Blinky一反常态地安静，坐在地板上紧抱法杖。水晶以缓慢而忧郁的节奏闪烁。她正在思索一些沉重的事。

第1轮——发现她安静地待着。普通选项+5至6。特殊选项（共情）：+8，并解锁变体。

第2轮——她的法杖保存着去过的每个地方的记忆。“一个有三颗太阳的世界。一片倒着生长的森林。一座完全由歌唱玻璃建成的城市。数百个地方。”变体语气：“美丽的地方。但我在每个地方都待得不够久，根本无法留下意义。” 三个普通选项分别为+5至7。

第3轮——她最脆弱的时刻。“有时我会怀疑，自己到底算不算一个真正的人。也许我只是……一件会发生的事。像风，或者闪电。”普通选项+7至8。特殊选项（情感纽带）：+10——“你不是发生在不同地方的一件事。你是一个值得拥有自己归宿的人。”

第4轮——希望。她问，自己不再闪现离开是否是因为玩家。各选项+7至8。

第5轮——平静与归属。她的水晶第一次散发温暖而稳定的光。她将法杖压在胸口：“家不是你路过的地方。家是有人会注意到你何时沉默，会在你难过时陪你坐着的地方。是你让我成为了真实的人。” 唯一的收尾选项+25。

叙事重要性： 这是奠定感情基础的谈心。它表明玩家主动终结了Blinky无休止的漂泊。她是因为玩家才停止闪现离开。

2. 从未被亲吻（h2h_blinky_never_been_kissed）——初吻

  • 解锁： 亲密朋友阶段、60分钟、每日上限1次
  • 5轮。 完成状态以completed_h2h_conversations中的h2h_blinky_never_been_kissed为准。

她正伏在 翡翠编织魔导书 上——这本书讨论情感如何塑造魔法能量。她读到，两个彼此关心的人接吻，是世间最强大的魔法催化剂，而她从未体验过。

第1轮——她全神贯注地读书，脸颊泛红。普通选项+5至6。特殊选项（共情）：+8，并解锁变体。

第2轮——她解释情感魔法——恐惧会强化结界，喜悦会增强治疗。接着是亲吻的章节。普通选项+5至7。变体：“我意识到，自己从未体验过。一次都没有。”

第3轮——她承认自己想试试看。“你是第一个留下来的人。或者也许……我是第一个留下来的‘我’。”普通选项+7至8。特殊选项（情感纽带）：+10——“你不需要魔导书告诉你，此刻正在感受什么。”

第4轮——前一刻。 面部特写，满怀期待。“我很紧张。我的心又开始做那件很快的事了。”三种安慰回应分别为+7至8。

第5轮——亲吻。 房间里的水晶骤然亮起，空气嗡鸣，地板上的法杖绽放出明亮的翡翠光芒。“那不只是魔法。那是……一切。”唯一的收尾选项+25。

机制意义： 这是她第一次达到恋爱高峰。该谈心的标准完成记录会解锁后续剧情，并为继续推进浪漫感／亲密阶段奠定基础。

集市约会（bazaar_blinky）

  • 要求： 已知阶段以上、$75、120分钟、−40精力、上午9点至下午2点
  • 一次性——设置bazaar_blinky_completed后隐藏

她在书中读到“集市”——“一个充满气味的地方”——对此困惑不已。“一个地方怎么会‘充满’气味？”玩家主动提出带她去看看。

三个选择点

选择点1——到达集市，她不知所措，抓住玩家的手臂：

  • 关怀：“跟紧我。我会带你看遍这里的一切。”→“以前从来没有人为我带路。”
  • 风趣：“没人知道自己要去哪儿。大家只是自信地往前走，然后抱着希望。”→“这和我闪现时做的完全一样！也许我一直都在正确地融入社会！”
  • 调情：“抓紧我就好。我还挺喜欢你靠这么近。”→“我的脸又开始做那件暖暖的事了。我想这代表我也喜欢。”（图像21，害羞）

选择点2——食品摊，Blinky发现烤焦的食物：

  • 关怀：“今天是属于你的一天。”→“以前从来没有人送给我整整一天。”
  • 风趣：“人类最伟大的成就，就是把食物放到火上烧。”→“比第四界的水晶桥还伟大？”
  • 调情： 擦去她唇边的酱汁→她僵住：“以后可以再做一次吗？不是因为酱汁的那种？”（图像21）

选择点3——小饰品摊，她发现一枚内部保存着雨之记忆的蓝色水晶： 玩家买下送给她。“以前从来没有人送过我礼物。别人倒是会把东西朝我‘扔’过来，但从没有把东西‘送’给我。”

  • 关怀：“有些记忆本来就会永远陪着你。”→“也许记忆和人是一样的。重要的那些不会离开。”
  • 风趣：“如果有谁能把记忆塞进石头里，那一定是你。”→“我有一次不小心把日落存进了茶杯里，那杯茶整整一周都像橙子。”
  • 调情：“我还打算送给你许多值得保存的回忆。”→“我的心又开始做那件很快的事了。”（图像21）

结尾

回到奥术房后，她小心翼翼地把水晶放在书桌上。“今天我学到了肉桂、烤焦的食物、保存着记忆的水晶，还有‘人’会为了开心而特意一起去某个地方。那就叫‘约会’，对吧？……我需要填什么表格吗？”设置bazaar_blinky_completed标记。

标志性台词： “肉桂。肉桂肉桂肉桂。”

日落散步约会（sunset_walk_blinky）

  • 要求： 亲密朋友阶段以上、120分钟、−30精力、下午4点至晚上7点
  • 一次性——设置sunset_walk_blinky_completed后隐藏
  • 不花钱（免费约会）

带Blinky去看 她人生中的第一次日落。她已经在时空中漂泊了数千年，却从未在一个地方停留足够久，从头到尾看完一次太阳落下。约会采用游戏中常见的三个选择点结构。

阶段门槛是亲密朋友——比集市约会的已知要求更晚。这是情感达到顶峰的约会，发生在“我属于哪里？”奠定感情基础之后。

写作语气说明

Blinky的说话方式是游戏中最鲜明的风格之一：

  • 以“呃……”作为标点。“呃……这里就是……我现在待的地方吗？”“我已经去过……呃……很久很久了。”每个念头里都带着迟疑。
  • 用“人”这个类别，而不是自然地说“大家”。 这是她从局外看待基本社会分类的方式。她必须认真思考“人”究竟是什么。
  • 用字面描述表达自己不会说的概念。“暖暖的棕色东西”（肉桂）、“很快的那件事”（心跳）、“暖暖的事”（脸红）、“让她的嘴唇做那件事”（微笑）。
  • 兴奋时会把词连在一起。“肉桂肉桂肉桂。”“烤焦的食物烤焦的食物。”
  • 一本正经地谈论荒唐事。“几百年来，我一直生吃东西。”“有一次我不小心把日落存进了茶杯里。”
  • 像描述地点一样描述人。 她称肉桂为“暖暖的棕色东西”，起初称玩家为“那个好心的人”。一切都是新奇的观察。
  • 前期不会使用缩略表达。 她的遣词生硬而略显古旧，随着关系升温才会变得柔和。“我不会”在更亲密的时刻会逐渐变得更加口语化。
  • 她通过字面比喻流露脆弱。“也许我只是……一件会发生的事。像风，或者闪电。”从不抽象——总是具体的自然现象。
  • 完全没有社交直觉。 她会询问约会是否需要填表，不知道“可爱”通常是称赞，只能通过身体症状推断情绪。

撰写Blinky时应把握：“呃……”式迟疑、把“人”当作陌生类别、对概念的字面描述、不使用缩略表达，以及始终以身体观察而不是情感词语表达自己的情绪。

提示与说明

  • 文化适应是她的特殊技能——尽早学会，让所有好感度翻倍。这项技能只要求智力14，门槛很低。
  • 她会教授3项神秘技能（防护结界、占卜、闪现）——任何神秘流派的玩家都一定要拜访她。
  • 闪现是独有技能——整个游戏中只有Blinky会教授，千万不要错过。
  • 谈心的镇定奖励独一无二。 她的两段谈心都会 恢复 5镇定，而不是消耗。可以在更费力的活动之间通过Blinky的谈心补充镇定。
  • 交谈每日上限5次——与少数角色并列为游戏中的最高交谈上限。拥有文化适应时，可以稳定刷取每次+2，也就是每天被动+10。
  • 集市约会在已知阶段便开放，时间很早——应当尽快完成。光是“肉桂肉桂肉桂”就值得跑这一趟。
  • 日落散步免费（$0）——第二次约会没有金钱门槛。
  • 亲密时刻需要故事节点043——设置故事标记前，视频行动会完全隐藏。无需担心好感度门槛；请推进主线故事。
  • 未定义礼物——不要在她身上浪费巧克力／花束／酒。点数只能通过交谈、技能、谈心和约会获得。
  • 没有公寓拜访系统——她没有40轮深夜对话。其亲密推进完全依赖视频行动。
