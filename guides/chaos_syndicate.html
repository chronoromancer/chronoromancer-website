Guide noticeThis page contains gameplay and story spoilers and may describe mature systems.

The Chaos Syndicate is a built-in Future Era faction story located beneath the Prime Sector. Its current implementation contains eight locations, seven separately tracked character identities, 65 registered one-time plots, eight heart-to-heart conversations, six repeatable relationship videos, and a main story that currently ends at After the Line.

This walkthrough was audited against the current game source. It contains route and unlock spoilers, but avoids reproducing major narrative reveals.

Critical relationship note: Do not expect Talk actions immediately after meeting the crew. Every Syndicate Talk action requires Acquaintance at 50 relationship points. Early relationship progress therefore comes from one-time story rewards and choices. Read Building Relationships Without Getting Stuck before rushing through those plots.

Quick Completion Route

For the cleanest route through all currently implemented Syndicate content:

  1. Enter Syndicate Gates from the Future Prime Sector map and complete The Gates of Chaos.
  2. Visit the Parlor, Big Top, Fortune Teller's Booth, Funhouse, Gates, Garden, and Motley's Lair to clear every introduction.
  3. Complete Collecting Dues and collect the payment at the Synthetic Beings Recharge Station.
  4. Finish all three post-collection branches: Cat's Eyes, New Blood → Taming the Rookie, and Faces.
  5. Complete Street Knowledge and Welcome to the Circus if you did not already do so.
  6. Complete The Boss Takes Notice, then enter the Parlor for Lines on the Board.
  7. Raise raw Rogue to 50 for Open Doors, then to 55 for The Honeypot. The two Syndicate training plots grant +1 Rogue each.
  8. Complete No Plan Survives, then do both The Abandoned Room and The Bug before returning to the Funhouse for The Performance.
  9. Follow the automatic aftermath chain through The Punchline, The Weight of a Secret, Badge and Bluster, The Second Knock, and After the Line.
  10. Build each relationship through Known, Friend, and Close Friend. Complete all new home-location plots, Den scenes, and H2Hs as they appear.
  11. Finish Kitty, Poison, Chamel, Zodiac, and Motley's advanced routes before attempting Bayne's final plot, The Order.
  12. Raise every desired character to In Love and collect the normal one-time relationship Crystal in a Present player-house bedroom.

Mainline Rewards and Current Endpoint

The two permanent player-stat rewards are:

  • Cat's Eyes: +1 Rogue
  • Taming the Rookie: +1 Rogue

All other registered mainline rewards are relationship points distributed among the cast. Completion and choice rewards go through the normal randomized relationship calculation, so two saves can finish a scene with different totals.

After the Line is the current endpoint of the implemented faction mainline. It is not followed by a faction victory screen, repeatable Syndicate job board, faction shop, faction-clout reward, or another registered operational plot. Several narrative hooks remain open for future development; a player who has finished After the Line is current on the faction story even if those hooks appear unresolved.

The Acquaintance bottleneck

Every Syndicate character starts at Stranger, while all seven Talk actions require Acquaintance. There are also no ordinary Syndicate gift, invite-date, resort, or remote-action bridges below 50 in the current character definitions.

The intended practical bridge is the collection of mainline completion and choice rewards. Those rewards are randomized and choices often favor different characters, so a crew member can finish the early story below 50.

Before consuming one-time Syndicate plots, improve the relationship calculation where possible:

  • meet the character's preferred-stat thresholds;
  • acquire the character's favorite special skill;
  • use Cologne and relevant relationship items;
  • use a matching active alignment;
  • select choices that award the character whose total is lagging.

Once Talk unlocks, it costs 15 minutes, starts from 1 base relationship point, and has five safe uses per character per day. Exceeding the daily limit can produce negative relationship points, so stop after five.

Current-build limitation: If a character remains below 50 after all of their available ungated one-time plots, that character has no standard repeatable catch-up action. Continue other story branches that reward them if any remain. If none remain, the route is mechanically stalled in the current implementation rather than hidden behind an undocumented location.

For the complete reward formula and over-limit behavior, see Relationship, Dating & Intimacy.

Motley and the control arc

Motley's advanced route is intertwined with Zodiac's route:

  1. Complete The Honeypot and Zodiac's The Confession.
  2. Enter Motley's Lair for The Game. It has no additional relationship-stage gate.
  3. Reach Friend with Motley and complete The Reckoning.
  4. Visit the Fortune Teller's Booth for Behind the Curtain.
  5. Complete Motley's Friend and Close Friend milestones through The Tell.
  6. Reach Crush with Motley and complete The Summons in her Lair.
  7. Enter the Den at Crush for The Fascination.
  8. Reach Romantic and complete The Dissection, then The Truth, in Motley's Lair.

Motley's route cannot currently bypass Zodiac's Crush-stage Confession and the resulting control storyline.

Bayne — Gates and Motley's Lair

  1. Complete The Post at Friend and Standing Watch at Close Friend.
  2. Reach Crush and enter the Gates for The Vigil.
  3. Prepare the following exact final requirements:
    • Bayne at Intimate;
    • Motley at Romantic;
    • The Vigil completed;
    • Motley's The Truth completed;
    • Kitty's Tamed completed;
    • Poison's The Test completed;
    • Chamel's The Real Thing completed.
  4. Enter Motley's Lair for The Order.

The Order checks Poison's first Crush plot, The Test—not Proving Ground or The Real Poison.

Chamella's Route

Chamella occupies the Funhouse from 9 AM to 3 PM while Chamel is absent. She has a separate relationship total.

Her one-time route content is:

  • No Plan Survives — first mainline relationship reward involving Chamella.
  • The Abandoned Room — after No Plan Survives.
  • The Performance — after The Abandoned Room and The Bug.
  • Mirror Duty — 9 AM-3 PM after Faces; separate from the mainline sequence.
  • The Weight of a Secret — later mainline reward.

Chamella does not have the Known/Friend/Close Friend milestone trilogy or an H2H. At 1,000 points, she unlocks the repeatable Stay a While video action.

Rogue Training with Kitty

After Kitty reaches Acquaintance, Rogue Training appears at the Big Top:

  • cost: $150 and 20 Energy;
  • duration: 60 minutes;
  • reward: +1 Rogue and 3 base Kitty relationship points;
  • safe daily limit: 3.

This is the Syndicate's repeatable solution to the Rogue 50 and 55 mainline gates, but it cannot help until Kitty first reaches 50 relationship points.

Cat's Eyes and Taming the Rookie each grant their +1 Rogue only once. Legacy-save migration remembers those permanent rewards so replaying reset Syndicate plots does not duplicate them.

Legacy Save Migration

The Syndicate was formerly distributed as separate mod content and is now built into the game. Current save compatibility code performs a one-time reset for saves older than the built-in mainline reset version:

  • Syndicate plot completion and relationship progress are reset;
  • the seven character entries are repaired and re-registered where necessary;
  • obsolete mod registration data is removed;
  • completed Cat's Eyes and Taming the Rookie rewards are remembered so their permanent +1 Rogue awards cannot be duplicated;
  • the internal harlequin ID is preserved while the display name is repaired to Motley.

This migration affects Syndicate-owned progress rather than unrelated game progress. If an older save appears to have restarted the faction, replay from The Gates of Chaos; it is not a second hidden Syndicate route.

Current migration exception: Mirror Duty is the 65th registered Syndicate plot, but chamella_mirror_duty is not currently included in the migration's explicit 64-plot reset list. Its completion flag can therefore survive an old-save reset even when Chamella's relationship points are reset. If Mirror Duty does not replay on a migrated save, this is the likely cause.

Troubleshooting and Completion Audit

A mainline plot will not trigger

Check the exact location and all prior branches. The most commonly missed requirements are:

  • Collecting Dues: all four required introductions;
  • The Boss Takes Notice: both Taming the Rookie and Welcome to the Circus;
  • Lines on the Board: Cat's Eyes, Street Knowledge, and The Boss Takes Notice;
  • No Plan Survives: The Honeypot and Faces;
  • The Performance: The Abandoned Room and The Bug.

Leave and re-enter the location after completing a prerequisite.

Taming the Rookie will not start

Visit the Prime Transportation Hub from 7 AM through 4:59 PM after completing New Blood.

Open Doors or The Honeypot will not start

The plot checks raw stored Rogue: 50 for Open Doors and 55 for The Honeypot. Equipment modifiers do not satisfy the check.

Talk is missing

Talk requires 50 relationship points with that individual character. It is not unlocked merely by completing their introduction or the faction mainline.

A relationship route stopped below Acquaintance

Finish every remaining ungated mainline plot that rewards the character. If no such plot remains, the current implementation has no normal repeatable catch-up action below 50. This is a current progression limitation, not a missing schedule window.

Chamel or Chamella is missing

Their Funhouse shifts alternate:

  • Chamel: midnight-9 AM and 3 PM-midnight;
  • Chamella: 9 AM-3 PM.

A Den scene will not start

Confirm Known stage, the correct time window, and that the one-time plot was not already completed. Zodiac and Chamel require separate entries during the same evening window.

A repeatable video is missing

Check the exact 1,000 or 1,500 point requirement. Stage names are too broad for these gates.

Is the faction story complete?

If After the Line is completed, the current operational mainline is complete. Personal route completion is separate. For the broadest character completion, also finish:

  • Kitty — Tamed;
  • Poison — The Real Poison;
  • Chamel — The Real Thing;
  • Zodiac — The Confession and the later control-arc scenes;
  • Motley — The Truth;
  • Bayne — The Order;
  • Chamella — Mirror Duty and her mainline appearances;
  • all eight H2Hs and six Den scenes.

No Syndicate content is intentionally timed out forever. Time windows repeat daily, and one-time plots wait until their requirements are met.

快速通关路线

如要以最顺畅的路线完成目前实装的全部辛迪加内容：

  1. 从未来核心区地图进入 辛迪加大门，完成 混沌之门。
  2. 依次前往议事厅、大帐篷、占卜摊位、欢乐屋、大门、花园与 Motley的巢穴，完成所有角色介绍。
  3. 完成 收取保护费，并前往合成人充能站收取款项。
  4. 完成收款后的三个分支：猫眼、新血液 → 驯服菜鸟 及 千面。
  5. 如果尚未完成，完成 街头情报 和 欢迎来到马戏团。
  6. 完成 老大注意到了你，然后进入议事厅触发 板上的线索。
  7. 将基础盗贼属性提升至50以触发 敞开大门，再提升至55以触发 美人计。两段辛迪加训练剧情各提供+1盗贼。
  8. 完成 计划终成泡影，然后同时完成 废弃房间 与 窃听器，再返回欢乐屋触发 表演。
  9. 沿自动触发的后续剧情依次完成 包袱、秘密的重量、徽章与虚张声势、第二次敲门 与 防线之后。
  10. 将每个人的关系依次提升至已知、朋友及亲密朋友。完成所有新出现的常驻地点剧情、巢穴场景与 H2H 谈心。
  11. 在尝试 Bayne 的最终剧情 命令 之前，完成 Kitty、Poison、Chamel、Zodiac 与 Motley 的进阶路线。
  12. 将想培养的每位角色提升至恋爱中，并在现代玩家住宅的卧室领取正常的一次性关系水晶。

主线奖励与当前终点

永久提升玩家属性的两项奖励为：

  • 猫眼： +1盗贼
  • 驯服菜鸟： +1盗贼

其余已登记的主线奖励都是分配给各角色的好感度。完成奖励与选项奖励会经过正常的随机好感度计算，因此两个存档完成同一场景后可能得到不同总数。

防线之后 是目前已实现阵营主线的终点。之后没有阵营胜利画面、可重复的辛迪加任务板、阵营商店、阵营声望奖励或另一段已登记的行动剧情。剧情仍留下若干线索供未来开发；只要完成“防线之后”，即使这些线索看似悬而未决，玩家也已经追上当前版本的阵营故事进度。

相识阶段的瓶颈

每位辛迪加角色都从陌生人开始，而全部七个交谈行动都要求达到相识。目前的角色定义中也没有普通的辛迪加送礼、邀请约会、度假村或远程行动，能在50点以下弥补差距。

实际设计的过渡手段，是主线完成奖励与选项奖励的总和。这些奖励会随机计算，而且各选项往往偏向不同角色，因此某些成员完成前期剧情后仍可能低于50点。

在消耗一次性辛迪加剧情之前，尽可能提高好感度计算结果：

  • 达到角色的偏好属性门槛；
  • 获得角色最喜欢的特殊技能；
  • 使用古龙水及相应好感度物品；
  • 启用与其匹配的阵营倾向；
  • 选择能奖励当前点数落后角色的选项。

交谈解锁后，每次耗时15分钟，以 1点基础好感度 开始计算，且 每名角色每天可安全使用五次。超过每日上限可能产生负好感度，因此五次后就应停止。

当前版本限制： 如果某角色完成其全部无门槛一次性剧情后仍低于50点，便没有标准的可重复追赶行动。如仍有奖励该角色的其他剧情分支，请继续完成；如果没有，说明该路线在当前实现中发生了机制性停滞，并非藏在某个未说明的地点后面。

完整的奖励公式与超限表现请参阅关系、约会与亲密互动。

旧存档迁移

辛迪加过去以独立模组内容发布，如今已内置于游戏。当前的存档兼容代码会对早于内置主线重置版本的存档执行一次性重置：

  • 重置辛迪加剧情完成状态与关系进度；
  • 必要时修复并重新登记七名角色的数据；
  • 移除已废弃的模组登记数据；
  • 记录已经完成的“猫眼”和“驯服菜鸟”奖励，防止其永久+1盗贼被重复取得；
  • 保留内部 harlequin ID，同时将显示名称修复为 Motley。

此迁移只影响辛迪加拥有的进度，不会影响无关游戏内容。如果旧存档看似重新开始了阵营剧情，请从“混沌之门”起重玩；这并非第二条隐藏的辛迪加路线。

当前迁移例外： “镜子值勤”是第65段已登记的辛迪加剧情，但 chamella_mirror_duty 目前没有列入迁移代码明确的64段剧情重置清单。因此，即使 Chamella 的好感度已经重置，其完成标记仍可能在旧存档重置后保留下来。如果迁移后的存档无法重玩“镜子值勤”，这很可能就是原因。
