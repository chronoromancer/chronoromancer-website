Overview

Sashina is an ancient sorceress, keeper of the Ancient Earth Shrine, who has waited centuries for a hero capable of helping her prevent VOID's universal extinction. Her prophetic visions brought her to the player — and her patience, magical mentorship, and eventual romance form one of the most complete character arcs in the game.

She teaches 3 core magical skills plus 2 exclusive skills (Arcane Surge and Mystic Seduction — both unavailable from any other source), has 4 H2H conversations, a nature date, a 40-round Final Exam intimate ritual, and she's also the central figure in the Five Maidens and Seraph storylines.

Major lore role: Sashina is the one who places Robin (Medieval) into magical stasis before the dragon extinction — Robin awakens in the Future Era as Artemis.

H2H Conversations (4 Total)

1. Visions of the Future (h2h_sashina_visions)

  • Unlock: Known stage, 120 min, daily limit 1, at the shrine exterior
  • 4 rounds. Sets sashina_shared_visions flag on completion.

Round 1 — She describes the fractured futures like shattered glass. Standard +14. Special (Empathy): +20, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — Visions center on the player. Choose between "What role do relationships play?" (+10) or "Can you teach me to see these?" (+15).

Round 3 — Love is the weapon VOID can't predict. Special (Mystic 30+): +25 — "Share your mystical sight with me."

Round 4 — Cinematic closer, +15.

2. Fears of the Void (h2h_sashina_fears)

  • Unlock: Acquaintance stage, 140 min, daily limit 1, at the altar chamber
  • 4 rounds. Sets sashina_knows_fears flag. +35 base from action itself.

Round 1 — She trembles despite her power. Standard +14. Special (Inner Peace skill): +20, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — She describes VOID as extinction, complete erasure. +15 across choices. Variant (with Inner Peace): her peace flows from the player.

Round 3 — You give her hope. +15.

Round 4 — "Hold her close" +20 — mystical embrace ending.

3. Walk in Nature (h2h_sashina_nature_walk)

  • Unlock: Friend stage, 90 min, daily limit 1, at the shrine exterior
  • 3 rounds. Philosophical discussion of fate, divine creation, and why she believes her visions point to the player. Sets sashina_shared_philosophy flag.

Round 1 — +8 choices across all three options. Round 2 — Touches a flowering tree. Divine creator philosophy. +9 choices. Round 3 — Single +12 closer: "I'm honored to walk this path with you."

4. Mystic Seduction (h2h_sashina_mystic_seduction) — THE FIRST KISS

  • Unlock: 300+ relationship points + Mystic 65, 180 min, daily limit 1, at the shrine exterior
  • 5 rounds — teaches the exclusive skill AND ends in their first kiss
  • Sets sashina_first_kiss flag. If rel points ≥ 400 on completion, forces promotion to Crush stage.

Round 1 — She hesitates about teaching morally complex magic. Standard +12. Special (Reading People): +18, unlocks variant.

Round 2 — The magic can be abused. +15 across choices. Variant: her tears of gratitude that someone sees the burden.

Round 3 — Her trust in the player breaks the hesitation. Special (Charm 40+): +25 — "I'll use this power to protect what we both hold dear."

Round 4 — Player learns the skill faster than anyone. She's affected by the magic (or her own heart). Single +20 — "Move closer to her."

Round 5 — THE FIRST KISS. +25. "Her mystical defenses completely down... soft, magical lips meeting yours."

Narrative importance: This H2H is the emotional turning point of Sashina's arc. It's also the one that unlocks the Mystic Seduction exclusive skill, which gates the Final Exam.

Three Decision Points (Three Branches Each)

Point 1 — Forest path, "Do you feel differently about your path?"

  • Embrace ("exactly where I'm supposed to be") → she reads acceptance in your aura
  • Committed ("have doubts but committed") → she normalizes doubt
  • Uncertain ("some days I still wonder if I'm the right person") → "The tree does not choose to be a tree"

Point 2 — Forest clearing, "How do you view the establishment you're building?"

  • Foundation ("wealth, allies, influence for what comes next") → she validates the strategic build
  • Practical ("if growing it helps defeat VOID") → "The universe cares nothing for half-measures"
  • Conflicted ("feels strange, the things I'm doing to build power") → she dismisses conventional morality in face of extinction

Point 3 — Hilltop vista, "Promise me you won't slow down"

  • Promise ("build my strength in every way possible") → image 14, "threads of fate shine brighter"
  • Personal ("won't let you down, Sashina") → image 13, her rare vulnerable admission: "It has been a long time since anyone made me feel like I mattered beyond my prophecies"
  • Honest ("I'll do my best") → image 8, mild acceptance

Best romantic route: Embrace → Foundation → Personal. The Personal ending on Point 3 is the most emotionally significant — she admits to being more than her prophecies.

Arcane Surge Training Scene

The plot sashina_arcane_surge triggers when the player clicks the Arcane Surge action with Mystic 60+ at Romantic stage. Brief plot:

Sashina reveals she stopped teaching Arcane Surge centuries ago — the energy cost draws from life force, not just mana. Most who attempted it collapsed. She trusts the player enough to share it.

She places her hands over the player's and floods raw magical force through the connection. The player's vision whites out, the altar stones crack, their magic rips free of elemental channels. They hold it under control — not tamed, just "leashed."

Closing line — rare praise from her: "You held it. Remarkable." (There is pride in her voice, not surprise.)

Teaches the skill. Uses mystical dialogue box style throughout.

Sashina's Final Exam — 26-Round Intimate Ritual

The apartment-equivalent visit. Requires Romantic stage, Mystic 90+, Master Wizard AND Mystic Seduction skills, 160 min, −120 energy, −50 calm, daily limit 1. Awards +160 rel pts and wizard clout on completion.

Structure — Hub and Spoke

  • Rounds 1-5: Introduction, growth reflection, first natural kiss (preparation for magic)
  • Round 6 — MAIN HUB: Six activity choices, all return to Round 6 after their sequence:
    • Focus Mouth (Rounds 7-10)
    • Focus Chest (Rounds 11-14)
    • Take Missionary (Rounds 15-17)
    • Take From Behind (Rounds 18-20)
    • Let Her Ride (Rounds 21-23 — Cowgirl)
    • "We're finished" (Rounds 24-26 — Completion sequence)

Every activity path returns to Round 6 so the player can experience all paths in one session before ending.

Ending Sequence (Rounds 24-26)

Three-round cinematic completion: finish sequence 1 → finish sequence 2 → cinematic ending (+160 pts, terminate conversation).

Image Set

Progression Path — Recommended

  1. Stranger → Acknowledged → Known: Mystical Training 6×/day at altar chamber (free +6 Mystic/day + rel pts). Talk 3×/day at any location.
  2. Acquaintance: Learn Healing Touch (Mystic 15, at shrine) and Illusion Weaving (Mystic 20, at altar). Unlock Fears of the Void H2H (altar).
  3. Known: Visions of the Future H2H + Learn Elemental Blast (Mystic 20, altar) + Emotional Magic one-time (+1 Mystic, at home, Communication 30).
  4. Friend: Walk in Nature H2H + Learn Telekinesis (Mystic 25, altar).
  5. ~300+ rel pts + Mystic 65: Mystic Seduction H2H — first kiss, exclusive skill, potentially force-promotes to Crush stage if 400+ pts.
  6. Nature Walk Date (any time after Acquaintance, 8 AM – 2 PM window).
  7. Romantic stage + Mystic 60: Arcane Surge ritual — exclusive skill.
  8. 1200+ rel pts: Mystical Intimacy video action at home.
  9. Romantic + Mystic 90 + Master Wizard + Mystic Seduction: Sashina's Final Exam — 26-round climax, +160 rel.

Writing Voice Notes

  • Archaic, mystical register. Uses mystical dialogue box style for major reveals. "Dear hero," formal address, rare contractions.
  • Italicized mystical-state actions. "eyes glowing with mystical energy", "magical aura intensifying", "tears of gratitude in her mystical eyes" — her physical state is constantly described in magical terms.
  • Fragments of vision vocabulary. "Shards of shattered glass," "threads of fate," "ripples across time," "the weave of possibility" — her speech never abandons the prophetic frame.
  • Immense age visible in tone. She references centuries of life, mages she's watched get corrupted, techniques she stopped teaching. Always from a long view.
  • Vulnerability through mystical breaks. Her moments of emotional openness are framed as breaks in her composure — "her mystical defenses completely down," "despite her powerful aura" — not as ordinary feelings.
  • Pride expressed as understatement. "Remarkable." Single word, rare in her speech, and it carries weight. When she praises, it lands.
  • Moral complexity handled directly. She doesn't pretend Mystic Seduction is clean. She doesn't pretend building power in the brothel is easy. She explicitly reasons through the utilitarian calculus — extinction vs discomfort — and expects the player to do the same.

When writing Sashina: mystical state descriptions in italics, archaic diction without being stiff, moral complexity addressed head-on, and vulnerability arrives sideways through cracks in her composure.

Tips & Notes

  • Mystical Training is the single best Mystic-stat farm in the game — 6×/day at the altar chamber, +1 Mystic each. Plan your energy budget.
  • She teaches four standard skills (Healing Touch, Elemental Blast, Illusion Weaving, Telekinesis) — more than almost any other character. Mystic-build players should live at her shrine.
  • Her Special Skill is Master Wizard — a Mystic 60 skill. Learn it ASAP (also required for Final Exam entry).
  • Mystic Seduction H2H has dual purpose — teaches the exclusive skill AND is a major H2H. Don't trigger it casually; save it for when you have 300+ rel pts and want to push toward Crush.
  • The Mystic Seduction skill is required for Final Exam — so the H2H is effectively mandatory for her full arc.
  • Final Exam gate is steep — Mystic 90 + Romantic + Master Wizard + Mystic Seduction + 1200 rel pts for prior Mystical Intimacy. This is late-late game content.
  • She has 3 time-gated locations — plan visits around her schedule: shrine (6 AM – 3 PM), altar (3 PM – 9 PM), home (9 PM – 6 AM). All three have Talk action; only altar has Mystical Training; only home has Emotional Magic, Mystical Intimacy, Final Exam.
  • Jewelry is her least favorite gift — don't waste expensive jewelry on her.
  • Book is her favorite gift.
  • Mystic Seduction H2H can force-promote to Crush — if you complete it with 400+ rel pts, the stage auto-jumps. Plan accordingly if you want to see intermediate dialogue stages.
  • Nature Walk Date is the best casual romance moment — her "I mattered beyond my prophecies" line at the Personal ending is her most emotionally bare dialogue.

概述

Sashina 是远古女巫和远古大地神殿的守护者。她等待了数百年，只为找到一位能协助她阻止 VOID 毁灭宇宙的英雄。预言幻象将她带到玩家身边；她的耐心、魔法指导和最终萌生的爱意，共同构成游戏中最完整的角色故事线之一。

她教授 3 项核心魔法技能和 2 项专属技能（奥术涌动与神秘诱惑，其他任何来源都无法取得），拥有 4 段 H2H 谈话、一场自然约会、40 回合的最终考试亲密仪式，同时也是五位少女与 Seraph 故事线的核心人物。

**重要世界观身份：**巨龙灭绝事件前，Sashina 将中世纪的 Robin 置于魔法静滞状态；Robin 后来在未来时代以 Artemis 的身份醒来。

专属技能（仅能从 Sashina 处取得）

奥术涌动（神秘 60+、浪漫感阶段）

  • **技能效果：**18% 概率释放毁灭性的无属性涌动——魔法伤害变为 3 倍，并无视所有防御
  • 在祭坛室通过 350 分钟仪式学习
  • 触发 sashina_arcane_surge 剧情场景
  • 根据设定，它消耗的是生命力，而非单纯的法力

神秘诱惑（300+ 好感度、神秘 65+）

  • **技能效果：**将魔力与诱惑技巧融合
  • 通过神秘诱惑 H2H 教授；谈话本身就是教学，并以两人的初吻结束
  • 处理函数会在 H2H 运行之前直接加入技能
  • 这是一种道德风险复杂的魔法；Sashina 在数百年前就已停止教授

H2H 谈话（共 4 段）

1. 未来幻象（h2h_sashina_visions）

  • **解锁：**已知阶段，120 分钟，每日上限 1，位于神殿外部
  • **4 回合。**完成后设置 sashina_shared_visions 标记。

第 1 回合——她将破碎的未来形容为碎裂玻璃。普通 +14；特殊（共情）+20，并解锁变体。

第 2 回合——幻象围绕玩家展开。选择“关系在其中扮演什么角色？”（+10）或“你能教我看见这些吗？”（+15）。

第 3 回合——爱是 VOID 无法预测的武器。特殊（神秘 30+）+25——“与我分享你的神秘视界。”

第 4 回合——演出式收尾，+15。

2. 对 VOID 的恐惧（h2h_sashina_fears）

  • **解锁：**相识阶段，140 分钟，每日上限 1，位于祭坛室
  • **4 回合。**设置 sashina_knows_fears 标记；行动本身提供基础 +35。

第 1 回合——尽管力量强大，她仍在颤抖。普通 +14；特殊（内心平静技能）+20，并解锁变体。

第 2 回合——她将 VOID 描述为灭绝与彻底抹除。所有选项 +15；内心平静变体中，她的安宁源自玩家。

第 3 回合——你给予她希望，+15。

第 4 回合——“紧紧抱住她”+20，以神秘拥抱结尾。

3. 漫步自然（h2h_sashina_nature_walk）

  • **解锁：**朋友阶段，90 分钟，每日上限 1，位于神殿外部
  • **3 回合。**讨论命运、神圣创造，以及她为何相信幻象指向玩家。设置 sashina_shared_philosophy 标记。

第 1 回合——3 个选项均 +8。 第 2 回合——她触碰开花的树，谈论神圣造物；所有选项 +9。 第 3 回合——唯一的 +12 收尾：“能与你并肩走上这条道路，我深感荣幸。”

4. 神秘诱惑（h2h_sashina_mystic_seduction）——初吻

  • **解锁：**300+ 好感度＋神秘 65，180 分钟，每日上限 1，位于神殿外部
  • 5 回合——教授专属技能，并以初吻结束
  • 设置 sashina_first_kiss 标记。若完成时好感度 ≥ 400，会强制提升至迷恋阶段。

第 1 回合——她对教授这种道德复杂的魔法犹豫不决。普通 +12；特殊（识人术）+18，并解锁变体。

第 2 回合——这种魔法可能遭到滥用。所有选项 +15；变体中，她因有人看见这份负担而感激落泪。

第 3 回合——对玩家的信任打破她的迟疑。特殊（魅力 40+）+25——“我会用这股力量保护我们共同珍视的一切。”

第 4 回合——玩家学习速度超过以往任何人。她受到魔法影响（也可能是自己的真心）。唯一选项 +20——“靠近她。”

第 5 回合——初吻。+25。“她的神秘防线完全卸下……柔软而带有魔力的双唇与你相触。”

**叙事意义：**此 H2H 是 Sashina 故事线的情感转折点，也会解锁最终考试所需的专属技能神秘诱惑。

自然散步约会（nature_sashina 剧情）

  • **要求：**相识阶段+、120 分钟、−40 精力、早上 8 点至下午 2 点
  • 一次性（设置 nature_sashina_completed 后隐藏）
  • 在神殿外部显示

3 个决定点（每处 3 条分支）

决定 1——森林小径，“你对自己的道路有不同感受吗？”

  • 接纳（“我正身处命定之地”）→她从你的气场中读出坦然
  • 坚定（“虽有疑虑，但仍会坚持”）→她说明怀疑很正常
  • 不确定（“有些日子我仍会怀疑自己是否合适”）→“树不会选择成为树”

决定 2——森林空地，“你如何看待自己建立的机构？”

  • 根基（“为未来积累财富、盟友和影响力”）→她认可这项战略建设
  • 务实（“若发展它有助于击败 VOID”）→“宇宙不在乎半途而废的措施”
  • 矛盾（“为积蓄力量而做这些事，感觉很奇怪”）→面对灭绝，她不认同常规道德束缚

决定 3——山顶远景，“答应我，不要放慢脚步”

  • 承诺（“以一切可能的方式增强力量”）→图像 14，“命运之线愈发明亮”
  • 个人（“我不会让你失望，Sashina”）→图像 13，她罕见地坦露脆弱：“已经很久没有人让我觉得，除了预言之外，我本身也有意义。”
  • 坦诚（“我会尽力”）→图像 8，温和接受

**最佳恋爱路线：**接纳→根基→个人。决定 3 的个人结局情感最为深刻，她承认自己不只是预言的载体。

奥术涌动训练场景

玩家在浪漫感阶段、神秘 60+ 时点击奥术涌动行动，会触发 sashina_arcane_surge 剧情：

Sashina 透露，数百年前她就停止教授奥术涌动，因为能量消耗来自生命力而非单纯法力，大多数尝试者都会倒下。如今她足够信任玩家，愿意传授。

她把双手覆在玩家手上，让原始魔力灌入连接。玩家眼前一片白光，祭坛石块开裂，魔力挣脱元素通道。他们成功控制住它——并非驯服，只是“拴上锁链”。

收尾是她罕见的赞许：“你控制住了。非凡。”（声音里是骄傲，而非惊讶。）

教授技能。全程使用 mystical 对话框样式。

Sashina的最终考试——26 回合亲密仪式

相当于公寓造访的事件。需要浪漫感阶段、神秘 90+、魔法大师和神秘诱惑技能，160 分钟，−120 精力、−50 镇定，每日上限 1。完成后奖励好感度 +160 及巫师声望。

结构——中心辐射式

  • **第 1–5 回合：**介绍、回顾成长、第一次自然亲吻（为魔法作准备）
  • **第 6 回合——主中心：**6 个活动选项，各自完成后都会回到第 6 回合：
    • 专注口部（第 7–10 回合）
    • 专注胸部（第 11–14 回合）
    • 传教士体位（第 15–17 回合）
    • 从后方（第 18–20 回合）
    • 让她骑乘（第 21–23 回合——女上位）
    • “结束了”（第 24–26 回合——完成段落）

每条活动路线都会返回第 6 回合，因此玩家可在一次事件中体验所有路线后再结束。

结尾段落（第 24–26 回合）

3 回合演出式收尾：完成段落 1→完成段落 2→演出结局（+160 点，结束谈话）。

  1. **陌生人→已遇见→已知：**每天在祭坛室进行 6 次神秘训练（每天免费获得神秘 +6 及好感度），并在任意地点每天交谈 3 次。

  3. 已知： 未来幻象 H2H＋学习元素爆破（神秘 20，祭坛）＋一次性情感魔法（神秘 +1，住所，沟通 30）。
  4. 朋友： 漫步自然 H2H＋学习念动力（神秘 25，祭坛）。
  5. 约 300+ 好感度＋神秘 65： 神秘诱惑 H2H——初吻、专属技能；若好感度达到 400+，可能强制提升至迷恋。
  6. 自然散步约会（相识后任意时间，时段为早上 8 点至下午 2 点）。
  7. 浪漫感阶段＋神秘 60： 奥术涌动仪式——专属技能。
  8. 1200+ 好感度：住所的神秘亲密视频行动。
  9. 浪漫感＋神秘 90＋魔法大师＋神秘诱惑： Sashina的最终考试——26 回合高潮，好感度 +160。

  • 进入最终考试的必需条件

写作语气备注

  • **古雅而神秘的语体。**重大揭晓使用 mystical 对话框样式；称呼“亲爱的英雄”，措辞正式，极少使用缩略表达。
  • 神秘状态动作使用斜体。“双眼闪烁神秘能量”、“魔法气场因决意而增强”、“神秘双眸中泛起感激泪光”——她的身体状态总以魔法词汇描写。
  • 破碎幻象词汇。“碎裂玻璃的残片”“命运之线”“跨越时间的涟漪”“可能性的织网”——她的言谈始终保留预言框架。
  • **语气体现漫长寿命。**她会提及数百年人生、亲眼看见堕落的法师，以及早已停止教授的技法，总是采取长远视角。
  • **脆弱通过神秘防线破裂显现。**她坦露感情时会被描述为“神秘防线完全卸下”“尽管气场强大”，而不是普通情绪。
  • 赞许总是轻描淡写。“非凡。”仅一个词，在她口中十分罕见，因而分量十足。
  • **直面道德复杂性。**她不假装神秘诱惑纯洁无害，也不否认借青楼积蓄力量的艰难。她会明确衡量灭绝与不适之间的功利得失，也要求玩家如此思考。

撰写 Sashina 时：神秘状态描写使用斜体；措辞古雅但不僵硬；直面道德复杂性；脆弱总从镇定外表的裂缝中侧面流露。

提示与备注

  • 神秘训练是游戏中最有效的神秘属性刷取方式——祭坛室每天 6 次，每次 +1。请规划精力预算。
  • 她教授 4 项标准技能（治愈之触、元素爆破、幻象编织、念动力），比几乎所有角色都多。神秘流派玩家应常驻她的神殿。
  • 她的特殊技能是魔法大师——神秘 60 技能。尽快学习，也是最终考试入场条件。
  • 神秘诱惑 H2H 有双重用途——教授专属技能，也是重要 H2H。不要随意触发；可留到 300+ 好感度、准备向迷恋推进时。
  • 最终考试需要神秘诱惑技能，因此要体验完整故事线就必须完成该 H2H。
  • 最终考试门槛很高——神秘 90＋浪漫感＋魔法大师＋神秘诱惑；此前的神秘亲密还要求 1200 好感度。这是极后期内容。
  • 她有 3 个分时地点——神殿（早上 6 点至下午 3 点）、祭坛（下午 3 点至晚上 9 点）、住所（晚上 9 点至次日早上 6 点）。三处都有交谈；仅祭坛有神秘训练；仅住所有情感魔法、神秘亲密和最终考试。
  • 珠宝是她最不喜欢的礼物，不要浪费昂贵珠宝。
  • 书是她最喜欢的礼物。
  • 神秘诱惑 H2H 可强制提升至迷恋——若完成时好感度达到 400+，阶段会自动跳升。若想体验中间阶段对白，请提前规划。
  • 自然散步约会是最轻松的恋爱时刻——个人结局中“除了预言之外，我本身也有意义”是她最直白脆弱的对白。
