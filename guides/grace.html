Overview

Grace’s early route uses the monastery and meditation grove. Recruitment changes her ordinary schedule, so a missing monastery portrait later can be expected progress rather than a lost character.

Where to find Grace

Start at Medieval → Village Outskirts → Monastery Scriptoria → Monastery Scriptoria(Lobby). Before recruitment she splits time between the lobby, manuscript room, underground catacombs and grove. The lobby has a 10:00–11:00 break.

After recruitment, the monastery appearances stop while the grove visits continue. Business-room appearances additionally depend on the conditions below; vacations and worker availability can also take her away.

Route and relationship milestones

At Known(25), visit the lobby 07:00–14:00 for A Gentle Reminder; at Acquaintance(50), visit the manuscript room 14:00–18:00 on even days for Lost in Translation. At Friend(100) with business reputation 5, return to the lobby 07:00–14:00 for A Mystic's Curiosity.

Continue at the grove 13:00–18:00 for A Different Kind of Light, then at Crush(400) visit the catacombs 18:00–22:00 for Whispers Among the Dead. Where My Light Belongs needs 500 points and the completed meeting, at the grove 20:00–24:00 on even days. Starting that heart-to-heart recruits Grace; there is no extra later hiring fee or recruitment-choice gate.

Her earlier heart-to-hearts and Nature Walk are separate activities. The Nature Walk starts during the lobby schedule:08:00–10:00 or 11:00–14:00. Recruitment does not erase already completed personal conversations.

Activities and rewards

Spiritual Awareness gives Perception+1 once at Known(25) with Communication 40 and 30 minutes. Talk actions share the same 5-per-day allowance across locations; Flowers use ordinary Flowers and have a separate 2-per-day allowance. Talk has+1 base relationship and Flowers+5 before the shared modifiers.

Once recruited, use normal worker training, equipment and resort activities. Grace is also in Kupie’s Love Training roster: main-game Friend(100) is one requirement, alongside that minigame’s own character unlock. The resort sunset walk is separate from her monastery Nature Walk.

Missing actions and useful checks

If Grace disappears from the monastery after recruitment, use the grove or the appropriate business-room schedule. Kitchen visits require business reputation 4; garden visits require Audrey active; bar visits require Brandy active.

For recruitment, check both the 500-point heart-to-heart and the preceding catacomb meeting. High affection alone does not complete the monastery/Sashina sequence. Changing business rooms does not refresh Talk’s daily allowance.

概览

Grace的前期路线在修道院与冥想林推进，招募后普通时间表会变化；之后修道院头像消失，可能只是正常进展，不代表人物丢失。

出现地点与时间

从中世纪 → 村庄郊外 → 修道院抄写室 → 修道院抄写室（大厅）开始。招募前，她会分时段在大厅、手稿室、地下墓穴与冥想林活动，大厅10:00—11:00有一段空档。

招募后不再在修道院出现，但冥想林行程保留。青楼房间还会检查下表条件，休假或员工状态也可能使她暂时离开。

路线与关系进展

已知（25）时于07:00—14:00去大厅推进「一句温和的提醒」；相识（50）时于双数日14:00—18:00去手稿室推进「失落在译文之间」。朋友（100）且青楼声誉5时，再于07:00—14:00去大厅推进「秘法师的好奇」。

之后13:00—18:00去冥想林推进「另一种光」；迷恋（400）时于18:00—22:00去地下墓穴完成「亡者之间的低语」。完成后，关系点数500、双数日20:00—24:00可在冥想林开始「我的光之归处」。开始这次谈心就会完成招募，不另收后续雇用费，也没有额外的招募选项门槛。

较早的谈心与自然散步是另外的活动。自然散步从大厅提供，所以实际开始时段为08:00—10:00或11:00—14:00。招募不会抹去已完成的个人谈心。

行动与奖励

已知（25）且沟通40时，可花30分钟进行一次灵性觉知，永久增加感知1。各地点交谈共用每天5次额度，送花消耗普通鲜花，另有每天2次额度。未计共用修正时，交谈基础关系+1，送花+5。

招募后可使用普通员工培训、装备与度假村活动。Grace也在Kupie 的爱之训练名单中，主游戏朋友（100）是其中一项要求，还要满足小游戏自己的角色解锁。度假村日落散步与修道院自然散步不是同一项活动。

动作缺失时的检查

招募后修道院没有Grace时，去冥想林或对应青楼房间。厨房需要青楼声誉4，花园需要Audrey在职，酒吧需要Brandy在职。

招募要同时检查500点谈心与前面的地下墓穴会面，高关系不能自动完成修道院及Sashina相关步骤。换青楼房间也不会刷新交谈额度。
