Overview

Grace is the curious 23-year-old novice nun of the Kings Demesne monastery — left as an orphan on the doorstep at birth, raised entirely within the cloister walls, and now quietly questioning whether her "inner light" was meant to stay locked inside. Her arc is the most narratively integrated of any romance character in the medieval era: a multi-plot path from doubt-filled novice to liberated brothel worker, with three heart-to-heart conversations that form a continuous spiritual journey.

When the liberation H2H completes, Grace leaves the monastery and joins your brothel as a worker. After that, her monastery actions disable and she takes on her brothel worker role — assigned to her own room, working 2 PM – 10 PM, with the Missionary special skill.

Where to Find Her (Pre-Liberation)

Grace has the most scattered schedule of any monastery NPC, with four different locations across her day:

1. Scriptorium Lobby (Primary)

  • Hours: 7 AM – 10 AM and 11 AM – 2 PM, every day
  • Note: 10 AM – 11 AM gap to avoid conflict with the monk's prayers
  • Most of her main actions live here

2. Illuminated Manuscript Room

  • Hours: 2 PM – 6 PM, even days only
  • Quiet workplace where she copies religious texts

3. Underground Catacombs

  • Hours: 6 PM – 10 PM, every day
  • Where Grace explores the ancient catacombs (and where her secret meeting plot triggers)

4. Mystic's Meditation Grove

  • Hours: 2 PM – 6 PM on odd days, 8 PM – 12 AM on even days
  • The most narratively important location — where her H2Hs happen
  • Where Grace becomes liberated and joins your brothel

Once she becomes a brothel worker, she disappears from all monastery locations automatically.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Charm 35, Rogue 40, Perception 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Seduction skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Flowers (base 5), all 3 favorite stats met, Seduction learned: (5 + 3) × 2 = 16 points — plus possible +1s from Ring/Cologne/Buffer/Attractiveness.

Min-max tip: Rogue 40 is the unusual stat here — this is the only major NPC favorite-stat that requires Rogue. Worth pushing if you want maximum point efficiency on Grace.

The Plot Chain — Her Liberation Story

Grace's narrative is one of the most layered in the medieval era. Six auto-triggered plots and three H2Hs build to her liberation.

Step 1 — "A Gentle Reminder"

  • Auto-triggers: Scriptorium Lobby, 7 AM – 2 PM, Known stage+
  • The monk (her superior) gently reminds her about her duties
  • Rewards: +4 Monk, +7 Grace, plus choice bonus

Step 2 — "Lost in Translation"

  • Auto-triggers: Manuscript Room, 2-6 PM, even days, Acquaintance+
  • Grace's manuscript work surfaces questions about the outside world
  • Rewards: +4 Monk, +9 Grace, plus choice bonus

Step 3 — "A Mystic's Curiosity"

  • Auto-triggers: Scriptorium Lobby, 7 AM – 2 PM, Friend stage+, Brothel reputation 5+
  • Sashina (the mystic) takes notice of Grace's restlessness
  • Rewards: +14 Grace, +5 Sashina

Step 4 — "A Different Kind of Light"

  • Auto-triggers: Meditation Grove, 1-6 PM, after Step 3
  • Sashina meets Grace in the grove and shares a different perspective on her "inner light" — one where connection with people doesn't dim it but feeds it
  • Rewards: +19 Grace, +10 Sashina (large reward — meaningful turning point)
  • Gates the Liberation H2H

Step 5 — "Whispers Among the Dead"

  • Auto-triggers: Underground Catacombs, 6-10 PM, Crush stage, after Step 4
  • Grace asks you to meet her in secret — she's been thinking deeply
  • Rewards: +58 Grace (the largest single auto-trigger reward in her arc)
  • Gates the Liberation H2H

Step 6 — "Where My Light Belongs" (Liberation H2H — manual)

  • Trigger: Meditation Grove action, 8 PM – 12 AM, requires 500+ rel + Whispers Among the Dead completed
  • An 8-round cinematic H2H — see "Heart-to-Heart Conversations" below
  • Activates Grace as a brothel worker before the H2H even begins
  • After completion, her monastery schedule disables and she takes on her brothel role

Heart-to-Heart Conversations

Grace has three H2Hs that form a connected emotional arc.

H2H 1 — "Life Beyond the Walls"

  • Requires: Acknowledged stage
  • 4 rounds: she asks why you keep visiting → opens up about Keeper teachings of the "inner light" → asks you to describe something real about the outside world → admits her light flickers differently than the others
  • Special choices: Reading People (round 1), Empathy (round 2 + 4), Storytelling (round 3)
  • Theme: First curiosity. The seeds of doubt about isolation.

H2H 2 — "A Flickering Light"

  • Requires: Friend stage
  • 5 rounds: found crying alone in the grove → the elders gave her until the new moon to take final vows → she describes her flickering inner light → fears both choices → genuine gratitude for being heard
  • Special choices: Empathy (rounds 1, 3, 4), Reading People (round 2)
  • Theme: The crisis. She has a deadline. The pressure is real.

H2H 3 — "Where My Light Belongs" (Liberation)

  • Requires: 500+ relationship points + Whispers Among the Dead completed
  • Time: 8 PM – 12 AM at Meditation Grove
  • 8 rounds: stargazing meeting → her decision → she asks to join your brothel → first kiss → her request to learn passion → the beginning → the climax → aftermath where she vows to come to you
  • Mechanically: Activates her as a brothel worker (the H2H starts after recruitment runs)
  • Theme: Liberation. She joins your establishment willingly. The Keeper teachings reframed: connection nurtures the inner light rather than dimming it.

Why this is unique: Most NPCs who become brothel workers do so via a recruitment plot. Grace's recruitment plays out as an 8-round intimate H2H where she gives you her first time. It's both a romance climax and her liberation from the monastery — narratively the same moment.

Recommended Progression Path

Phase 1: Stranger → Acknowledged

  • Visit her at the Scriptorium Lobby (7-10 AM or 11 AM – 2 PM)
  • Daily Talk (5/day, dialogue only — pushes stage)
  • Stockpile Flowers for the Known gate

Phase 2: Acknowledged → Known

  • Run H2H 1 ("Life Beyond the Walls") — the conversational opener
  • Switch to Flowers daily once Known unlocks
  • Push Communication to 40 for the Spiritual Awareness one-time

Phase 3: Known → Acquaintance

  • Spiritual Awareness at Comm 40+: free +1 Perception permanent, +8 rel
  • "A Gentle Reminder" plot auto-triggers when at Known
  • Continue daily Flowers + Talk in all four locations
  • Run Nature Walk Date (one-time, 8 AM – 2 PM)

Phase 4: Acquaintance → Friend

  • "Lost in Translation" plot auto-triggers in the Manuscript Room (even days, 2-6 PM)
  • Push Brothel reputation to 5+ to unlock the Sashina path

Phase 5: Friend → Close Friend → Crush

  • Run H2H 2 ("A Flickering Light") at the Grove
  • "A Mystic's Curiosity" plot auto-triggers (needs Brothel rep 5)
  • "A Different Kind of Light" at the Grove with Sashina (huge +19 Grace + +10 Sashina)
  • Continue daily gifts; push to Crush

Phase 6: Crush → 500+ Points

  • "Whispers Among the Dead" auto-triggers in the Catacombs (6-10 PM, Crush+) — +58 Grace
  • After this, you have 500+ rel and the secret meeting flag — Liberation is available

Phase 7: The Liberation

  • Visit the Meditation Grove 8 PM – 12 AM (even days have grove access at this time)
  • Run "Where My Light Belongs" — 8-round liberation H2H
  • Grace becomes a brothel worker mid-conversation
  • Her monastery schedule disables; she's now in your brothel

Phase 8: Brothel Worker Phase

  • Grace is assigned to her own brothel room
  • Working hours: 2 PM – 10 PM
  • Starting skill: Meditation (already known)
  • Special skill: Missionary
  • Her brothel actions are governed by the standard worker management system

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Her daily Talk gives 0 base points — Talk pushes the stage progression but isn't a grind action. Use Flowers (Known+) for actual point gain.
  • Her 4 locations are not simultaneous — she moves through them on a strict schedule. Plan your visits accordingly to maximize daily Talk + Flowers across them.
  • Spiritual Awareness gives free +1 Perception permanent — needs Comm 40 and Known stage. Don't miss it; Perception 35 is also her favorite stat.
  • Brothel reputation 5 is a soft gate — without it, the Sashina arc never starts. Push brothel reputation in parallel.
  • Rogue 40 is her unusual favorite stat — most NPCs use Charm/Strength/Stamina. Worth investing in Rogue for her bonuses.
  • The Liberation H2H is a one-way door — once you run it, Grace leaves the monastery permanently. Don't trigger it until you're ready to convert her to brothel worker mode.
  • Plot rewards stack massively — her 6-plot auto-trigger chain awards +7+9+14+19+58 = +107 relationship points just from plots, before any gifts or H2Hs.
  • Seduction skill doubles all her points — uniquely fitting for a former nun discovering passion. Keep it learned.
  • The +500 point Liberation gate is achievable with chained plot rewards + daily gifts + H2H 1/2 completions.

概述

Grace是国王领地修道院里一名充满好奇心的23岁见习修女——她出生时便以孤儿身份被遗弃在门阶上，完全在修道院高墙内长大，如今开始悄悄怀疑自己的“内在之光”是否注定要被锁在墙内。她的故事线是中世纪恋爱角色中叙事整合度最高的一条：通过多段剧情，从满怀疑虑的见习修女转变为获得自由的青楼员工，并有三段谈心对话构成一场连贯的精神旅程。

完成解放谈心后，Grace会离开修道院，以员工身份加入你的青楼。之后，她在修道院的行动会停用，并开始担任青楼员工——拥有自己的房间，工作时间为下午2点至晚上10点，特殊技能为传教士位。

加成计算（准确顺序）

好感度公式按以下顺序计算：

  1. 从行动的基础点数开始
  2. 每满足一项喜爱属性便+1（魅力35、盗贼40、感知35——最高+3）
  3. 玩家拥有诱惑技能时**×2**（她的特殊／喜爱技能）
  4. 玩家拥有**“1枚戒指”关键物品时+1**
  5. 古龙水增益生效时**+1**
  6. 缓冲区优化等级生效时**+1**
  7. 根据吸引力进行**+1**概率判定（最高50%；拥有华丽挂坠时为60%）

**计算示例：**花束（基础5），满足全部3项喜爱属性，并已学会诱惑： (5 + 3) × 2 = 16 points——之后还可能从戒指／古龙水／缓冲区／吸引力取得若干+1。

极限优化提示：盗贼40在这里很特殊——这是唯一要求盗贼属性的主要NPC喜爱属性。如果想让Grace的点数效率最大化，就值得提升。

剧情链——她的解放故事

Grace的故事是中世纪最具层次的剧情之一。六段自动触发剧情和三段谈心会逐步引向她的解放。

第1步——“一句温和的提醒”

  • **自动触发：**抄经室大厅、上午7点至下午2点、已知阶段以上
  • 僧侣（她的上级）温和地提醒她履行职责
  • **奖励：**Monk+4、Grace+7，另有选项加成

第2步——“失落在译文之间”

  • **自动触发：**彩饰手稿室、偶数日下午2点至6点、相识以上
  • Grace在抄写手稿时，显露出对外界的疑问
  • **奖励：**Monk+4、Grace+9，另有选项加成

第3步——“秘法师的好奇”

  • **自动触发：**抄经室大厅、上午7点至下午2点、朋友阶段以上、青楼声望5以上
  • 神秘者Sashina注意到了Grace的不安
  • **奖励：**Grace+14、Sashina+5

第4步——“另一种光”

  • **自动触发：**冥想林、下午1点至6点、完成第3步后
  • Sashina在树林中与Grace见面，为她的“内在之光”提供另一种解释——与人建立联系不会使其黯淡，反而会滋养它
  • **奖励：**Grace+19、Sashina+10（高额奖励——意义重大的转折点）
  • 解锁解放谈心

第5步——“亡者之间的低语”

  • **自动触发：**地下墓穴、晚上6点至10点、迷恋阶段、完成第4步后
  • Grace请你秘密与她见面——她已经深思许久
  • **奖励：**Grace+58（她的故事线中数值最高的单次自动触发奖励）
  • 解锁解放谈心

第6步——“我的光之归处”（解放谈心——手动）

  • **触发方式：**冥想林行动、晚上8点至午夜12点；要求好感度500以上＋完成“亡者之间的低语”
  • 一段8轮电影式谈心——详见下文“谈心对话”
  • 甚至会在谈心开始前便让Grace成为青楼员工
  • 完成后，她的修道院日程会停用，并开始担任青楼员工

谈心对话

Grace拥有三段相互连贯的谈心，构成完整的情感历程。

谈心1——“墙外生活”

  • **要求：**已遇见阶段
  • **4轮：**她询问你为何不断来访→谈起守护者有关“内在之光”的教诲→请你讲述外界真实存在的某样事物→承认自己的光与其他人的闪烁方式不同
  • **特殊选项：**识人（第1轮）、共情（第2＋4轮）、讲故事（第3轮）
  • **主题：**最初的好奇。对与世隔绝产生怀疑的种子。

谈心2——“摇曳之光”

  • **要求：**朋友阶段
  • **5轮：**发现她独自在树林中哭泣→长老要求她在新月前立下最终誓言→她描述自己摇曳的内在之光→两种选择都令她恐惧→她由衷感谢有人愿意倾听
  • **特殊选项：**共情（第1、3、4轮）、识人（第2轮）
  • **主题：**危机。她面临期限，压力真实存在。

谈心3——“我的光之归处”（解放）

  • **要求：**好感度500以上＋完成“亡者之间的低语”
  • **时间：**晚上8点至午夜12点，神秘者冥想林
  • **8轮：**星空下的会面→她的决定→请求加入你的青楼→初吻→请求学习激情→开始→高潮→事后发誓会来找你
  • 机制：让她成为青楼员工（谈心会在招募流程执行后开始）
  • **主题：**解放。她自愿加入你的产业。守护者的教诲获得新解：人与人的联系会滋养内在之光，而非使其黯淡。

**独特之处：**多数成为青楼员工的NPC都会通过招募剧情加入。Grace的招募发生在一段8轮亲密谈心中，她会把自己的第一次交给你。这既是恋情的高潮，也是她从修道院中获得解放的时刻——两者在叙事上是同一件事。

约会——自然散步

Esperanza、Mindy等人的约会剧情较为精致；Grace的约会则更亲密、质朴。

自然散步约会

  • 相识以上阶段、120分钟、−40精力、上午8点至下午2点
  • 可在抄经室大厅选择
  • 一次性
  • 带Grace到修道院高墙之外散步——对她而言，这是重要的一步

跨角色剧情

Grace会出现在三段重要的跨角色剧情中：

剧情 触发地点 共同出演 说明
Blinky与Grace相遇 （跨角色） Blinky Grace结识穿越时空的巫师——每个友善／风趣选项使Grace+5
Grace与魔法 奥术商店、上午10点至下午6点 Esperanza＋Aziza（两人均需已遇见以上） 跨角色魔法讨论
Fernando为Grace制作服装 裁缝店 Fernando Grace通过Fernando的裁缝系统取得新衣服

推荐推进路线

阶段1：陌生人→已遇见

  • 在抄经室大厅拜访她（上午7点至10点或上午11点至下午2点）
  • 每天交谈（每天5次，仅对话——推进阶段）
  • 为已知阶段的门槛储备花束

阶段2：已遇见→已知

  • 进行谈心1（“墙外生活”）——开启对话的第一步
  • 达到已知并解锁后，改为每天送花
  • 将沟通提高到40，以进行一次性的灵性觉知

阶段3：已知→相识

  • 沟通40以上时进行灵性觉知：免费永久感知+1、好感度+8
  • 达到已知时，会自动触发“一句温和的提醒”剧情
  • 继续在全部四个地点每天送花＋交谈
  • 进行自然散步约会（一次性，上午8点至下午2点）

阶段4：相识→朋友

  • “失落在译文之间”剧情会在彩饰手稿室自动触发（偶数日下午2点至6点）
  • 将青楼声望提高到5以上，以解锁Sashina路线

阶段5：朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 在树林中进行谈心2（“摇曳之光”）
  • “秘法师的好奇”剧情自动触发（需要青楼声望5）
  • 在树林中与Sashina进行“另一种光”（Grace+19、Sashina+10的高额奖励）
  • 继续每天送礼，将关系推进至迷恋

阶段6：迷恋→500点以上

  • “亡者之间的低语”会在地下墓穴自动触发（晚上6点至10点、迷恋以上）——Grace+58
  • 此后，你会拥有500以上好感度和秘密会面标记——解放内容已经开放

阶段7：解放

  • 在晚上8点至午夜12点前往冥想林（偶数日可在此时进入树林）
  • 进行**“我的光之归处”**——8轮解放谈心
  • Grace会在对话中途成为青楼员工
  • 她的修道院日程会停用；此后会出现在你的青楼

阶段8：青楼员工阶段

  • Grace会被安排到自己的青楼房间
  • 工作时间：下午2点至晚上10点
  • 初始技能：冥想（已经学会）
  • 特殊技能：传教士位
  • 她在青楼中的行动受标准员工管理系统控制

关系阶段与对话语气

Grace的对话发展完整展现了从见习修女到获得解放的旅程。她起初正式而好奇，之后变得脆弱、怀疑自己，最终在Sashina的介入后找到确定的答案。

青楼员工资料（解放后）

Grace成为青楼员工后，属性如下：

  • **耐力：**18、**智力：**25、**灵巧：**14、**能力：**8
  • **外貌：**20、**魅力：**16
  • **特殊技能：**传教士位
  • **初始技能：**冥想
  • **服装等级：**2
  • **工作时间：**下午2点至晚上10点
  • **基础工资：**110金币
  • **名气：**6
  • **忠诚度：**50（初始）
  • 分配到自己的房间（要求房间最低达到2级）

“怀有世俗欲望的见习修女”这一原型塑造了她作为青楼员工时的对话和反应。

提示与最佳玩法

  • 她的每日交谈提供0基础好感度——交谈会推进关系阶段，但不适合刷取好感度。达到已知以上后，请用花束实际获取好感度。
  • 她的4个地点并非同时出现——她会严格按照日程在不同地点之间移动。请相应规划拜访时间，最大化每天的交谈＋送花次数。
  • 灵性觉知会免费永久提供感知+1——需要沟通40和已知阶段。不要错过；感知35也是她的喜爱属性。
  • 青楼声望5是一项软门槛——未达到时，Sashina故事线永远不会开始。请同步提高青楼声望。
  • 盗贼40是她独特的喜爱属性——大多数NPC使用魅力／力量／耐力。为取得她的加成，很值得投资盗贼。
  • 解放谈心是一扇单向门——完成后，Grace会永久离开修道院。在准备好将她转换为青楼员工模式前，不要触发。
  • 剧情奖励可以大量叠加——她的6段自动触发剧情仅靠剧情便会奖励+7+9+14+19+58＝好感度+107，还不包括礼物或谈心。
  • 诱惑技能会让她的所有点数翻倍——与一名正在发现激情的前修女格外契合。请保持已学会状态。
  • 解放要求好感度达到500以上——连锁剧情奖励＋每日礼物＋完成谈心1／2便足以达到。

内容状态

  • **开发优先级：**充足（高开发等级）
  • 解放前恋情：✓完整（3段谈心＋1次约会＋6段自动剧情）
  • 转换为青楼员工：✓完成解放谈心后成为在职员工
  • **动画：**作为修道院NPC时没有；可通过标准员工系统使用青楼员工动画
  • **技能教学：**不直接教授技能（灵性觉知会使感知+1）
  • **跨角色整合：**Sashina（招募）、Monk（警告）、Esperanza＋Aziza（魔法剧情）、Blinky、Fernando

截至当前版本，Grace的内容功能完整，并拥有游戏中叙事整合度最高的恋爱转员工故事线。
