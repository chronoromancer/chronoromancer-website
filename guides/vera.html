Overview

Vera is the 25-year-old hustler queen of the Downtown Bar — a cunning, calculating gambler in a black dress who runs the bar's games like she owns the place. Sharp-eyed, smug, mocking, and married to her own confidence, she sees every man who approaches her as either a mark or a waste of her time. Her romance arc is the only one in the game where you don't grind gifts or stats — you win her heart by winning her money.

Her entire progression is gated by net gambling winnings against her across four bar games (Darts, Dice, Hold'em, Blackjack). Plot triggers, H2H access, and the late-game intimate content all require specific net-winnings thresholds. The more money you take from her, the more her cocky business persona cracks — until eventually she drops it entirely and becomes vulnerable for the first time.

She's coded as evil-aligned. Three personality phases govern her dialogue and images: Business Persona (early), Conflicted Attraction (mid), Vulnerable Personality (late).

The Net Winnings System — Her Core Mechanic

Vera's relationship is gated by your net gambling winnings against her across all four bar games. The system tracks money won and lost through Darts, Liar's Dice, Texas Hold'em, and Blackjack.

Net Winnings = Won (all 4 games) − Lost (all 4 games)

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Attractiveness 35, Charm 40, Stamina 45 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Perfect Metabolism skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: When Tables Turn (base 100), all 3 favorite stats met, Perfect Metabolism learned: (100 + 3) × 2 = 206 points — plus possible +1s. The single highest H2H reward in the game.

Min-max tip: Her favorite stat thresholds are mid-tier (Attractiveness 35, Charm 40, Stamina 45). Push all three, then Perfect Metabolism, before running the big H2Hs and plot completions for maximum point gain.

The Plot Chain — Gambling-Gated Story

Vera's plot chain is the most uniquely structured in the game. Each plot requires a higher net winnings threshold plus a higher relationship stage. Drift up both at the same pace, and the plots will fire as expected.

"The Downtown Bar" — Intro (auto-triggers)

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown Bar
  • Required: None
  • Vera notices you walking into her bar. First impression — three-choice response (caring/funny/flirty)
  • Rewards: +5 Vera + choice bonus

"The Tell" — Acquaintance + $800 net

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown Bar
  • Requires: Acquaintance stage + intro completed + +$800 net gambling winnings
  • Vera notices you've been winning more than usual. She tries to read your tell — and fails
  • Rewards: +8 Vera + choice bonus

"Bad Night" — Friend + $1,200 net

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown Bar
  • Requires: Friend stage + The Tell completed + +$1,200 net winnings
  • Vera's having a bad streak. Walls down a fraction. First crack in the business persona
  • Rewards: +10 Vera + choice bonus

"Crossing the Line" — Close Friend + $1,800 net

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown Bar
  • Requires: Close Friend stage + Bad Night completed + +$1,800 net winnings
  • Boundary erodes. She blurs the line between business and personal
  • Rewards: +15 Vera + choice bonus

"Last Card" — Romantic + H2H 2 done + $4,000 net

  • Auto-triggers: Downtown Bar
  • Requires: Romantic stage + Crossing the Line completed + When Tables Turn H2H done + +$4,000 net winnings
  • The final piece falls. She's fully transformed by your influence
  • Rewards: +25 Vera + choice bonus

Total auto-plot relationship rewards: +5+8+10+15+25 = +63 base across the chain, before choice bonuses or skill multipliers.

Heart-to-Heart Conversations

H2H 1 — "Cards and Confidence"

  • Location: Downtown Bar
  • Requires: Known stage + +$500 net winnings
  • Cost: $100, 60 min, −15 energy
  • 5 rounds: she calls you over from the wall → bar prep ritual before the cards → reading tells philosophy → admits you're unreadable, "the most fun I've had at this table in years" → she wins the hand (Kings over queens) and slides the pot away
  • Theme: Her peak. Cocky, playful, in full control. She wins this round. First sign she's finding you genuinely interesting.

H2H 2 — "When the Tables Turn"

  • Location: Downtown Bar (then moves player to Love Hotel)
  • Requires: Crush stage + +$2,500 net winnings
  • Cost: $600, 180 min, −50 energy, −30 calm
  • 6 rounds: her proposition (you've taken too much money — let me win some back, somewhere private) → hotel room confession → growing closer → intimate connection → first sexual encounter → afterglow
  • Theme: Her first surrender. The vulnerable persona emerges for the first time. The walls come all the way down.
  • Climax: First sexual encounter with Vera. The narrative bridge to all post-Romantic content.

Reward note: H2H 2 awards +100 base plus per-round choice points (15-25 per choice). With Perfect Metabolism + favorite stats met, this single H2H can yield 200+ relationship points. The single highest H2H reward in the game.

The Apartment Visit — "Spend the Night"

Once you reach Romantic stage with $5,000+ net winnings, the Night with Vera remote action at the Love Hotel (8 PM – 12 AM) opens up her apartment branching conversation: a 13-round hub-spoke intimate scene.

Structure

The conversation runs through 3 intro rounds (Arrival → Kiss Anticipation → Kiss) into a central HUB at Round 4. From the hub, you choose between 5 paths:

The 5 Hub Paths

  1. Oral Path (Round 5) — Vera giving to player → returns to hub
  2. Missionary Path (Rounds 6-7) — missionary → completion → returns to hub
  3. Cowgirl Path (Rounds 8-9) — cowgirl → completion → returns to hub
  4. Doggie Path (Rounds 10-11) — doggie → completion → returns to hub
  5. Finish Path (Rounds 12-13) — preend → ending (terminate conversation)

Path Replay

  • All position paths (Oral/Miss/Cow/Dog) return to the hub — you can chain them across replays
  • The Finish path (Round 12-13) ends the conversation
  • Each intro round (1-3) gives +10 points per choice
  • Repeatable — daily limit 1 per Night with Vera action

Strategy

  • For maximum points per visit: chain hub-loop paths (Oral → Miss → Cow → Dog → Finish)
  • For specific content: pick the path you want, hit the Finish at the end
  • Apartment uses 12 unique images — 5 begin/anticipate/kiss/hub plus position-specific images

Patreon paywalled — free demo players cannot access the apartment visit.

Skill Training — Cheating and Seduction

Vera teaches two unique skills that are otherwise hard to obtain:

Cheating

  • Friend stage, $350, 180 min
  • +20 Vera + teaches Cheating skill
  • The Cheating skill bends rules in your favor in games and contests across the game

Seduction

  • Close Friend stage, $400, 200 min
  • +20 Vera + teaches Seduction skill
  • The Seduction skill is used in many other characters' apartment visits and dialogue gates

Both skills are exclusive to her. Other characters offer related skills, but Cheating and Seduction are Vera's specialty.

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Vera is gambling-gated, not gift-gated. No flowers, no chocolate, no jewelry. Her affection comes from winning her money.
  • Net winnings are tracked across all 4 bar games (Darts, Liar's Dice, Hold'em, Blackjack). Use whichever ones you're best at.
  • Win Darts daily — guaranteed +10 rel + $100 if you win. It's the most consistent grind action.
  • Learn Cheating early (Friend, $350) — it makes bar games much more reliable and helps you blast through net-winnings gates.
  • Don't lose money to her early. Net negative puts you in the Mocking tier — her dialogue treats you with contempt. Stay net-positive from the start.
  • Perfect Metabolism doubles all her points — keep it learned, especially before H2H 2 (the single highest-reward H2H in the game).
  • Bar Game Psychology gives +1 Luck permanent — Comm 50 is the gate. Don't miss it; Luck helps you win bar games.
  • The H2H 2 reward (+100 base, doubled = 200+) is the single highest single-action reward in the game — make sure favorite stats and Perfect Metabolism are stacked before running it.
  • The plot chain keeps pace with the H2Hs and stages — drift up your gambling winnings and your stage at the same rate. Don't get ahead of either.
  • Most late-game actions are Patreon paywalled — Back to the Hotel, Night with Vera. Free demo players have access to bar games + H2Hs but not the post-Romantic content.
  • Her gambling-tier dialogue rewards repeat visits — visit her after every big winning session to hear the next tier of reactions.
  • Her three personality phases match her image ranges — Business uses 1-12, Conflicted uses 1-15, Vulnerable uses 31-34. The image set itself transforms.
  • One traditional date — Vera's Boardwalk Date is available at Friend+ from 8 PM to 11 PM; her other relationship content remains centered on bar games and hotel visits.

概述

Vera 是市中心酒吧里25岁的赌局女王——她身穿黑裙，是个狡猾而精于算计的赌徒，把酒吧里的赌局经营得仿佛那是自己的产业。她目光锐利、自负傲慢又尖酸刻薄，对自己的本事深信不疑；在她眼中，每个前来搭讪的男人不是待宰的肥羊，就是不值得浪费时间的人。她的恋爱路线是游戏中唯一不靠反复送礼或堆属性推进的路线——你要靠 赢走她的钱来赢得她的心。

她的整个进度取决于你在四种酒吧游戏（飞镖、骰子、德州扑克和二十一点）中对她取得的 赌博净赢额。剧情触发、H2H解锁和后期亲密内容全都要求达到特定净赢额。你从她手中赢走的钱越多，她那副自信满满的生意人面具就崩裂得越厉害——直到最后彻底卸下面具，第一次显露脆弱。

她被设定为 邪恶阵营。她的对话和立绘由三个性格阶段决定：生意人面孔（前期）、矛盾的爱慕（中期）和 脆弱的真我（后期）。

加成算法（准确计算顺序）

好感度按以下顺序计算：

  1. 以行动的 基础点数 开始
  2. 每项达标的偏好属性 +1（吸引力35、魅力40、耐力45——最多+3）

  4. 若玩家拥有关键物品 “1枚戒指”，则 +1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 +1
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时 +1
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，成功则 +1（上限50%；持有华丽挂坠时为60%）

极限优化提示： 她的偏好属性门槛处于中等水平（吸引力35、魅力40、耐力45）。先让三项全部达标并学会完美代谢，再完成大型H2H和剧情，便可获得最多点数。

  • H2H 2的奖励（基础+100，翻倍后200以上）是游戏中单次行动奖励最高的一项——行动前务必使偏好属性全部达标并学会完美代谢。
  • 剧情链会与H2H和关系阶段同步推进——让赌博净赢额和关系阶段以相同速度增长，不要让其中一项领先太多。
  • 大部分后期行动属于Patreon付费内容——包括“回到旅馆”和“与 Vera 共度夜晚”。免费试玩版玩家可以体验酒吧游戏与H2H，但无法体验浪漫感阶段后的内容。
  • 赌博等级对话鼓励你反复拜访——每次赢下一大笔钱后都去找她，听听下一等级的反应。
  • 她的三个性格阶段对应不同立绘范围——生意人阶段使用1-12，矛盾阶段使用1-15，脆弱阶段使用31-34。立绘本身也会随她蜕变。
  • 一次传统约会——Vera的木板路约会在朋友阶段后开放，时间为晚上8点至11点；她的其他关系内容仍以酒吧游戏和旅馆拜访为核心。
