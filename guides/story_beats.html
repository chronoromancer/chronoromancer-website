Chronoromancer's main story currently contains 62 sequential Story Beats. Most begin automatically when you enter the correct location with the required prior beat complete. A smaller number require a relationship stage, side plot, worker, skill, item, faction score, dungeon victory, or large payment.

This guide was inspired by the community-created Story Beats wiki page by Sh1n7, then rebuilt from the current Story Beat registry and completion handlers. The community page stopped at Beat 60; the current build continues through Beat 62.

Heavy spoiler warning: This guide names late-story events, character transformations, recruits, and major rewards.

How Story Beats Work

The story is linear: Beat 2 requires Beat 1, Beat 3 requires Beat 2, and so on. Completing a beat advances the current Story Beat and records its completion flag.

Automatic and Manual Beats

Most beats are automatic. Entering their location while all conditions are true starts the scene.

Two beats are manual location actions:

  • Beat 8: choose Ask Lilu to Train You at the Dojo.
  • Beat 11: choose Discuss the Research Findings at the University Research Lab.

Simply entering those locations is not enough.

Time Window Convention

Story windows use a start-inclusive, end-exclusive rule. A listed window of 9 AM–5 PM means the trigger works from 9:00 through 4:59 PM. At exactly 5:00 PM, the window has closed.

“Any time” means the Story Beat itself has no time restriction, although the location may still have opening hours.

Choices and Progression

Choices can change relationship rewards and, in several beats, alignment. They do not replace the next Story Beat or create a separate main-story branch. Choose based on roleplay or desired relationship gains unless a note below calls out a special effect.

Current Time-Window Quirk for Beats 48, 49, 50, 51, and 53

Those five entries store their intended hours under start_hour and end_hour. The current plot-condition engine only enforces min_hour and max_hour, so those Story Beat windows are not directly enforced in the current build.

In practice:

  • The location's own opening hours can still restrict when you enter.
  • The scene may trigger outside the intended window if the location remains accessible.
  • The intended hours are listed above because they describe the designed route, but they are not currently reliable trigger gates.

This quirk does not affect the correctly encoded min_hour/max_hour windows used by the other Story Beats.

Time Costs and Forced Exhaustion

Most early Story Beats rely on their scenes or actions for time handling. From Beat 22 onward, many completion handlers explicitly advance time.

The largest jumps are:

  • Beat 9: +8 hours, then Energy 1 / Calm 1 / Health 20
  • Beat 22: +18 hours, then Energy 10 / Calm 10 / Satiation 10
  • Beat 45: +2 hours
  • Beat 49: +2 hours
  • Beat 57: +2 hours

Do not begin Beat 9 or Beat 22 immediately before a separate narrow appointment unless you are prepared to lose the remainder of that time window.

Troubleshooting

The next beat does not trigger

Check, in order:

  1. The previous Story Beat is complete.
  2. You are at the exact location in the table.
  3. The current hour is inside the start-inclusive, end-exclusive window.
  4. The relationship, worker, skill, faction, item, side-plot, or dungeon gate is satisfied.
  5. The location itself is open and unlocked.

Beat 8 does not start when entering the Dojo

Choose Ask Lilu to Train You. Beat 8 is a manual Story action.

Beat 11 does not start at the Research Lab

Choose Discuss the Research Findings. Beat 11 is also manual.

Beat 24 is stuck

Robin must be at Crush or higher. Return to her relationship content before revisiting the Hunting Lodge from 6 PM to midnight.

Beat 36 or 37 is stuck

Willow must be active as Worker #18. Beat 37 also requires access to the Rose & Thorn Nurse's Room.

Beat 38 is stuck

The player must know Blink. Progress Blinky's training/content until it is learned, then enter the Arcane Room.

Beat 39 is stuck

Kasumi must be active as Worker #20, and the Rose & Thorn Bar must be accessible. Enter from 3 PM to 6 PM.

Beat 43 is stuck

All three gates must be true:

  • Bella is Romantic or higher.
  • Blinky is Romantic or higher.
  • Blinky's first-kiss event is complete.

Beat 47 is stuck

Miki must be Friend or higher, and both prerequisite side plots must be complete:

  • ComicCon Miki
  • Miki's GuguDolly Emergency

Beat 51 is stuck

Carry at least 1 Flowers and have Miki at Crush. The flower is removed when the beat completes.

Beat 55 is stuck

You need the full $10,000,000 in available Wealth before entering the Town Hall Office. The payment is mandatory and is deducted on completion.

Beat 58 is stuck

Defeat the Dungeon Floor 25 miniboss first, then visit Torea's Chamber.

Nothing appears after Beat 62

Beat 62 is the current end of the registered main-story sequence. Additional relationship, faction, dungeon, brothel, resort, and side-story content remains available.

故事节点的运作方式

故事采用线性结构：节点2要求完成节点1，节点3要求完成节点2，依此类推。完成节点会推进当前故事节点，并记录其完成标记。

自动节点与手动节点

大多数节点会自动触发。只要所有条件均已满足，进入对应地点就会开始场景。

有两个节点需要手动选择地点行动：

  • 节点8： 在道场选择 请 Lilu 来训练你。
  • 节点11： 在大学研究实验室选择 讨论研究发现。

仅仅进入这些地点并不足以触发节点。

时间窗口规则

故事时间窗口采用包含起始时间、不包含结束时间的规则。标示为 上午9点至下午5点 的窗口，意味着触发时间为上午9:00至下午4:59。到下午5:00整时，窗口便已关闭。

“任意时间”表示故事节点本身没有时间限制，但所在地点仍可能有营业时间。

选择与进度

选择可能改变关系奖励，并会在若干节点中影响阵营倾向。它们不会替换下一个故事节点，也不会创建独立的主线分支。除非下文特别注明某项特殊效果，否则请依据角色扮演偏好或想要的关系收益进行选择。

节点48、49、50、51和53当前的时间窗口问题

这五个条目将预定开放时间存储在start_hour和end_hour中。当前剧情条件引擎只会执行min_hour与max_hour，因此这些故事节点的时间窗口在当前版本中不会被直接限制。

实际游玩时：

  • 地点本身的营业时间依然可能限制你的进入时间。
  • 如果地点仍可进入，场景可能在预定窗口之外触发。
  • 上文列出预定时间，是因为它们体现了设计路线，但目前不能作为可靠的触发门槛。

该问题不会影响其他故事节点正确编码的min_hour／max_hour时间窗口。

时间消耗与强制疲惫

大多数前期故事节点依靠场景或行动本身来处理时间。从节点22开始，许多完成处理程序会明确推进时间。

幅度最大的时间变化为：

  • 节点9： +8小时，随后精力为1／镇定为1／生命为20
  • 节点22： +18小时，随后精力为10／镇定为10／饱腹感为10
  • 节点45： +2小时
  • 节点49： +2小时
  • 节点57： +2小时

如果你不希望错过另一个时间窗口狭窄的约会，就不要在紧邻该约会前开始节点9或节点22。

问题排查

下一个节点没有触发

请按顺序检查：

  1. 前一个故事节点已经完成。
  2. 你位于表中指定的确切地点。
  3. 当前时间位于包含起始时间、不包含结束时间的窗口内。
  4. 已满足关系、员工、技能、阵营、物品、支线剧情或地下城门槛。
  5. 地点本身已经开放并解锁。

进入道场时，节点8没有开始

选择 请 Lilu 来训练你。节点8是一个手动故事行动。

抵达研究实验室时，节点11没有开始

选择 讨论研究发现。节点11同样需要手动触发。

节点51卡住了
