Overview

Jessie is a 22-year-old woman the player encounters crying outside the local cafe — she's just left her emotionally abusive ex-boyfriend, has $300 to her name, and nowhere to live. The player has just listed their basement apartment for rent. The timing is too perfect.

She becomes the player's tenant, and her romance arc plays out as a slow rebound built on the gym (her favorite trigger), drinking nights, and her open admission that she's looking for someone strong enough to help her forget. She's the tutorial NPC for the gym → Strength/Stamina → Alcohol Tolerance progression — her plot chain literally tells the player to learn those skills.

Unlock Path — The Tenant Story

Jessie's full arc unfolds as an auto-triggering plot chain. The player doesn't trigger plots manually — they simply meet conditions and walk into the right location, and each plot fires.

Step 1 — Rent Out Your Basement

Inside your home (Living Room → Go to Basement), use the "Rent Out Apartment" action.

Detail Value
Location present_basement_uptown
Time cost 30 min
Money cost None (sets up $1000/month rental)
Type One-time only
Sets flag jessie_apartment_listed

Description in-game: posts fliers around the neighborhood, uploads listing to rental sites. After completing this, nothing visibly changes — the player has to physically visit the cafe next.

Step 2 — "A Girl in Need" (jessie_cafe_encounter)

Detail Value
Trigger location present_cafe_exterior (outside the cafe, not inside)
Required flag jessie_apartment_listed
Auto-triggers Yes
Reward +10 jessie

She's sitting on a bench, crying. Her boyfriend just exploded at her, she walked out, has $300 and nowhere to go.

Three-choice approach:

  • Polite: "Excuse me, are you alright?"
  • Normal: "Hey, what's going on?"
  • Flirty: "A beautiful girl like you shouldn't be crying."

She explains her situation. The player offers the basement.

Three-choice rent decision:

  • Gentleman: "$300 is fine to start"
  • Business: "$300 now, pay the rest later"
  • Flirty: "Having a beautiful roommate? $300 sounds perfect"

She accepts, hands over the cash, and moves in. Completing jessie_cafe_encounter unlocks the Visit Jessie action at the basement (2 PM – 10 PM).

Step 3 — "Jessie Notices Your Gains" (jessie_gym_recognition)

Detail Value
Trigger location present_jessies_room_uptown
Faction requirement gym reputation ≥ 10
Auto-triggers Yes
Reward +15 jessie

Build gym reputation through workouts at City Gym Uptown. The Full Body Workout, Run on Treadmill, No Pain No Gain, and Socialize actions all award gym clout (+1 to +3 each).

When the player visits Jessie's room with gym 10+, she immediately notices the change: "Wait... have you been working out? Your arms are bigger. Your shoulders too." She admits her ex was a gym guy and that fitness draws her in.

Three-choice response:

  • Humble: "Just trying to stay healthy"
  • Confident: "I like pushing myself"
  • Flirty: "If it impresses you, that's a nice bonus"

Closes with: "Maybe we should have some drinks together sometime..." — direct setup for the next plot.

Step 4 — "What Jessie Really Needs" (jessie_rebound_request)

Detail Value
Trigger location present_jessies_room_uptown
Required flag jessie_gym_recognition_completed
Required stage Jessie at Friend stage+
Auto-triggers Yes
Reward +30 jessie

She tells the player she's been thinking about her ex too much and knows what would actually help her move on: a rebound. She wants the player. But she has conditions:

  • Bring wine. "Bring some wine. Come back after dark - like, late. After 7pm at least."
  • Be able to keep up with her drinking. "I don't want you passing out before I do."
  • Practice at the bar. "If you need to practice, the bar downtown has good wine and... they can teach you how to drink properly."

This is the explicit in-game pointer to learn Alcohol Tolerance at Vera's downtown bar.

Three-choice response: Supportive / Eager / Understanding — all lead to the same conclusion.

Step 5 — "Jessie's Rebound" (jessie_rebound_night)

Detail Value
Trigger location present_jessies_room_uptown
Required flag jessie_rebound_request_completed
Required skill Alcohol Tolerance
Required item 1 Wine in inventory
Required time 7 PM – 10 PM
Auto-triggers Yes
Reward +40 jessie

Player arrives at her room with wine after 7 PM. She declares she's done talking about her ex. They drink. She gets tipsy ("slightly slurred Wow, this wine is strong"), gets too warm, takes off her shirt, and the scene proceeds to its intimate conclusion using the jessie_intimate_plot video animation.

Aftermath: "That was exactly what I needed." She's not looking for a relationship yet — but she's open to "doing this again sometime." The plot is single-fire (sets jessie_rebound_night_completed).

After this, the standard intimate Intimate Time action becomes the repeatable equivalent.

  • Intimate Time is paywalled (requires_paid_version: True) and awards +50 base relationship + 60 erotic experience plus the video.

Dialogue Voice — Hint-Heavy Tutorial

Jessie's dialogue is the game's tutorial for the gym/Strength/Stamina/Alcohol Tolerance progression. Her stage-progression dialogue explicitly tells the player what to do:

Acknowledged stage Talk Responses:

  • "My ex was really into fitness. He spent so much time at the gym."
  • "There is just something about a fit guy that I find really attractive."
  • "I know it sounds superficial, but I cannot help what I am drawn to."

Acquaintance/Friend stage Talk Responses:

  • "You know what girls do to get over a bad breakup? Get really drunk and have rebound sex with some random guy."
  • "There is something about a man who can handle his liquor that really impresses me."
  • "A guy with real stamina, who can party all night and still have energy left over when things get interesting? That is attractive."

Crush stage:

  • "I keep thinking about you when you are not around."
  • "You make my heart race in ways I did not expect."

Intimate stage:

  • "From that crying girl at the cafe to this... it feels perfect."

The dialogue is structured to push the player toward Strength, Stamina, gym faction, and Alcohol Tolerance — exactly the unlock chain. Even the Talk action's dialogue is functional: it tells the player what plot prerequisites to work on next.

Progression Path — Recommended

  1. Day 1: Living Room → Basement → Rent Out Apartment (one-time, 30 min). Sets jessie_apartment_listed.
  2. Walk to the Cafe Exterior → "A Girl in Need" auto-triggers. Jessie moves into the basement. +10 rel.
  3. Talk to Jessie at her room (Known stage required — she may need a few visits to reach Known via gifts first if she starts at Stranger). Give Chocolate ($15 from convenience store, +5 each) to push to Known.
  4. Build gym faction reputation to 10+ through gym workouts (Full Body Workout, Run on Treadmill, No Pain No Gain, Socialize).
  5. Visit her room → "Jessie Notices Your Gains" auto-triggers. +15 rel.
  6. Continue daily Talk + gifts until Friend stage.
  7. Visit her room at Friend → "What Jessie Really Needs" auto-triggers. +30 rel. She tells you to learn alcohol and bring wine.
  8. Learn Alcohol Tolerance (taught by Vera at the downtown bar 8 PM – 12 AM, requires Charm 18+, $200, 180 min).
  9. Buy Wine at the alehouse ($600 medieval — easier than other Wine sources for repeated runs).
  10. Visit her room with Wine + Alcohol Tolerance, 7-10 PM → "Jessie's Rebound" auto-triggers. +40 rel + intimate video. Single-fire.
  11. Continue daily routine: Talk (5/day) + Wine gifts (5/day, +10 each, Close Friend+) + Chocolate (5/day) + occasional Teddy/Flowers (1/day each).
  12. Friend stage: Run Zoo Date (one-time, $100, 2-5 PM).
  13. Close Friend stage + boardwalk built: Run Boardwalk Sunset Date (one-time, $50, 5-8 PM, Patreon).
  14. 400+ rel pts: Intimate Time unlocks (paywalled video, +50 + 60 erotic exp, daily limit 1).

Why Alcohol Tolerance Matters

  • Her Special Skill — doubles all relationship points
  • Hard-required for the Rebound Night plot
  • Vera teaches it at the downtown bar — natural side benefit of Vera's questline
  • Once learned, the Wine gift action (+10 base, daily limit 5) becomes the highest-volume relationship grind in the present era

Why Gym Investment Pays Off Triple

  • Builds gym faction → unlocks jessie_gym_recognition (+15)
  • Builds Strength + Stamina → both are her favorite stats (+1 bonus each at 30)
  • Strength training is needed for many Jennifer/Lucy/Talena interactions

Tips & Notes

  • She's the gym/strength/alcohol tutorial — the dialogue literally tells the player what to learn. Take the hint.
  • Lowest favorite-stat thresholds in the game (30/30/30) — easiest character to stack favorite-stat bonuses with.
  • Alcohol Tolerance + Wine grind is the highest-velocity Present-era relationship farm when fully unlocked: +10 base × 2 (Alcohol Tolerance) × 5/day = +100/day from Wine alone, plus stat bonuses.
  • Don't skip the Rent Out Apartment action. Without it, the cafe encounter never triggers and the entire questline is locked.
  • Visit Jessie respects her hours. Outside 2-10 PM, knocking gets "no answer."
  • Wine costs $600 at the medieval alehouse. Plan your time-travel runs accordingly. The convenience store doesn't sell wine.
  • The Rebound Night auto-triggers the moment you walk in with Wine + Alcohol Tolerance during 7-10 PM. Don't enter her room with these prerequisites unless you want the plot to fire.
  • Boardwalk Date requires the boardwalk built — community development project. The Patreon gate (requires_higher_power) is on top of that.
  • No "Compliment" action — Jessie's main interactions are Talk + 4 gifts + 2 dates + intimate. Talk is daily 5, max +5/day base.
  • Her starting stage is Stranger, but the plot chain carries you past the gates. The Cafe Encounter's +10 lifts her to Acknowledged, and the Gym Recognition plot adds +15 base plus choice points — enough to reach Known, which unlocks Talk and the gift actions. You never need to grind a Stranger-stage Jessie manually.

概述

Jessie是一名22岁的女性。玩家会在当地咖啡馆外遇见正在哭泣的她——她刚离开对自己施加情感虐待的前男友，身上只有$300，也无处可住。玩家恰好刚把自己的地下室公寓挂牌出租。这个时机实在太巧了。

她会成为玩家的房客；她的恋爱路线是一段缓慢升温的过渡关系，以健身房（她最喜欢的触发点）、喝酒之夜，以及她坦率承认自己想找个足够强壮、能帮她忘记过去的人为基础。她是健身房→力量／耐力→酒精耐受进度的教学NPC——她的剧情链会直接告诉玩家去学习这些内容。

解锁路线——房客故事

Jessie的完整路线以自动触发的剧情链展开。玩家无需手动启动剧情——只要满足条件并走进正确地点，每段剧情就会自行触发。

第1步——出租地下室

在家中（客厅→前往地下室）使用**“出租公寓”**行动。

详情 数值
地点 present_basement_uptown
时间消耗 30分钟
金钱消耗 无（设定为每月$1000租金）
类型 仅限一次
设置标记 jessie_apartment_listed

游戏内描述：在社区张贴传单，并将房源上传至租房网站。完成后，表面上不会发生任何变化——接下来玩家必须亲自前往咖啡馆。

第2步——“需要帮助的女孩”（jessie_cafe_encounter）

详情 数值
触发地点 present_cafe_exterior（咖啡馆外，而非店内）

她坐在长椅上哭泣。男朋友刚刚对她大发雷霆；她离家出走，身上只有$300，也无处可去。

三种接近方式：

  • 礼貌： “打扰一下，你还好吗？”
  • 普通： “嘿，发生什么事了？”
  • 调情： “像你这么漂亮的女孩不该哭泣。”

她解释了自己的处境，玩家则提出可以住进地下室。

三种租金决定：

  • 绅士： “先给$300就够了”
  • 公事公办： “现在付$300，其余之后再补”
  • 调情： “有这么漂亮的室友？$300听起来很合适”

她接受条件，交出现金并搬了进来。完成jessie_cafe_encounter后，地下室会解锁“拜访Jessie”行动（下午2点至晚上10点）。

第3步——“Jessie注意到你的变化”（jessie_gym_recognition）

详情 数值

在上城区城市健身房锻炼，提升健身房声望。全身锻炼、跑步机跑步、不痛不强和社交行动都会奖励健身房影响力（每次＋1至＋3）。

玩家在健身房达到10以上后造访Jessie的房间，她会立即注意到变化：“等等……你最近在锻炼吗？你的手臂变粗了，肩膀也是。” 她承认前男友就是个健身爱好者，而健美的身体很吸引她。

  • 谦逊： “只是想保持健康”
  • 自信： “我喜欢挑战自己”
  • 调情： “如果能让你留下好印象，那就是不错的额外收获”

最后她会说：“也许哪天我们该一起喝几杯……”——直接为下一段剧情铺路。

玩家在晚上7点后带着葡萄酒来到她的房间。她宣布自己不想再谈前男友。两人开始喝酒；她渐渐微醺（“口齿略显含糊 哇，这酒真烈”），觉得太热，于是脱下上衣，场景随后通过jessie_intimate_plot视频动画进入亲密结局。

事后： “这正是我需要的。” 她还没打算开始一段恋情——但愿意“以后再来几次”。该剧情只会触发一次（设置jessie_rebound_night_completed）。

完成后，标准的亲密行动亲密时光会成为可重复的对应内容。

对话语气——提示密集的教学内容

Jessie的对话是游戏中关于健身房／力量／耐力／酒精耐受进度的教学。她随阶段推进的对话会直接告诉玩家该做什么：

已遇见阶段的交谈回应：

  • “我前男友很喜欢健身。他花了好多时间泡在健身房。”
  • “身材健美的男人就是有种特别吸引我的地方。”
  • “我知道这听起来很肤浅，但我没办法控制自己的喜好。”

相识／朋友阶段的交谈回应：

  • “你知道女孩都是怎么走出糟糕分手的吗？喝个烂醉，再随便找个男人来一场过渡性爱。”
  • “酒量好的男人就是有种让我印象深刻的地方。”
  • “真正有耐力、能狂欢整晚，等事情变得有趣时还留有精力的男人？那才叫迷人。”

迷恋阶段：

  • “你不在身边时，我总会想起你。”
  • “你让我心跳加速，这是我没想到的。”

亲密阶段：

  • “从咖啡馆外那个哭泣的女孩走到现在……一切都很完美。”

这些对话的结构就是为了把玩家引向力量、耐力、健身房派系和酒精耐受——也就是完整解锁链。就连交谈行动的对话都有实际用途：它会告诉玩家接下来应该满足哪些剧情前置条件。

推荐推进路线

  1. 第1天： 客厅→地下室→出租公寓（一次性、30分钟）。设置jessie_apartment_listed。
  2. 前往咖啡馆外→自动触发“需要帮助的女孩”。Jessie搬进地下室，好感度＋10。
  3. 在她的房间和 Jessie 交谈（要求已知阶段——如果初始为陌生人，可能需要先造访几次并送礼物，才能达到已知）。送巧克力（便利店$15，每份＋5），推进至已知。
  4. 通过健身房锻炼（全身锻炼、跑步机跑步、不痛不强、社交），将健身房派系声望提高至10以上。
  5. 造访她的房间→自动触发“Jessie注意到你的变化”，好感度＋15。
  6. 继续每日交谈＋送礼，直到达到朋友阶段。
  7. 达到朋友后造访她的房间→自动触发“Jessie真正需要的东西”，好感度＋30。她会让你学习酒量并带来葡萄酒。
  8. 学习酒精耐受（由Vera在市中心酒吧晚上8点至午夜12点传授；要求魅力18以上、$200、180分钟）。
  9. 在酒馆购买葡萄酒（中世纪售价$600——重复流程时，比其他葡萄酒来源更方便）。
  10. 携带葡萄酒＋酒精耐受，在晚上7点至10点造访她的房间→自动触发“Jessie的过渡之夜”。好感度＋40并播放亲密视频。仅触发一次。
  11. 继续每日流程： 交谈（每天5次）＋送葡萄酒（每天5份，亲密朋友以上，每份＋10）＋巧克力（每天5份）＋偶尔送泰迪熊／鲜花（每天各1份）。
  12. 朋友阶段： 进行动物园约会（一次性、$100、下午2点至5点）。
  13. 亲密朋友阶段＋木板路已建成： 进行木板路日落约会（一次性、$50、下午5点至8点、Patreon）。
  14. 好感度400以上： 解锁亲密时光（付费视频、＋50、＋60情色经验、每日上限1次）。

酒精耐受为何重要

  • 这是她的特殊技能——使所有好感度翻倍
  • Jessie的过渡之夜剧情的硬性要求
  • Vera会在市中心酒吧传授——推进Vera任务线时自然就能顺便取得
  • 学会后，送葡萄酒行动（基础＋10、每日上限5次）会成为现代好感度刷取量最高的手段

投资健身房为何能带来三重回报

  • 提升健身房派系→解锁jessie_gym_recognition（＋15）
  • 提升力量＋耐力→两者都是她的偏好属性（达到30时各＋1加成）
  • 许多Jennifer／Lucy／Talena互动都要求力量训练

提示与备注

  • 她是健身房／力量／酒量教学角色——对话会直接告诉玩家该学习什么。请听取提示。
  • 拥有全游戏最低的偏好属性门槛（30／30／30）——最容易叠加偏好属性加成的角色。
  • 酒精耐受＋葡萄酒是现代刷取好感度最快的组合，完全解锁后：基础＋10×2（酒精耐受）×每天5份＝单靠葡萄酒每天＋100，另有属性加成。
  • 不要跳过出租公寓行动。 没有它，咖啡馆遭遇就永远不会触发，整条任务线也会锁定。
  • 拜访Jessie必须遵守她的开放时间。 下午2点至晚上10点之外敲门，只会收到“无人应答”。
  • 中世纪酒馆的葡萄酒售价为$600。 请据此规划时空旅行。便利店不卖葡萄酒。
  • 晚上7点至10点期间，只要携带葡萄酒＋酒精耐受走进她的房间，就会自动触发过渡之夜。 除非想立即启动剧情，否则不要在满足这些前置条件时进入她的房间。
  • 木板路约会要求先建成木板路——这是一项社区发展项目，此外还有Patreon限制（requires_higher_power）。
  • 没有“赞美”行动——Jessie的主要互动是交谈＋4种礼物＋2种约会＋亲密内容。每天可交谈5次，基础每天最多＋5。
  • 她的初始阶段是陌生人，但剧情链会带你越过各个门槛。 咖啡馆遭遇的＋10会将她提升至已遇见，健身认可剧情则会再提供基础＋15及选择点数——足以达到已知，解锁交谈和送礼行动。你从不需要在陌生人阶段手动刷取Jessie。
