Overview

Abby is an energetic dance instructor who works at Lucy's Dance & Yoga Studio in Present Day Downtown. She's 20 years old, bubbly, and absolutely obsessed with dancing — both on the job and out at the nightclub. Beneath the endless energy and playful teasing is a young woman who looks up to Lucy as both a mentor and a role model.

Abby is a fully developed romance character in the Present Era, with a dedicated plot chain, an intimate encounter at the Love Hotel (a 17-round stamina challenge), and an Extreme Date for late-game relationships.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Stamina 40, Creativity 35, Charm 30 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has the Dance Mastery skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Chocolate (base 3), all 3 favorite stats met, Dance Mastery skill: (3 + 3) × 2 = 12 points — plus possible +1s from Ring/Cologne/Buffer/Attractiveness.

Min-max tip: Hit Stamina 40, Creativity 35, and Charm 30 before grinding, and learn Dance Mastery before that. Every favorite-stat bonus is doubled by Dance Mastery, so stat investment pays double.

  • Do Party Responsibility once (+1 Ability, +8 points)

  • Unlocks the Challenge Abby dance competition action

  • Clear the Love Hotel stamina challenge (see Love Hotel Encounter section)

  • Extreme Date unlocks at the nightclub
  • Awards +100 relationship points on completion

The Dance Studio Quest Chain

This is Abby's main story content and the path to her animated scene.

Step 1: "Dance Studio Confrontation" (Auto-Triggers)

Trigger Conditions:

  • Location: Dance & Yoga Studio (Downtown)
  • Time: 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Dance Studio faction: 10+ clout (earned from Dance Lessons — each lesson gives +1 clout)
  • Both Lucy and Abby at Stranger stage or higher
  • Flag dance_studio_confrontation_completed not yet set

Plot Summary: You walk into the studio to find Lucy and Abby arguing about your progress. Lucy praises your dedication; Abby scoffs, insisting dancing is about "hips, sweat, and attitude" — natural talent, not practice. Lucy proposes a friendly wager: if you can beat Abby in a dance battle, she has to give you a "victory reward."

Abby blusters, accepts the bet, and promises to "grind you into the floorboards."

Rewards: +15 relationship with both Lucy and Abby.

After this plot, a new action — "Challenge Abby" — becomes available at the studio.

Step 2: Challenge Abby (Dance Minigame)

The "Challenge Abby" action launches the Dance Timing Test rhythm minigame. Hit the notes with perfect/good timing to rack up score.

  • Victory threshold: 14,000+ score sets the challenge_abby_victory flag
  • You can retry the minigame as many times as needed

Buffs that help:

  • Time Potion (active buff): ×2 score multiplier on every hit — this is the single biggest boost available
  • Lute (key item): Widens timing windows by +5ms — Perfect becomes ±49ms (up from ±44ms) and Good becomes ±93ms (up from ±88ms)

With both active, hitting 14,000 becomes dramatically easier — Perfect hits score 200 each and Good hits score 100 each.

Step 3: "Dance Victory Reward" Plot (Auto-Triggers on Victory)

Trigger Conditions:

  • Location: Dance & Yoga Studio
  • Time: 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Requires flags: dance_studio_confrontation_completed + challenge_abby_victory
  • Triggers immediately upon scoring 14,000+ if both flags are satisfied

Plot Summary: Stunned by your performance, Abby admits she lost fair and square. Lucy — ever the playful matchmaker — suggests the storage room in the back for a very private "sugar-coated cream pie award."

Three dialogue choices affect rewards:

  • Gracious: "That sounds like a wonderful reward, Abby."
  • Respectful: "Only if you're comfortable with it." (+5 bonus relationship with Abby)
  • Cautious: "Are you sure that's what you want to do?"

Base Rewards: +20 Lucy, +21 Abby. The Respectful choice is the optimal pick for total relationship gain.

Step 4: "Private Reward Session" Unlocked

After the Dance Victory Reward plot completes, a new repeating action appears at the studio:

  • Private Reward Session (45 min, −30 energy, daily limit 1)
  • Video/animated scene in the storage room
  • +35 relationship points per visit
  • Available whenever Abby is at the studio (5PM–10PM)

Love Hotel Encounter — The Stamina Challenge

Meeting Abby at the Love Hotel (10PM–12AM, 100g) triggers a 17-round progressive intimate encounter built around stamina management and energy conservation. This is unique to Abby and one of the more mechanically involved intimate scenes in the game.

Preparation Checklist

  • Energy: Must stay above 10 to continue — rounds drain 5–15 energy each
  • Stamina stat: Higher is better; checks scale up in difficulty
  • Consider bringing: 1× Desire potion (gives +15 stamina to all checks — consumed once, lasts the whole session)
  • Skills that unlock shortcut choices (each allows skipping a stamina check):
    • Seduction, Intimate Communication, Defensive Stance, Power Strike, Exploit Weakness, Pain Tolerance, Counterattack, Adrenaline Rush, Endurance Training, Berserker Rage
  • For Beast Mode ending: Need Erotic Experience 3000+ AND Beast stat 20+

Every round lets you quit early (jumps to Round 17 — the quit/failure ending) or push through via stamina check or skill.

How the Stamina Check Works

Each stamina check rolls 1–100 against your Stamina + modifier + desire_bonus (capped at 95% success). Example: at Round 9 with Stamina 50 and no potion, you'd pass on a roll of 45 or lower (Stamina 50 − 5 modifier = 45%). The desire potion adds +15 to every check for the whole session.

Total Energy Cost (Full Run)

  • Rounds 3–11 (normal path): ~−103 energy total
  • Plan to enter with at least 115+ energy to comfortably clear the standard path without skill shortcuts

Endings

  • Round 12 Normal Ending — Sweet, satisfied finish. Both collapse happily together.
  • Round 16 Beast Ending — Requires Erotic Exp 3000+ AND Beast 20+. Primal, intense, awards extra Beast stat.
  • Round 17 Quit Ending — Abby gets dismissive and disappointed. ("Pathetic. Come back when you've built up some stamina.")

The encounter is fully repeatable once unlocked.

At the Club

Abby uses entirely separate dialogue (club_talk_response, club_dancing image range) — all themed around dancing, rhythm, and being "in the zone." Different outfit, different energy. The club sees her at her most playful and cocky.

Daily Limit Responses

Talk to her too many times in a day and she gets pouty-but-cute ("I love chatting with you, but even I need breaks sometimes!"). Buy her too many drinks at the club and she starts getting suspicious ("Are you trying to get me drunk or something?").

Relationships With Other Characters

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Chocolate is god-tier — it's her favorite gift AND her chocolate_response dialogue gives you unique, adorable reactions. Stockpile chocolate before visiting.
  • The "Challenge Abby" requirement is Stamina 40 via dance lessons — the Dance Studio Confrontation requires 10 studio clout, which you earn +1 at a time per lesson. That's 10 lessons minimum, each requiring Stamina 40.
  • Don't spam talk her over the daily limit — the −1 record deducts from your record. Stay within 3 talks per day at the studio.
  • The Party Responsibility action is one-time and free +1 Ability — don't miss it. Needs Communication 30 and Known stage.
  • Stack favorite stats BEFORE grinding — otherwise you're leaving 3 bonus points per action on the table (doubled by Dance Mastery that's potentially 6 points lost per action).
  • For the Love Hotel encounter, entering with high Stamina and a Desire potion makes every check trivial. Skills like Seduction, Defensive Stance, and Power Strike are the cleanest skips.
  • For Beast Mode ending, build Erotic Experience over multiple Love Hotel runs with different characters before attempting.

加成计算（准确顺序）

好感度公式按以下顺序计算：

  1. 从行动的 基础点数 开始
  2. 每满足一项 偏好属性＋1（耐力40、创造力35、魅力30——最多＋3）
  3. 玩家拥有 舞蹈精通 技能（她的特殊／偏好技能）时 ×2
  4. 玩家拥有 “1 Ring” 关键物品时 ＋1
  5. 古龙水增益生效时 ＋1
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时 ＋1
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，成功则 ＋1（上限50%；持有华丽挂坠时为60%）

计算示例： 巧克力（基础3）、满足全部3项偏好属性，并拥有舞蹈精通技能： (3 + 3) × 2 = 12 points——此外还可能获得戒指／古龙水／缓冲优化／吸引力带来的＋1。

极限优化提示： 在 刷取好感度前，先让耐力达到40、创造力达到35、魅力达到30，并提前学会 舞蹈精通。每项偏好属性加成都会被舞蹈精通翻倍，因此属性投入会带来双倍回报。

推荐推进路线

阶段1：陌生人→已遇见（前期）

  • 下午5点至晚上10点前往舞蹈工作室
  • 每天 和 Abby 交谈 3次
  • 开始将 耐力 提升至40（用于舞蹈课），并将 沟通 提升至30（用于派对责任）

阶段2：已遇见→已知

  • 解锁 送巧克力——每天带来巧克力
  • 达到已知后，解锁 舞蹈课（需要耐力40）

阶段3：已知→朋友／亲密朋友

  • 达到全部三项偏好属性门槛（耐力40、创造力35、魅力30）
  • 完成一次 派对责任（＋1能力、＋8点）
  • 反复参加舞蹈课（基础＋4→加成后更多，每天上限2次）
  • 在 她跳舞的夜晚前往夜店，通过交谈／买饮料获得额外好感度

阶段4：亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 舞蹈室对峙剧情 会自动触发（参见下方任务链）
  • 解锁 挑战Abby 舞蹈比赛行动

阶段5：迷恋→浪漫感→亲密

  • 达到迷恋阶段后，爱情旅馆会解锁 见 Abby 行动（晚上10点至午夜12点、100g、120分钟）
  • 完成爱情旅馆的耐力挑战（参见爱情旅馆邂逅一节）

阶段6：终局（好感度1000以上）

  • 夜店会解锁 极限约会
  • 完成后奖励 ＋100好感度

舞蹈工作室任务链

这是Abby的主要故事内容，也是解锁她动画场景的路线。

第1步：“舞蹈室对峙”（自动触发）

触发条件：

  • 地点：舞蹈与瑜伽工作室（市中心）
  • 时间：中午12点至下午5点
  • 舞蹈工作室派系影响力达到10以上（通过舞蹈课获得——每节课＋1影响力）
  • Lucy和Abby都达到陌生人或更高阶段
  • 尚未设置标记dance_studio_confrontation_completed

剧情梗概： 你走进工作室，发现Lucy和Abby正在争论你的进步。Lucy称赞你的投入；Abby却不以为然，坚持认为跳舞靠的是“胯部、汗水和态度”——也就是天赋，而不是练习。Lucy提议进行一场 友好赌约：如果你能在斗舞中击败Abby，她就必须给你一份“胜利奖励”。

Abby虚张声势地接受赌约，并保证要“把你碾进地板缝里”。

奖励： Lucy和Abby的好感度各＋15。

完成该剧情后，工作室会出现新行动——“挑战Abby”。

第2步：挑战Abby（舞蹈小游戏）

“挑战Abby”行动会启动 舞蹈节奏测试 节奏小游戏。以完美／良好时机击中音符来累积分数。

  • 胜利门槛： 分数达到 14,000以上 会设置challenge_abby_victory标记
  • 可以按需反复挑战小游戏

实用增益：

  • 时间药水（生效中的增益）： 每次击中的分数都获得 ×2倍率——这是可用的最大单项加成
  • 鲁特琴（关键物品）： 判定窗口扩大 ＋5毫秒——完美从±44毫秒扩至±49毫秒，良好从±88毫秒扩至±93毫秒

两者同时生效后，达到14,000分会容易得多——每次完美得200分，每次良好得100分。

第3步：“舞蹈胜利奖励”剧情（获胜后自动触发）

触发条件：

  • 地点：舞蹈与瑜伽工作室
  • 时间：中午12点至下午5点
  • 需要标记：dance_studio_confrontation_completed＋challenge_abby_victory
  • 两项标记均满足时，取得14,000以上分数后立即触发

剧情梗概： Abby被你的表现惊呆，只得承认自己输得心服口服。一向爱撮合人的Lucy提议去后面的储藏室，领取一份非常私人的“糖衣奶油派奖励”。

三个对话选项会影响奖励：

  • 大方： “听起来是很棒的奖励，Abby。”
  • 尊重： “只要你觉得舒服就好。”（Abby额外＋5好感度）
  • 谨慎： “你确定自己想这么做吗？”

基础奖励： Lucy＋20，Abby＋21。 若想获得最高好感度收益，尊重选项是最佳选择。

第4步：解锁“私人奖励会”

完成舞蹈胜利奖励剧情后，工作室会出现一个可重复的新行动：

  • 私人奖励会（45分钟、－30精力、每日上限1次）
  • 储藏室中的视频／动画场景
  • 每次造访获得 ＋35好感度
  • Abby在工作室时可用（下午5点至晚上10点）

爱情旅馆邂逅——耐力挑战

在爱情旅馆与Abby会面（晚上10点至午夜12点、100g），会触发一场围绕耐力管理和精力节省展开的 17轮渐进式亲密邂逅。这是Abby的专属内容，也是游戏中机制较为复杂的亲密场景之一。

准备清单

  • 精力： 必须保持在10以上才能继续——每轮消耗5—15精力
  • 耐力属性： 越高越好；检定难度会逐步提高
  • 建议携带： 1瓶 欲望药水（所有检定＋15耐力——只消耗一次，整场持续生效）
  • 可解锁捷径选项的技能（每项都能跳过一次耐力检定）：
    • 诱惑、亲密沟通、防御姿态、强力打击、弱点利用、疼痛耐受、反击、肾上腺素爆发、耐力训练、狂战士之怒
  • 野兽模式结局要求： 情色经验达到3000以上 且 野兽属性达到20以上

轮次详解

每一轮都可以 提前退出（跳至第17轮——退出／失败结局），也可以通过耐力检定或技能 坚持下去。

耐力检定的运作方式

每次耐力检定都会掷出1—100，并与Stamina + modifier + desire_bonus比较（成功率上限为95%）。例如，在第9轮拥有50耐力且没有药水时，掷出45或以下即可通过（耐力50－修正5＝45%）。欲望药水会在整场会面中为每次检定增加＋15。

精力总消耗（完整流程）

  • 第3—11轮（普通路线）：合计约－103精力
  • 建议入场时至少拥有115以上精力，以便在不使用技能捷径的情况下轻松完成标准路线

结局

  • 第12轮普通结局——甜蜜而满足的收尾。两人幸福地一起瘫倒。
  • 第16轮野兽结局——要求情色经验3000以上且野兽20以上。原始而激烈，并额外奖励野兽属性。
  • 第17轮退出结局——Abby会变得冷淡失望。（“真没用。练出点耐力再回来吧。”）

解锁后，这场邂逅 可以无限重复。

木板路日落约会

Diane的木板路项目完成后，达到 亲密朋友以上 便可在 下午5点至晚上8点 从舞蹈工作室发起该约会。消耗 $50、60分钟和30精力。这是一次性的付费内容，需要较高的赞助者等级。

极限约会

Abby的好感度达到 1000以上 后，会在夜店解锁。消耗240分钟和－50精力。奖励 ＋100好感度。

流程概览

在夜店

Abby会使用完全独立的对话（club_talk_response和club_dancing图片范围）——内容都围绕跳舞、节奏以及“进入状态”展开。服装不同，活力也不同。在夜店里，她会展现出最俏皮、最自信的一面。

达到每日上限后的回应

一天内与她交谈太多次，她会可爱地撅起嘴（“我喜欢和你聊天，但就算是我偶尔也得休息一下！”）。在夜店请她喝太多酒，她则会开始怀疑你的动机（“你该不会是想把我灌醉吧？”）。

与其他角色的关系

  • Lucy（老板、导师）——Abby非常崇拜Lucy，经常赞同地引用她的话。也是Lucy促成了舞蹈挑战／胜利奖励剧情。
  • 舞蹈工作室（派系）——Abby与舞蹈工作室派系相连。每次跟她上舞蹈课都会获得＋1工作室影响力。达到10影响力后会触发对峙剧情。

提示与最佳玩法

  • 巧克力是顶级礼物——它既是她最喜欢的礼物，她的chocolate_response对话也会带来独特而可爱的回应。造访前先囤积巧克力。
  • “挑战Abby”的要求是通过舞蹈课达到耐力40——舞蹈室对峙要求10工作室影响力，而每节课只会获得＋1。因此至少需要上10节课，每节课都要求耐力40。
  • 不要在达到每日上限后继续反复交谈——－1记录会从你的记录中扣除。每天在工作室交谈不要超过3次。
  • 派对责任行动只能完成一次，并会免费提供＋1能力——不要错过。需要沟通30和已知阶段。
  • 刷取好感度前先叠满偏好属性——否则每次行动都会损失3点加成（经舞蹈精通翻倍后，可能每次损失6点）。
  • 进行爱情旅馆邂逅时，带着高耐力和一瓶欲望药水入场，所有检定都会轻而易举。诱惑、防御姿态和强力打击等技能是最直接的跳过方式。
  • 若要取得野兽模式结局，请先通过其他角色的多次爱情旅馆会面积累情色经验，再来挑战。
