Overview

Akira is the doctor at City Hospital (Uptown). Her route begins with a requested examination, continues through research visits and dates, and later opens private time. Her fertility consultations also unlock the shared pregnancy system; those consultations are separate from choosing a family route with her.

Where to find them

At City Hospital (Uptown), enter Dr. Akira’s Office, every day, 06:00–18:59. Return here for the automatic research follow-ups and use her portrait for actions. For Boardwalk Sunset Date, the office schedule makes the practical start window 17:00–18:59, even though the date itself allows a later ending hour.

Begin with Request Exam: 30 minutes and 30 Energy. Come back on a later calendar day for The Results. The first request itself cannot be repeated.

Route and relationship milestones

The examination record controls the return visits. Most follow-ups need a later calendar day than the last recorded examination as well as the listed stage. Standard Psychological Screening is a separate one-time visit after The Results and does not replace the research chain.

Each ordinary gift consumes one item. Flowers give 4 base affection, Books 6 and Wine 7. Sample Collection opens after The Results and gives 10 base affection, once per day. The four dates complete once; Farmers Market Date additionally needs the zoo date, and the fair outing consumes one Voucher rather than money.

Boardwalk Sunset Date also needs Diane’s boardwalk completed and the supporter access indicated by its lock. The menu calls the outing Boardwalk Sunset Date; its destination is Boardwalk.

After Published Findings at Romantic (800), Private Time is available once daily for 60 minutes and 30 Energy. Finish the chosen conversation to receive its rewards; the daily reservation is shared by its branches. While Akira is visibly pregnant or recovering, ordinary private time pauses; the dedicated pregnancy intimacy activity is available during the early/final pregnancy stages for 30 minutes and 20 Energy, once daily.

For the shared pregnancy unlock, enable pregnancy content, finish The Results, then choose Review Fertility Safeguards. After Breakthrough Discovery, complete Study the Waiting Window, then Finalize Pregnancy Protocol. Each consultation takes 30 minutes and completes once. These are the three menu actions for A Deliberate Choice → The Waiting Window → The Pregnancy Protocol.

For Akira’s own optional family route, complete Published Findings and the shared protocol, enable the content, reach 2,000 affection and 500 Akira NPC intimacy experience, and choose the family-planning invitation in Private Time. She must not already be pregnant, recovering or have a child from this route. The Pregnancy guide covers the later visits and timing.

Her favorite stats are Intelligence, Technology, Perception. The learned Researching skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. H2Hs and automatic personal scenes complete once; ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The Results or a research visit is missing: leave and return on a later calendar day after the recorded examination. Raising affection does not remove the wait.
  • Standard Psychological Screening is its own optional follow-up, not the next name in the research sequence.
  • Private Time needs Published Findings as well as Romantic (800), and can only be used once that day.
  • Farmers Market Date needs the completed zoo date. Win Her a Prize needs a Voucher in inventory.
  • For the boardwalk outing, arrive before 19:00 while Akira is still in the office; check Diane’s completed construction and the content lock.
  • Fertility consultations do not themselves start a pregnancy. A family invitation also needs Akira’s NPC intimacy experience, not the player’s Erotic Experience.
  • Talk starts at Acknowledged (10). If it is still locked, finish the available examination follow-ups and Standard Psychological Screening, then use daily conversation when that stage is reached.

概览

Akira是城市医院（上城区）的医生。个人路线从申请检查开始，随后通过研究回访、约会与谈心逐步开放私人相处。她的生育咨询也负责解锁共通生育系统；完成咨询，与选择和她建立家庭，是两件不同的事。

去哪里找

到城市医院（上城区）的“Akira医生办公室”找她，每天06:00–18:59。研究回访在这里触发，其他行动从头像开始。“海滨栈道日落约会”虽然本身允许更晚开始，但受办公室出没时间限制，实际应在17:00–18:59开始。

先选择“请求检查”，消耗30分钟、30精力，再于之后的日期回来获取“检查结果”。首次申请不能重复进行。

“同行评审”会再次记录检查日。“突破性发现”要在较后的日期开始，但它不会重新计算等待日，因此若已达到浪漫感（800），当天也可能继续“发表成果”。“转移规程”是独立谈心，在亲密朋友（200）开放。

达到恋爱中（3000）后，可在办公室完成一次“真正重要的事”，让本轮战地医疗提高1。这个共通支援数值的作用见军队指南。

每次普通送礼消耗一件对应物品：鲜花给4基础好感、书给6、葡萄酒给7。“样本采集”在取得“检查结果”后开放，每天一次，给10基础好感。四场约会各只能完成一次；“农贸市场约会”还要先完成动物园约会，集市赢奖品则消耗1张兑换券，不另收金钱。

“海滨栈道日落约会”还需要Diane已建成木板路，并满足锁形提示所示的支持者内容权限。菜单名称保留“海滨栈道”，目的地在地图上叫“木板路”。

完成“发表成果”并达到浪漫感（800）后，每天可进行一次“私人时光”，消耗60分钟、30精力。完成所选对话后结算奖励，所有分支共用当天次数。显怀或产后恢复期间，普通私人相处会暂停；怀孕前期／末期另有专属亲密活动，每天一次，消耗30分钟、20精力。

解锁共通生育系统时，先开启生育内容并取得“检查结果”，再选择“查看生育安全措施”。完成“突破性发现”后，依次进行“研究等待窗口期”和“完成妊娠规程”。三次咨询各30分钟、各只能完成一次，对应事件顺序为“有意的选择”→“等待之窗”→“妊娠规程”。

若选择Akira自己的可选生育路线，需要完成“发表成果”和共通规程、开启内容，达到2000好感及500点Akira的NPC亲密经验，再在“私人时光”中选择共同要孩子的邀请。她不能已怀孕、处于产后恢复期，或已有这条路线的孩子。后续探望和阶段时间见生育指南。

她偏好的属性为智力、科技、感知。学会研究可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。谈心与个人自动事件只完成一次；普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • “检查结果”或研究回访没出现：在记录检查日之后换一天回来。提高好感不会取消等待。
  • “标准心理筛查”是独立的可选回访，不是研究链下一项的替代名称。
  • “私人时光”同时需要“发表成果”和浪漫感（800），每天只能使用一次。
  • “农贸市场约会”要先完成动物园约会；“替她赢个奖品”则需要随身带着兑换券。
  • 木板路约会要在19:00前、Akira仍在办公室时开始；另检查Diane的建造进度和内容权限。
  • 完成生育咨询不会自动开始怀孕；邀请建立家庭还需要Akira的NPC亲密经验，不能用玩家情趣经验代替。
  • 交谈从已遇见（10）开始；还没开放时，先完成可进行的检查回访与“标准心理筛查”，达到阶段后再用每日交谈培养关系。
