Overview

Robin is a 96-year-old elven master archer who runs the medieval archery range and retreats to her private hunting lodge each evening. Undefeated in competition, she supplies fresh game meat to the community while avoiding the dangerous territory near Dragon's Peak. She's reserved, professional, slow-to-trust — but genuine emotional vulnerability hides beneath the huntress exterior, and the stargazing date reveals she's quietly terrified of facing the dragon that's been hunting her people.

Future Era Connection: Robin is placed in magical stasis by Sashina before the dragon extinction event. She wakes in the Future Era as Artemis — see artemis.md for that walkthrough. The romance arcs are parallel but distinct.

The Forest Tracking Plot — "The Elusive Quarry"

Auto-triggers at medieval_forest_clearing after completing the Join the Hunt H2H conversation.

This is the plot that unlocks Robin's hunting lodge location. Robin has been tracking a strange humanoid creature (later revealed to be Foxy) and shares her frustration with the player.

Three-Choice System — Response to Frustration

  • Caring: "Your patience and skill are unmatched." → +3, image 9 (thankful)
  • Funny: "Maybe it's a ghost fox? Spooky forest specter that steals socks?" → +3, image 23 (smirk, "You're ridiculous. But a bit of levity helps.")
  • Flirty: "The way your eyes light up when you talk about tracking..." → +3, image 21 (shy blush, "Focus. We have a creature to track. Though... I won't pretend I didn't hear that.")

The Reveal

Robin shoots at rustling brush. Her arrow misses for the first time in memory — Foxy dodges and vanishes. Robin's hunter's worldview starts cracking.

Three-Choice System — Reaction to the Sighting

  • Caring: "She seemed more curious than dangerous. Maybe she's just scared." → leads to reflection
  • Funny: "Going in my 'weird things' journal." → Robin's smirk deepens
  • Flirty: "At least we got to witness something incredible together. This moment, with you." → Robin's shy blush, warmest response

Ending

Robin reveals the location of her private hunting lodge east of the main hunting grounds and invites the player to visit:

"If you ever wish to continue our discussions about tracking, or... other matters... you're welcome there."

This opens the lodge as a secondary location where gifts, stargazing, and intimate encounters become possible.

The Foxy Convergence — "A Proper Introduction"

The follow-up plot robin_meets_foxy resolves the creature hunt when Robin corners Foxy for the kill. The player intervenes:

Three-Choice System — Stopping the Shot

  • Caring: "Please, don't shoot — I know her!"
  • Funny: "Whoa whoa whoa! That's not dinner, that's my friend!"
  • Flirty: "Robin, trust me — lower your bow and I'll make it worth your while."

Robin's bow wavers. Foxy speaks in broken English ("F-friend...? Friend here...!"), and Robin realizes she's been hunting a person, not a beast. She admits her mistake — something her character rarely does — and an unlikely friendship forms between the huntress and her former quarry.

Three-Choice System — Introducing Them

  • Gentle: "Foxy is gentle and kind. You'll like her." → warm acceptance
  • Humorous: "Meet the lady who's been playing hide-and-seek with you for weeks!" → Robin concedes Foxy was impressive
  • Charming: "Two of the most remarkable women I know." → Robin blushes, "You are incorrigible."

H2H Conversation — "The Hunt"

Unlocks at Acquaintance stage via the "Join the Hunt" action at the archery range. 90 minutes, −25 energy, daily limit 1.

Four rounds using hunting pose images (hunt_01 through hunt_04). Each round has a standard +3 or +4 choice (three-way: supportive / observational / curious) plus a special skill choice worth +5 or +6 that requires a specific skill the player has learned.

Round 1 — The Invitation

Robin proposes hunting the strange humanoid creature together. Standard choices +3; special choice requires Reading People (+5, "Humanoid but clawed, sometimes quadrupedal... that's an unusual combination") which unlocks a variant.

Round 2 — In the Forest

Robin points out disturbed undergrowth. Standard +3; special requires Animal Care (+5, "These movement patterns suggest it's more curious than predatory").

Round 3 — Dead End

The trail doubles back. Robin admits it's the third time. Standard +4; the third standard option ("Something that clever might be worth befriending rather than hunting") gives +5. Special requires Empathy (+6, "Maybe it's not prey at all. Maybe it's just scared and alone") — unlocks variant.

Round 4 — Bonding Moment

Robin admits she usually hunts alone but enjoyed the day. Standard +4; special requires Empathy (+6, "I think you're better with company than you admit to yourself").

Completion of this H2H is the prerequisite for the forest tracking plot to auto-trigger.

The Emotional Arc

Three scenes across three backgrounds (sunset → night → deeper night). Robin opens up in a way she doesn't with anyone else.

Scene 1 — Sunset: Robin discusses elven "immortality" — the myth vs the truth. Elves age slowly, but no elf has ever died of old age because something always kills them first.

Scene 2 — Dragon Talk: The core revelation — the dragon hunts elves specifically. Robin's clan's numbers have been diminishing for years. Elders who survived centuries are gone in a single night of fire.

Scene 3 — The Confession: Robin admits the secret she's never told anyone:

"My clan wants me to lead the attack on the dragon. I'm their best archer. But when I think about actually facing that thing... I want to run. Disappear. Let someone else be brave." "I've never told anyone this. I'm supposed to be fearless. But I'm terrified. Absolutely terrified."

Three-Choice System — Response to Her Confession

  • Caring: "Being afraid doesn't make you a coward. Acting despite fear — that's courage." → image 17 (surprised) → 10 (happy thankful), then 11 (flirty)
  • Funny: "Coward? You're the smartest person here. Heroes die young. You're playing the long game." → image 23 (smirk), Robin laughs about existential dread
  • Flirty: "You're incredibly brave for telling me this. Now come here." → image 21 (shy) → 14 (seductive) → 15 (most intimate), "Maybe that's what I've been missing. Someone to be afraid with."

Closing

Robin thanks the player for letting her be "just Robin — not the legendary archer, not the fearless huntress. Just... me." Sets up the emotional groundwork for the romantic/intimate stages.

Narrative importance: This date is the key that turns Robin from an admired mentor into a genuine romantic partner. It also foreshadows the dragon conflict that leads to her being put in stasis as Artemis.

Dialogue System — 4-Type Weighted Selection

Robin uses the standard community dialogue system with weighted random selection:

Type Weight Requires

Relationship Dialogue — Progressive Warmth

Ten stages of dialogue from stranger ("You need something at the archery range?") through in_love ("Forever isn't long enough to show you how deeply I love you"). Robin's voice shifts from professional reserve to genuine warmth to romantic vulnerability.

Brothel Dialogue

Stage-matched professional assessments that evolve into personal interest. Robin respects competent management and ethical operations — her dialogue treats the brothel as a legitimate business first, then gradually reveals admiration for the player's leadership.

Worker-Specific Dialogue

Triggers when specific workers are active: Sophie, Sandra, Elara, Jade, Honey, Willow. Robin offers professional assessments of each with quiet respect:

  • Sophie — "Better than her previous circumstances certainly. Your establishment provided her a chance."
  • Sandra — "Her performances have improved since joining. Less financial stress helps creativity."
  • Elara — "Court training serves her well. The King's daughter demonstrates grace under pressure."
  • Jade — "Career advancement through skill application rather than family obligation suits her better."
  • Honey — "She prioritized family necessity. The farm produces better crops with adequate funding."
  • Willow — "Her healing skills improve with superior tools and supplies available."

Gossip Dialogue

Community observations — Gastoff's craftsmanship and crush on Hailey, Ben Arthur's philosophy, the King's taxation, Silas's profit focus, Wilfred's farm struggles. Always closes with personal reflections about the player's impact on the community.

Community Integration

  • Undefeated archer — has never been beaten in competition
  • Meat supplier — provides fresh game to the community
  • Territorial expert — knows every hunting trail in the demesne
  • Gossip web role: "Territorial expert and archery instructor with professional respect for quality work"

Robin is one of the community's most respected professionals. She avoids Dragon's Peak entirely ("Calculated risks only") and references Whispering Woods magic she senses but doesn't understand.

Progression Path — Recommended Order

  1. Stranger → Acknowledged: Visit the archery range, use Talk action (3/day). Reach Acknowledged naturally.
  2. Learn Archery Focus (Perception 15+, $200). Hunting skill unlocked.
  3. Acknowledged → Known: Continue Talk at range. Learn Animal Care (Perception 20+, $280).
  4. Known → Acquaintance: Complete Tracking Wisdom one-time reward (Communication 50+, +1 Perception).
  5. Acquaintance stage: Trigger Join the Hunt H2H. Bring Reading People / Animal Care / Empathy skills for max points.
  6. Auto-trigger "The Elusive Quarry" at medieval_forest_clearing → unlocks lodge location and "A Proper Introduction" with Foxy (if Foxy has been befriended separately).
  7. Acquaintance → Friend: Visit lodge in evenings. Give Teddy Bears (+8 base × Anthropology = +16 effective). Keep Talking.
  8. Friend stage: Complete the Stargazing Date at the lodge (9 PM – 12 AM). Major emotional milestone — unlocks deeper progression.
  9. Friend → Close Friend → Crush: Continue daily Talks, Teddy gifts. Build toward Romantic.
  10. Romantic stage: Intimate Evening at lodge unlocks. Bring Animal Care/Charm 35/Empathy for better entry path (+25 vs +10).
  11. 1000+ points (paid version): Unlock Forest Walk video action at the range.
  12. Beast 30+: Trigger Beast Mode ending during Intimate Evening for +150 erotic, +1 beast.

Tips & Notes

  • Anthropology is her Special Skill — learn it early if possible. Doubles all relationship points earned with Robin (and Artemis in the Future).
  • Teddy Bears stack over time — visit the lodge daily once unlocked. +16 effective points per day with Anthropology.
  • Perception investment pays off — both skill teachings and Tracking Wisdom require Perception gates. Worth building early.
  • Stargazing Date should be saved for an emotionally heavy playthrough moment — it's a single-use scene and sets up the rest of the romance.
  • The Foxy meets plot requires having already befriended Foxy separately through the discovery quest chain. Do that first.
  • Beast stat 30+ is a significant investment — typically built through Raven encounters or monster girl content. Worth it if aiming for maximum Robin content.
  • Forest Walk video requires the paid (Patreon) version — free demo players cap at the Intimate Evening content.
  • Robin has no general favorite gift — don't bother bringing chocolate/flowers/wine to the range. Stick to the Teddy Bear at the lodge.

Future Era Connection

Robin is put in magical stasis by Sashina before the dragon extinction event — a mercy to preserve at least one elf. She wakes in the Future Era centuries later to find her entire race extinct, takes the name Artemis, and fights alongside Ghost's resistance against VOID.

The Stargazing Date foreshadows this: Robin's clan wants her to lead the attack on the dragon. She's terrified. The outcome of that attack — and Sashina's intervention — becomes the Artemis arc.

Mechanical connection:

  • Same Favorite/Special Skill (Anthropology) — learning it once benefits both
  • Same three favorite stats (Ability / Perception / Stamina) — but thresholds jump from 50/45/55 to 80/80/80 in the Future
  • Both relationship systems begin at Stranger. Artemis remembers the player, but her present-day trust and consent are rebuilt from the beginning
  • Artemis has a complete post-recruitment route: 30 portraits, ten main plots, two dates, three H2Hs, skill training, and a TD Armory/Barracks schedule

See walkthroughs/artemis.md for the Future Era counterpart.

概述

Robin 是一位 96 岁的精灵神射手，经营中世纪射箭场，每晚则回到私人狩猎小屋。她在比赛中从未落败，也会为社区供应新鲜猎物，但始终避开龙峰附近的危险区域。她寡言、专业且不轻易信任他人；然而，猎手外表下藏着真切的脆弱。观星约会中，她会坦白自己其实非常害怕面对那条一直猎杀其族人的巨龙。

未来时代关联： 巨龙灭绝事件发生前，Sashina 将 Robin 置于魔法静滞状态。她在未来时代醒来后成为 Artemis——相关攻略见 artemis.md。两条恋爱线彼此呼应，但各自独立。

森林追踪剧情——“难寻的猎物”

完成 加入狩猎 H2H 谈话后，会在 medieval_forest_clearing 自动触发。

该剧情会解锁 Robin 的 狩猎小屋地点。Robin 一直在追踪一种奇怪的人形生物（后来揭晓为 Foxy），并向玩家吐露挫败感。

三选一系统——回应挫败感

  • 关怀：“你的耐心和技巧无人能及。”→ +3，图像 9（感激）
  • 幽默：“也许是幽灵狐狸？会偷袜子的诡异森林幽灵？”→ +3，图像 23（坏笑，“你真荒唐。不过轻松一下确实有帮助。”）
  • 调情：“你谈起追踪时，眼睛发亮的样子……”→ +3，图像 21（羞红，“专心。我们还要追踪生物。不过……我不会假装没听见。”）

真相揭晓

Robin 朝摇动的灌木射箭，竟有生以来第一次射失——Foxy 躲开并消失。Robin 的猎人世界观开始动摇。

三选一系统——回应目击事件

  • 关怀：“她似乎只是好奇，并不危险。也许她只是害怕。”→ 引发反思
  • 幽默：“我要把它记进‘怪事日志’。”→ Robin 笑意更深
  • 调情：“至少我们一起见证了不可思议的事。此时此刻，与你在一起。”→ Robin 害羞脸红，是最温暖的回应

结尾

Robin 告诉玩家，她的 私人狩猎小屋位于主要猎场以东，并邀请玩家造访：

“若你还想继续讨论追踪，或者……其他事情……欢迎来那里找我。”

小屋由此成为次要地点，可在此赠礼、观星和进行亲密互动。

Foxy 汇合剧情——“正式介绍”

后续剧情 robin_meets_foxy 中，Robin 把 Foxy 逼入绝境，准备射杀她。玩家出面阻止：

三选一系统——阻止射击

  • 关怀：“请别射——我认识她！”
  • 幽默：“等等等等！那不是晚餐，是我的朋友！”
  • 调情：“Robin，相信我——放下弓，我会让你觉得值得。”

Robin 的弓开始动摇。Foxy 用不流利的英语说：“朋、朋友……？这里……朋友！”Robin 意识到自己猎杀的是人，而非野兽。她罕见地承认错误，猎手与昔日猎物之间由此形成意外的友谊。

三选一系统——介绍双方

  • 温和：“Foxy 温柔又善良，你会喜欢她的。”→ 温暖接纳
  • 幽默：“来认识一下这位陪你玩了几周捉迷藏的女士！”→ Robin 承认 Foxy 很厉害
  • 迷人：“我认识的两位最非凡的女性。”→ Robin 脸红：“你真是无可救药。”

H2H 谈话——“狩猎”

相识阶段解锁，在射箭场通过“加入狩猎”行动开始。耗时 90 分钟，−25 精力，每日上限 1。

共 4 回合，使用狩猎姿势图像（hunt_01 至 hunt_04）。每回合都有 +3 或 +4 的普通三选一（支持／观察／好奇），以及一个 价值+5或+6的特殊技能选项，需要玩家已学会特定技能。

第 1 回合——邀请

Robin 提议一起追踪奇怪的人形生物。普通选项 +3；特殊选项需要 识人术（+5，“人形却有利爪，有时四足行走……这种组合很不寻常”），可解锁变体。

第 2 回合——森林中

Robin 指出被扰动的灌木。普通选项 +3；特殊选项需要 动物照料（+5，“这些行动模式说明它更好奇，而非更具攻击性”）。

第 3 回合——死路

足迹折返回去，Robin 承认这已是第三次。普通选项 +4；第三个普通选项（“如此聪明的生物，也许值得结交，而非猎杀”）+5。特殊选项需要 同理心（+6，“也许它根本不是猎物，只是害怕而孤独”），可解锁变体。

第 4 回合——建立羁绊

Robin 承认自己通常独自狩猎，但很享受这一天。普通选项 +4；特殊选项需要 同理心（+6，“我觉得你其实比自己愿意承认的更需要陪伴”）。

完成此 H2H 是森林追踪剧情自动触发的前置条件。

约会——观星约会

  • 地点： 狩猎小屋后方（视野绝佳的私人空地）
  • 时段： 晚上 9 点至午夜 12 点
  • 时长： 120 分钟，−40 精力
  • 要求： 朋友阶段+
  • 可见性： 设置 stargazing_robin_completed 标记后隐藏（一次性剧情）

情感历程

三段场景分别使用三种背景（日落→夜晚→深夜）。Robin 展现出从未向他人流露的一面。

场景 1——日落： Robin 谈论精灵“永生”的传说与真相。精灵衰老缓慢，却没有精灵真正寿终正寝，因为总有其他事物先夺走他们的生命。

场景 2——谈论巨龙： 核心真相是——巨龙会专门猎杀精灵。Robin 的氏族多年来人数不断减少，活过数百年的长者也会在一夜龙火中消逝。

场景 3——坦白： Robin 说出从未告诉任何人的秘密：

“我的氏族希望我带队攻打巨龙。我是他们最优秀的弓箭手。但一想到真正面对那东西……我只想逃跑、消失，让别人去勇敢。” “我从未对任何人说过。我应该无所畏惧，可我害怕得不得了。真的怕极了。”

三选一系统——回应她的坦白

  • 关怀：“害怕不会让你变成懦夫。心怀恐惧仍采取行动——那才是勇气。”→ 图像 17（惊讶）→ 10（开心感激），再到 11（调情）
  • 幽默：“懦夫？你是这里最聪明的人。英雄早逝，你是在放长线。”→ 图像 23（坏笑），Robin 笑着谈起存在主义恐惧
  • 调情：“你愿意告诉我这些，已经勇敢得不可思议。现在，到我身边来。”→ 图像 21（害羞）→ 14（诱惑）→ 15（最亲密），“也许我缺少的正是这个——一个能陪我一起害怕的人。”

收尾

Robin 感谢玩家让她可以“只是 Robin——不是传奇弓箭手，也不是无畏猎手，只是……我自己。”这为浪漫感／亲密阶段奠定情感基础。

叙事意义： 这场约会是 Robin 从受人敬仰的导师转变为真正恋爱伴侣的关键，也预示了最终令她进入静滞状态、成为 Artemis 的巨龙冲突。

关系对话——逐渐升温

青楼对话

按阶段变化的专业评价逐渐转为个人兴趣。Robin 尊重称职管理和合乎道德的经营；她先把青楼当作正当生意，随后逐渐流露对玩家领导能力的欣赏。

员工专属对话

在 Sophie、Sandra、Elara、Jade、Honey、Willow 工作时触发。Robin 对每个人都给出带有低调敬意的专业评价：

  • Sophie——“至少比她过去的处境好。你的机构给了她机会。”
  • Sandra——“加入后，她的表演进步了。经济压力减轻有助于创造力。”
  • Elara——“宫廷训练让她受益良多。国王的女儿在压力下仍举止优雅。”
  • Jade——“靠技能而非家族义务发展事业更适合她。”
  • Honey——“她优先考虑家庭需求。有充足资金后，农场的收成更好了。”
  • Willow——“有了更好的工具和物资，她的治疗技艺也在进步。”

闲谈对话

社区观察包括 Gastoff 的手艺和对 Hailey 的暗恋、Ben Arthur 的哲学、国王的税收、Silas 的逐利心以及 Wilfred 的农场困境，结尾总会带上对玩家如何影响社区的个人感想。

社区联系

  • 不败弓箭手——从未在比赛中落败

推荐推进顺序

  1. 陌生人→已遇见： 前往射箭场，每天交谈 3 次，自然达到已遇见。
  2. 学习弓术专注（感知 15+，$200），解锁狩猎技能。
  3. 已遇见→已知： 继续交谈。学习动物照料（感知 20+，$280）。
  4. 已知→相识： 完成一次性 追踪智慧 奖励（沟通 50+，感知 +1）。
  5. 相识阶段： 触发 加入狩猎 H2H。携带识人术／动物照料／同理心可取得最高点数。
  6. 在 medieval_forest_clearing 自动触发“难寻的猎物” →解锁小屋，以及与 Foxy 的“正式介绍”（需另行结识 Foxy）。
  7. 相识→朋友： 晚间造访小屋。赠送泰迪熊（基础 +8×人类学＝实际 +16），并继续交谈。
  8. 朋友阶段： 晚上 9 点至午夜 12 点在小屋完成 观星约会，达成重大情感里程碑。
  9. 朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋： 继续每日交谈和赠送泰迪熊，向浪漫感推进。
  10. 浪漫感阶段： 解锁小屋的 亲密之夜。拥有动物照料／魅力 35／同理心可取得更优开场（+25 而非 +10）。
  11. 1000+ 点（付费版本）： 射箭场解锁 森林漫步 视频行动。
  12. 兽性 30+： 亲密之夜中触发兽性模式结局，获得 +150 情欲经验、兽性 +1。

提示与备注

  • 人类学是她的特殊技能——尽早学习，会让从 Robin（以及未来的 Artemis）处获得的所有好感度翻倍。
  • 泰迪熊可以日积月累——小屋解锁后每天造访；拥有人类学时每天实际 +16。
  • 投资感知很划算——两项教学和追踪智慧都有感知门槛，值得前期培养。
  • 观星约会适合留到情感沉重的游玩节点——该场景只能进行一次，并为后续恋爱铺路。
  • Foxy 相遇剧情要求已通过发现任务链另行结识 Foxy，请先完成。
  • 野兽 30+ 投入不小，通常通过 Raven 邂逅或魔物娘内容培养；若追求 Robin 全部内容则值得投入。
  • 森林漫步视频需要付费（Patreon）版本——免费试玩玩家的上限是亲密之夜内容。
  • Robin 没有通用的最爱礼物——不必带巧克力／鲜花／葡萄酒去射箭场，只需在小屋赠送泰迪熊。

未来时代关联

巨龙灭绝事件发生前，Sashina 将 Robin 置于 魔法静滞状态，希望至少保住一名精灵。数百年后，她在未来时代醒来，发现整个种族都已灭绝。她改名为 Artemis，与 Ghost 的反抗军并肩对抗 VOID。

观星约会预示了这一切：Robin 的氏族希望她带队攻打巨龙，而她心中无比恐惧。那场战斗的结果以及 Sashina 的干预，最终构成 Artemis 的故事线。

机制关联：

  • 相同的 最喜欢／特殊技能（人类学）——学习一次，两人都能受益
  • 相同的三项偏好属性（能力／感知／耐力），但未来时代门槛由 50／45／55 提高至 80／80／80
  • Robin 从陌生人开始；Artemis 从已知开始（她记得一千年前的玩家）
  • Artemis 拥有全部 10 阶段对白，但目前只实装 3 张图像和 1 个交谈行动

未来时代对应攻略见 walkthroughs/artemis.md。
