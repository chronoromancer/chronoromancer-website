Overview

Momosagi is recruited through a hidden passage in the ordinary dungeon. Afterward she has a short evening portrait schedule, while her worker management and vacation content use their own menus.

Where to find Momosagi

Own the brothel and raise Beast to 20. On ordinary dungeon floor 2, explore the hidden passage at column 3, row 11 when counting the top-left tile as 1,1. Complete the rescue to recruit her.

Her portrait appears in the brothel Master Bedroom on even-numbered days, 21:00–23:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Recruitment awards 10 base relationship points. Her resort Sunset Walk is a separate vacation heart-to-heart. At In Love (3000), her two later training-upgrade scenes become available; one also requires that Sunset Walk.

Shared worker encounters and her training upgrades do not require repeating the rescue. Use the worker and resort guides for their common conditions and equipment requirements.

Automatic encounters: complete start conditions

Enter the indicated location during its scene window. These encounters complete once; fixed base relationship rewards below are separate from choice bonuses. A missing hour restriction means the scene itself has no extra clock gate.

Activities and rewards

Talk, Pet and Feed each take 15 minutes, with separate allowances of 80 safe uses daily. Their base relationship rewards are 1, 2 and 2 respectively. Feed consumes one Food; without Food it gives no successful feeding reward.

The practical limit on her ordinary portrait is time: an unmodified two-hour evening allows eight 15-minute actions. Praise, paid worker visits, training and vacation activities have separate costs and limits.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

The hidden passage checks Beast, not the Taming stat. If her portrait is missing after recruitment, check the date’s parity, 21:00–23:00 window, worker status and any active vacation. Bring Food before selecting Feed; the attempt still uses time when food is missing.

概览

Momosagi通过普通地下城的隐藏通道招募。之后她的个人头像只在较短的晚间时段出现，员工管理和度假内容则使用各自的入口。

出现地点与时间

先拥有青楼，并将兽性提升到20。探索普通地下城第2层的隐藏通道：以上左角为第1列、第1行计，入口在第3列、第11行。完成救援后即可招募。

她在双数日21:00—23:00出现在青楼主人卧室。

地点头像出现时间条件
主卧双数日，21:00–23:00已招募且员工启用

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

招募奖励10点基础关系。度假中的日落漫步是独立的假期谈心。达到恋爱中（3000）后，两项后期训练升级事件会开放，其中一项还要求完成日落漫步。

员工共同事件和训练升级不需要重复救援；共通条件与装备要求见员工及度假指南。

行动与奖励

交谈、抚摸、喂食各消耗15分钟，每种动作各有每日80次安全额度，基础关系奖励分别为1、2、2。喂食会消耗一份食物；没有食物时不能获得成功喂食的奖励。

普通头像的实际限制是时间：不受时间修正时，两小时晚间最多容纳八次15分钟动作。赞扬、付费员工拜访、训练与假期活动都有独立的消耗和限制。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

隐藏通道检查兽性，不检查驯服属性。招募后缺少头像时，请确认日期为双数、时间在21:00—23:00、员工仍启用，以及是否正在度假。选择喂食前先准备食物；缺少食物的尝试仍会耗时。
