Reach the Hollow and choose a purchase

Travel to Medieval → Village Outskirts → Whispering Woods → Darkened Path → Sylwithe’s Hollow. Darkened Path and the final approach each take 5 minutes. Entering the Hollow requires the game’s paid-content access; there is no relationship or guild-clout prerequisite. Sylwithe is present all day, every day.

The shop sells immediate Crystal purchases, including ordinary equipment upgrades and the newer Master Equipment upgrade. It also offers the Enchanted Harp, maid/worker accessories and Tempest Strike training. Bring Crystals for purchases; the skill lesson additionally needs a very large wealth and resource budget.

To leave, choose Leave to return to Darkened Path, then Return to Woods. Each return leg also takes 5 minutes.

Four equipment upgrade routes

PurchaseCrystals per levelResultCap / additional condition
Upgrade Weapon10Stored melee weapon level +110
Upgrade Armor10Stored armor level +110
Upgrade Ranged10Stored ranged weapon level +110
Master Equipment 0→1, then onward10Stored proficiency-equipment level +110; requires current-cycle Proficiency Upgrade and applicable access

Every purchase costs no game time and adds exactly one stored level. There is no requirement to reach Gastoff’s cap first. Using cheaper wealth/material upgrades where available can save Crystals; Forge pieces and other effective armor bonuses are separate from the main armor’s stored level.

Budget 10 × (target level − current stored level) Crystals per slot. From level 5 to 10 costs 50 per slot. Upgrading three level 7 slots to 10 costs 90 in total. These upgrades do not raise the Grimoire.

For Master Equipment, buy Proficiency Upgrade from Lillian or Seraph first if eligible. The shop checks that upgrade in the current New Game+ cycle. Retained high equipment levels alone do not satisfy it. Level is also the eligible career-income multiplier: ordinary career equipment uses at most ×5; current Master ownership and access permit up to ×10. It does not add an extra independent ×2. The cap and reset interaction are explained in Wealth Guide and New Game+.

Accessories and Enchanted Harp

The Guidebook and Badge belong in the maid system; the Pendant belongs in worker equipment. Ownership alone does not activate an equippable accessory. Multiple purchases of the Pendant are useful for different workers, rather than stacking copies in one worker’s slot.

The Harp needs no equipment slot. Its phantom contribution stacks with relevant bonuses such as Celestial Trumpet, but still needs an active Charm job and its normal inputs. It does not create a job, produce real sylphs or help unrelated production. See Kupie’s Love Training and Maid System.

Tempest Strike training and combat effect

RepeatabilityOne lesson; hidden after Tempest Strike is learned

The lesson teaches Tempest Strike and grants +30 base relationship. No high relationship stage is required. Stormslash must be learned separately to use the benefit: it is the Ability 22 combat technique. The lesson does not explicitly require Stormslash to appear, and Tempest Strike does not create a separate attack button.

When you use Stormslash with Tempest Strike learned, the opening hit gains +5% damage and a lightning-element check. Lightning weakness doubles that stage; lightning resistance halves it. If the target survives, extra lightning hits can chain against the same target.

Chain continuation reference

The first extra-hit check is 50%, falling by 5 percentage points after each success:50%, 45%, 40% …5%. A failure or defeated target stops the chain. Extra hits pass through the applicable combat guard and class calculations; an active Luck Potion can rescue at most one failed continuation in the chain. It is not a multi-enemy attack.

Spending priorities and missing actions

Use ordinary forging or dungeon blacksmith upgrades before Crystals when their materials are easier to spare. Finish the equipment slot you need rather than assuming every slot must reach 10 immediately. Budget recovery around the long skill lesson.

If the entrance is blocked, check the displayed content-access message. If a purchase is missing, check its stored level, Crystal balance, owned-copy limit and—in the Master Equipment case—this cycle’s Proficiency Upgrade. If an accessory seems inactive, check the correct maid/worker equipment slot. Sylwithe’s ordinary Talk takes 15 minutes, gives+1 base relationship and allows 7 talks per day; it is optional for shopping.

进入幽谷并选择购买项目

路线为中世纪 → 村庄郊外 → 低语森林 → 幽暗小径 → Sylwithe的幽谷。「幽暗小径」与最后进入幽谷的路程各耗5分钟。幽谷入口需要游戏的付费内容访问权限，不要求关系等级或公会声望。Sylwithe每天全天都在。

这里可用水晶立即升级装备，包括普通装备和新增的大师装备升级；还出售附魔竖琴、女仆／员工配件，并提供暴风打击训练。普通购买主要准备水晶；技能训练还需要大量财富、时间和当前资源。

离开时先选「离开」回到小径，再选「返回森林」。两段返程同样各耗5分钟。

四类装备升级

购买项目每级水晶结果上限／额外条件
升级武器10已保存的近战武器等级+110
升级护甲10已保存的主护甲等级+110
升级远程武器10已保存的远程武器等级+110
大师装备0→1，之后逐级购买10职业装备等级+110；须已购买本轮熟练度升级，并具备相应访问权限

每次购买不推进时间，只增加一级已保存的装备等级，不要求先到达Gastoff的升级上限。能用财富和材料便宜升级时，先走那些路线可以节约水晶。锻造部件及其他有效护甲加成，与主护甲已保存的等级分开计算。

每个部位可按10×（目标等级−当前等级）估算水晶。5级升到10级，每个部位需要50枚；三个7级部位都升到10级，合计90枚。这里的升级不会提高魔导书等级。

大师装备需先在符合条件时向Lillian或Seraph购买「熟练度升级」。商店检查的是本轮新游戏+是否已购买；仅保留了高等级装备并不足够。职业装备等级也是适用职业工作的收入倍率：普通状态最多×5，本轮大师升级与访问权限都满足时，最多×10；不会另外再独立乘一次×2。详见财富指南和新游戏+。

购买项目水晶效果持有与使用

精通指南和经理徽章属于女仆装备，禅意吊坠属于员工装备。需要装备的配件，单纯放在库存里不会生效。重复购买吊坠适合分配给不同员工，不是在同一人的装备栏叠放多件。

附魔竖琴不占装备栏，可与适用的魅力生产加成叠加，但仍须有正在运作的魅力工作，并提供正常生产所需的材料。它不会自动创建工作、增加真实小天使，或提高无关工作的产量；天界号角则作用于爱心工作。详见Kupie 的爱之训练与女仆系统。

重复性只需学习一次；学会暴风打击后隐藏

训练会教会暴风打击，并增加基础关系30，不需要先刷到高关系阶段。要实际获得效果，仍须另外学会风暴斩——这是能力22的战斗技能。训练动作本身没有把风暴斩列为显示条件，而学会暴风打击也不会新增独立攻击按钮。

已学会暴风打击时使用风暴斩，起始一击会增加5%伤害，并进行雷电属性判定：敌人弱雷时该阶段翻倍，抗雷时减半。如果目标仍存活，还可能对同一个目标连续追加雷击。

连击判定参考

第一次追加判定为50%，每次成功后下降5个百分点，即50%、45%、40%……直到5%。判定失败或目标被击败便停止。追加攻击仍会经过适用的防御与职业计算；幸运药水在同一连击中最多挽救一次失败判定。这不是同时攻击多名敌人的招式。

消费顺序与动作缺失

普通锻造或地下城铁匠所需材料更容易筹集时，可以先用它们升级，再花水晶补足。优先完成当前需要的部位，不必立即把每一项都升到10级。长时间技能训练前，也要安排好资源恢复。

入口受阻时，查看游戏显示的内容权限提示。购买项目消失时，依次检查当前已保存等级、水晶、同款持有限制；大师装备还要检查本轮「熟练度升级」。配件没效果时，确认是否装在正确的女仆／员工装备栏。普通「与Sylwithe交谈」耗15分钟、基础关系+1，每日额度7次；购物并不要求先完成这些交谈。
