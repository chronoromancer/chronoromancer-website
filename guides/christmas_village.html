Guide noticeThis page contains gameplay and story spoilers and may describe mature systems.

Christmas Village is a permanent, player-controlled event hub in the Future Fringe Zone. It contains the Claw Machine, Candy Cane collection, Christmas Tokens, three full relationship routes, 25 cross-world Christmas tasks, 11 special Gift scenes, Christmas Hold'em integration, Extras milestones, and several permanent rewards.

This walkthrough was verified against the current game implementation. It includes task requirements, reward mechanics, Claw Machine weights, and current-build quirks, so it contains extensive spoilers.

Important current-build note: Christmas Village is not restricted to December and does not require a redeem code. Its entrance is always registered at the Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station. The Christmas Spirit toggle activates the game-wide event layer; it does not unlock or remove the village itself.

Quick Completion Route

For a reliable start and an efficient route through the whole system:

  1. Unlock normal travel to the Future era.
  2. Go to Future → Fringe Zone → Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station.
  3. Select Christmas Village. The first visit automatically plays Welcome to Christmas Village, gives +10 relationship with Ginger, Holly, and Noella, lets one of them receive another +5 through your choice, and awards 1 Christmas Token.
  4. Select Toggle Christmas Spirit until the Christmas counter strip appears at the lower-right of the location screen. You can also switch Christmas: ON/OFF from the Relationships screen.
  5. Visit all three residents. They are present 24 hours a day:
    • Ginger — Gingerbread House
    • Holly — Winter Wonderland
    • Noella — Mistletoe Lodge
  6. Click the Christmas checklist icon beside the counters and review Holly's Christmas Tasks. The checklist tracks 25 tasks spread throughout the game.
  7. Collect the two nearby Candy Canes in Christmas Village and Mistletoe Lodge, then collect the other 21 world spawns as you travel.
  8. Spend the introductory Token at the Claw Machine if you want an early chance at Christmas Spirit, Christmas Wreath, a Crystal, or a Gift. The roll is random and can miss.
  9. Raise all three Christmas residents. Every relationship stage from Known through Intimate gives a +1 Token plot; In Love gives +2.
  10. Give Claw Machine Hot Chocolate to a resident after Known for a strong +10 base relationship action.
  11. Trade Candy Canes with Ginger in batches of 10. Each batch gives 1 Token and +10 base Ginger relationship. Continue until the lifetime traded counter reaches 150 to trigger all five candy milestones.
  12. Complete the 25 tasks. Tasks 18 → 19 → 20 → 25 form a strict Search-and-Dungeon chain and should be done in that order.
  13. Raise eligible non-Christmas NPCs to Close Friend, win Gifts from the Claw Machine, and use each Gift at the NPC's required location. Each Gift scene gives +100 relationship and completes an Extras entry.
  14. Ask Noella to Check the List. Visit her again when Extras completion reaches 15%, 30%, 45%, and 60%. At 80%, use Noella's Reward for her repeatable final scene and +2 Tokens.
  15. Keep playing the Claw Machine until you have the one-time Christmas Spirit skill and Christmas Wreath key item. If you own the brothel, also pursue the Velvet Ribbon and eligible worker outfit upgrades.

The system has no single all-purpose finale flag. True completion means clearing the individual checklists in the 100% Completion Checklist.

Access and the Christmas Toggle

How to reach the village

Use this route:

Future → Fringe Zone → Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station → Christmas Village

The entrance action costs no in-game time. There is no Christmas redeem code, real-world calendar check, relationship requirement, or separate quest unlock.

What Toggle Christmas Spirit actually controls

Turning Christmas Spirit ON enables:

  • The Christmas resource counter strip and 25-task checklist button.
  • Clickable Candy Cane pickups at 23 locations.
  • All Christmas-task actions that are otherwise hidden.
  • Special Gift actions for eligible Close Friend NPCs.
  • The stranded Christmas elf Search encounter for Task 18.
  • Tinsel's progressive dungeon encounters for Tasks 19, 20, and 25.

Turning it OFF hides or suspends those event-layer activities. It does not erase progress, consume resources, remove permanent rewards, or make the village disappear.

The following remain accessible even with the toggle off in the current build:

  • Christmas Village and its three sub-locations.
  • Ginger, Holly, and Noella's ordinary relationship actions.
  • Ginger's Candy Cane trade, if you already hold at least 10 canes.
  • The Claw Machine and its odds screen, if you already have Tokens.
  • Noella's Extras actions.

The toggle is stored as a persistent setting, so its state can carry between saves. If event actions seem to be missing, confirm that the counter strip is visible rather than assuming the event has ended.

Candy Cane Collection

Pickup rules

With Christmas Spirit ON, a small Candy Cane appears as a clickable object at each eligible location.

  • Each pickup gives 1 Candy Cane.
  • Each location has its own cooldown.
  • A collected cane becomes eligible again on current day + 2.
  • Revisit the location after the cooldown so the game can refresh that spawn.
  • There are 23 spawn locations, allowing 23 canes per complete fresh sweep.

One full sweep therefore funds two Ginger trades, leaving three canes toward the next batch.

Claw Machine

Basic rules

  • Location: Christmas Village square.
  • Cost: 1 Christmas Token.
  • Time: 5 minutes per play.
  • Result: one weighted roll from the currently eligible prize pool.
  • Use View Prize Odds beside the machine to see the current calculated percentages.

The pool changes as you win one-time prizes, activate brothel workers, reach worker outfit caps, own the brothel, or exhaust the free Gift allowance. Percentages therefore cannot be represented by one permanent table.

Worker Clothing Upgrade rules

Only active workers in the machine's supported outfit list can be rolled. A worker contributes weight only while below clothing level 5 and below the per-worker Claw win cap.

  • Free build: 1 Claw clothing win per worker.
  • Premium build: 2 Claw clothing wins per worker.
  • Each eligible worker contributes weight 3 separately.
  • The prize name identifies the worker whose outfit was upgraded.

Supported workers are:

Elara, Sophie, Honey, Sandra, Grace, Raven, Shadow, Foxy, Bellavia, Valkyra, Lindia, Ava & Eva, Gem, Jade, Brandy, Willow, Vixen, Kasumi, A.R.I.A., Vespa, Audrey, Whiskers, Momosagi, Maize, and Pepper.

Current odds-screen omission

The real prize pool adds Velvet Ribbon at weight 3 when the brothel is owned. The current odds display builds that entry but does not include Velvet Ribbon in the final list it renders. As a result, the shown percentages use the Ribbon's weight in the denominator but do not show a Ribbon row. The prize can still be won.

Christmas Hold'em and Task 10

Christmas Hold'em is played from Present Downtown → Bar → Play Table Games while the bar is open from 20:00 to 03:00.

  • Deck: 52 standard cards plus 2 wild Jokers.
  • Best possible rank: Five of a Kind.
  • Antes: 10, 50, or 100 Wealth.
  • Vera begins with a separate $2,500 poker bankroll.
  • Each hand costs 5 minutes, or 3 minutes with the Time buff.
  • A winning hand gives +2 base Vera relationship and records the gambling result.
  • Drain Vera's poker bankroll to 0 to complete Christmas Task 10.
  • Task completion gives +1 Token and +20 direct Vera relationship.
  • Vera's poker bankroll resets on a new in-game day.

Use the highest ante your own bankroll can safely support. The Task checks Vera's remaining poker bankroll, not your lifetime net winnings against her.

For full hand rankings, Joker logic, betting behavior, and Vera's other gambling gates, see Downtown Bar Games.

Tinsel Dungeon Chain

Tasks 18, 19, 20, and 25 must be completed in sequence.

Step 1 — Task 18

Turn Christmas ON, go to the Present Downtown Back Alley, and use Search.

The stranded elf is added to the area's encounter pool. Its listed 90% chance is applied after that encounter has been selected from the larger pool, so 90% is not the overall chance per Search. Repeat the action until the scene appears.

Step 2 — Task 19

After Task 18, enter the dungeon and explore at floor 18 or deeper with Christmas ON.

Tinsel Encounter 1 gets a 40% roll when its eligible scenario check occurs. Completing it awards 1 Token and enables the next encounter.

Step 3 — Task 20

After Task 19, explore at floor 21 or deeper.

Tinsel Encounter 2 also gets a 40% eligible roll. It awards 1 Token and unlocks the Krampus encounter.

Step 4 — Task 25

After Task 20, explore at floor 23 or deeper.

The Krampus scenario gets a 50% eligible roll. Krampus has:

  • Base HP 150 plus floor/level scaling.
  • Strong element: Dark.
  • Weak element: Light.
  • 35% magic resistance.
  • 15% damage reduction.
  • A 60% chance to absorb Ice spells and a 50% chance to absorb Dark spells; a successful absorption heals Krampus for the spell's full resolved damage.
  • Armor-breaking physical attacks and a telegraphed Chain Storm.

Bring strong Light damage, healing, and durable armor. The fight prevents normal boss-flight shortcuts. Winning completes Task 25 and awards 3 Tokens.

If no Tinsel event appears, verify both the previous task and the Christmas toggle before assuming the random roll is broken.

At 80% Extras completion, Noella's Reward becomes visible.

  • Cost: 30 Energy and 30 minutes.
  • Plot: The Complete List.

  • The plot is explicitly repeatable.

The action declares a daily limit of 3. In the current implementation, its custom handler does not record the action in Noella's daily-action log, so the declared cap may not be enforced. Energy is therefore the practical limiter in affected builds. This is a current-build quirk, not a recommended balance assumption for future versions.

Permanent Rewards

Christmas Spirit

  • Source: one-time Claw Machine prize, weight 10 while unowned.
  • Type: permanent Social skill.
  • Effect here: serves as Ginger, Holly, and Noella's special skill, doubling their relationship-point calculations.
  • Once learned, it leaves the Claw pool permanently.

This is unrelated to the Christmas ON/OFF setting despite sharing the same name. The skill is a permanent player reward; the toggle is a persistent event control.

Christmas Wreath

  • Source: one-time Claw Machine prize.
  • Weight: 2 free, 7 premium while unowned.
  • Type: permanent key item.
  • Effect: +5% brothel income and +5% brothel reputation.

The current brothel calculations check only whether the key item is owned. They do not require Christmas to be ON. The Wreath works throughout the year and remains active when the event layer is toggled off.

Velvet Ribbon

  • Source: Claw Machine at weight 3 while the brothel is owned.
  • Type: rare equippable worker item.
  • Effect on assigned worker: +15% income and +3 Appearance.
  • It is not one-time; further copies can be won.

Worker clothing levels

Claw outfit prizes permanently raise the chosen worker's clothing level. Turning Christmas off does not revert the upgrade. The Claw-specific one- or two-win limit is separate from the worker's overall maximum clothing level of 5.

Other persistent results

  • Crystals and Wealth are immediate permanent gains.
  • Teddy, Lingerie, and Jewelry enter the normal consumable inventory.
  • Completed Gift scenes and task scenes remain available through their Extras records where applicable.
  • Relationship points, plot flags, Candy Cane lifetime totals, and task completion flags persist.

Recommended Farming Strategy

Early game

  1. Trigger the village intro for the free Token and relationship head start.
  2. Turn Christmas ON.
  3. Collect easy canes whenever they overlap your current route.
  4. Talk to all three residents up to their safe daily limit.
  5. Use the first Token if you accept the risk of a miss. Christmas Spirit is the best early long-term hit.
  6. Begin low-requirement tasks: 1, 6, 7, 13, 14, and 18 become available relatively early once their relationships are raised.

Mid game

  1. Prioritize Ginger trades. They simultaneously produce Tokens, Ginger relationship, and lifetime milestone progress.
  2. Feed Hot Chocolate to whichever resident is furthest behind.
  3. Return to each home whenever a relationship stage changes so Token plots do not queue unnoticed.
  4. Clear Tasks 18 and 19 as soon as dungeon floor 18 is comfortable.
  5. Raise the 11 Gift recipients to Close Friend before spending scarce free-version Gift access.
  6. Own and develop the brothel before heavily farming the Claw if you want Velvet Ribbon and worker clothing entries in the pool.

Late game

  1. Clear the relationship routes to In Love for 27 milestone Tokens total.
  2. Reach 150 lifetime traded canes.
  3. Complete the floor 21 and 23 Tinsel encounters.
  4. Finish all version-accessible Holly tasks and revisit Holly at every five-task threshold.
  5. Push Extras completion to 80% and use Noella's repeatable reward as needed.
  6. Farm the machine until Christmas Spirit and Christmas Wreath are removed from the pool as obtained.

Managing Claw odds

  • If you chiefly want Christmas Spirit, roll before adding many active worker clothing entries; every eligible outfit increases the denominator.
  • If you chiefly want worker clothing, activate the desired supported workers and avoid activating unrelated supported workers until after the upgrade lands.
  • If you want Velvet Ribbon, the brothel must already be owned.
  • Winning Wreath or Christmas Spirit changes the whole pool. Reopen View Prize Odds after every unique prize.
  • A free player's first Special Gift is a one-time decision. Do not consume it accidentally on a recipient you did not intend to prioritize.

Troubleshooting and Current-Build Quirks

Christmas Village is visible, but Christmas actions are missing

The location itself is permanent. Turn Christmas ON from the village or Relationships screen and confirm the lower-right event counters appear.

A Candy Cane does not appear

Check all of the following:

  • Christmas is ON.
  • The current location is one of the 23 listed spawns.
  • Two in-game days have passed since that location's last collection.
  • Clean View has not hidden the Christmas overlay.
  • You revisited the location after the cooldown so its saved pickup state could refresh.

Ginger's milestone did not trigger at 25 or 75

The game tracks lifetime canes given only when you trade a batch of 10. The first reachable totals are 30 and 80, not 25 and 75. Re-enter the Gingerbread House after the trade.

A relationship milestone did not trigger

The plot is location-triggered. Enter the correct resident's home after reaching the stage. If multiple stages were crossed quickly, leave and return until all queued milestones have played.

A Christmas task action does not appear

Verify:

  1. Christmas is ON.
  2. The previous one-time task has not already completed.
  3. You are at the exact location.
  4. The NPC meets the listed relationship stage.
  5. The current hour is inside the action's time range and the location itself is open.
  6. Required items or workers are present.
  7. The task is available in your game version.

Task 8 appears in several eras

That is intentional in the current location registry. The same one-time Nerida task is attached to the Present Tranquil Spa, Medieval Royal Hot Springs, and Future Virtual Wellness Immersion Center. Completing it at one location removes it everywhere.

Tasks 19, 20, or 25 do not fire immediately

They are random progressive dungeon scenarios, not guaranteed floor-entry events. The first two use 40% eligible rolls; Krampus uses 50%. Stay at or above the required floor, keep Christmas ON, and continue triggering scenario checks.

The odds table does not list Velvet Ribbon

This is a display omission. After brothel ownership, Ribbon weight 3 is included in the real pool and in the total used to calculate other percentages, but the final odds-list renderer omits its row.

The Wreath bonus stops when Christmas is OFF

It should not in the current implementation. Both income and reputation calculations check Wreath ownership only. If a report shows no Wreath line, confirm that Christmas Wreath is present in Key Items and that the relevant calculated gain is large enough for integer rounding to produce a nonzero 5% amount.

Noella's Reward exceeds three uses in one day

The action declares a limit of three, but its custom handler currently bypasses normal daily-action recording. The repeatable plot and Token reward still execute. Treat this as an implementation quirk that may be corrected later.

The free build cannot reach 25/25

That is expected under the current task definitions. Nine tasks are premium-gated, leaving 16 legitimately completable tasks in the free build. The 20- and 25-task Holly milestones therefore require premium access.

快速完成路线

若要可靠起步并高效推进整套系统，请按以下路线进行：

  1. 解锁前往未来时代的正常旅行。
  2. 前往 未来 → 边缘区 → 天空级大气管理站。
  3. 选择 圣诞村。首次造访会自动播放 “欢迎来到圣诞村”，使 Ginger、Holly 和 Noella 的好感度各 +10，并让你通过选择再为其中一人 +5，同时奖励 1 枚圣诞代币。
  4. 选择 切换圣诞精神，直到地点界面右下角出现圣诞计数条。你也可以在关系界面切换 圣诞：开启／关闭。
  5. 拜访三位居民。她们每天 24 小时都在：
    • Ginger——姜饼屋
    • Holly——冬日仙境
    • Noella——槲寄生小屋
  6. 点击计数器旁的圣诞检查表图标，查看 Holly 的圣诞任务。检查表会追踪散布在游戏各处的 25 项任务。
  7. 收集 圣诞村 和 槲寄生小屋 附近的两根拐杖糖，之后在旅行途中收集世界各地的另外 21 个刷新点。
  8. 如果想尽早抽取 圣诞精神、圣诞花环、水晶或礼物，可以把开场获得的代币投入抓娃娃机。结果随机，可能未中奖。
  9. 提升三位圣诞居民的好感度。从已知到亲密的每个好感阶段都会触发奖励 +1 代币的剧情；恋爱中阶段奖励 +2。

  • 来源：拥有青楼时，作为权重 3 的抓娃娃机奖品。
  • 类型：可装备的稀有员工道具。
  • 对被分配员工的效果：收入 +15%，外貌 +3。
  • 不是一次性奖品；可以继续抽到更多份。

员工服装等级

抓娃娃机服装奖品会永久提高所选员工的服装等级。关闭圣诞不会撤销升级。抓娃娃机的一次或两次获奖限制，与员工服装等级的总上限 5 相互独立。

其他持久结果

  • 水晶与财富会立即成为永久收益。
  • 泰迪熊、内衣和珠宝会进入普通消耗品库存。
  • 已完成的礼物场景和任务场景会在适用时通过其附加内容记录保持可用。
  • 好感度、剧情标记、终身拐杖糖累计数和任务完成标记都会保留。

推荐刷取策略

前期

  1. 触发村庄开场，取得免费代币和好感起步优势。
  2. 开启圣诞活动。
  3. 当前路线经过容易取得的拐杖糖时顺手收集。
  4. 在安全的每日上限内与三位居民交谈。
  5. 如果能接受未中奖的风险，就使用第一枚代币。圣诞精神是前期最佳长期收益。
  6. 开始处理要求较低的任务：好感度提升后，1、6、7、13、14 和 18 会较早开放。

中期

  1. 优先与 Ginger 交换。这会同时产出代币、Ginger 好感度和终身里程碑进度。
  2. 把热巧克力送给当前进度最落后的居民。
  3. 每当好感阶段变化时返回各自住所，避免代币剧情悄悄排队。
  4. 能轻松应对地牢第 18 层后，尽快完成任务 18 和 19。
  5. 在消耗免费版稀缺的礼物机会前，先将 11 位收礼人提升至亲密朋友。
  6. 如果想让天鹅绒缎带和员工服装条目进入奖池，请先拥有并发展青楼，再大量刷抓娃娃机。

后期

  1. 将三条好感路线推进到恋爱中，取得总计 27 枚阶段里程碑代币。
  2. 让终身交换的拐杖糖达到 150 根。
  3. 完成第 21 层和第 23 层的 Tinsel 遭遇。
  4. 完成当前版本允许的全部 Holly 任务，并在每五项任务门槛时再次拜访 Holly。
  5. 将附加内容完成度推至 80%，按需使用 Noella 的可重复奖励。
  6. 持续刷机器，直到圣诞精神和圣诞花环因已经获得而从奖池移除。

管理抓娃娃机概率

  • 如果主要目标是 圣诞精神，请在加入许多在职员工服装条目前抽取；每件符合条件的服装都会增加分母。

故障排查与当前版本特殊问题

能看到圣诞村，但圣诞行动消失

地点本身是永久开放的。从村庄或关系界面开启圣诞活动，并确认右下方活动计数器出现。

拐杖糖没有出现

检查以下所有条件：

  • 圣诞活动已开启。
  • 当前地点属于列出的 23 个刷新点之一。
  • 距离上次在该地点收集已经过去两个游戏日。
  • 清爽视图没有隐藏圣诞覆盖层。
  • 冷却结束后你再次造访了该地点，让已保存的拾取状态得以刷新。

Ginger 的 25 或 75 里程碑没有触发

游戏只有在每次交换一批 10 根时，才会记录终身赠送的拐杖糖数量。因此首次可达总数是 30 和 80，而不是 25 和 75。交换后重新进入姜饼屋。

好感里程碑没有触发

剧情由地点触发。达到阶段后进入正确居民的住所。如果短时间跨过多个阶段，请反复离开并返回，直到所有排队里程碑都已播放。

圣诞任务行动没有出现

请核实：

  1. 圣诞活动已开启。
  2. 该一次性任务此前尚未完成。
  3. 你位于精确指定的地点。
  4. NPC 达到列出的好感阶段。
  5. 当前小时位于行动时间范围内，且地点本身正在营业。
  6. 所需道具或员工均已准备好。
  7. 你的游戏版本支持该任务。

任务 8 出现在多个时代

这是当前地点登记表的有意设计。同一项一次性 Nerida 任务同时附加在现代宁静水疗中心、中世纪皇家温泉和未来虚拟健康沉浸中心。在任一地点完成后，其他地点都会移除该任务。

任务 19、20 或 25 没有立即触发

它们是随机渐进式地牢场景，不是保证在进入楼层时发生的事件。前两项使用 40% 的符合条件判定；Krampus 使用 50%。保持在要求楼层或更深处，确保圣诞开启，并继续触发场景检查。

概率表没有列出天鹅绒缎带

这是显示遗漏。拥有青楼后，真实奖池和其他百分比使用的总权重都会计入缎带的 3 点权重，但最终概率列表渲染器漏掉了这一行。

关闭圣诞后花环加成停止

当前实现中不应如此。收入和声望计算都只检查花环所有权。如果报告中没有花环条目，请确认关键道具中存在 圣诞花环，并确认相关计算收益足够大，使整数取整后的 5% 结果不为零。

Noella的奖励一天内使用超过三次

行动声明的上限是三次，但其自定义处理器目前会绕过常规每日行动记录。可重复剧情与代币奖励仍会执行。请将其视为未来可能修正的实现特殊问题。

免费版无法达到 25/25

按照当前任务定义，这是预期结果。九项任务受高级版限制，免费版只剩 16 项可以正常完成。因此 Holly 的 20 项与 25 项里程碑需要高级版权限。
