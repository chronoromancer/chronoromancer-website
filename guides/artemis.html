Overview

Artemis is the Future Era version of Robin — literally the same person, nine-hundred-plus years apart. She was placed in magical stasis by Sashina before the extinction of the immortal races, then woke in the Future to find her entire civilization reduced to ash and metal. She took the name Artemis (the huntress goddess who worked alone) because Robin — the woman who loved forests, animals, and people — died with her race.

She now fights alongside Ghost's resistance against VOID, carrying the weight of being the last living elf.

Note on current development: Artemis is currently Low priority / Story-Locked. As of this build she has only 3 character images and a single Talk action. Her full romance content will be developed later — this walkthrough covers what's implemented plus what her placeholder data tells us about planned progression.

Favorite Stats (each adds +1 bonus before Anthropology doubling)

The stats are the same as Robin's but with dramatically higher thresholds — Artemis demands the player prove themselves at a much higher level before she considers them worthy of attention:

  • Ability ≥ 80 (Robin: 50)
  • Perception ≥ 80 (Robin: 45)
  • Stamina ≥ 80 (Robin: 55)

The grief, trauma, and trust issues are encoded mechanically — what impressed Robin barely registers with Artemis.

Appearance

  • Hair: Long dark olive hair, now often tied back for combat
  • Eyes: Sharp blue, harder and colder than Robin's
  • Build: Tall, athletic — frame honed by centuries of archery, adapted to modern combat
  • Ears: Pointed elf ears — the last in existence
  • Outfit: Futuristic tactical gear with hints of elven craftsmanship
  • Presence: Commands respect through demonstrated skill and cold determination

She is visually distinct from Robin but clearly recognizable — the hair and eyes are the same, but nine centuries of loss have hardened every line.

Story Context

Artemis unlocks after Story Beat 28 completes. Before that she doesn't exist in the world. The reveal that the last surviving elf is the player's medieval archer love interest is a key emotional beat of the Future Era storyline.

She operates under Ghost's resistance — and that's the only reason she tolerates the player's presence at first. Ghost vouching for them is the one thing keeping her from dismissing them entirely.

The thematic arc: Robin was playful, observant, and slow-to-trust. Artemis is everything Robin became after losing everyone she knew — including, from her perspective, the player, who died a thousand years before she woke up.

The romance arc, if/when fully built, will be about the player convincing Artemis that Robin's love survived the centuries. Her own dialogue hints strongly at this: "Love survived the fire. I did not expect that."

Writing Style Notes

Artemis's dialogue has a very specific voice that differs from everyone else in the game:

  • Short, declarative sentences. She doesn't waste words.
  • No contractions. "I do not" instead of "I don't" — feels archaic, elvish, formal. It's the verbal equivalent of armor.
  • Rejects the old identity repeatedly. "Robin is dead. I am Artemis." is nearly a mantra.
  • Dry gallows humor emerges at Friend+ stages — "Artemis was a goddess who turned men to animals for getting too close."
  • Vulnerability always arrives sideways. Never direct confession, always through what she reveals by accident: "I feel something when you are near. I hate it."
  • The walls come down in Romantic+ stages but she never becomes effusive — even her "I love you" is grudging: "I will not say it often. But I love you. There."

When writing Artemis dialogue, the rule is: what would the most guarded version of Robin say after losing everything?

概述

Artemis 是 Robin 在未来时代的身份——两人确实是同一个人，只是相隔了九百多年。永生种族灭绝前，Sashina将她置于魔法停滞状态；在未来醒来时，她发现自己的整个文明只剩灰烬与金属。她选择了Artemis这个名字（独自狩猎的女猎神），因为那个热爱森林、动物与人群的Robin，已经与她的种族一同死去。

如今，她背负着 最后一名幸存精灵 的沉重身份，与Ghost的抵抗组织并肩对抗VOID。

当前开发状态说明： Artemis目前属于 低优先级／剧情锁定 角色。截至本版本，她只有3张角色图片和一项交谈行动。完整恋爱内容将在日后开发——本攻略会介绍目前已经实装的内容，以及占位数据透露出的计划推进方向。

她在视觉上与Robin截然不同，却仍让人一眼便能认出——头发与眼睛没有改变，但九百年的失去，让她脸上的每一道线条都变得坚硬。

故事背景

完成 剧情点28 后，Artemis才会解锁。在此之前，她不会出现在游戏世界中。最后幸存的精灵，竟是玩家在中世纪爱上的那位弓箭手——这一揭晓是未来时代故事线中的关键情感转折。

她在Ghost的抵抗组织麾下行动；起初，这也是她容忍玩家出现在身边的唯一原因。Ghost愿意为玩家担保，才使她没有把对方彻底赶走。

文风说明

Artemis的说话方式非常鲜明，与游戏中的所有其他角色都不相同：

  • 句子简短而肯定。 她从不浪费言语。
  • 不用英语缩略形式。 她会说完整的“I do not”，而不是“I don't”——听起来古雅、带有精灵气质且十分正式。这是语言化成的铠甲。
  • 反复否定过去的身份。 “Robin已经死了。我是Artemis。”近乎一句自我告诫的咒语。
  • 达到朋友以上后，会流露冷峻的黑色幽默——“Artemis是一位女神，会把靠得太近的男人变成动物。”
  • 脆弱总是从侧面显露。 她从不直接坦白，总会通过不慎透露的内容表现出来：“你靠近时，我会产生某种感觉。我讨厌它。”
  • 达到浪漫感以上后，高墙才会倒下，但她依然不会变得热烈奔放——就连“我爱你”也说得勉强：“我不会经常说。但我爱你。说完了。”

撰写Artemis的对话时，请遵循这条规则：失去一切之后，戒心最重的那个Robin会怎么说？

  • 目前只有交谈行动——每天可进行3次，以标准点数推进关系。
  • 她的属性门槛很高（80／80／80）——如果没有投入大量资源，不要指望获得偏好属性加成。大多数玩家要到游戏后期才能达到这些数值。
  • 人类学技能会使点数翻倍——与Robin相同。如果玩家为了Robin学会了人类学，对Artemis依然有效。
  • 她目前刻意表现得非常冷淡，这既符合她的背景故事，也符合内容有限的开发状态。即使暂时只是一个占位角色，这套文笔也依然成立。
