Overview

Mindy is the kind, average girl from your high school days who had a secret crush on you back then. Now 20 years old, she's a couple grades behind you and lives in the same neighborhood. After bumping into her at the metro station, her quiet history with you slowly resurfaces — from butterfly-loving college girl, to confessed old flame, to deeply trusting partner. She's the childhood-crush-reconnected romance: gentle pace, nostalgic tone, full sweet-to-intense relationship arc.

She has a single intro plot, three core heart-to-heart actions, three dates (Stargazing, Fair, and Boardwalk Sunset), an intimate housing video action, and a deeply branching apartment visit conversation with 42 rounds of content.

The Reunion Plot — "A Familiar Face"

Mindy's intro plot auto-triggers on her schedule with no setup needed.

Trigger Conditions

  • Location: Present Metro Station (Uptown)
  • Time: 9 AM – 3 PM
  • Even days only (per Mindy's metro schedule)
  • Mindy at Stranger stage or higher

What Happens

You're on the platform when Mindy spots you. She's clearly nervous but excited — "Oh! I thought I recognized you... it's me, Mindy! Do you remember me?"

Three-choice greeting:

  • Standard: "Mindy? Oh right, from high school!"
  • Polite: "Of course I remember you!"
  • Flirty: "How could I forget those brown eyes?"

She catches you up — she's taking a couple of university courses, enjoying the city, not too busy. Then a follow-up choice on what you've been up to (casual / mysterious / joking — she rolls with all three).

She says she has to meet a friend, hopes to run into you again, then leaves.

Reward

+12 relationship points with Mindy.

What This Unlocks

After this plot completes, her park and housing block schedules activate — opening up her main daily routine.

Important: The reunion plot grants +12 points but doesn't push her past Stranger stage by itself. You'll still need to talk to her at the metro a few times (or build via her newly-unlocked locations) to push to Acknowledged and beyond.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Charm 30, Intelligence 30, Strength 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has the Emotional Bonding skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Teddy Bear (base 8), all 3 favorite stats met, Emotional Bonding learned: (8 + 3) × 2 = 22 points — plus possible +1s from Ring/Cologne/Buffer/Attractiveness.

Min-max tip: Stack Charm 30, Intelligence 30, and Strength 35 before you start the gift grind. Those bonuses are doubled by Emotional Bonding, so each favorite stat met is effectively worth +2 points per action.

Heart-to-Heart Conversations

Mindy has two main park H2Hs plus the Metro's Vending Machine Trouble action. They're cinematic and rich with character moments.

H2H 1 — "Walk in the Park"

  • Requires: Known stage
  • 4 rounds: meeting up → butterfly spotting → vulnerable confession about feeling comfortable being herself → invitation to see her favorite pond spot
  • Special choices: Charm 25 (round 1), Empathy skill (round 3)
  • Theme: Comfortable companionship, butterfly trivia, growing trust

H2H 2 — "Getting Closer"

  • Requires: 300+ relationship points
  • 5 rounds: another walk → school memories → the confession (she still has feelings) → invitation to visit her at home → first kiss
  • Special choice: Intelligence 30 (round 2)
  • Climax: This is the H2H where Mindy openly admits her old crush never went away. Round 5 is your first kiss with her.

Why H2H 2 matters: It's the narrative bridge to the apartment visit. Both H2Hs together set the emotional foundation for the deeper content at her place.

The Apartment Visit — Branching Conversation

This is the showpiece of Mindy's content: a 42-round branching apartment visit with multiple paths, special-choice gates, item-redemption routes, and ending variations.

How to Unlock

  • Reach Crush stage with Mindy
  • Use "Knock on Mindy's Door" at the housing block, 7 PM – 11 PM
  • Daily limit 3 — repeatable

Opening Path Branches (Round 1)

When you arrive, you can go in five different directions based on choices and unlocks:

Choice Requirements Destination
Normal greeting None Round 4 (warm, slow-build path)
Discuss her butterflies Intelligence 35 Round 2 (butterfly path)
Compliment her looks Charm 35 Round 3 (charm path — bigger payoffs)
Come on strong Seduction skill Round 5 (seduction path — fast escalation)
Talk about trust Trust Building skill NOT yet learned Round 41 (Trust Building learning path)

Trust Building Skill Path

The trust path is a special non-intimate route. If you choose to discuss trust on Round 1 (and don't already have the skill), you go through Round 41 → 42 as a quiet two-round conversation. At the end she helps you internalize what trust really means, and you learn the Trust Building skill (terminate conversation, cinematic ending).

Item Routes

Several rounds let you spend a key gift item for big rewards:

  • Round 2 (butterfly path) or Round 4 (normal path): offer her a Teddy Bear → +50 points → Round 38 (joyful teddy reaction) → loops back to Round 6
  • Round 3 (charm path): offer her Jewelry → +40 points → Round 36 (overwhelmed reaction) → Round 9 (the "anything" path)

The "Kiss Anticipation" Hub (Round 6)

Most paths converge here at Round 6 — the moment before your first apartment kiss. From Round 6, you proceed to Round 27 (the actual kiss), then Round 19 (point-check round).

Round 19 — The Point Check

Round 19 silently checks your relationship points:

  • Below 600 points: routes to Round 11 (Mindy gets sleepy, you understand and leave) — terminates conversation
  • At 600+ points: routes to Round 10 (intimate continuation paths open)

Tip: Make sure you're well past 600 relationship points before the apartment visit if you want to avoid the early-out tired ending.

Intimate Path Tree (Round 9 — "Anything")

If you reach Round 9 (the "Do anything" path), Mindy gives you four position choices:

  • Missionary → Round 39 → Round 14 (with Stamina 45+ extending to Round 16)
  • Fellatio → Round 12 (with mouth/face finish choices → Round 30 or 31)
  • Cowgirl → Round 17 (with Stamina 45+ extension)
  • Doggie → Round 18 (with Stamina 45+ extension)

Special High-Stat / Skill Routes

  • Charm 50 (Round 7 → Round 9): unlock the "anything" path with +50 points
  • Erotic Experience 1500 (Round 8): unlock the Epic Sequence (Rounds 20-23) — three full scenes plus a sleeping ending, +150 erotic exp total
  • Beast Stat 35 (Round 15): unlock the Beast Path (Rounds 37 → 24 → 40 → 25 → 28) — most intense ending, +150 erotic exp + +1 Beast stat permanent
  • Pleasure Arts skill (Round 10): unlocks cunnilingus extended path → Round 13 → 32 → 33 → 34 (the "Making Love" route, ends with her saying "I love you")
  • Strength 35 (Round 10): "Take control" option → Round 39 (Mindy looking up at you in trust)
  • Mystic Seduction skill (Round 3): "Perfect timing" gives +40 points and pushes to the Charm 50 route
  • Stamina 45 (Round 14, 17, 18): extend missionary/cowgirl/doggie scenes, +25 erotic exp bonus

Ending Types

  • Standard satisfied ending (Round 15 → "Say goodnight") — cinematic ending, fully repeatable
  • Beast ending (Round 25 → 28) — sleeping beast ending, cinematic
  • Epic ending (Round 23 → 29) — exhausted epic ending, cinematic
  • Cunnilingus/Making Love ending (Round 34) — most romantic ending, +50 erotic exp, cinematic
  • Trust ending (Round 42) — learn Trust Building skill, cinematic
  • Tired/early-out (Round 11) — failed point check, no rewards
  • Mouth/Face fellatio endings (Round 30/31) — quick endings, cinematic

The apartment visit is a content-rich showpiece. Multiple replays unlock different paths via different stats/skills/items. It's worth running it many times across a save.

Recommended Progression Path

Phase 1: Stranger → Acknowledged

  • Visit the Metro Station on an even day, 9 AM – 12 PM
  • Reunion plot auto-triggers (+12 rel)
  • After plot completes, her park and housing block schedules unlock

Phase 2: Acknowledged → Known

  • Visit the Park 1 PM – 4 PM daily
  • Talk (5/day until daily limit kicks in — daily limit is technically 7)
  • Stockpile Flowers for the Known-stage gate

  • Run High School Stories once (Communication 24+, +1 Charm permanent, +8 rel) — don't miss it
  • Daily Talk + Flowers
  • Run the "Walk in the Park" H2H once for the cinematic conversation
  • Hit favorite stats: Charm 30, Intelligence 30, Strength 35

Phase 4: Friend → Close Friend

  • Switch to Housing Block evenings for Teddy Bear gifts (+8 base, daily limit 1)
  • Run the Stargazing Date (one-time, Friend+, $50)
  • Continue daily Talk

Phase 5: Close Friend → Crush

  • Stockpile Jewelry (+10 base, daily limit 1)
  • Run "Getting Closer" H2H (300+ points required) — this includes her confession and first kiss
  • Run the Boardwalk Sunset Date if available (Patreon, requires boardwalk built)
  • Crush stage unlocks the Knock on Door action

Phase 6: Crush → Romantic → Intimate

  • Use "Knock on Mindy's Door" at the housing block 7 PM – 11 PM
  • Stack to 700+ relationship points before peak runs
  • The apartment visit becomes the major content path
  • At 700+ points, the "Intimate with Mindy" housing action unlocks (+25 base, paywalled video, daily limit 1)

Phase 7: Endgame

  • Replay apartment visit for different paths (Beast, Epic, Cunnilingus, Trust)
  • Daily: Knock on Door (3/day) + Intimate housing (1/day) + Talk (7/day) + Teddy/Jewelry
  • Keep Emotional Bonding learned for permanent point doubling

Relationship Stages & Dialogue Tone

Mindy's dialogue evolves from cautious-but-friendly stranger to head-over-heels devotion. The arc has a strong nostalgic thread — her old crush gets confessed at Close Friend, and from Crush onward she frames everything in terms of "high school dreams come true."

Dialogue Voice Highlights

  • Heavy butterfly motif (monarch migrations, painted ladies, swallowtails) — she's been quietly into lepidoptera the whole time
  • Frequent references to her shy schoolgirl past vs who she's become
  • Genuinely warm, never cynical, never pushes
  • Daily-limit responses are gentle ("Slow down a bit, I need some time to process")

Relationships With Other Characters

  • Emily and Rachel — Mindy is part of the "Three Girls Comparison" cross-character plot. There's a moment where you can compliment one of the three (Emily, Rachel, or Mindy). Complimenting Mindy gives +10 rel with Mindy (and similarly for Emily/Rachel if you choose them).
  • High school past — Mindy went to the same school as the player. She was a couple grades younger. Her crush was real but unstated; you were "nice to her a few times" but barely knew her.

Tips & Optimal Play

  • The metro reunion is the gate to her entire arc. Without it, her park and housing schedules never unlock. Visit on an even day, 9 AM – 12 PM.
  • Teddy Bear is god-tier — favorite gift (+8 base, doubled by Emotional Bonding to +16+), and her dialogue reactions to teddy gifts are uniquely sweet. It's also one of the apartment-visit item routes (+50 points).
  • High School Stories is a free +1 Charm permanent — needs Communication 24 and Known stage. Don't miss it; the Charm 30 favorite-stat threshold benefits.
  • Don't enter the apartment visit below 600 points — the Round 19 point check will route you to the early-out tired ending. Stack to 700+ for safety.
  • Keep Emotional Bonding learned at all times — it doubles every action's points for the entire game session, including post-stat-bonus.
  • Her favorite stat thresholds are mid-tier (30/30/35) — easier than late-game characters. Stack them before Friend stage for maximum gift grind efficiency.
  • The apartment visit has six unique endings — Standard, Beast (Beast 35), Epic (Erotic 1500), Making Love (Pleasure Arts), Trust Building (skill not yet learned), and Tired (failed point check). Each is worth seeing.
  • Knock on Door is daily limit 3 — repeatable, great for grinding the Beast/Epic paths once stat-gated.
  • The Boardwalk Date is the most narratively rich — Mindy opens up about her "list of things to do when brave enough" and her old roof-watching ritual. It's the date that confirms her transformation.
  • Round 14 Stamina 45 extension is worth investing — the bonus +25 erotic exp on Stamina-45-gated rounds adds up fast over multiple replays.

重逢剧情——“熟悉的面孔”

Mindy 的介绍剧情会按照她的日程自动触发，无需任何准备。

触发条件

  • 地点： 现代地铁站（上城区）
  • 时间： 早上9点至下午3点
  • 仅限偶数日（依照 Mindy 的地铁日程）
  • Mindy 处于陌生人阶段或以上

剧情内容

你正在站台上，Mindy 认出了你。她显然紧张又兴奋——“哦！我就觉得好像认出你了……是我，Mindy！你还记得我吗？”

三种问候选择：

  • 普通： “Mindy？哦对，高中时认识的！”
  • 礼貌： “我当然记得你！”
  • 调情： “我怎么可能忘记那双棕色眼睛？”

她会告诉你近况——正在大学选修几门课程，享受城市生活，也不算太忙。接着你可以选择如何描述自己最近的经历（随意／神秘／开玩笑——三种她都能接得住）。

她说自己得去见一个朋友，希望以后还能碰到你，然后离开。

奖励

与 Mindy 的关系**+12点**。

解锁内容

该剧情完成后，她在公园和住宅区的日程便会启用，从而开放主要的每日活动路线。

重要提示： 重逢剧情提供+12点，却不足以让她独自跨过陌生人阶段。你仍需在地铁与她交谈几次（或利用新解锁的地点培养关系），才能推进到已遇见及以后。

按地点分类的行动

地铁站（上城区）

  • 与 Mindy 交谈——15分钟，无花费。已遇见+。每日上限7次。+2基础点数。
  • 自动售货机麻烦（H2H）——30分钟，−10精力。完成“地铁偶遇”剧情后可用；每日上限1次。

公园（上城区）

  • 与 Mindy 交谈——15分钟。已遇见+。每日上限7次。+2基础点数。
  • 高中故事（仅一次）——30分钟，要求已知阶段 + 沟通24以上。永久+1魅力，+8关系点。
  • 送花——10分钟，消耗1件鲜花。已知+。每日上限2次。+5基础点数。
  • 公园散步（H2H）——45分钟，−15精力。已知+。每日上限1次。谈心对话（参见 H2H 部分）。
  • 逐渐靠近（H2H）——45分钟，−20精力。要求300以上关系点。每日上限1次。谈心对话（参见 H2H 部分）。
  • 游乐园约会——120分钟，$150，−45精力。朋友+，下午1点至3点。仅一次。

住宅区（上城区）

  • 与 Mindy 交谈——15分钟。已遇见+。每日上限7次。+2基础点数。
  • 送泰迪熊——10分钟，消耗1件泰迪熊。朋友+。每日上限1次。+8基础点数。（她最喜欢的礼物。）
  • 送珠宝——10分钟，消耗1件珠宝。亲密朋友+。每日上限1次。+10基础点数。
  • 观星约会——120分钟，$50，−40精力。朋友+，下午5点至晚上7点。仅一次。

  • 与 Mindy 亲密相处——30分钟，−20精力。要求700以上关系点，付费视频。每日上限1次。+25基础点数。

住宅区——远程行动（敲门）

奖励计算（精确顺序）

关系点公式按以下顺序计算：

  1. 从行动提供的基础点数开始
  2. 每满足一项偏好属性便**+1**（魅力30、智力30、力量35——最多+3）
  3. 如果玩家拥有情感联结技能，则**× 2**（她的特殊／偏好技能）
  4. 如果玩家拥有**“1 Ring”关键物品，则+1**
  5. 古龙水增益生效时**+1**
  6. 缓冲区优化等级生效时**+1**
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，有机会**+1**（上限50%，持有华丽挂坠时为60%）

计算示例： 泰迪熊（基础8），满足全部3项偏好属性，且学会情感联结： (8 + 3) × 2 = 22 points——另有可能从戒指／古龙水／缓冲区／吸引力分别获得+1。

收益最大化提示： 开始反复送礼之前，先将魅力提升至30、智力提升至30、力量提升至35。这些奖励会被情感联结翻倍，因此每满足一项偏好属性，实际上每次行动就多得+2点。

谈心对话

Mindy 有两次主要的公园 H2H，以及地铁站的自动售货机麻烦行动。它们都采用电影式表现，并包含丰富的角色时刻。

H2H 1——“公园散步”

  • 要求： 已知阶段
  • 4轮： 会面 → 发现蝴蝶 → 坦白只有在这里才能自在做自己 → 邀请你去看她最喜欢的池塘角落
  • 特殊选项： 魅力25（第1轮）、共情技能（第3轮）
  • 主题： 舒适的陪伴、蝴蝶知识与不断加深的信任

H2H 2——“逐渐靠近”

  • 要求： 300以上关系点
  • 5轮： 再次散步 → 学校回忆 → 告白（她依然对你有感觉）→ 邀请你去她家 → 初吻
  • 特殊选项： 智力30（第2轮）
  • 高潮： 在这次 H2H 中，Mindy 坦承当年的暗恋从未消失。第5轮是你与她的初吻。

H2H 2 的重要性： 它是通往公寓拜访的叙事桥梁。两次 H2H 共同为她住所中的深入内容奠定感情基础。

  • 三个决定点（甜蜜／幽默／调情），最终都会得到同样真挚的结局：手牵着手，穿过安静的夜间街道送她回家
  • 一句令人难忘的台词：“你可能不记得，但我一直希望你能注意到我。”

木板路日落约会（Patreon）

  • 要求已建成木板路（社区发展项目）
  • Patreon 付费内容
  • 仅一次
  • 串灯下的木板路，眺望灯塔闪烁；Mindy 会谈起自己过去那份“等到足够勇敢时要做的事情清单”
  • 她路线中情感最强烈的约会——她承认，过去觉得自己无人看见时，会在住宅区屋顶眺望灯塔

公寓拜访——分支对话

这是 Mindy 内容的重头戏：一段42轮的分支式公寓拜访，拥有多条路线、特殊选项门槛、物品兑换路线及不同结局。

解锁方式

  • 与 Mindy 达到迷恋阶段
  • 晚上7点至11点，在住宅区使用**“敲 Mindy 的门”**
  • 每日上限3次——可重复

开场路线分支（第1轮）

抵达后，你可以根据选项和解锁条件走向五个不同方向：

选项 要求 目的地
普通问候 无 第4轮（温暖、缓慢升温的路线）
谈论她的蝴蝶 智力35 第2轮（蝴蝶路线）
赞美她的外貌 魅力35 第3轮（魅力路线——回报更高）
强势进攻 诱惑技能 第5轮（诱惑路线——迅速升温）
谈论信任 尚未学会建立信任技能 第41轮（学习建立信任的路线）

建立信任技能路线

信任路线是一条特殊的非亲密路线。如果在第1轮选择讨论信任（且尚未拥有该技能），你会经过第41轮 → 第42轮，完成一段安静的两轮对话。结尾时，她会帮助你真正领悟信任的含义，使你学会建立信任技能（对话终止，电影式结尾）。

物品路线

有几轮允许你消耗关键礼物，换取大量奖励：

  • 第2轮（蝴蝶路线）或第4轮（普通路线）： 送她一只泰迪熊 → +50点 → 第38轮（欣喜的泰迪熊反应）→ 返回第6轮
  • 第3轮（魅力路线）： 送她一件珠宝 → +40点 → 第36轮（感动得不知所措）→ 第9轮（“什么都可以”路线）

“亲吻前的期待”中心节点（第6轮）

大多数路线会在第6轮汇合——公寓初吻前的一刻。第6轮后进入第27轮（正式亲吻），随后进入第19轮（点数检查轮）。

第19轮——点数检查

第19轮会在不提示的情况下检查关系点：

  • 低于600点： 进入第11轮（Mindy 困了，你表示理解并离开）——结束对话
  • 达到600点以上： 进入第10轮（开放后续亲密路线）

提示： 如果不想触发提前离开的疲惫结局，公寓拜访前请确保关系点明显高于600。

亲密路线树（第9轮——“什么都可以”）

进入第9轮（“什么都可以”路线）后，Mindy 会让你选择四种体位：

  • 传教士位 → 第39轮 → 第14轮（耐力45以上可延伸至第16轮）
  • 口交 → 第12轮（可选择射入口中／脸上结束 → 第30或31轮）
  • 女上位 → 第17轮（耐力45以上可延伸）
  • 后入式 → 第18轮（耐力45以上可延伸）

特殊高属性／技能路线

  • 魅力50（第7轮 → 第9轮）： 解锁“什么都可以”路线，+50点
  • 情欲经验1500（第8轮）： 解锁史诗序列（第20-23轮）——三个完整场景及睡眠结尾，共+150情欲经验
  • 兽性属性35（第15轮）： 解锁兽性路线（第37轮 → 24轮 → 40轮 → 25轮 → 28轮）——最激烈的结局，+150情欲经验 + 永久+1兽性属性
  • 愉悦技巧技能（第10轮）： 解锁舔阴延伸路线 → 第13轮 → 32轮 → 33轮 → 34轮（“做爱”路线，以她说“我爱你”结束）
  • 力量35（第10轮）： “掌控局面”选项 → 第39轮（Mindy 带着信任仰望你）
  • 神秘诱惑技能（第3轮）： “完美时机”提供+40点，并进入魅力50路线
  • 耐力45（第14、17、18轮）： 延伸传教士位／女上位／后入式场景，并额外获得+25情欲经验

结局类型

  • 标准满足结局（第15轮 → “道晚安”）——电影式结尾，可完全重复
  • 兽性结局（第25轮 → 28轮）——兽性路线的睡眠结局，电影式表现
  • 史诗结局（第23轮 → 29轮）——精疲力尽的史诗结局，电影式表现
  • 舔阴／做爱结局（第34轮）——最浪漫的结局，+50情欲经验，电影式表现
  • 信任结局（第42轮）——学会建立信任技能，电影式表现
  • 疲惫／提前离开（第11轮）——点数检查失败，无奖励
  • 口中／脸上口交结局（第30／31轮）——快速结局，电影式表现

公寓拜访是内容丰富的重头戏。 多次重玩时，可以利用不同属性／技能／物品解锁不同路线。值得在同一个存档中体验许多次。

推荐推进路线

阶段1：陌生人 → 已遇见

  • 偶数日早上9点至中午12点前往地铁站
  • 重逢剧情自动触发（+12关系点）
  • 剧情完成后，她在公园与住宅区的日程解锁

阶段2：已遇见 → 已知

  • 每天下午1点至4点前往公园
  • 交谈（达到每日上限前可用5次／天——技术上的每日上限是7次）
  • 为已知阶段的门槛囤积鲜花

阶段3：已知 → 朋友

  • 完成一次高中故事（沟通24以上、永久+1魅力、+8关系点）——不要错过
  • 每日交谈 + 鲜花
  • 完成一次**“公园散步”H2H**，观看电影式对话
  • 达到偏好属性：魅力30、智力30、力量35

阶段4：朋友 → 亲密朋友

  • 晚间改去住宅区赠送泰迪熊（+8基础点数，每日上限1次）
  • 完成观星约会（仅一次，朋友+，$50）
  • 继续每日交谈

阶段5：亲密朋友 → 迷恋

  • 囤积珠宝（+10基础点数，每日上限1次）
  • 完成**“逐渐靠近”H2H**（要求300以上点数）——包含她的告白与初吻
  • 如果可用，完成木板路日落约会（Patreon，要求已建成木板路）
  • 迷恋阶段解锁敲门行动

阶段6：迷恋 → 浪漫感 → 亲密

  • 晚上7点至11点，在住宅区使用**“敲 Mindy 的门”**
  • 正式体验主要路线前，将关系提升至700以上
  • 公寓拜访成为主要内容路线
  • 达到700以上点数后，解锁住所的**“与 Mindy 亲密相处”**行动（+25基础点数、付费视频、每日上限1次）

阶段7：终局

  • 重玩公寓拜访，体验不同路线（兽性、史诗、舔阴、信任）
  • 每日：敲门（3次／天）+ 住所亲密互动（1次／天）+ 交谈（7次／天）+ 泰迪熊／珠宝
  • 始终学会情感联结，以永久让点数翻倍

关系阶段与对话语气

Mindy 的对话会从谨慎但友善的陌生人，发展到全心全意的倾慕。整条路线带有浓厚的怀旧主线——她会在亲密朋友阶段坦白旧日暗恋，从迷恋开始，则会把一切描述为“高中梦想成真”。

阶段 语气要点
陌生人 礼貌闲谈，天气与街区
已遇见 亲切地认出你，开始谈论蝴蝶
已知 “你正如我所希望的那样成长了”
相识 珍惜共处时光，发现自己逐渐敞开心扉
朋友 “全世界我最喜欢的人之一”
亲密朋友 坦白自己上学时非常迷恋你
迷恋 “心脏还像高中时一样狂跳，只是更强烈”
浪漫感 “我爱你。我不敢相信自己终于能说出来”
亲密 “我的每一部分——过去与现在”
恋爱中 与亲密阶段同样炽烈

对话风格要点

  • 大量使用蝴蝶意象（帝王蝶迁徙、小红蛱蝶、凤蝶）——她一直悄悄热爱鳞翅目昆虫
  • 经常对比她害羞的学生时代与如今的自己
  • 真诚温暖，从不尖酸，也从不强求
  • 超过每日上限时的回应很温和（“慢一点，我需要时间消化”）

与其他角色的关系

  • Emily 与 Rachel——Mindy 是**“三个女孩的比较”跨角色剧情的一员。你会遇到一个可以赞美三人之一（Emily、Rachel 或 Mindy）的时刻。赞美 Mindy 会使与 Mindy 的关系+10**（选择 Emily／Rachel 时，她们也会获得相同奖励）。
  • 高中往事——Mindy 与玩家曾就读同一所学校，比你低几个年级。她确实暗恋你，却从未说出口；你曾“对她友好过几次”，但几乎不了解她。

提示与最优玩法

  • 地铁重逢是整条路线的门槛。 未完成时，她在公园和住宅区的日程不会解锁。偶数日早上9点至中午12点前往地铁。
  • 泰迪熊极其强力——既是最喜欢的礼物（+8基础点数，情感联结翻倍后为+16以上），她收到泰迪熊时的对话也格外甜蜜。它还是公寓拜访的物品路线之一（+50点）。
  • 高中故事可免费永久+1魅力——要求沟通24与已知阶段。不要错过；这有助于达到魅力30的偏好属性门槛。
  • 关系低于600点时不要进入公寓拜访——第19轮的点数检查会把你送往提前离开的疲惫结局。提升至700以上更稳妥。
  • 始终保持学会情感联结——它会在属性奖励之后，使整场游戏中每次行动的点数翻倍。
  • 她的偏好属性门槛处于中等水平（30／30／35）——比后期角色容易达到。朋友阶段前完成这些属性，可以最大化送礼效率。
  • 公寓拜访拥有六种独特结局——标准、兽性（兽性35）、史诗（情欲经验1500）、做爱（愉悦技巧）、建立信任（尚未学会技能）及疲惫（点数检查失败）。每种都值得体验。
  • 敲门每日上限3次——可以重复；属性满足后，非常适合反复体验兽性／史诗路线。
  • 木板路约会的叙事最为丰富——Mindy 会谈起“等到足够勇敢时要做的事情清单”，以及过去在屋顶眺望灯塔的习惯。这场约会确认了她的转变。
  • 第14轮的耐力45延伸值得投资——在多个耐力45门槛轮次中，额外+25情欲经验会随多次重玩迅速累积。
