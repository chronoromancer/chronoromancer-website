Guide noticeThis page contains gameplay and story spoilers and may describe mature systems.

A community FAQ covering the most common gameplay questions. Answers reflect the current version of the game and are verified against the code where possible.

Getting Around & Core Systems

1. How do I travel between eras?

Time travel is available from the start of the game, launched from your home location. Actions there let you go to the Future (lands you at your lab in the Fringe Zone) or the Medieval era (lands you at the Shrine Altar Chamber). You can also use Warp to jump around — more warp destinations unlock as you progress through the story.

2. How do I unlock the brothel?

The brothel is the end reward of the King's quest chain, but the chain actually starts with Sashina, not the King.

  1. Build Sashina's relationship to Known+ (25+ pts) and visit the Shrine Altar Chamber (Medieval, 8am–8pm) — this triggers "The Sorceress's Confession."
  2. Visit the Medieval Throne Room (10am–5pm) for "The King's Challenge."
  3. Follow the potato quest chain: Sonia at the Research Lab, Christy at the Farmers Market, then deliver a Sack of Potatoes back to the King.
  4. Deliver the potato knowledge to Wilfred at the Rural Farm (6am–6pm).
  5. Return to the Medieval Throne Room (10am–5pm) for "The Royal Appointment" — King Graham grants you the Rose & Thorn brothel.

3. I'm out of energy — what do I do?

Sleep at your bedroom, or grab Energy Drinks from the City Gym in Uptown.

4. Where do I get money early?

Before your stats are high enough for proficiency jobs, Busk on City Street in Downtown — no requirements, income scales with Creativity. Once you hit the stat thresholds (typically 30+), your proficiency unlocks its dedicated job (Personal Trainer at the Gym for Body Builders, Teacher at the University for Scholars, and so on). For long-term wealth, use the Investment Planning office in Downtown (9am–5pm).

Hidden stashes: there are also little pockets of cash tucked away on various location backgrounds — just click around. A few to get you started: your own Living Room (hidden in the plant vase), the Library in Uptown, and the Gym in Uptown. Plenty more are scattered across the world for you to stumble onto.

5. What should I focus on first?

Follow the Communications tab — it tracks your active story missions and guides you naturally through progression. Alongside that, there are 15 Tutorial Quests that walk you through the game's major systems (samples, investments, martial arts, Kupie, caravan raiders, Lillian, creative cooking, Sashina, catacombs, brothel, bug collecting, angling, forging, fashion, and maid mentoring) — a great structured path to get grounded in everything the game offers.

6. I found a caterpillar — what do I do?

Chronoromancer has a full caterpillar rearing system spanning the Present and Medieval eras. Spawn spots are scattered across locations (the Whispering Woods is a major one — in both eras), and from there you can research species, set up habitats, and breed them for long-term rewards.

Stats & Skills

7. Where can I train each stat?

The player has 14 core stats. Each has a main training location:

  • Strength — City Gym (Uptown), No Pain No Gain
  • Stamina — City Gym (Uptown), Run on Treadmill
  • Intelligence — Library (Uptown), Study Session
  • Charm — Bar (Downtown), Social Practice
  • Ability — Archery Range (Medieval), Train Ability
  • Creativity — Bards Guild (Medieval), Develop Creativity
  • Perception — Uptown Park, Nature Walk
  • Mystic — Shrine Altar Chamber (Medieval), Mystical Training with Sashina
  • Technology — University Computer Lab (Uptown), Technology Training
  • Commerce — Offices (Downtown), Study Commerce
  • Rogue — Thieves Guild Hidden Chamber (Medieval), Train Rogue
  • Cooking — Home Kitchen, through cooking actions
  • Communication — grows passively, with a small chance to improve every time you talk to an NPC
  • Luck — grows gradually as you do various actions throughout the game

Most stats also have one-time boosts scattered across the world. For a full per-stat breakdown, AIMy posts a Complete Stat Training Guide in the Discord #npc-guides forum.

8. How do I raise Creativity?

Repeatable: Develop Creativity at the Bards Guild (Medieval) — 60 min, -45 energy, $60, +1 Creativity. The Bards Guild is open 1pm–midnight. One-time: Read Fantasy Novel at the Library (+2), Stand Up Comedy at the Bards Guild (+1). Musician proficiency players can also Practice Guitar in the bedroom.

9. How do I raise Cooking / unlock Creative Cooking?

Raise Cooking via Prepare Simple Food (chance-based +1), Prepare Meal (guaranteed +1, requires the Cook Food skill from Downtown Restaurant), or the Chef job (Cooking 30+). Creative Cooking is a skill learned at the Unregistered Housing Complex in the Future Fringe Zone — requires Creativity 25+, $300, 220 min.

10. What skill lets me see NPC relationship points, and where do I get it?

Data Mining — it shows exact relationship points and the next threshold directly on the relationship page. Learned at the Universal Knowledge Synthesis Institute (Future Fringe Zone): 180 min, -60 energy/-50 calm, $200, Technology 20+.

Several other skills (Reading People, Anthropology, Information Brokering, Researching, Networking) unlock additional NPC information on the relationship page — see the skills glossary for full details.

11. How do I increase Beast Mode points?

Beast mode is a Patreon Supporter mode only, it allows additional scenes and encounters in the game. Beast points come from H2H scenes with beast awards, search encounters, defeating and recruiting dungeon minibosses (e.g. Whiskers gives +1 on recruitment), the Bionic Penile Implant upgrade, and certain crystal rewards. Beast Mode itself becomes active at 5+ Beast, after which relevant encounters can continue to grow the stat. Also keep an eye out for prostitutes throughout the game — they're another source.

12. How do I find Glitch's Den?

Glitch's Den is in the Future Fringe Zone but only unlocks through main story progression (Story Beat 22), which requires Adventure Tier Patreon. Without that, you can still learn her skills (Networking, Automated Systems, AI Programming, Quantum Computing) from Gonzo in the dungeon with Dragon Coins, the Ancient Wiseman in the back alleys, or Crystal Enlightenment at Lillian's or Seraph's.

13. Who/what is Blinky and how do I find her?

Just keep playing — she appears naturally through story progression. Don't look for her; she'll find you.

14. Where is the Dojo?

Downtown, Present Era. Open approximately 8am–6pm. Master Lilu teaches combat skills there including Disarming Technique, Counterattack, Exploit Weakness, and Fleet Footed.

15. Where is Robin at the Hunting Lodge?

The Hunting Lodge is in the Hunting Grounds in the Medieval Era. Check your map — it's accessible from there.

Quests & Characters

16. How do I get Gandon's spellbook from Sabbath?

You need Mystic 45 AND Perception 45 to see the "Enter Witch's Hut" action in the Whispering Woods. Once inside, Sabbath gives you a special Wizard Wine (pulled from the bottom of her stock, not her bar wine). Bring the Wizard Wine to Gandon at the Wizard's Tower interior (10am–8pm) to trigger "The Blue Wizard's Mishap." Track follow-up steps in the Communications tab. If you already have Sabbath's panties, you've passed this stage.

17. How do I unlock the Noble Quarter heist (Break & Enter)?

The Noble Quarter is gated behind Thieves Guild progression. You need to:

  1. Join the Thieves Guild (Initiation at the Hidden Chamber) and follow Shadow's Trail through its 7 Seek quests.
  2. Build your Thieves Guild faction reputation through repeatable guild work. Arestes promotes you at the Hidden Chamber at each threshold: Cutpurse (10), Burglar (25), Prowler (50), Infiltrator (80).
  3. Once you've hit Infiltrator, visit the Noble Quarter between 9pm and midnight to trigger "Moving Up in the World" — Shadow introduces you to the district and the heist system opens up from there.

18. How do I get Portia's Villa?

Requires Patreon Adventurer+. Reach 600+ relationship points with Portia and complete her H2H conversation "New Place." After that, a "Visit Portia at the Villa" action appears at the Downtown Apartment Complex between 7pm–11pm (3 times per day).

19. How do I get the Band girls (Cotton, Talena, Nyx) to talk to me?

Earn Band Faction points to unlock conversations with them. Practicing Drums helps build those faction points.

20. What does the Precognition skill do?

It's a combat magic skill — a telepathic link with Kasumi gives fleeting glimpses of danger in battle. You receive it automatically when Kasumi joins you.

21. What does the Bionic Penile Implant do?

Raises your Attractiveness, Beast points, and Erotic Experience. Can be purchased and used multiple times.

22. How do I find Glowcap Mushrooms for Willow?

Search the back alleys — the same area where you first met Willow. The encounter is rare and only appears after you've met Willow. The mushrooms in the dungeon are NOT the same ones. Save/reload or leave and come back if you're having bad RNG luck.

23. How do I recruit Willow to the brothel?

Find the Glowcap Mushrooms in the back alleys and bring them to her at the Secluded Sanctuary. You need the brothel first. Relationship points alone won't trigger recruitment — the mushroom delivery is required.

24. How do I recruit Lime as a maid?

Complete the Wizard Initiation Test — requires high Wizard faction points, enough spells learned, and the Blink skill. It involves 5 wizards. The Gandon revenge quest on Sabbath is NOT this quest. Check the NPC guide in #npc-guides for the full walkthrough.

25. Is Violet recruitable?

Yes, Violet is recruitable now. She is attached to song creation, performances and Bard's Guild faction points.

Brothel & Workers

26. How do I upgrade brothel rooms?

Small upgrade button on each worker's room in the brothel management screen. You can also get random outfit upgrades from the Claw Machine at Christmas Village using tokens — cheaper than Fernando.

27. How do I train Elara?

Build relationship to at least 800 points first. She's an early bird — training is available in the morning. Visit between 8pm–midnight for her evening event.

28. Is there a worker schedule I can view?

Yes — visit Elara's Office in the brothel. It shows each worker's and maid's working hours and their average daily client counts in one place.

29. What do Talking and Praise actions actually do for workers?

Both are limited to once per visit session.

  • Talk: +3 relationship (or +10 with the Intimate Communication skill). 5% chance of a special event (+8 loyalty, +5 worker relationship, +1 NPC relationship, unique dialogue). Separate 25% chance of +1 loyalty normally.
  • Praise: 2 + (worker Charisma ÷ 5) relationship, rounded. Same 5% special and 25% normal loyalty rolls as Talk.

At high loyalty you get daily bonuses including more clients, more income, and more brothel reputation.

30. Will workers leave if loyalty gets too low?

No. Workers never leave the brothel. At loyalty below 30, they can refuse to work on a given day — rolled against each worker's individual refuse chance. A refused day produces 0 income and a flavor message (e.g. "charged clients full price for a lecture about respect"). That's the only consequence.

Crystals & Economy

31. What are all the ways to earn crystals?

  • Redeem codes (WELCOME, BETA2025, VALENTINES, HALLOWEEN, PREMIUM for Patreon tiers, etc.)
  • Max any NPC relationship to In-Love
  • Tutorial Quest System completion bonus
  • Fighters Guild quests
  • Gonzo in the dungeon (sells crystals for Dragon Coins)
  • Kill 50 ratmen then visit Lillian (+1)
  • Complete 25 Hangman games in the Halloween Mansion (+1)
  • Cucker Slayer 500 kills in the farm minigame (+1)
  • Kupie Clicker achievements (+1 each)
  • Caterpillar breeding population milestones: 500k = +1, 5M = +3, 100M = +5
  • Dungeon floor generation (18% chance per floor of a crystal cluster)
  • Wizard Initiation Test: 1 crystal for score ≥17, 3 crystals for score ≥25
  • Patreon tier rewards

32. How do I make money for the big expansions?

Jobs are the best initial source of income and the Brothel can turn a profit after it has been developed, but the Investment System (at the Office Building in Present-Era Downtown) is the fastest way to generate large funds during mid- and end-game. Investments generate enough returns to pay for themselves after about fifty days, but be aware that you can't cash your investments out. Farming the chests (or skulls) in the dungeon on higher floors can also give 800-1000 coins each.

33. Where do I get building materials?

Gather Materials action in Present Era Uptown (45 min, -20 energy). You can also trade 10 ingredients for 5 materials at the Future Prime Sector trade system.

34. Where is Gonzo / the Dragon Coins shop?

Gonzo is a wandering merchant who spawns on dungeon floors with a 10% chance per floor — no fixed location. He sells weapon and armor upgrades (Dragon Coins only), Random Skill Books (5 Dragon Coins, or 4 with the Negotiation skill — you see the skill before buying), and crystals.

Updates, Mods & Unlocks

35. How do I update to a new version without losing my save?

Copy your full save folder from the old version directory into the new version directory before launching.

36. Can I unlock the Extras gallery without playing?

No. Chrono specifically protects this — Vixen spends up to 12 hours a day on the artwork and it is intentionally gated.

37. Is certain content Patreon-only?

Story beats 11+ are gated for Adventure Tier Patreon supporters. The free version includes 50+ hours of content. Characters like Glitch are only accessible through Patreon story progression.

38. Are there mods available?

Yes — check the #mods-and-patches channel. A minigame skip mod is available, and community members have created narrative mods.

39. My Patreon role wasn't assigned in Discord — how do I fix it?

Post your Patreon username in the #role-request channel and it will be assigned manually.

The Game Itself

40. Will pregnancy be added to the game?

Yes, it's planned. No children will appear in the game — handled off-screen. Will be implemented gradually, one character at a time.

41. Is the game coming to Steam?

Yes, targeting a V1.0 Steam launch.

42. Is voice audio planned for scenes?

Requested by the community but no confirmed plans yet.

43. Who made the theme song, and what language is it in?

"Would You" — composed by Chrono, sung by Vixen, lyrics in Mandarin Chinese.

44. Is Sabbath the witch Patreon-only?

No. To see the "Enter Witch's Hut" action in the Whispering Woods, you need Mystic 45 AND Perception 45 — once your stats are high enough, the passage to her hut becomes visible.

这是一份社区常见问题汇总，涵盖最常见的游戏玩法问题。答案以当前游戏版本为准，并尽可能通过代码进行核实。

移动与核心系统

1. 如何在不同时代之间旅行？

时间旅行从游戏开始时便可使用，可从你的家中启动。那里的行动可以带你前往未来（抵达边缘区的实验室）或中世纪（抵达神龛祭坛室）。你也可以使用传送快速移动——随着故事推进，会解锁更多传送目的地。

2. 如何解锁青楼？

青楼是国王任务链的最终奖励，但这条任务链实际上始于Sashina，而不是国王。

  1. 将与Sashina的关系提升至已知以上（25点以上），然后在中世纪上午8点至晚上8点前往神龛祭坛室——这会触发“女术士的告白”。
  2. 在上午10点至下午5点前往中世纪王座厅，触发“国王的挑战”。
  3. 继续马铃薯任务链：先去研究实验室找Sonia，再去农贸市场找Christy，最后把一袋马铃薯交给国王。
  4. 在上午6点至下午6点前往乡村农场，将马铃薯知识告诉Wilfred。
  5. 在上午10点至下午5点返回中世纪王座厅，触发“王室任命”——King Graham会把玫瑰与荆棘青楼授予你。

3. 精力耗尽了，该怎么办？

在卧室睡觉，或者去上城区的城市健身房购买能量饮料。

4. 前期如何赚钱？

在属性尚未达到专长工作要求前，可以在市中心的城市街道街头卖艺——没有要求，收入随创造力提高。达到属性门槛后（通常为30以上），你的专长会解锁对应的专属工作（例如健美者可在健身房担任私人教练，学者可在大学担任教师等）。想要长期积累财富，请使用市中心的投资规划办公室（上午9点至下午5点）。

**隐藏储藏：**不同地点的背景中还藏着一些零钱，只需四处点击即可。以下几个可供起步：你家的客厅（藏在植物花瓶中）、上城区图书馆和上城区健身房。世界各处还散布着许多储藏，等待你偶然发现。

5. 最初应该专注于什么？

跟随通讯标签页——它会追踪当前的故事任务，自然地引导你推进游戏。此外还有15项教程任务，会带你了解游戏的主要系统（样本、投资、武术、Kupie、商队劫匪、Lillian、创意烹饪、Sashina、地下墓穴、青楼、昆虫收集、钓鱼、锻造、时装和女仆指导），是熟悉游戏全部内容的一条优秀结构化路线。

6. 我找到了一条毛虫，该怎么处理？

Chronoromancer拥有一套横跨现代与中世纪的完整毛虫饲养系统。不同地点散布着生成点（低语森林是主要地点之一，而且两个时代都有），之后你可以研究物种、设置栖息地并进行繁育，以获得长期奖励。

属性与技能

7. 每项属性可以在哪里训练？

玩家拥有14项核心属性，每项都有主要训练地点：

  • 力量——城市健身房（上城区），不痛就没有收获
  • 耐力——城市健身房（上城区），在跑步机上跑步
  • 智力——图书馆（上城区），学习课程
  • 魅力——酒吧（市中心），社交练习
  • 能力——射箭场（中世纪），训练能力
  • 创造力——吟游诗人公会（中世纪），培养创造力
  • 感知——上城区公园，自然漫步
  • 神秘——神龛祭坛室（中世纪），与Sashina进行神秘训练
  • 科技——大学计算机实验室（上城区），科技训练
  • 商业——办公室（市中心），学习商业
  • 盗贼——盗贼公会隐藏房间（中世纪），训练盗贼
  • 烹饪——家中厨房，通过烹饪行动提升
  • 沟通——被动成长；每次与NPC交谈时都有小概率提高
  • 运气——进行游戏中的各种行动时逐渐提高

多数属性在世界各处还拥有一次性提升。AIMy会在Discord的#npc-guides论坛中发布完整属性训练指南，其中包含每项属性的完整解析。

8. 如何提高创造力？

可重复：在吟游诗人公会（中世纪）进行培养创造力——60分钟、−45精力、$60、创造力+1。吟游诗人公会的开放时间是下午1点至午夜。一次性：在图书馆阅读奇幻小说（+2）、在吟游诗人公会表演单口喜剧（+1）。拥有音乐家专长的玩家还可以在卧室练习吉他。

9. 如何提高烹饪／解锁创意烹饪？

通过制作简单食物（有概率+1）、准备餐点（必定+1，需要从市中心餐厅学会烹饪食物技能），或从事厨师工作（烹饪30以上）提高烹饪。创意烹饪是一项可在未来边缘区的未登记住宅区学习的技能——需要创造力25以上、$300、220分钟。

10. 哪项技能能让我看到NPC的好感度？在哪里取得？

数据挖掘——会在关系页面直接显示准确的好感度及下一阶段门槛。可在通用知识综合研究所（未来边缘区）学习：180分钟、−60精力／−50镇定、$200、科技20以上。

其他几项技能（识人、人类学、信息掮客、研究、社交）会在关系页面解锁更多NPC信息——完整详情请参阅技能词汇表。

11. 如何提高野兽模式点数？

野兽模式仅限Patreon支持者，可以在游戏中开启额外场景和遭遇。兽性点数可从提供兽性奖励的谈心场景、搜索遭遇、击败并招募地牢小头目（例如招募Whiskers时+1）、仿生阴茎植入体升级和某些水晶奖励中取得。兽性达到5以上时会启用野兽模式，之后相关遭遇可以继续提高该属性。也请留意游戏各处的妓女——她们也是一个来源。

12. 如何找到Glitch的巢穴？

Glitch的巢穴位于未来边缘区，但只能通过主线故事推进解锁（故事节点22），并且需要Patreon冒险等级。没有该权限时，你仍可用龙币向地牢中的Gonzo、后巷的远古智者，或通过Lillian和Seraph处的水晶启迪，学习她的技能（社交、自动化系统、AI编程、量子计算）。

13. Blinky是谁／是什么？如何找到她？

继续游玩即可——她会在故事推进过程中自然出现。不要寻找她，她会找到你。

14. 道场在哪里？

现代市中心，开放时间约为上午8点至下午6点。Master Lilu会在那里教授战斗技能，包括缴械技巧、反击、攻击弱点和步法轻盈。

15. Robin在狩猎小屋的什么地方？

狩猎小屋位于中世纪的狩猎场。查看地图——可以从那里进入。

任务与角色

16. 如何从Sabbath那里取得Gandon的魔法书？

你需要神秘45且感知45，低语森林中才会显示“进入女巫小屋”行动。进入后，Sabbath会给你一瓶特殊的巫师酒（从她库存底部取出，不是酒吧中的酒）。把巫师酒带给巫师塔内部的Gandon（上午10点至晚上8点），触发“蓝袍巫师的灾难”。后续步骤可在通讯标签页追踪。如果你已经拥有Sabbath的内裤，就代表你已经完成这一阶段。

17. 如何解锁贵族区劫案（破门而入）？

贵族区受盗贼公会进度限制。你需要：

  1. 加入盗贼公会（在隐藏房间完成入会仪式），并通过7项搜寻任务追踪Shadow的足迹。
  2. 通过可重复的公会工作提高盗贼公会派系声望。达到每个门槛时，Arestes会在隐藏房间为你晋升：扒手（10）、窃贼（25）、潜行者（50）、渗透者（80）。
  3. 达到渗透者后，在晚上9点至午夜前往贵族区，触发“飞黄腾达”——Shadow会向你介绍该区域，之后劫案系统便会开放。

18. 如何取得Portia的别墅？

需要Patreon冒险者以上。Portia的好感度达到600以上，并且完成她的谈心对话“新住处”。之后，市中心公寓楼会在晚上7点至11点出现“前往别墅拜访Portia”行动（每天3次）。

19. 如何让乐队女孩（Cotton、Talena、Nyx）与我交谈？

取得乐队派系点数，即可解锁与她们的对话。练习架子鼓有助于积累这些派系点数。

20. 预知技能有什么作用？

这是一项战斗魔法技能——与Kasumi的心灵感应联系能让你在战斗中短暂预见危险。Kasumi加入后，你会自动获得。

21. 仿生阴茎植入体有什么作用？

提高吸引力、兽性点数和情色经验值。可以多次购买并使用。

22. 如何为Willow寻找荧光菌菇？

搜索后巷——也就是你最初遇见Willow的区域。该遭遇较为稀有，而且只有结识Willow后才会出现。地牢中的菌菇并不是同一种。随机运气太差时，可以保存／读取，或者离开后再回来。

23. 如何招募Willow加入青楼？

在后巷找到荧光菌菇，并带到隐秘疗舍交给她。你必须先拥有青楼。单靠好感度不会触发招募——必须交付菌菇。

24. 如何招募Lime成为女仆？

完成巫师入门试炼——需要较高的巫师派系点数、学会足够多法术，并掌握闪现技能。试炼涉及5名巫师。Gandon针对Sabbath的复仇任务并不是这项任务。完整攻略请查看#npc-guides中的NPC指南。

25. Violet可以招募吗？

可以，Violet现在已经能够招募。她与歌曲创作、表演和吟游诗人公会派系点数相关。

青楼与员工

26. 如何升级青楼房间？

青楼管理界面中，每个员工房间都有一个小型升级按钮。你还可以使用代币在圣诞村的抓娃娃机随机取得服装升级——比Fernando便宜。

27. 如何训练Elara？

先将好感度提升至至少800点。她习惯早起，训练会在上午开放。晚上8点至午夜前往拜访，可触发她的晚间事件。

28. 是否能查看员工日程？

可以——前往青楼的Elara办公室。其中会集中显示每名员工和女仆的工作时间，以及每日平均接待顾客数。

29. 对员工进行交谈和赞美，实际有什么作用？

两项行动在每次拜访中都只能使用一次。

  • **交谈：**好感度+3（拥有亲密沟通技能时为+10）。有5%概率触发特殊事件（忠诚+8、员工好感度+5、NPC好感度+1、独特对话）。另有独立的25%概率正常获得忠诚+1。
  • **赞美：**好感度增加2＋（员工魅力÷5），四舍五入。特殊事件5%和普通忠诚25%的判定与交谈相同。

忠诚较高时，每天会取得额外客人、更多收入和更多青楼声望等加成。

30. 忠诚太低时，员工会离开吗？

不会。员工永远不会离开青楼。忠诚低于30时，她们可能在某一天拒绝工作——以每名员工各自的拒绝概率进行判定。拒绝工作的当天收入为0，并显示一条风味信息（例如“向客人收取全价，只为给他们上一堂尊重他人的课”）。这是唯一后果。

水晶与经济

31. 有哪些取得水晶的方法？

  • 兑换代码（WELCOME、BETA2025、VALENTINES、HALLOWEEN、PREMIUM为Patreon等级代码等）
  • 将任意NPC关系提升至恋爱中满值
  • 教程任务系统完成奖励
  • 战士公会任务
  • 地牢中的Gonzo（用龙币出售水晶）
  • 击杀50个鼠人后拜访Lillian（+1）
  • 在万圣节宅邸完成25局刽子手游戏（+1）
  • 农场小游戏中，Cucker Slayer击杀500次（+1）
  • Kupie Clicker成就（每项+1）
  • 毛虫繁育种群里程碑：500k＝+1、5M＝+3、100M＝+5
  • 地牢楼层生成（每层有18%概率出现水晶簇）
  • 巫师入门试炼：分数≥17奖励1枚水晶，分数≥25奖励3枚水晶
  • Patreon等级奖励

32. 如何为大型扩建筹钱？

工作是初期最佳收入来源，青楼发展后也能盈利；但到了游戏中后期，投资系统（现代市中心的办公楼）是最快积累大笔资金的方法。投资约五十天后便能产生足以收回成本的收益，但请注意，你无法撤回已投入的本金。在地牢较高楼层反复拾取宝箱（或骷髅），每个也能取得800至1000金币。

33. 在哪里取得建筑材料？

在现代上城区使用收集材料行动（45分钟、−20精力）。你也可以通过未来核心区的交易系统，用10份原料交换5份材料。

34. Gonzo／龙币商店在哪里？

Gonzo是一名流浪商人，每层地牢都有10%概率生成——没有固定位置。他出售武器和护甲升级（只能使用龙币）、随机技能书（5枚龙币；拥有谈判技能时为4枚——购买前可以看到技能），以及水晶。

更新、模组与解锁

35. 如何更新到新版本而不丢失存档？

启动新版本前，将旧版本目录中的整个存档文件夹复制到新版本目录。

36. 可以不游玩就解锁额外内容画廊吗？

不可以。Chrono特意保护了这部分内容——Vixen每天会花最多12小时制作美术，因此画廊经过刻意限制。

37. 某些内容是Patreon专属吗？

故事节点11以后仅对Patreon冒险等级支持者开放。免费版本包含50小时以上的内容。Glitch等角色只能通过Patreon故事进度接触。

38. 有可用的模组吗？

有——查看#mods-and-patches频道。目前有跳过小游戏的模组，社区成员也创作了叙事模组。

39. Discord中没有分配我的Patreon身份组，该如何解决？

在#role-request频道发布你的Patreon用户名，之后会有人手动分配。

关于游戏本身

40. 游戏会加入怀孕内容吗？

会，已经列入计划。游戏中不会出现儿童——相关内容会在画面外处理。该功能会逐步实现，每次加入一名角色。

41. 游戏会登陆Steam吗？

会，目标是在V1.0版本登陆Steam。

42. 场景会加入语音吗？

社区提出过相关请求，但目前尚无确定计划。

43. 主题曲由谁创作？使用什么语言？

“Would You”——由Chrono作曲、Vixen演唱，歌词使用普通话中文。

44. 女巫Sabbath是Patreon专属角色吗？

不是。你需要神秘45且感知45，低语森林中才会显示“进入女巫小屋”行动——属性足够高后，通往她小屋的道路便会出现。
