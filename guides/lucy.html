Overview

Lucy’s route uses both affection and Dance Studio Clout. Her repeatable Dance Lesson is the practical source of that Clout and also raises Ability.

Dance & Yoga Studio12:00–17:00—

Route and relationship milestones

Take Dance Lessons at Known (25) and Stamina 30: each costs $200, 45 minutes and 20 Energy, granting Ability +1, 4 base affection and 1 Studio Clout, normally twice a day.

The personal H2H gates then use 25 Clout at Known, 30 at Close Friend (200), and 40 at Crush (400). Drive Her Home also needs a Sports Car and a 16:00–17:00 start. These Clout numbers are thresholds and are not spent.

Take Her Home requires 1200 affection and the Drive Her Home H2H. This is a specific point requirement below Intimate (1600); use the actual button if your exact point total is hidden.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Ability 50; Creativity 45; Commerce 55

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • For a missing H2H, check Studio Clout as well as affection; general money or unrelated faction Clout cannot replace it.
  • Bring the Sports Car for the drive and a Voucher for the separate prize date.

概览

Lucy 的路线同时检查好感度与舞蹈工作室声望。可重复的舞蹈课是提升该声望的实用途径，也会增加能力。

路线与关系节点

已知（25）、耐力 30 时可上舞蹈课，每次消耗 $200、45 分钟与 20 精力，获得能力 +1、4 基础好感度及 1 工作室声望，常规为每天两次。

个人谈心分别要求已知阶段与 25 声望、亲密朋友（200）与 30 声望、迷恋（400）与 40 声望。送她回家还要求跑车，并在 16:00–17:00 开始。这些声望都是门槛，不会扣除。

带她回家要求 1200 好感度，并完成送她回家的谈心。这是低于亲密（1600）的具体分数要求；若界面不显示精确分数，可根据按钮是否出现判断。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。

能力 50; 创造力 45; 商业 55

偏好技能：杂技

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择还可能在事件完成基础奖励之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

  • 谈心未出现时，除了好感度，也要检查工作室声望；金钱或其他派系声望不能代替。
  • 开车行动要有跑车，独立奖品约会则需携带兑换券。
