Overview

Mandarin is the 24-year-old warrior maid of the Fire Element — a strong-willed protector with vibrant orange hair and intense orange eyes that assess strength and weakness on first glance. She's recruited from the deepest parts of the dungeon, where you find her calmly mopping the stone floor with a bucket, completely unbothered by the demons watching from the shadows. They don't dare approach. They're afraid of her.

She's the most direct, blunt, and protective of the five maids — she doesn't do small talk, doesn't play social games, and judges people entirely by their actions. Trained by General Ironheart in Fire Element philosophy: "strength with purpose. Raw power means nothing without something worth protecting." After watching you defeat a Greater Demon to free her, she accepts your offer and joins as Maid #2.

She covers the 10 AM – 2 PM brothel cleaning shift (the second of five), and her arc is built around the player having earned her respect through demonstrated strength rather than charm.

She's coded as evil-aligned — a thematic match for her dungeon-dwelling, intimidation-based protective philosophy.

Where to Find Her

Mandarin has no pre-recruitment location — unlike Violet, you don't build relationship with her before recruitment. Her acquisition is a one-time dungeon scenario, then she shows up at the brothel.

Brothel Maids' Quarters (Post-Recruitment Only)

  • Hours: 10 AM – 2 PM, every day
  • Her permanent home and shift after joining the brothel
  • Covers the late-morning/early-afternoon cleaning slot (second of the five maids)
  • Hands off to Lychee at 2 PM and takes over from Violet at 10 AM (the Violet + Mandarin Shift Change plot fires here)

How to Recruit Her — The Greater Demon Scenario

Mandarin's recruitment is the dungeon scenario scenario_mandarin_encounter. Unlike Violet's faction-rep gate, this is an encounter-based recruitment — you find her while exploring the dungeon.

Trigger Conditions

  • Location: Dungeon (deeper exploration)
  • Pre-requisite: Brothel must be owned (so you have somewhere to offer her)
  • The encounter triggers as part of dungeon exploration

The Setup

You hear soft humming echoing from a nearby chamber. Peering around the corner, you see an extraordinary sight: a young woman with striking orange hair calmly cleaning the stone floor with a mop and bucket, completely unbothered by her dangerous surroundings. In the shadows behind her, several demons watch with obvious lustful interest, but none dare approach. They seem afraid of her quiet confidence.

She notices you, looks up with calm assessing eyes, and says matter-of-factly: "Oh, hello there. I'm Mandarin. I'm cleaning."

Three-Choice Opening (No Mechanical Difference)

  • Concern: Ask if she needs help leaving this dangerous place
  • Offer: Offer her work at your brothel (only available if you own the brothel)
  • Curiosity: Ask why she's cleaning in such a dangerous place

She declines all three — she likes the danger, she's content where she is, and the demons don't bother her ("They know better"). The brothel offer is "safer than a dungeon full of demons," but she prefers the unpredictability.

The Greater Demon Appears

Before you can press the issue, the largest demon in the shadows suddenly steps forward — a massive Greater Demon with burning eyes and flames flickering around clawed hands.

"WHO DARES SPEAK TO MY MAID? She belongs to ME! I have claimed her as my personal servant!"

Mandarin sighs and rolls her eyes ("Here we go again") and tells the demon firmly: "I don't belong to anyone. I clean here because I choose to." The demon ignores her completely and threatens to kill you for daring to covet his "property."

Mandarin steps back, crosses her arms, and watches with professional interest.

"Well, this should be entertaining. Let's see what you're made of."

The Boss Fight

You are forced to fight the Greater Demon (boss flag set, no flee/sleep allowed). Stats:

  • Base HP: 115 (+ dungeon level × 6)
  • Skills: Claw, Flame Breath, Unholy Strike, Dark Pulse, Firestorm (telegraphed)
  • Strong vs: Fire (75% absorb)
  • Weak vs: Light
  • Damage multiplier: 1.8× (a heavy hitter)
  • Magic Resistance: 30
  • Telegraphed Firestorm: When his rage breaks, hellfire erupts. "Mandarin backs away, wide-eyed. 'Oh no. Not this...'"

There's also a negotiation choice that doesn't actually work — the demon refuses to talk. The fight happens either way.

Victory and Recruitment

The Greater Demon collapses, defeated. Mandarin walks over, examines him with professional detachment ("He'll live, but he won't be bothering anyone for quite some time"), and turns to you with newfound respect:

"Now THAT was impressive. I've never seen someone handle a Greater Demon with such skill and determination. You know what? I'm reconsidering your offer."

Three closing-choice variants (all lead to the same recruitment):

  • Ask if she'd like to work at your brothel
  • Tell her you're impressed by her composure
  • Offer to protect her from other demons

She accepts: "Mandarin reporting for duty. I'll bring my cleaning supplies and my standards. Your establishment will be spotless."

She joins as Maid #2. Her schedule activates immediately: 10 AM – 2 PM at the brothel Maids' Quarters daily.

Why this is unique: Mandarin has no pre-recruitment relationship phase. There's no Watch and Clean equivalent at the dungeon. She joins the brothel already convinced you've earned her respect — having proven yourself in combat is the entire courtship.

Bonus Math (Exact Calculation Order)

The relationship point formula runs in this order:

  1. Start with base points from the action
  2. +1 per favorite stat met (Stamina 45, Ability 40, Strength 35 — up to +3)
  3. × 2 if the player has Intimidating Presence skill (her special/favorite skill)
  4. +1 if player owns the "1 Ring" key item
  5. +1 if Cologne buff is active
  6. +1 if Buffer Optimization level is active
  7. +1 chance roll based on Attractiveness (capped at 50%, or 60% with Ornate Locket)

Worked example: Maid Service H2H (base 15), all 3 favorite stats met, Intimidating Presence learned: (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points per H2H — at daily limit 3, that's +108/day base.

Min-max insight: Talk's daily limit of 30 matches Violet's — fastest stage-progression grind in the maid system. With Intimidating Presence + favorite stats, you can hit 100+ relationship/day from Talk alone.

Voice & Personality Highlights

Mandarin's dialogue is direct, clipped, professional — she never uses contractions sloppily, never gossips, never plays at being something she isn't:

  • Universal: Short declarative sentences, no small talk, simple statements, results-focused
  • General Ironheart references: "General Ironheart taught me Fire Element means strength with purpose."
  • Element philosophy: Fire is protection through controlled strength. "The strong exist to shield the weak. That's not weakness — that's responsibility."
  • Intimidation as method: "Fear is a tool. When enemies fear you, fewer people get hurt overall."
  • Cleaning as strategy: "Every surface I clean gets inspected for weaknesses, hiding spots, potential weapons. Organized spaces are defensible spaces."
  • Prophecy awareness (blunt): "The prophecy mentioned five maidens serving together. We're here. Coincidence or destiny? Don't care — works either way."
  • Respect for player: "You proved yourself against that demon. Actions speak louder than words. I follow strength I respect, not weakness that demands obedience."

Sister Dynamics (post other maids acquired)

  • Violet: "Too loud and dramatic. Effective cleaning requires focus, not performances." (eventually softens — "Violet's dramatic flair is actually excellent misdirection. Enemies won't see her real capabilities.")
  • Lychee: "Wastes time on appearance when efficiency matters more. Soft." (later: "Lychee's attention to appearance includes spotting details others miss. Useful reconnaissance skill.")
  • Lime: "Strange mystic stuff, but I respect her commitment. Needs to be more assertive." (later: "Lime's mystical knowledge helps predict problems before they happen. Strategic advantage.")
  • Rain: "Smart. Systematic. I can work with her methods. Still too theoretical." (later: "Rain's systematic approach creates efficient defensive patterns. Logical force multiplication.")

She approaches the maid sisterhood the same way she approaches everything: tactically. The other four bring useful capabilities; she's the muscle and the security layer.

The Arrival at the Brothel

Unlike Violet, Mandarin does not have a dedicated arrival plot. The recruitment scenario itself is the arrival — once she accepts, she immediately joins your brothel and shows up the next morning at 10 AM.

Note: The mandarin_arrives_brothel.json plot file exists in the plots folder, suggesting the arrival scene may be triggered separately at first visit to the Maids' Quarters post-recruitment. Check there if you don't see it during the dungeon scenario.

Recommended Progression Path

Phase 1: Find Her in the Dungeon

  • Explore the dungeon until the Mandarin encounter triggers
  • Your brothel must be owned for the work offer to appear
  • Choose any conversation path — the demon appears regardless
  • Defeat the Greater Demon (boss fight, no flee/sleep)
  • Mandarin joins as Maid #2

Phase 2: Stranger → Acknowledged → Known

  • Visit her at the Brothel Maids' Quarters 10 AM – 2 PM daily
  • Spam Talk (+2 base, daily 30)
  • Push Communication to 45 for Strength in Service

Phase 3: Known → Acquaintance → Friend

  • Run Strength in Service at Comm 45: +1 Strength permanent, +10 rel
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Push favorite stats: Stamina 45, Ability 40, Strength 35
  • Cross-maid plots fire as you recruit more maids:
    • Violet + Mandarin Shift Change (10 AM transition, automatic)
    • Lime + Mandarin Protection (Acquaintance+ with both)
    • Three Maidens First Meeting (Mandarin/Lychee/Rain all join)

Phase 4: Friend → Close Friend → Crush

  • Continue daily Talk
  • Learn Intimidating Presence skill for permanent point doubling
  • Maids Compete Bedroom plot fires at Friend+ with all three (Mandarin/Lychee/Lime, Master Bedroom)

Phase 5: Intimate (Maid Service Unlocks)

  • Maid Service H2H unlocks at Intimate stage (+15 base, daily 3)
  • Continue daily Talk
  • Whispers of Six plot fires at Close Friend+ with all five maids

Phase 6: Endgame

  • Run Maid Service daily (+15 × 3 = +45 base/day)
  • Equip her with Mandarin's Iron Will epic item (Kupie Love Training reward) — +15 Cleaning, −8 Mess/day, +10% Cleaning — for a brothel-wide cleaning powerhouse build

Equipment — Mandarin's Signature Item

Mandarin's Iron Will (Epic Tier)

  • Source: Kupie Love Training achievement
  • Maid: Mandarin only
  • Effects: +15 Cleaning (one), −8 Mess/day, +10% Cleaning
  • The flat +15 cleaning + 10% cleaning combo makes Mandarin one of the highest-output single-maid builds in the system
  • The −8 Mess/day is brothel-wide — every other maid benefits

Recommended Stack

  • Slot 1 (L1+): Sturdy Broom ($300, +3 cleaning) early; replace with Master Mop (Maid Mentor reward) or Mandarin's Iron Will at L4
  • Slot 2 (L4+): Lucky Coin Pouch (+15% Cleaning Variance) for predictable output, or Mentorship Manual (+25% Apprentice EXP) since Mandarin's protective philosophy fits a master role

Tips & Optimal Play

  • No pre-recruitment grind — Mandarin is encounter-based. You can't build relationship with her before the demon fight. Just find her, fight, and recruit.
  • The Greater Demon is a real boss fight — 115 HP base + (dungeon level × 6), 1.8× damage multiplier, telegraphed Firestorm. Don't trigger the encounter before you can survive a real fight.
  • Her demon is Fire-strong — it has a 75% chance to absorb a Fire spell, healing for its full resolved damage on a proc. Use Light element if you have it; Lightning or other non-Fire attacks avoid that absorption roll.
  • Talk's 30/day daily limit is huge — fastest maid stage-progression grind. With Intimidating Presence + 3 favorite stats, easily 100+ relationship/day.
  • Strength in Service is free +1 Strength permanent — Comm 45 gate. Don't miss it; Strength 35 is also her favorite stat.
  • Intimidating Presence skill doubles all her points — keep it learned. Thematically perfect for the warrior maid.
  • Her favorite stats are mid-tier (Stamina 45, Ability 40, Strength 35). Stamina 45 is the highest threshold — push it with general training.
  • Mandarin's Iron Will epic item is the brothel-wide cleaning powerhouse — +15 flat cleaning + 10% on her, plus −8 mess globally. Pursue Kupie Love Training for it.
  • She doesn't do small talk — her dialogue is short, declarative, professional. Players who expect maid banter sometimes find her cold. That's the character.
  • No animations, no dates — Mandarin's content is purely brothel-based: Talk, Strength in Service, Maid Service H2H. Like all maids, the romance is the relationship grind, not extensive content.
  • All 6 images are interchangeable — her intensity is constant. She doesn't soften visually as she warms up to you; the change is only in dialogue.
  • She's evil-aligned — fits her dungeon-dwelling, intimidation-as-method philosophy. No alignment shift quests; she's just always evil.

概述

Mandarin是24岁的火元素战士女仆——一名意志坚定的守护者，拥有鲜艳的橙色头发和锐利的橙色眼睛，第一眼就会评估对方的强项与弱点。玩家会在地下城最深处招募她；在那里，她正提着水桶，泰然自若地拖洗石头地面，完全不在意阴影中注视自己的恶魔。那些恶魔不敢靠近，因为它们害怕她。

她是五位女仆中最直接、最坦率，也最具保护欲的一位——她不闲聊、不玩社交游戏，只通过行动评判他人。她曾受General Ironheart教导火元素哲学：“有目标的力量。没有值得保护的东西，蛮力便毫无意义。”看到你击败高阶恶魔并解救她后，她会接受邀请，加入青楼成为2号女仆。

她负责青楼上午10点至下午2点的清洁轮班（五班中的第二班）。她的故事围绕玩家凭借展示出的力量赢得尊重，而非依靠魅力展开。

她在代码中属于邪恶阵营——这与她居住地下城、以威慑为基础的守护哲学相当契合。

在哪里能找到她

Mandarin没有招募前地点——与Violet不同，招募前无法和她培养关系。她会通过一次性的地下城情景加入，随后出现在青楼。

  • 加入青楼后的永久住所和轮班地点
  • 负责上午晚些时候／下午早些时候的清洁时段（五位女仆中的第二班）

如何招募她——高阶恶魔情景

Mandarin的招募内容是地下城情景scenario_mandarin_encounter。这与Violet的派系声望门槛不同，属于遭遇式招募——玩家会在探索地下城时发现她。

触发条件

  • 地点： 地下城（深入探索）
  • 前置条件： 必须拥有青楼（这样才有地方邀请她工作）
  • 该遭遇会在地下城探索过程中触发

开场

附近房间传来轻柔的哼唱声。你从拐角探头，看到一幅非同寻常的景象：一名有着醒目橙色头发的年轻女性正拿着拖把和水桶，泰然自若地清洁石头地面，对危险环境毫不在意。她身后的阴影里，有几个恶魔明显带着欲望注视着她，却没有一个胆敢靠近。 它们似乎畏惧她沉静的自信。

她注意到你，以平静而审视的目光抬起头，直截了当地说：“哦，你好。我是Mandarin。我在打扫。”

三种开场选项（机制上没有区别）

  • 担心： 询问她是否需要协助离开这个危险的地方
  • 邀请： 邀请她到你的青楼工作（仅拥有青楼时可选）
  • 好奇： 询问她为什么要在如此危险的地方打扫

三个提议都会被拒绝——她喜欢危险，对现状很满意，恶魔也不会打扰她（“它们知道不该乱来”）。青楼的确*“比满是恶魔的地下城安全”*，但她更喜欢这里的不可预测性。

高阶恶魔出现

还没等你继续劝说，阴影中最大的恶魔突然走上前——那是一头巨大的高阶恶魔，双眼燃烧，利爪周围跳动着火焰。

“谁敢和我的女仆说话？她属于我！我已经指定她做我的私人仆从！”

Mandarin叹气并翻了个白眼（“又来了”），坚定地告诉恶魔：“我不属于任何人。我在这里打扫，是因为我自己愿意。” 恶魔完全无视她，并威胁要杀死胆敢觊觎其“所有物”的你。

Mandarin后退一步，双臂交叉，带着专业兴趣观看。

“好吧，这应该会很有意思。让我看看你有多少本事。”

首领战

你被迫与高阶恶魔战斗（设置首领标记，不能逃跑／睡觉）。属性如下：

  • 基础HP： 115＋地下城等级×6
  • 技能： 利爪、火焰吐息、邪恶打击、黑暗脉冲、火焰风暴（会预警）
  • 强项： 火（75%吸收）
  • 弱点： 光
  • 伤害倍率： 1.8×（重击型敌人）
  • 魔法抗性： 30
  • 预警火焰风暴： 怒气爆发时，地狱火焰会喷涌而出。“Mandarin睁大眼睛向后退去。‘噢，不。别来这个……’”

这里还有一个实际上无效的谈判选项——恶魔拒绝交谈。无论如何都会进入战斗。

胜利与招募

高阶恶魔战败倒地。Mandarin走上前，以专业而冷静的态度检查它（“它死不了，但很长一段时间都不会再来烦任何人”），随后带着全新的尊重转向你：

“刚才那一下真令人印象深刻。我从未见过有人能以如此技巧和决心对付高阶恶魔。知道吗？我要重新考虑你的邀请了。”

结尾有三个选项（全都会成功招募）：

  • 询问她是否愿意到你的青楼工作
  • 告诉她，你对她的镇定印象深刻
  • 提出保护她免受其他恶魔侵扰

她接受邀请：“Mandarin前来报到。我会带上清洁用品和自己的标准。你的场所会一尘不染。”

她会成为2号女仆。 日程立即启用：每日上午10点至下午2点出现在青楼女仆宿舍。

独特之处： Mandarin没有招募前的关系阶段。地下城中也没有类似“观看与清扫”的行动。她加入青楼时，已经相信你赢得了她的尊重——用战斗证明自己，就是完整的追求过程。

交谈

主要的日常行动。会触发Mandarin的三类对话系统（日常闲聊／尚未找到其他女仆／已找到其他女仆）。她的对话涉及：将清洁视为安保、威胁评估、地下城经历、直接沟通的理念，以及职业自豪感。

服务中的力量 （仅限一次）

她的行动组中唯一能永久提升属性的一项。要求达到已知阶段且沟通45以上。奖励：永久＋1力量、好感度＋10。

“General Ironheart教过我，火元素意味着有目标的力量。没有值得保护的东西，蛮力便毫无意义。训练身体不是为了自己，而是为了让其他人可以依靠你。真正的力量，是永不破裂的盾牌，是永不倒塌的高墙。”

加成计算（准确顺序）

好感度公式按以下顺序计算：

  1. 从行动的基础点数开始
  2. 每满足一项偏好属性＋1（耐力45、能力40、力量35——最多＋3）
  3. 玩家拥有威慑气场技能（她的特殊／偏好技能）时**×2**
  4. 玩家拥有**“1 Ring”关键物品时＋1**
  5. 古龙水增益生效时**＋1**
  6. 缓冲优化等级生效时**＋1**
  7. 根据吸引力进行一次概率判定，成功则**＋1**（上限50%；持有华丽挂坠时为60%）

计算示例： 女仆服务H2H（基础15）、满足全部3项偏好属性，并已学会威慑气场： (15 + 3) × 2 = 36 points——H2H每日上限3次，也就是每天基础＋108。

极限优化心得： “交谈”的每日30次上限与Violet相同——这是女仆系统中推进关系阶段最快的刷取方式。叠加威慑气场和偏好属性后，单靠交谈每天就能获得100以上好感度。

说话方式与性格亮点

Mandarin的对话直接、简短而专业——她不会随便使用缩略语，不会说闲话，也绝不会假装成并非自己的样子：

  • 通用特点： 简短的陈述句，不闲聊，表达简单，以结果为中心
  • 提及General Ironheart： “General Ironheart教过我，火元素意味着有目标的力量。”
  • 元素哲学： 火是通过受控力量实现保护。“强者的存在就是为了保护弱者。那不是软弱——而是责任。”
  • 以威慑为手段： “恐惧是一种工具。敌人畏惧你时，总体上受伤的人会更少。”
  • 将清洁视为策略： “我清洁每处表面时，都会检查弱点、藏身处和潜在武器。井然有序的空间更容易防守。”
  • 直白看待预言： “预言提到五位少女会共同侍奉。我们都在这里。巧合还是命运？无所谓——两种说法都行。”
  • 尊重玩家： “你在那个恶魔面前证明了自己。行动胜于言语。我追随自己尊重的力量，而不是要求别人服从的软弱。”

姐妹互动（获得其他女仆后）

  • Violet： “太吵，也太爱表演。有效的清洁需要专注，不需要演出。”（后来会缓和——“Violet夸张的风格其实是极好的障眼法。敌人不会看见她真正的能力。”）
  • Lychee： “效率更重要，她却把时间浪费在外表上。软弱。”（后来：“Lychee对外表的关注，也包括发现别人忽略的细节。很实用的侦察技能。”）
  • Lime： “神秘的东西很奇怪，但我尊重她的投入。她需要更加果断。”（后来：“Lime的神秘知识有助于提前预测问题。战略优势。”）
  • Rain： “聪明，系统性强。我可以配合她的方法。不过还是太理论化。”（后来：“Rain的系统化方法能形成高效的防御模式。合乎逻辑的战力倍增。”）

她对待女仆姐妹情谊的方式与其他一切相同：从战术角度出发。另外四人各有实用能力；她则负责提供力量和安全保障。

抵达青楼

与Violet不同，Mandarin没有专属的抵达剧情。招募情景本身就是她的到来——一旦接受，她就会立即加入青楼，并于第二天上午10点出现。

备注： 剧情文件夹中存在mandarin_arrives_brothel.json文件，说明招募后首次造访女仆宿舍时，抵达场景可能会单独触发。如果在地下城情景中没有看到，请前往那里检查。

推荐推进路线

阶段1：在地下城找到她

  • 探索地下城，直到Mandarin遭遇触发
  • 必须拥有青楼，才会出现工作邀请
  • 选择任意对话路线——恶魔无论如何都会出现
  • 击败高阶恶魔（首领战，不能逃跑／睡觉）
  • Mandarin加入并成为2号女仆

阶段2：陌生人→已遇见→已知

  • 每日上午10点至下午2点前往青楼女仆宿舍找她
  • 反复使用交谈（基础＋2、每日30次）
  • 将沟通提升至45，以解锁服务中的力量

阶段3：已知→相识→朋友

  • 沟通45时进行服务中的力量：永久＋1力量、好感度＋10
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 提升偏好属性：耐力45、能力40、力量35
  • 招募更多女仆后会触发女仆联动剧情：
    • Violet＋Mandarin换班（上午10点交班时自动触发）
    • Lime＋Mandarin的保护（两人均达到相识以上）
    • 三位少女初次见面（Mandarin／Lychee／Rain全部加入）

阶段4：朋友→亲密朋友→迷恋

  • 继续每日交谈
  • 学会威慑气场技能，使点数永久翻倍
  • Mandarin／Lychee／Lime三人均达到朋友以上后，女仆卧室竞赛剧情会在主卧室触发

阶段5：亲密（解锁女仆服务）

  • 女仆服务H2H在亲密阶段解锁（基础＋15、每日3次）
  • 继续每日交谈
  • 五位女仆均达到亲密朋友以上后，会触发六之低语剧情

阶段6：终局

  • 每日进行女仆服务（＋15×3＝每天基础＋45）
  • 为她装备史诗物品Mandarin的钢铁意志（Kupie爱之训练奖励）——＋15清洁、每天－8脏乱、＋10%清洁——打造覆盖整个青楼的清洁强者

装备——Mandarin的标志物品

Mandarin的钢铁意志（史诗级）

  • 来源： Kupie爱之训练成就
  • 女仆： 仅限Mandarin
  • 效果： ＋15清洁（单人）、每天－8脏乱、＋10%清洁
  • 固定＋15清洁和＋10%清洁的组合，让Mandarin成为系统中单名女仆产出最高的流派之一
  • 每天－8脏乱对整个青楼生效——其他所有女仆都能受益

推荐搭配

  • 槽位1（L1以上）： 前期使用结实扫帚（$300、清洁＋3）；到L4时换成大师拖把（女仆导师奖励）或Mandarin的钢铁意志
  • 槽位2（L4以上）： 使用幸运钱币袋（清洁浮动＋15%）以获得可预测的产出；也可以选择导师手册（学徒经验＋25%），因为Mandarin的守护哲学很适合担任导师

提示与最佳玩法

  • 无需招募前刷取——Mandarin通过遭遇招募。在恶魔战前无法和她培养关系。只需找到她、战斗并完成招募。
  • 高阶恶魔是真正的首领战——基础115HP＋地下城等级×6、1.8×伤害倍率，并拥有会预警的火焰风暴。没有能力挺过真正的战斗前，不要触发这场遭遇。
  • 她的恶魔擅长火元素——它有75%概率吸收火焰法术，触发时会恢复该次攻击结算的全部伤害。如果拥有光元素，请优先使用；闪电或其他非火攻击也能避开这项吸收判定。
  • 交谈每天30次的上限极高——这是女仆关系阶段最快的刷取方式。叠加威慑气场和3项偏好属性后，每天很容易获得100以上好感度。
  • 服务中的力量会免费永久＋1力量——要求沟通45。不要错过；力量35同时也是她的偏好属性。
  • 威慑气场技能会使她的所有点数翻倍——务必保留该技能。它也与战士女仆的主题完美契合。
  • 她的偏好属性处于中等门槛（耐力45、能力40、力量35）。耐力45是最高门槛——通过通用训练提高它。
  • Mandarin的钢铁意志是能惠及整个青楼的强力清洁物品——她自己获得固定＋15清洁和＋10%，全局脏乱则－8。请为此完成Kupie爱之训练。
  • 她不闲聊——对话简短、直白而专业。期待女仆间轻松闲聊的玩家有时会觉得她冷漠。这就是她的性格。
  • 没有动画，也没有约会——Mandarin的内容完全围绕青楼展开：交谈、服务中的力量、女仆服务H2H。与所有女仆相同，她的恋爱路线重在刷取好感度，而不是大量独立内容。
  • 全部6张图片可以交替使用——她的强烈气场始终不变。她逐渐向你敞开心扉时，外表不会变得柔和；变化只体现在对话中。
  • 她属于邪恶阵营——这与她居住地下城、以威慑为手段的哲学相符。没有改变阵营的任务；她始终都是邪恶阵营。
