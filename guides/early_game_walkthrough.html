A complete guide to your first major milestones in Chronoromancer. This walkthrough takes a non-Patreon player from character creation through the 15 Tutorial Quests, the Brothel acquisition, Story Beats 1-10, dungeon Floor 10, full romance arcs with Jessie and Dr. Akira, and partial progress with Emily and Bella. Everything here is achievable in the free version of the game.

Section 1 — Getting Started: The Essentials

This section covers everything you need to know in your first in-game day. It's the foundation the rest of the walkthrough builds on.

What Kind of Game Is This?

Chronoromancer is a time-travel sandbox RPG. You play as a character with the unique ability to warp between three eras — the modern Present, a fantasy Medieval kingdom, and a dystopian Future — and each era has its own map, NPCs, economy, jobs, skills, and storylines.

Unlike a linear visual novel, there is no "right" order. You're free to chase relationships, grind stats, dungeon-crawl, fish, collect caterpillars, run a brothel, or investigate the main story at your own pace. This walkthrough gives you an efficient route through the early game without missing major content.

Character Creation

At the start of a new game, you'll choose your character's name, appearance, gender, and — most importantly — your Proficiency. Proficiency is a permanent choice that defines your starting skills, starting stats, and unlocks a career path that becomes your best source of income once the corresponding stat hits 30.

There are 11 proficiencies. Each starts you with a signature skill that doubles relationship points with the matching romance interests and unlocks proficiency-specific story content. A short summary:

Recommendations for this walkthrough:

  • Scholar is the single most efficient starting choice. The Researching skill doubles relationship points with characters like Dr. Akira, Sonia, and several others — including making early Story Beats easier.
  • Martial Artist makes early combat and the Tutorial Quest dojo training trivial, and Lilu (Story Beat 8) responds well to martial characters.
  • Salesman gives you Networking, which reveals every NPC's favorite skill on the relationship screen — a huge efficiency boost for romance routes.
  • Player grants Reading People, which unlocks hidden NPC descriptions and advanced dialogue.

There's no wrong pick, but if this is your first playthrough and you want to follow this walkthrough efficiently, pick Scholar or Salesman.

Understanding the UI

After character creation you'll wake up in your Apartment in Present Uptown. Take a moment to get oriented:

Top Left — Time Display Shows the current in-game time (24-hour clock) and day counter. Actions advance time by a set number of minutes. The game runs on a 24-hour clock from midnight to midnight.

Top Right — Stats Panel Your four variable stats you'll interact with constantly:

  • Energy — Spent by most actions. Restore by sleeping.
  • Health — Damaged by combat and some scenarios. Restore by sleeping or healing items.
  • Calm — Your mental/emotional state. Some actions require a minimum calm level.
  • Wealth — Your money. Core currency for purchases, upgrades, and gifts.

Bottom — Action Buttons Every location has a list of available actions. Actions are filtered automatically based on:

  • Current time of day
  • Required stats, skills, or items
  • Relationship stages with NPCs
  • Story progression flags

If an action isn't showing up, one of those conditions isn't met.

Tutorial Quest Icon (right of stats panel) A glowing icon for the 15 Tutorial Quests. Click it any time to check progress and claim completed rewards. The icon disappears after you finish all 15.

Map Access From any outdoor location, you can open the area map. From there you can reach other districts in the same era. To travel between eras you'll need to unlock warping (explained below).

Stats cap increases slowly over time — you'll typically finish the early game with your primary stat at 30-50.

Relationships: How They Work

Every major NPC has a relationship stage ladder:

Stranger → Acknowledged → Known → Acquaintance → Friend → Close Friend → Crush → Romantic → Intimate → In Love

You climb the ladder by accumulating relationship points through actions, gifts, plot scenes, and dates. Each NPC has a required point total to advance to each stage.

Three key multipliers determine how much each action is worth:

  1. Favorite Stats — Each NPC has 2-3 preferred stats with a threshold (usually 45). Meeting each adds +1 to the base action points. Use the Researching skill to see these on the relationship screen.

  2. Favorite Skill — Every NPC has one signature skill that doubles the total points earned after favorite-stat bonuses are added. Use the Networking skill to see this on the relationship screen.

  3. Attractiveness chance + buffs — Cologne, Buffer Optimization, certain key items, and high Attractiveness all give small bonus points or per-action chance bonuses.

Efficiency tip: Learn Researching and Networking as early as possible. They literally show you the cheat sheet for every romance route.

The Three Eras at a Glance

You'll spend time in all three during the early game. A quick tour:

Present Era

The modern city, split into two districts:

Uptown — Your apartment, the Hospital (Dr. Akira), the University, the Gym, the Cafe (Jessie), the Beach, the Mall (Emily and Bella), the Community Center, the Farmers Market, the Research Lab, the Convenience Store, and more. This is where you spend most of your first 10 in-game days.

Downtown — The Bar, Nightclub, Dojo, Restaurant, Dance Studio, Offices (investment system), Music Studio, Back Alley, Police Station, Mansion Foyer, Spa, and Arcade. Most Story Beats take you here starting around Beat 6.

The Present is your home base. Modern money ($), modern goods, and most of the "daily life" systems live here.

Medieval Era

The fantasy kingdom, split into:

King's Demesne — The royal center. Throne Room, Marketplace, Fighters Guild, Thieves Guild, Bards Guild, Arcane Shop (magic), Tailor Shop (Fernando), Blacksmith (Gastoff), General Goods, Cobbler, Noble Quarter, and the Rowdy Alehouse (Vixen).

Outskirts — The countryside. Rural Farm (Wilfred and Honey), Fishing Village (Marina), Monk's Temple, Hunting Grounds, Archery Range (Robin), Witch's Hut (Sabbath), Ancient Earth Shrine (Sashina), and more.

Medieval uses its own gold currency. No electricity, no modern technology. This is also where the dungeon is located — beneath the Monastery — and where you'll acquire the Brothel later in this walkthrough.

Future Era

The dystopian sci-fi city, split into:

Prime Sector — The corporate-controlled upper city. Synergy Corp offices, holographic entertainment, high-tech shops, and the Synthetic Beings Recharge Station.

Fringe Zone — The underground resistance-era slums. Your secret lab (where Nova and AIMy are based), the Unregistered Housing Complex (Creative Cooking skill), Glitch's Den, the Resistance Hideout (Ghost), the Waste Processing Zone, Orchid's Distribution Center, and more.

The Future uses credits. You'll spend less time here early on, but Story Beats 3, 14, and beyond are all Future-based, and several Tutorial Quests require you to visit.

Warping Between Eras

At the start of the game you're locked to the Present era. Warping unlocks through the prologue/tutorial sequence, after which you can travel freely between eras from your Apartment or from certain other trigger points.

Once unlocked, warping is instant and costs no energy — the time doesn't advance when you switch eras, so don't hesitate to jump around. Each era maintains its own separate clock for time-of-day triggers, but game days advance together.

Key point: Each NPC exists in ONE era only. Jessie is only in the Present. Sashina is only in the Medieval. Ghost is only in the Future. Plan visits accordingly.

Time, Days, and the Doomsday Clock

The Day Cycle

Each in-game day runs 24 hours. Actions cost minutes (or hours). Sleep restores energy and advances to the next morning. Many plot triggers require a specific time window — for example, "Jessie's Rebound" only triggers between 7 PM and 10 PM, and most shops close by 9 PM.

Some NPCs also have specific working hours. Dr. Akira is at the Hospital 6 AM-7 PM every day. Gastoff the blacksmith works 6 AM-9 PM. Marina at the fishing hut is 4 AM-5 PM. If you show up outside an NPC's hours, you'll find an empty location.

The Doomsday Clock

Chronoromancer has a game-over timer. You start with a set number of days, and if the counter reaches zero, the game ends. Don't panic — the timer is generous, and there are many ways to extend it.

Crystals are the currency that buys you more time. Both Seraph (Future era) and Lillian (Medieval era) will exchange Crystals for extra days.

Main Crystal sources in the early game:

  • Tutorial Quests — Several of the 15 reward Crystals directly (Quests 5, 6, 7, 10 all give +1 each; +3 bonus for completing all 15)
  • Relationship maxes — Reaching the highest stage with any NPC awards a Crystal
  • Fighters Guild quests — Certain quests in the chain reward Crystals
  • Cucker Chucker — Hit 500 total Cuckers across all plays for a Crystal
  • Caterpillar discoveries — Certain milestones award Crystals
  • Rat extermination — Kill 50 rats in the Medieval Back Alleys for a Crystal
  • Discord redeem codes — Shared by the developer on the official Discord (join the community to get them)

Following this walkthrough you'll naturally accumulate 10+ Crystals before even touching the Patreon content, so the doomsday timer is a non-issue.

Your First In-Game Day — Quick Roadmap

Here's what to do in your very first day, immediately after character creation wakes you up in your Apartment:

1. Explore your Apartment Click around to learn the layout. Note the sleep option (restores energy), and take stock of your starting stats and inventory.

2. Use the "Post Apartment Listing" one-time action This is a no-cost action in your apartment that advertises your spare room for rent. Do this immediately on Day 1 — it's the prerequisite for Jessie's entire questline (covered in detail in a later section). Once posted, nothing happens until you visit the Cafe Exterior, so you can post it and forget about it.

3. Head to the Downtown Office Building (Investment Office) Open the map. Travel to Downtown. Visit the Investment Planning Office (9 AM-5 PM). Spend a little starting cash on Coffee Shop ($50, cheapest investment). This plants the seed for Tutorial Quest #2 and starts earning you passive income. If you can afford more, buy 3 investments total today to finish Quest #2.

4. Check the Tutorial Quest panel Click the glowing icon. Read the 15 quests. This walkthrough references them by number — knowing the list helps you plan efficient routes.

5. Start earning money Depending on your proficiency, hit an appropriate early job:

  • Musicians: Busk at City Street or Downtown (Creativity × 2, doubled by Master Performer)
  • Charm-based characters: Distribute Flyers (City Street) or Beg for Change (Uptown)
  • Athletic characters: Stock Shelves at the Convenience Store (Stamina 10+)
  • Scholars: Library Assistant at the University (Intelligence 10+)

You want at least $200-500 by end of Day 2 to afford the first gift for Jessie and a weapon/armor at Gastoff's.

6. Sleep and end the day Sleep in your Apartment. Day 2 begins.

That's it for Day 1. Don't try to do everything at once. The next section covers Days 2-7, where you'll unlock Dr. Akira's research chain, meet Jessie, start investing seriously, and warp to the Medieval era for the first time.

Section 2 — Foundations: Skills, Stats, Money, and Efficiency

Chronoromancer rewards players who understand its systems. This section covers the fundamentals of efficient play: which skills unlock quality-of-life UI upgrades, which stats matter most and how to raise them, and how to progress from broke to comfortable.

If you skip this section and just "play the game," you'll still have fun — but you'll also waste time grinding relationships blind, stacking the wrong stats for your favorite NPCs, and missing the best income sources for your stat level. Ten minutes reading this section saves you hours of in-game time.

The "UI-Reveal" Skills — Read This First

A handful of skills don't do combat damage or unlock dungeon rewards — they reveal hidden information on the Relationship Screen. Every one of these pays for itself within a couple of relationship grinds.

Skill What It Reveals Where to Learn Cost
Researching Each NPC's preferred stats (the +1 bonus triggers) Scholar proficiency (free starting skill) OR University Campus (Present Uptown) 180 min, −50 energy/−40 calm, $200, Intelligence 15+
Anthropology Each NPC's age University Campus (Present Uptown) 180 min, −50 energy/−40 calm, $200, Intelligence 15+
Networking Each NPC's favorite skill (the 2× multiplier trigger) Salesman proficiency (free starting skill) OR Glitch's Den (Future Fringe Zone) Free if Salesman, otherwise from Glitch
Reading People Advanced NPC descriptions + enhanced text formatting Player proficiency (free starting skill) OR Vixen at Rowdy Alehouse (Medieval) Free if Player, Vixen training varies
Information Brokering Each NPC's location on the relationship screen Cognitive Optimization Academy (Future) OR Vixen at Bards Guild (Medieval) Varies
Data Mining Exact relationship points + thresholds with progress tracking Universal Knowledge Synthesis Institute (Future Fringe Zone) 180 min, −60 energy/−50 calm, $200, Technology 20+

Data Mining is the most valuable of these. Once learned, your relationship screen shows literal numbers — "Jessie: 47/60 to Crush" — so you know exactly when the next plot will trigger.

Suggested early-game skill priority:

  1. Your starting proficiency skills (free)
  2. Researching — if you're not already Scholar, prioritize this at the University. Revealing preferred stats changes how you build your character.
  3. Networking — if you're not already Salesman, head to Glitch in the Future once warping unlocks.
  4. Data Mining once you can afford the Future tuition and Technology 20.
  5. Alcohol Tolerance (see Jessie section — required for her romance).

Other High-Value Early Skills

Beyond the UI-reveal skills, a handful of other skills have outsized early-game value:

Alcohol Tolerance — Required to complete Jessie's romance (needed for "Jessie's Rebound" scene). Also unlocks bar interactions and prevents wine gifts from penalizing your stats.

Networking / Silver Tongue (Salesman starting skills) — Silver Tongue gives a second chance roll on relationship points with every social action. If you're not Salesman, Silver Tongue is also learnable from the Bards Guild or the Demon Brothel (higher cost).

Creative Cooking — Required for Tutorial Quest #7 (+1 Crystal). Learned at the Unregistered Housing Complex in the Future Fringe Zone. A quick one-stop quest.

Martial Arts — If you didn't pick Martial Artist, you can still learn it at Master Lilu's Dojo (Present Downtown). Essential for Story Beat 9 and good for early dungeon combat.

Researching, Engineering Genius, Arcane Knowledge — Three "multiplier" skills that buff specific activities (caterpillar research, tech encounters, magic damage). Pick up whichever suits your build.

The Stats That Matter Most

Chronoromancer has 14+ stats but you can't max them all. Here's what to prioritize based on this walkthrough's goals:

Priority 1: Intelligence (target 45+ early)

The single most important stat for the early game. Why:

  • Dr. Akira's favorite stat (reaches 45 = +1 per action)
  • Required to learn Researching, Anthropology, Resource Optimization, Data Mining (directly or through related stat gates)
  • Gates Sonia's research plots (Story Beats 1, 4, 11)
  • Enables Scholar career for income once you hit 30

How to raise: University Campus (Present Uptown) — the primary training location. Cafe reading actions also raise it slowly. Sample Collections from Akira passively boost it.

Priority 2: Charm (target 20-30)

  • Enables social jobs like Distribute Flyers, Tutor Students
  • Emily's favorite stat
  • Required for Combat Leadership (Fighters Guild) and Silver Tongue (Bards Guild)
  • Unlocks romance dialogue options with many NPCs

How to raise: Community Center (Present Uptown), social jobs, flirty dialogue choices, performing at various locations.

Priority 3: Stamina (target 20-30)

  • Determines your maximum energy pool — directly affects how many actions per day
  • Required for most physical jobs
  • Tutorial Quest #3 trains it at the Dojo

How to raise: Gym (button masher minigame, high yield), Dojo training, Lifeguard Duty at the Beach. The Gym Button Masher is the single best Stamina gainer in the early game.

Priority 4: Strength (target 20-30)

  • Weapon damage in combat — critical for dungeon crawling
  • Required for Sparring Partner job ($15+ income tier)
  • Enables Gastoff's higher-tier weapon upgrades

How to raise: Gym button masher, Dojo sparring, Community Volunteer (moving furniture).

Priority 5: Perception (target 18-45)

  • Unlocks hidden actions, secret dialogue, and Shadow's alley discovery questline (45 Perception required)
  • Bella's favorite stat
  • Required for Lockpicking, Acrobatics

How to raise: Gym Button Masher gives a small Perception boost; certain dungeon scenarios and one-time rewards.

The Rest

Ability, Creativity, Technology, Mystic, Beast — all useful later, but don't focus on them in the first 10-15 days unless your build specifically requires them.

Earning Money: From Broke to Comfortable

Income scales in three tiers based on your stats. Pick the best available job each day.

Tier 3: Proficiency Careers (Primary Stat 30+)

Proficiency careers are the real money makers. Hit your primary stat of 30 as quickly as possible to unlock yours.

Investments: Passive Income Engine

Visit the Investment Planning Office at the Downtown Offices (9 AM-5 PM). This is your wealth engine for the mid-to-late game.

How it works:

  • 50 investments available, from Coffee Shop ($50) up to Space Mining ($1,000,000)
  • Each investment can be upgraded 5 times (Level 1 → Level 5)
  • Every investment pays back over 50 days based on total invested
  • Earnings accumulate passively — just visit to collect
  • Three risk tiers: Low (80-110% return), Medium (60-140%), High (0-200%)

Early strategy:

  1. Day 1: Buy Coffee Shop ($50). Even $1-2/day adds up.
  2. Stack low-risk investments first — steady returns fund higher-cost unlocks.
  3. Pick up the Investment Savvy skill — it gives 15% discount on all future investment purchases. Two paths to learn it: (a) deep romance arc with Portia culminating in her Villa apartment H2H, or (b) build relationship with Arestes (Master Thief, Thieves Guild Hidden Chamber) to Known stage — she'll teach it for $500 (needs Intelligence 19). Arestes is the faster path for a wealth build.
  4. Own 3 investments to complete Tutorial Quest #2 (+300 Wealth reward).

Don't over-invest early. Keep a cash reserve of at least $500 for gifts, weapon upgrades, and skill training.

Other Money Sources

  • Fishing — Visit the Medieval Fishing Village. Catch fish, sell them to Marina or fry at the coast. Good mid-income, builds Tutorial Quest #12 progress (10 species for +400 Wealth).
  • Dungeon loot — Direct gold drops from enemies + materials for crafting/upgrading + rare key items. Runs well after Strength/Stamina 15+.
  • Bar Minigames (Downtown Bar) — Liar's Dice, Darts, and Christmas Hold'em against Vera. Wins grow your balance but losing hurts. Vera's romance actually unlocks through gambling (Tell at $800 net won, Bad Night at $1,200, etc.) — a nice side benefit.
  • Cucker-Chucker (Medieval Rural Farm) — After completing The Cuckers Return, hit 500 cumulative Cuckers across all plays to unlock a 1-Crystal recognition plot.
  • Trading — Convert materials between tiers at the trade system. Useful once you're running dungeons regularly.

Section 3 — The Three Eras: A Navigator's Guide

Once the prologue ends (about 20-30 minutes of cutscenes spanning 4 acts), you're dropped back in your Present apartment with warp access to all three eras. No additional unlock is required — every era is open from Day 1 of free play. This section walks you through every important location in each era so you know where to go and why.

How Warping Works

  • Where to warp from: Your Apartment (Present Uptown) has era-selection buttons. Certain shrine/lab locations in other eras also allow re-warping.
  • Time cost: Instant. No in-game minutes pass during the warp itself.
  • Energy cost: None.
  • Per-era clocks: Each era maintains its own time-of-day display, but the global day counter advances together.
  • Limitations: None meaningful in the free version. You can hop eras as often as you like.

Practical usage: If it's night in the Present and you need to hit a daytime shop in the Medieval, warp there — the Medieval clock might still be in daytime hours. Each era's clock progresses on its own local day/night cycle.

Section 4 — The 15 Tutorial Quests: Complete Walkthrough

The Tutorial Quest system is Chronoromancer's onboarding ladder. All 15 quests together give you a complete tour of the game's systems — relationships, investments, combat, cooking, crafting, dungeon, brothel, caterpillars, fishing, the full loop. Completing all 15 also awards a +3 Crystal completion bonus on top of every individual reward.

Access: Click the glowing Tutorial Quest icon to the right of your stats panel. The panel lists all 15 quests with progress bars, rewards, and claim buttons. Rewards must be claimed manually once a quest is done — they aren't auto-granted.

Quests can be completed in any order. The walkthrough below orders them roughly by when it's efficient to attempt them in a typical playthrough.

Quest 1 — Dr. Akira's Research

  • Objective: Provide samples for Dr. Akira 3 times at the Uptown Hospital
  • Reward: +200 Wealth

How to complete:

  1. Visit Akira's Office at the Hospital (6 AM-7 PM, any day).
  2. Use the action "Request Exam" (30 min, −30 energy). This starts Akira's plot chain.
  3. Return the next in-game day. "The Results" auto-triggers on your second visit — this is where the butterscotch-compound reveal happens. Upon completion, the Sample Collection action unlocks.
  4. Use Sample Collection (30 min, −20 energy, 1× per day) three times across three different days.

Each Sample Collection also gives you +10 relationship with Akira, which pushes her romance along. See the Dr. Akira romance section later in this walkthrough for the full chain.

Quest 2 — Smart Investor

  • Objective: Own 3 investments at the Downtown Office Building
  • Reward: +300 Wealth

How to complete:

  1. Travel to Downtown → Office Building (Investment Planning Office, 9 AM-5 PM).
  2. Buy any 3 investments. The cheapest starting options:
    • Coffee Shop — $50 (low risk, steady return)
    • Food Truck — similar low-tier starter
    • Any Low-Risk tier option you can afford
  3. You don't need to upgrade them — just own 3 at Level 1+.

Tip: Even if you only have $50, buy Coffee Shop immediately on Day 1. Save up and buy two more as soon as possible. The investments start paying you back on Day 1 and compound daily, so the earlier you own them the better.

Quest 3 — Martial Training

  • Objective: Train Martial Arts 3 times at the Downtown Dojo
  • Reward: +2 Stamina

How to complete:

  1. Travel to Downtown → Dojo (10 AM-7 PM).
  2. Use the "Train Martial Arts" action three times across three separate visits (usually 1× per day due to energy cost).

No stat requirements, just energy. A very quick quest to knock out in your first 3-4 days.

Quest 4 — Cupid's Arrow

  • Objective: Redeem the VALENTINE code, which automatically starts the Kupie minigame
  • Reward: +1 Sylph (Kupie minigame starting worker)

How to complete:

  1. Travel to either Seraph's Sanctuary (Future Fringe Zone) or Lillian's Lair (Medieval Demesne).
  2. Use the "Enter Redeem Code" action at either location.
  3. Type VALENTINE (case-sensitive) and confirm.
  4. A cinematic plays confirming the code worked. Kupie appears in your Attic (accessible from your Present Uptown Apartment), and the Kupie Love Training minigame unlocks.
  5. Also hunt for hidden Sylph collectibles scattered on every map to grow your workforce faster.

This quest is free content — you do not need Patreon to use the VALENTINE code. It unlocks Kupie's entire minigame, 6 plot scenes, and an additional relationship path.

Quest 5 — Caravan Defense

  • Objective: Fight off raiders at the Medieval Caravan Rest Stop
  • Reward: +1 Crystal

How to complete:

  1. Warp to the Medieval era.
  2. Travel to the Caravan Rest Stop (Village Outskirts) in the evening (9 PM-midnight).
  3. A tutorial-style combat plot auto-triggers on your first visit in the correct time window — raiders ambush you and the caravan.
  4. Win the fight. Quest completes.

Preparation tips:

  • Buy at least a Basic Sword from Gastoff ($150) before attempting, and ideally Basic Armor ($150) too.
  • Have full health and some healing items.
  • This is also the trigger for the Fighters Guild's Caravan Defense I quest chain — you meet Kalb here, which can be handy later.

Quest 6 — Demon's Whisper

  • Objective: Find Lillian in the Medieval Back Alleys and talk to her 3 times
  • Reward: +1 Crystal

How to complete:

  1. Warp to Medieval Demesne.
  2. Travel to the Back Alleys. Lillian appears here at certain hours (try evening/night).
  3. Use the "Talk to Lillian" action three separate times across three visits.

Each talk also builds relationship with Lillian, which is valuable for unlocking her crystal shop and Dark Magic teaching later.

Quest 7 — Improvised Chef

  • Objective: Learn the Creative Cooking skill at the Unregistered Housing Complex (Future Fringe Zone)
  • Reward: +1 Crystal

How to complete:

  1. Warp to the Future Fringe Zone.
  2. Travel to the Unregistered Housing Complex.
  3. Use the "Learn Creative Cooking" action.

Creative Cooking is a useful skill outside this quest — it unlocks Future-era survival cooking (since real ingredients are scarce there). Minor cost, huge payoff.

Quest 8 — Kindred Spirit

  • Objective: Become Close Friends with Sashina
  • Reward: +3 Mystic

How to complete:

  1. Warp to the Medieval era.
  2. Travel to the Shrine Altar Chamber (Village Outskirts, 8 AM-8 PM). Sashina is here.
  3. Build relationship points through repeated visits:
    • Talk to Sashina (daily limit applies)
    • Give her Flowers (Known+), Books (Friend+), Wine (Close Friend+)
    • Complete "The Sorceress's Confession" plot at Known stage (also launches the Brothel chain — double win)
  4. Continue until you hit Close Friend stage (~80-100 relationship points total depending on your multipliers).

Efficiency tips:

  • Sashina's favorite skill doubles your points — check the relationship screen with Networking to see it.
  • Many Sashina visits also advance the main story and the Brothel chain, so this quest tends to complete naturally while you pursue both of those.

The +3 Mystic is valuable — Mystic is a hidden stat needed for several advanced skills (Inner Peace at 30, Mystic Seduction, Elemental skills from Sashina).

Quest 9 — Crypt Clearer

  • Objective: Defeat 10 enemies in the Catacombs beneath the Monastery
  • Reward: +1 Strength, +1 Ability

How to complete:

  1. Warp to the Medieval era → Monastery (Village Outskirts).
  2. Enter the Dungeon.
  3. Fight enemies on Floors 1-5 (kills on any floor count). The quest counter tracks only dungeon combat kills, not overworld fights.

Preparation:

  • Level 2-3 weapon and armor from Gastoff strongly recommended.
  • Carry Bread / Meat / healing items from the General Goods store.
  • Don't try this before Strength/Stamina 10-15 — enemies hit hard.

This is one of your earlier goals but it stretches across several dungeon runs. See Section 7 for full dungeon mechanics and tactics.

Quest 10 — Brothel Baron

  • Objective: Acquire the Brothel in the Medieval Demesne
  • Reward: +1 Crystal

How to complete:

Follow the full Brothel Quest Chain (covered in detail in Section 6). Short version:

  1. Build relationship with Sashina to Known → triggers "The Sorceress's Confession"
  2. Visit King Graham in the Throne Room → "The King's Challenge" (dragon food crisis)
  3. Visit Sonia at the Research Lab → "Agricultural Research Discussion" (needs Sonia at Known stage)
  4. Visit Christy at the Farmers Market → "Potato Cultivation Mastery"
  5. Return to King Graham with your Sack of Potatoes key item → "The Potato Solution"
  6. Deliver the knowledge to Wilfred at the Rural Farm → "Potato Knowledge Delivery"
  7. Return to King Graham a final time → "The Royal Appointment" — you are named Brothel Owner

Quest completes when you acquire the brothel. This is a multi-day sequence; most players finish it around Day 10-15.

Quest 11 — Bug Collector

  • Objective: Discover 10 unique caterpillar or moth species
  • Reward: 5 Comet Moths

How to complete:

  1. Travel to caterpillar-bearing locations. Good early spots:
    • Present Uptown Forest — start here with Seraph's Caterpillar Tutorial
    • Medieval Hunting Grounds and Mountain Foothills
    • Future Artificial Nature Preserve
  2. Use search / collect actions at each location. Each new species you find counts toward the quest.
  3. You can also see your tally in the Fishing/Caterpillar Ledger if unlocked.

Tip: Different species spawn in different eras. You'll hit 10 unique species much faster if you rotate between all three eras instead of grinding one location.

The reward (5 Comet Moths) is a premium caterpillar species useful for crystallization, habitat building, and Marina's caterpillar bait quest.

Quest 12 — Angler's Progress

  • Objective: Catch 10 different fish species
  • Reward: +400 Wealth

How to complete:

  1. Warp to Medieval Outskirts → Fishing Village.
  2. Meet Marina at the Fishing Hut (4 AM-5 PM). She'll teach you how to fish through the "Fishing Lesson" plot.
  3. Use the fishing minigame — navigate the boat, collect fish rings of varying rarity (common, rare, epic).
  4. Each new species caught counts. Track your progress in the Fishing Ledger.

Efficiency tip: Different weather/time conditions spawn different fish. Visit at different times of day and across multiple in-game days to vary your catches.

Quest 13 — Forged in Fire

  • Objective: Upgrade your melee weapon to Level 3 at the Blacksmith
  • Reward: +2 Strength

How to complete:

  1. Warp to Medieval Demesne → Blacksmith Shop (6 AM-9 PM).
  2. Progress through Gastoff's upgrade tiers:
    • Buy Sword — $150, 20 min (Level 0 → Level 1)
    • Upgrade Sword — $250 + 30 common materials, 20 min (Level 1 → Level 2)
    • Upgrade Sword — $600 + 20 rare materials, 20 min (Level 2 → Level 3 — quest complete)

Materials: Common materials drop from Floor 1-5 dungeon enemies. Rare materials from Floor 5-10. You'll likely need a few dungeon runs to accumulate enough.

Upgrading weapons also dramatically improves your dungeon survivability, so this quest synergizes with Quest 9 (Crypt Clearer). Do both in parallel.

Quest 14 — Fashion Forward

  • Objective: Upgrade your casual outfit to Level 3
  • Reward: +2 Attractiveness

How to complete:

  1. Level 1 → Level 2 — Upgrade at the Mall Clothing Store (Present Uptown). Sold as the "Cool Clothing" upgrade.
  2. Level 2 → Level 3 — Upgrade at Fernando's Tailor Shop in Medieval Demesne (6 AM-9 PM). Fernando handles all L3+ outfit upgrades.

Attractiveness boosts your per-action relationship chance bonuses with every NPC — this is a compounding buff that pays off across every romance route.

Quest 15 — Maid Mentor

  • Objective: Train any brothel maid to Level 3
  • Reward: Master Mop (permanent key item for brothel cleaning efficiency)

How to complete:

  1. First acquire the Brothel (Quest 10).
  2. Recruit/assign at least one maid via the brothel worker management screen. Free-version maids available early include Violet (after her recruitment chain).
  3. Use the Train Maid actions at the brothel repeatedly until she reaches Level 3.

This quest can only begin after you own the brothel, so it's usually among the last you'll complete.

Completing All 15 — The Bonus

Once all 15 quests have been claimed (not just completed — you have to press the Claim button on each one), a final "COMPLETE ALL" button appears in the Tutorial Quest panel.

Click it to receive:

  • +3 Crystals

The Tutorial Quest icon then disappears permanently from your UI — the tutorial phase is officially over. You keep all earned rewards, and the game has no more hand-holding. From this point on, you're working on Story Beats, romance routes, the brothel, and deeper dungeon exploration.

Section 7 — Dungeon Crawling: Floors 1 Through 10

The dungeon is Chronoromancer's combat and loot engine. 40 procedurally generated floors (plus the endless Infinity Dungeon beyond them) house enemies, scenarios, resources, bosses, and key items. This section covers everything you need to reach and defeat the Floor 10 miniboss (Serpina) in the free version, including tactics for every miniboss along the way.

Access: Monastery (Medieval Demesne) — enter the dungeon from inside the Monastery Scriptoria.

Paywall note: Floors 21 and beyond (including the Infernal Depths, the Void floors, and the Infinity Dungeon) are Patreon content. Floors 1-20 are free. This walkthrough targets Floor 10 since it's the first meaningful "gate" boss.

How the Dungeon Works

Map structure: Each floor is a procedurally generated grid of tiles. Walls (WALL), floors (FLOOR1/FLOOR2), doors, stairs down, keys, chests, water, spikes, and many special tiles.

Movement: Step onto tiles to interact. Some tiles (Water, Boulders, Trolls, Trick Doors) require skills or stat checks to traverse.

Encounter rates per step on a floor tile:

  • 8% chance: A random Scenario triggers (priority over combat)
  • 15% chance: A random enemy battle triggers
  • 100%: Special tile (minibosses, chests, stairs, etc.) — always triggers on step

Energy and time: Each step costs small energy and a minute or two. Running out of energy mid-dungeon is bad — always carry Meat/Bread for a buffer.

Escape: You can flee most combat. Boss fights disable flee — you must win or die. Dying in the dungeon triggers death handling (stat losses, game over if Doomsday timer also expires).

Saving: You cannot save while inside the dungeon. Plan a complete run and exit cleanly before saving.

Combat Basics

Regular combat is turn-based. Your options each turn:

Action Effect
Attack Strength × weapon bonus damage to enemy
Magic Mystic/Intelligence × grimoire bonus damage; needs mana or grimoire
Skill Specific combat skills do special effects (e.g., Power Strike, Counterattack)
Defend Reduce incoming damage next turn
Item Use consumables (Bread, Meat, Apple, healing potions)
Flee Chance to escape — disabled during bosses/minibosses

Damage formula essentials:

  • A basic attack builds from a small base plus roughly half your Strength (scaled by your fighting-experience modifier) plus weapon level × 2
  • With Weapon Mastery skill, the weapon contribution becomes weapon level × 3
  • Armor reduces incoming damage on a scaling curve — each armor level cuts noticeably more than the last
  • Pain Tolerance skill adds +1 effective armor level
  • For the full damage math, see the Combat System guide

Enemy tiers by exp reward (from enemy_data.rpy):

  • Weak (6-16 exp): Giant Rat, Goblin, Kobold, Slime, Wolf, Zombie, Forest Nymph, Orc, Hobgoblin, Lightning Sprite, Harpy, Dryad — typical on floors 1-5
  • Medium (17-22 exp): Skeleton Warrior, Witch, Ice Maiden, Dark Elf, Fire Elemental, Siren, Banshee, Hellhound, Werewolf, Succubus, Shadow Assassin — floors 5-15
  • Strong (23-35 exp): Naga, Basilisk, Vampire, Demon, Gargoyle, Manticore, Earth Elemental, Minotaur, Troll, Crystal Golem — floors 15-20+

Minibosses: Floor Locations & Tactics

Each miniboss occupies a specific floor tile (MINIBOSS tile). Step on it to trigger a narrative combat encounter — a 5-round stat-check fight with dialogue, choices, and scoring. These are NOT standard battles; they use a separate combat system.

Key facts:

  • Each miniboss has an energy pool (their HP) and an attack_power (damage dealt per round).
  • You pick choices each round — choices are stat-based (Strength, Intelligence, Charm, Ability, etc.) or skill-based (if you know the required skill).
  • Choice effectiveness: excellent (skill-based, 50% enemy damage), good (75%), normal (100%), poor (125%).
  • Damage caps apply — rounds 1-3 max out at 25% HP drain, round 5 is the kill round.
  • Winning gives you loot, relationship with the boss (some become recruitable), and optionally a Beast Mode finisher.

Miniboss Combat Tactics

General rules for all minibosses:

  1. Skill choices are always best. A skill-based choice deals 1.5× base damage AND usually has "excellent" effectiveness (50% return damage). Every skill you've learned from Lilu, the Scholar proficiency, etc., shows up as a choice option when relevant.
  2. Round 5 is the kill round. Round 5 damage is doubled. Save your best skill choice for round 5 if possible.
  3. Diversify your stats. Different rounds need different stats. A Strength-only character struggles in Intelligence rounds.
  4. Bring buffs. The Power buff (1.25× damage dealt) and Shield buff (0.8× damage taken) from consumables are huge for miniboss fights.

Raven (Floor 1):

  • Forces Strength, Intelligence, Charm, Ability choices across 5 rounds
  • Skills that shine: Power Strike, Combat Reflexes, Intimidating Presence, Exploit Weakness, Silver Tongue, Reading People, Acrobatics, Tactical Awareness, Counterattack
  • She's easy — designed as a tutorial fight. Win with basic Strength attacks even without skills.

Spectra (Floor 3):

  • Perception and Charm heavy
  • Bring Reading People skill if possible
  • Higher damage than Raven — have Armor L2+ or full healing stock

Whiskers (Floor 5):

  • Catgirl/agility encounter — favors Ability and Perception choices
  • Fleet Footed skill shines here
  • Consider also having Acrobatics

Lindia (Floor 7):

  • Magic/Intelligence focus — Mystic and Magic skills dominate
  • Elemental Blast, Arcane Knowledge, Mana Channeling all give excellent choices
  • If you lack magic skills, pivot to Intelligence-based non-skill options

Serpina (Floor 10 — YOUR TARGET):

  • 300 energy is roughly twice Raven's
  • Damage output around 40 per round — 5 rounds = up to 200 damage taken if you pick poorly
  • Bring armor L3, Pain Tolerance skill if possible, and at least 4 healing items
  • Mix of stats across rounds; come with a varied build
  • Winning Serpina completes the Floor 10 goal and grants access to Floor 11

Random Scenarios — The Big Rewards

Every step you take on a dungeon floor tile has an 8% chance of triggering a scenario — a narrative event with choices that can grant key items, gold, stat boosts, or trouble. Some scenarios are worth far more than grinding combat.

Priority scenarios to look for on Floors 1-10:

The Vampire Ring Scenario (any floor) — The highest-value early scenario.

  • A vampire lord lounges in an opulent chamber. Three options:
    • Challenge him to combat — fight Vampire Lord (70 HP + floor scaling). Win = 1 Ring key item.
    • Light Magic skill option — banish him with light magic. Instant win, same reward.
    • Back away — no reward, no fight.
  • The 1 Ring is permanent +1 to all relationship point gains. Get it as early as possible. This single key item speeds up every romance route for the rest of the game.

Magic Spectacles Scenario — An arrogant scholar offers a gift if you can tell him something new. Intelligence check (−20 modifier, so Intelligence 30+ is safe). Win = Magic Spectacles key item, boosts Intelligence actions.

The Fairy Encounter — Pixie/fairy scenario. Various outcomes based on choices. Some paths unlock the Fairy/Pixie questline and related rewards.

Wounded Wolf — Animal Care skill unlocks a big reward path. Otherwise a modest choice-based reward.

Auriana's Wolves — Long-running questline starting at low floors. Continuing it rewards skills (Fleet Footed, Endurance Training, Adrenaline Rush) as dungeon drops.

Sylph Scenario — Related to the Kupie/Sylph system. Rewards a Sylph for the minigame.

Fight Club — Strength-based scenario chain, multi-floor progression.

Torture Chamber — Dark scenario with Blackmail skill reward and Lilith mini-fights at higher floors.

Mysterious Shops — Random merchants selling goods at odd prices.

Gambling Dens (Orc, Leprechaun) — Risk-for-reward encounters with Sleight of Hand/Deception skill bonuses.

General rule: Read every scenario carefully. "Back away" is always an option and always safe. When in doubt and you don't meet a skill/stat check, back away — the dungeon regenerates scenarios on repeat runs.

Resources and Loot

What you'll accumulate on a typical Floor 1-10 run:

  • Gold — direct drops from combat and chest tiles. Expect $50-200 per run.
  • Common materials (Tier 1) — from floors 1-5 enemies. Used for sword/armor L2 upgrades.
  • Rare materials (Tier 2) — from floors 5-10 enemies. Used for sword/armor L3 upgrades.
  • Epic materials (Tier 3) — from floors 10-20 enemies. Used for L4 upgrades.
  • Fabrics (Tier 1-3) — for clothing upgrades via Fernando.
  • Ingredients (Tier 1-3) — for cooking.
  • Dragon Coins — premium currency from scenarios, some Sylph encounters, and boss defeats. Used for advanced training and rare purchases.
  • Food tiles — Bread, Meat, Apple, Meal tiles. Pick them up to replenish healing stock.
  • Coins (small drops) — direct wealth bumps from floor tiles.
  • Key items — Vampire Ring, Magic Spectacles, Awesome Crown, Ornate Locket, Emerald Dragon (boss scenarios), etc.

Run Strategy by Floor

Floor 1-2 runs (starting out):

  • Bring Sword/Armor L1, 3 healing items
  • Target: kill 10 enemies (Tutorial Quest #9 — "Crypt Clearer"), loot chests, find Raven on Floor 1
  • Exit after Floor 2 to upgrade gear

Floor 3-5 runs (mid-early):

  • Sword/Armor L2, 5 healing items
  • Target: Spectra on Floor 3, Whiskers on Floor 5, Vampire Ring scenario if it appears
  • Farm common → rare materials for L3 upgrades

Floor 6-10 runs (targeting completion):

  • Sword/Armor L3, 10 healing items, some combat skills
  • Target: Lindia on Floor 7, Serpina on Floor 10 (your walkthrough goal)
  • Floor 10 completion = major stat/experience gain + access to Floor 11+

How many in-game days: A single dungeon trip can run from dawn to late afternoon in terms of in-game time (depends on how many steps). Plan one or two dungeon days per week. Between trips, earn money, upgrade gear, and do plots.

Skill-Gated Movement Cheats

Certain skills let you bypass dungeon obstacles that would otherwise block you:

Obstacle Bypass Method
Water tiles Swim skill required to cross
Boulder Strength check (20 + floor level) — passes, converts to walkable
Troll Strength check (30 + 2× floor level) — overpower, converts to walkable
Trick Door Creativity 35+ solves the mechanism
Standard Door Requires player_has_key flag (find a Key tile on same floor)

Having Swim unlocks shortcut paths across many floors. Lockpicking skill doesn't just help scenarios — it also helps with chest tiles.

Dying in the Dungeon

If your health (or targeted stat in a miniboss fight) reaches 0, you die. Consequences:

  • Game ejects you to your apartment
  • Stats stay but major health/energy/calm penalties apply
  • If the Doomsday timer hasn't run out, you recover and can play on
  • If Doomsday is up, game ends

To avoid:

  • Don't go deeper than your gear can handle
  • Always keep 3+ healing items in reserve
  • Flee when possible — regular combat allows it, minibosses do not
  • If a miniboss is losing badly, let the timer run out (max rounds + 1) — you get ejected from dungeon but don't die

General Romance Efficiency Tips

Stack multipliers:

  • Favorite stat thresholds = +1 per action per met threshold
  • Favorite skill = × 2 on total
  • Attractiveness roll = chance for +1 per action
  • 1 Ring key item (Vampire Ring scenario) = +1 per action
  • Cologne buff = +1 per action
  • Buffer Optimization (Patreon) = +1 per action, but plenty of free stacking is available

A fully stacked action on a character with Researching + 3 met favorite stats + 1 Ring + Cologne can give 2× (base + 3 + 1) + 1 = 2× (base+4) + 1. For a base 7 wine gift, that's ~23 points per use — massive compared to unoptimized ~8-10.

Rotate routes daily. Each NPC has a daily-limit action stack (3 Talks, 1 Chocolate, 1 Flowers, etc.). You cap out on one character's daily gains quickly. Use remaining energy on other routes. Running 3-4 concurrent romances is efficient.

Plan around time windows. Akira 6 AM-7 PM, Emily 7 AM-3 PM + 7-9 PM even days, Bella 8 AM-6 PM + 7-9 PM even days, Jessie evening. A well-planned day hits 3-4 characters.

Don't sleep on one-time +stat actions. Emily's "Service & Emotion Tips" (+1 Charm) and Bella's "Books vs Technology" (+1 Perception) are permanent stat boosts. Do them the moment you can.

Section 9 — Other Early-Game Activities Worth Doing

Beyond the tutorial quests, story beats, brothel, dungeon, and romance routes, Chronoromancer has dozens of smaller systems that reward regular attention. This section covers the ones worth prioritizing as a free-version player.

Redeem Codes

Chronoromancer supports redeem codes that unlock extra content. Enter codes at Seraph's Sanctuary (Future Fringe Zone) or Lillian's Lair (Medieval Demesne) via the "Enter Redeem Code" action. Codes are case-sensitive.

Two codes that are safe to use for the free version:

  • VALENTINE — Unlocks the Kupie character in your Attic and starts the Kupie Love Training minigame (see below). Also completes Tutorial Quest #4 (+1 Sylph).
  • HALLOWEEN — Unlocks the Haunted Mansion map with 36 locations, 21 collectible spirit entries, and an entire side-questline with its own NPC (Friday).

More codes exist — some are community rewards from the developer's Discord, others unlock Patreon-only content. This walkthrough doesn't list them; join the official Discord if you want to see what's available.

The Kupie Love Training Minigame (VALENTINE)

Once you redeem VALENTINE, Kupie appears in your Attic (access from your Present Uptown Apartment). This unlocks:

  • Kupie as a relationship NPC with her own progression (Talk, Give Chocolates, Give Wine)
  • The Love Training idle/clicker minigame — assign Sylph workers to generate Hearts and Charms
  • 6 Kupie plot scenes spread across her relationship progression
  • NPC dating tracks inside the minigame — each game NPC has levels, date scenes, and milestone rewards
  • Hidden Sylph collectibles scattered on every map — find them to expand your Sylph workforce
  • The Sylph Shop — spend in-game currency for upgrades like Hermes Shoes (2× speed), Cupid's Wings (4×), Divine Blessing (6×)
  • Crystal rewards at milestone progression points

Why do it: Kupie's minigame runs passively while you do other things. Assigning Sylphs in the morning generates resources throughout your day with zero time cost. Several NPC dating tracks inside the minigame also give Crystal rewards at key milestones — meaningful Doomsday timer extensions for basically free.

Start point: Once redeemed, visit the Attic to launch the minigame. Follow the in-game tutorial from there.

The Haunted Mansion (HALLOWEEN)

Redeeming HALLOWEEN unlocks access to the Haunted Mansion — a separate multi-room map with its own systems.

What's inside:

  • 36 locations to explore, many with unique puzzles or encounters
  • 21 collectible spirit entries scattered through the mansion (the Attic Twins currently add two Soul Jar units)
  • Piano puzzle — musical puzzle for a reward
  • Hangman minigame — word puzzle for rewards
  • The Bride Mystery — a multi-room investigation plot chain
  • Friday (the Soul Collector) — a full romance NPC with her own relationship progression, introduced via the mansion's foyer soul jar
  • 2 possible Crystal rewards — one from the final soul ceremony and one from completing all 25 Hangman phrases
  • Ornate Locket key item — awarded for completing Friday's arc (all 21 souls)

Why do it: The Haunted Mansion is extremely content-rich relative to a single redeem code unlock. Its two Crystals and Ornate Locket are valuable, Friday is a meaningful romance option, and the puzzles/minigames are fun changes of pace. Also: this content is not time-gated — unlike real-world Halloween, you can play it any time of the year.

Start point: After redeeming, the mansion becomes accessible from the map. The Foyer is your entry — Friday introduces herself there via the soul jar encounter.

Fishing (Marina's Minigame)

Fishing is a full minigame that also completes Tutorial Quest #12 (+400 Wealth for 10 species) and builds Marina's romance.

How to start:

  1. Warp to Medieval Outskirts → Fishing Village → Fishing Hut (interior) (4 AM-5 PM)
  2. Meet Marina for her first encounter plot
  3. Take the "Fishing Lesson" — she teaches you the mechanics
  4. Fishing becomes available as a regular action

Mechanics:

  • You navigate a boat on a stylized water map
  • Fish rings appear in three rarities: common, rare, epic
  • Collect rings to catch fish
  • Each catch is logged in the Fishing Ledger by species

Income potential: Varies by species and rarity. Rare and epic fish sell for significantly more than common. Selling at the Fishing Hut or Marina directly gives the best prices.

Long-term rewards:

  • Boat upgrades from Marina (Close Friend+, $5,000, then $15,000, then $50,000) — increases fishing speed and range
  • Leviathan reward — catching the massive Leviathan triggers a major plot + bonus
  • Sashimi encounter — a water-themed worker can be recruited via fishing content at high progression

Tutorial Quest #12: 10 different fish species caught = +400 wealth. Fish variety is tied to in-game time of day and weather — run fishing at different times of day to vary your catches.

Section 10 — Hidden Map Collectibles: Free Flowers and Gold

This one's a secret most new players miss entirely: many location backgrounds have invisible clickable spots that give you free flowers, free gold, or other rewards. Some respawn every 6 days, some are one-time finds. There are dozens of these scattered across all three eras.

How it works: While standing at certain locations, click around the background (especially corners, benches, planters, dumpsters, and behind objects). If there's a hidden pickup there, a short message appears and the reward goes straight to your inventory or wealth. If the spot is empty or already collected, nothing happens — no penalty for missing.

Two types:

  • Flowers (respawning) — Most common. Every 6 in-game days the spot regenerates. Free gifts for Emily, Bella, and every romance NPC who takes Flowers.
  • Gold stashes (one-time) — $50 to $150 per spot. You only get each one once, but 40+ of them exist across the three eras. That's a potential $3,000-4,000 in free early-game wealth if you find them all.

Why this matters enormously for early-game players: Flowers are worth +20 relationship points with most romance NPCs at Close Friend+. Buying them from shops costs money. Finding them for free is pure value. And the early-game gold stashes alone can fund your first few Gastoff upgrades and investment purchases.

第1节——开始游戏：基础要点

本节涵盖游戏第一天需要了解的一切，是后续全部攻略的基础。

这是一款怎样的游戏？

Chronoromancer是一款时间旅行沙盒RPG。你的角色拥有在 三个时代 间穿梭的独特能力——现代的 现代、奇幻的 中世纪 王国，以及反乌托邦式的 未来时代。每个时代都有自己的地图、NPC、经济、工作、技能和故事线。

本作并非线性视觉小说，没有唯一“正确”的顺序。你可以按自己的节奏发展关系、刷属性、探索地下城、钓鱼、收集毛毛虫、经营青楼，或调查主线故事。本攻略会提供一条高效的前期路线，同时避免错过重要内容。

时间、天数与末日倒计时

每日循环

每个游戏日为24小时。各种行动会消耗几分钟（或几小时）。睡眠可以恢复精力并推进至次日早晨。许多剧情触发需要特定时段——例如“Jessie的反弹恋情”只会在 晚上7—10点 之间触发，而大多数商店会在晚上9点前关门。

部分NPC也有特定工作时间。Dr. Akira每天 上午6点至晚上7点 在医院。铁匠Gastoff的工作时间是 上午6点至晚上9点。Marina在渔屋的时间为 凌晨4点至下午5点。如果你在NPC工作时间之外前往，只会看到空荡荡的地点。

末日倒计时

Chronoromancer拥有一个 游戏结束计时器。开局时你会获得固定天数；计数归零后，游戏结束。不必惊慌——期限十分宽裕，而且有许多延长方法。

投资：被动收入引擎

在上午9点至下午5点之间，前往市中心办公楼的 投资规划办公室。这是你中后期积累财富的引擎。

运作方式：

  • 共有50项投资，从 咖啡店（$50） 到 太空采矿（$1,000,000）
  • 每项投资均可升级5次（1级→5级）
  • 每项投资会根据总投资额在 50天 内回本
  • 收益会被动积累——只需前往领取
  • 三种风险等级：低（80—110%回报）、中（60—140%）、高（0—200%）

前期策略：

  1. 第1天：购买 咖啡店（$50）。哪怕每天只有$1—2，积少也能成多。
  2. 优先叠加低风险投资——用稳定回报为更昂贵的解锁提供资金。
  3. 学习 投资敏锐 技能——它会让 今后所有投资购买享受15%折扣。有两条学习路线：（a）深入 Portia 的恋爱线，直至她在别墅公寓中的谈心；或（b）培养与 Arestes（盗贼大师，盗贼公会隐藏密室）的关系至已知阶段——她会以$500教授该技能（需要智力19）。对财富流配装而言，Arestes路线更快。
  4. 拥有3项投资即可完成教程任务2（奖励+300财富）。

前期不要过度投资。 至少保留$500现金，用于礼物、武器升级和技能训练。

  • 钓鱼——造访中世纪渔村。捕鱼后卖给Marina，或在海岸煎鱼。中等收入，还能推进教程任务12的进度（捕获10个鱼种，奖励+400财富）。

任务1——Dr. Akira的研究

  • 目标： 在上城区医院为Dr. Akira提供样本 3次
  • 奖励： +200财富

完成方法：

  1. 在任意一天上午6点至晚上7点之间，造访医院的 Akira办公室。
  2. 使用 “请求检查” 行动（30分钟，−30精力）。这会开始Akira的剧情链。
  3. 下一个游戏日再次前来。第二次造访时，“结果” 会自动触发——你会在这里得知奶油硬糖化合物的秘密。完成后解锁 样本采集 行动。
  4. 在三个不同的日子使用 样本采集（30分钟，−20精力，每天1次）共三次。

每次样本采集还会提供+10 Akira好感度，推进她的恋爱线。完整流程请见本攻略后面的 Dr. Akira恋爱章节。

第5节——主线故事：故事节点1至10

Chronoromancer的主线故事通过 故事节点 讲述——这些按顺序连接的剧情场景会自动触发。每个节点都有地点、时间窗口与条件（通常只是“上一节点已完成”，有时会要求关系阶段）。在正确时间到达正确地点，场景便会自动播放。

核心机制： 每个节点都会使用required_flags和blocked_flags。节点完成后会设置其completed标志，从而解锁下一节点。错过时间窗口只意味着“稍后再来”——不会永久错过任何内容。

地下城机制

地图结构： 每个楼层都是程序生成的方格地图，包含墙壁（WALL）、地板（FLOOR1／FLOOR2）、门、下行楼梯、钥匙、宝箱、水域、尖刺及多种特殊格。

移动： 踏上方格即可互动。部分方格（水域、巨石、巨魔、机关门）需要技能或属性检定才能通过。

每次踏上地板格时的遭遇概率：

  • 8%概率： 触发随机情景（优先于战斗）
  • 15%概率： 触发随机敌人战斗
  • 100%： 特殊格（小首领、宝箱、楼梯等）——踏上后必定触发

精力与时间： 每一步都会消耗少量精力并花费一两分钟。在地下城中途耗尽精力非常糟糕——务必携带肉类／面包作为缓冲。

逃跑： 多数战斗都能逃跑。首领战会禁用逃跑——不是胜利就是死亡。在地下城死亡会触发死亡处理（损失属性；如果末日倒计时也已结束，则游戏结束）。

保存： 地下城内无法保存。请规划完整行程，安全离开后再存档。

战斗基础

常规战斗采用回合制。每回合可选行动如下：

行动 效果

  2. 第5轮是击杀轮。 第5轮伤害翻倍。可以的话，把最好的技能选项留到第5轮。
  3. 让属性多样化。 不同轮次需要不同属性。只堆力量的角色会在智力轮陷入苦战。
  4. 携带增益。 消耗品提供的力量增益（造成伤害1.25×）与护盾增益（受到伤害0.8×）在小首领战中作用巨大。

Raven（1层）：

  • 5轮中会强制进行力量、智力、魅力、能力选择
  • 表现优秀的技能：强力打击、战斗反射、威吓气场、弱点利用、巧舌如簧、察言观色、杂技、战术意识、反击
  • 她很简单——本来就是教程战。即使没有技能，也能靠基础力量攻击获胜。

Spectra（3层）：

  • 重视感知与魅力
  • 可以的话带上察言观色技能
  • 伤害高于Raven——请准备L2+护甲或足量治疗品

Whiskers（5层）：

  • 猫娘／敏捷主题遭遇——偏爱能力与感知选项
  • 疾步技能在此非常出色
  • 也可考虑学习杂技

Lindia（7层）：

  • 注重魔法／智力——神秘与魔法技能占据优势
  • 元素爆裂、奥术知识、法力引导都能提供极佳选项
  • 如果缺少魔法技能，请改用基于智力的非技能选项

Serpina（10层——你的目标）：

  • 300精力约为Raven的两倍
  • 每轮伤害约40——选项较差时，5轮最多会承受200伤害
  • 携带3级护甲；如果可以，学习疼痛耐受，并至少带4件治疗物品
  • 每轮考验的属性混合多样，请使用均衡配装
  • 击败Serpina即可完成10层目标，并开放11层

随机情景——巨额奖励

每次踏上地下城地板格都有 8%的概率触发情景——这类叙事事件会提供选择，并可能奖励关键物品、金币、属性提升，也可能带来麻烦。部分情景的价值远高于刷战斗。

1—10层值得寻找的优先情景：

吸血鬼戒指情景（任意楼层）——前期价值最高的情景。

  • 一名吸血鬼领主慵懒地躺在奢华房间中。有三个选项：
    • 向他发起战斗挑战——对阵吸血鬼领主（70 HP＋楼层缩放）。获胜＝ 1枚戒指 关键物品。
    • 光魔法技能选项——用光魔法驱逐他。立即获胜，奖励相同。
    • 退后离开——没有奖励，也不战斗。
  • 1枚戒指会永久使所有好感度获取+1。 越早获得越好。单是这件关键物品，就会在剩余游戏中加速每条恋爱路线。

魔法眼镜情景——一名傲慢学者表示，如果你能告诉他一件新鲜事，就会送你礼物。智力检定（−20修正，所以智力30+很安全）。获胜＝ 魔法眼镜 关键物品，可增强智力行动。

仙女遭遇——小仙女／精灵情景。根据选择会出现不同结果。部分路线会解锁仙女／精灵任务线及相关奖励。

受伤的狼——动物照料技能会解锁高额奖励路线，否则只能获得普通的选项奖励。

Auriana的狼群——从低层开始的长期任务线。继续推进会以地下城掉落的方式奖励技能（疾步、耐力训练、肾上腺素激增）。

小天使情景——与Kupie／小天使系统相关，会为小游戏奖励一名小天使。

搏击俱乐部——基于力量的情景链，跨多个楼层推进。

刑讯室——黑暗情景，会奖励勒索技能；高层还会出现Lilith小型战斗。

神秘商店——随机商人以古怪价格出售商品。

赌窝（兽人、矮妖）——以风险换取回报的遭遇，巧手／欺骗技能会提供加成。

一般原则： 仔细阅读每段情景。“退后离开”永远可选，也永远安全。拿不准并且不满足技能／属性检定时，请直接离开——重复探索时，地下城会重新生成情景。

在地下城死亡

如果生命（或小首领战中被针对的属性）降至0，你就会死亡。后果如下：

  • 游戏会把你送回公寓
  • 属性保留，但生命／精力／镇定会受到严重惩罚
  • 如果末日倒计时尚未归零，你会恢复并能继续游玩
  • 如果末日时间已尽，游戏结束

避免方法：

  • 不要深入超过装备承受能力的楼层
  • 始终保留3件以上治疗物品
  • 能逃就逃——常规战斗允许逃跑，小首领战不允许
  • 如果小首领战形势极差，让计时器耗尽（最大轮数＋1）——你会被赶出地下城，但不会死亡

第10节——隐藏地图收藏品：免费鲜花与金币

这是大多数新玩家完全没发现的秘密：许多地点背景中都有 不可见的可点击位置，会免费提供鲜花、金币或其他奖励。部分每6天重新生成，部分只能发现一次。三个时代中散落着数十个这样的地点。

运作方式： 站在特定地点时，点击背景各处（尤其是角落、长椅、花盆、垃圾箱和物体后方）。如果那里有隐藏拾取物，就会出现一条简短消息，奖励会直接加入物品栏或财富。如果地点为空或已经收集，则什么也不会发生——没点中也没有惩罚。

两种类型：

  • 鲜花（重新生成）——最常见。每经过6个游戏日，地点会重新生成。可作为Emily、Bella及所有接受鲜花的恋爱NPC的免费礼物。
  • 金币藏匿点（一次性）——每处$50至$150。每个地点只能领取一次，但三个时代共有40+处。如果全部找到，前期可获得 $3,000—4,000免费财富。

这对前期玩家极其重要的原因： 达到亲密朋友+后，鲜花对多数恋爱NPC价值+20好感度，而从商店购买需要花钱。免费找到它们就是纯收益。单是前期金币藏匿点，就能为Gastoff的最初几次升级和投资购买提供资金。

前期优先鲜花地点

鲜花每6天重新生成，每个地点通常有2—5个独立位置。最适合定期采集的路线如下：

现代——上城区公园（4处）——最容易的前期鲜花农场。四处都会每周重新生成。检查花坛、长椅区域和草地。

现代——市中心公园（4处）——其中两处一次会提供 2朵鲜花，而不是1朵。检查喷泉附近、道路沿线和花圃。

现代——咖啡厅外部（2处）——花盆与窗台花箱。非常合适——反正你也要为Jessie的遭遇造访咖啡厅外部。

现代——上城区瀑布（3处）——故事节点9已经会要求你在上午7—11点造访。检查瀑布水雾区域、河边和悬崖底部。

中世纪——城堡花园（8处！！！）——鲜花天堂。多处一次会提供 2—3朵鲜花，是游戏中最好的单一鲜花农场。从城堡庭院进入，每周造访。

中世纪——神秘冥想林地（2处）——远古石块附近的紫色鲜花。

未来时代——核心区变异植物保护区（4处）——四处均各提供2朵鲜花，包括基因强化玫瑰与水晶树花。

拥有青楼后的额外地点——青楼庭院（5处）——其中两处各提供2朵鲜花。获得青楼后，这里会成为另一个固定采花点。

粗略计算： 每周扫荡上城区公园＋市中心公园＋瀑布＋城堡花园，每6天大约能获得 20—25朵完全免费的鲜花。在亲密朋友+阶段，相当于每周价值400—500好感度的礼物。
