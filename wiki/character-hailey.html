Overview

Hailey’s current route includes four personal scenes, three ordered heart-to-hearts, three optional dates and an evening home visit. Her shop and community activities are separate from that personal progression.

Where to find them

Her ordinary portrait and shop are at General Goods Store daily 06:00–21:00.

After You Can Stay, Romantic 800 unlocks Visit Hailey at Gilded Row21:00–24:00:120 min/40 Energy, once/day. This is a separate location invitation, not a late shop portrait.

Route and relationship milestones

Complete the shop introduction → A Place for Herself → The Quiet After → Kindness Is Not a Promise → What I Can Offer → Come Closer → You Can Stay → A Comfortable Understanding.

The middle heart-to-heart additionally requires A Respectable Arrangement. Visit the shop while owning Rose & Thorn and with Elara home from vacation for that supply scene; it has no added Hailey affection gate.

The bazaar date follows Kindness at Friend 100; the festival follows the middle heart-to-heart at Close Friend 200; the courtyard date follows Come Closer at Crush 400. They are optional outings, not replacements for the route conversations.

Her community scenes below are independent of the personal conversation chain. Check each scene’s other participants and visit window rather than treating every row as the next required personal step.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the middle conversation is missing, check the food-supply scene and Elara’s vacation status.
  • The evening visit starts at Gilded Row; the shop closes at 21:00.
  • The family discussion requires the later personal scene and pregnancy setting; it is not needed for ordinary dates or private visits.

概览

Hailey当前有四段个人事件、三段按顺序进行的交心、三次可选约会及晚间居家拜访。商店和社区活动与个人路线分开。

去哪里找

普通头像及商店每天06:00–21:00位于杂货店。

完成你可以留下、达到浪漫感800后，可在鎏金街21:00–24:00使用拜访Hailey，120分钟、40精力，每天一次。这是独立地点邀约，不是深夜商店头像。

依次完成商店介绍→给自己留一点位置→忙碌过后的宁静→善意不是承诺→我能给你的→再靠近一点→你可以留下→自在的默契。

商店购买不会增加Hailey好感；补给、制作材料、枕头及女仆装备属于地点服务。社区事件连接Kalb、Ben Arthur、Sophie、Shadow、Willow及Nerida，其他参与者的条件见各自指南。 商店的偷金币行动属于独立盗贼公会活动，不推进Hailey关系。

开启圣诞活动后，已知25可在06:00–21:00进行给Hailey节日祝福，10分钟，仅一次，获得1代币和10直接好感，不消耗物品或金钱。

偏好的属性为力量、耐力、能力。学会共情可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 第二次交心不出现时，检查供货事件及Elara是否在度假。
  • 晚间拜访从鎏金街开始，商店21:00已经关门。
  • 生育谈话需要后面的个人事件及生育设置；普通约会和私密拜访不要求它。
