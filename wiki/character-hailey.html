概览

Overview

Hailey is the kind shopkeeper at the General Goods Store in the King's Demesne. Her current route focuses on everyday conversations, a one-time wisdom activity, community scenes and an optional Christmas task. She is not recruitable as a brothel worker and has no personal romance, date, apartment or pregnancy route.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Go to the Merchants Quarter in the King's Demesne, then General Goods Store, during 6 AM–9 PM. The General Goods Store introduction starts automatically once. Choose Caring for 9 base Hailey points: 5 completion points plus 4 for the response.
  2. Use Talk to Hailey, up to five times per game day. Favorite stats and already knowing Empathy can improve the final gain. The handler records 1 base point, despite the action metadata listing 2.
  3. At Acknowledged, check the shared community scenes below. Some require Sophie, Willow or Nerida to reach their own stages; Hailey's affection alone does not start them.
  4. At Known, raise Communication to 46 and choose Empathy Wisdom. This takes 30 minutes, awards 8 fixed Hailey affection, and can be completed once.
  5. With Hailey Known, Kalb Acknowledged and Ben Arthur Acknowledged, return to the store during 6–9 PM for The Missing Ingredients Mystery. The guide gives its entry conditions without revealing the mystery's answer.
  6. If Christmas activities are enabled, use Holiday Cheer for Hailey while she is Known for a separate one-time affection and Token reward.
Buying shop supplies is useful for your inventory, but purchases do not award Hailey affection. Her higher relationship stages do not unlock an unimplemented date or worker route.

Talk and Empathy Wisdom

Talk costs 15 minutes and records 1 base point on each of the first five daily uses. Further attempts record −1 and use the shared over-limit handling. Ordinary base points are adjusted by favorite-stat, special-skill and general relationship bonuses.
Empathy Wisdom requires Known and Communication 46, at the store during Hailey's schedule. It has no money, energy or calm payment configured beyond its 30-minute time cost. Its 8 affection is added directly and is therefore fixed.
The activity advertises “+1 Empathy”, but the current reward tries to increase a core stat named Empathy. Empathy is a skill, not a core stat, so this does not increase a stat or teach the Empathy skill. The fixed affection reward and one-time completion still apply. Learn Empathy through an actual skill lesson elsewhere if you want its relationship bonus.
The introduction, Talk and Empathy Wisdom are separate rewards; the one-time activities do not consume the Talk allowance.

Christmas Activity

When Christmas activities are enabled, visit the store during 6 AM–9 PM with Hailey Known and choose Holiday Cheer for Hailey. It takes 10 minutes, can be completed once, gives 10 fixed Hailey affection and awards 1 Token. It has no gift item or money payment configured.
This is Christmas task 14 and does not require a real-world holiday date. Enable the event's activities through the Christmas setting. The task's fixed affection is separate from the modified base points used by Talk and community scenes.

Optional Store Theft

The location also offers Steal Coins with Thieves Guild clout 10 and Sleight of Hand. It takes 20 minutes, is available once per game day, and uses Rogue as its success percentage, capped at 85%.
On success it gives $10–50 plus the Luck/2 stat bonus and has a 25% chance of +1 Thieves Guild clout. The action separately has a 25% chance of +1 Evil alignment, regardless of success. It does not raise Hailey affection or advance her community scenes.

Troubleshooting

  • Store or Hailey unavailable: return during 6 AM–9 PM; the store itself has opening hours.
  • Talk gain differs from its description: the current handler uses 1 base point, with five daily uses and ordinary relationship modifiers.
  • Empathy Wisdom missing: reach Known and Communication 46, and check whether you already completed it.
  • No Empathy skill after Wisdom: its current core-stat reward does not teach the skill; the fixed +8 affection still applies.
  • Community scene missing: check every participant's listed stage, Sophie's recruitment where required, vacation status and the scene's individual time window.
  • No romance or recruitment: Hailey's current content is a community and shopkeeper route. Higher affection does not add a worker, apartment or date activity.
