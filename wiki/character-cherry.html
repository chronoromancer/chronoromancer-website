Overview

Cherry is a nighttime sex worker in the Present-era Downtown Back Alley. Her implemented gameplay consists of conversation and two paid services. She has no quest recruitment or separate romance finale in her current character data.

Quick Info

Attribute Value
Age / era / alignment 24 / Present / Evil
Location Back Alley, Downtown
Schedule Every day, 9 PM–2 AM
Favorite stats / special relationship skill None configured
Service unlock Modified Beast 6 or higher

Finding Cherry

Visit the Back Alley in Present Downtown between 9 PM and 2 AM. Her shift crosses midnight: 9 PM–midnight and midnight–2 AM are both valid windows. There is no introduction quest or affection-stage requirement for her ordinary menu.

Actions and Rewards

Action Requirement Cost Reward Daily allowance
Talk Cherry present 20 minutes 1 base affection Four
Oral Cherry present; Beast 6 45 minutes, $150 2 base affection; 30 player Erotic Experience; 40% chance of Beast +1 One
Doggy Cherry present; Beast 6 45 minutes, $150 2 base affection; 30 player Erotic Experience; 40% chance of Beast +1 One
The two service allowances are separate. Completing Oral does not consume Doggy's allowance. Their costs contain no configured energy or calm deduction. Each requires $150 ordinary money, not an item payment.
Oral plays a registered video sequence. Doggy displays its one-time-action illustration and message; it is not a second registered video sequence. Both repeat on a later day rather than completing a permanent quest flag.
The service gate checks Beast with modifiers. Higher Cherry affection does not replace Beast 6, and lowering your effective Beast below 6 can hide the services again. Each successful service independently rolls its 40% Beast +1 chance; the increase is not guaranteed.
Affection values are base rewards. Easy-mode base adjustments and the shared relationship calculation can change the final gain. Cherry has no favorite-stat or special-skill bonus of her own. The 30 player Erotic Experience is separate from NPC intimacy experience; these handlers and her video data do not configure a separate NPC intimacy-experience award.

Practical Walkthrough

  1. Arrive during her 9 PM–2 AM shift.
  2. Use Talk if you want to increase her affection; it needs no starting stage.
  3. Raise effective Beast to 6 to unlock both services.
  4. Bring $150 for one service, or $300 for both. Each takes 45 minutes and uses its own once-per-day allowance.
  5. Choose the video service or illustrated service you want to see. Their affection, player-experience and Beast-chance rewards match.
  6. Return on a later day for refreshed allowances. There is no further H2H, date, gift action, apartment visit, skill lesson or worker recruitment registered for Cherry.

If Progress Stalls

  • Check the overnight schedule; she leaves at 2 AM and returns at 9 PM.
  • Missing service buttons usually mean effective Beast below 6. Affection is not the service gate.
  • Check money and the chosen service's own daily allowance. The other service may still be available.
  • Beast gains are a 40% roll per successful service, not a guaranteed increase.
  • Her action list remains the same at higher affection. There is no additional personal quest chain to unlock.
