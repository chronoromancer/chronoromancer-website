概览

治安官Ruxandra 是吸血鬼实验室的 260岁炼金师 输入 现她目前的路线包含一个介绍,每日谈话, 6次相继的吸血鬼-stat相遇, 因果关系窗口 Torea,并 a 血液炼金厂 交换血球 永久物品和升级。
这个走行部列出了访问,要求,选择和机械奖励等内容. 实验室的重大发现和个人故事启示被留给了游戏.

Magistra Ruxandra Walkthrough and Blood Alchemy Guide
Spoiler warning: This guide covers the vampire depths, late-story vampire access, Ruxandra's lore scenes, Blood Orbs, and every current Blood Alchemy purchase.
This guide expands on Sh1n7's community-created Ruxandra wiki page, used under CC BY-SA 4.0. All requirements, prices, and effects below were rechecked against the current game source.

Overview

Magistra Ruxandra is the vampire laboratory's 260-year-old alchemist in the Present. Her current route contains an introduction, daily Talk, six sequential Vampire-stat encounters, The Causal Window with Torea, and a Blood Alchemy shop that exchanges Blood Orbs for permanent items and upgrades.
This walkthrough lists access, requirements, choices and mechanical rewards. The laboratory's major discoveries and personal story revelations are left to the game.
NB: The walkthrough sometimes refers to "effective" Vampire stat vs. raw Vampire stat (with or without bonus), but to this day, there's no Key Item or Potion buff that awards a Vampire stat bonus (9.8.5).

Reach the Vampire Laboratory

Two routes reach the laboratory:

Suggested Progression

  1. Reach the Vampire Laboratory through either route. The automatic Magistra Ruxandra introduction completes once; Caring or Flirty gives 8 total base, versus Funny's 7.
  2. Use Talk to Ruxandra, up to five positive talks daily. Five talks cost 100 minutes for 5 base, before shared relationship bonuses.
  3. Raise your effective Vampire stat. After the introduction, revisit the laboratory for the six encounters at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60. Each requires the previous encounter complete.
  4. At effective Vampire 25, use the location action Ruxandra's Blood Alchemy. Carry enough unconsumed Blood Orbs for the desired purchase.
  5. After the introduction and Torea Acquaintance, return to the laboratory for The Causal Window. It is independent of the six lore thresholds and Ruxandra's affection stage.
Only one eligible automatic plot is selected on a location visit. If several are ready, leave and re-enter the laboratory to continue. Raising Ruxandra's affection alone does not satisfy a Vampire-stat requirement.

Blood Alchemy Shop

For the dedicated shop guide, illustrated interface, purchase instructions, and buying advice, see Ruxandra's Alchemy Shop.
Use Ruxandra's Blood Alchemy in the Vampire Laboratory with effective Vampire 25 or higher. The shop does not require the introduction, a lore encounter, Ruxandra affection, Alchemy skill or a separate supporter flag. Opening it takes zero game time and spends nothing.
All eight entries require effective Vampire 25. Purchases consume the listed Blood Orbs from consumable inventory, with no configured cash, time, Energy, Calm, affection or intimacy XP reward/cost.

Six permanent key items

Each item can be bought once. An owned item cannot be purchased again. Their total cost is 365 Blood Orbs.

The shop sells these items, not the Vampirism skill itself. You do not need Vampirism to buy or benefit from Fangs or Grimoire at their ordinary 15% chance. Hypnotic Gaze and Sleep Spores can both contribute to Sleep; enemy penalties and immunity still apply.

Crimson Grail is hidden without the Stone. It raises the existing Stone's level rather than creating or consuming a Stone. Blood Exchange raises the maximum; it does not include a configured current-Energy refill.

Obtain and Use Blood Orbs

Blood Orbs are both shop currency and a usable inventory consumable. Spending an orb at the shop does not apply its inventory-use effect.
Source / use Current behavior
Eligible vampire combat, dungeon floors 21–25 Victory has a 15% base chance to add one Blood Orb; drop-affix and Thieves Gloves bonuses can modify the chance, and a guaranteed-drop flag overrides the roll. No pickaxe is required
Goblin Blood Orb random encounter Trade 5 Dragon Coins for one orb, when the encounter appears
Goblin theft alternative Requires Sleight of Hand; the raw Rogue +20 value is checked against a 1–100 roll. Success gives one orb; failure costs 25 Health
Use Blood Orb from inventory Consumes one orb, raises raw Vampire +1 up to 999, and refills Health, Energy, Calm and Satiation to their current maximum values
Eligible combat enemies include the vampire, vampire boss, huntsman, acolyte, ripper, enforcer and stalker families and registered blood enemies. The combat drop's floor 21–25 condition still applies. Random dungeon encounters are not guaranteed on every tile or every visit.
To reach a stat gate, use or otherwise raise Vampire; to pay the shop, keep the required orbs unconsumed. Ruxandra's lore and shop check effective Vampire, whereas the inventory-use increase changes your raw Vampire stat.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Library route blocked: complete Story Beat 061; its starting window is City Library 8–10 PM after Story Beat 060. The floor-23 castle remains an independent entrance.
  • Ruxandra absent: reach Vampire Laboratory, rather than stopping in the Vampire Lair. Her laboratory schedule is all day.
  • Lore does not trigger: check introduction completion, effective Vampire, and the previous encounter; leave and re-enter after another eligible scene.
  • High affection does not unlock lore: the six gates use Vampire 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60, without an ordinary relationship-stage substitute.
  • The Causal Window missing: check Torea Acquaintance and Ruxandra's introduction; the six-scene lore chain is not required.
  • Shop unavailable: check effective Vampire 25 and the laboratory's location actions.
  • Cannot afford a purchase: carry the specified number of Blood Orbs, not cash or Dragon Coins. Inventory use consumes orbs before they can be spent.
  • Crimson Grail missing: already own an Elemental Stone.
  • Unique item cannot be bought again: it remains owned; only Blood Exchange and Crimson Grail are repeatable.
Her implemented completion goals are the introduction, all six Vampire encounters, The Causal Window and the available alchemy services. No heart-to-heart, date, apartment, private movie, Kupie selection, venue invitation, worker recruitment, pregnancy route or NPC intimacy reward-image files are currently configured for Ruxandra.
