Elara — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Elara is the court-appointed manager of the Rose & Thorn in the Medieval Demesne. Her current content combines office conversations, management-related shared scenes, a Bazaar outing, an office encounter, worker training, two crafted-gift upgrades, Queenstone bonding and Sirena Isle resort activities. This guide lists the requirements and rewards while leaving her major story revelations for the game.

Starting Her Route

  1. Follow the King's early food-supply route: Christy's Potato Education and the Sack of Potatoes open The Potato Solution at the Throne Room; then complete Wilfred's potato delivery.
  2. Visit the Throne Room, 10 AM–5 PM, after that delivery for The Royal Appointment. Completion grants ownership of the establishment, supplies the Office and master bedroom, and activates Elara as worker/manager 1. It also awards 5 base Elara affection.
  3. Go to Elara's Office. Use Personal Talk twice per day and Discuss Workers three times per day. They have separate allowances and award the same 1 base affection per positive use. More active workers change the discussion dialogue, not its base reward.
  4. At Known, use Business Excellence if your Communication is 72 for +1 Commerce and 14 direct affection, once.
  5. At Friend, take Visit the Bazaar, starting from the Office 8 AM–6 PM. It takes 60 minutes, with no configured money or energy payment, and completes once.
  6. At 550 affection, visit her Office, 9 PM–midnight, while Elara is active and home, for After Hours with the Manager. It completes once, grants 20 base affection, and records 30 NPC missionary experience. This scene also opens her worker-visit/training eligibility.
  7. Continue building affection and shared scenes. At In Love, her two crafted-gift upgrades become available after their separate prerequisites; holding the Queenstone also opens daily Intimate Bonding.
  8. Once the resort system is unlocked, send her on vacation through the worker-room system for her resort activities and repeatable Sunset Walk H2H.
The Bazaar outing, office encounter and resort Sunset Walk are separate activities. Her only currently registered personal H2H is the resort Sunset Walk, rather than a one-time office conversation chain.

Office Encounter and Worker Training

After Hours with the Manager triggers automatically at 550 affection, with Elara active, at her Office, 9 PM–midnight, while she is not vacationing. It does not require the Bazaar date or a separate skill in its plot conditions. Its 20-base affection and 30 NPC experience are one-time rewards.
After completing that scene, use the worker-visit system for Elara while she is active, not resting, not on vacation and inside her assigned shift. Her initial worker shift is 10 AM–6 PM, independently of her all-day Office portrait. A worker visit is available once per day.
The normal visit costs 60 minutes, 60 energy and 40 calm. The corresponding Time, Power and Focus potion buffs reduce those separately to 30 minutes, 30 energy and 20 calm. Training grants 50 player Erotic Experience, improves worker stats, adds worker experience and advances the training sequence.

If Progress Stalls

  • Complete The Royal Appointment to own the establishment and activate Elara. Her Office is separate from the general brothel location.
  • Personal Talk and Discuss Workers have separate daily allowances. A larger workforce changes dialogue, while Discuss Workers still grants 1 base point.

  • For the office encounter, meet 550 affection, keep Elara active and home, and visit 9 PM–midnight. A daytime visit does not satisfy its window.

  • The Cowgirl gift needs Bazaar Date; the Oral gift needs The Old Archives. Both need After Hours, In Love and the correct crafted item.
  • Queenstone bonding needs the actual Queenstone and In Love, and repeats on a new day.
  • For resort group scenes, send all listed workers together and visit the correct location during the listed window. Bring Elara home again for home community and court scenes.
