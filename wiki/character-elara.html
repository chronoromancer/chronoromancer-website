Overview

Elara manages Rose & Thorn. Her route combines office conversations, a bazaar date and business meetings, followed by worker training and crafted gifts.

Where to find them

Complete The Royal Appointment in Throne Room, 10:00–16:59, after Wilfred’s potato delivery. Follow the food-supply chain through Christy, King Graham and Wilfred. The appointment opens the office and master bedroom and brings Elara onto the staff.

At home, her portrait is in Elara's Office all day. Staff working hours are separate; a resort trip temporarily moves her away.

Route and relationship milestones

The bazaar date needs Friend (100), starts 08:00–17:59 and takes 60 minutes with no money or Energy cost. After Hours with the Manager instead needs 550 affection, active employment and her return home: enter the office 21:00–23:59. It does not require the bazaar date.

The office event gives 20 base affection and 30 NPC Missionary experience and opens worker training. Ordinary training takes 60 minutes, 60 Energy and 40 Calm, once per day; her initial shift is 10:00–17:59.

At In Love (3,000), bring Void-Kissed Charm after the bazaar date and office event for A Gift Without Obligation. Bring Golden Pulse Bracelet after The Old Archives and the office event for What the Ledgers Leave Out. Each begins at home in her office, consumes the gift and takes 0 minutes, without extra affection. Each doubles matching training experience and adds 0.5 of base income: 1.5× with one, 2× with both.

Owning Queenstone and reaching In Love enables 60-minute bonding, once per day, for one stone level.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the office has not opened, finish the appointment chain rather than raising affection.
  • For the office event, check 550 affection, 21:00–23:59, active employment and her return from vacation.
  • Crafted upgrades need the exact item and preceding scenes. Return to her office after crafting.
  • Staff shifts, personal portraits and resort presence are different schedules.

概览

Elara管理玫瑰与荆棘，路线包含办公室交谈、集市约会及经营相关会面，后续还能训练员工和交付锻造礼物。

去哪里找

完成Wilfred的土豆递送后，于10:00—16:59到王座厅触发王室任命。供粮任务会依次涉及Christy、King Graham和Wilfred；任命完成后，办公室与主卧开放，Elara加入员工队伍。

她在家时，Elara的办公室的头像全天出现。员工上班时间另算，度假时会暂时离开。

路线与关系进展

集市约会要求朋友（100），08:00—17:59开始，花费60分钟，不消耗金钱或精力。与经理的下班后时光则要求550好感、员工已启用且回到家，于21:00—23:59进入办公室，不要求先完成集市约会。

办公室事件给予20基础好感和30点NPC传教士位经验，并开启员工训练。普通训练每次60分钟、60精力、40镇定，每天一次；初始班次为10:00—17:59。

恋爱中（3,000）后，完成集市约会和办公室事件，携带虚吻护符触发一份不附带义务的礼物；完成旧档案馆和办公室事件，携带金脉手镯触发账本未载之事。都在她回家后的办公室开始，消耗礼物，不消耗时间，也没有额外好感门槛。每项使对应训练经验翻倍，并增加基础收入的0.5倍：一项为1.5倍，两项为2倍。

持有女王石并达到恋爱中后，可每天花60分钟建立一次羁绊，使石头提升1级。

个人交谈与讨论员工各有独立次数。她在塞壬岛可交谈、用餐、放松、水疗、看日落及进行海滩私密时光；最后一项给予50基础好感和25点NPC女上位经验。地点与时段见度假村指南。

她偏好的属性为商业、魅力、智力。学会领导力可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 办公室未开放时，继续任命任务，不必靠刷好感解锁。
  • 深夜办公室事件需检查550好感、21:00—23:59、员工已启用，以及她是否结束度假。
  • 礼物升级需指定物品和前置事件，锻造后回办公室交付。
  • 员工班次、个人头像和度假地点分别计算。
