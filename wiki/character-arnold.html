Overview

Arnold, designated A.R.N.O.L.D. — Autonomous Residential Networked Operations & Lifestyle Domestic, is a prototype service android at Evangeline's downtown mansion. He handles household and concierge duties. His current content is an introduction, repeatable conversation, a five-round household-risk heart-to-heart and supporting appearances in the investment/household route.

Meeting Arnold

Enter the downtown mansion foyer to trigger Meeting Arnold once. Afterward, Talk takes 15 minutes and gives 1 base affection, with 90 uses per day and no affection-stage prerequisite. The very high daily allowance is a limit, not a requirement to use all ninety conversations.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Household Risk Walkthrough

Arnold's heart-to-heart depends on the Miki/Maggie investment route, rather than affection alone. Follow these requirements in order:
  1. Complete Story Beat 55, then visit Miki's Bedroom, 8–11 PM, for Some Guy With Miki.
  2. Visit the Office Building's Offices, 9 AM–5 PM, for The Wrong Office.
  3. Complete Maggie's Acumen, then visit the Investment room, 1–5 PM, for The Eye for Acquisition.
  4. Own at least one company and return to the Investment room, 9 AM–5 PM, for His Name on the Door.
  5. At Friend with Miki, own five companies and complete her Terms of Ownership H2H in her bedroom 8–11 PM. This Miki conversation needs the paid version, costs 75 minutes and 20 energy, and is one-time.
  6. Own ten companies, then visit Downtown Park, 2–5 PM, for Ten Little Kingdoms. Visit the mansion foyer, 8–11 PM, for Projected Household Growth.
  7. Own 25 companies and visit the Boardroom, 9 AM–3 PM, for Still Not an Appointment, followed by the Investment room, 1–5 PM, for Hostile Mentorship.
  8. Reach Known with Arnold. In the mansion foyer between 8 PM and midnight, select Household Risk. It takes 75 minutes, has no configured money, energy or calm charge, and completes once.
  9. With that H2H complete and 40 companies owned, visit the Boardroom, 9 AM–3 PM, for On the Radar. The household route then continues through Miki and Evangeline.
The listed investment scenes are unavailable after Controlling Interest completes. This is a route-state restriction, not a reason to grind more affection with Arnold. The later story-dependent recovery is outside the currently released story and is not part of this walkthrough.

Mansion Access

Miki's Bedroom normally appears as a foyer travel action. If its route state withdraws access, the replacement Ask Arnold About Miki action explains the restriction and has no configured time cost. It does not restore access through repeated use or relationship points. Arnold's ordinary Talk and already-earned relationship history remain separate from that travel restriction.

If Progress Stalls

  • Household Risk needs Known with Arnold and completed Hostile Mentorship, and starts only 8 PM–midnight.
  • Company ownership thresholds are 1, 5, 10, 25 and 40 at different points. A high company count does not replace earlier scenes and Miki's H2H.
  • The required Miki H2H needs Friend and paid access, so Arnold's route can indirectly depend on that access even though his own H2H has no paid flag.
  • After Controlling Interest, the blocked household scenes and Miki-room access cannot be reopened by more Talk actions in the current release.
  • Arnold offers no authored skill lesson despite having Master Engineer as his relationship bonus skill.
