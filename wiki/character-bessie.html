Bessie — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Bessie is a proud minotaur woman recruitable from the normal dungeon. After recruitment she lives in an assigned brothel cell, with conversation, three personal video scenes, five shared corridor scenes and the separate monster care and training system.

Finding and Recruiting Bessie

  1. Build the Cell Corridor and a vacant built cell among cells 2–8. Cell 1 is reserved for Bobo and does not count as her vacancy.
  2. Fight the named Bessie enemy on normal dungeon floors 27–35. Generic Minotaur, Minotaur King and Mech Minotaur are different enemy entries and do not increase her recruitment counter.
  3. For a reliable named encounter, investigate the hidden wall at floor 29, tile (1, 6) after building the corridor. It starts a Bessie battle while she is unrecruited. This special interaction does not run in Infinity mode.
  4. Win five named Bessie battles. The first recruitment roll is on the sixth victory.
  5. A successful roll with a vacant cell launches her recruitment scene. Accept to activate her record, assign her to the offered cell and receive 10 direct affection.
  6. Visit that assigned cell for her ordinary NPC menu, or use monster management for care and training.
The recruitment chance is 10 + 10 × (total victories − 5) + player Taming, capped at 100%. On victory six it is 20% + Taming. Further eligible victories increase it by ten percentage points each. Infinity victories do not increase the counter or offer recruitment.
If there is no vacant cell, the no-cell scene leaves her unrecruited. Build a vacant cell and win another eligible battle for another opportunity. Releasing her also leaves her unrecruited.
In battle, Bessie is weak to Ice and strong against Fire, and uses Horn Charge, Earth Slam and Strike. The stronger named opponent's victory is the one that counts.

Monster Care and Training

Her affection meter and tamed percentage are separate. Recruitment begins her monster record at 25 tamed, with 110 health, 0 stress and 0 experience. Regular care helps maintain access to training.
  • Use the monster-management screen to select Bessie for a daily care session. The session is unavailable while she is resting or after she has already had that day's care visit.
  • Feed consumes 8 food and adds 10 tamed, subject to the normal cap. A failed feeding attempt without enough food can be retried after obtaining food.
  • A care session permits one use of each available category: Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Tool effects differ; inspect the selected tool's effects on tamed, stress, health and experience.
  • Teach and Training need at least 30 tamed. Feed first if her tamed value has fallen below that threshold.
  • Ordinary daily decay is 6 tamed per day after her first care visit. Tamed is normally capped at 100; the Claw tool uses the shared higher-cap behavior. Her three personal video visits also add 5 tamed each.


Feed first after recruitment: her starting 25 tamed is below the 30 needed for Teach and Training. Spending 8 food raises it by 10, normally making those categories available in that care session. Affection does not replace the tamed threshold.

Eight Training Levels

Training unlocks sequentially from level 1 through level 8. Completing a training session advances the level by one, up to 8, and leaves earlier types available. It improves Bessie's monster stats, rather than teaching the player those named skills.

If Progress Stalls

  • Defeat Bessie, not generic minotaurs, in the normal dungeon. The first recruitment roll is victory six.
  • Build a vacant cell 2–8 before a successful recruitment offer; the corridor alone is insufficient.
  • Her post-recruitment portrait requires Acknowledged, normally 10 affection. The recruitment accept award supplies that amount.
  • For locked monster Teach or Training, raise tamed to 30 with Feed and check rest/visit status. Raising NPC affection cannot substitute.
  • Each personal video needs the paid version, its relationship stage and 100 energy before cost reductions.
  • The video allowances are per scene; corridor scenes are one-time and need Friend with both participants.
  • The Leather Boots comment is random and optional. It is not a prerequisite for recruiting her or advancing her relationship.
