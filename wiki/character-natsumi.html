Overview

Natsumi is a 27-year-old fox woman in the optional Wildheart: Wild Woods content. Find her at Moonflower Gardens, where her current personal route consists of two automatic story scenes and daily Talk.
This walkthrough covers the complete currently registered route and the requirements for reaching her world.

Reaching Moonflower Gardens

  1. In the main menu's Mod Manager, enable Wildheart: Wild Woods. Restart the game after changing its enabled state.
  2. Fulfill the mod's Wildheart redeem requirement. Enabled status and redeemed access are separate checks.
  3. Reach the Deep Woods entrance to Wild Woods and choose Enter Wild Woods. This leads to the Wild Woods Gateway when both access requirements are met.
  4. Use the world's map to travel to Moonflower Gardens, then open Natsumi's portrait.
Natsumi's schedule has no even/odd-day restriction, minimum relationship stage or completed-story requirement. You do not need to finish Leona's or Taiga's introduction first. The Gateway's Return to Deep Woods action leaves the world.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter Moonflower Gardens for A Welcome in Moonflower Gardens, which triggers automatically once, at any hour, with no minimum affection.
  2. Funny gives the largest introduction reward: 4 choice points plus 5 fixed completion points, for 9 base affection. Caring and Flirty each give 8 base total.
  3. Use Talk, up to five times daily, until Natsumi reaches Known, 25 ordinary affection. The introduction plus daily Talk are sufficient; no gift or other character's scene is required.
  4. Revisit Moonflower Gardens, with the introduction complete and Natsumi Known or higher, for The Paths We Choose.
  5. Choose Caring or Flirty, each giving 4 choice points plus 8 fixed completion points, for 12 base affection. Funny gives 11 base total.
Both scenes are once-only and can trigger at any hour. Their replies converge on the same scene completion; the choice changes affection and the immediate response rather than locking out the follow-up scene.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

The Paths We Choose is the end of Natsumi's currently registered personal story. You can continue daily Talk and raise affection afterward. Her current definition has no personal gifts, skill lesson, date, heart-to-heart, apartment conversation, pregnancy route, Kupie album, Christmas encounter, brothel-worker training or resort vacation.
  • Wild Woods entrance missing: check both Mod Manager enabled status and the redeem requirement, then restart after changing the mod's enabled state.
  • Natsumi missing: visit Moonflower Gardens inside Wild Woods. Her registered schedule is all day, rather than only night hours suggested by the garden's name.
  • Second scene missing: complete A Welcome in Moonflower Gardens, reach Known, and revisit the gardens. No other NPC's scene is required.
  • No later activity appears: the current implementation has the two scenes and Talk listed above; higher affection does not unlock an additional registered personal route.
