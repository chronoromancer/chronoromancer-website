Keira

Overview

Keira’s route starts with a rooftop race found while searching Back Alleys. Winning opens her regular portrait, a personal rooftop chain, three H2Hs, a night-market date and a way to obtain Thieves Gloves.

Where to find them

Back Alleys, Kings Demesne: 00:00–03:59 every day, after winning the race. To find the first race, search Back Alleys at 20:00–03:59 with at least 10 Thieves Guild Clout. It is a random encounter, so one search need not find it. Below 10 Clout she does not offer the race.

Route and relationship milestones

Accept the race and improve Ability before trying again after a loss. The first race checks modified Ability against a 1–300 roll, then has a separate 15% upset chance: even Ability 300+ is not a guaranteed win. Winning gives 3 guild Clout and opens Keira’s regular route. Later search rematches are harder and use a separate result; they are not required for the personal chain.

Once both Keira and Arestes are Friend (100), enter Hidden Chamber for The Early Watch, which opens the public guild from 20:00 instead of 22:00. The separate Phantom Stone route needs Arestes, Keira and Shadow all Romantic (800), active recruited Shadow, Thieves Gloves and Dark Consolador; start at the public guild.

Her favorite stats are Ability, Rogue and Perception, and Fleet Footed improves the relationship bonus. Search rematches award 3 guild Clout and 5 direct Keira affection on a win. Ordinary Route Intel is its own once-daily portrait action; the search encounter’s casual intel conversation is not the same reward.

What usually blocks the next step

  • No portrait yet: meeting her or accepting the race is not enough; win the first race.
  • No race offered: first raise guild Clout to 10, search at night and allow for random encounters.
  • High Ability but a loss: the independent upset roll remains. Try another encounter.
  • Night Bazaar is absent after 02:00; start before then, even though Keira stays until 04:00.
  • A Quiet Favor uses 150 Valuables, not $150; it is hidden if you already own Thieves Gloves.
  • Loose Tiles also needs the first H2H. More affection alone will not replace it.

概览

Keira的路线从后巷搜寻时遇到的屋顶竞速开始。获胜后可解锁固定头像、个人屋顶事件、三次谈心、夜市约会，以及获取盗贼手套的委托。

去哪里找

中世纪国王领地的后巷，每天00:00–03:59，先赢过竞速才会固定出现。首次竞速需要至少10盗贼公会声望，再于20:00–次日03:59搜寻后巷。这是随机遭遇，一次搜寻不保证出现；声望不足10时不会提出比赛。

路线与关系进展

接受比赛；失败后可提高能力，再次搜寻挑战。首次比赛先以含加成的能力对比1–300的随机数，再额外有15%的翻盘机会，因此能力300以上也不能保证获胜。首次获胜可得3公会声望并开放固定路线。之后搜寻到的重赛更难，使用另一套判定，但不是个人路线的必需步骤。

她偏好的属性为能力、盗贼和感知，步履轻盈可提高关系加成。搜寻重赛获胜会给3公会声望及5直接好感。固定头像的“路线情报”是独立的每日行动，不要把搜寻遭遇里的普通情报对话当成同一奖励。

下表时间均指开始行动的时间；例如，结束于23:00的时段，最晚可在22:59开始。基础好感会受关系加成影响；直接好感按所列数值增加。

卡住时检查什么

  • 没有固定头像：见过她或接受比赛还不够，必须实际赢过首次比赛。
  • 没有比赛选项：先把公会声望升到10，夜间搜寻，并考虑随机遭遇不保证每次出现。
  • 能力很高还是输了：独立的翻盘判定仍会生效，可以再次遇到她后挑战。
  • 02:00后看不到夜市约会：必须在02:00前开始，虽然Keira会留到04:00。
  • “静默委托”消耗150贵重品，不是$150；已持有盗贼手套时不会显示。
  • “松动的瓦片”还要完成第一次谈心，单独提高好感不够。
