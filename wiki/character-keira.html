概览

Keira 是中世纪盗贼协会的屋顶选手。在一条小巷搜索中赢得她的比赛,以解锁她正常的肖像,然后跟随两次自动碰面、三次心对心和一次深夜约会。她的路线也获得了奖项。 盗贼手套,修改盾的营业时间并支持 幽灵石 进展本指南涵盖各项要求和奖励,但不重述个人场景。

Overview

Keira is the Medieval Thieves Guild's rooftop runner. Win her race during an alley search to unlock her normal portrait, then follow two automatic encounters, three Heart-to-Hearts and a late-night date. Her route also awards Thieves Gloves, changes the guild's opening hours and supports Phantom Stone progression. This guide covers the requirements and rewards without retelling the personal scenes.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Build 10 Thieves Guild clout, following Arestes's and Shadow's guild progression or repeatable clout activities.
  2. At Back Alleys, 8 PM–4 AM, repeatedly choose Explore Back Alleys until Keira's random encounter appears. Accept the race and win it. A first win gives 3 guild clout and permanently opens her normal portrait during midnight–4 AM.
  3. Use Talk to Keira, three times daily, to reach Known. Return to Back Alleys midnight–4 AM for The Roofroads. Caring or Flirty at both choices gives 14 total base affection and 1 guild clout.
  4. After The Roofroads, use Route Intel once daily. At Known and Ability 35, complete Roofline Drills for Ability +1, 8 direct affection and 1 guild clout, once.
  5. At Acquaintance, complete The Good Ledge. At Close Friend, revisit the alleys for Loose Tiles, then choose Stop Moving.
  6. After Stop Moving, start Night Bazaar, midnight–2 AM, while Close Friend. Choose Flirty → Caring or Flirty → Flirty for 22 total base affection.
  7. After Night Bazaar, reach Crush and complete No Escape Route. All three H2Hs are one-time conversations; their best answers are below.
  8. At Friend, carry 150 Valuables and choose A Quiet Favor for Thieves Gloves. It is a separate item branch and does not require the romance H2Hs or The Roofroads completed.
  9. At Keira and Arestes Friend, enter Hidden Chamber for The Early Watch. At Keira, Arestes and Shadow Romantic, meet the additional raid requirements below to obtain the Phantom Stone. At Keira In Love, use Intimate Bonding to raise that stone once daily.
The search race, normal portrait, and Night Bazaar use different windows. A successful first race at 9 PM unlocks her contact, but her portrait still appears only from midnight.

Unlocking Keira Through Alley Searches

Explore Back Alleys is a location action in Medieval Back Alleys, costing 45 minutes and 15 Energy per attempt before buffs and time penalties. Keira enters its random encounter pool during 8 PM–4 AM. The pool contains many other encounters; the configured 50% check applies when her encounter is selected, not a 50% overall chance of meeting her on every search.
Before your first race win:
  • Below 10 Thieves Guild clout, she does not offer the race. The encounter checks modified Perception against a random 1–300 roll. On a failed check, there is a 50% chance she takes a random 10–150 gold, capped to leave at least one gold.
  • At 10+ guild clout, she offers the race. Choose Accept the challenge. There is no minimum Keira affection stage, fee, Fleet Footed skill, or fixed Ability threshold on accepting.
  • Winning requires a 1–300 roll at or below modified Ability, and an independent 1–100 roll above 15. For Ability between 0 and 300, the win probability is Ability ÷ 300 × 85%. Even Ability 300 retains the separate 15% upset chance.
  • A first win sets the persistent race-unlock state and awards 3 Thieves Guild clout. It has no direct Keira affection reward configured. Losing or declining leaves the normal portrait locked; you can search again on a later attempt.
Fleet Footed helps Keira's relationship multiplier and one H2H answer; it is not a separate race-success check. Ordinary game stat modifiers can improve the Ability used by the roll.

After unlocking her, her random search encounter changes to Ask what she has noticed tonight / Challenge her to a rematch / Move on. These are separate from her normal portrait's Talk and Route Intel daily counters.
A rematch uses modified Ability minus 30, clamped to 0–299, against a 1–300 roll, plus an independent roll above 25. Its chance is clamped(Ability − 30, 0, 299) ÷ 300 × 75%, at most 74.75%. A win gives 3 guild clout and 5 direct Keira affection. A loss does not remove her permanent contact unlock. The search intel response itself has no configured affection reward.

A Quiet Favor and Thieves Gloves

At Friend, open Keira's portrait during midnight–4 AM and choose A Quiet Favor with 150 Valuables in your consumable inventory. You must not already own Thieves Gloves, and the scene must be unfinished. The action has no additional Roofroads, H2H, date or clout gate beyond unlocking Keira's portrait.
The action consumes 150 Valuables and awards the Thieves Gloves key item, once. Its three replies all converge on the reward; no Keira affection bonus is registered for these choices.
Current-code time cost: 90 minutes before buffs or time penalties. The action's ordinary time pipeline advances 45 minutes, then its completion handler advances another 45 minutes. The displayed 45-minute action cost therefore understates the full time spent. No extra gold or Energy payment is configured.
Owning Thieves Gloves adds five percentage points to supported Infinity Dungeon item-drop and Break & Enter checks, capped by each check's own maximum. Owning Phantom Stone also adds 0.1 percentage points per stone level to that supported glove bonus.
With both Gloves and Phantom Stone, a separate post-battle extra-item roll has 0.002% chance per stone level, reaching 100% at level 50,000. Its reward is one item from the configured pool. The existing Luck Potion retry applies to a failed extra-item roll. These glove mechanics do not change Keira's rooftop-race formula.

Phantom Stone Raid and Daily Bonding

For The Phantom Stone Raid, visit Thieves Guild while meeting all of these requirements:
  • Arestes, Keira and Shadow at Romantic or higher.
  • Shadow recruited and active as worker 8.
  • Own Thieves Gloves and Dark Consolador, the latter from Arestes's separate item route.
  • Do not already own Phantom Stone; the raid must be unfinished.
The raid has no extra hour restriction, but normal guild entry uses its travel hours. It contains six consecutive battles at floor level 50: two cutthroats, two enforcers, a lieutenant and a raidmaster. All have Dark strength / Light weakness. Their HP and combat values scale through the plot-combat system. The final enemy blocks fleeing and instant-kill effects. Complete the battles for 8 base affection each for Arestes, Keira and Shadow, 3 guild clout, and the Phantom Stone, initialized to at least level 1.
At Keira In Love, while owning Phantom Stone, choose Intimate Bonding from her Back Alleys portrait during midnight–4 AM. It costs 60 minutes, repeats once per game day, and adds one Phantom Stone level. It does not consume the stone or award ordinary affection, Energy, player Erotic or NPC intimacy experience. Other NPCs' stone-bond actions have their own allowances.
Keira has no worker recruitment, resort-vacation, apartment-visit, Kupie, pregnancy or Present KTV/Night Club invitation route registered.

Troubleshooting

  • Keira appears in searches but has no portrait: build 10 guild clout and win her first race, then visit midnight–4 AM.
  • No race offered: below 10 guild clout, the initial encounter uses the pickpocket branch.
  • High Ability still loses: the first race retains a separate 15% upset chance; rematches retain 25%, and use Ability minus 30.
  • The Roofroads is missing: check Known, the first race win and midnight–4 AM.
  • Route Intel is missing: finish The Roofroads and reach Known; its daily limit is independent of Talk.
  • Loose Tiles is missing: finish The Good Ledge, reach Close Friend, and return during midnight–4 AM.
  • Night Bazaar is missing: finish Stop Moving, remain at least Close Friend, and start before 2 AM.
  • No Escape Route is missing: finish Night Bazaar and Stop Moving, then reach Crush.
  • A Quiet Favor is missing: check Friend, 150 Valuables, an unfinished scene and that you do not already own Thieves Gloves.
  • The guild now opens at 8 PM but Keira is absent: The Early Watch changes guild travel hours; her portrait still starts at midnight.
  • Phantom Stone Raid is missing: check all three Romantic stages, Shadow's active worker status, both required items and that you do not already own the stone.
  • Intimate Bonding is missing: reach In Love, own Phantom Stone, visit midnight–4 AM, and check its daily allowance.
  • An older save uses different kiss flags: the current load migration reconciles Keira's retired H2H aliases with the canonical conversations; the live route uses the completed H2Hs listed above.
