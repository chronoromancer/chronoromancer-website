Violet——完全走过

概览和时间表

Violet是中世纪时代一位26岁的戏剧女仆,有着良好的对齐。 魅力40、感知45和创意35她特殊的关系技巧是 戏剧艺术.
在招募前,在 酒吧协会,晚上10点每天,在获取后 1号女佣她的盾形肖像消失她出现在 Rose& Thorn Maids的季度, 6–10上午她是一个女佣,而不是一个编号的妓院工人:清洁工和女仆设备使用自己的系统。
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

征聘

  1. 构建 酒吧协会 在会场上首次演唱一首特定歌曲时,会授予一股影响力;重复有10%的机会,或20%的机会与表演大师。Clout与普通的感情是分开的。
  2. 时 25+ 酒吧协会声誉/俱乐部中,返回自动 她自己的一个阶段 Flirty 给出了5个选择点加10个完成点,为15个基数; Caring 给出了14个和Funny13。在本次注册中,没有爱心或明确的事件时间门。
  3. 时 50+酒吧协会声誉/俱乐部,返回为 长期排卵。Caring给出了5个选择点加15个完成点,为20个基数;Flirty给出了19个和Funny18。该事件没有配置前置后置或最小亲情前提。
  4. 完成获得 1号女佣 和赠款 50个额外的直接Violet感情。这是与活动调整的完成/选择点分开的。之后在上午访问女佣的分区。
自动盾牌事件并没有明确的10PM大门,尽管她的盾牌肖像使用了10PM-midnight。 盾牌的名声能解锁它们;普通的守望和清洁行动本身并不能提高盾牌的影响力。

女佣服务

从她的早晨肖像, 女佣服务 要求 近亲+ 这是一次可重复的单回合对话,唯一的回应是 “我爱你的戏剧激情,Violet。”,值15个基点。随着玩家浪漫技巧的学习,共享对话的完成也添加了 25个最后的感情在关系计算之后.
动作的15 点字段不是额外的前置奖励。当前的对话和处理器配置 没有玩家 Erotic 体验或 NPC 亲密体验,且完成后不授予任何特殊设备项目。虽然动作列出三种用途/天,但操作者不检查或增加每日动作计数器,因此目前这不是强制规定的三用途限制。

Violet — Complete Walkthrough

Overview and Schedule

Violet is a26-year-old theatrical maid in the Medieval era, with Good alignment. Her favorite stats are Charm40, Perception45 and Creativity35; her special relationship skill is Dramatic Arts.
Before recruitment, find her at the Bards Guild,10 PM–midnight, every day. After acquiring Maid #1, her guild portrait disappears and she appears in the Rose & Thorn Maids' Quarters,6–10 AM, every day. She is a maid rather than a numbered brothel worker: cleaning progress and maid equipment use their own system.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Recruitment Walkthrough

  1. Build Bards Guild Clout through performances. A first performance of a particular song at a venue awards one clout; repeats have10% chance, or20% with Master Performer. Clout is separate from ordinary affection.
  2. At 25+ Bards Guild reputation/clout, return to the guild for the automatic A Stage of Her Own event. Flirty gives5 choice points plus10 completion, for15 base; Caring gives14 and Funny13. There is no affection or explicit event-hour gate in this registration.
  3. At 50+ Bards Guild reputation/clout, return for The Standing Ovation. Caring gives5 choice points plus15 completion, for20 base; Flirty gives19 and Funny18. The event has no configured prior-backstory or minimum-affection prerequisite.
  4. Completion acquires Maid #1 and grants 50 additional direct Violet affection. This is separate from the event's adjusted completion/choice points. Visit the Maids' Quarters in the morning afterwards.
The automatic guild events have no explicit10 PM gate even though her guild portrait uses10 PM–midnight. Guild reputation is what unlocks them; the ordinary Watch and Clean action does not itself raise guild clout.

Maid Service

From her morning portrait, Maid Service requires Intimate+ and takes30 minutes. It is a repeatable, single-round conversation whose only response is “I love your theatrical passion, Violet.”, worth15 base points. With the player's Romantic skill learned, shared conversation completion also adds 25 final affection after the relationship calculation.
The action's15-point field is not an additional upfront reward. The current conversation and handler configure no player Erotic Experience or NPC Intimacy Experience, and no special equipment item is awarded by completing it. Although the action lists three uses/day, its handler does not check or increment that daily action counter, so this is not currently an enforced three-use limit.

Maid Levels, Cleaning and Equipment

Maid Experience comes from daily cleaning, independently of NPC affection. When maid restoration is positive, acquired maids share a base amount of max(1, restoration divided by acquired-maid count, rounded down); equipped personal and global maid Experience bonuses then apply, with truncation.
