寻找和招募Arachne

  1. 构建 单元格走廊 备注a 单元格2-8中的空单元格 第1组不被视为招聘她的空缺。
  2. 和名字战斗 Arachne 敌人在正常的地牢上 20-28楼常规 钻机 敌人是一个不同的条目,不计为Arachne失败。
  3. 对于一个可靠的 命名的遭遇, 调查隐藏的墙在 22层,瓦片(1,6) 。在她还没有被招募时,它会触发一场 Arachne 战斗。这次特殊遭遇不会以无限模式运行。
  4. 赢 5场名为Arachne的战斗征聘名册始于 第六次胜利而不是第五个
  5. 在成功卷 空置的已建单元格, 居住条件 接受激活她的怪物记录 把她分配到提供细胞和获得 直接十点.
  6. 去拜访她 指定单元格 用于普通关系动作,或者使用怪物管理屏幕进行护理和训练.
胜利后的招募机会是: 10+10×(总胜利−5)+玩家塔明,封顶 100%胜利六号... 20% + 调制进一步胜利使机会各增加10个百分点。 无穷地牢的胜利不会增加柜台或提供招募. 一旦她活动,进一步捕捉处理停止.
如果没有可用的单元格,则 纸张上的房间 建立空格,然后赢得另一场符合资格的战斗,以获得另一个机会。释放她的选择也使她没有被招募。
在战斗中,她是 弱至于火,强至于黑暗,,与 Cocoon, Poison Dart 和 Strike 的技巧组合。为相遇做准备,而不是期待通用的Drider 更容易的统计。

Overview

Arachne is a 29-year-old silver-haired drider and exacting silk weaver. Her current content includes dungeon recruitment, ordinary relationship visits, five corridor encounters and the shared monster care and training system.

Finding and Recruiting Arachne

  1. Build the Cell Corridor and a vacant cell among cells 2–8 through the brothel construction system. Cell 1 is not considered a vacancy for her recruitment.
  2. Fight the named Arachne enemy in the normal dungeon on floors 20–28. The generic Drider enemy is a different entry and does not count as an Arachne defeat.
  3. For a reliable named encounter, investigate the hidden wall at floor 22, tile (1, 6) after building the corridor. It triggers an Arachne battle while she is not already recruited. This special encounter does not run in Infinity mode.
  4. Win five named Arachne battles. Recruitment rolls begin on the sixth victory, rather than the fifth.
  5. On a successful roll with a vacant built cell, the Terms of Residence scene offers recruitment. Accept to activate her monster record, assign her to the offered cell and gain 10 affection directly.
  6. Visit her assigned cell for ordinary relationship actions, or use the monster-management screen for care and training.
The post-victory recruitment chance is 10 + 10 × (total victories − 5) + player Taming, capped at 100%. On victory six this is 20% + Taming. Further victories increase the chance by ten percentage points each. Infinity dungeon victories do not increase the counter or offer recruitment. Once she is active, further capture processing stops.
If no cell is available, the A Room on Paper scene does not recruit her. Build a vacant cell, then win another eligible battle for another opportunity. The choice to release her also leaves her unrecruited.
In battle she is weak to Fire and strong against Dark, with Cocoon, Poison Dart and Strike in her skill set. Prepare for the encounter rather than expecting a generic drider's easier statistics.

Monster Care and Training

Her affection meter and tamed percentage are separate. Recruitment begins her monster record at 35 tamed, with 60 health, 0 stress and 0 experience. Regular care helps maintain access to training.
  • Use the monster-management screen to select Arachne for a daily care session. The session is unavailable while she is resting or after she has already had that day's care visit.
  • Feed consumes 5 food and adds 10 tamed, subject to the normal cap. A failed feeding attempt without enough food can be retried after obtaining food.
  • A care session permits one use of each available category: Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Tool effects differ; inspect the selected tool's effects on tamed, stress, health and experience.
  • Teach and Training need at least 30 tamed. Feed first if her tamed value has fallen below that threshold.
  • Ordinary daily decay is 6 tamed per day after her first care visit. Tamed is normally capped at 100; the Claw tool uses the shared higher-cap behavior. The later relationship visits can also add tamed as described above.

Eight Training Levels

Training unlocks sequentially from level 1 through level 8. Completing a training session advances the level by one, up to 8, and leaves earlier types available. It improves Arachne's monster stats, rather than teaching the player those named skills.

Stat gains are capped at 100. Each session also grants monster experience from one random roll between 1 and player Taming, plus one between 1 and monster Cunning; each upper bound is at least 1. Monster experience can buy another stat level: raising a stat from its current value to the next value costs experience equal to the new value.
Her training scene images are present in the current game assets. There is no separate personal date, illustrated H2H chain or animated relationship route to unlock after these activities.

If Progress Stalls

  • Fight Arachne, not generic Drider enemies, and use normal rather than Infinity dungeon mode.
  • The first possible recruitment roll is the sixth victory.
  • Recruitment needs a built, vacant cell 2–8; the corridor alone does not provide a room.
  • After recruitment, look in her assigned cell. She is not a permanent NPC at the dungeon encounter tile.
  • Corridor scenes need Friend with both monsters, and both must be active.
  • For locked care training, restore 30 tamed and check today's visit/rest status; affection stage does not replace that threshold.
