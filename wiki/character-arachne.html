Overview

Arachne combines ordinary relationship visits with the separate monster-care and training systems. Her affection, Tamed level and monster experience are different forms of progress.

Where to find them

Fight Arachne encounters on normal Dungeon floors 20–28. The ordinary Drider enemy is a different encounter and does not count toward her recruitment. A repeatable encounter is hidden on floor 22 at tile(1,6), after the cell corridor is built and before she joins.

Prepare an already built, vacant cell after reserved cell 1. Once she joins, her portrait follows her assigned cell all day; it is not tied to a fixed cell number.

Route and relationship milestones

Recruitment rolls begin on the sixth victory against Arachne:20% plus your base Taming. Each later victory adds 10 percentage points, up to 100%. Infinity Dungeon victories neither recruit her nor advance this counter.

A successful roll still needs the vacant built cell. A no-room conversation is not recruitment; build space and win again.

Admire Her Weaving, Share Her Quiet and Kiss Arachne each add 5 Tamed as well as their listed base affection. They do not use the daily taming session.

The separate taming menu allows one visit/day, unless she is resting. Feeding costs 5 Food and adds 10 Tamed; teaching and training require at least 30 Tamed. Training advances through eight unlocked types, one training selection per session, with monster-stat and experience rewards. Use the brothel guide for tools, rest and daily care.

Favorite stats are Ability, Intelligence, Taming. Learning Deception improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Do not farm generic Driders or Infinity Dungeon encounters for her recruitment.
  • A room must be constructed and vacant; merely owning a room plan does not provide space.
  • If the taming portrait is grey, check today’s visit and her rest state. Affection does not replace Tamed 30.

概览

Arachne同时使用普通关系探访与独立的魔物照料、训练系统。好感、驯服度和魔物经验是不同的进度。

去哪里找

在普通地下城第20–28层寻找Arachne。名为蛛化精灵的普通敌人是另一种遭遇，不能累计她的招募进度。建好牢房走廊且她尚未入住时，第22层坐标(1,6)有可重复的固定遭遇。

准备保留牢房1之后的一间已建成空牢房。招募后，她全天出现在实际分配的牢房，不固定在某个编号。

路线与关系进展

第六次击败Arachne开始判定招募：概率为20%加玩家基础驯服；之后每胜一次再增加10个百分点，上限100%。无限地下城的胜利既不招募她，也不累计这项进度。

判定成功仍需要已建成空牢房。没有空房的谈话不算招募；先建好空房，再次获胜。

欣赏她的编织、静静陪着她、亲吻Arachne除表中基础好感外，每次还增加5驯服度，不占当天的驯服访问。

独立驯服菜单每天可访问一次，休息期间不可用。喂食消耗5食物、增加10驯服度；教导和训练要求驯服度至少30。训练依次解锁八种类型，每次访问可选一次，增加魔物属性与经验。工具、休息和日常照料见青楼指南。

偏好的属性为能力、智力、驯服。学会欺骗可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 不要用名为蛛化精灵的普通敌人或无限地下城遭遇累计她的招募进度。
  • 房间必须建好且无人入住；仅持有房间图纸不能提供床位。
  • 驯服头像变灰时，检查当天是否已访问以及是否正在休息。好感不能替代30驯服度。
