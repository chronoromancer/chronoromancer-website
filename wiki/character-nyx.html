Overview

Nyx’s route connects the Music Studio, Band Clout and quieter visits to her room. Her two personal conversations and rockband date lead into a repeatable evening visit.

Where to find them

Music Studio: daily 17:00–21:00. Nyx's Room: daily 12:00–17:00 and 21:00–23:00. Enter the room from the studio; the travel action has no affection gate.

Route and relationship milestones

Reach Band Clout 20 and enter the studio for The Guitarist's Warning. At Band Clout 30, the studio conversation Studio Session is available after that introduction, without an added affection-stage requirement. This is useful if the introduction left you below Acknowledged (10), where ordinary room Talk begins.

At Friend (100), also raise Cotton and Talena to Friend (100) and Band Clout to 25 for Long and Loud. That scene opens the once-only Rockband Date: studio, 18:00–21:00, 120 minutes, $80 and 40 Energy.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Band Clout is separate from Nyx’s affection and from Bards Guild Clout.
  • The studio conversation is at the studio, while ordinary Talk is in her room. Check the correct portrait location.
  • For No Encore, complete both Feedback Loop and the rockband date. Raising affection alone does not replace either scene.

概览

Nyx的路线结合音乐工作室、乐队声望和房间来访。两次谈心与摇滚乐队约会之后，还可继续可重复的晚间相处。

去哪里找

音乐工作室：每天17:00–21:00。Nyx的房间：每天12:00–17:00及21:00–23:00。从工作室进入房间，通行本身没有好感要求。

路线与关系进展

乐队声望达到20后进入工作室，触发吉他手的警告。完成介绍且乐队声望达到30后，可在工作室进行录音室时段，没有额外好感阶段要求；如果介绍后尚未达到已遇见（10好感），可先用这次谈心推进，房间里的日常交谈则需达到已遇见。

Nyx、Cotton和Talena均达到朋友（各100好感），乐队声望达到25后，触发又长又响。随后可在工作室18:00–21:00选择一次性的摇滚乐队约会，消耗120分钟、$80和40精力。

卡住时检查什么

  • 乐队声望、Nyx的好感与吟游诗人公会声望是不同进度。
  • 工作室谈心需在工作室发起，日常交谈则在她的房间；先确认所处地点。
  • 不再返场同时要求反馈回路和摇滚乐队约会，单纯提高好感不能代替这两项。
