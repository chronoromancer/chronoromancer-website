概览

Nyx 是当地乐队的黑暗 干燥的吉他手 现为市中心音乐工作室她的路线结合了乐队影响力,房间对话,两个心对心,一个一次性摇滚乐队约会,共享乐队场景和一个可重复的私人夜晚.

Overview

Nyx is the local band's dark, dry-humored lead guitarist at the Present Downtown Music Studio. Her route combines Band clout, room conversations, two Heart-to-Hearts, a one-time Rockband Date, shared band scenes and a repeatable private evening.

Access and Band Clout

Visit the Present Downtown Music Studio. Choose To Nyx's Room to reach her room inside the studio; the travel takes zero minutes. The room starts visible and unlocked, without an affection gate. Use her room portrait noon–5 PM or 9–11 PM, and her studio portrait 5–9 PM.
Raise Band clout to 20 and enter the studio for The Guitarist's Warning, a one-time automatic introduction worth 10 base Nyx affection. This automatic encounter has no additional Nyx affection or clock requirement; it can trigger independently of her studio portrait's hours.
Use scored Practice Drums at the studio to build clout. The action is offered noon–11 PM, costs 25 minutes, $30 and 20 Energy, and bases its rewards on accuracy and difficulty. Aim for 80% or better in scored mode. Practice mode does not award clout. Supporter Chill mode awards one clout at 80%+, and One Button mode halves earned clout, rounded up.
At Band clout 30, after her introduction, Studio Session becomes available from her studio portrait. It has no additional affection-stage gate and is a useful early source of Nyx affection.

Daily Talk

At Acknowledged, use Talk to Nyx in her room. Each conversation takes 20 minutes and gives one base affection, up to five per game day. The two room windows share the same counter: five daytime conversations use that day's allowance for the evening too.
Five successful conversations take 100 minutes and give five base points. Further attempts can lose affection. The studio portrait supplies Studio Session and the Rockband Date rather than a second ordinary Talk menu.

Personal Route in Order

  1. At Band clout 20, enter the Music Studio for The Guitarist's Warning.
  2. Reach Known and enter Nyx's Room for Something to Say. This scene has no extra clock window.
  3. At Friend, after Something to Say, enter her room, 9–11 PM, for Feedback Loop.
  4. Separately, reach Band clout 25 and Friend with Cotton, Talena and Nyx, then enter the studio for Long and Loud.
  5. With Long and Loud completed and Nyx at Friend, choose Rockband Date from her studio portrait, 6–9 PM.
  6. Reach Close Friend; after both Feedback Loop and the Rockband Date, enter her room, 9–11 PM, for No Encore.
  7. At Crush, after No Encore, Bedroom Confession becomes available in her room, 9–11 PM.
  8. At Romantic, after No Encore, Intimate Evening is available in that same room window. It does not require Bedroom Confession's completion.
  9. With Nyx at Romantic, Cotton at Close Friend, and No Encore completed, the shared Cotton and Nyx scene can trigger in Nyx's Room, 9–11 PM.
Studio Session is an additional one-time conversation. Its completion is not a prerequisite for Something to Say, the date, No Encore or the private evening. The Rockband Date's shared band requirement is important: raising Nyx alone does not complete Long and Loud.

Current Route Boundaries and Troubleshooting

Nyx's current content includes her introduction, three additional personal automatic scenes, two Heart-to-Hearts, one Rockband Date, the shared band and Cotton scenes, and her private branching evening. There is no separate ordinary gift menu, worker recruitment, resort-worker, Kupie date route or Nyx pregnancy protocol configured.
  • No ordinary Talk: reach Acknowledged and use her room, not the studio, during her room portrait hours.
  • Introduction missing: raise Band clout to 20 and enter the Music Studio.
  • Studio Session missing: finish the introduction, reach Band clout 30, and use her studio portrait, 5–9 PM.
  • Rockband Date missing: reach Friend with all three band members and clout 25 for Long and Loud, complete that shared scene, then use Nyx's studio portrait, 6–9 PM. The date is one-time.
  • No Encore missing: complete Feedback Loop and Rockband Date, reach Close Friend, and enter Nyx's Room, 9–11 PM.
  • Bedroom Confession missing: reach Crush and complete No Encore. Studio Session is not required.
  • Private evening missing: reach Romantic, complete No Encore, and start from her room portrait, 9–11 PM.
  • Wine gift missing: Wine is a profile preference; the current menus have no Wine-giving action.
  • Cotton and Nyx missing: Nyx must be Romantic, Cotton Close Friend, No Encore complete, and the location Nyx's Room, 9–11 PM.
