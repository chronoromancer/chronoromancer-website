Overview

Abby’s personal route moves between dance practice, daytime outings and one recurring club night. Her lessons, competition reward and personal choreography story have separate requirements.

Where to find them

Visit Dance & Yoga Studio daily 17:00–22:00. At Known 25 she also appears in Downtown Park10:00–13:00 and Food Court (Mall)14:00–17:00.

Her club night starts on days 1,4,7…22:00–24:00 and continues on days 2,5,8…00:00–02:00. The separate Meet Abby invitation is offered at Romantic Retreat Hotel every day 22:00–24:00 from Crush 400; it does not require her club portrait.

Dance Lesson adds 1 Stamina,1 Dance Studio Clout and 4 base affection per lesson, twice/day. The lesson’s Stamina 40 requirement is separate from the competition.

Back in Step is a later shared club visit with Kimi after Room for One More. It costs 45 min/15 Energy, once, while Abby is on her actual club schedule; either participant being pregnant blocks it.

Favorite stats are Stamina, Creativity, Charm. Learning Dance Mastery improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

Abby also appears in the separate KTV and Night Club Invite Date rosters at Crush 400 after Dance Victory Reward. Start at the venue 20:00–23:00:KTV 120 min/$150/40 Energy; Night Club 120 min/$100/30 Energy, with its supporter-access prompt. Each venue has one shared daily invitation allowance across all invited NPCs. Her Kupie’s Love Training entry instead requires ordinary Friend 100, its menu’s content access and 60 claimed minigame achievements; its affection and currencies are separate from ordinary relationship points.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the club scene is missing, check both the calendar remainder and the midnight change of date.
  • At the fair step, check the second heart-to-heart as well as the zoo and studio scenes. More affection alone does not finish these prerequisites.
  • The boardwalk date also needs Diane’s completed boardwalk construction and the content access shown by its lock.
  • Pregnancy can suspend physical dates and dance activities while keeping ordinary conversation available.

概览

Abby的个人路线穿插舞蹈练习、白天出游和轮换的夜店行程。课程、比赛奖励与编舞路线各有条件，不能互相替代。

去哪里找

每天17:00–22:00可在舞蹈与瑜伽工作室找到她。达到已知25后，她还会在10:00–13:00前往市中心公园，14:00–17:00前往美食广场（商场）。

夜店行程从第1、4、7……天22:00持续到次日02:00。独立的见 Abby邀约在每天22:00–24:00于浪漫幽会酒店提供，要求迷恋400；不必等她的夜店头像出现。

舞蹈课每次增加1耐力、1舞蹈教室声望和4基础好感，每天两次。课程要求耐力40，与比赛的解锁条件分开。

替她赢个奖品是凭兑换券领取的独立出游，不是路线中的付费游乐场约会。需朋友100、兑换券×1，从14:00–17:00的美食广场头像开始。

再度合拍是与Kimi的后续夜店活动，要求完成还有一个人的位置；消耗45分钟、15精力，仅一次，须在Abby实际的夜店行程中开始。任一参与者怀孕时不可开始。

可选生育续篇要求2000好感、500NPC亲密经验、Akira的妊娠规程，以及她自己的拍子和再数一拍。完整准备条件见怀孕指南。

偏好的属性为耐力、创造力、魅力。学会舞蹈精通可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

完成舞蹈胜利奖励、达到迷恋400后，Abby也可参加KTV和夜店独立的“邀请约会”。从场所于20:00–23:00开始：KTV为120分钟、$150、40精力；夜店为120分钟、$100、30精力，并需满足其赞助者权限提示。每个场所每天共用一次邀请额度，邀请其他角色也会占用。她在Kupie 的爱之训练中的条目则需普通关系朋友100、菜单所示内容权限，并领取60项小游戏成就；其中好感与货币不等于普通关系点数。

卡住时检查什么

  • 夜店事件不出现时，同时检查日期轮换与午夜后的日期变化。
  • 游乐场环节除动物园、教室事件外，还要求第二次交心；只提高好感不能补齐这些步骤。
  • 木板路约会另需Diane完成木板路建设，并满足锁形提示所示的内容权限。
  • 怀孕可能暂停舞蹈和身体活动类约会，普通交谈仍可进行。
