Abby——完全走过

概览

Abby 她是一位精力充沛的舞蹈导师,在Lucy的Dance& Yoga Studio工作。她已经20岁了,在工作上和在夜总会里都沉迷于跳舞。在无尽的精力和乐趣的取笑下,是一个年轻女性,她既作为导师,又作为榜样。
Abby 是一个 完全成熟的浪漫人物 在"当代"中,有一个专门的情节链,在爱酒店的亲密相遇(一个17回合的耐力挑战),以及游戏后期关系的极端约会.

增益数学(当前计算)

游戏从动作的基点开始,每遇到一个最爱的STAT就增加+1,如果知道,则将运行总量增加一倍 舞蹈大师。可适用的关键项目,科隆,支持者奖金,以及吸引力机会可以修改。 随机范围:下端是总和四舍五入到最接近整数(至少1)的一半,上端是总和加1,在对齐和其他范围修饰符之前. Talk也可以授予额外的基于通信的积分.
例如,巧克力有基础3. 满足所有三个最喜爱的数据 了解Dance Mastery 的预卷总计 12,没有其他的修饰器和不结盟, 6-13点,而不是保证12点。对齐和相关项目可以改变这个范围。
Abby 的对齐是 邪恶。匹配对齐可以改善增益范围;相反的对齐可以减少增益范围。无论最后的卷,最喜爱的STAT和Dance Mastery仍然有用。游戏显示每次动作后的实际收益。

每日限制

留在室内 每天在工作室、公园和商场进行3次正常谈话, 3次俱乐部会谈, 1块巧克力, 2 舞蹈课,以及 4杯饮料超越极限的反复尝试,会逐渐增加失去关系的风险。 一次性故事行动、完成的心对心和完成的日期不会因为第二天开始而变得重复。

舞蹈工作室查询链

这个可选的舞蹈比赛链解锁了存储室的场景,编剧的舞蹈路线有自己的进步,不需要赢得比赛.

第1步:"舞厅对峙"(Auto-Triggers)

触发条件 :
  • 地点:舞蹈与瑜伽工作室(下城)
  • 时间 : 中午12:00 -下午5时
  • 舞蹈工作室派系:10+块. Abby的舞蹈课给出+1;其他工作室活动也授予影响力,因此10个Abby课程不是强制性的.
  • Lucy和Abby在陌生人阶段或以上
  • 旗帜 dance_studio_confrontation_completed 尚未设置
绘图摘要 :
你走进工作室寻找Lucy和Abby争论你的进步。Lucy赞扬你的奉献精神;Abby 嘲笑,坚持跳舞是"鞭子,汗水,态度"——天生的才华,而不是练习。Lucy提议一个 友好赌注:如果你能在舞蹈战中击败Abby,她必须给你"胜利奖赏".
Abby 胡言乱语,接受赌注,承诺"把你放进地板上".
基本奖励: 与Lucy有15个关系,与Abby有15个关系,每个关系都由目前的关系卷处理.
在这个阴谋之后,一个新的行动—— "挑战Abby". - 工作室可以使用。

第2步:挑战Abby(舞蹈迷你游戏)

"挑战Abby"行动启动 舞蹈时间测试 节奏迷你游戏,以完美的/良好的时机击打音符来打分.
  • 胜利阈值 : 14,000+ 分数 设置 challenge_abby_victory 旗帜
  • 行动费用: 45分钟20个能量,
  • 你可以按需要重试小游戏
帮助:
  • 时态( 活动布夫 ) : ×2 分乘数 每一次击球,这是最大的一次助力
  • 卢特(关键项目): 宽度计时窗口 +5ms 组合键 - 完美变为±49ms(从±44ms上),Good变为±93ms(从±88ms上)
在两者活跃的情况下,一万四千的命中率变得非常容易——完美命中率每分200分,Good命中率每分100分.

第3步:"舞蹈胜利奖"版图(由迷你游戏发行)

触发条件 :
  • 地点: 舞蹈和瑜伽工作室
  • 时间:中午12:00-下午5:00
  • 需要标记 : dance_studio_confrontation_completed + challenge_abby_victory
  • 如果两面旗帜都满意,立即触发14,000+
绘图摘要 :
被你的表演所震惊,Abby承认她失去了公平与平坦. Lucy——永远是玩家的媒人——建议在后面的储藏室举办一个非常私人的"糖装奶油派奖".
三种对话选择影响到奖励:
  • 谢曰: "这听起来是一个美妙的奖励, Abby。"
  • 敬礼: "只有你对它感到舒服"(注:+5 与Abby的奖金关系)
  • 谨慎: "你确定这是你想做的吗?"
基本关系奖(在关系卷前): +20 Lucy,+21 Abby.
尊重选择为Abby增加了5个基点;所有关系奖励都使用当前关系卷.

爱情酒店相遇——斯塔米纳挑战

Abby在爱酒店(10PM-12AM,100g)举行会议 17轮渐进式亲密接触 这是Abby独有的,也是游戏中机械性较强的亲密场景之一。

编制清单

  • 能源: 必须保持10以上才能继续——每轮消耗5至15个能量
  • 斯塔米纳统计: 更高更好;检查在困难中扩大

Abby is an energetic dance instructor who works at Lucy's Dance & Yoga Studio in Present Day Downtown. She's 20 years old, bubbly, and absolutely obsessed with dancing — both on the job and out at the nightclub. Beneath the endless energy and playful teasing is a young woman who looks up to Lucy as both a mentor and a role model.
Abby is a fully developed romance character in the Present Era, with a dedicated plot chain, an intimate encounter at the Love Hotel (a 17-round stamina challenge), and an Extreme Date for late-game relationships.

Bonus Math (Current Calculation)

The game starts with the action's base points, adds +1 for each favorite stat met, and doubles that running total if you know Dance Mastery. Applicable key items, Cologne, supporter bonuses, and the Attractiveness chance can modify it. The resulting total is converted into a random range: the lower end is half the total rounded to the nearest integer (at least 1), and the upper end is the total plus 1, before alignment and other range modifiers. Talk may also award extra Communication-based points.
For example, Chocolate has base 3. Meeting all three favorite stats and knowing Dance Mastery produces a pre-roll total of 12. With no other modifiers and Unaligned, that gives a 6–13 point roll, rather than a guaranteed 12 points. Alignment and relevant items can change that range.
Abby's alignment is Evil. Matching alignment can improve the gain range; opposite alignment can reduce it. Favorite stats and Dance Mastery remain useful regardless of the final roll. The game displays the actual gain after each action.

Daily Limits

Stay within 3 normal talks per day across the studio, park, and mall combined, 3 Club Talks, 1 Chocolate, 2 Dance Lessons, and 4 drinks. Repeated attempts beyond a limit carry progressively greater risk of relationship loss. One-time story actions, completed heart-to-hearts, and completed dates do not become repeatable simply because the next day begins.

Trigger Conditions:

  • Dance Studio faction: 10+ clout. Abby's Dance Lesson gives +1; other studio activities also award clout, so ten Abby lessons are not mandatory.

  • Flag dance_studio_confrontation_completed not yet set
Plot Summary:
You walk into the studio to find Lucy and Abby arguing about your progress. Lucy praises your dedication; Abby scoffs, insisting dancing is about "hips, sweat, and attitude" — natural talent, not practice. Lucy proposes a friendly wager: if you can beat Abby in a dance battle, she has to give you a "victory reward."
Abby blusters, accepts the bet, and promises to "grind you into the floorboards."

Step 2: Challenge Abby (Dance Minigame)

The "Challenge Abby" action launches the Dance Timing Test rhythm minigame. Hit the notes with perfect/good timing to rack up score.
  • Victory threshold: 14,000+ score sets the challenge_abby_victory flag
  • Action cost: 45 minutes and 20 energy; available noon–11 PM after Dance Studio Confrontation.
  • You can retry the minigame as needed
Buffs that help:
  • Time Potion (active buff): ×2 score multiplier on every hit — this is the single biggest boost available
  • Lute (key item): Widens timing windows by +5ms — Perfect becomes ±49ms (up from ±44ms) and Good becomes ±93ms (up from ±88ms)
With both active, hitting 14,000 becomes dramatically easier — Perfect hits score 200 each and Good hits score 100 each.

Love Hotel Encounter — The Stamina Challenge

Meeting Abby at the Love Hotel (10PM–12AM, 100g) triggers a 17-round progressive intimate encounter built around stamina management and energy conservation. This is unique to Abby and one of the more mechanically involved intimate scenes in the game.

Preparation Checklist

  • Energy: Must stay above 10 to continue — rounds drain 5–15 energy each
  • Stamina stat: Higher is better; checks scale up in difficulty
  • Optional item: 1× Desire provides an alternate continuation at round 2 and is consumed. Do not rely on it to pass the later Stamina checks; prepare your Stamina and the relevant skills.
  • Skills that unlock shortcut choices (each allows skipping a stamina check):
  • Seduction, Intimate Communication, Defensive Stance, Power Strike, Exploit Weakness, Pain Tolerance, Counterattack, Adrenaline Rush, Endurance Training, Berserker Rage
  • For the optional Beast branch: Need Erotic Experience 3000+ AND Beast stat 20+

How the Stamina Check Works

Each later Stamina continuation is checked against Stamina plus the round modifier, with a 95% maximum success chance. At round 9, Stamina 50 and its −5 modifier give 45% before any separately active bonus. If that continuation is unavailable, use an eligible skill option or end the encounter. Skill shortcuts still pay the round energy cost.

Total Energy Cost (Full Run)

  • Rounds 3–11 (normal path): ~−103 energy total
  • Selecting Meet Abby also costs 40 energy, 30 calm, $100, and 120 minutes, before the round costs.
  • Budget 155+ energy before selecting Meet Abby for a comfortable normal clear with no action-cost reductions. Skill shortcuts bypass Stamina rolls; they do not remove the round energy costs.

Optional Dates and Invitations

Win Her a Prize

At Friend+, select Win Her a Prize from Abby's Mall Food Court actions while she is present (2 PM–5 PM). It costs one Voucher and 120 minutes. The action's own window is noon–6 PM, but it does not make Abby appear outside her regular schedule. It is one-time, is blocked during pregnancy, and is separate from the authored route's cash-paid Fair Date.

KTV and Nightclub Invitations

After Dance Victory Reward and Crush+, Abby can appear in Invite Date at Downtown KTV and the Nightclub. KTV costs $150, 120 minutes, and 40 energy; the Nightclub costs $100, 120 minutes, and 30 energy. Time and Power buffs can halve the invitation's time and energy costs respectively. Both invitations use the game's evening date window (8 PM–11 PM); the Nightclub itself opens at 9 PM. The allowance is once per day at each location, shared with invitations to other NPCs. These are separate from meeting Abby on her rotating club schedule, the choreography route dates, and Extreme Date. The game may block a date during pregnancy, and Nightclub invitations require the current Patreon supporter code.

Christmas Gift

With the Christmas system enabled, reach Close Friend and meet Abby at the Dance/Yoga Studio during her normal studio schedule. Select Give Christmas Gift while carrying one shared Christmas gift. This once-per-NPC scene takes 30 minutes, consumes one gift, and gives 100 affection directly. It includes a four-image reward sequence, rather than a video. Its availability is separate from Dance Victory Reward and the choreography dates.

Unlocked at the Nightclub once Abby is at 1000+ relationship points. Costs 240 minutes and −50 energy. Awards 100 base relationship points.

At the Club

Abby uses entirely separate dialogue (club_talk_response, club_dancing image range) — all themed around dancing, rhythm, and being "in the zone." Different outfit, different energy. The club sees her at her most playful and cocky.

Daily Limit Responses

Talk to her too many times in a day and she gets pouty-but-cute ("I love chatting with you, but even I need breaks sometimes!"). Buy her too many drinks at the club and she starts getting suspicious ("Are you trying to get me drunk or something?").

Tips & Optimal Play

  • Bring Chocolate and meet Abby's favorite-stat thresholds to improve ordinary relationship gains.
  • Learn Dance Mastery at the studio if you can: Ability 20+, $500, 180 minutes, 50 energy and 50 calm, during noon–11 PM. It also gives 5 studio clout.
  • Challenge Abby does not require Stamina 40. That requirement belongs to Abby's Dance Lesson. The competition requires the Confrontation plot; use any available studio clout activities to reach its 10-clout gate.
  • Complete Party Responsibility once at Known+ with Communication 30 for the permanent +1 Ability reward.
  • At Known+, daytime visits let you progress the route and use the shared normal Talk allowance. Changing locations does not reset that allowance.
  • Keep Zoo Date and Fair Date in the authored route order. The Voucher prize date is a separate optional activity and does not substitute for either route date.
  • For the Love Hotel, budget the entry cost as well as round energy costs. High Stamina improves later checks, but the success chance is capped at 95%. Desire is an alternate round-2 choice; skill options can bypass later Stamina checks.
