Lucy — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Lucy owns the Dance & Yoga Studio in Present Downtown. Her route includes studio friendship and training, shared scenes with Abby, three Heart-to-Hearts, four one-time dates, repeatable KTV and Night Club invitations, a private animation, a holiday activity and Kupie's Love Training.
This walkthrough gives the current unlocks, costs and useful choices while leaving her personal scenes and the wider story to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Meet Lucy at the Downtown Dance & Yoga Studio, noon–5 PM. Use Talk up to three times daily across the studio and mall.
  2. Build Dance Studio clout with Dance Training, or with Lucy's Dance Lesson once Known and Stamina 30. Her lesson gives 4 base affection, Ability +1 and clout +1, twice daily.
  3. At clout 10, visit the studio noon–5 PM for Dance Studio Confrontation. If desired, use Challenge Abby and score 14,000 or more to launch the once-only victory reward. This competition is separate from Lucy's H2H progression.
  4. At Known, claim Leadership Balance with Communication 45, give Flowers if paid access is enabled, and complete Chat Behind Studio at clout 25.
  5. At Friend and clout 30, after Behind the Studio, return noon–5 PM for Practice Together. Her Zoo, Fair and Voucher prize dates also open at Friend, using their separate time and access requirements.
  6. At Close Friend and clout 30, complete Stretching Together with paid and recent supporter access. The Boardwalk Sunset Date opens separately after Diane's construction, from Lucy's mall portrait, 5–7 PM on even-numbered days.
  7. Reach Crush and clout 40, own a Sports Car, and choose Drive Her Home at the studio, 4–5 PM, with paid access. Neither Stretching Together nor Practice Together is an extra prerequisite for this H2H.
  8. After Drive Her Home, venue invitations open at Crush, 8–11 PM. At 1,200 ordinary affection, Take Her Home opens at the studio with paid and recent supporter access.
  9. Continue the independent holiday activity and Kupie progression using the requirements below.

Troubleshooting

  • Lucy missing while the studio is open: her portrait is only noon–5 PM, though location activities remain open until 11 PM.
  • Mall portrait missing: visit 5–7 PM on an even-numbered game day.
  • Flowers or Talk refused at the mall: their daily counters are shared with the studio.
  • Lesson unavailable: check Known, Stamina 30, $200 and Energy, and the two daily lessons.
  • Behind the Studio missing: check Known, clout 25 and paid access.
  • Practice Together missing: finish Behind the Studio, reach Friend and clout 30, and visit noon–5 PM.
  • Stretching Together missing: check Close Friend, clout 30, paid and recent supporter access. It does not require Practice Together.
  • Drive Her Home missing: check Crush, clout 40, exact Sports Car key item, paid access and 4–5 PM.
  • Victory reward missing: finish Confrontation, then score 14,000+ in Challenge Abby. Lucy's H2Hs do not replace that victory.
  • Boardwalk date missing: check construction, Close Friend, access, 5–7 PM and an even-numbered day; it starts from the mall.
  • Voucher date absent after 5 PM: the studio portrait ends at 5 PM, before the action's nominal 6 PM end.
  • Invitation roster missing Lucy: reach Crush and finish Drive Her Home, then use Invite Date, 8–11 PM; Night Club needs the latest supporter code.
  • Night Club gift point missing: Chocolate gives 0 there. Use Wine or Flowers for the sixth perfect-score point.
  • Take Her Home missing: check 1,200 ordinary affection, Drive Her Home, paid and recent supporter access, from the studio portrait.
  • Kupie selection missing: check main-game Friend and Kupie Ghost level 5. These are separate friendship systems.
Her current personal route has no configured worker recruitment, resort vacation, generic apartment visit or pregnancy continuation. Intimacy experience remains useful in its own system, but does not create a Lucy pregnancy route.
