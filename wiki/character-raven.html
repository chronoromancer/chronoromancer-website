Raven is a 28-year-old dungeon mistress in the Medieval era. Her recruitment follows the ordinary dungeon's floor 1 and floor 20 narrative miniboss encounters; the second activates worker #7. How these battles work

Player damage uses the choice's listed raw core stat, or the integer average when two stats are listed, plus equipment bonuses. Proficiency adds twice its level; Strength/Ability choices also use their corresponding weapon, at twice its level, or three times with Weapon Mastery. A learned-skill choice gets 1.5× offensive damage before the final round cap. Power adds 1.25× offensive damage before that cap.
Choice effectiveness primarily sets the enemy counterattack multiplier, rather than being a second automatic player-damage multiplier. Explicit defensive multipliers in the tables override the usual excellent 0.5× / good 0.75× / normal 1× / poor 1.25× counter. Shield reduces the counter by 20%, learned Pain Tolerance reduces it by one, then Armor reduces it by one per level, with the current minimum-damage rules. Easy difficulty halves the final positive damage, minimum one.
The five-round structure caps player damage at 25% of enemy maximum Energy in rounds 1–3, at 95% of remaining Energy in round 4, then doubles damage in round 5. Strong stats do not skip the required final narrative round. Choose a useful damage stat with a manageable counterattack rather than assuming one reply guarantees victory for every build.
Avoid each battle's round-2 trap: grabbing the whip on floor 1, or repeating the old finishing move on floor 20. Both have an explicit 2× base counterattack. Defensive options offer reduced counter damage; learned skills require the named skill already learned.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Raven absent after floor 1: recruitment activates on floor 20's completed encounter, not the first battle.
  • Recruitment choice uncertain: worker 7 is activated before the floor-20 optional ending; the ordinary ending recruits her too.
  • Combat damage unexpectedly low early: the five-round narrative caps apply through round four; skills and Power are included before the cap.
  • Large round-2 damage: avoid the explicit 2× counterattack trap in each battle.
  • Home portrait missing: own/access Rose & Thorn, keep worker 7 active and not vacationing, and use 6–11 PM or midnight–3 AM. 11 PM–midnight is closed.
  • Submit rewards differ from the action field: its handler awards 1 base, not the displayed definition's two.
  • Session missing: use the Dungeon room action list with paid access, its own stage, starting hours and Raven home from vacation.
  • Sessions do not double affection with Pain Tolerance: those six rewards are direct, while Talk, Tamer's plot and Get Punished use base affection.
  • Tamer Training missing: check Crush, Communication 20, paid access, home hours and whether its one-time scene completed.
  • Get Punished missing: check 1,000 ordinary affection, paid access and the separate daily video use.
  • Worker training menu lacks Raven: the current visit selector excludes her. Use her authored sessions and animation for their listed worker XP instead.
  • Shared home scene missing: check the named workers, affection, vacations and earlier Esperanza scene; their auto-trigger windows are separate.
  • Resort walk/activity missing: keep Raven active and vacationing, find her current resort portrait in the named room and meet the activity hours/daily allowance.
  • Shared resort encounter missing: every listed worker must vacation together, and each scene uses its own room/window and one-time completion.
