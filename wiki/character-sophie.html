Sophie — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Sophie begins at the hiding spot behind the Medieval Bazaar. Her route builds trust through small acts of kindness, then brings her to the Rose & Thorn as worker #2. Current content includes a Bazaar Date, shared home scenes, worker training, private visits, crafted-gift upgrades, Queenstone bonding and Sirena Isle vacations.
This guide lists practical unlocks and rewards. Late personal revelations are left for the game.

Troubleshooting

  • No bazaar portrait before recruitment: start at the hiding spot's location actions.
  • Ten talks did not unlock the invitation: they build kindness, but not affection. Reach Known through Food/Coin and finish all three kindness crossover scenes while owning the establishment.
  • The hiding spot vanished: rescue completes once; use Sophie's new schedule afterward.
  • Her home portrait is missing: check worker #2 is active, the time window and vacation status.
  • The Master Bedroom scene is missing: check 550 affection, active #2, 9 PM–midnight, home and no earlier completion.
  • Private Moment is missing: its separate requirement is 800 affection at the Worker Rooms portrait, 3–6 PM.
  • A gift upgrade will not appear: check In Love, the exact crafted gift, its listed earlier milestones and the correct portrait. The third includes a late guild milestone.
  • The island portrait shows someone else: select Sophie through the available character-cycle control.
  • Christmas gift is unavailable: use the Master Bedroom location menu, both workers active and both Crush, with Christmas Spirit and paid access.
  • Gallery progress seems unchanged: build NPC intimacy experience through the listed sources, rather than ordinary affection.
