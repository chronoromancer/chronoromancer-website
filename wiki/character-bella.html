概览

Bella 她的路线是从图书馆捐赠开始,通过三个心对心和日期发展,然后继续分到几小时后的访问。她还参与了主要的故事,并有可选的后期家庭续作。本指南在解释大门时没有叙述后来的故事启示。

Bella — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Bella is the Present's book-loving librarian. Her route starts with library donations, develops through three heart-to-hearts and dates, and continues into branching after-hours visits. She also participates in the main story and has an optional late-game family continuation. This guide explains the gates without recounting later story revelations.

Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Visit the library in the daytime and Donate to Library to move beyond Stranger. Talk is not available until Acknowledged.
  2. At Acknowledged, use Talk to Bella and the one-time Talk about Medieval Era heart-to-heart. Its three highest answers are listed below.
  3. At Known and Communication 24, complete Books vs Technology for Perception +1 and 8 direct affection.
  4. At Acquaintance, Give Chocolate once per day. Continue donations while still at Acquaintance or below; they end at Friend.
  5. At Friend, Give Flowers, go on the Zoo Date, and optionally use a Voucher for Win Her a Prize.
  6. At 160 affection, choose Invite to Woods for the six-round Whispering Woods heart-to-heart. Its gate is a point total rather than a named stage, and it does not require the earlier medieval conversation.
  7. At 300 affection, choose Give Ancient Manuscript. Bring one Book, which the action consumes. Complete the six-round conversation, including its optional kiss.
  8. At Close Friend, the Boardwalk Sunset Date is available after Diane builds the boardwalk, with paid and supporter/redeem-code access.
  9. At Crush, find Bella at the library 7 PM–9 PM on an even-numbered day, then select Find Bella after hours. See the branch guide below.
  10. At 700 affection, Private Library Moment unlocks during the same even-day 7 PM–9 PM presence window.
The three ordinary heart-to-hearts complete once. Their listed daily limits do not make completed conversations repeatable. Dates are also one-time. After-hours visits are repeatable, up to three per day, whenever you can meet the presence and resource requirements.

Optional Family Continuation

Bella's pregnancy branch is a deliberate after-hours conversation choice, separate from ordinary intimacy outcomes. To make it available, unlock and enable pregnancy content, complete Akira's Pregnancy Protocol, reach 2000 affection and 500 NPC intimacy experience with Bella, and finish Story Beat 60. She cannot already be pregnant, recovering from delivery, or have a child with the player.
At the opening of an eligible after-hours visit, select “Tell Bella you want to write a new chapter together: a family.” Follow that dedicated sequence to completion. Ordinary intimacy choices do not substitute for it.
Once started, return to the library while Bella is present for its three automatic milestones:
Milestone Availability Base affection
A New Chapter Reveal event when pregnancy enters the early stage, from age 11 days 5
Room to Become Final-stage visit, at age 31 days or later and at least the day after the reveal 7
Something True Delivery event after 30 days in the final stage and at least the day after the final visit 10
The minimum progression is 61 days, but delaying required visits delays later stages. Recovery lasts 8 days. During visible pregnancy, the normal Woods, Zoo, Boardwalk, medieval/manuscript conversations, private video and after-hours visit are blocked; ordinary conversation, Chocolate, Flowers and the stat reward remain pregnancy-safe. The Voucher date is blocked for the whole pregnancy. Complete desired ordinary outings first if you want to see them before this continuation.
Check In takes 30 minutes, grants 5 direct affection and is available once per day during pregnancy and recovery. It becomes Family Check In after recovery. An eligible pending lifecycle scene can launch during the check-in; the required milestones still need completion.

Christmas Activities

  • Read with Bella is a one-time library location action, requiring Christmas content enabled and Friend with Bella. It takes 20 minutes, grants 10 direct affection and one Christmas Token, and consumes no Christmas Gift.
  • Give Christmas Gift requires Christmas content enabled, Close Friend, one Christmas Gift and Bella present at the library. It takes 30 minutes, consumes the Gift and grants 100 direct affection, once. Its scene uses three images. This is separate from Read with Bella.

If Progress Stalls

  • Donate until Acknowledged; Talk does not begin at Stranger.
  • Donations stop at Friend. Chocolate and Flowers are the later repeatable gifts.
  • The Woods gate is 160 affection and the manuscript gate is 300; a named stage alone is not their exact requirement.
  • Bella is present 8 AM–6 PM daily, with 7 PM–9 PM only on even-numbered days. After-hours actions use that actual evening presence.
  • The kiss-path intimacy check uses existing affection plus this visit's earned points against 600. It is a separate gate from entering at Crush.
  • The video action needs 700 affection. Storytelling is her relationship bonus skill; it does not remove action prerequisites.
  • The Boardwalk needs its construction flag and both paid and supporter access. The Zoo and Voucher outings have their own windows.
  • Beat 43 needs Romantic with both Bella and Blinky and Blinky's specified H2H.
  • If the library closes during the main story, continue through Beat 59; the closure is a story gate, not a missed daytime schedule.
  • For the family branch, check 2000 affection, 500 NPC intimacy experience, Beat 60, Akira's protocol, pregnancy unlock and the enabled option. Player Erotic Experience is not a replacement for NPC intimacy experience.
