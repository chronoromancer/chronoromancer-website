概览

Summer是 上城海滩的救生员和游泳教练.她的路线从普通的谈话和游泳课开始,然后打开六个自动海滩场景,两个心对心,四个可选的日期和可重复的付费岛逃逸.

当前内容和解决问题

  • 游泳课没了: 您已经知道游泳; 教训隐藏了一次。

Overview

Summer is the lifeguard and swim instructor at the Present Uptown Beach. Her route starts with ordinary conversation and a Swim lesson, then opens six automatic beach scenes, two heart-to-hearts, four optional dates and the repeatable paid Island Escape.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit 9 AM–5 PM, build Acknowledged and learn Swim. Take Breathing Techniques at Known with Communication 40.
  2. Continue the early main story through Beat 5, then revisit the beach with Swim to see Keeping Your Head Above Water.
  3. Follow the automatic scenes below as affection rises. Use Flowers from Known and Run the two heart-to-hearts when their stages are reached.
  4. Add optional dates for affection. Their gates differ: Win Her a Prize does not require Keeping Your Head Above Water.
  5. At Crush, Keeping Your Head Above Water unlocks Island Escape with paid access. Completing every later personal scene or either heart-to-heart is not required for that action.

Island Escape

At Crush, with Keeping Your Head Above Water completed and paid access, choose Island Escape at Summer's ordinary beach portrait. It costs 120 minutes and 50 Energy, and can run once daily.
The handler awards 50 base affection and permanently adds 1 to maximum Energy on an eligible use. It increases the maximum, rather than refilling current Energy; no separate cumulative cap is configured in this handler. The video has no configured NPC intimacy or player Erotic experience reward.
Island Escape is separate from the one-time Island Swim heart-to-heart. Neither Island Swim nor Clear Water is checked as an extra prerequisite for the repeatable action.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • Summer is missing: her portrait runs 9 AM–5 PM, even though some story events use 8 AM–6 PM.
  • Swimming Lessons is gone: you already know Swim; the lesson hides once learned.
  • Keeping Your Head Above Water does not trigger: both Story Beat 5 and Swim are required, then a beach visit during 9 AM–5 PM.
  • A later beach scene is missing: check the preceding automatic scene and its listed relationship stage.
  • The market or voucher date disappears earlier than expected: the ordinary portrait ends at 5 PM and starts at 9 AM, narrowing the date's own window.
  • Island Escape is unavailable: check Crush, the introductory personal scene, paid access, resources and today's video allowance.
  • An island kiss did not increase a gallery counter: these authored heart-to-hearts have no NPC intimacy metadata. Summer currently has no affection-unlocked NPC intimacy gallery assets or worker recruitment route.
