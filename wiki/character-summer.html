Overview

Summer teaches Swim at City Beach and has daytime personal events, H2Hs and dates. Her initial route event needs Story 5 and Swim, while her portrait and lesson can be used earlier.

Where to find them

Find her at City Beach (Uptown), daily 09:00–17:00. Personal dates start from that portrait; later KTV and Night Club invitations instead start from their venues 20:00–23:00.

PlacePortrait hoursCondition
City Beach (Uptown)09:00–17:00—

Route and relationship milestones

Swimming Lessons needs Acknowledged (10), costs $200, 180 minutes, 60 Energy and 4 Calm, and teaches Swim in one lesson. Continue the six ordered beach events from the introduction through Known (25), Acquaintance (50), Friend (100), Close Friend (200) and Crush (400). These events do not require the two H2Hs first.

On Watch opens at Known and Island Swim at Close Friend after her introduction; neither requires the other H2H. Four direct dates each complete once. After Crush and the sixth personal event, shared KTV and Night Club invitations become available under their venue-wide daily limits.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Strength 35; Stamina 45; Ability 35

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

The Farmers Market date effectively starts 09:00–13:00, and the Voucher date 12:00–17:00, because Summer must be present. Practice ordinary swimming does not advance a missing personal Objective automatically. Island Escape raises max Energy, not Stamina.

概览

Summer 在城市海滩教授游泳，并有白天个人事件、谈心及约会。路线开场要求主线 5 与游泳技能，但普通肖像和课程可以更早使用。

在哪里找到

每天 09:00–17:00 可在城市海滩（上城区）找到她。个人约会从该肖像开始；后期 KTV 与夜店共同邀请则改在对应场所 20:00–23:00 发出。

城市海滩（上城区）09:00–17:00—

路线与关系节点

游泳课要求已遇见（10），消耗 $200、180 分钟、60 精力与 4 镇定，一次课程即可学会游泳。之后按顺序推进六项海滩事件，从开场依次经过已知（25）、相识（50）、朋友（100）、亲密朋友（200）与迷恋（400）；这六项事件不额外要求先完成两段谈心。

开场后，已知开放值守谈心，亲密朋友开放海岛游泳谈心，两者不要求彼此先完成。四项直接约会各自只完成一次。迷恋并完成第六项个人事件后，还可参加 KTV 与夜店共同邀请，次数按场所共用的每日限制计算。

呼吸技巧要求已知与沟通 40，耗时 30 分钟，一次性给予耐力 +1 和 10 直接好感度。海岛出逃要求迷恋、开场完成及完整版权限，消耗 120 分钟与 50 精力；成功使用增加 50 基础好感度，并永久增加精力上限 1，每天一次。

救生员值勤是独立海滩工作，时段 08:00–18:00，要求耐力 15 与游泳，消耗 60 分钟、30 精力与 5 镇定。通用工作加成前的基础收入为耐力 + $5–12，另有 1 基础 Summer 好感度。工作不能代替个人课程或约会。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

力量 35; 耐力 45; 能力 35

偏好技能：耐力训练

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

受 Summer 在场时段限制，农贸市场约会实际只能在 09:00–13:00 开始，兑换券约会则为 12:00–17:00。普通游泳练习不会自动补完缺失的个人目标。海岛出逃增加的是精力上限，不是耐力。
