Overview

Nyxara is encountered deep in the normal dungeon. After the floor-40 challenge, her chamber offers an ongoing personal route, two conversations, a date and the final Grimoire upgrade.

Where to find them

Explore normal dungeon floors 36–39 for four encounters in sequence, first eligible at floors 36, 37, 38 and 39. A later encounter waits for the earlier one; a missing encounter is not guaranteed on the first eligible floor.

After winning the floor-40 challenge, use Enter the Void Chamber from Underground Catacombs. The entrance is 0 minutes and has its own content-access lock. Nyxara’s chamber portrait is then available all day.

Route and relationship milestones

Begin with After the Gate. At Friend (100), return for Rules That Stay Put, then complete Not His Last Gate. Close Friend (200) and that conversation open The Fold She Kept.

Choose The Unnumbered Shore for the once-only 120-minute date. Return at Crush (400) for Come Back With Me, then A Moment of Her Own.

For Tenth Anchor, reach In Love (3,000), complete the second conversation and bring a Grimoire at exactly level 9. The upgrade takes 180 minutes, 120 Energy and 90 Calm, with no added money or Crystal cost. It upgrades the book to level 10 once; its content lock is separate from the chamber entrance.

For the deep-floor combat route and Infinity Dungeon entry, use the linked dungeon references. The personal chamber route does not require completing Infinity floors.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The four introductory encounters are sequential. Explore later eligible normal floors if a step has not appeared; do not skip directly to a later introduction.
  • Winning floor 40 opens the chamber entrance; affection by itself does not open it.
  • If the final upgrade is absent, check the second conversation and the book’s exact level before spending resources elsewhere.

概览

Nyxara的初次相遇发生在普通地下城深处。通过第40层挑战后，可继续密室中的个人路线、两次谈心、一次约会与魔导书最终升级。

去哪里找

在普通地下城第36–39层探索，依次遇到4次事件；最低可出现层数分别为36、37、38、39层。每次都需完成前一次，并不保证刚到最低层就遇到。

通过第40层挑战后，从地下墓穴选择进入虚空密室。进入不耗时间，但入口有独立的内容权限提示；进入后，Nyxara的头像全天可用。

路线与关系进展

先完成关卡之后。朋友（100好感）时回访触发不再变动的规则，再进行不再是他最后的关卡。达到亲密朋友（200好感）并完成这次谈心后，触发她藏起的折叠空间。

随后选择一次性的没有编号的海岸，消耗120分钟。迷恋（400好感）时回访完成陪我再去一次，再进行属于她的一刻。

卡住时检查什么

  • 4次初遇按顺序出现；缺少某一步时，可继续探索达到条件的普通楼层，不能直接跳到后一次介绍。
  • 密室入口由第40层挑战开放，单纯提高好感不能代替。
  • 最终升级未出现时，先核对第二次谈心及魔导书是否恰为9级。
