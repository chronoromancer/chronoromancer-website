概览

Nyxara,则 Void 公主,是一个晚敦格昂字符,其持续路线在 Void 中世纪古代古墓穴下的密室她目前的内容包括四次相继的地牢相遇、四次室内场景、两次心对心、一次岸上日期和 备忘录第10级 升级。
该指南在不透露她的起源或主要故事成果的情况下,提供了准入条件和可玩的步骤.

Overview

Nyxara, the Void Princess, is a late-dungeon character whose continuing route takes place in the Void Chamber beneath the Medieval Ancient Catacombs. Her current content includes four sequential dungeon encounters, four chamber scenes, two Heart-to-Hearts, a shore date and the Grimoire level 10 upgrade.
The guide gives access conditions and playable steps without revealing her origins or major story outcomes.

Floor 40 Battle and Chamber Access

Complete the floor 40 Nyxara boss encounter to unlock the chamber entry. This is a seven-round narrative battle targeting your Energy, with 2,400 enemy Energy and 340 configured attack power before difficulty adjustments. On Easy, the shared system halves enemy Energy and final incoming damage.
Prepare Energy and armor before the battle. Her ordinary empathetic Charm answer in each round has 80% incoming-damage reduction for that choice and a 1.5 damage boost. These options do not require a learned skill. The following cues identify them without quoting the encounter's story revelations:
Round Ordinary Charm answer to choose
1 Greet her by name
2 Tease her back
3 Compliment her fighting
4 The answer beginning “Tell her the truth.”
5 The answer about not having to defend a cage
6 Speak softly through the noise
7 The ordinary Charm answer, rather than the skill-gated Empathy answer
At the end of Round 7, making at least five of these 80%-reduction choices triggers the encounter's special victory condition. You still need to survive the earlier rounds; a low-Energy build can fail before reaching that final check. Other choices can win through ordinary damage if your build is strong enough.
At Round 6, avoid the other Charm answer that suggests replacing her. It has poor effectiveness and double counter damage, without the protective reduction. A skill-gated answer is not automatically stronger than the ordinary Charm choice.
After victory, return to the Ancient Catacombs and choose Enter the Void Chamber. The travel costs zero minutes, and its access action requires both paid-version and Higher Power/supporter access. Inside, Nyxara's portrait is available all day. This dungeon access does not require an unreleased later main-story beat.

Daily Talk and Chamber Route

Talk is available from Stranger, takes 15 minutes, and gives one base affection, up to five times per game day. Five successful conversations take 75 minutes and give five base points. Further attempts can lose affection. She has no ordinary gift-giving action configured.
Follow this chamber route:
  1. Enter for After the Gate. It has no affection prerequisite.
  2. Reach Friend and return for Rules That Stay Put.
  3. Complete Not His Last Gate, the first Heart-to-Heart.
  4. Reach Close Friend and return for The Fold She Kept.
  5. Choose The Unnumbered Shore, her one-time date.
  6. Reach Crush and return for Come Back With Me.
  7. Complete A Moment of Her Own, her second Heart-to-Heart.
  8. At In Love, with a level 9 Grimoire, use Tenth Anchor to upgrade it to level 10.
The chamber scenes and portrait actions have no additional clock window. Affection alone does not replace an earlier scene, date or completed conversation.

The Unnumbered Shore

At Close Friend, after The Fold She Kept, select The Unnumbered Shore from Nyxara's chamber portrait. The outing takes 120 minutes and is one-time, with no additional start-time window, money, Energy, Calm or consumable cost.
Its best result is 19 base affection: fixed 5 plus answer bonuses 4, 5 and 5. Choose Caring or Flirty for the view, Funny for the path, then Caring for the final moment. Exact answer texts appear in the choice table.
The date completes its own milestone and opens Come Back With Me at Crush. It does not itself complete either Heart-to-Heart. No separate player erotic-experience or NPC intimacy-experience reward is configured.

Tenth Anchor: Grimoire Level 10

After A Moment of Her Own, reach In Love and carry a Grimoire at exactly level 9. With paid-version access, select Tenth Anchor from Nyxara's chamber portrait. It takes 180 minutes, 120 Energy and 90 Calm, with no extra money or consumable cost.
The action plays The Tenth Anchor, upgrades the Grimoire from 9 to 10, and gives 12 base affection: fixed 10 plus a two-point answer bonus. All three answers give the same bonus. The upgrade is one-time, and it does not raise lower-level Grimoires directly to 10.
If your Grimoire is below level 9, complete its earlier upgrades first; see Isolde's walkthrough for the preceding level 9 step. Nyxara's second Heart-to-Heart remains necessary even if you already have high affection and the correct equipment. The current load repair also adds her continuing route to older character records while preserving completed progress.

Current Route Boundaries and Troubleshooting

Nyxara's ongoing personal route is the chamber sequence described above. There is no separately configured private apartment visit, repeatable intimate-video action, worker recruitment, resort-worker, Kupie date route or pregnancy protocol for her. The similarly named Nyxara's minion dungeon scenario is a separate encounter and does not complete Nyxara's personal milestones.

  • Chamber entry is missing: complete the floor 40 boss victory, return to Ancient Catacombs, and check paid-version and Higher Power/supporter access.
  • Nyxara's portrait is missing: enter the Void Chamber; she stays there all day.
  • The route stalls at Friend: complete Rules That Stay Put, then Not His Last Gate.
  • The shore date is missing: reach Close Friend, complete The Fold She Kept, and check that the date has not already been completed.
  • Second Heart-to-Heart is missing: reach Crush and finish the shore date, Come Back With Me and the first conversation.
  • Tenth Anchor is missing: reach In Love, finish A Moment of Her Own, equip a Grimoire exactly level 9, and check paid-version access and previous upgrade completion.
