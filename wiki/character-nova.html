Overview

Nova is the scientist at Nova’s Quantum Research Lab in the Future Fringe Zone. Her personal route begins after Story 65 and contains seven automatic scenes, four H2Hs, two dates and later private activities. Daily Talk, Chocolate and a one-time scientific exercise can build affection beforehand.

Where to find them

Her lab portrait is available all day, every day. The automatic personal scenes have their own 08:00–21:59 start window; all seven begin at the lab. Dates, H2Hs and private time are chosen from her portrait rather than by traveling to their narrative venues first.

Complete Story 65, reach Friend (100), and return for The Answer We Make. Continue through the automatic scenes below, interleaving the H2Hs and required dates. The two dates are real prerequisites in this route.

Talk gives 1 base affection, six times daily; Chocolate gives 3 base affection, five times daily. Scientific Method Chaos is a one-time Known (25), Communication 60 exercise for Technology +1 and 12 direct affection.

Private Time with Nova and An Unmeasured Moment each require Romantic (800), The Unproven Result and the supporter access indicated by the lock. Each costs 180 minutes and 50 Energy and has its own once-daily allowance. Private Time is a branching conversation with choice-based rewards; An Unmeasured Moment is the separate animation and gives 50 base affection. Neither is required to finish the earlier personal story.

Build the relevant relationship independently of army or stronghold equipment progression.

Her favorite stats are Intelligence, Charm, Cooking. The learned Alchemy skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. H2Hs and automatic personal scenes complete once; ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Affection is high but no first scene: finish Story 65 and enter the lab between 08:00 and 21:59.
  • Access Revoked needs the first date, not just The Slower Method.
  • The Harder Protocol needs Because I Like It and Off the Network as well as the preceding scene.
  • The last H2H requires the earlier H2Hs and One Variable Left Uncontrolled. The private activities also require Romantic (800).
  • A positive Calm value in the table restores Calm; Blind Calibration instead consumes 5 Calm.
  • Chocolate and ordinary affection do not count as NPC intimacy experience.

概览

Nova是未来时代边缘区“Nova的量子研究实验室”的科学家。个人路线在主线65后开始，包含七场自动事件、四次谈心、两场约会，以及后续私人活动。日常交谈、送巧克力和一次科学练习，可以提前积累好感。

去哪里找

实验室头像全天可见，但七场自动个人事件各自限定08:00–21:59开始，地点都在实验室。约会、谈心和私人相处从她的头像选择，不需要先跑到故事里的约会场所。

完成主线65、达到朋友（100）后，回实验室触发“我们共同作出的答案”。之后按表推进自动事件，穿插谈心与约会。这条路线的两场约会都是实际前置条件。

交谈给1基础好感、每天6次；巧克力给3基础好感、每天5次。“科学方法混乱”是已知（25）、沟通60后的一次性练习，获得科技+1及12直接好感。

“与 Nova 的私密时光”和“不测量的一刻”都需要浪漫感（800）、完成“未经证实的结果”，并满足锁形提示所示的支持者内容权限。各消耗180分钟、50精力，各自每天一次。私人时光是按选择结算奖励的分支对话；后者是独立动画，给50基础好感。两项都不是完成前面个人故事的必需步骤。

军队或要塞装备的成长，不会代替个人关系进展。

她偏好的属性为智力、魅力、烹饪。学会炼金术可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。谈心与个人自动事件只完成一次；普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 好感很高仍没有开场事件：先完成主线65，再于08:00–21:59进入实验室。
  • “访问权限已撤销”需要第一场约会，只完成“较慢的方法”还不够。
  • “更难的方案”除了前一事件，还要求“因为我喜欢”及“脱离网络”。
  • 最后一次谈心要完成前面的谈心和“仅剩一个不受控变量”；私人活动还需要浪漫感（800）。
  • 表中写“恢复”的镇定会增加；“盲校准”则消耗5镇定。
  • 送巧克力增加的普通好感，不会计入NPC亲密经验。
