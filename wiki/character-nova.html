概览

Nova 26岁的科学家 Nova未来弗林格区的量子研究实验室她的个人路线是在 Story 击打 65 每日对话、巧克力和一次性科学活动有助于事先建立感情。
这种穿行方式给出了可玩性的要求和选择,同时让主要的故事启示没有被描述出来.

当前路线边界和解决问题

Nova目前的个人内容包括7个自动场景,4个心对心,2个一次性日期,私人分公司访问和实验室动画,没有Rose& Thorn工人招聘,度假工,Kupie日期路线或Nova怀孕协议配置.
  • Nova 缺席: 访问 Nova的量子研究实验室 她的肖像整天呆在那里

  • 下一个自动场景不见了: 检查上面命令的路径以了解其先前的对话或日期。在现场窗口期间重新进入实验室。

Overview

Nova is the 26-year-old scientist at Nova's Quantum Research Lab in the Future Fringe Zone. Her personal route develops after Story Beat 65 through seven automatic scenes, four Heart-to-Hearts and two dates. Daily conversation, Chocolate and a one-time scientific activity help build affection beforehand.
This walkthrough gives the playable requirements and choices while leaving major story revelations unstated.

Finding Nova and Early Progress

Travel to the Future Fringe Zone and enter Nova's Quantum Research Lab. The location starts visible and unlocked, and her portrait remains there all day. She does not move elsewhere after an affection milestone.
The new-game initial relationship setup adds 10 Nova affection directly. Ordinary talk and Chocolate are available from Stranger, so begin building affection without waiting for her later personal route. The lab also connects the eras through its location-menu travel actions.
Continue the main story normally. Her personal opening, The Answer We Make, specifically needs Story Beat 65 completed. Reaching Friend early does not bypass that story gate. Nova's Communications panel supplies current story and gameplay hints; consult it when uncertain about the next main-story objective.

Personal Route in Order

All portrait activities below start from the lab. Return there 8 AM–10 PM for the automatic scenes; their clock window is narrower than Nova's all-day portrait schedule.
  1. Complete Story Beat 65, reach Friend, and trigger The Answer We Make.
  2. Choose No Useful Purpose, her first Heart-to-Heart.
  3. Return during the automatic-scene window for Peer Review, then The Slower Method.
  4. Complete Unmeasured Afternoon, her first date. Start it 8 AM–4 PM.
  5. Reach Close Friend and return for Access Revoked.
  6. Complete Blind Calibration, then return for Data That Wasn't Ours.
  7. Complete Because I Like It, her second date. Start it 4 PM–10 PM.
  8. Reach Crush and complete Off the Network.
  9. Return for The Harder Protocol, followed by One Variable Left Uncontrolled.
  10. Complete The Unproven Result, the fourth Heart-to-Heart.
  11. At Romantic, with paid-version access, Private Time with Nova and An Unmeasured Moment become available. Their daily counters are separate.
The route's conversations are recorded through the built-in Heart-to-Heart completion system. Current load migration recognizes earlier saved completion aliases and reconciles later progress with the ordered conversation chain.

Current Route Boundaries and Troubleshooting

Nova's current personal content includes seven automatic scenes, four Heart-to-Hearts, two one-time dates, the private branching visit and laboratory animation. No Rose & Thorn worker recruitment, resort-worker, Kupie date route or Nova pregnancy protocol is configured.
  • Nova is absent: visit Nova's Quantum Research Lab in the Future Fringe Zone; her portrait stays there all day.
  • The personal story does not begin: complete Story Beat 65, reach Friend, and enter the lab 8 AM–10 PM.
  • The next automatic scene is missing: check the ordered route above for its preceding conversation or date. Re-enter the lab during the scene window.
  • A date is missing: check its stage, prerequisite scene, exact start window and whether it has already been completed. Start from Nova's lab portrait.
  • Blind Calibration is blocked: complete No Useful Purpose and Access Revoked. Technology 40 is only for its optional answer.
  • Off the Network is blocked: complete Blind Calibration and Because I Like It, and reach Crush.
  • Private activities are missing despite Romantic: finish The Unproven Result and check paid-version access. Affection alone does not complete this conversation.
  • The Lingerie choice is missing: carry one Lingerie before starting the private visit.
  • The Beast extension is missing: reach Beast 35 and take an ordinary position route to its Round 14 choice.
  • Christmas gift is missing: enable Christmas activities, reach Close Friend, hold a wrapped gift, use the lab, and check that Nova has not already received hers.
