Overview

Sashina offers repeatable Mystic training, four ordinary spell lessons, advanced magic and a Grimoire upgrade. Her H2Hs, the nature date and the Final Exam have separate requirements.

Where to find them

Before her later cross-era departure: Ancient Earth Shrine 06:00–15:00, Altar Chamber 15:00–21:00, Home 21:00–24:00 and 00:00–06:00. During the cross-era stay, use Allied Quarters for Talk and Elemental Stone bonding. After the later return Objective, the Medieval schedule resumes; earned army recruitment persists. Follow current Objectives for the late travel sequence.

Route and relationship milestones

Take the basic spell lessons at their individual affection and Mystic thresholds. Mystic Seduction is learned through her H2H at 300 affection and Mystic 65. Master Wizard is a separate skill learned from Gandon; it is not one of Sashina’s own lessons.

The Final Exam starts from Home at Romantic (800), Mystic 90, with both Master Wizard and Mystic Seduction, and costs 160 minutes, 120 Energy and 50 Calm. Its dialogue branches return to a central choice hub; use the finish choice to reach the final rounds. Rewards follow the choices made, rather than a guaranteed 160 affection.

Eighth Star needs In Love (3000) and a Grimoire at exactly level 7, upgrading 7 → 8. The separate late preparation requires Story 55, In Love and 200 Energy; its outcome is left to the game. Both start from the Altar, and family-stage restrictions can temporarily pause them.

Mystical Training raises Mystic by 1 on each of its six positive daily uses. It also awards 2 base affection and 1 Wizard Clout, while really spending its listed Energy and Calm. Elemental Stone bonding requires that exact owned key item and In Love, takes 60 minutes and raises its level by 1 once daily.

Emotional Magic is the separate one-time Mystic +1 lesson with 8 direct affection. Ordinary learned spells and advanced Arcane Surge use their own listed costs. The optional family route is explained in the Pregnancy guide; ordinary safe actions remain available according to its current stage.

Optional reference: NPC intimacy gallery

This character has three current gallery rewards at 100, 250 and 500 total NPC intimacy experience. Open the character details, then Intimacy details, and select an unlocked thumbnail. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience, worker Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate; viewing a reward adds no experience.

Optional reference: Christmas gift

Give Christmas Gift needs Close Friend (200), an unused Christmas gift token and no previous gift to this character. Use her ordinary portrait at the shrine exterior 06:00–15:00. It spends one token and 30 minutes, giving 100 direct affection once. There is no calendar-date requirement or extra ingredient purchase.

On restored saves, the Grimoire upgrade can be offered without its initial full-version access restriction. Its affection, exact equipment level and one-time completion requirements still apply.

Optional late-game navigation: cross-era reinforcements

After Story 94, follow the Arcane Room Objectives with Blinky and Yrsa. Learn Blink and finish Yrsa’s required personal route and H2H. Before crossing, complete Sashina’s four personal H2Hs, nature date, advanced magic, level-8 Grimoire event and late preparation, plus the connected Foxy and Grace recruitment steps and the two Sashimi Pool ritual events. Resolve any unfinished Wizard transformation task. An optional family route adds no requirement if unused; if already started, finish its required visits and recovery before leaving. The quarters Objective recruits the three army members without a new payment. Follow the Army guide for their deployment.

During the optional family route’s showing and postpartum-recovery stages, Talk, Emotional Magic and Elemental Stone bonding remain the safe options. Her spells, strenuous training, H2Hs, dates and private options can temporarily pause. Check the Pregnancy guide for the protocol, 2000 affection, 500 NPC intimacy experience and required Story milestones.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Mystic 50; Intelligence 60; Charm 70

Favorite skill: Master Wizard

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

Daily Talk is shared across her locations. Walk in Nature is a Friend (100) H2H; Nature Walk Date is a different Acquaintance (50) outing at 08:00–14:00. A level-8 Grimoire uses Sabbath’s next upgrade, not Eighth Star again. For late reinforcements, use the current Objectives and the Army guide without skipping the personal H2Hs.

路线与关系节点

基础法术课程各自检查好感度与神秘门槛。神秘诱惑通过她的谈心学习，要求好感度 300 与神秘 65。魔法大师是向 Gandon 学习的另一项技能，不是 Sashina 自己提供的课程。

最终考试从住处开始，要求浪漫感（800）、神秘 90，并同时掌握魔法大师与神秘诱惑；消耗 160 分钟、120 精力及 50 镇定。分支结束后会回到中央选择菜单，要选择结束才能进入最后几轮。好感度取决于实际选择，不保证固定增加 160。

魔法书升级要求恋爱中（3000）且魔法书恰为 7 级，升级至 8 级。另一项后期准备要求主线 55、恋爱中与 200 精力，结果请在游戏中体验。两者从祭坛开始，也可能因孕期阶段限制而暂时暂停。

神秘训练每天前六次正面使用各增加神秘 1，并给予 2 基础好感度及 1 巫师声望；表中的精力与镇定会实际扣除。元素石羁绊要求持有该关键物品并达到恋爱中，每天一次、耗时 60 分钟，使石头等级增加 1。

情感魔法是独立的一次性神秘 +1 课程，另有 8 直接好感度。普通法术与高级奥术涌动各按自身表列消耗执行。可选家庭路线详见孕期系统指南，安全行动会按当前阶段继续提供。

可选查阅：NPC 亲密画廊

该角色目前有三项画廊奖励，分别要求累计 NPC 亲密经验 100、250 与 500。打开角色详情，再进入亲密详情，点击已解锁缩略图查看。普通好感度、玩家情欲经验、员工经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；查看奖励不会增加经验。

可选查阅：圣诞礼物

送圣诞礼物要求亲密朋友（200）、持有未使用的圣诞礼物次数，且此前未向她赠送过。请在普通肖像处使用：远古大地神殿外部，06:00–15:00。消耗 1 次礼物与 30 分钟，一次性给予 100 直接好感度，不要求特定日期或另购场景中提到的材料。

读取存档后，魔法书升级可能不再使用最初的完整版权限限制；好感度、确切装备等级与一次性完成条件仍然有效。

可选后期导航：跨时代增援

主线 94 后，和 Blinky、Yrsa 一起跟随奥术房目标推进；先学会闪现，并完成 Yrsa 指定的个人路线与谈心。跨越前还需完成 Sashina 的四段个人谈心、自然约会、高级魔法、魔法书 8 级事件与后期准备，以及相连的 Foxy、Grace 招募和 Sashimi 的两项水池仪式事件。若有未结束的巫师变形任务，也需先解决。没有开始的可选家庭路线不会新增要求；若已经开始，则须完成指定探访与恢复。居所目标会招募三名军队成员，没有新费用；部署详见军队指南。

可选家庭路线显怀及产后恢复期间，交谈、情感魔法与元素石羁绊仍是安全选项；法术课程、费力训练、谈心、约会与私人行动可能暂时暂停。协议、好感度 2000、NPC 亲密经验 500 及所需主线节点详见孕期指南。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

神秘 50; 智力 60; 魅力 70

偏好技能：魔法大师

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

各地点共用每日交谈次数。自然漫步谈心要求朋友（100）；自然约会是另一项相识（50）活动，开始时段为 08:00–14:00。魔法书已到 8 级后，要去 Sabbath 的下一项升级，不能重复此处。后期增援请结合当前目标与军队指南推进，并完成指定个人谈心。
