Sashina — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Sashina is the ancient sorceress at the Ancient Earth Shrine in Medieval Village Outskirts. She offers repeatable Mystical Training, four one-time heart-to-hearts, four ordinary spell lessons, Mystic Seduction, the advanced Arcane Surge lesson, a separate Nature Walk Date, and the repeatable Sashina's Final Exam home scene.
Her later content includes A Final Preparation, Eighth Star, Elemental Stone bonding, a one-time Christmas gift, three NPC intimacy gallery rewards and an optional family continuation. She also participates in Foxy, Grace and Sashimi's routes. This guide gives the requirements and mechanical rewards while leaving the prophecies, revelations and personal scene outcomes to the game.

Recommended Progression

  1. Meet Sashina at the Shrine and use Talk to build ordinary affection. Use Mystical Training at the Altar Chamber to raise Mystic; its successful uses are limited to six daily and the afternoon portrait window.
  2. At Known, with Communication 30, claim Emotional Magic once at her home. It gives a fixed affection reward and Mystic +1.
  3. Take her ordinary spell lessons as their Mystic and relationship requirements become available. At Known, complete Visions of the Future; at Acquaintance, complete Fears of the Void and take the separate Nature Walk Date.
  4. At Friend, complete the Walk in Nature H2H. At 300 ordinary affection and Mystic 65, start Mystic Seduction from the Shrine exterior; its handler teaches the skill before the H2H begins.
  5. At Romantic and Mystic 60, take the paid Arcane Surge lesson at the Altar Chamber. With Master Wizard, Mystic Seduction and Mystic 90, take the Final Exam at home.
  6. At 1,200 ordinary affection, use Mystical Intimacy at home. Complete the one-time Christmas gift at the Shrine exterior from Close Friend, when a gift token is available.
  7. At In Love, take A Final Preparation after Story Beat 55, and Eighth Star while the grimoire is exactly level 7. If you own an Elemental Stone, add its daily home bonding action.
  8. If the optional family content is unlocked and enabled, finish the current personal / story prerequisites, then use its separate Final Exam branch when ready.
These actions have their own gates. The four ordinary H2Hs do not require one another merely because they are listed in this convenient order.

At In Love, after Story Beat 55 is completed, select A Final Preparation from the Altar portrait, 3–9 PM. It costs 180 minutes and 200 Energy, completes once, and has no additional character-action paid-access flag. Its current pregnancy policy can block it during showing / recovery stages.
It gives 25 completion base, with best choice bonuses 4 +4 +4 +5 +5, for 47 total base affection, and teaches Sashina's Gift at completion. It does not separately require the Final Exam, grimoire level 8 or Arcane Surge.

Troubleshooting

  • Her portrait is missing: check the three-part schedule: exterior 6 AM–3 PM; Altar 3–9 PM; Home 9 PM–6 AM. Moving rooms does not reset the daily Talk allowance.
  • Mystical Training stops raising Mystic: only its six positive daily uses give the stat gain. Extra attempts use exhausted handling. Also budget its 80 minutes / 50 Energy / 40 Calm per use and the portrait window.
  • Master Wizard will not appear as a lesson: Sashina's relationship multiplier uses that skill, but Gandon teaches it. Sashina's Mystic Seduction H2H is a different lesson.
  • The nature scene seems already completed: check the action name. Walk in Nature is a Friend H2H; Nature Walk Date is an Acquaintance date, 8 AM–2 PM, with its own completion flag.
  • The Final Exam keeps returning to the same choices: the branch endings intentionally return to its hub. Select “I think we're finished” to advance to the final rounds.
  • Final Exam is locked despite high Mystic: it requires Romantic, both Master Wizard and Mystic Seduction, Mystic 90, the Home portrait, and the listed resources.
  • Eighth Star is missing: the grimoire must be exactly level 7, with In Love, paid access, and the Altar portrait. Level 8 uses Sabbath's next upgrade route instead.
  • A Final Preparation is missing: complete Story Beat 55, reach In Love, visit the Altar 3–9 PM, and cover 200 Energy / 180 minutes; check temporary family-stage restrictions if applicable.
  • Christmas gift is missing: reach Close Friend, have one gift token, visit her exterior portrait, and ensure her one-time gift has not already been used. There is no calendar-date check.
  • The family branch is missing: verify the content unlock / preference, Akira's protocol, 2,000 affection / 500 NPC intimacy XP, Beat 62 and A Final Preparation. The ordinary Exam's skill and Mystic requirements still apply.
  • A family milestone is delayed: return to her Home portrait at night for the reveal and final visits. Advancing the calendar alone does not complete those milestones or remove their next-day transition checks.
