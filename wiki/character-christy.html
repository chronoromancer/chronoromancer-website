Christy——完全走过

概览

Christy 在当代经营土豆摊 上城农民市场她的路线包括一个有用的马铃薯课,一个打开两个日期的市场追赶,两个H2Hs,一个单独的沃瑟出道,以及一个晚上的棋盘行走日期。这个指南给出了追求的要求,而没有透露后来的市场故事情节的结果。

Christy — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Christy runs the potato stand at the Present-era Farmers Market in Uptown. Her route includes a useful potato lesson, a market catch-up that opens two dates, two H2Hs, a separate Voucher outing, and an evening boardwalk date. This guide gives the quest requirements without revealing the later market storyline's outcome.

Practical Progression

  1. Visit the Farmers Market, 7 AM–5 PM. Talk is available from Stranger, with six uses each day.
  2. Buy Potato Education for $50 and two hours. Complete the lesson to receive Intelligence +1, Cooking +1 and the Sack of Potatoes key item. It is a one-time lesson and needs no starting affection.
  3. Raise Christy to Acquaintance and revisit the market after the lesson for Catching Up at the Market. Choose Flirty for its best +4 base bonus. Completing this automatic scene opens Zoo Date and Explore Market.
  4. Complete both dates, in either order. Also meet Mary Ann at the Pavilion on her own route. Return to the market to trigger Meet Christy's Cousin Mary Ann.
  5. At Known, take Runnin the Market and, with Communication 33, the one-time Relationship Wisdom reward.
  6. At Friend, Wine gifts become available. Win Her a Prize is a separate one-time Voucher date; it does not require the market catch-up or the two earlier dates.
  7. At Close Friend, complete What I've Been Feelin after the first H2H. After Diane's boardwalk construction, Christy also appears there from 5–8 PM. Her Boardwalk Sunset Date additionally requires the paid version and Higher Power/supporter access.
  8. Higher affection opens the two paid-version repeatable video actions listed below. Neither requires completion of her second H2H or all four dates.
The potato lesson awards 10 base affection through its action handler and another 10 base at scene completion. Both awards pass through the relationship calculation separately. Its three student responses do not have different configured choice bonuses. The lesson's completed flag hides it permanently; using the Sack of Potatoes later does not reopen the lesson.
The Sack of Potatoes is used on the King's potato quest. Keep it for that quest; the relevant solution/delivery steps consume it. It is a key item, separate from ordinary cooking ingredients.

If Progress Stalls

  • For Zoo Date or Explore Market, finish Potato Education, reach Acquaintance, and let Catching Up at the Market trigger during market hours.
  • For the Mary Ann introduction, finish both market dates and Mary Ann's Pavilion introduction, then revisit the market.
  • For the second H2H, check Close Friend and completion of the first conversation. A date does not replace its H2H flag.
  • For Win Her a Prize, bring a Voucher, reach Friend, and arrive between noon and 5 PM.
  • For the boardwalk menu, complete Diane's construction and visit from 5–8 PM. For its date, also check Close Friend, paid-version and supporter access.
  • Missing Wine means Christy is below Friend, absent from the market, out of gifts, or at her two-gift daily limit. There is no current Flowers action.
  • The potato lesson and Wisdom reward disappear after completion; the dates and H2Hs also cannot be repeated through their original buttons.
  • Christy has no registered apartment route, worker recruitment or Animal Care lesson in her current character data.
