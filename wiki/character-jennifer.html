概览

Overview

Jennifer is the trainer at City Gym in Present Uptown. Her current route includes Flowers, a one-time fitness reward, four automatic gym encounters, two personal Heart-to-Hearts, five dates, a repeatable private training animation and a Brute Stone bond. This walkthrough gives their requirements and rewards while leaving the personal story outcomes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit City Gym in Uptown during 6 AM–9 PM and use Talk to Jennifer, up to four times per game day. Her schedule applies on both odd and even days.
  2. At Known, give Flowers, up to three per day, and visit during 8 AM–6 PM for Form Check. If you have Communication 35, complete Health & Fitness Tips once for Strength +1 and 15 direct affection.
  3. At Acquaintance, return during 9 AM–6 PM after Form Check for Between Sets.
  4. At Friend, return during 5–8 PM after Between Sets for Old Injury. Also choose The Plateau in the 5–8 PM Gym portrait window. The H2H itself requires Friend, without an extra Old Injury completion gate.
  5. At Friend, take Zoo Date, Fair Date, and the separate Win Her a Prize Voucher date in the windows below. These dates are independent and do not replace one another's completion.
  6. At Close Friend, return during noon–6 PM after Old Injury and The Plateau for Recovery Bet. Choose Rest Day during noon–5 PM after The Plateau. Rest Day does not additionally require Recovery Bet.
  7. If you have built Diane's Boardwalk and unlocked the supporter content, take Boardwalk Daytime Date at Close Friend. At Crush, take Stargazing Date, starting 7–9 PM.
  8. Reach 500 ordinary Jennifer affection and Strength 70 for Private Training Session in her Gym portrait. At In Love, carry the Brute Stone and use its once-per-day bonding action.
Her main automatic story chain is Form Check → Between Sets → Old Injury → Recovery Bet, with The Plateau additionally required for Recovery Bet. Her two H2Hs form a separate The Plateau → Rest Day chain. Dates and the private animation use their own requirements; completing every earlier scene is not an extra gate to them.

It takes 45 minutes and 30 Energy, is available once per game day, and gives 30 base affection plus 70 player Erotic experience. There is no separate money or Calm cost configured.
Time reduces the time cost to 23 minutes, using the game's rounded-up half-cost rule. Power reduces Energy to 15. Her current video data has no additional Jennifer NPC intimacy experience award configured; the player's 70 Erotic experience is a separate reward.
This action checks points and Strength, rather than requiring a completed date or both personal H2Hs. The current handler uses the game's animation access rules.

Brute Stone Bond

Reach In Love, carry the Brute Stone key item, and select the bonding action from Jennifer's Gym portrait. Each bond takes 60 minutes, adds one Brute Stone level, and is available once per game day. The stone remains in your inventory.
The action has no separate money, Energy, ordinary affection or player experience reward configured. It improves a stone you already possess; it does not award the initial Brute Stone.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Jennifer has no apartment invitation, Rose & Thorn worker recruitment, worker resort vacation, Kupie selection or pregnancy route configured. Her current personal route is the Gym story, two H2Hs, five dates, repeatable private training and Brute Stone bond.
  • Jennifer missing: visit City Gym in Present Uptown, 6 AM–9 PM; odd and even game days both work.
  • Form Check or Between Sets missing: meet Known / Acquaintance, visit the correct daytime window and complete the earlier automatic scene.
  • Recovery Bet missing: finish Old Injury and The Plateau, reach Close Friend, and visit during noon–6 PM.
  • Rest Day missing: finish The Plateau, reach Close Friend, and select it at the Gym during noon–5 PM.
  • Voucher date missing after the Fair: these dates are separate; carry one Voucher, reach Friend and use the noon–6 PM Gym window.
  • Boardwalk date blocked: complete Diane's construction, reach Close Friend, and check paid access plus the current supporter redeem-code unlock.
  • Private Training missing: meet both 500 affection and Strength 70; exercise alone or affection alone is insufficient.
  • Brute Stone bond missing: reach In Love, carry the stone and check whether today's bond has already been used.
  • Date or H2H already completed: its ordinary button is one-time; use eligible EXTRAS entries for replay rather than expecting the original progression action to repeat.
