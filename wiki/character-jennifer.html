Overview

Jennifer’s gym route has four automatic scenes, two heart-to-hearts and several optional outings. Her repeatable private training and Brute Stone bonding are separate from that story chain.

Where to find them

Find her at City Gym (Uptown) daily 06:00–21:00. The gym scenes, conversations and dates have narrower start windows below.

Route and relationship milestones

Follow Form Check at Known 25 → Between Sets at Acquaintance 50 → Old Injury at Friend 100. At Friend 100, complete The Plateau; Close Friend 200 then opens Recovery Bet, after the injury scene and that conversation.

Favorite stats are Strength, Stamina, Attractiveness. Learning Adrenaline Rush improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Private Training checks Strength 70 in addition to 500 affection.
  • Recovery Bet needs the injury scene and The Plateau; Rest Day needs The Plateau but not an invented extra date.
  • The Voucher prize outing is separate from the cash-priced Fair date.

概览

Jennifer的健身房路线包含四段自动事件、两次交心及可选出游。可重复私教活动和蛮力之石羁绊与这条剧情链分开。

去哪里找

每天06:00–21:00在城市健身房（上城区）。健身房事件、交心和约会各有更窄的开始时段，见下表。

路线与关系进展

依次完成已知25的动作检查→相识50的组间间隙→朋友100的旧伤。朋友100进行平台期后，亲密朋友200可在旧伤事件及该交心完成后触发恢复之赌。

健康与健身建议要求已知25、沟通35，30分钟，一次性增加1力量与15直接好感。送花10分钟、4基础好感，每天三次，各消耗一件鲜花。

私人训练课要求500好感、力量70，45分钟、30精力、30基础好感，每天一次。完成剧情不能替代力量要求。

偏好的属性为力量、耐力、吸引力。学会肾上腺素爆发可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 私教活动同时检查500好感和70力量。
  • 恢复之赌需要旧伤与平台期；休息日需要首次交心，不额外要求某次约会。
  • 兑换券奖品出游与现金游乐场约会是不同活动。
