Overview

Uhura is the manager and welcoming host of Sirena Isle. Her current walkthrough includes ordinary Talk, the arrival choice in Marina's island boat scene, and seven one-time shared encounters with vacationing workers. She is a permanent island resident; she does not need worker recruitment or a vacation assignment of her own.

Reaching Sirena Isle

Follow Sashimi's An Island in Mind proposal, then complete Marina's A Boat to Sirena Isle project. The boat project requires the proposal flag, costs 120 minutes and $1,000,000, and requires paid access. See Marina's walkthrough and Sashimi's walkthrough for the pool, discovery and proposal prerequisites.
Completion unlocks the vacation system and Travel to Sirena Isle from the Rose & Thorn Pool or Marina's Fishing Hut. Travel takes 45 minutes and requires both paid access and the vacation-content unlock. The entrance's return to the house Pool takes 30 minutes.
In the boat scene's arrival conversation, Caring gives Uhura4 base affection, Flirty3 and Funny2. There is no separate fixed Uhura completion reward on top of that choice. This is a one-time arrival scene, not an extra repeatable Talk action.
Arriving on the island outside 8 AM–9 PM does not guarantee her portrait is available. Return during her normal window to Talk or trigger the entrance encounters.

Talk and Affection

Select Uhura's portrait and choose Talk. It takes 20 minutes, gives 1 actual base affection, and has 50 positive uses per day configured. There is no affection, money, Energy or Calm requirement. The island's opening window and elapsed time limit how many conversations fit into an ordinary day.
The handler records negative affection after the positive allowance is exhausted. Wait until the next game day. Uhura's ordinary Talk counter is separate from the shared Talk counter used by vacationing workers.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.
Reputation Management is her relationship bonus if already learned, rather than a lesson she teaches. Her current NPC menu has no gift, date, heart-to-heart, apartment intimacy, worker training or one-time stat reward. Raising her affection does not reveal an additional authored romance action in this build.

All Seven Shared Entrance Encounters

Each scene triggers automatically once at Sirena Isle Entrance, 8 AM–9 PM, while its named worker is active and currently vacationing. It has no extra affection-stage requirement and no separate action money, time, Energy or Calm cost.

Every scene gives 3 completion base affection to Uhura and the companion NPC. Caring or Flirty adds 5 to each, making 8 base each; Funny adds4 for7 each. These are normal calculated relationship awards, not fixed direct totals.
The worker only needs to satisfy the scene's vacation conditions; that companion does not have to be selected as the random ordinary resort portrait first. Uhura remains the entrance's fixed host.
Completion also rolls the shared Sea Shell reward: 25% chance of one shell, with one retry after a failed roll if a Luck Potion is active. The two-roll overall chance is 43.75%, and the reward remains one shell. Ordinary Talk does not use this activity shell roll. Shells are spent at Shelly's Gift Shop. These encounters do not configure player Erotic or NPC intimacy experience.

Recommended Order and Troubleshooting

  1. Finish the island proposal and Marina's boat project, then unlock paid vacation travel.
  2. Visit Uhura during 8 AM–9 PM and use Talk for ordinary affection.
  3. Send one of the listed active workers on vacation through the establishment's vacation controls.
  4. Visit the entrance during its scene window and choose Caring or Flirty for the highest shared affection reward.
  5. Repeat for the other companions when they are vacationing; each named scene completes once.
  • Travel to Sirena Isle is missing: finish Marina's boat project and check the paid vacation-content unlock.
  • Uhura is missing: visit the island entrance during 8 AM–9 PM.
  • A shared scene will not trigger: the specific worker must be active and currently vacationing, and the scene must not already be completed.
  • I sent Uhura on vacation but nothing happened: she is the island host, not a recruitable worker; send the scene's companion.
  • No Sea Shell appeared: the activity reward is a chance roll, rather than a guaranteed shell.
  • No romance action appears: her current NPC content is Talk, the arrival choice and seven shared encounters; no NPC intimacy gallery assets are present for her.
