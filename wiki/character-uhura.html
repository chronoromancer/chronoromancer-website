Overview

Uhura welcomes visitors at Sirena Isle Entrance. She is an island resident rather than a worker who must be assigned a vacation. Her personal menu offers conversation; the shared entrance encounters depend on which workers are visiting the island.

Where to find Uhura

Complete Sashimi’s island proposal and Marina’s boat project first. The project costs 120 minutes and $1,000,000 with the required content access. The unlocked island travel from the brothel Pool or Fishing Hut takes 45 minutes; returning to the Pool takes 30 minutes.

Uhura’s ordinary entrance portrait is available daily 08:00–21:00. See the resort guide for the full proposal, travel and vacation requirements.

Route and relationship milestones

The boat arrival scene offers a one-time Uhura relationship choice: Caring gives 4 base points, Flirty 3 and Funny 2. It is not a repeatable greeting reward.

Seven shared entrance encounters involve Elara, Willow, Valkyra, Sashimi, Sophie, Brandy or Pixie. Each requires that specific worker to be active and currently vacationing, and starts 08:00–21:00. They complete once; ordinary Uhura affection is not an additional gate.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 20 minutes for 1 base relationship point, with 50 safe uses daily. Its allowance is separate from the shared resort Talk allowance for vacationing workers. The relationship skill in the reference is a bonus when already learned, rather than a lesson Uhura teaches.

Shared entrance scenes also use the resort’s relationship and Sea Shell reward rules. No separate gift, date, heart-to-heart or one-time player-stat action opens simply by raising Uhura’s relationship.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

概览

Uhura在塞壬岛入口迎接来宾。她是岛上居民，不需要先作为员工安排度假。个人菜单提供交谈，入口共同事件则取决于哪些员工正在岛上度假。

出现地点与时间

先完成Sashimi的岛屿提案，再完成Marina的船只工程。工程消耗120分钟、1,000,000财富，并需要相应内容访问权限。开放后可从青楼泳池或钓鱼小屋花45分钟前往岛屿，返回泳池消耗30分钟。

Uhura的普通入口头像每天08:00—21:00出现；完整提案、交通与度假条件见度假村指南。

路线与关系进展

船只抵达事件有一次性的Uhura关系选择：关心给予4点基础关系，调情3点，搞笑2点，并不是可重复领取的欢迎奖励。

七项入口共同事件分别涉及Elara、Willow、Valkyra、Sashimi、Sophie、Brandy或Pixie。对应员工必须已启用且正在度假，于08:00—21:00触发，每项一次，不额外要求Uhura的普通关系阶段。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗20分钟，给予1点基础关系，每日安全额度50次，与度假员工共用的假期交谈额度分开计算。参考栏中的关系加成技能需要事先学会，并不是Uhura提供的教学。

入口共同事件也使用度假系统的关系与贝壳奖励规则。单纯提高Uhura关系不会另行开放送礼、约会、谈心或一次性玩家属性奖励。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。
