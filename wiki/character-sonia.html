Overview

Sonia’s personal route alternates lab visits with three heart-to-hearts. Early testing, agricultural help and optional dates have separate requirements.

Where to find them

Her portrait is at University Research Lab, daily 09:00–16:59. Heart-to-hearts use the narrower windows below.

Route and relationship milestones

After Story 11 and Acquaintance (50), visit for The Unfiled Variable. Continue in order: An Hour Without Data → Outside the Protocol → Failed Calibration → Personal Results → Stay Without a Reason → A Forecast for Two → A Result Worth Keeping → No Control Group.

The boardwalk and farmers-market dates are optional for the personal chain. Each still needs its own preceding conversation; the boardwalk also needs Diane’s construction.

Biobot Transfer opens after the final heart-to-heart at 600 affection: 45 minutes, 40 Energy, 50 base affection, once per day, with content access required. Romantic (800) is not an extra gate.

Sonia Decodes an Encrypted Signal needs Story 4 and Friend (100). Agricultural Research Discussion follows King Graham’s food-supply problem at Known (25) and opens farmers-market travel. Neither replaces the personal chain.

Allow Testing is available only at 0–99 affection, three times per day, and disappears at Friend. Flowers are available from Acquaintance (50), twice per day.

Her favorite stats are Intelligence, Perception, Technology. The learned Nano-Engineering skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Check the preceding heart-to-heart and exact start window, not affection alone.
  • Allow Testing disappearing at 100 affection is normal.
  • Opening farmers-market travel through agriculture does not complete the personal date prerequisites.
  • For Biobot Transfer check the final heart-to-heart, 600 affection, today’s use and access lock.

概览

Sonia的个人路线由实验室到访事件与三次交心对话交替推进。前期测试、农业协助和可选约会各有条件。

去哪里找

她每天09:00—16:59在大学研究实验室出现，交心对话的开始时段更短，详见下表。

路线与关系进展

主线11后达到相识（50），到访触发未归档的变量，之后依次进行：没有数据的一小时 → 规程之外 → 校准失败 → 私人结果 → 无理由地留下 → 两个人的天气预报 → 值得留下的结果 → 没有对照组。

Sonia破译加密信号要求主线4与朋友（100）。农业研究讨论要求先处理King Graham的供粮问题并达到已知（25），完成后开放农贸市场交通。两者都不能代替个人路线。

卡住时检查什么

  • 检查前一次交心对话和准确开始时段，只有好感达标还不够。
  • 100好感后允许测试消失是正常情况。
  • 通过农业研究开放交通后，约会仍需满足自己的个人路线条件。
  • 生物机器人转移需最后一次交心对话、600好感、当天剩余次数及对应权限。
