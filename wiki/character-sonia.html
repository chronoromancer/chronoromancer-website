概览

Sonia是美国科学院的研究科学家。 大学研究实验室 本指南给出了解锁顺序和最佳关系答复,同时对更广泛的故事的发现留下了解释。

建议的进展

  1. 在实验室时间进行访问。 使用Talk和允许测试进行早期的亲情, 并在了解和交流 30 时进行索赔研究讨论。
  2. 通过 Story第11话。返回Acquaintance的实验室开始未归档变量。
  3. 遵循下面的九步个人序列. 自动场景到期后重新进入实验室;从 Sonia 菜单中选择三个心对心.
  4. Acquaintance 之后根据需要使用花卉,可选的日期会给予更多的爱,但不会取代任何路线里程碑.
  5. 没有控制组后, 联系 六百个感情 重复支付Biobot转让行动。

解决问题

  • Sonia 缺席: 9点到5点去实验室
  • 测试已消失 : 它在Acquaintance之后结束;使用Talk和Flowers代替.
  • 花赠比老指南说: 当前的操作者根据阶段授予10或15个基数。
  • 个人路线不启动 : 完成 Story第11话并到达Acquaintance。
  • 下一个自动场景不会触发 : 检查它狭窄的时间窗口 和之前的心脏对心脏,然后重新检查实验室。
  • 一个心对心缺失: 其具体的图案里程碑、舞台和时间窗口都适用;已完成的图案消失。
  • 板行日期缺失 : 建造 Diane 的木板行走并完成 协议之外,有朋友,高功率付费访问及其日间窗口.
  • 市场旅行缺失: 完成农业研究讨论;浪漫约会本身不是旅行解锁.
  • Biobot 传输缺失 : 需要600个感情组和无控制组 再加上付费访问

Overview

Sonia is the research scientist at the University Research Lab in the Present era. Her personal route alternates automatic lab scenes with three selectable heart-to-hearts. This guide gives the unlock order and best relationship replies while leaving the wider story's discoveries unexplained.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit during lab hours. Use Talk and Allow Testing for early affection, and claim Research Discussion when Known and Communication 30 are reached.
  2. Complete the early main-story research sequence through Story Beat 11. Return to the lab at Acquaintance to start The Unfiled Variable.
  3. Follow the nine-step personal sequence below. Re-enter the lab when an automatic scene is due; choose the three heart-to-hearts from Sonia's menu.
  4. Use Flowers as needed after Acquaintance. Optional dates give more affection but do not replace any route milestone.
  5. After No Control Group, reach 600 affection for the repeatable paid Biobot Transfer action.

After No Control Group, 600 affection unlocks this lab action during Sonia's 9 AM–5 PM schedule. It requires the paid version, takes 45 minutes and 40 Energy, and gives 50 base affection once per day through Sonia's video-action handler.
The current scene has no configured NPC intimacy experience or player Erotic experience, and no additional heart-to-heart choices. Its ordinary affection reward is calculated separately from watching the scene. Sonia has no current affection-unlocked portrait gallery entries or worker recruitment route.

Troubleshooting

  • Sonia is absent: visit the lab between 9 AM and 5 PM.
  • Testing disappeared: it ends after Acquaintance; use Talk and Flowers instead.
  • Flowers give more than the old guide says: the current handler awards 10 or 15 base according to stage.
  • The personal route does not start: complete Story Beat 11 and reach Acquaintance.
  • The next automatic scene does not trigger: check its narrower time window and the preceding heart-to-heart, then revisit the lab.
  • A heart-to-heart is missing: its specific plot milestone, stage and time window all apply; completed ones disappear.
  • Boardwalk date is missing: build Diane's boardwalk and finish Outside the Protocol, with Friend, higher-power paid access and its daytime window.
  • Market travel is missing: finish Agricultural Research Discussion; the romantic date alone is not the travel unlock.
  • Biobot Transfer is missing: both 600 affection and No Control Group are required, plus paid access.
