Overview

Marcus is found at the Future’s transportation hub. His portrait has no personal Talk, gifts, training, dates or heart-to-hearts. His practical route is a shared Syndicate mission rather than a personal relationship ladder.

Where to find Marcus

Go to Future → Prime Sector → Prime Transportation Nexus Hub, daily 07:00–17:00. His portrait can appear before the mission is ready.

LocationPortrait scheduleCondition
Prime Transportation Nexus Hub07:00–17:00

Windows refer to the time you start an action; the ending time is excluded.

Route and relationship milestones

Start at Syndicate Gates. Complete The Gates of Chaos and The Gatekeeper, then the introductions at The Parlor, The Big Top, The Funhouse and Fortune Teller’s Booth. The Funhouse introduction follows The New Variable at the Parlor.

Return to Fortune Teller’s Booth for Collecting Dues, then visit Robot Bar for Collecting Dues - The Bar. Return to the booth for New Blood, then visit Marcus’s transport hub 07:00–17:00 for Taming the Rookie. All are once-only automatic encounters; Marcus needs no affection threshold.

Activities and rewards

Finishing Taming the Rookie grants Rogue +1 once. It also gives Kitty and Zodiac 3 base relationship points each, with up to 3 extra Kitty points from the closing choice. New Blood gives the same fixed 3 points to each, with up to 3 extra Zodiac points.

For the follow-up, finish Welcome to the Circus and return to Motley’s Lair for The Boss Takes Notice. The separate Cat’s Eyes surveillance encounter is not required for Marcus’s mission.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

An empty personal menu is expected. If the mission does not trigger, check New Blood and the 07:00–17:00 start window. If another automatic encounter plays first, finish it and enter again. The mission does not add Marcus to the player’s staff.

概览

Marcus出现在未来的交通枢纽。他的头像没有个人交谈、送礼、训练、约会或谈心菜单；相关进展属于辛迪加共同任务，不是独立的关系培养路线。

出现地点与时间

前往未来 → 核心区 → 核心区交通枢纽，每天07:00—17:00。他的头像会先于相关任务开放，这是正常情况。

地点头像出现时间条件
核心区交通枢纽07:00–17:00

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

先到辛迪加大门完成混沌之门和守门人，再分别完成会客厅、大帐篷、欢乐屋与占卜摊位的初见。欢乐屋初见需要先完成会客厅的新变量。

返回占卜摊位推进收取保护费，再到机器人酒吧继续酒吧阶段。随后回占卜摊位完成新血，最后于07:00—17:00到交通枢纽触发涉及Marcus的任务。以上都是一次性自动事件，不要求Marcus的关系阶段。

行动与奖励

完成涉及Marcus的任务可一次性获得盗贼+1，同时给予Kitty和Zodiac各3点基础关系，结尾选择最多再给Kitty3点。新血同样固定给予两人各3点，选择最多再给Zodiac3点。

后续请先完成欢迎来到马戏团，再回Motley的巢穴触发老大注意到了你。另一个独立的监视事件并不是Marcus任务的前置。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

个人菜单为空是正常设计。任务没有触发时，请检查新血是否完成，以及是否在07:00—17:00进入。若其他自动事件优先播放，完成后再次进入即可。这项任务不会将Marcus加入玩家的员工名单。
