Overview

Pepper is the enthusiastic bunny-costumed worker 29 at the Rose & Thorn. Her current content includes three home locations, daily Talk and gifts, two bedroom movies, eleven home/shared encounters, worker visits and eight training options, two crafted upgrades, a resort sunset walk, daily resort activities and twenty-two shared resort encounters.
This guide covers how to access and complete her playable content while leaving her backstory and the scenes' personal revelations to the game.

Recruitment and Recommended Route

Redeem the game's valid Easter promotional code through its code-redemption system. Its Pepper reward activates worker 29. Her portrait checks the worker's active state; the code's Easter theme does not add a current-date or calendar-holiday check to her ordinary schedule. This route does not recruit her from a random dungeon encounter or a normal worker purchase.
  1. With Pepper active, enter the Rose & Thorn courtyard for An Easter Offering. Follow her three daily home locations for Talk and, at Acquaintance, gifts.
  2. At Acknowledged, visit Elara's Office for The New Hire. At Known, visit the Bar, Kitchen and the appropriate companion rooms for her early shared encounters.
  3. Complete Cooking Lessons, The Pet Bunny and Checkup, and progress Vixen's VIP introduction to unlock A Gentle Soul.
  4. Use Visit Worker for worker 29 during her assigned shift and train through levels 1–8. Daily Encourage Pepper is a separate home action.
  5. Send her to Sirena Isle and complete Sunset Walk with Pepper at the Beach 5–8 PM. Explore her daily resort activities and shared encounters, then return her home.
  6. At In Love, 3,200 ordinary affection, give the exact crafted gifts from her Master Bedroom portrait, 5–9 AM, for her doggie and oral training upgrades. Their prerequisites are listed separately below.
The two home bedroom movies open at Known and do not require those crafted upgrades or the resort walk.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Pepper absent everywhere: check that the Easter redemption activated worker 29, that she remains active, and that she is home from vacation.
  • Wrong portrait location: follow Bedroom 5–9 AM, Bar 9 AM–3 PM, West Hallway 3–10 PM, separately from the assigned work shift.
  • A companion scene missing: check its own stage, active workers, vacation state and hours. A companion's usual portrait shift is not the automatic scene's clock.
  • Gift limit persists after moving rooms: Chocolate and Flowers each share their daily count across all home locations. Only the three home Talk counters are separate.
  • Bedroom movie missing: check Known, morning Bedroom presence, shared movie access and that movie's own daily use.
  • Doggie upgrade missing: finish its five listed encounters, including VIP recruitment; bring Prismatic Spell Brooch, reach In Love and use the morning Bedroom portrait.
  • Oral upgrade missing: complete Checkup and the resort Sunset Walk, return her home and carry Void-Kissed Charm at In Love.
  • Resort walk missing: check vacationing state, actual Beach presence, 5–8 PM and today's walk allowance.
Her current content does not add an ordinary apartment invitation, pregnancy continuation, Kupie album, personal Christmas scene or maid cleaning progression. Her promotional recruitment, home encounters, worker training and resort activities use the distinct systems above.
