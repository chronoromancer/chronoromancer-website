概览

Swish是 Chronos篮球队11号时, 现任篮球法庭她的路线包括5个自动法庭场景、常规演习、2个心对心、《法庭愿景》和1个 " Boardwalk Date " ,该指南提供了解锁器和最佳答复,但没有重述她的个人场景。

Overview

Swish is number 11 on the Chronos basketball team, found at the Present Basketball Court. Her route includes five automatic court scenes, regular drills, two heart-to-hearts, Court Vision and a Boardwalk Date. This guide gives the unlocks and best replies without retelling her personal scenes.

Detail Value
Age / era / alignment 20 / Present / Neutral
Location and schedule Basketball Court, 10 AM–6 PM every day
Favorite stats Ability 40; Stamina 45; Perception 40
Special relationship skill Tactical Awareness
Favorite gift None configured
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.
Court Vision's 8 affection is a fixed direct reward. Tactical Awareness is a bonus from a skill you already know, rather than a lesson she teaches.

Recommended Progression and Automatic Scenes

Visit the court 10 AM–6 PM to trigger First Drill. Use Talk to reach Known, then Run Drills and Court Read for more affection. Continue returning as stages rise for the remaining scenes. At Close Friend, build Diane's boardwalk for the optional date. At Crush, Open Look requires Court Read completed.
Each automatic scene below runs once at the court 10 AM–6 PM, and each after First Drill checks the preceding scene. No separate action time, money or Energy cost is configured.

Boardwalk Date

At Close Friend, with Diane's boardwalk built, invite Swish from her Basketball Court portrait. The date action's own window is 5–9 PM, but Swish's portrait ends at 6 PM, making the current usable invitation window 5–6 PM.
The date takes 120 minutes, 35 Energy and $80, occurs once, and has no explicit paid-version or higher-power gate. It does not require a completed heart-to-heart.
For every question, Caring and Flirty tie at 5 base; Funny gives 4. The three best replies plus 8 completion base total 23 base affection.

The date does not configure NPC intimacy or player Erotic experience. Its plot rewards use the normal relationship calculation without the heart-to-heart Romantic completion bonus.

Troubleshooting and Current Content

  • Swish is absent: visit the Basketball Court between 10 AM and 6 PM.
  • Court Read is missing: reach Known and complete The Shot Chart, which follows First Drill.
  • Open Look is missing: reach Crush, finish Court Read and visit 4–6 PM.
  • Boardwalk Date is missing in the evening: invite her before her 6 PM portrait departure; the action's nominal 9 PM ending does not extend that schedule.
  • Court Vision is missing: reach Friend and Ability 35; it hides after completion.
  • Drills did not raise a stat: they award affection; Court Vision gives the one-time Perception increase.
  • The route seems finished: the current authored content is the five court scenes, two heart-to-hearts, date and actions above. No worker recruitment, repeatable video, apartment route or NPC intimacy gallery assets are configured.
