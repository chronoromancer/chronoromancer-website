Overview

Anthrax is a 26-year-old competitive assassin who becomes the fortress's close-combat instructor. Her current content includes a twelve-scene recruitment and training story, three heart-to-hearts, two optional dates, skill lessons and stat training. Her professional route and later romance have separate requirements.

Recruitment and Professional Walkthrough

  1. After Beat 74, return to Operations Center, 8 AM–10 PM, for Unscheduled Instructor.
  2. Build affection with Talk and Chocolate at the Underground Modification Clinic. At Known, return there for Three Seconds.
  3. Reach Acquaintance and return to the clinic for The Wrong Measure.
  4. Reach Friend, then return to Operations Center for Report for Training. This recruits Anthrax and unlocks the hall connection.
  5. Visit Training Hall, 8 AM–6 PM, for Everyone Starts Somewhere. This opens her daytime training actions and first evening heart-to-heart.
  6. Return to the hall during daytime for A Different Answer at Friend.
  7. Reach Close Friend and continue the daytime hall scenes in order: The Familiar Counter → Dismissed → Pass It On → Everyone Comes Back.
  8. After Everyone Comes Back, manually claim Advanced Footwork from the hall during 8 AM–5 PM. It is an independent one-time action: 60 minutes, 20 energy, +1 Perception and 8 base relationship points. Finishing the professional story does not automatically grant this reward.
The professional storyline reaches its ending at Close Friend. Its progression does not require dates, sparring counts, or completion of the romance heart-to-hearts.

If Progress Stalls

  • Start at Operations Center after Beat 74; her normal clinic appearance needs the introduction completed.
  • After recruitment, use the Training Hall, not the clinic.
  • Daytime story scenes use 8 AM–6 PM; the two romantic automatic plots use 6 PM–10 PM; H2Hs end their start window at 9 PM.
  • Training needs the first class, Everyone Starts Somewhere, even after the hall is unlocked.
  • Advanced Footwork must be selected separately after Everyone Comes Back.
  • Chosen Company needs Romantic and No Lesson Tonight; completing optional dates alone cannot replace the three H2Hs.
