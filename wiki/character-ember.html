概览

Ember 她目前的内容包括9个自动家庭场景、3个一次性家庭心脏对心脏、2个日期、工人参观和培训、2个精心制作的礼物以及Sirena Isle度假胜地活动。走过的路提供了战斗选择和进步要求,而没有重述相遇故事的转折或迟到的主要结局。

征聘和出入巢穴

  1. 到达 地牢地板 50 并赢得 Ember 的 十轮 叙事相遇,她从 3 200个敌人能量 和 420攻击力她的反击针对你 卫生.
  2. 胜利激活 Ember作为工人 25 两个分支都给予 +2 仓库。可选的额外接触分支要求 野兽60,记录 30 NPC口服/按摩类的亲密经验和赠款 +2 野兽 当“野兽”系统启用时,它不需要用于招募。
  3. 构建 菜单 如果它还没有可用。 Gastoff 的 美纳杰里委员会 需求 Rose& Thorn 名声 9,则 修建庭院, $2,000,000,以及 50种史诗材料,50种史诗原料和50种史诗织物.。建筑需要 200分钟这座建筑大门与赢得地牢之交是分开的
  4. 旅行经过 Ember的巢穴进入Lair可使用,招聘了25名工人;进入Lair的费用 0分钟.
  5. 开始 与 Ember 交谈 并遵循下面的家谱链 。在 Acquaintance 添加珠宝和日常 Sparring 。当每个场景链暂停时完成相关的 H2H 。

工人访问和培训

招聘使Ember的工人路线成为可能。 指定班次,初始时 上午9时至3时她每天的Lair肖像不能绕过工人轮班的资格。
一个工人访问是 每天一次 通常费用 60分钟,60个能量,40个平静活动 时间、权力和焦点 将各自费用降低到 30分钟,30个能量,20个平静在访问中,每个菜单类别都有自己的会议津贴;培训是一次选择,没有额外的资源支付,对话、赞美和礼物也可以在同一次访问中使用。

Overview

Ember has a dungeon recruitment route followed by a complete relationship route in her private Menagerie lair. Her current content includes nine automatic home scenes, three one-time home heart-to-hearts, two dates, worker visits and training, two crafted gifts, and Sirena Isle resort activities. The walkthrough gives battle choices and progression requirements without retelling the encounter's story turns or late main-story outcomes.

Recruitment and Lair Access

  1. Reach dungeon floor 50 and win Ember's ten-round narrative encounter. She starts with 3,200 enemy energy and 420 attack power; her counterattacks target your health.
  2. Winning activates Ember as worker 25 on either post-battle choice. Both branches grant +2 Stamina. The optional extra encounter branch requires Beast 60, records 30 NPC intimacy experience across its oral/masturbation categories, and grants +2 Beast when the Beast system is enabled. It is not required for recruitment.
  3. Build the Menagerie if it is not already available. Gastoff's Commission Menagerie needs Rose & Thorn reputation 9, the Courtyard built, $2,000,000, and 50 epic materials, 50 epic ingredients and 50 epic fabrics. Construction takes 200 minutes. This construction gate is separate from winning the dungeon encounter.
  4. Travel through the Courtyard → Menagerie → Ember's Lair. Enter Lair becomes available with worker 25 recruited; travel into it costs 0 minutes.
  5. Begin Talk to Ember and follow the home-scene chain below. Add Jewelry at Known and daily Sparring at Acquaintance. Complete the relevant H2H when each scene chain pauses.

Worker Visits and Training

Recruitment enables Ember's worker route. Use the worker-room visit system during her assigned shift, initially 9 PM–3 AM, while she is available at home. Her all-day Lair portrait does not bypass worker-shift eligibility.
A worker visit is once per day and normally costs 60 minutes, 60 energy and 40 calm. Active Time, Power and Focus buffs reduce their respective costs to 30 minutes, 30 energy and 20 calm. Inside the visit, each menu category has its own session allowance; training is one selection with no additional resource payment. Dialogue, praise and a gift can also be used within the same visit.
The worker gift menu has its own preferences: Gemstone Heels are Ember's favorite, and Chocolate is her least favorite. This is separate from her ordinary Give Jewelry action. Favorite worker gifts award 15 worker-relationship points, 8 base NPC affection and 4–8 Loyalty; ordinary gifts give 8 / 5 / 2–6, while Chocolate gives 3 / 3 / 1–3. Giving a gift consumes one item and uses the session's gift allowance. NPC affection uses the shared relationship calculation.

If Progress Stalls

  • Win the floor-50 fight, rather than merely surviving ten rounds. Either post-battle choice recruits her.
  • Build the Menagerie and travel into Ember's Lair. A recruited worker alone does not unlock the Courtyard's unbuilt Menagerie entrance.
  • Visit the Lair again after each scene, and complete Worth the Climb and Nothing Left to Guard where the chain requires them.
  • Furnace Discipline requires Communication 90. Her favorite Strength, Stamina and Charm thresholds affect affection calculations instead.
  • The dates have separate H2H prerequisites and start windows; neither date is required to unlock the next home H2H.
  • For worker visits, check the assigned shift and daily visit allowance. The ordinary Jewelry action and worker gift menu have different preferences and limits.
  • For crafted gifts, reach In Love, finish the final home scene and H2H, and bring the correct crafted item.
  • Send both characters together for resort group scenes, then bring the required workers home for main-story milestones.
