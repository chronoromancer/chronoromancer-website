Master Time. Build Relationships. Save Existence.

Chronoromancer is a time-traveling adult sandbox RPG developed by Chronoromancer Studio using Ren'Py. An AI named VOID wants to erase all of existence — and the only person who can stop it is you: an unemployed dude with no skills, no plan, and no idea what's happening. Travel between three eras, build relationships, explore dungeons, and maybe save the universe along the way.

Current Version: V0.9.7b (MALICE) — Available on Windows, Mac, Linux & Android
