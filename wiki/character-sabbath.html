概览

Sabbath 这是 240岁的女巫 在隐藏的小屋里 中世纪的Whipering Woods她目前的内容包括日常的爱行动、Alchemy和Ritual Mastery课程, 四个心脏对心脏,则 月亮广场日期三次共同的会面,Gandon的差事,两张精心制作的食谱, 第九届神秘诱惑 和象征性的圣诞礼物
这种走过会给出要求,选择和机械奖励,个人启示和场景结果留给游戏.

找到隐藏的标记

前往 中世纪的Whipering Woods. 进入巫婆之营 仅以 有效的神话45和有效的看法45设备和其他修饰器都用于这两个能见度检查。 零配置时间成本.
她的普通肖像和手动动作都有 奇数游戏日下午六点半。小屋入口本身没有奇特的日间或晚间限制。自动场景有自己的窗户;下面的白天共享场景不需要她的晚间小屋肖像。
茅屋进入门槛 与她最喜欢的奖金分开 神秘55和观念55仅提高一个统计数据不能显示入口。

Overview

Sabbath is the 240-year-old witch at the hidden hut in the Medieval Whispering Woods. Her current content includes daily affection actions, Alchemy and Ritual Mastery lessons, four heart-to-hearts, the Moonlit Bazaar Date, three shared encounters, Gandon's errands, two crafting recipes, Ninth Hex, Mystic Seduction, and a token-funded Christmas gift scene.
This walkthrough gives the requirements, choices and mechanical rewards. Personal revelations and scene outcomes are left to the game.

Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Deception improves her affection calculation when learned; Sabbath has no Deception teaching action. Her lessons teach Alchemy and Ritual Mastery instead. Rewards explicitly called fixed affection below are added directly and do not use the ordinary base-point multiplier.

Find the Hidden Hut

Travel to the Medieval Whispering Woods. Enter Witch's Hut appears only with both effective Mystic 45 and effective Perception 45. Equipment and other modifiers count toward these two visibility checks. Entering and returning to the woods have zero configured time cost.
Her ordinary portrait and manual actions are available 6 PM–midnight on odd-numbered game days. The hut entrance itself has no odd-day or evening restriction. Automatic scenes have their own windows; the daytime shared scenes below do not require her evening hut portrait.
The hut access thresholds are separate from her favorite-stat bonuses at Mystic 55 and Perception 55. Raising only one stat does not reveal the entrance.

Suggested Progression

  1. Reach Mystic 45 and Perception 45, then visit the hut on an odd game day after 6 PM.
  2. Use Talk to Sabbath. At Acknowledged, add Give Epic Ingredient; at Friend, add Give Lingerie. Stop each action at its positive daily limit.
  3. At Known, learn Alchemy if needed, complete The Black Cabinet, and claim Forbidden Knowledge once Communication reaches 60.
  4. At Acquaintance, complete Cabinet Lesson after The Black Cabinet. Alchemy and Mystic 60 unlock better optional replies.
  5. At Friend, complete Price of Pretty Things, take the Moonlit Bazaar Date, and learn Ritual Mastery if needed. The bazaar date is independent of the cabinet chain.
  6. At Close Friend, complete Witch-Marked after Cabinet Lesson and Price of Pretty Things. Also visit Amelia, the Whispering Woods and the temple for the shared encounters.
  7. At Crush, complete Closer after Witch-Marked and craft The Second Pulse when its ingredients are ready. The craft does not require Closer completed.
  8. At 500 ordinary affection, Seduce Sabbath supplies the Black Panties key item for Gandon's second errand. This encounter is independent of the cabinet chain.
  9. At In Love, bring a level-8 Grimoire for Ninth Hex. A higher or lower level does not qualify.
  10. At Close Friend, a Christmas gift token opens the separate Give Christmas Gift scene once. The token substitutes for assembling its narrated gift ingredients.
Affection stages and completed scenes are separate checks. Gift grinding cannot replace Cabinet Lesson or the earlier Witch-Marked conversation.

The scene gives the permanent The Second Pulse key item, multiplying maximum Health by 1.10 at its step of the Health calculation, rounded down to a whole number, and fully healing to the new maximum. Other maximum-Health equipment and bonuses remain relevant. It is one-time and requires neither the cabinet chain nor a paid-version flag.
Completion awards 25 base; “I like giving you things that make your eyes light up.” adds 5, for 30 total base. Caring adds 4, Funny 3. There is no extra money, energy or calm payment, nor NPC intimacy or player-experience award, configured for the craft.

Ninth Hex: Grimoire Level 9

At In Love, bring a Grimoire at exactly level 8 — Starforged Grimoire and select Ninth Hex. The one-time lesson costs 180 minutes, 110 energy and 80 calm, with no money or item ingredients configured.
Completion upgrades the equipped Grimoire 8 → 9, Mythic Grimoire. Any of the three replies gives 2 base, added to 10 completion base, so every answer gives 12 total base. No personal H2H is additionally required.
The initial character action marks Ninth Hex as paid-version content. The current save-load action restoration omits that paid flag while preserving In Love, exactly level 8, and the one-time completion check; availability can therefore differ between a new game and a restored save. Neither route accepts a level-7 or already-level-9 book.
The later level-10 upgrade belongs to Nyxara and has its own requirements. Ninth Hex does not grant NPC intimacy or player experience.

Mystic Seduction and Extras

Use Mystic Seduction requires the Mystic Seduction skill, effective Mystic 70, and paid-version access. It starts at her hut portrait from Stranger+, costs 30 minutes and 50 energy, and grants 40 base affection, up to three positive uses daily.
Each successful use randomly selects one of three authored scenes and marks that scene's Extras entry. Repeated random selection is possible, so three uses do not guarantee all three entries. Afterward Sabbath uses her mystically impressed dialogue for the next three applicable portrait/Talk appearances; this state does not increase Talk's recorded 1 base award.
The action and shared scene do not award NPC intimacy or player experience. Her current NPC intimacy rewards directory has no separate threshold-gallery images; the Mystic Seduction Extras and Christmas sequence are separate content.

Christmas Gift Scene

At Sabbath Close Friend, her Witches' Hut portrait, odd days 6 PM–midnight, offers Give Christmas Gift when an unused Christmas gift token is available and she has not already received one.
The action costs 30 minutes and one Christmas gift token, awards 100 fixed affection, and plays the authored gift sequence. There is no calendar-date requirement, no separate cash charge, and no requirement to collect the rare ingredients described by the scene. Both questions are dialogue choices with no additional score or affection difference.
The gift is once per character and its six reward images are separate from Mystic Seduction. Sabbath's current gift data does not attach an NPC intimacy-experience reward, and the sequence grants no player experience.

If Progress Stalls

  • Hut entrance missing: reach both effective Mystic 45 and Perception 45 in the Whispering Woods.
  • Sabbath portrait missing: use an odd-numbered game day, 6 PM–midnight. Entering the hut at another time does not make her manual menu available.
  • Epic Ingredient unavailable: reach Acknowledged and carry the Epic tier, not Common or Rare.
  • Lingerie unavailable: reach Friend and carry Lingerie. Stop at three gifts daily.
  • Cabinet Lesson missing: complete The Black Cabinet and reach Acquaintance.
  • Witch-Marked missing: complete the Cabinet Lesson H2H and Price of Pretty Things, then reach Close Friend.
  • Closer missing: complete Witch-Marked and reach Crush.
  • Bazaar date missing: reach Friend and begin 6–11 PM on an odd day; it disappears after completion.
  • Black Panties missing: use Seduce Sabbath at 500 ordinary affection. The item is supplied on entry; there is no separate shop purchase or theft action. A completed encounter does not reset after delivery.
  • Gandon's first gift scene missing: Accept Quest at Gandon before the hut visit. Talk alone does not accept his errand.
  • Second Pulse unavailable: reach Crush, carry all four ingredient quantities, and do not already own the key item.
  • Ninth Hex missing: reach In Love and check the Grimoire is exactly level 8, the scene is uncompleted, and energy/calm cover 110/80.
  • Mystic Seduction missing: check skill learned, Mystic 70 and paid access; a high Mystic stat alone does not teach the skill.
  • Christmas gift unavailable: check Close Friend, her portrait window, at least one gift token, and that she has not already received it.
Sabbath has no currently configured apartment conversation, venue Invite Date, Kupie entry, brothel-worker recruitment or resort route. Her daytime appearances are the shared automatic plots listed above.
