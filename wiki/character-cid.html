Overview

Cid offers a one-time Technology lesson, clinic resource exchanges and repeatable late-game fortress defense. He has no separate full romance chain.

Where to find them

His portrait is at Underground Modification Clinic all day. The clinic’s Operations Center entrance opens after Story 31.

Route and relationship milestones

At Known (25) and Communication 78, Human vs Machine takes 30 minutes for Technology +1 and 15 direct affection, once.

After Story 65, Something That Holds takes 0 minutes, gives one whole Chronium ingot and opens the defense service.

Fortress Defense exchanges 10 Chronium ingots for +1 Defenses at 0 minutes. There is no rising cost, daily cap, money, Army EXP or extra affection gate. It uses mining ingots rather than raw ore.

Defenses protects barriers and turrets, not Lives or hero armor. Structural damage reduction is explained in the Tower Defense guide.

Clinic location exchanges: 10 common ingredients → 5 common materials in 5 minutes; 25 common ingredients → 1 rare material in 10 minutes; 25 rare ingredients → 1 epic material in 15 minutes. Deception adds 1 output to each, giving 6, 2 or 2.

The local Iron Will lesson needs Stamina 16, 150 minutes, $200, 30 Energy and 10 epic materials, once. Cybernetics is taught at a different clinic; Body Modifications are installed by the engineering service.

Built to Last is a one-time Army equipment gift at the clinic: reach 45 Resistance Clout and have Cid acquired in the Army. Cid is in the default Army roster. The scene takes 0 minutes, spends no Clout and grants Heavy-Caliber Adapter. See the Army guide for equipment effects.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Finish Story 65 and his introduction, then check for 10 whole Chronium ingots.
  • Affection does not reduce the ingot price or unlock another romance.
  • Resource trades are clinic location actions, not portrait actions.
  • For Body Modifications follow the engineering service guide.

概览

Cid提供一次性科技课程、诊所资源交换与后期可重复的要塞防御服务，没有另一条完整恋爱路线。

去哪里找

他全天在地下改造诊所出现，主线31后诊所可进入行动中心。

路线与关系进展

已知（25）与沟通78后，人类对机器花费30分钟，一次性给予科技+1及15直接好感。

主线65后，坚守之物不消耗时间，给予一整块时源矿锭，并开放防御强化。

要塞防御每次消耗10时源矿锭，使要塞防御+1，不消耗时间，没有递增价格、每日上限、额外金钱、军团经验或好感门槛。消耗采矿矿锭，不是原矿。

要塞防御保护屏障与炮塔，用于减轻屏障与炮塔承受的伤害，建筑减伤见塔防指南。

这里的钢铁意志课程要求耐力16，花费150分钟、$200、30精力、10史诗材料，仅一次。控制论由另一间诊所教授，身体改造则由工程服务安装。

在诊所可进行一次经得住考验：反抗军声望达到45，且Cid已加入军团即可；Cid属于默认军团成员。事件不消耗时间或声望，完成后获得大口径转接器，装备效果见军团指南。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 先完成主线65与坚守之物，再检查10整块时源矿锭。
  • 好感不会降低矿锭价格，也不开放另一条恋爱路线。
  • 资源交换在诊所地点行动中，不在头像菜单里。
  • 身体改造安装请按工程服务指南进行。
