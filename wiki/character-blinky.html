Overview

Blinky teaches three spells, offers personal conversations and dates, and provides a late Grimoire upgrade. Follow the current Objectives first when a story step involves her; the route below gives navigation without revealing its outcomes.

Where to find them

Her arrival requires Rose & Thorn reputation 7. Afterwards she is normally available all day in Arcane Room. During the later allied visit, find Talk and Intimate Bonding at Allied Quarters; the normal lessons and dates remain tied to her home availability. Follow Objectives for the return route.

Route and relationship milestones

Learn Protective Ward at Mystic 14, Divination at Mystic 22, then Blink at Close Friend (200) and Mystic 28. These named lessons do not charge Crystals or money.

Story 43 also needs Never Been Kissed completed and both Bella and Blinky at Romantic (800). Check the Main Story guide for the surrounding locations and order.

Seventh Fold needs In Love (3000) and a Grimoire at exactly level 6, then raises it to 7. Queenstone bonding needs ownership of that stone and In Love (3000), once each day.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Missing lessons during a later move: check her current home and personal Objectives instead of waiting at the empty Arcane Room.
  • Seventh Fold does not work at Grimoire levels below 6. Ordinary earlier upgrades come first.
  • Bonding requires Queenstone in your key items, not another Soul Stone.

概览

Blinky 提供三种法术课程、个人谈心与约会，以及后期魔导书升级。涉及主线时请先查看当前目标；下列内容只说明如何推进，不透露结果。

在哪里找到

玫瑰与荆棘声誉达到 7 后可触发她的到来，之后通常全天位于奥术房。后期盟军行程期间，交谈与亲密羁绊会移至盟友居所；普通课程与约会仍要求她可在原住处活动。返回路线请跟随当前目标。

路线与关系节点

防护结界课程要求神秘 14，占卜要求神秘 22；闪现还要求亲密朋友（200）与神秘 28。这些指定课程不收水晶或金钱。

主线 43 还要求完成「从未被亲吻」，并使 Bella 与 Blinky 都达到浪漫感（800）。前后顺序与地点请参照主线指南。

第七折叠要求恋爱中（3000），且魔导书等级恰为 6，完成后提升至 7 级。女王石羁绊则要求持有该石并达到恋爱中（3000），每天一次。

常见卡点

  • 后期搬动期间看不到课程时，请先检查她当前住处与个人目标，避免一直等在空的奥术房。
  • 魔导书低于 6 级时不能使用第七折叠，需先完成常规升级。
  • 羁绊要求关键物品中的女王石，其他灵魂石不能代替。
