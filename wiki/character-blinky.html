Blinky — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Blinky is a wandering wizard who settles in the Rose & Thorn's Arcane Room. Her current route includes recruitment, conversation, three magic lessons, two heart-to-hearts, two dates, shared scenes, main-story requirements, a grimoire upgrade and Queenstone bonding. Later narrative revelations are left undisclosed.

Meeting Blinky

Raise brothel reputation to 7, then visit the Brothel Demesne outside the Rose & Thorn. A Confused Arrival starts automatically once, with no additional configured hour restriction. Completion recruits her, opens the Arcane Room at room level 1, and grants an affection reward calculated from 10 base points.
Enter the Arcane Room from the West Hallway. Blinky is ordinarily present 24 hours. There is no dungeon battle required for this recruitment.

Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Use Talk from Stranger: 20 minutes, 1 base affection, five times per day.
  2. At Acknowledged and Mystic 14, learn Protective Ward. At Known and Mystic 22, learn Divination.
  3. At Known, take the Bazaar Date from her room 9 AM–2 PM. At Known and Communication 50, complete Wisdom of Moments.
  4. At Acquaintance, select Where Do I Belong? for her first five-round heart-to-heart.
  5. At Close Friend, select Never Been Kissed, and optionally take Sunset Walk 4 PM–7 PM.
  6. At Close Friend and Mystic 28, learn Blink through Learning to Blink in the paid version. This is a scene-based lesson, not the same route as the first two skills.
  7. To pass main-story Beat 43, complete her Never Been Kissed H2H, raise both Blinky and Bella to Romantic, and finish Beat 42. Return to the Arcane Room.
  8. After Beat 43, at Romantic, the paid Intimate Moment video becomes available.
  9. At In Love, use Seventh Fold with a level-6 Grimoire in the paid version, or Intimate Bonding once you own the Queenstone.
Dates and the two ordinary H2Hs complete once. The two H2Hs do not require one another's completion flags; the specified stage is their action gate. Later main-story progression specifically checks Never Been Kissed.

Musical Performance Reaction

After composing a song, perform at the Brothel Bar: 60 minutes, 30 energy, once per venue per day. Blinky is one of seven randomly selected bar reactions after a successful performance-scene roll. The shared reaction chance starts at 25%, adds 5% for each of Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity and Storytelling, and 5% for owning a Lute, capped at 50%.
If her repeatable reaction appears, it gives 8 base completion affection. Caring +4 is her best response; Flirty gives 2 and Funny gives 1. Her reaction is not selected directly from her ordinary room menu.

If Progress Stalls

  • Arrival needs brothel reputation 7 and a visit outside the brothel. It opens the Arcane Room.
  • Talk gives 1 base affection, up to five times per day. Cultural Adaptation improves rewards but is not one of her lessons.
  • Protective Ward, Divination and Blink have different Mystic, stage and resource gates. Blink also needs the paid version and 105 energy before reductions.
  • Listen to Her Journeys is a separate location action, not an extra Talk allowance.
  • Beat 38 needs Blink learned. Beat 43 needs Never Been Kissed complete and Romantic with both Blinky and Bella.
  • Intimate Moment needs Beat 43 complete in addition to Romantic and the paid version.
  • Seventh Fold needs Grimoire exactly level 6, and Queenstone bonding needs the stone owned and In Love.
  • Shared scenes can depend on other characters' stages, active worker/maid records and vacation status. More Blinky affection does not replace those gates.
