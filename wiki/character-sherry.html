Overview

Sherry is found at the house Bar at night. Her three linked H2Hs, one-time Garden Walk and late private invitation form the practical personal route.

Where to find them

Open the Bar by recruiting Brandy and keeping her active. Sherry’s ordinary portrait appears 19:00–24:00 and 00:00–04:00. Shared automatic Bar events can have their own broader windows.

PlacePortrait hoursCondition
The Bar19:00–24:00 / 00:00–04:00—

Route and relationship milestones

Complete Getting to Know You at Known (25), The Deeper Questions at Friend (100), then Something More at Crush (400). Each requires the preceding H2H and completes once. Garden Walk is a separate Friend (100) date starting 19:00–22:00.

Private Time requires Romantic (800) and Something More, and starts from the Bar after midnight, 00:00–04:00. It takes 120 minutes and 50 Energy. Its activity branches and affection depend on choices; the conversation’s finish returns you to ordinary play.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Commerce 60; Charm 60; Stamina 70

Favorite skill: Reading People

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

Check the Bar entrance as well as the clock. Private Time starts after midnight, not at 19:00. A shared event may require another worker home from vacation or active on the roster; the normal Sherry portrait alone does not satisfy those conditions.

概览

Sherry 夜间出现在宅邸酒吧。三段连续谈心、一次性庭园漫步与后期私人邀请构成她的个人推进路线。

在哪里找到

先招募 Brandy 并让她在职，才能使用酒吧入口。Sherry 的普通肖像在 19:00–24:00 及 00:00–04:00 出现；共同自动事件可能使用更宽的独立时段。

地点肖像在场时段条件
酒吧19:00–24:00／00:00–04:00—

路线与关系节点

已知（25）完成第一段谈心，朋友（100）进行第二段，迷恋（400）进行第三段。后两段各要求上一段已经完成，均为一次性内容。庭园漫步是独立的朋友（100）约会，可于 19:00–22:00 开始。

私人相聚要求浪漫感（800）及第三段谈心完成，只能从午夜后的酒吧 00:00–04:00 开始，耗时 120 分钟并消耗 50 精力。活动分支与好感度由选择决定，结束对话后回到普通游玩。

普通交谈每次 15 分钟、增加 1 基础好感度，每天有 10 次正面使用机会。共同事件涉及 Elara、Blinky、Brandy、Sandra、Ava and Eva、Pepper、Poppy 与 Ryia，各有自己的条件。演奏原创歌曲时，可能随机抽中 Sherry 的酒吧反应，并非好感度提高就保证触发。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

商业 60; 魅力 60; 耐力 70

偏好技能：识人术

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

除了时间，也要检查酒吧入口。私人相聚从午夜后开始，不是 19:00。共同事件可能要求另一名员工结束度假或处于在职名单，只有 Sherry 的普通肖像还不够。
