Overview

Sherry manages the Rose & Thorn Bar. Her current personal route has three one-time heart-to-hearts, the one-time Garden Walk date and repeatable Private Time after closing. She also appears in shared bar and kitchen scenes and can react to your song performances.

Recommended Progression

  1. Own the Rose & Thorn, make the Bar available and visit with Elara home rather than vacationing for The New Bar Manager introduction.
  2. Use Talk to Sherry during her evening or early-morning portrait hours.
  3. At Known, complete Getting to Know You. Empathy and Charm 45 improve optional replies.
  4. At Friend, complete The Deeper Questions after the first H2H. Reading People and Intelligence 40 improve optional replies.
  5. At Friend, invite her on Garden Walk between 7 and 10 PM. This date is independent of the H2H chain.
  6. At Crush, complete Something More after The Deeper Questions. Seduction and Charm 50 improve optional replies.
  7. At Romantic, revisit her Bar portrait between midnight and 4 AM for Private Time after Something More.
The personal action definitions configure no extra paid-access flag, gift, money cost or introduction-completion prerequisite on the H2Hs, Garden Walk or Private Time beyond the requirements listed below.

Talk and Affection

Talk to Sherry takes 15 minutes, gives 1 actual base affection at every stage, and allows ten positive talks daily. The handler records negative affection after the positive limit. The counter follows the current game day, including after midnight. There are no ordinary portrait gift or skill-training actions in her current definition.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Song Performance Reaction

With a composed song, use Perform Song at the Brothel Bar. It takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy and allows one performance per day at that venue.
The scene chance starts at 25%, with +5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling and owning Lute, capped at 50%. If a scene is rolled, Sherry is one of the seven Bar reaction scenes selected randomly. A successful performance does not guarantee her reaction, and this reaction is not a one-time personal route milestone.
Her Flirty reply gives 4 choice base +8 completion =12 total best base affection. Caring gives 10 and Funny 9. This affection is separate from the performance's other venue rewards, and no NPC intimacy experience is attached.

Troubleshooting

  • Sherry is absent: her personal portrait is 7 PM–4 AM, and the Bar room must be available.
  • The next H2H is locked: complete the previous conversation and reach its own affection stage; its stat/skill special replies are optional.
  • Garden Walk is missing: reach Friend and invite her before 10 PM. The one-time date hides after completion.
  • Private Time is missing: reach Romantic, complete Something More and visit the Bar between midnight and 4 AM.
  • I cannot return to the five activity choices: each selected activity jumps straight to afterglow. Choose a different activity on a later visit.
  • The Beast path gave less affection: it replaces the ordinary ending's +10 base with extra player experience and Beast +1.
  • Erotic Experience did not unlock a Sherry gallery: she has no authored current gallery reward assets or NPC intimacy-experience metadata in these conversations.
  • A shared scene is not starting: check its exact other-character stage, active-worker state, completed introductions and time window; different shared scenes have different prerequisites.
