Overview

Barric’s current player content is a small set of alehouse conversations and one practical stat lesson. He is useful for relationship progress and local flavor, without a separate quest or romance chain.

Where to find them

Find him at Rowdy Alehouse daily 20:00–03:00. After midnight it is the new calendar day, so daily allowances reset accordingly.

Route and relationship milestones

Begin with Talk over the Counter. Known 25 opens Ask about the Regulars; Acquaintance 50 opens Hear an Alehouse Story.

At Friend 100 and Perception 35, The Keeper's Ear takes 30 min and awards 1 Communication plus 8 direct affection once.

Favorite stats are Communication, Perception, Commerce. Learning Reading People improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The alehouse portrait is a night schedule; arriving at 03:00 is already too late.
  • The Keeper’s Ear checks Perception 35 and Friend 100, and disappears after its one-time reward.

概览

Barric目前提供几种酒馆谈话和一门实用属性课程，可用来提高关系并了解当地见闻，没有独立任务或恋爱链。

去哪里找

每天20:00–次日03:00在喧闹酒馆。午夜后已是新一天，每日额度也随之刷新。

路线与关系进展

先使用柜台闲谈；已知25开放聊聊常客，相识50开放听个酒馆故事。

朋友100、感知35可进行店主的倾听之道，30分钟，一次性增加1沟通和8直接好感。

三种可重复谈话均增加2基础好感。柜台交谈15分钟、每天三次；常客话题和酒馆往事各20分钟、各每天两次，额度分别计算。

偏好的属性为沟通、感知、商业。学会识人术可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 头像只在夜间出现，03:00已经超出时段。
  • 课程检查感知35、朋友100，领取一次性奖励后即消失。
