Overview

Julian is a kind young adult noble in the King's Demesne. He visits the Grand Bazaar to learn about trade and the people behind it. His current personal route builds a platonic friendship through four conversation topics and a one-time Commerce reward. He can also appear in the optional private-client rotation.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Meet Julian at the Grand Bazaar from 9 AM–2 PM, or the Noble Quarter from 2–8 PM.
  2. Start with up to four Talks per game day. Improve his favorite stats and use Accounting if you have it to increase the modified relationship reward.
  3. At Acknowledged, add Discuss the Market, up to three times daily. At Known, add Ask about community, up to twice daily. At Acquaintance, add Ask about His Family, up to twice daily.
  4. Reach Friend and Intelligence 30. Select Read the Fine Print during either location window to gain +1 Commerce and 8 direct affection, once.
  5. If you operate a brothel with NTR enabled, reach brothel reputation level 11 for Julian to join the separate Receive a Client rotation. This is optional and does not advance his personal friendship.
Each conversation has its own allowance, so using Talk does not consume Market, Community or Family uses. There is no required order between the unlocked topics, and no gift, court quest or private booking is required for Read the Fine Print.

This action compares two offers and grants a stat boost; it does not teach Accounting. Its affection is added directly rather than processed as an ordinary modified conversation reward. After claiming it, the completion flag prevents another claim at either location.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Julian enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level11. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Julian. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Julian has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is kindness and warmth, with Sophie listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Honey, Shadow, Sophie.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Julian affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Julian.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • Julian missing: check the district and time. He moves from the Grand Bazaar to the Noble Quarter at 2 PM, and leaves his regular schedule at 8 PM.
  • Conversation unavailable: reach the topic's minimum relationship stage. Daily counters are separate by topic and shared between locations.
  • No Fine Print: reach Friend and Intelligence 30, and check whether it was already claimed. Brothel reputation and worker recruitment are not prerequisites.
  • No private booking: check NTR, brothel operation, reputation 11, available workers and the shared daily limit. Another unlocked client may be selected instead.
  • Looking for another route: the current character definition has no personal Heart-to-Heart, date, ordinary gift, apartment, worker recruitment, resort, Kupie or invitation-date route. His authored player friendship is platonic.
