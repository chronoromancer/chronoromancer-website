解决问题

  • 在陌生或已知的演讲中失踪: 它打开在 知识。从介绍开始,共享辛迪加任务。
  • 大顶部缺失 : 输入 辛迪加门,然后选择 到大顶.
  • 猫眼不见了: 填写 Zodiac's 收取会费——律师协会,然后返回到 大顶.
  • 缺少 Rookie 场景 : 完成 财富报社的新血,然后访问 下午7时至5时.
  • 早期的爱情摊: 检查最喜欢的数据和杂技,并遵循更多共享的场景机会。已完成的介绍无法重播以获得奖励。
  • 轮廓培训花费资源而不增加数据: 检查它的 三用途每日津贴;超限尝试不会训练Rogue.
  • 个人连锁摊位 : 满足下一个爱情阶段,并完成它之前的个人场景。普通的谈话不能代替情节的完成。
  • 在普罗尔失踪时: 到达 粉笔访问 大头,下午6点到10点,并检查是否已经完成。
  • Kitty 样式缺失 : 检查 1000个普通的感情 以及今天的视频津贴
  • Saber 遇缺: 检查 Saber的特定完成的H2H,两个字符所需的阶段,前一个共享场景,位置和 下午6时至11时 图标。

Overview

Kitty is the feisty, tiger-themed member of the Chaos Syndicate, normally found at The Big Top in the Future Prime Sector. Her route includes shared Syndicate missions, personal automatic scenes, repeatable Rogue training, a one-time evening date, an intimate animation and three shared scenes with Saber. This walkthrough focuses on requirements and rewards without explaining her origins or later story resolutions.

Meeting Kitty and Unlocking Talk

  1. Travel to Syndicate Gates in Future Prime Sector, then use To The Parlor for The New Variable. This gives 7 base Kitty affection; Flirty adds 2, making 9 base.
  2. Use To The Big Top and complete Fresh Meat. There is no affection prerequisite. It gives 1 base affection, with Flirty +4 as the highest choice, making 5 base.
  3. Meet Zodiac and Chamel, and complete the Syndicate's Gatekeeper encounter, then follow Zodiac's collection missions. Kitty's ordinary Talk and Rogue Training both require Acquaintance, so use shared quest rewards before expecting either repeatable action.
  4. After Zodiac's Collecting Dues — The Bar, return to Big Top for Cat's Eyes, and to Fortune Teller's Booth for New Blood.
  5. After New Blood, visit Prime Transportation Hub, 7 AM–5 PM, for Taming the Rookie. Both this and Cat's Eyes give a one-time Rogue +1 on completion.
  6. At Known, optionally visit The Den, midnight–6 AM, for Kitty's separate personal encounter. At Acquaintance, return to her Big Top portrait for daily Talk and Rogue Training.
  7. Progress the personal chain Friend → Close Friend → Crush → Romantic → Intimate using the scenes below. On the Prowl is independently available at Crush, and Kitty Style at 1,000 ordinary affection.
  8. After Story Beat 77, follow Saber's H2Hs for their three shared evening encounters. After Story Beat 85, the separate Syndicate alliance continuation adds more Kitty affection opportunities.
The first two meetings do not guarantee Acquaintance. Final gains depend on the shared relationship calculation and your bonuses. Continue the Syndicate missions rather than trying to repeat a completed introduction.

Kitty Style

At 1,000 ordinary Kitty affection, choose Kitty Style at Big Top, any hour. The animation costs 30 minutes and 20 Energy, gives 80 base affection, and its video allowance is once per game day. There is no separate On the Prowl or Tamed completion prerequisite in this action's requirements.
The looping movie is implemented. Its authored video data has no NPC intimacy experience metadata, and this handler has no player Erotic experience award. Watching an intimate scene does not automatically imply either experience reward.

Troubleshooting

  • Talk missing at Stranger or Known: it opens at Acquaintance. Start with introductions and shared Syndicate missions.
  • Big Top missing: enter Syndicate Gates, then choose To The Big Top.
  • Cat's Eyes missing: complete Zodiac's Collecting Dues — The Bar, then return to Big Top.
  • Rookie scene missing: complete New Blood at Fortune Teller's Booth, then visit Prime Transportation Hub, 7 AM–5 PM.
  • Early affection stalls: check favorite stats and Acrobatics, and follow more shared scene opportunities. Completed introductions cannot be replayed for rewards.
  • Rogue training costs resources without a stat increase: check its three-use daily allowance; over-limit attempts do not train Rogue.
  • Personal chain stalls: meet the next affection stage and complete its previous personal scene. Ordinary Talk does not substitute for plot completion.
  • On the Prowl missing: reach Crush, visit Big Top, 6–10 PM, and check whether it already completed.
  • Kitty Style missing: check 1,000 ordinary affection and its video allowance for today.
  • Saber encounter missing: check Saber's particular completed H2H, both characters' required stages, previous shared scene, location and 6–11 PM window.
  • Alliance continuation missing: complete Story Beat 85 and After the Line, then start at Operations Center.
Her current route has no ordinary gifts, personal H2H, apartment visit, brothel-worker recruitment, resort invitation, Kupie roster entry, venue Invite Date or pregnancy route.
