Overview

Kasumi is an outspoken, literal-minded oracle with teal twin tails. Her current route includes automatic brothel encounters, daily conversation, a Garden Walk date, recruitment as worker 20, a shared scene with Shadow, a performance reaction, a main-story skill reward, two crafted training upgrades, Queenstone bonding and Sirena Isle resort activities. This guide gives the playable requirements and answers without retelling her visions or the larger story.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Raise brothel reputation to 6, 8 and 10, returning to the entrance for Kasumi's automatic scenes. Complete The Oracle's Choice to recruit worker 20.
  2. Build affection with four Bar Talks per day, 3–6 PM. Improve her favorite stats and use Spirit Communication if learned. Worker gifts and resort activities provide other affection sources after recruitment.
  3. At Friend, start Garden Walk from the Bar, 3–5 PM. Choose Funny all three times for its maximum 23 base points. This date later unlocks her Cowgirl upgrade.
  4. For Shadow Arts Apprentice, have Shadow Known and both Shadow and Kasumi active and home, then visit the Bar, 8 PM–midnight. Choose Supportive for 7 base points each.
  5. After Story Beat 38, with Kasumi active, visit the Bar, 3–6 PM for Story Beat 39 and the Precognition player skill. Choose Caring or Flirty in both rounds for the maximum Kasumi affection.
  6. Unlock Sirena Isle through Marina's boat route and send Kasumi on vacation. Use Walk with Kasumi at the resort Beach, 5–8 PM, to complete her repeatable Sunset Walk H2H. This unlocks the prerequisite for her Oral upgrade.
  7. At In Love, bring the appropriate crafted gifts to her Bar portrait, 3–6 PM, after bringing her home. Complete her two upgrades and, if you own the Queenstone, use daily Intimate Bonding.
Garden Walk, Shadow Arts and Story Beat 39 do not require a prior resort Sunset Walk. The resort walk is a separate activity and does not replace the Garden Walk completion needed by the Cowgirl upgrade.

Daily Talk and Reassurance

Talk costs 20 minutes, gives 1 base point, and allows four uses per game day. Its action definition has a zero placeholder, but the current Talk handler records one point. Further attempts record −1 and use the annoyed response. Favorite-stat, Spirit Communication and general modifiers affect final affection.
Once worker 20 is found, Reassure Kasumi can appear with her actual Bar portrait, 3–6 PM, while she is home. It costs 20 minutes, gives +1 Loyalty up to 100 and 1 base ordinary affection, and can be used once per day. It follows her portrait schedule rather than imposing an additional worker-shift requirement. Its allowance is separate from Talk.
She has no ordinary portrait gift or player-skill lesson. Gifts in her worker visit menu are described separately below.

Garden Walk Date

Start Garden Walk from Kasumi's Bar portrait, 3–5 PM, at Friend or higher. It costs 120 minutes and 20 Energy, with no money charge. It completes once, despite the action's daily-limit metadata. It has no explicit paid-version or supporter-code requirement.
The scene gives 8 base points plus three choice bonuses. For 23 base points, select Funny or Flirty in round 1, Caring or Funny in round 2, and Funny or Flirty in round 3. Funny all three rounds achieves the maximum.

Story Beat 39 and Precognition

After Story Beat 38, visit the Bar from 3–6 PM, with worker 20 active, for Story Beat 39. The published progression's normal story checks also apply. The encounter gives 10 base Kasumi points, 5 Sashina and 5 Elara, before choices.
For Kasumi, Caring or Flirty adds 5 in each of two rounds, reaching 20 base points. Funny adds 2 in round 1 and 3 in round 2. All paths complete the encounter. Its completion advances 30 minutes and teaches Precognition.
Precognition gives a 10% base chance to avoid a dodgeable enemy attack, before the shared dodge-chance bonuses. It is a player combat skill. Kasumi's worker Oracle skill and her favorite relationship skill are distinct. The guide omits the revelation and vision details from this story encounter.

Sirena Isle Vacation Activities

Unlock the vacation system through Marina's Boat to Sirena Isle action after Sashimi's vacation-island proposal. The boat action takes 120 minutes and 1,000,000 gold, requires paid access and the required subscriber code, and completes once. See Marina's and Sashimi's guides for their route prerequisites.
Use the Sirena Isle management panel to send active worker 20 to an available resort slot; she cannot be assigned as an apprentice at the same time. Bring her home through that panel when you want to use Bar actions or ordinary worker visits again.
At the resort, her portrait can appear at the Beach, Buffet, Bedroom or Spa from 7 AM–11 PM, depending on the arrival roll. The chance to get a vacationer's portrait is 25% per vacationing girl, capped at 100%, and a vacationer is then chosen. Leaving and returning to a resort presence location can produce another roll. Her Bar hours do not control this vacation schedule.

Queenstone Bonding

At In Love, carry the Queenstone key item and select Intimate Bonding with Kasumi at the Bar, 3–6 PM. It takes 60 minutes, raises the stone's level by 1, and can be used once per game day. It does not consume the stone and has no configured money, Energy, Calm or direct affection award.
The Queenstone provides +1 maximum Health per stone level while owned. This bonding action upgrades an existing stone; it does not grant the first Queenstone or teach Spirit Communication.

Troubleshooting

  • Kasumi missing from the Bar: check 3–6 PM, Bar room level 1+, and whether she is vacationing. Her worker shift is a different schedule.
  • Recruitment missing: use the entrance at reputation 10+, with Kasumi home, and complete The Oracle's Choice. Garden Walk does not recruit her.
  • Night Shadow scene missing: visit the Bar, 8 PM–midnight, with Shadow Known and workers 8 and 20 active and home. Her daytime portrait is not required.
  • Garden Walk missing: reach Friend, start 3–5 PM, and check whether the one-time date is already completed.
  • Worker visit unavailable: check active status, resting, vacation, assigned shift and today's visit allowance.
  • Crafted action missing: reach In Love, carry the exact gift, complete its Garden Walk or resort Sunset Walk prerequisite, and use the home Bar portrait from 3–6 PM.
  • Resort walk missing: send worker 20 on vacation and find her Beach portrait, 5–8 PM. Completing it on an earlier day does not permanently remove the repeatable walk.
  • Stone action missing: own the actual Queenstone, reach In Love and leave today's bonding allowance unused.
  • Looking for additional systems: Kasumi has no current apartment, Kupie, pregnancy or KTV/Nightclub invitation-date route configured. Her current private video activity is the resort's Private Beach Time.
