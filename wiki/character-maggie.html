Overview

Maggie is a recently graduated finance associate at the Present Downtown Office Building. Her route combines work experience and Formal Outfit upgrades with friendship, two personal Heart-to-Hearts, three one-time dates, repeatable venue invitations and private visits. She also participates in the shared corporate route with Miki, Arnold and Evangeline.
This guide explains the current requirements and useful choices without revealing the scenes' personal conversations or corporate outcomes.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit Investment Planning, 1–5 PM, for An Ambitious Introduction. Use her Talk action up to five times daily across both office rooms.
  2. Earn 15 Working Experience through jobs and upgrade Formal Outfit to level 2. Return to Investment Planning, 1–5 PM, for A Partnership Worth Earning and the Diligent Worker skill.
  3. At Known, Maggie also appears in Offices, 9 AM–1 PM. After Partnership, choose A Quiet Moment there for The View. Her Zoo Date opens separately in Investment Planning.
  4. Reach 80 Working Experience and Formal Outfit level 3, then visit Investment Planning 1–5 PM for The Eye for Value and Business Acumen. Her Picnic Date opens afterward.
  5. Reach 150 Working Experience, keeping Formal Outfit level 3 or higher, and return 1–5 PM for Work Hard, Play Harder. Choose Between Meetings for The Best Investment.
  6. At Friend, use Win Her a Prize with a Voucher if she is not pregnant. At Crush, after Work Hard, Play Harder, use the KTV or Night Club Invite Date menu, 8–11 PM.
  7. At Romantic, 800 ordinary affection, her Lunch Break opens 11 AM–3 PM across the two office rooms. Visit Maggie opens at the Downtown Apartment Complex, 8 PM–midnight, after Work Hard, Play Harder.
  8. Continue the separate Kupie, corporate encounters and optional parenthood continuation using their requirements below.

Troubleshooting

  • Maggie missing in Offices: reach Known and visit 9 AM–1 PM. Her Investment Planning portrait is 1–5 PM without that affection gate.
  • Career quest stalled: check Working Exp, the Formal Outfit equipment level, the previous encounter's completion and Investment Planning, 1–5 PM. Affection alone does not advance it.
  • Diligent Worker or Business Acumen missing: complete Partnership or Eye for Value, respectively; reaching their thresholds alone does not grant the skill.
  • A Quiet Moment missing: finish Partnership and use her Known office portrait, 9 AM–1 PM.
  • Between Meetings missing: finish Work Hard, Play Harder and use Investment Planning 1–5 PM. The View is not an extra prerequisite.
  • Dates unavailable in the morning: all three start from her afternoon Investment portrait, despite their wider action hours.
  • Lunch Break missing at 3 PM: its start window ends at 3 PM. Use Offices 11 AM–1 PM or Investment Planning 1–3 PM, at Romantic.
  • Visit Maggie missing: use the Downtown Apartment Complex, 8 PM–midnight, at Romantic, after Work Hard, Play Harder; check Energy and pregnancy/recovery status.
  • Venue roster missing Maggie: finish Work Hard, Play Harder, reach Crush, and use Invite Date, 8–11 PM. Night Club also needs its current supporter access.
  • Perfect invitation score missed: choose five accepted answers and consume Flowers, Wine or Jewelry for the sixth point. Both venues accept all three gifts for Maggie.
  • Parenthood choice absent: check the shared protocol/settings, 2,000 ordinary affection, 500 Maggie NPC intimacy experience, career completion and family/recovery status. Her personal H2Hs award no NPC intimacy experience.
  • Kupie selection missing: reach main-game Friend, claim 80 achievements and complete one rebirth.
Maggie's current route has no configured brothel worker recruitment, maid recruitment, resort vacation or Maggie-specific Christmas activity.
