NPC 亲密画廊

她的三个实际画廊资产解锁在 100、250和500 NPC亲密经验。没有第四至第八个 Shadow 资产存在,尽管共享画廊系统支持更多的插槽。
目前的资料来源是: 工人培训, 日落漫步的5次接吻经历,以及 沙滩时间的25次传教经验普通情感、工人经验、玩家情感体验和NPC亲密体验是独立的计数器,巷道发现、盾牌对话、夜间集市、拼写课和精心制作的礼物不会给NPC额外配置的亲密体验。开设画廊或简介不会给体验。

  • 她缺席协会: 检查日平时和狭长的肖像时间,然后是休假状态,房间开放时间不是她的日程安排.

  • 我不能开始盾牌启动: 准备Shadow 已知和Rogue 35,然后使用Arestes的隐藏分庭行动。
  • 卧室谈话不见了: 招聘她为8号工人,并在日出前的对等窗口期间参观布罗瑟尔主卧室.
  • 夜市消失: 完成后隐藏;两个邀请地点共用一个完成旗.
  • 招募现场不见了: 请检查date=中的日期值 (帮助) 。
  • An In Love 礼物行动不见了: 请检查date=中的日期值 (帮助) 校对:Soup
  • Shadow步骤没有让我逃离: 老大们,无法承受的遭遇,阻塞逃亡状态的影响以及无穷敦格失败卷仍然适用.

Shadow — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Shadow is a nocturnal thief in the Medieval King's Demesne. Her current content includes outdoor conversations, a one-time alley encounter, the Thieves Guild route, Night Bazaar, Pickpocketing Lessons, Shadow Step, brothel recruitment and training, pre-dawn bedroom visits, Phantom Stone bonding, and Sirena Isle activities.
The guide gives requirements and rewards while leaving the guild arrangement and scene outcomes to the game.

Recommended Progression

  1. Talk to Shadow at the Thieves Guild during her portrait hours. You can build affection there before the optional alley encounter.
  2. For the one-time alley encounter, prepare Perception 45 and preferably Ability 50 or Fleet Footed, then visit the Back Alleys 10 PM–2 AM while she is home.
  3. Reach Known for her other outdoor locations and Pickpocketing Lessons. Prepare Communication 40 for the lesson.
  4. With Shadow Known and Rogue 35, request Thieves Guild Initiation from Arestes in the Hidden Chamber. Follow the seven-location trail below.
  5. At Friend, complete Night Bazaar from her Guild or Back Alleys portrait during the late-night invitation window.
  6. Raise Thieves Guild reputation to 120 and revisit the Guild for The Arrangement, which recruits worker #8.
  7. At Romantic, Ability 60 and paid access, learn Shadow Step from her Guild portrait.
  8. At In Love, unlock her two crafted-gift training upgrades and daily Phantom Stone bonding.
  9. Send her active worker on a Sirena Isle vacation for the repeatable sunset H2H, private beach video and shared encounters.

Troubleshooting

  • She is absent from the Guild: check the day parity and her narrow portrait hours, then vacation status. The room's open hours are not her schedule.
  • The alley event no longer repeats: even its low-Ability path completes the one-time event. You can continue her relationship through Guild Talk.
  • I cannot start guild initiation: prepare Shadow Known and Rogue 35, then use Arestes's Hidden Chamber action.
  • Bedroom Talk is missing: recruit her as worker #8 and visit the Brothel Master Bedroom during the parity-specific pre-dawn window.
  • Night Bazaar vanished: it hides after completion; the two invitation locations share one completion flag.
  • The recruitment scene is missing: check Thieves Guild reputation 120 and whether The Arrangement already completed. The scene is distinct from the date and Shadow Step lesson.
  • An In Love gift action is missing: check the correct crafted gift, its completed scenes, the specified ordinary portrait and vacation status.
  • Shadow Step did not let me flee: bosses, unfleeable encounters, flee-blocking status effects and the Infinity Dungeon failure roll still apply.
  • The gallery is not progressing: use the actual NPC intimacy sources above; ordinary affection and player Erotic Experience do not unlock it.
  • A shared island scene will not appear: all its worker participants must be active vacationers in the same listed window.
