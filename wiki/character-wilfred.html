Overview

Wilfred offers farm friendship and a one-time Stamina reward. His shared farm encounters connect to Honey’s route; his ordinary menu does not add a separate romance or worker-recruitment path.

Where to find Wilfred

Visit Medieval → Kings Demesne → Rural Farm, daily 06:00–13:00. Automatic farm scenes use their own broader start windows and can occur after his portrait has left.

LocationPortrait scheduleCondition

Windows refer to the time you start an action; the ending time is excluded.

Route and relationship milestones

Use Talk to reach Acknowledged (10), then Help with Farm Work. At Known (25) and Communication 55, Farming Endurance grants Stamina +1 and 8 direct relationship points once, for 30 minutes.

For shared progression, build both Wilfred and Honey’s relationships and follow Honey’s personal objectives. Potato Knowledge Delivery requires the earlier Potato Solution; it is a one-time delivery rather than a repeatable farming reward.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 15 minutes for 1 base point, four safe uses daily. Farm Work takes 45 minutes, 25 Energy and 10 Calm for 6 base points, three safe uses daily. Completing all four Talks and three Farm Work sessions costs 195 minutes, 75 Energy and 30 Calm for 22 base points before modifiers.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

概览

Wilfred提供农场关系互动和一次性的耐力奖励，共同农场事件与Honey的路线相连；普通菜单没有独立的恋爱或员工招募分支。

出现地点与时间

前往中世纪 → 国王领地 → 乡村农场，每天06:00—13:00。自动农场事件有各自更宽的时段，可能在头像离开后仍可触发。

路线与关系进展

先靠交谈达到已遇见（10），再开放帮忙农活。已知（25）、沟通55时，农活耐力可花30分钟，一次性增加耐力1点和8点直接关系。

共同路线需要同时培养Wilfred和Honey，并继续Honey的个人目标。土豆知识交付要求前一项土豆方案，是一次性交付，不是可重复的农活奖励。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，获得1点基础关系，每日安全额度4次；帮忙农活消耗45分钟、25精力、10镇定，获得6点基础关系，每日安全额度3次。四次交谈加三次帮工合计195分钟、75精力、30镇定，共22点基础关系，之后再套用修正。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。
