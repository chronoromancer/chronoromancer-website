概览和时间表

Wilfred是Honey的62岁父亲,中世纪时代的一位有良好配乐的农民,他的个人路线是农场友谊和共享家庭活动;并不增加未列出的浪漫或招聘工人的路线.
去找他的普通画像 农村农场,6上午1时每天,最爱的数据: 力量35、Stamina50和烹饪35他特殊的关系技巧是 资源优化;没有配置普通的礼品动作。
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

Overview and Schedule

Wilfred is Honey's62-year-old father, a farmer in the Medieval era with Good alignment. His personal route is farm friendship and shared family events; it does not add an unlisted romance or worker-recruitment route.
Find his ordinary portrait at the Rural Farm,6 AM–1 PM, every day. Favorite stats: Strength35,Stamina50 and Cooking35. His special relationship skill is Resource Optimization; no ordinary gift action is configured.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Wilfred enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level5. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Wilfred. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Honey #3 is excluded from his booking choices by the family check. His authored preference is quiet, uncomplicated company, with A.R.I.A. listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for A.R.I.A., Brandy and Valkyra.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Wilfred affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Wilfred.


Troubleshooting

  • No ordinary farm actions: visit6 AM–1 PM.
  • Farm Work missing: reach Acknowledged; its allowance is separate from Talk.
  • Stamina reward missing: reach Known,raise Communication to55 and check whether already claimed.
  • A shared event stalls: check its exact Honey/Wilfred relationship gates,preceding flag,time and Honey's vacation status.
  • Potato delivery missing: finish the Throne Room Potato Solution first,then return to the farm.
  • Honey is absent from his client list: the booking system explicitly excludes her for this client.
