Overview

Pixel’s route follows visits across several Future locations, three conversations and two optional dates. A later private visit is separate from ordinary talk and chocolate gifts.

Where to find them

Her ordinary portrait is at Optimized Urban Thoroughfare Alpha-7, daily 10:00–17:59. After The Unoptimized Variable, she also appears at Operations Center, 19:00–21:59. The daytime portrait remains available.

Route and relationship milestones

Visit for Memory Leak. Later automatic scenes change location, so follow the location column rather than repeatedly waiting on the street. The route reaches Story 31 before Return to Sender; its next stage requires In Love (3,000) at the Operations Center.

The conversations run Pocket Full of Tomorrow → When the Static Wins → A Memory Made on Purpose. Their preceding personal scenes are listed below. Neither optional date is required by the automatic scene chain.

Talk gives 1 base affection, six times per day. Chocolate from Known (25) gives 4, four gifts per day; gifts are on her street menu. Improvisation training is available at both workplaces: Friend (100), Creativity 16, 150 minutes, $150, 30 Energy and 10 Calm, with its access lock.

Favorite stats are Luck, Charm, Creativity. The learned Improvisation skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • A route scene may be at the clinic, enhancement center, temple, archive or Operations Center rather than her ordinary portrait.
  • Private Time needs the third conversation as well as Romantic and its preceding scene.
  • Moving between the street and Operations Center does not reset Private Time’s daily allowance.
  • The Army gift additionally needs recruitment and Resistance Clout; ordinary affection alone does not open it.

概览

Pixel的路线包含未来多处地点的到访、三次交心对话和两次可选约会。后续私人活动与普通交谈、巧克力礼物分别计算。

去哪里找

普通头像每天10:00—17:59在优化城市干道Alpha-7；完成未优化变量后，每天19:00—21:59还会到作战中心，白天头像仍保留。

路线与关系进展

先到访进行记忆泄漏，后续自动事件会换地点，应按表中地点前往。退回寄件人要求主线31；接下来的阶段要求恋爱中（3,000），到作战中心继续。

交心对话顺序为装满明天的口袋 → 当杂讯占上风时 → 有意创造的记忆，各自所需前置事件见表。两次约会都不是自动事件链的必经步骤。

二人时光要求浪漫感（800）、远程访问终止、有意创造的记忆及对应权限。在任一实际头像处开始，花费90分钟、25精力，每天一次，两处共用次数。完整结束给予50基础好感和50玩家情趣经验，NPC亲密经验另算。

值得铭记的事需最终个人事件完成、Pixel已加入军团及反抗军声望25。在作战中心晚间头像开始，不消耗时间或声望，一次性给予老兵记忆核心。

偏好的属性为运气、魅力、创造力。学会即兴发挥可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 路线事件可能在诊所、强化中心、神殿、档案馆或作战中心，不一定在普通头像处。
  • 二人时光除浪漫感与前置事件外，还要求第三次交心对话。
  • 在街道和作战中心之间移动，不会重置二人时光每日次数。
  • 军团礼物还要求招募和反抗军声望，只有普通好感不够。
