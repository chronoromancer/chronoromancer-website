概览

Pixel 是一个充满活力的网络女性 最佳城市闪光灯 她现在的个人路线包括: 11次自动碰头, 三颗心对心, 两个日期每天谈和巧克力,一个 临时课程,以后则 招募抵抗军.
本指南在将她的过去和路线的启示留给游戏的同时给出了可玩性的要求和奖励选择.

Overview

Pixel is the energetic cybernetic woman found along the Optimized Urban Thoroughfare in the Future era. Her current personal route includes eleven automatic encounters, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, daily Talk and Chocolate, an Improvisation lesson, and later Resistance army recruitment.
This guide gives the playable requirements and reward choices while leaving her past and the route's revelations to the game.

Finding Pixel and Starting Her Route

Visit Optimized Urban Thoroughfare, 10 AM–6 PM, for Memory Leak, her once-only introduction. It has no affection-stage or numbered main-story prerequisite. Her ordinary street portrait also follows this daily window.
Continue the personal automatic chain by building the listed stage and visiting its next location. Several scenes occur away from her ordinary street portrait: Cid's Underground Modification Clinic, the Human Performance Optimization Lab in the Fringe Zone, the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple, and the Modified Historical Archive Facility.
After The Unoptimized Variable, Pixel also appears at Operations Center, 7–10 PM. This is an additional portrait, rather than a replacement for the street schedule. Its menu offers Talk and the Improvisation lesson; Chocolate, dates and the three H2Hs remain street actions.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Complete Memory Leak on the street, then use Talk to reach Acknowledged for Walking Pattern Violation and Known for Notes to Tomorrow. At Known, add Chocolate to the daily routine.
  2. At Acquaintance, visit Underground Modification Clinic for Read-Only Eye.
  3. At Friend, complete Pocket Full of Tomorrow from her street portrait. Visit the Fringe Human Performance Optimization Lab for Optimization Candidate. Her first date and paid Improvisation lesson also open at Friend.
  4. At Close Friend, return to the street for The Missing Hour, then visit Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple, 9 AM–6 PM, for Not Divine Enough. Complete When the Static Wins, after Pocket Full of Tomorrow.
  5. At Crush, visit Modified Historical Archive Facility for Disposed on Paper. Complete the street action Chosen Moment, whose conversation is titled A Memory Made on Purpose, after When the Static Wins. Her second date also opens at Crush.
  6. At Romantic, return to Underground Modification Clinic for No More Remote Access.
  7. At Intimate, with Story Beat 31 complete, visit the street 10 AM–6 PM for Return to Sender.
  8. At In Love, enter Operations Center for The Unoptimized Variable, finishing the personal chain and acquiring Pixel for the Resistance army.
The automatic chain does not additionally require her dates or H2Hs. Those have their own prerequisites for playing all of Pixel's content. The H2Hs are one-time conversations; completing the third does not replace the In Love finale.

Daily Talk and Chocolate

Talk to Pixel takes 15 minutes and awards one actual base point, despite the action's zero-point field. There are six positive uses daily, shared between her street and later Operations Center portrait.
At Known, give Chocolate from the street portrait for 10 minutes, one Chocolate and four base affection, up to four positive gifts per game day. There is no additional personal-scene gate for this gift action.
Six talks and four Chocolates take 130 minutes, consume four Chocolates, and give 22 base affection before shared bonuses. Her street window easily accommodates that routine when other visits and dates leave enough time. Changing locations does not reset Talk's shared daily count.
Over-limit conversations or gifts can lose affection under the shared escalating annoyance rule. A gifted Chocolate is consumed as the action cost even if the response is annoyed. She has no ordinary Flowers, Wine, Book, Teddy or Jewelry gift action here.

Learn Improvisation

At Friend, with Creativity 16 and paid access, select Improvisation from her street portrait, or her later Operations Center portrait. It costs 150 minutes, $150, 30 Energy and 10 Calm and grants the player Improvisation, plus 10 base Pixel affection.
The lesson disappears once the skill is learned. It has no additional H2H, date or personal-story gate at the street portrait. For the Operations Center version, Pixel's later portrait must first be unlocked and present 7–10 PM.
The current NPC lesson's actual stat gate is Creativity 16. The general skill catalogue's different threshold is not a second check performed by this lesson. Improvisation then improves ordinary affection rewards with Pixel through her special-skill multiplier; learning it does not itself recruit her army unit.

Resistance Army Recruitment

Complete The Unoptimized Variable at Operations Center, at In Love, after Return to Sender. The live completion handler acquires Pixel for the Resistance army. There is no additional H2H completion, cash purchase, crafted gift or equipment ownership requirement for that acquisition.
The scene awards 12 fixed base affection, plus its reply bonus; Funny adds three for the best 15 base total. Her hero acquisition and the new evening Operations Center portrait follow the completed finale. An EXTRAS replay does not acquire another unit.
Use the Barracks roster and army equipment systems for her recruited hero. Pixel's hero progression is distinct from ordinary affection and her player Improvisation lesson. The base unit has Health 66, Power 3, Accuracy 80, range 310 and attack speed 1.4, before equipment and hero training. Its deployment cost is 15 Ammo and 40 Command, zero Salvage; recruiting the character is not itself a paid deployment.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Next personal encounter missing: complete the preceding personal flag, meet its relationship stage and visit its own location and hours. Standing at Pixel's street portrait does not trigger clinic, archive or temple scenes.
  • Temple scene missing: finish The Missing Hour, reach Close Friend and visit Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple, 9 AM–6 PM.
  • Chosen Moment missing: finish Disposed on Paper and When the Static Wins, reach Crush and use the street portrait. Its internal conversation title is A Memory Made on Purpose.
  • Date unavailable late in the evening: use the actual 4–6 PM / noon–6 PM street windows; Operations Center has no date action.
  • Lesson missing: check Friend, Creativity 16, paid access and not already knowing Improvisation.
  • Return to Sender missing: check No More Remote Access, Intimate and Story Beat 31, then street 10 AM–6 PM.
  • Operations Center portrait missing: finish The Unoptimized Variable, then return 7–10 PM. The finale itself does not require that portrait to exist beforehand.
  • Army entry missing after only dating her: complete the In Love finale; the dates and H2Hs alone do not recruit her.
Her current route has no separate ordinary apartment invitation, intimacy movie, pregnancy continuation, Kupie album, venue-invite minigame, numbered brothel-worker training, resort vacation or personal Christmas scene. The eleven automatic scenes, three H2Hs, two dates, daily actions, lesson and army finale are the authored personal route covered here.
