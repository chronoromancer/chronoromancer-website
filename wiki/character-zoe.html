概览和时间表

Zoe是现时代20岁的计算机科学学生,有Good coording.她的路线从Lab Rats事件开始,用Master Lilu的Dojo事件替代4个计算机场景,然后打开普通的友谊动作,日期和后来的私人场景.这种走过让力学没有透露第四个计算机场景的发现或个人结果.
地点 可用性
大学计算机实验室 上午9时5分,每天
横行 下午5点到8点每天在Diane之后建造
最喜欢的数据 : Technology50,智能50和魅力60她特殊的关系技巧是 量子计算。当已经学习到时,它会改善感情;Zoe没有教它的课程,即目前配置的一次性玩家-按部就班的奖励、隔离或工人-招聘路线。
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

密室 从 Zoe 开始 计算机实验室肖像,9上午5时,与 1 000个感情、付费访问和高级权力。这需要 60分钟45 能源图像 50个真爱 通过处理器,和记录 25 NPC传教经验. 它每天有一个视频使用,没有额外的钱或Calm成本。它的动作卡列出零点,但实际操作者给出50。

解决问题

  • Zoe在场,但Talk/赠品缺失: 完成所有四个计算机场景和中间Lilu事件。
  • 缺少第一个计算机动作 : 在Rachel Acquaintance完成 Lab Rats 并学习系统黑客.
  • 缺少下一个计算机动作 : 完成相应的下城道场景,并满足Lilu的Acquaintance/Friend/Crush门.
  • 缺少的日期 : 大部分需要"来清洁";接触Zoe Acquaintance,并在第四幕之后重新访问实验室.
  • 农贸市场早上8点缺: Zoe的实验室肖像开始9 AM.
  • 缺少证书日期 : 找到朋友,持有一张保险券,检查怀孕状况及其一次性完成情况。
  • 缺少棋盘日期 : 建立 Diane 的棋盘行走, 访问 5–8 PM, 完成到来清洁, 联系好朋友并检查付费接入/ 更高电源 。
  • 密室丢失 : 在她画像出现时 访问实验室

Overview and Schedule

Zoe is a20-year-old computer science student in the Present era, with Good alignment. Her route starts with the Lab Rats event, alternates four computer scenes with Master Lilu's dojo events, then opens ordinary friendship actions, dates and later private scenes. This walkthrough gives mechanics without revealing the fourth computer scene's discovery or personal outcomes.
Location Availability
University Computer Lab 9 AM–5 PM,every day
Boardwalk 5–8 PM,every day,after Diane builds it
Favorite stats: Technology50,Intelligence50 and Charm60. Her special relationship skill is Quantum Computing. It improves affection when already learned; Zoe has no lesson teaching it,one-time player-stat reward,apartment or worker-recruitment route currently configured.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Main Route: Lab and Dojo Visits in Order

  1. Reach Rachel Acquaintance+, then visit the Computer Lab for Lab Rats. There is no extra Zoe affection gate. Defending Zoe gives3 base;defusing gives1 each to Zoe/Rachel;siding with Rachel givesRachel3 and no Zoe reward;staying out gives neither. Complete the event to unlock the first computer action.
  2. Learn System Hacking. At Zoe's lab portrait, use Hack Zoe's Computer, first scene. It requires Lab Rats completed and takes 20 minutes and20 Calm, with no money or Energy cost.
  3. Reach Master Lilu Acquaintance+, then visit the Downtown Dojo for Zoe's First Lesson, after the first computer scene. It givesZoe3 completion+2 from any response=5 base.
  4. Return to the lab for the second computer scene, after the first scene and First Lesson.
  5. Reach Master Lilu Friend+, then revisit the Downtown Dojo for Zoe's Second Wind, after the second computer scene and First Lesson. Any response givesZoe4+3=7 base.
  6. Return to the lab for the third computer scene, after the second scene and Second Wind.
  7. Reach Master Lilu Crush+, then revisit the Downtown Dojo for Zoe Stands Her Ground, after the third scene and Second Wind. Caring/Funny giveZoe5+4=9 base;Flirty gives8.
  8. Return to the lab for the fourth computer scene, after the third scene and Stands Her Ground. Completing it opens Talk and gifts.
  9. Reach Zoe Acquaintance+, then revisit the lab for Coming Clean. Any response gives5 completion+3 choice=8 base and unlocks most dates.
  10. At Zoe Romantic+, after Coming Clean, return to the lab for Through the Screen. Any response gives10+3=13 base;its completion records 20 NPC masturbation experience.
Each computer action has the same name,requires System Hacking,costs20 minutes/20 Calm and completes once. Each gives5 completion+2 from any response=7 base. Their prerequisites control which entry appears; wait for the next dojo event rather than repeating a completed computer scene. All four have only a Stranger affection gate.
Despite their internal h2h names, these computer scenes use the plot system: they award their listed choice/completion rewards, rather than the regular conversation system's Romantic-skill completion bonus. The dojo scenes configure no extra Zoe affection gate or separate time window beyond visiting the location; their Lilu affection gates are essential. See Master Lilu's guide for his own progression.

Troubleshooting

  • Zoe is present but Talk/gifts are missing: finish all four computer scenes and the intervening Lilu events.
  • First computer action missing: finish Lab Rats at Rachel Acquaintance and learn System Hacking.
  • Next computer action missing: finish the corresponding Downtown Dojo scene and meet Lilu's Acquaintance/Friend/Crush gate.
  • Dates missing: most require Coming Clean;reach Zoe Acquaintance and revisit the lab after the fourth scene.
  • Farmers Market missing at8 AM: Zoe's lab portrait starts9 AM.
  • Voucher date missing: reach Friend,hold a Voucher and check pregnancy status and its one-time completion.
  • Boardwalk date missing: build Diane's boardwalk,visit5–8 PM,finish Coming Clean,reach Close Friend and check paid access/Higher Power.
  • Secret Room missing: reach1,000 affection with paid access/Higher Power and visit the lab while her portrait is present.
