概览

Talena是 目前下城乐队的贝斯手她的路线从音乐工作室的乐队影响力开始,然后通过房间谈话和礼物,个人场景,摇滚乐队之夜, 靠近点 和可重复的, 亲密的晚间 以四支为支.

解决问题

  • 工作室里没有谈话: 使用她的房间,到达确认 并参观它的肖像窗。
  • 她的介绍没有出现: 建立Band Clut 10 并重温工作室。
  • 摇滚乐队之夜不见了: 联系朋友,请她下午6点到9点;完成后就消失。
  • 附近没有了 : 到达Crush和Band clut 35,然后到8–11号房间。
  • 紧紧的晚间, 请检查drum和Bass Jam的准确得分; 另一首歌曲的最佳或无轨的练习模式不算.
  • 缺少分支选择 : 创意需要创意80,魅力需要魅力80,保定需要情感保定,野兽续作需要野兽30.
  • 每天的访问再次出现: 其名义上限不由当前处理器记录;可重复的可用性受时间表和资源的限制。
  • 玩家体验增加,但没有解锁画廊: 这些是单独的计数器;她目前的公寓数据没有NPC的亲密性奖励.

Overview

Talena is the bassist in the Present Downtown band. Her route starts with Band clout at the Music Studio, then continues through room conversations and gifts, personal scenes, Rockband Night, Closer and a repeatable Intimate Evening with four branches.

Band Progression and Recommended Order

  1. Build Band clout 10 through the Music Studio's scored Practice Drums sessions. The action costs 25 minutes, 20 Energy and $30, with a noon–11 PM action window.
  2. Visit the Studio at clout 10 for The Bassist's Nod, an automatic once-only scene giving 10 base Talena affection.
  3. Visit her room noon–2 PM or 6–11 PM for Just Around. Choose “Wanted to see you. That's it.” for 4 choice +5 completion =9 base. Appreciative gives 7 total; Casual 6.
  4. Use room Talk and gifts as stages rise. What It Means is available at Known; On Her Own at Friend. Add Rockband Night if desired.
  5. Raise Band clout to 35, reach Crush and complete Closer at 8–11 PM.
  6. Record at least 80% best accuracy on Drum and Bass Jam, in normal or eligible Chill mode, for Intimate Evening at 8–11 PM after Closer.
The song menu unlocks Drum and Bass Jam at Band clout 30. Its recorded best accuracy, rather than general band reputation or another song's score, is the apartment gate. Keep Up at clout 26 does not replace playing the song.

Intimate Evening Unlock and Frequency

Use Intimate Evening at Talena's room 8–11 PM, at Crush, after Closer, and with Drum and Bass Jam normal best accuracy or Chill best at least 80%. It costs 120 minutes and 50 Energy, with no extra money, item, paid-version or higher-power requirement.
The action declares a daily limit of 1, but its current handler and apartment completion do not record that daily action counter. The repeatable conversation therefore remains available within the window while resources allow; the nominal limit is not a reliably enforced once-per-day restriction in the current code.
Choose one branch at the initial path menu. Its later rounds follow that branch rather than returning to a repeatable activity hub.

Troubleshooting

  • Talk is missing at the Studio: use her room, reach Acknowledged and visit its portrait windows.
  • Her introduction has not appeared: build Band clout 10 and revisit the Studio.
  • Rockband Night is missing: reach Friend and invite her 6–9 PM; it disappears after completion.
  • Closer is missing: reach Crush and Band clout 35, then visit the room 8–11 PM.
  • Intimate Evening is missing after Closer: check the exact Drum and Bass Jam score; another song's best or untracked Practice mode does not count.
  • A branch choice is missing: Creative needs Creativity80, Charm needs Charm80, Bonding needs Emotional Bonding and Beast continuation needs Beast30.
  • The daily visit appears again: its nominal limit is not recorded by the current handler; repeatable availability is bounded by the schedule and resources.
  • Player experience increased but no gallery unlocked: these are separate counters; her current apartment data has no NPC intimacy reward.
