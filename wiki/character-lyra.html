概览

Lyra 是百合的商家,在 中世纪村庄的村市场她的路线结合了普通的友谊、商业课、与Gem的共享供货交流、两个个人心对心和灯笼市场日期,她还出现在一个与Amelia的护理场景、四个战斗机协会合同、一个阿莱豪斯表演反应和一个节日活动。
这种走过让当前的需求和有益的选择,同时将她的个人历史和场景分辨率留给游戏.

Overview

Lyra is the centaur merchant at the Village Market in the Medieval Village Outskirts. Her route combines ordinary friendship, a Commerce lesson, shared supply-run encounters with Gem, two personal Heart-to-Hearts and a lantern-market date. She also appears in a care scene with Amelia, four Fighters Guild contracts, an Alehouse performance reaction and a holiday activity.
This walkthrough gives the current requirements and useful choices while leaving her personal history and the scenes' resolutions to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Meet Lyra at Village Market, 6 AM–9 PM. Talk up to five times daily, and give Flowers at Acknowledged, up to three times daily.
  2. At Known and Communication 42, claim Market Wisdom once for Commerce +1 and 8 direct affection.
  3. Complete Gem's Mining training scene, reach Gem Friend and Lyra Known, then visit Village Market, 7 AM–5 PM, for Short Legs, Long Road.
  4. At Lyra Friend, after the supply run, complete Market Routes from her market portrait. Separately, visit Mountain Foothills, 7 AM–6 PM, with Gem Friend and Lyra Friend, for The High Road.
  5. At Friend, optionally visit Village Market, 8 AM–8 PM, for The Crimson Bull, while Lyra is not vacationing; and Village Healer's Hut, 8 AM–7 PM, for Specialized Care, after Amelia's introduction at Amelia Acknowledged.
  6. Reach Crush, finish Market Routes, and choose Quiet Stride, Village Market, 6–9 PM.
  7. Reach Romantic, finish Quiet Stride, and choose Lantern Market, Village Market, 6–9 PM. This date completes once.
  8. Her Caravan Defense contracts, Alehouse reaction and holiday activity remain separate content, with their own requirements below. They are useful affection sources but are not extra romance gates.

Where to Find Her

Her ordinary portrait is in Village Market, Village Outskirts, 6 AM–9 PM every day. Talk, Flowers, Market Wisdom, both H2Hs and Lantern Market start there. The market's building hours match her regular portrait window.
Shared scenes at the Healer's Hut, Mountain Foothills, Caravan Rest Stop and Rowdy Alehouse use their own locations and hours. They do not add an ordinary portrait menu for Lyra there, and her market schedule is not an extra gate on those scenes.

Market Wisdom

At her market portrait, reach Known and Communication 42, then choose Market Wisdom. It completes once, takes 30 minutes, and gives Commerce +1 and 8 direct Lyra affection, with no configured money, Energy or consumable cost.
The affection is added directly rather than multiplied. The lesson does not teach Animal Care and does not require a Gem scene, guild contract, H2H or date.

Four Caravan Defense Contracts

These are Fighters Guild contracts, separate from the personal romance. Use Quest Board at Fighters Guild in Kings Demesne, 10 AM–8 PM, choose Accept Contract, then complete the active contract and return to Claim Reward. The board itself takes zero minutes.
Contracts advance in order when their reward is claimed. Finish and claim contracts 1–3 before accepting 4; 5–8 before 9; 10–13 before 14; and 15–18 before 19. Reaching a higher guild rank or previously clearing a caravan scene does not skip the intervening contracts.
With the matching caravan contract active, visit Caravan Rest Stop, Village Outskirts, 9 PM–midnight. Its scene triggers automatically, even though Lyra's normal market portrait is absent. There is no Lyra-affection gate on these four encounters.

Alehouse Performance Reaction

At Rowdy Alehouse in Kings Demesne, 8 PM–midnight or midnight–3 AM, choose Perform Song after composing at least one song. A performance takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy, with one performance per game day at this venue, before buffs.
A performance can trigger one of five randomly selected Alehouse reactions. The overall reaction chance starts at 25%, rises by 5 percentage points for each of Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity and Storytelling, and another 5 for owning a Lute, capped at 50%. Lyra's reaction is one possible selection; you cannot choose her directly.
If selected, her reaction gives 8 base completion affection and Caring +4, Funny +1 or Flirty +2. Caring gives the highest total, 12 base affection. The reaction is repeatable on later performances, but the venue's once-daily performance allowance still applies.
This reaction has no Lyra-affection or portrait-presence requirement. Owning a Lute and the named performance skills improves the reaction chance but is not mandatory. Ordinary performance gold, valuables and bard-clout rewards are separate from her reaction affection.

Holiday Antlers Activity

During the active Christmas Village task event, choose Give Reindeer Antlers as a Village Market location activity, 6 AM–9 PM, at Lyra Known. This is Task 13, completing once for 25 minutes, one holiday Token and 10 direct Lyra affection.
Despite the activity's name, it configures no inventory-antlers requirement, consumable deduction, money cost, H2H gate or paid-version requirement. It gives no NPC intimacy or player Erotic experience. It is separate from ordinary Flowers and the personal romance.

Buying Market Supplies

The separate Village Market location activity Buy Supplies takes 20 minutes and 18 gold, restores 5 Health, and supplies one food unit and one common ingredient. It does not award Lyra affection or require her friendship. Market shopping does not replace Talk, gifts or the Gem supply-run encounter.

Troubleshooting

  • Portrait missing: check Village Market, 6 AM–9 PM. Other scenes do not move her ordinary portrait there.
  • Flowers missing: reach Acknowledged, carry Flowers and use her market portrait. The normal allowance is three daily.
  • Market Wisdom missing: check Known, Communication 42 and whether the once-only reward was already claimed.
  • Supply run missing: finish Gem's Mining training scene, reach Gem Friend and Lyra Known, and visit Village Market, 7 AM–5 PM.
  • Market Routes missing: reach Friend and complete the supply run. The High Road is not required.
  • Specialized Care missing: finish Amelia's introduction, reach Amelia Acknowledged and Lyra Friend, then visit Healer's Hut, 8 AM–7 PM, while Lyra is not vacationing.
  • Quiet Stride missing: reach Crush, complete Market Routes, and start 6–9 PM.
  • Lantern date missing: reach Romantic, finish Quiet Stride, and check the 6–9 PM window and its costs.
  • Caravan scene missing: accept the matching contract 4, 9, 14 or 19, then visit Rest Stop, 9 PM–midnight. The previous contracts must be claimed in order.
  • Next contract does not appear after a caravan victory: return to Quest Board, 10 AM–8 PM, and Claim Reward.
  • No Alehouse reaction: compose a song and use Perform Song during Alehouse hours. Reaction selection is random and may feature another NPC.
  • Antlers activity missing: check the active holiday event, Lyra Known and Village Market hours. It is a location activity.
