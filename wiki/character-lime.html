Overview

Lime is recruited by passing the Wizard initiation test, then moves to the Maid Quarters. Talking at the tower does not replace that test.

Where to find them

Route and relationship milestones

Reach 50 Wizard Clout, Gandon Acquaintance (50) and Blinky Known (25). Ask Gandon for the initiation in the Wizard’s Interior from 10:00–20:00; it takes 60 minutes. Clout is the entry threshold, not a payment.

The five questions require at least 17 of 25 points. Passing recruits Lime, gives 1 Crystal (3 total at a perfect 25), 10 Wizard Clout and direct affection: Lime +50, Gandon +25, Sashina +15 and Aziza +15. A failed attempt can be retried after midnight.

After recruitment, her stat lesson is available in the Maid Quarters. Maid Service at Intimate (1600) is a repeatable 30-minute H2H with a 15-point base conversation reward; the Romantic skill can add its normal conversation bonus. Equipment and work effects belong to the Maid management guide.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Intelligence 45; Mystic 40; Perception 35

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Before recruitment, look at the tower 05:00–10:00. After recruitment, use Maid Quarters 18:00–22:00.
  • For the test, Gandon and Blinky have explicit relationship requirements; there is no additional Sashina or Aziza affection gate.

路线与关系节点

先取得 50 巫师声望，并使 Gandon 达到相识（50）、Blinky 达到已知（25），再于 10:00–20:00 在巫师塔内部向 Gandon 申请入门考验，耗时 60 分钟。声望是进入门槛，不会扣除。

五道题共 25 分，至少 17 分通过。通过后招募 Lime，获得 1 水晶（满分 25 时共 3 水晶）、10 巫师声望，并直接增加 Lime 50、Gandon 25、Sashina 15、Aziza 15 好感度。失败后可在午夜过后重试。

招募后的属性课程位于女仆宿舍。亲密（1600）开放的女仆服务是可重复的 30 分钟谈心，对话基础奖励为 15 好感度；浪漫技能还可提供其常规谈心加成。装备与工作效果见女仆管理指南。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。

智力 45; 神秘 40; 感知 35

偏好技能：占卜

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择还可能在事件完成基础奖励之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

  • 招募前请在 05:00–10:00 去巫师塔，招募后改到女仆宿舍 18:00–22:00 探访。
  • 考验明确检查 Gandon 与 Blinky 的关系，没有额外的 Sashina 或 Aziza 好感度门槛。
