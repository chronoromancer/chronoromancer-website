Lime — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Lime is a shy, magic-minded maid in the Medieval era. Before recruitment, she works in the Wizard's Interior early in the morning. Passing Wizard Initiation recruits her as maid #4 and moves her portrait to Maids Quarters in the evening.
Her current content includes recruitment, daily conversation, a permanent Mystic lesson, a repeatable illustrated Maid Service and ten shared automatic scenes. This guide explains their requirements and rewards while leaving the maid sisterhood's later discoveries to the game.

Recruiting Lime

Visit Gandon's portrait in the Wizard's Interior, 10 AM–8 PM. His Wizard Initiation action requires:
* Gandon: Acquaintance or higher.
* Blinky: Known or higher.
* Wizard faction clout: at least 50, for the action to appear.
* Lime must not already be recruited.
* You must not have failed the test earlier that game day.
The action takes 60 minutes, with no configured money, Energy or Calm cost. Lime's own affection is not an entry requirement, and her morning portrait does not need to be present. Standard thresholds are 25 for Known and 50 for Acquaintance.

Test Scoring

The opening answer does not contribute to the score. The five visions each award 1–5 points. 17–24 passes; 25 earns the larger crystal reward. These answers give 5 points each:

Passing and Retrying

A successful attempt awards:
* Lime's recruitment and 50 direct affection with Lime.
* 25 direct affection with Gandon.
* 15 direct affection each with Sashina and Aziza.
* 10 Wizard clout.
* 1 crystal for 17–24, or 3 crystals for 25.
Recruitment updates the Maids Quarters level to the number of acquired maids, capped at six. Lime then moves to Maids Quarters, 6 PM–10 PM. The recruitment reward alone reaches standard Acquaintance from zero.
Failure gives no success rewards and blocks another attempt until the next game day. The score resets after the check. Passing blocks the portrait action because Lime is acquired. Extras replay does not recruit her or award these rewards again.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Build Gandon Acquaintance, Blinky Known and 50 Wizard clout. Morning Talks with Lime are optional.
  2. Take Wizard Initiation from Gandon during 10 AM–8 PM. Score at least 17; the five answers above give 25.
  3. Visit Maids Quarters, 6 PM–10 PM. Lime's Arrival can trigger when Elara is available and not vacationing.
  4. Use Talk up to five times each day. Before and after recruitment, the handler gives 1 base affection per successful talk.
  5. At Known with Communication 45, claim Spiritual Awareness once for +1 permanent Mystic and 10 direct affection. Recruitment alone already clears Known.
  6. Recruit other relevant maids and raise their relationships for the shared scenes below. Their own hour windows can differ from Lime's evening portrait schedule.
  7. At Intimate, open Maid Service in her evening portrait. Finish its single response for 15 base affection, before modifiers and any learned Romantic skill bonus.
Completing all shared scenes is not a requirement for Maid Service. Its personal gate is Intimate, rather than a chain of earlier scenes.

Base cleaning contributes to the combined daily calculation before equipment, apprentices and its random modifier. It is not a guaranteed personal daily affection or XP reward.
Lime's Burning Passion is her exclusive epic maid equipment. While equipped, it gives +25% Lime cleaning experience, +20% experience for all recruited maids and +10% combined maid cleaning. Obtain it by completing and claiming Willow Maxed in Kupie's minigame, requiring Willow's tenth minigame date. It is equipment, rather than a personal gift or Maid Service reward.

Current Route and Troubleshooting

* Wizard Initiation missing: check 50 Wizard clout, Gandon's 10 AM–8 PM schedule, Gandon Acquaintance, Blinky Known, today's failed-test flag and whether Lime is already recruited.
* Lime missing at the tower: before recruitment she appears only 5 AM–10 AM. Gandon administers the test later, during his own schedule.
* Lime missing after recruitment: use her evening portrait in Maids Quarters, 6 PM–10 PM. Her morning tower portrait is removed by recruitment.
* Talk differs from an older guide: the handler gives 1 base point at either location and shares a five-talk allowance.
* Shared scene missing: check every maid's recruitment and relationship, the room, its hours and prior completion. The Onyx scenes require their named earlier scenes.
* Maid Service missing: Lime must be recruited, available in her evening portrait and Intimate. Shared scenes, Divination and Spiritual Awareness are not additional gates.
* Cleaning level unchanged after conversations: Talk and Maid Service give affection, not maid cleaning experience.
Lime currently has no separate personal date, apartment conversation, Kupie romance entry or pregnancy route. Her minigame equipment reward does not make her a Kupie date character. This guide covers her recruitment, personal actions and all ten registered shared scenes.
