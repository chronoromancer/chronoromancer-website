Kyou at a glance

Kyou is the sharp-tongued, caring park attendant at the Artificial Nature Preserve in the Future Fringe Zone. Get to know her through daily conversation, ten personal events and three Heart-to-Hearts. Three optional dates offer more time together along the way.

Where and when to meet her

Travel to Future → Fringe Zone → Artificial Nature Preserve and open Kyou’s portrait. She is there every day from 09:00 until 22:00. Visit between 09:00 and 16:59 for her first event, Please Do Not Feed the Attendant. You can start at Stranger without a prior character introduction or a relationship requirement.

The Fringe Zone park is her home location. Check the district when planning your visit: the Future has other artificial green spaces with different names.

Daily visits, dates and private time

Talk is available from Stranger: 15 minutes, 1 base affection, up to five times per day. Give Flowers opens at Known: 10 minutes, one Flowers, 8 base affection, up to twice per day. Their allowances are separate.

Her preferred stats are Perception 45, Charm 40 and Ability 40. Empathy improves her affection gains. The affection figures above are base rewards before those and other relationship bonuses.

Private Time

After I’ll Be Here and the completed Come Here, Then conversation, the full version offers Private Time at the park. Start between 09:00 and 16:59, at Romantic or higher. A visit costs 90 minutes and 25 Energy, with no Wealth cost, once per day. Completing either continuation gives 50 base affection and 50 Erotic Experience. Scene images become available in EXTRAS as you encounter them.

先认识 Kyou

Kyou 是未来边缘区人工自然保护区的管理员，嘴上犀利，却很关心来访的人。通过日常交谈、十段个人事件与三段谈心，你能逐渐和她熟悉起来。沿途还有三场可自由安排的约会。

到哪里、几点找她

前往未来 → 边缘区 → 人工自然保护区，点开 Kyou 的头像。她每天 09:00–21:59都在这里。想开始路线，请在09:00–16:59进入公园，触发《请勿投喂管理员》；初次认识时即可开始，不用先完成其他角色的介绍。

她所在的是边缘区的公园。规划行程时可以先确认地区，避免把未来其他名字相近的绿地当成同一个地点。

关系提升后，在表中时段离开并重新进入公园，就能检查下一段事件。关系阶段后的数字是通常所需的好感点数。

交谈、约会与独处时光

「交谈」从陌生人阶段即可使用：15 分钟、1 点基础好感，每天最多五次。达到已知（Known，25 点）后开放「送花」：10 分钟、1 鲜花、8 点基础好感，每天最多两次。交谈与送花的次数分别计算。

她偏好的属性是感知 45、魅力 40、能力 40，学会共情也能提高好感收益。上面的好感数值是基础值，实际获得量还会受到关系加成影响。

三场可选约会

从她在公园的个人菜单发出邀请。每场约会只能完成一次，到达相应阶段就能分别开放；不必完成这些约会，也能继续推进十段个人事件。

独处时光

完成《我会在这里》以及谈心「那就过来吧」后，完整版可在公园选择「独处时光」。关系须维持在浪漫感（Romantic）或以上，并在09:00–16:59开始。每次消耗90 分钟、25 精力，不花费财富，每天一次。完成任一后续可获得50 点基础好感与 50 情趣经验，遇到的场景图片也会逐步收录到 EXTRAS。
