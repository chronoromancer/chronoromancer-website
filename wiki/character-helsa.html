Overview

Helsa is a vampire hunter whose investigation in the Present Era crosses Torea's route. Her playable content includes a gated introduction, daily conversations at four locations, two dates, three Heart-to-Hearts and a shared story chain. Her background and the hunt's outcomes are left for you to discover.

Unlocking Helsa

  1. Complete Story Beat 61, then reach Torea Acknowledged. Visit Torea's Room, 7–10 PM, for The Hunt in Neon.
  2. After that scene, visit the Downtown Back Alley, midnight–3 AM, for Torea's The Scent of Silver.
  3. Return to the Downtown Back Alley, 1–4 AM, after The Scent of Silver for The Hunt Arrives. It starts automatically once and awards 10 base Helsa points and 3 base Torea points. Caring or Flirty adds 4 Helsa points; Funny adds 3.
  4. The Hunt Arrives unlocks Helsa's ordinary school, library and late-night alley schedules. Use Talk up to three times per game day to continue building affection.
Her introduction has no Helsa affection-stage requirement. Do not wait for her ordinary evening portrait to appear before it: the initial 1–4 AM story window is separate from her later 8 PM–midnight alley schedule. All three early scenes complete once, and their events are left undisclosed.

Recommended Relationship Walkthrough

  1. After The Hunt Arrives, reach Helsa Acknowledged and visit University Hall, 9 AM–1 PM on an odd-numbered day, for A Student of Monsters. Caring or Flirty gives the best Helsa reward: 10 + 5 = 15 base points. Flirty also gives Torea's best +5 choice bonus.
  2. Reach Helsa Friend, then choose Comic Con Date from her University Hall menu during 9 AM–1 PM on an odd-numbered day. It requires A Student of Monsters completed, costs 120 minutes, $120 and 40 energy, and restores 5 calm.
  3. On an even-numbered day, 10 AM–1 PM, visit the Uptown City Library and choose The Hunter I Chose, after Comic Con. It requires Friend and costs 75 minutes and 15 energy, restoring 5 calm.
  4. After that Heart-to-Heart, return to the library, 10 AM–2 PM on an even-numbered day, for the post-convention library scene, while Helsa is Friend. This guide uses a neutral label for its revealing in-game title. Its completion gives 14 base points, with Flirty +8 as the best response.
  5. Progress Torea to Friend, complete her Fair Date and The Shape of Appetite Heart-to-Heart, and keep Helsa at Friend. Visit Torea's Room, 7–10 PM, after Helsa's library scene for Loose the Cat. It also checks The Hunter I Chose completed.
  6. After Loose the Cat, visit the Vampire Lounge, 6–10 PM, for Teeth With Ribbons, and the Downtown Back Alley, 9 PM–midnight, for A Hunter Bleeds. Both are needed for the later shared route; A Hunter Bleeds unlocks Helsa's morning Housing Block schedule.
  7. At Helsa Close Friend, choose The Long Pursuit in the Back Alley, 9 PM–midnight, after A Hunter Bleeds and The Hunter I Chose. It costs 90 minutes and 20 energy, restoring 5 calm.
  8. After The Long Pursuit, choose Stargazing Date at the Back Alley, 8–10 PM, with Helsa Close Friend and A Hunter Bleeds completed. It costs 120 minutes, $50 and 25 energy, restoring 10 calm.
  9. After Stargazing Date, Teeth With Ribbons and The Long Pursuit, visit the Back Alley, 9 PM–midnight, while Helsa is Close Friend for The Arrow She Does Not Loose.
  10. Progress Torea to Close Friend, complete her A Larger Night Heart-to-Heart and Boardwalk After Sunset date, then visit Torea's Room, 7–10 PM, after A Hunter Bleeds for Blood Owed, Blood Chosen. Becky must currently be available through her own route.
  11. With both Torea and Helsa Close Friend, visit the Back Alley, 9 PM–midnight, after Blood Owed, Blood Chosen and The Arrow She Does Not Loose for Five Minutes of Night. It checks A Larger Night and The Long Pursuit completed.
  12. At Helsa Crush, choose No Weapon Between Us at the Housing Block, 6–9 AM, after Five Minutes of Night and The Long Pursuit. It costs 90 minutes and 15 energy, restoring 15 calm.
  13. Reach Helsa Romantic, then return to the Housing Block, 6–9 AM, after Five Minutes of Night and No Weapon Between Us, for The Hunter Who Returns. It awards 30 base points, with Flirty +13 as its best response, for 43 base points total.
Torea's two early required activities are Fair Date and The Shape of Appetite; her separate Voucher fair outing does not replace the Fair Date completion flag. A Larger Night is the displayed name of her later required Heart-to-Heart. Complete the named activities rather than relying on similarly named older walkthrough entries.

Talk and Relationship Rewards

Talk to Helsa costs 20 minutes and records 1 base point on each of the first three daily uses. Further attempts record −1 and use the shared over-limit handling. Her dialogue varies by location, but the daily allowance and relationship are shared.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.
Her high favorite-stat thresholds improve applicable rewards; they are not prerequisites for meeting her, taking the dates or starting her Heart-to-Hearts. Tactical Awareness is her relationship bonus skill; she does not teach it through a personal lesson.

Troubleshooting

  • Helsa never appears: complete Torea's The Scent of Silver and return to the Back Alley during 1–4 AM for The Hunt Arrives.
  • Wrong daytime location: odd day = University Hall; even day = Uptown Library.
  • The Hunter I Chose missing at 1 PM: its start window ends at 1 PM, one hour before her library portrait's 2 PM end.
  • Housing Block portrait missing: complete A Hunter Bleeds first; morning affection alone does not unlock it.
  • The Long Pursuit missing: reach Close Friend and complete both A Hunter Bleeds and The Hunter I Chose; start after 9 PM.
  • Stargazing missing: complete The Long Pursuit and start during 8–10 PM. It does not use the later midnight end of her normal alley schedule.
  • Shared story stalled: check both characters' named dates, both required Heart-to-Hearts and Becky availability where shown.
  • No Weapon Between Us missing: complete Five Minutes of Night and The Long Pursuit, reach Crush and visit the Housing Block during 6–9 AM.
  • Final morning scene missing: reach Romantic and finish No Weapon Between Us as well as Five Minutes of Night.
