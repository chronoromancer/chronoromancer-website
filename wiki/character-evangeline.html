概览

Evangeline协同公司的首席执行官有一条公司进步路线 Miki、Maggie和Arnold,然后是3个个人心跳和晚间邀请。公司所有权和早期的场景打开了路径;仅爱并不能取代它们。她重复的神秘诱惑行动开启了 控制利息之后而不是作为满足她的早期捷径。

Maggie的先决条件 价值之眼 发生于 下午1时至5时,投资室。第一个完成 雄心勃勃的导言,则 值得一提的伙伴关系 与 15 工作经验和正式外配二级。然后,“价值之眼”要求 80 工作经验和正式外配3级 和教诲, 商业 这些是工作经验和装备水平的检查,而不是Evangeline感情要求。

路线之后神秘的诱惑

可重复动作使用玩家的 自导的气息在Evangeline的邀请下,她的选择仍然是自己的。 10个基本感情 随机选择 三个会议室图像之一。它的Extras 条目根据所选择的图像解锁;由于选择可以重复,可能需要每日重复使用才能看到所有三个。
她的后场面和对话可以简要地反映这种遭遇,这些反应不会降低行动的要求,也不会提供公司路线的捷径。 神秘诱导技巧和神秘90以及控制利息和有偿使用。
目前没有单独的NPC亲密EXP被授予. 控制利息, 关闭记录 或 使用神秘诱导这些相遇会给予上述的爱意。

当前内容

公司路线,三条个人H2H和后路线神秘诱导在目前的代码中可用。 Story第85次释放限制今天,这不是完成这一路线的又一个可以达到的步骤。 Evangeline 没有任何作者的公寓谈话、普通礼物行动、工人培训或锡雷纳岛度假路线。

Overview

Evangeline, CEO of Synergy Corp, has a corporate progression route involving Miki, Maggie and Arnold, followed by three personal heart-to-hearts and an evening invitation. Company ownership and earlier scenes open the route; affection alone does not replace them. Her repeatable Mystic Seduction action opens after Controlling Interest, rather than serving as an early shortcut to meeting her.

Maggie and Actual Company Ownership

Maggie's prerequisite The Eye for Value occurs in the Investment room, 1–5 PM. First complete An Ambitious Introduction, then A Partnership Worth Earning with 15 Working Experience and Formal Outfit level 2. The Eye for Value then requires 80 Working Experience and Formal Outfit level 3 and teaches Business Acumen on completion. These are Working Experience and outfit-level checks, rather than Evangeline affection requirements.
Corporate thresholds count fully acquired companies, not ordinary investment stakes. Raise a company to investment level 5, then pay its separate BUYOUT quote. A buyout costs 15 times the sum of its five investment-level costs; Investment Savvy reduces the buyout payment by 15%, with the cash quote rounded to the nearest whole amount. Level 5 investment by itself is not ownership. There are 50 companies in the current investment list; Requested by Name needs all of them acquired.

Mystic Seduction After the Route

The repeatable action uses the player's self-directed aura, at Evangeline's invitation. Her choices remain her own. Completing it awards 10 base affection and randomly selects one of three Boardroom images. Its Extras entries unlock according to the image selected; repeated daily uses may be needed to see all three because the selection can repeat.
Her post-scene appearance and dialogue can briefly reflect that encounter. Those responses do not lower the action's requirements or provide a shortcut to the corporate route. The current action requires both the Mystic Seduction skill and Mystic 90, as well as Controlling Interest and paid access.
No separate NPC-intimacy EXP is currently awarded by Controlling Interest, Off the Record or Use Mystic Seduction. These encounters award the affection listed above.

Current Content Status

The corporate route, three personal H2Hs and post-route Mystic Seduction are available in the current code. A later main-story continuation is authored beyond the current Story Beat 85 release limit. It is not an additional reachable step for completing this route today. Evangeline has no authored apartment conversation, ordinary gift action, worker training or Sirena Isle vacation route.

Troubleshooting

  • For the empire route, complete Story Beat 55, then visit Miki's Bedroom, 8–11 PM. The Boardroom is not its first trigger.
  • If a company threshold is stuck, count buyouts. Fully upgrading an investment to level 5 does not acquire the company until the separate buyout is paid.
  • Requested by Name needs all 50 acquired companies, the prior Skyline scene and Miki's What Are You Building? H2H.
  • Not a Plus-One opens only after The Price of Attention, at Acknowledged and with paid access.
  • Family Business uses the Dining Room, not the Foyer or Miki's Bedroom.
  • Unpriced needs Private Valuation and Not a Plus-One, plus Known. Controlling Interest needs Unpriced completed.
  • Off the Record needs Crush, Unpriced and an entry between 1 and 3 PM. It is optional for the main personal chain.
  • Mystic Seduction is post-route. Mystic 90 and the skill alone do not open it before Controlling Interest.
  • Ruthless Excellence needs Communication 90; her Commerce, Attractiveness and Charm preferences are relationship bonuses, not substitutes for that check.
