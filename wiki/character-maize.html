概览

Maize 是一个快乐的寿司王后 中世纪 时代,通过 斗士们 赏金她变得 28岁 在玫瑰和桑恩 并有一个白天的肖像 敦煌岛.
她目前的路线包括普通的谈话和礼物、日常赞美、工人参观和培训、两次精心设计的训练升级、与Esperanza和Raven共用的场景、度假胜地心对心、私人海滩内容和 增加22次共用度假村聚会.本指南在将她的个人历史和场景对话留给游戏的同时解释了他们的要求.

Overview

Maize is a cheerful succubus queen from the Medieval era, recruited through a Fighters Guild bounty. She becomes worker 28 at the Rose & Thorn and has a daytime portrait in its Dungeon.
Her current route includes ordinary Talk and gifts, daily praise, worker visits and training, two crafted training upgrades, a shared scene with Esperanza and Raven, a resort Heart-to-Heart, private beach content and 22 additional shared resort encounters. This guide explains their requirements while leaving her personal history and the scenes' dialogue to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Complete and claim Fighters Guild contracts 1–14 in order, then accept contract 15: Bounty — The Succubus Queen.
  2. Explore floor 30 of the ordinary dungeon with that bounty active. Its encounter has a 15% spawn roll on eligible movement steps. Complete the battle and recruitment encounter.
  3. Return to the guild to Claim Reward. Find Maize's home portrait in the Rose & Thorn Dungeon, 9 AM–5 PM, while worker 28 is active and not vacationing.
  4. Use Talk up to three times daily and Praise Maize once daily. At Acquaintance, add one Chocolate and one Flowers per day, using their separate counters.
  5. Use the separate worker visit menu during her assigned shift for Talk, praise, a gift and training. These visits develop her worker stats, worker experience and intimacy experience.
  6. Send active Maize on a Sirena Isle vacation. Find her resort portrait and complete Sunset Walk at the Beach, 5–8 PM. Its first completion is required for her later oral upgrade.
  7. At In Love, 3,200 ordinary affection, bring the Moonfang Leather Choker or Hexbound Rose Sachet to her home Dungeon portrait, 9 AM–5 PM, and complete the matching once-only training-upgrade scene.
  8. Continue her independent resort activities and shared encounters. They are not extra sequential gates on the two crafted upgrades.

Finding and Recruiting Maize

Fighters Guild Contract 15

Use the Fighters Guild Quest Board in King's Demesne, 10 AM–8 PM, and Accept Contract. Contracts advance in order when you Claim Reward, so finish and claim all fourteen earlier contracts before accepting Maize's bounty. Contract 14 is the third Caravan Defense encounter covered on Lyra's page. Raising guild clout alone does not skip those contracts.
With Bounty: The Succubus Queen active, explore dungeon floor 30. The bounty check rolls 15% per eligible movement step, before ordinary random encounters. It is not a guaranteed encounter on entering the floor, and floors 29 and 31 do not satisfy this bounty. The Infinity Dungeon does not spawn Fighters Guild bounties.
Maize launches a scripted encounter with its own battle, rather than the ordinary direct bounty-fight flow. Prepare healing, equipment and resources before exploring the floor.

Battle Preparation

Her authored encounter uses floor-30 combat scaling, with 1,300 base HP, a level bonus of 18, a 2.3 damage multiplier, 15% dodge, 15% damage reduction and 55 magic resistance. These are enemy configuration values; the actual battle applies the game's combat and difficulty calculations.
Her moves include Life Drain, Shadow Strike, Dark Pulse, Hypnotic Gaze, Fire Slash, Enthrall and Damnation. Damnation is telegraphed. She has Dark strength, no listed elemental weakness, and absorbs Fire at 80% and Light at 10%. Avoid treating Fire or Light as a weakness. Prepare for her healing/control moves as well as direct damage.

Recruitment Choices and Guild Reward

After the battle, the four responses all converge on the recruitment completion. There is no additional romance-stage, gift or preferred-response requirement to recruit her. The first three replies correspond to Good, Neutral and Evil alignment, while the indifferent reply has no registered alignment assignment. Choose the response that fits your intended character.
Completing the encounter activates worker 28, marks the bounty fulfilled and awards 50 base Maize affection. The responses have no separately registered affection-choice bonus. Return to the Quest Board and Claim Reward for 800 gold, Fighters Guild clout +10 and two Sylphs. The guild reward is separate from recruitment and its affection; claiming also advances the next contract.

Worker Visits and Training

Use the separate Visit Worker menu with worker 28 active, home, not resting and within her assigned work shift, initially 9 AM–5 PM. Maize allows one visit per game day. Its entry cost is 60 minutes, 60 Energy and 40 Calm. Time, Power and Focus Potions halve their respective costs to 30 minutes, 30 Energy and 20 Calm.
The visit allows one session Talk, one session praise, one ordinary gift and one training, with their own session checks. These allowances are separate from the home portrait's three Talks, daily Chocolate, daily Flowers and Praise Maize.

Troubleshooting

  • No bounty encounter: accept contract 15, use ordinary dungeon floor 30, and keep exploring eligible tiles. The 15% roll is random; Infinity Dungeon and other floors do not spawn it.
  • Contract 15 unavailable: finish and claim all contracts 1–14. Completing the previous scene without claiming its guild reward does not advance the board.
  • Maize absent at home: complete recruitment, keep worker 28 active, return her from vacation and visit Dungeon, 9 AM–5 PM.
  • Chocolate or Flowers missing: reach Acquaintance, 50 affection, carry the item and stay within its once-daily allowance.
  • Praise Maize absent: she must be present at home, and the daily praise must still be unused. Worker-session praise is a separate activity.
  • Worker visit unavailable: check her assigned shift, active status, resting/vacation status, daily visit counter and 60 Energy / 40 Calm before buffs.
  • Crafted upgrade missing: reach In Love, bring the exact gift and use her home Dungeon portrait. Cowgirl requires bounty completion; oral additionally requires Sunset Walk completed.
  • Oral upgrade missing after a resort trip: finish the full sunset H2H, then return Maize home. Vacationing Maize does not offer the home upgrade action.
  • Resort shared encounter absent: send every listed worker on vacation together, then visit the correct room during its own window. Check whether it already completed.
  • Training or intimate XP confused with affection: worker experience, worker relationship points, Maize NPC intimacy experience and player Erotic experience do not replace ordinary relationship thresholds.
Maize currently has no ordinary apartment conversation, Kupie date entry, personal pregnancy continuation, separate one-time stat lesson or Maize-specific Christmas activity. Her implemented romantic and worker progression is covered above.
