概览

Gem 她的路线结合了采矿课、与Lyra的供给、两个个人心脏对心脏、Rose& Thorn的招募以及一个一次性的忽略日期。在招聘之后,她还进行了家庭对话、八个工人培训选择、三个精心设计的升级和度假场景。

Overview

Gem is the dwarf miner at the Mountain Foothills. Her route combines a Mining lesson, supply runs with Lyra, two personal heart-to-hearts, recruitment to the Rose & Thorn, and a one-time overlook date. After recruitment she also has a home conversation, eight worker-training options, three crafted upgrades and resort scenes.

Recruitment Walkthrough

  1. Meet Gem at the Mountain Foothills, 7 AM–5 PM. Use Talk to Gem up to five times per game day, at 15 minutes and 1 actual base point each. Its metadata lists zero, but the current handler records 1.
  2. At Known, use Give Jewelry, consuming one Jewelry, for 10 base points, up to twice per game day. Both outdoor Talk and Jewelry use −1 base points after their allowances and the shared over-limit handling.
  3. Reach Friend and Strength 25, then choose Gem's Mining lesson. It requires Adventure Tier+ paid access, costs 180 minutes, $600 and 100 energy, and appears only until the player learns Mining. Complete The Dwarven Way to learn Mining and gain 25 base points. The funny answer, “Curious about the rock? Should I buy it dinner first?”, adds 4; caring adds 3 and flirty 2.
  4. Raise Lyra to Known, keep Gem Friend, then enter the Village Market, 7 AM–5 PM, for Short Legs, Long Road. It requires the Mining lesson scene completed and gives Gem 10 / Lyra 10 base points. “I refuse to be outsmarted by a goat today.” adds Gem 4 / Lyra 2; the other two replies give 3 each.
  5. Raise Lyra to Friend and visit the Mountain Foothills, 7 AM–6 PM, with Gem Friend and the supply-run scene complete, for The High Road. It gives 12 base points each. “Declare this the proudest cavalry unit in history.” adds Gem 5 / Lyra 3; “Make sure Gem is steady before Lyra moves.” adds 4 each. This automatic scene's window extends beyond Gem's ordinary 5 PM portrait cutoff.
  6. At Gem Close Friend, with The High Road completed and the brothel owned, enter Worker Rooms, 8 AM–8 PM, for Foundation Survey. It gives 15 base points. Choose “Tell me how to make this safer for everyone working here.” or the flirty response for +5; the cost joke gives +4.
  7. At Gem Crush, with Foundation Survey completed and the brothel owned, return to Worker Rooms, 8 AM–8 PM, for Stone and Silk Contract. It gives 20 base points. Choose “You will be respected here, Gem. That is part of the contract.” or the flirty answer for +5; the fine-print joke gives +4.
  8. Contract completion recruits Gem as worker 14 and grants a separate 10-base-point recruitment bonus, calculated independently. With a five-point response, the scene has 25 base points plus that separate 10-base bonus. Recruitment does not require completing either personal H2H or the overlook date.
The four post-lesson scenes are one-time automatic encounters. Visit their named locations during their own windows; selecting Talk does not substitute for the automatic scene. The initial paid Mining lesson is required for this recruitment chain, even if a player can already enter the mining minigame.

Gem's Delve and Daily Appreciation

After recruitment, enter Gem's Delve from the Cell Corridor. The Delve door itself requires Gem found as worker 14; reaching the corridor through the ordinary travel route also requires its construction. See Gastoff for the corridor commission.
Gem continues going to the Mountain Foothills, 7 AM–5 PM, even after recruitment. She is in her Delve during the remaining hours: midnight–7 AM and 5 PM–midnight. This is her NPC portrait schedule, separate from her worker shift.
Delve Talk takes 15 minutes, gives 1 base point, and has four positive uses per game day, separate from the outdoor Talk's five. Further attempts use −1 base points and the shared over-limit handling. The Delve's current personal menu offers Talk; it does not relocate the Foothills lesson, gifts, H2Hs or date.
At her home portrait, Appreciate Gem's Craft takes 20 minutes, gives 1 base affection point and Loyalty +1 up to 100, and completes once per game day. It follows her presence and home-from-vacation status, with no extra on-duty requirement.
Upgrade Rose and Thorn is currently on hold and hidden, including in the Delve. Recruiting Gem or redeeming a construction code does not currently make that action available. Gastoff's active facility commissions remain the available construction route.

If Progress Stalls

  • Recruitment scenes missing: follow Mining lesson → supply run → High Road → Foundation Survey → Contract, and meet both Gem's and Lyra's affection requirements.
  • Free-version recruitment blocked: the authored chain requires Gem's paid Mining lesson scene; entering the minigame does not complete it.
  • Pickaxe purchase missing: the $200 bundle resets next game day; Pickaxe Haul requires its paid access and recent supporter redeem unlock.
  • Home empty: Gem is at the Foothills 7 AM–5 PM. The home door needs recruitment, and the route passes through the built Cell Corridor.
  • Evening date missing: start Stonewatch at her outdoor portrait 4–5 PM; its broader listed window does not keep that portrait present.
  • Training option missing: complete training sessions to advance the separate 1–8 training level; affection and player Erotic level do not directly unlock those menu levels.
  • Crafted gift missing: be In Love, recruited, home from vacation, and at her Foothills portrait with the item and the specific completed scenes.
  • Resort group scene missing: every listed worker must be active and vacationing together during its own window. Bring Gem home before attempting her ordinary Foothills, Delve or crafted-gift content.
