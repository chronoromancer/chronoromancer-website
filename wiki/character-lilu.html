  1. Visit the Dojo, 10 AM–10 PM, for The Downtown Dojo. The Caring reply gives 4 choice affection plus 5 completion affection, for 9 base affection. Her portrait schedule does not require Story Beat 8.
  2. Talk up to three times daily. Martial Arts is a separate location activity that also grants Ability and Lilu affection. Her four ordinary skill lessons are available from Stranger when you meet their stat requirements.
  3. At Known, with Communication 55, claim Martial Discipline for Ability +1 and 10 direct affection.
  4. At Acquaintance, choose Tai-Chi Form and, optionally, Picnic Date. Learn Inner Peace beforehand if you want the Form's two special replies and her relationship multiplier.
  5. At Close Friend, choose Shared Stillness. Boardwalk Sunset Date opens separately once Diane has built the Boardwalk and you have its required supporter access.
  6. At Romantic, Private Lesson becomes available. Tai-Chi Mastery opens at Romantic with Ability 60, paid access and sufficient Energy/Calm, if the skill is still unlearned.
  7. At 1,500 ordinary affection, the separate One on One animation opens with paid access and Higher Power. It has its own daily allowance.
  8. At In Love, an owned Balance Stone unlocks Intimate Bonding.
  9. Continue the separate Zoe dojo scenes, Kupie progression and, if enabled and eligible, parenthood route using their requirements below.
The two H2Hs, dates and private lesson do not add each other as prerequisites. Story Beats 8–9 are important main-story training events and later parenthood requirements, but they are not extra unlock gates on ordinary Talk, the introductory dojo scene or the first H2H.

Optional Parenthood Continuation

This later route requires pregnancy content unlocked and enabled, Akira's Pregnancy Protocol completed, 2,000 ordinary Lilu affection, 500 Lilu NPC intimacy experience, Story Beat 9 completed and Zoe Stands Her Ground completed. Player Erotic experience and Kupie affection do not substitute for those requirements.
She cannot already be pregnant, recovering or have a child through this route; the current system allows one child per NPC. There is no additional Lilu personal H2H completion requirement in the parenthood readiness table.
  1. Start Private Lesson and choose “Ask Lilu to choose parenthood with you.” at arrival. Follow the commitment choices through the ending. The branch records conception and grants 50 base conversation points, 50 player Erotic experience and 40 Lilu NPC intimacy experience, using the same 120-minute, 80-Energy cost and daily allowance.
  2. Revisit the Dojo while Lilu is present for A Second Heartbeat, available from pregnancy age 11 days, for 5 base affection. Conception is age zero; the hidden stage lasts through age 10.
  3. Complete A Beginner Again from age 31, and at least one day after the reveal, for 7 base affection. A delayed reveal delays entry into this stage.
  4. When Joy Becomes Labor becomes ready 30 days after the final stage starts, and at least one day after the final-stage visit, for 10 base affection. Prompt milestone visits allow delivery from age 61 days; delayed milestones can postpone it.
  5. Postpartum recovery lasts eight game days, followed by family check-ins.
Check In / Family Check In takes 30 minutes, once per game day, and adds 5 direct affection. A check-in can start a ready milestone at its registered location. No extra numerical count of prenatal visits is required for delivery.
Talk, Martial Discipline and the ordinary introductory/shared dojo scenes remain pregnancy-safe. Her ordinary skill lessons, Tai-Chi Mastery, dates, both H2Hs, Private Lesson and One on One are blocked while she is showing in the early/final stages and during postpartum recovery. Her parenthood opening is a deliberate special branch; ordinary intimacy outcomes do not start this route automatically.

Troubleshooting

Check the Dojo's 10 AM–10 PM hours first. Picnic begins at 10 AM at the earliest because of Lilu's portrait schedule, even though the action lists 9 AM. For Boardwalk Sunset, check Close Friend, Diane's construction, paid access and Higher Power. For Tai-Chi Mastery, check Romantic, Ability 60, paid access, unlearned Tai-Chi and the unusually high Energy/Calm requirements.
For a special H2H reply, learn Inner Peace or Empathy as appropriate. For Private Lesson, wait for a new game day after starting it; One on One uses a separate allowance. For Kupie selection, check main-game Friend, Charm Enchanting level 9 and paid selection. For the parenthood opening, check both Story Beat 9 and Zoe's completed shared dojo chain, as well as the global protocol and experience requirements.
