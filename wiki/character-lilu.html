Overview

Master Lilu teaches combat skills at the downtown dojo. Main-story training, optional dates, conversations and private lessons have separate requirements.

Where to find them

Her portrait is at Dojo, daily 10:00–21:59.

Route and relationship milestones

After Story 7, ask Lilu to train you at the dojo for Story 8 (60 minutes). For Story 9, visit Hidden Waterfall at 07:00–10:59 (480 minutes). Plan recovery after the long training.

The four ordinary lessons do not require high affection. Inner Peace is taught separately at Monk's Temple Training Hall: Mystic 30, 250 minutes, $350, 70 Energy and 70 Calm.

The picnic actually starts 10:00–15:59, when her portrait is present. The boardwalk date needs Close Friend (200), Diane’s completed construction and its access lock, starting 17:00–19:59.

Private Lesson needs Romantic (800), 120 minutes and 80 Energy, once per day, with affection from conversation choices. One on One is separate: 1,500 affection, 60 minutes, 40 Energy, its own daily use and content lock; it gives 80 base affection and 25 NPC Cowgirl experience. It does not award Balance Stone. Owning that stone and reaching In Love (3,000) instead enables 60-minute daily bonding for one level.

The optional family continuation needs 2,000 affection, 500 NPC intimacy experience, Akira’s pregnancy protocol, Story 9 and Zoe Stands Her Ground. Continue through the private lesson. Extra Lilu heart-to-hearts are not required for this branch.

Zoe’s three dojo episodes require the corresponding Zoe conversations in order, as well as Lilu’s affection. Her separate Kupie Love Training entry uses its own minigame requirements.

Her favorite stats are Ability, Stamina, Perception. The learned Inner Peace skill improves her relationship bonus.

What usually blocks the next step

  • A missing lesson may already be learned, or its stat requirement may be unmet.
  • For Zoe’s next dojo episode, finish the previous dojo episode and next Zoe conversation.
  • Visible pregnancy and recovery temporarily block physical lessons, dates and private activities. Check the shared Pregnancy guide.
  • Private Lesson and One on One have separate daily uses; neither needs waiting until the dojo closes.

概览

Master Lilu在市中心道场教授战斗技能。主线训练、可选约会、交谈与私人课程分别解锁，不能互相代替。

去哪里找

她每天10:00—21:59在道场出现。

路线与关系进展

主线7后，到道场请求Lilu训练即可进行主线8，花费60分钟。之后于07:00—10:59到隐藏瀑布进行主线9，花费480分钟，结束后应安排恢复。

四项普通课程不要求高好感。内心平静由僧侣寺院修行厅另行教授，要求神秘30，花费250分钟、$350、70精力和70镇定。

Zoe的三段道场事件还要依次完成对应的Zoe对话，单刷Lilu好感不能跳过。她在Kupie 的爱之训练中的条目另有小游戏条件。

她偏好的属性为能力、耐力、感知。学会内心平静可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 技能课消失时，检查是否已经学会，以及属性是否达标。
  • Zoe的下一段道场事件同时要求前一次道场事件和下一次Zoe对话。
  • 显怀和产后恢复会暂时阻止体力活动、约会与私人课程，先查看生育指南。
  • 私人课程与一对一各有每日次数，不必等道场关门。
