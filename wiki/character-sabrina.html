Sabrina——完全走过

概览

Sabrina 这是 21岁的幻想家 现场 现市中心神秘好奇心商店她目前的路线包括每日谈话和礼物,8次个人自动会面, 五个日期, 两个心脏对心脏深夜沙发入口两段可重复的亲密视频 三个NPC亲密画廊奖.
这种穿行方式在将个人发现和场景结果留给游戏的同时给予要求,选择和机械奖励.

如果进程 Stalls 显示

  • 9点后店面的肖像不见了: 时间 浪漫主义+ 她搬到沙发上,主店的手工日期和H2H必须提前开始。
  • 库克屏蔽 : 完成 我住在大店里,浪漫,9点11分,然后使用房间旅行动作 9点后.
  • 讲习班被封锁 : 完成 在密友的窗帘后面进入讲习班本身并不能完成这一计划。
  • 丢失的礼物 : 到达 知识 并携带巧克力或天经,他们的正限是: 每天一本巧克力/两本书.
  • 缺少魔法练习 : 检查 已知、交流50和未声称完成.
  • 街道场景缺失: 访问 市中心街道,上午11时至4时,在它们各自的 知识/ 朋友/ 粉碎 阶段。
  • 星空缺失: 开始 下午7时至9时,浪漫主义+使用主店的肖像画。
  • 缺少证书日期 : 携带 一张券覆盖 朋友们访问 中午6点,并检查单独的完成日期和怀孕限制。
  • 缺少棋盘日期 : 结束 Diane的建筑覆盖 关闭朋友并访问 下午5时至8时.

Sabrina — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Sabrina is the 21-year-old illusionist at the Mystic Curiosities Shop in Present Downtown. Her current route includes daily Talk and gifts, eight automatic personal encounters, five dates, two heart-to-hearts, late-night couch access, two repeatable intimate videos, and three NPC intimacy gallery rewards.
This walkthrough gives requirements, choices and mechanical rewards while leaving the personal discoveries and scene outcomes to the game.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the shop for the one-time The Witch of Downtown introduction, awarding 1 base affection. All introduction replies have the same configured reward.
  2. Use Talk up to three positive uses daily. At Acquaintance, add one Chocolate and two Book gifts daily.
  3. At Known, return to the shop for The Debunker. With Communication 50, claim Magic Practice once.
  4. Visit Downtown City Street, 11 AM–4 PM, for the three street encounters at Acquaintance, Friend and Crush. They are independent one-time scenes rather than a chained attendance counter.
  5. At Friend, take Comic Con, the paid Fair Date, and the separate Win Her a Prize Voucher date as available.
  6. At Close Friend, visit the shop for Behind the Curtain, unlocking Workshop access. Take Stargazing and, after Diane builds the boardwalk, the supporter Boardwalk Sunset Date.
  7. At Crush, revisit the shop for The Impossible Trick. Its automatic scene does not additionally require the workshop, a prior date, or a portrait-paid flag.
  8. At Romantic, visit the main shop, 9–11 PM, for I Live Here, then enter the couch room during 9 PM–midnight. The paid Late Night Unwind H2H becomes available there.
  9. At Intimate, choose the paid Show Me Real Magic H2H from the main shop portrait, 9 AM–9 PM.
  10. With couch access, paid access and 1,000 ordinary affection, use Intimate Night; at 1,200, add Couch Cuddle. Each has its own once-daily allowance.
Dates, stat rewards and the two H2Hs have their own gates. Their current actions do not require one another's completion merely because they appear in this recommended order.

If Progress Stalls

  • Shop portrait missing after 9 PM: at Romantic+ she moves to the couch. Main-shop manual dates and H2Hs must start earlier.
  • Couch blocked: complete I Live Here in the main shop, Romantic, 9–11 PM, then use the room travel action after 9 PM.
  • Workshop blocked: complete Behind the Curtain at Close Friend. Entering the Workshop does not itself complete that plot.
  • Gifts missing: reach Acquaintance and carry Chocolate or Book. Their positive limits are one Chocolate / two Books daily.
  • Magic Practice missing: check Known, Communication 50 and unclaimed completion.
  • Street scenes missing: visit Downtown City Street, 11 AM–4 PM, at their respective Acquaintance / Friend / Crush stages.
  • Stargazing missing: begin 7–9 PM at Romantic+, using the main-shop portrait. Earlier Close Friend stages allow until 11 PM.
  • Voucher date missing: carry one Voucher, reach Friend, visit noon–6 PM, and check the separate date completion and pregnancy restriction.
  • Boardwalk date missing: finish Diane's construction, reach Close Friend, have paid access and the latest supporter code, and visit 5–8 PM.
  • Late Night Unwind missing: use the unlocked Couch, 9 PM–midnight, at Romantic, with paid access and sufficient energy.
  • Show Me Real Magic missing: use the main shop, 9 AM–9 PM, at Intimate, with paid access. It is not a couch action.
  • Video missing: check 1,000 / 1,200 ordinary affection, couch access, paid access and today's separate video counter.
  • Gallery reward locked despite high affection: the thresholds use total Sabrina NPC intimacy XP, not ordinary affection or player Erotic XP.
Her current route has no apartment conversation, venue Invite Date, Kupie entry, brothel-worker recruitment or resort vacation route.
