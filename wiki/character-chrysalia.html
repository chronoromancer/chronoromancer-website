Overview

Chrysalia has an ordered personal route with three H2Hs and two dates. Its access is connected to forest Objectives; the guide leaves that connection’s explanation inside the game.

Where to find them

Industrial Waste Processing Zone: all day after the relevant forest Objective is completed. If she has not appeared, check current Objectives before repeatedly waiting in the Future.

Route and relationship milestones

Alternate the automatic event with the newly available H2H or date. One Voice in the Chorus comes first; then The Lanternwing Path, Say My Name, Last Lamp Before Dawn and Beneath My Wings fit between the personal events. Exact stages and start windows are below.

A separate gift event requires Friend (100) and Onyx active as a maid. It grants Onyx's Mourning Veil. Equip it to Onyx for +25% maid cleaning, −20% mess and +8% Rose & Thorn income. The gift does not require every later personal event.

Optional reference: relationship bonuses

Meeting each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before the remaining relationship bonuses. The favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct one-time affection rewards are added as listed.

Charm 75; Mystic 70; Perception 70

Favorite skill: Animal Care

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Listed time and resources are base values; applicable skills, difficulty and active bonuses can change the shown cost or affection. Base affection, direct affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Affection alone does not bypass the interleaved H2Hs and dates. Check the completed steps in order.
  • The evening actions have different start windows, even though her portrait is available all day.
  • The gift requires Onyx’s actual maid recruitment, not merely meeting her.

概览

Chrysalia 的个人路线包含三段谈心与两次约会，需按顺序推进。入口与森林目标相连；这里不解释这段联系的剧情。

在哪里找到

工业废料处理区：完成相关森林目标后全天可探访。若她尚未出现，请先检查当前目标，不必在未来反复等待。

路线与关系节点

请在自动事件后检查新开放的谈心或约会：先完成合声中的一个声音，再依个人事件顺序穿插灯笼翼之路、叫出我的名字、黎明前的最后一盏灯与我的羽翼之下。确切阶段与开始时段见下表。
