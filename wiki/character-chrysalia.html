概览

Chrysalia 是一个成年的蛾科妇女,她的路线通过毛虫种群的故事线可以找到。她有七个个人自动场景,三个H2H,两个日期和一个单独的女仆-物品奖赏。她的进取式故事场景带有对话和日期;光靠爱不能绕过这些先决条件。

Chrysalia — Complete Character Guide

Overview

Chrysalia is an adult mothkin woman whose route becomes available through the caterpillar population storyline. She has seven personal automatic scenes, three H2Hs, two dates and a separate maid-item reward. Her progression alternates story scenes with conversations and dates; affection alone cannot bypass these prerequisites.

Unlocking Chrysalia

Chrysalia's portrait requires The Moth Lady's Gratitude to be completed. To trigger that scene, visit the Present Forest in Uptown after meeting every requirement below:

The population threshold counts the Medieval total plus Present total, not one era alone and not specimens held in your collection. Released specimens and the ecosystem's population growth contribute through the caterpillar habitat system. Reaching one billion does not replace the six required completed scenes.
The two smaller Moth Lady scenes occur in the Medieval woods, while Gratitude occurs in the Present forest. If you have reached a later threshold without visiting the earlier locations, return to the relevant woods and let those scenes complete first. The Blessing awards Silken Moth Cloth and the Gift awards Silk Uniform, but the unlock checks their scene flags rather than whether you have equipped the rewards.
Once Gratitude completes, travel to the Future Fringe Zone's Evolved Whispering Woods for Chrysalia's portrait and Every Wing Remembers. This guide lists the required quest names while leaving their revelations and outcomes to the game.

Complete Personal Progression

  1. After the unlock, enter the Future woods at any hour for Every Wing Remembers. It gives 50 base affection, with Caring +12 as the best choice.
  2. Use Talk to reach Acquaintance. Complete One Voice in the Chorus after Every Wing Remembers; it can start at any hour.
  3. At Friend, revisit the woods for A City Without Walls, after the first H2H and Every Wing Remembers.
  4. Start The Lanternwing Path from 6–10 PM, after A City Without Walls and the first H2H. Complete this first date.
  5. At Close Friend, revisit for The Few Who Walk Upright, after both A City Without Walls and the first date.
  6. Complete Say My Name from 6–11 PM, after The Few Who Walk Upright and the first H2H.
  7. Revisit from 6 PM–midnight for The Scent of Choice, after Say My Name and The Few Who Walk Upright.
  8. At Crush, start Last Lamp Before Dawn from 8–11 PM, after The Scent of Choice, the first date and Say My Name. Complete this second date.
  9. Revisit at any hour for The Missing Pages, after The Scent of Choice and the second date.
  10. Complete Beneath My Wings from 8–11 PM, after The Missing Pages and both earlier H2Hs.
  11. At Crush, return at any hour for Roots Against the Poison, after The Missing Pages and Beneath My Wings.
  12. Return from 6 PM–midnight for Not an Offering, after Roots Against the Poison. This is the current final personal automatic scene.
The route's final steps require Crush, rather than an additional Romantic or Intimate threshold. Dates, H2Hs and automatic scenes all complete once. Talk is the repeatable action used to fill any affection gaps between these milestones.

The Mourning Veil Reward

At Friend with Chrysalia, with Onyx acquired as maid 6, visit the Future woods at any hour for The Mourning Veil. It is a separate one-time scene; it does not require the personal route's later dates or H2Hs.
It grants 5 base Chrysalia affection, with Flirty +4 as the best choice, and awards Onyx's Mourning Veil if you do not already own it. The item is specific to Onyx and provides 25% maid cleaning, 20% mess reduction and 8% brothel wealth when used through the maid-item system. Completing the scene does not recruit Chrysalia or turn her into a maid.

If Progress Stalls

  • Missing Chrysalia's portrait means The Moth Lady's Gratitude has not completed. Check all six scene flags, combined wild population, and the Present Forest trigger location.
  • Her route takes place in the Future woods after unlock, not the Present forest where Gratitude triggers.
  • Missing the first H2H means Acquaintance or Every Wing Remembers is still missing.
  • Missing The Few Who Walk Upright usually means the first date is incomplete, even if affection is already high.
  • Missing the second date means Crush, The Scent of Choice, the first date, or Say My Name is incomplete. Start from 8–11 PM.
  • Missing the final H2H means The Missing Pages or one of the earlier H2Hs is incomplete. It also starts only from 8–11 PM.
  • Missing the last automatic scene means Roots Against the Poison is incomplete or it is outside 6 PM–midnight.
  • The Mourning Veil scene additionally needs Onyx. It is an optional item reward alongside the personal route.
  • Chrysalia has no current apartment, repeatable intimate-video, worker-recruitment or separate gift menu registered.
