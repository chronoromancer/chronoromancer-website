Overview

Rachel’s route starts at the university and continues through tutoring, optional dates and apartment visits. Her evening Boardwalk conversations use a separate daily allowance from school Talk.

Where to find them

University Hall: daily 09:00–17:00. After Diane builds it, Boardwalk: daily 17:00–20:00.

At Crush (400), Knock on Rachel's Door is available remotely from Residential Block, daily 19:00–23:00; it does not require waiting for her ordinary school portrait.

Route and relationship milestones

Talk gives 1 base affection, 15 minutes, four positive uses per day. Her Boardwalk Talk gives 1 base affection in 20 minutes, three per day, separately. At Known (25), Life Balance Wisdom needs Communication 28 and 30 minutes for +1 Perception and 10 direct affection, once.

The school tutoring conversations open independently: Physics Tutoring Help at Known (25), 45 minutes; Advanced Calculus Help at Close Friend (200), 60 minutes. Neither has an added Intelligence gate. Return to the school hall at Friend (100) for Rachel's Quiet Crack; the Computer Lab event opens at Acquaintance (50).

Apartment visits begin at Crush (400), take 180 minutes and 40 Energy, and offer the Material Science teaching topic while that skill is unlearned. Follow that topic through its conclusion to learn the skill. The research-methods topic is a separate Intelligence 35 branch.

Her optional family continuation begins through the apartment’s dedicated choice once the shared Pregnancy requirements are met. Use the Pregnancy guide for the 2,000-affection/500-Intelligence/protocol requirements and stage timing. Her school visits host the subsequent automatic pregnancy events.

Her Book gift currently needs two Books for a successful gift and consumes both; carry at least two before selecting it. It gives 5 base affection, takes 10 minutes and has two positive uses per day.

Zoo, Fair, Voucher and Boardwalk dates are separate once-only outings. The Voucher date starts at school, so its practical window is 12:00–17:00. Private Moment instead needs 700 ordinary affection, 30 minutes and 20 Energy, once per day.

KTV/Night Club invitations need Crush (400) and Rachel's Quiet Crack, 20:00–23:00. KTV: 120 minutes/$150/40 Energy; Night Club: 120 minutes/$100/30 Energy plus its content lock. Each venue’s allowance is shared for the day.

In Kupie's Love Training, ordinary Friend (100), selection access and three hours of tracked minigame play unlock Rachel. Her Christmas Study with Rachel needs Romantic (800), active Christmas activities and 30 minutes at school, once; it awards one Token and 10 direct affection.

Favorite stats are Intelligence, Charm, Communication. Learning Networking improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Ordinary school and Boardwalk Talk have separate counters; check the location before assuming all conversations are used up.
  • Bring two Books for the gift. With only one, it is spent before the successful-gift check.
  • A missing Material Science topic usually means the skill is already learned.
  • Tutoring, the 700-affection private action, home access and pregnancy each have different requirements.

概览

Rachel从大学路线开始，之后有辅导、可选约会和公寓来访。傍晚在木板路的交谈，与学校交谈分别计算每日次数。

去哪里找

大学大厅：每天09:00–17:00。Diane完成建设后，木板路：每天17:00–20:00。

迷恋（400好感）后，可从住宅街区远程选择敲 Rachel 的门，每天19:00–23:00，不需等她在学校的日常头像。

路线与关系进展

学校交谈消耗15分钟，获得1基础好感，每天4次；木板路交谈消耗20分钟，获得1基础好感，每天3次，额度独立。已知（25好感）且沟通28后，生活平衡智慧消耗30分钟，一次性获得感知+1与10直接好感。

学校里的辅导谈心独立开放：物理辅导需已知（25好感）、45分钟；高等微积分辅导需亲密朋友（200好感）、60分钟，均无额外智力门槛。朋友（100好感）回学校大厅触发Rachel 的安静裂痕；计算机实验室事件则需相识（50好感）。

动物园、嘉年华、兑换券及木板路约会为彼此独立的一次性出游。兑换券约会从学校发起，实际时间为12:00–17:00。私人时刻另需700普通好感，消耗30分钟、20精力，每天一次。

KTV／夜店邀请需迷恋（400好感）并完成Rachel 的安静裂痕，时间20:00–23:00。KTV消耗120分钟、$150、40精力；夜店消耗120分钟、$100、30精力，另查内容权限。各场所共用当天额度。

Kupie 的爱之训练中，普通条件为朋友（100好感）、选择权限及累计游玩3小时。圣诞任务和Rachel一起学习需浪漫感（800好感）、开启圣诞活动，在学校消耗30分钟，仅一次，奖励圣诞代币1枚与10直接好感。

偏好的属性为智力、魅力、沟通。学会人脉经营可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 学校与木板路的交谈额度独立，先确认地点，再判断当天是否已用完。
  • 赠书请带2本；仅有1本时，会在成功赠送检查前被扣除。
  • 材料科学教学话题不见时，先确认是否已经学会。
  • 辅导、700好感的私人行动、公寓入口与生育各有不同条件。
