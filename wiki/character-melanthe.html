概览

Melanthe 是成年人 拉米娅女祭司 中遇到的 中世纪地牢招募她进入一个已建成的 Rose& Thorn 手机 进入日常谈话, 能源排水培训三部个人动画 5次共用走廊相遇 以及不同的怪物护理/训练系统,这种穿行提供了他们的要求和奖励,而没有透露她在现场讲述的故事。

Overview

Melanthe is an adult lamia priestess encountered in the Medieval dungeon. Recruit her into a built Rose & Thorn cell to access daily conversations, Energy Drain training, three personal animations, five shared corridor encounters and the separate monster care/training system. This walkthrough gives their requirements and rewards without revealing the stories told in her scenes.

Recruiting Melanthe

  1. Own the Rose & Thorn, build Cell Corridor, and prepare a built, unoccupied cell among cells 2–8. Cell 1 is reserved for Bobo and does not count as an available recruitment cell.
  2. Fight the named Melanthe enemy in the ordinary dungeon, floors 24–32. Other serpent enemies do not increase her personal capture counter.
  3. For a reliable named battle, walk into the hidden wall on floor 27, x=2, y=10 while Cell Corridor is built and Melanthe is not already active. In one-based grid counting, this is the third column, eleventh row.
  4. Win five named Melanthe battles. Capture rolls start on the sixth victory. The percentage is min(100, 10 + 10 × (total victories − 5) + player Taming): the first roll is 20% + Taming, with ten extra percentage points for each later victory.
  5. On a successful roll with a cell available, choose recruit. Completion assigns her to the selected cell, activates her monster record and adds 10 direct ordinary affection.
  6. Visit her assigned numbered cell for the portrait. It requires Acknowledged, which the recruitment affection normally supplies. Her placeholder cell in the character definition is replaced with the actual assignment.
If no built vacant cell is available, the successful capture attempt does not recruit her. Prepare a cell and win another named battle to roll again. Choosing release also leaves her unrecruited; no particular romantic reply is required beyond accepting recruitment.
Infinity Dungeon does not increment these counters or offer captures. Use the ordinary dungeon. The hidden-wall encounter remains available while she is not active and its corridor requirement is satisfied, making repeated named battles possible.

Battle Preparation

Melanthe's named enemy has 87 base HP, a 1.6 damage multiplier, 13% dodge, 18% damage reduction and 25 magic resistance, before the shared floor/difficulty scaling. Her attacks include Poison Bite, Hypnotic Gaze, Magical Vortex and Magical Shield.
She is strong against Dark, weak to Light, and absorbs Dark at 90%. Prepare recovery resources and equipment for the deeper floors, and use Light rather than Dark as your elemental advantage. The floor-27 encounter uses the regular Melanthe enemy and capture system, rather than an automatic single-win recruitment.

Daily Monster Care

Select active Melanthe through monster management for a care session. She starts with 20 tamed, 80 Health, 0 Stress and 0 monster experience. Care is unavailable while she is resting or after that day's care visit has already been used.
  • Feed consumes 7 Food, raises tamed by 10, normally capped at 100, and has its own session allowance. Feeding from the initial 20 reaches 30, unlocking Teach and Training. If feeding fails because you lack Food, obtain Food and retry.
  • A care session permits one use of each available category: Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Their allowances are separate, so feeding and one training can occur during the same visit.
  • Teach and Training need 30 tamed or higher. Affection stage does not replace that threshold. Teach selects a dialogue topic and does not grant the player Ritual Mastery, Constrict or Seduction.
  • Discipline tool effects vary in tamed, Stress, Health and monster experience. Inspect the selected tool. Claw uses the shared higher tamed cap of 150.
  • Her ordinary daily tamed decay is 7, applied after care begins. Her three personal animations can help raise tamed, but do not replace the daily care system.
  • One Smelling Salts reduces Stress by 10 for all active monsters through the shared care interface.
Feeding and monster training do not have a separate direct Melanthe-affection award configured. Track ordinary relationship stage and tamed value independently. High affection does not prevent the tamed meter from falling below the care threshold.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • Recruitment never appears: defeat the named Melanthe, use the ordinary dungeon, and exceed five victories. Successful capture also needs a built vacant cell 2–8 to recruit her.
  • Floor-27 wall is blocked: build Cell Corridor and check whether Melanthe is already active. The hidden tile is x=2, y=10.
  • Cell portrait is absent: check active recruitment, her actual cell assignment and Acknowledged affection. Visit that numbered cell rather than expecting her portrait in the corridor.
  • Energy Drain is missing: check Friend, Mystic 24, paid access, whether the skill is already known, and the resource costs.
  • Later animation is missing: check its Friend / Close Friend / Crush stage, paid access and individual daily use. Previous animations are not mandatory unlocks.
  • Training is locked despite friendship: restore 30 tamed and check resting and daily care status.
  • Shared encounter is missing: reach Friend with both characters and visit Cell Corridor; Arachne's scene also requires both active.
Melanthe has no configured personal H2H, ordinary date, apartment visit, pregnancy route, Kupie album, Christmas scene, maid cleaning progression or resort vacation. Her monster training is separate from the numbered brothel-worker roster.
