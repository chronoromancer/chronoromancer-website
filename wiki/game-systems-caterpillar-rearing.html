卡特彼勒后退 - 完整的系统指南
卡特彼勒回旋系统是一个跨越中世纪和现在时代的长期育种生态系统,玩家从分布在几乎每一个地图的可点击产卵中采集野生毛虫,放入野外,让种群通过真实的生命周期模拟来生长. 大量种群解锁了结晶奖,特殊物品,神秘育种奖金,丝绸用于织物的制作,以及Marina的溢价钓饵.
本指南涵盖一切:如何找到毛虫,繁殖生命周期如何运作,研究系统,所有70个物种,每个产卵位置都有加权概率,奖励阈值,与捕鱼和丝绸制作相结合.

快速启动

要开始收集毛毛虫,你不需要任何东西——它们会自动出现在地图上。要开始 繁殖 他们,你需要访问 Whispering Woods Uptown (Present) 或 Whispering Woods (Medieval) 并使用管理卡特彼勒育种动作。
第一次访问Whispering Woods Uptown(Present),Seraph自动触发一个教程情节("蝴蝶效应")解释系统.
循环 :
  1. 环绕任何地图——在一定时间可以点击的毛毛虫图案产卵.
  2. 单击它们添加到您的收藏中 。
  3. 访问任何Whipering Woods地点。
  4. 利用育种行动开启人居屏.
  5. 按下"Ad All"按钮,将所有收集的毛虫释放到适当的时代.
  6. 等等 毛虫在游戏中成熟 繁殖和繁衍生态系统

购置方法

将新的毛毛虫输入你的收藏有四种方法:地图发现,徒步旅行,辅导奖励和研究里程碑.

1.可点击的发现(主要方法)

毛毛虫在世界地图上显示为摇摇欲坠的可点击图案。每个位置都用加权概率来定义自己的物种池——一个位置只会在其池中产卵物种。 点击图案将毛毛毛虫添加到您的收藏中。在另一个毛毛虫出现之前, 触发一次重生冷却(2–10天) 。

2. 登机和特殊发现行动

两个动作与可点击产卵不同:
  • 高山山脉 (前期,在山上镇)
  • 爬山 (中世纪时代,在山脚山)
这些不会从一个位置池中滚开,而是从玩家当前时代的每一个池中每个物种的结合中拉动,并用同样概率选择一个.
这意味着当前时代任何池子中存在的每一个物种都有同样的机会通过徒步被发现,包括在普通产卵地点重量较低的稀有和史诗物种. 不需要事先发现贡献地;徒步直接使用其时代前缀池.
动作会显示一个独特的弹出,并带有关于徒步小径和寻找毛毛虫的口味文字。没有像可点击产卵那样的回弹冷却,你可以像动作的能量/时间/日限限制允许的那样经常进行动作。
关键含义:一个时代的任何池中没有的物种也不能通过徒步获得,池是大门.

3. 教学查询奖励

一些辅导求学授予特定物种,并在求学完成时直接相当于玩家的收藏.
  • 求学 "发现10种特有物种". 5× 彗星蛾(62种)
这是除进化追踪50点研究里程碑外彗星摩斯的第二条获取路径.

研究里程碑(第4条路径)

5个"特别研究"物种(61–65)通过研究系统的进化跟踪里程碑(25/50/75/100分)和大大师奖(所有5条路径均在100分)授予. 彗星摩斯还有上面的教程-遗赠路径;另外4条是只研究的.

发现系统(Clickable Spawns)

产卵方式:
  • 每个地点都定义了一个物种池(一个species_id的指令)到重量.
  • 当进入位置时,系统会滚动一个物种以这些数字加权.
  • 选定物种在采集之前保持不变。采集后,当该地点的重生日到来时,另一个物种就可供使用。
  • 毛虫只在其位置的时间窗口内渲染(有些是24小时,有些是日光)。
  • 点击后,该位置会输入按天数测量的再生冷却。 常见位置的再生速度更快(2至3天 ) , 史诗/罕见位置的速度更慢(5至10天 ) 。
  • 可点击的图示是每个物种的相同的通用图形,只是用位置的标志颜色来涂色——所以在点击之前无法分辨出那里有什么物种.
点击毛虫 :
  1. 增加你的收藏数量该物种。
  2. 设置位置的重发定时器( 下一步可用 = current_day + respawn_days) 。
  3. 打开卡特彼勒收藏弹出显示物种形象,名称,科学名称,稀有性,大小,生长时间,时代,描述,以及一个"NEW DISCVERY!!"/"Found Again!"/"生态系统物种"的横幅.
收藏对 Wild:
  • 你点击的卡特彼勒进入个人收藏.
  • 释放它们会将它们移入野生生态系统(每个时代跟踪),在那里繁殖.
  • 一旦发布,他们就会离开你的收藏。你可以有一个物种在收集,野生,或两者兼而有之。
  • 栖息地屏幕有一个"Ad All to Auto Era"按钮,将每一个收集的毛虫都丢入其本土时代.
添加所有路由:每个物种都有固定的原生时代。当你按Ad All时,每个采集的物种都会被引导到自己的时代。中世纪的物种总是去中世纪的,现在的物种总是去现在的。
没有部分发布: 栖息地屏幕只曝光添加全部按钮。没有UI可以释放单个物种, 拖住某些物种, 或将一个物种分化到各个时代。 添加全部是全无的 。
总物种:70种(月球1–30,蝴蝶31–60,特别61–65,水66–70).

通过卡特彼勒信息按钮打开. Modal 屏幕显示结合生态系统的全部逐个物种分解.

  • 人口总数——中世纪+目前
  • 物种计数——有多少不同的物种还活着.

  • 壁花——运行的成年人中,未成功交配的卷子总数

饲养寿命周期

生态系统更新一次 游戏中的日子。收集标本不繁殖:首先将其与 全部添加在这两个时代,生活总量结合了毛虫、成年人和大量人口。

人口少:成熟和繁殖

  1. 毛虫在它们的物种成熟 增长时间 (见目录),从该组的出生日测量。 70%,通过物种研究以及目前中世纪的协同改进.
  2. 幸存的成年人到达繁殖日 4天后, 成熟. 第一代成年人都有资格;后世拥有 floor(adult count × 0.5) 合格育种者,资格不能成功。
  3. 合格育种者对阵 50%的基础交配成功率成功育种者生产 每名50-100名婴儿 在研究之前 婴儿们会反抗 10%的基本存活率研究和当前协同作用可以提高这些比率。
  4. 成年人在一个确切的四岁天上繁殖,而不是每天。 最高的中世纪协同级可以在当天给一个成功的当代育种者第二批。
  5. 成年群体在年龄时被淘汰 超过 有效寿命: 7天前的研究/协同。
在1 000人或更少的人数中,存活率是单个的;较大的群体使用四舍五入的预期存活率。 单个收集的毛虫可能无法成熟或交配。 经常引用的“5-10个婴儿”是大致结果。 以成功交配为条件而不是每个标本的保证回报;婴儿存活也是随机的.
示例 第10天释放一个生长8个物种它可以在 第 18 号,在 第22天它的存活的婴儿开始自己的成长。 第26天研究/协同可以改变生存和寿命,因此这是一个时机性的例子,而不是预期的人口增长。
新鲜的采集标本被添加到现有毛虫类中,如果存在这种标本的话;它们不一定另组新的标本。一旦一个物种被跟踪成一个大种群,新的释放就会直接增加该种群。

Caterpillar Rearing - Complete System Guide
The Caterpillar Rearing system is a long-term breeding ecosystem that spans the Medieval and Present eras. Players collect wild caterpillars from clickable spawns scattered across nearly every map, release them into the wild, and let populations grow through a real lifecycle simulation. Large populations unlock crystal rewards, special items, mystical breeding bonuses, silk for fabric crafting, and Marina's premium fishing bait.
This guide covers everything: how to find caterpillars, how the breeding lifecycle works, the research system, all 70 species, every spawn location with weighted probabilities, the reward thresholds, and integration with fishing and silk crafting.

Quick Start

To start collecting caterpillars you need nothing — they appear as clickable wiggling sprites on maps automatically. To start breeding them, you need to visit either the Whispering Woods Uptown (Present) or the Whispering Woods (Medieval) and use the Manage Caterpillar Breeding action.
The first time you visit Whispering Woods Uptown (Present), Seraph auto-triggers a tutorial plot ("The Butterfly Effect") explaining the system.
The loop:
  1. Walk around any map — clickable caterpillar sprites spawn at certain hours.
  2. Click them to add to your collection.
  3. Visit either Whispering Woods location.
  4. Use the breeding action to open the Habitat screen.
  5. Press the "Add All" button to release every collected caterpillar to its appropriate era.
  6. Wait. Caterpillars mature, breed, and populate the ecosystem over in-game days.

Story Introduction

The tutorial plot "The Butterfly Effect" auto-fires the first time you enter Whispering Woods Uptown (Present), one time only. Awards +5 Seraph relationship.
Seraph greets you and explains that small actions can have large consequences across time. Raising butterflies and moths in the Present and Medieval eras helps restore the future — VOID's toxic influence on the Future-era forest can be reversed by sustaining large enough populations across the past and present.
Seraph covers:
  • Caterpillars produce silk for crafting and fish bait when populations get high
  • Use the Manage Caterpillar Breeding action at this location to oversee everything
  • Medieval populations provide mystical benefits to Present-era breeding
  • Researching caterpillar biology improves survival, breeding success, and lifespan
The plot ends with three response choices (polite / practical / enthusiastic) — flavor only, no mechanical difference.

Acquisition Methods

There are four ways to get new caterpillars into your collection: map discovery, hiking, tutorial rewards and research milestones.

1. Clickable Discovery (primary method)

Caterpillars appear as wiggling clickable sprites on world maps. Each location defines its own species pool with weighted probabilities — a location only spawns species in its pool. Click the sprite to add the caterpillar to your collection. Triggers a respawn cooldown (2–10 days) before another caterpillar can appear at that location.

2. Hiking & Special Discovery actions

Two actions work differently from clickable spawns:
  • Hike Mountains (Present-era, at Mountains Uptown)
  • Climb Mountain (Medieval-era, at Mountain Foothills)
These don't roll from a single location pool. Instead they pull from the union of every species in every pool of the player's current era, and pick one with equal probability.
This means every species that exists in any pool of the current era has the same chance of being found via hiking, including rare and epic species with low weights at ordinary spawn locations. No prior discovery of the contributing locations is required; hiking uses their era-prefixed pools directly.
The action displays a unique popup with flavor text about hiking the trail and finding a caterpillar. No respawn cooldown like clickable spawns — you can keep performing the action as often as the action's energy/time/daily-limit constraints allow.
Key implication: species not in any pool of an era cannot be obtained via hiking either. The pool is the gate.

3. Tutorial Quest Rewards

Some tutorial quests grant a specific species and amount directly to the player's collection on quest completion.
  • The tutorial quest "Discover 10 unique species" awards 5× Comet Moth (species 62)
This is the second acquisition path for the Comet Moth in addition to the Evolution Tracking 50-point research milestone.

Research milestones (4th path)

Five "Special Research" species (61–65) are awarded via the Research System's Evolution Tracking milestones (25/50/75/100 points) and the Grand Master reward (all 5 paths at 100). The Comet Moth also has the tutorial-quest path above; the other four are research-only.

Discovery System (Clickable Spawns)

How spawns work:
  • Each location defines a species pool (a dict of species_id to weight).
  • When you enter the location, the system rolls one species weighted by these numbers.
  • The selected species stays fixed until collected. After collecting, another becomes available when that location's respawn day is reached.
  • Caterpillars only render during their location's time window (some are 24-hour, some are daylight only).
  • After clicking, the location enters a respawn cooldown measured in days. Common locations respawn faster (2–3 days), epic/rare locations slower (5–10 days).
  • The clickable sprite is the same generic graphic for every species, just tinted with the location's signature color — so you can't tell what species is there until you click.
Clicking a caterpillar:
  1. Increments your collection count for that species.
  2. Sets the location's respawn timer (next available = current_day + respawn_days).
  3. Opens the Caterpillar Collection Popup showing species image, name, scientific name, rarity, size, growth time, era, description, and a "NEW DISCOVERY!" / "Found Again!" / "Ecosystem Species" banner.
Collection vs. Wild:
  • Caterpillars you click go into your personal collection.
  • Releasing them moves them into the wild ecosystem (tracked per era), where breeding happens.
  • Once released, they leave your collection. You can have a species in collection, in wild, or both.
  • The Habitat screen has an "Add All to Auto Era" button that drops every collected caterpillar into its native era.
Add All routing: every species has a fixed native era. When you press Add All, each collected species is routed to its own era. Medieval-era species always go to Medieval, present-era species always go to Present.
No partial release: the Habitat screen only exposes the Add All button. There's no UI to release a single species, hold some back, or split a species across eras. Add All is all-or-nothing.
Total species: 70 (Moths 1–30, Butterflies 31–60, Special 61–65, Water 66–70).

Breeding Lifecycle

The ecosystem updates once per in-game day. Collection specimens do not breed: first release them with Add All. Living totals combine caterpillars, adults and large populations in both eras.

Small populations: maturation and breeding

  1. Caterpillars mature at their species' growth time (see the catalog), measured from the group's birth day. Base maturation survival is 70%, improved by Species Research and, in the Present, Medieval synergy.
  2. Surviving adults reach their breeding day four days after maturation. Generation 1 adults are all eligible; later generations have floor(adult count × 0.5) eligible breeders. Eligibility is not mating success.
  3. Eligible breeders roll against 50% base mating success. Successful breeders produce 50–100 babies each before research. The babies then roll against 10% base survival. Research and Present-era synergy can improve these rates.
  4. Adults breed on that one exact age-four day, not every day. The highest Medieval synergy tier can give a successful Present-era breeder a second batch on the same day.
  5. Adult groups are removed when their age exceeds the effective lifespan: seven days before research/synergy. This is independent of the species' caterpillar growth time.
At counts of 1,000 or fewer, survival is rolled individually; larger groups use rounded expected survival. A single collected caterpillar may fail to mature or mate. The often-quoted “5–10 babies” is the approximate result conditional on successful mating, not a guaranteed return from every specimen; baby survival is random too.
Example: release a growth-eight species on Day 10. It can mature on Day 18, breed on Day 22, and its surviving babies begin their own growth then. With the base seven-day adult lifespan, that adult group is removed on Day 26, when adult age reaches eight. Research/synergy can change survival and lifespan, so this is a timing example rather than a promised population increase.
Fresh collection specimens are added to an existing caterpillar group for that species if one exists; they do not necessarily start a separate new cohort. Once a species is tracked as a large population, new releases add directly to that population instead.

Large populations: the 1,000-per-species threshold

At 1,000 living members of one species in one era, that species switches from age groups to a population total. The threshold is not the combined ecosystem total. Its mature/caterpillar split in the information display becomes an estimate rather than a count of age-tracked individuals.
Large populations grow once daily using:
new population = min(99,000,000, floor(population × (1 + 0.5 × mating rate × average babies × baby survival / 1000)))
Here the mating rate includes Breeding Research and the Present's Medieval mating bonus. Average babies is the midpoint of 50 to (100 + Population Analysis). Baby survival is 0.10 + Environmental Research × 0.002.

Species growth time, larval survival, adult lifespan and the second-batch bonus do not enter this large-population formula. Species and Evolution research remain useful for age-tracked groups and Evolution's rewards, but they do not accelerate an already consolidated population.
The cap is 99 million per species per era, not per ecosystem. Multiple species can therefore exceed the 100-million and billion combined milestones. Once consolidated, a species does not run through a new age-four breeding cycle every day.

Open Research from the Habitat screen. Each of the five paths caps at 100. A session costs 120 minutes, 30 Energy and 30 Calm; you need both resources available.
points = min(20, (5 + floor(modified Intelligence / 8)) × skill multiplier)

Progression by Milestone

This is a long-term ecosystem. Calendar-day promises are unreliable because early survival is random, species have different growth times, and 1,000-per-species populations switch to slower daily percentage growth.
  1. Discover and release: collect from maps you can access, visit either Whispering Woods and press Add All. Start both native-era populations; specimens in collection do not grow.
  2. Research early: learn Researching if available, then use modified Intelligence to improve session efficiency. Environmental, Population and Breeding improve offspring and established-population growth; Evolution also supplies special species.
  3. Widen biodiversity: use map cooldowns and hiking to build multiple species. Multiple established populations grow in parallel and each has its own 99M cap.
  4. Build the Medieval foundation: its 1K, 10K, 50K and 100K tiers progressively help the Present. Watch the era-specific total rather than the combined counter for these bonuses.
  5. Claim practical milestones: visit the woods at 500K; use Marina's free Caterpillar Bait at 1M and Acknowledged+; revisit at 5M, and use the Medieval woods specifically at 10M and 15M.
  6. Complete research and larger goals: release the special research rewards, continue growing diverse populations, and claim the 100M plot. The billion follow-up requires its other story/character completions too.
Do not plan on a guaranteed 5–10× increase every twelve days or a guaranteed 500K by Day 60. Check actual living totals after daily updates and use the correct formula for each species' tracking stage.

Tips and Troubleshooting

Collection and hiking

* Clickable spawns are free, but each location has its own 2–10-day cooldown and inclusive hour window. A room's locked access can still prevent ordinary collection there.
* Hike Mountains: Present Mountains Uptown, base 100 minutes and 75 Energy, +1 Stamina. Climb Mountain: Medieval Mountain Foothills, base 130 minutes and 85 Energy, +1 Stamina. Neither has a configured daily limit.
* Hiking uniformly chooses from all species in location pools whose keys begin with the current era's prefix. You do not need to have visited or unlocked every contributing location. The species' native era can differ from the era where it is found; Add All still routes by the native era.
* There are 46 Present hiking candidates and 24 Medieval candidates: each candidate has a 1/46 or 1/24 chance per relevant hike. Research-only species 61–65 and the unprefixed Brothel Garden's 20/22 are absent from both hiking pools.
* Lime and Poplar Hawkmoths therefore still require ordinary Brothel Garden collection. Its two-species pool gives each a 50% chance. Water species 66–70 have their ordinary spawn at Waterfall Uptown and are also possible through Present hiking.

Population and research

* The collection grew, but the ecosystem did not: release it with Add All. The button releases all held specimens to their native eras; the screen has no partial-release selector.
* A new adult did not produce offspring: maturation survival, eligibility, mating and baby survival are separate checks. Later generations have only half their adults eligible, rounded down; a single later-generation adult can have no eligible breeder.
* Growth slowed near 1,000 of a species: it changed to daily percentage growth. This is expected. Use the large-population rates above instead of projecting repeated full batches.
* Research appears capped: each path ends at 100 and sessions award at most 20. Intelligence 40 plus Researching already reaches that session cap.
* A special species is missing from the ecosystem: Evolution/Grand Master rewards enter the collection first. Check there, then Add All.
* A population reward has not appeared: reach its combined living total and visit the correct era's woods. For the billion scene, check all its separate completion requirements.

Silk and fishing

Collect Moth Silk is available at both woods, with base 70 minutes, 20 Energy and 20 Calm. Its fabric tier uses the whole living ecosystem, including butterfly populations, not only species called moths. Use the reward table above under Practical Uses.
Marina's Caterpillar Bait checks population as a prerequisite; it does not subtract a million specimens. It is a permanent upgrade, with no bait-selection toggle. Return during her 04:00–17:00 hut duty for the action; Tobbin's around-the-clock public fishing services do not replace her personal upgrade scene.

Backup and restoration

The ecosystem backup becomes active after a daily update with more than 1,000 combined population and some research progress. Subsequent daily validation can restore the last good snapshot if population drops by at least 500 or research decreases, and otherwise refreshes the backup when the data is valid.
The Habitat screen's manual Restore Backup uses that stored snapshot. It restores ecosystem populations, research, collection and discovery cooldown data; it is not a way to reroll only one unsuccessful mating. The button is useful when the displayed ecosystem needs restoration, and can return those records to their earlier snapshot values.
