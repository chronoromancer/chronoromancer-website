Overview

Coradio teaches Sovereign Edge and appears across three medieval destinations. His named skill lesson is a resource purchase; it does not require a Fighters Guild rank or an additional Ability 60 check.

Where to find them

  • Tournament Grounds: odd-numbered days, 07:00–18:00. The skill lesson starts here.
  • Fighters Guild: even-numbered days, 12:00–20:00.
  • Training Room: odd days 18:00–21:00; even days 20:00–23:00, once the room exists.

Route and relationship milestones

Bring 5 Crystals, 120 Energy and 45 Calm for the 300-minute lesson at Tournament Grounds. It teaches Sovereign Edge once. The full version and extra access in its notice are required.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If you see him at the Guild or Training Room, wait for an odd day and visit Tournament Grounds for the lesson.
  • Already knowing Sovereign Edge hides the one-time lesson.
  • Crystals are spent by the lesson; they are not merely an ownership threshold.

概览

Coradio 教授王权锋刃，并在三个中世纪地点活动。这项指定课程按资源付费，不要求战士公会阶级，也没有额外的能力 60 检查。

在哪里找到

  • 锦标赛场地：奇数日 07:00–18:00，技能课程从这里开始。
  • 战士公会：偶数日 12:00–20:00。
  • 训练室：房间建好后，奇数日 18:00–21:00、偶数日 20:00–23:00。

路线与关系节点

前往锦标赛场地学习时，需准备 5 水晶、120 精力与 45 镇定，耗时 300 分钟，一次性学会王权锋刃。还需完整版及进入提示所列的额外内容权限。

常见卡点

  • 若在公会或训练室见到他，学习课程仍需等到奇数日并前往锦标赛场地。
  • 已经学会王权锋刃后，一次性课程会隐藏。
  • 课程会消耗水晶，并非只要求持有。
