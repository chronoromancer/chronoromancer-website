Overview

Onyx is a recruitable maid with a five-part dungeon search and a late-night shift at Rose & Thorn. Her ongoing content includes shared maid encounters, a Perception lesson and repeatable Maid Service.

Where to find them

First recruit Violet, Mandarin, Lychee, Lime and Rain, and reach Close Friend (200) with each. Visit Maids Quarters 08:00–20:00 for Whispers of Six. That shared scene is required before the first dungeon sighting.

Find Onyx in this order: floors 46–50 → 31–35 → 41–45 → floor 13 → 36–40. The first sighting belongs to the ordinary encounter pool. Each later step has a 75% priority roll on an eligible random encounter, after its predecessor. Completing the fifth meeting recruits her.

After recruitment, find her in Maids Quarters daily 02:00–06:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Return during her shift for Onyx's Arrival, with Elara home from vacation. Her four subsequent shared scenes need the other recruited maids listed below; they have their own event windows.

Read the Negative Space opens at Known (25), Communication 45:30 minutes, +1 Perception and 10 direct affection, once. Ordinary Talk gives 1 base affection per 15 minutes, with a nominal daily allowance of 30.

At Chrysalia Friend (100), with Onyx recruited, visit Chrysalia at Industrial Waste Processing Zone for her maid-equipment gift. The actual item is Onyx's Mourning Veil: +25% maid cleaning, −20% mess and +8% business Wealth. Equip it to Onyx through Maid Management. It is not the similarly named ordinary key item.

Use the Maid System guide for cleaning levels, equipment slots, daily mess and apprentice management. Her personal menu does not teach Ritual Mastery.

Favorite stats are Perception, Mystic, Intelligence. Learning Ritual Mastery improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The first sighting requires all five earlier maids and Whispers of Six; raising only one maid’s affection is insufficient.
  • Follow the five floor groups in order, including the return to floor 13. A later floor group cannot advance an unfinished earlier meeting.
  • Shared maid events use their listed event windows, which may extend outside Onyx’s ordinary 02:00–06:00 portrait shift.

概览

Onyx是可招募的女仆，需先完成5段地下城寻访，随后在玫瑰与荆棘值深夜班。她还有女仆共同事件、感知奖励与可重复的女仆服务。

去哪里找

先招募Violet、Mandarin、Lychee、Lime和Rain，并分别达到亲密朋友（各200好感）。08:00–20:00前往女仆宿舍，完成六之低语，之后地下城才可能出现第一次相遇。

按顺序寻找：46–50层 → 31–35层 → 41–45层 → 第13层 → 36–40层。第一次属于普通随机事件；之后每一步在符合楼层的随机遭遇中有75%的优先触发判定，且必须先完成前一步。第5次见面后招募她。

招募后，她每天02:00–06:00出现在女仆宿舍。

路线与关系进展

Elara结束度假后，在Onyx的班次回访，触发Onyx的到来。其余4次共同事件需招募下表所列的女仆，并遵守事件自身的时段。

解读负空间需已知（25好感）与沟通45，消耗30分钟，一次性获得感知+1和10直接好感。日常交谈每15分钟获得1基础好感，每日标示额度为30次。

清洁等级、装备槽、每日脏乱与学徒管理请看女仆系统指南。她的个人菜单不教授仪式精通。

偏好的属性为感知、神秘、智力。学会仪式精通可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 首次相遇要求前5位女仆全部招募，并完成六之低语，只提高一位女仆的好感不够。
  • 按5组楼层顺序探索，中途需回到第13层；未完成前一步时，后面的楼层不会推进寻访。
  • 女仆共同事件按各自时段触发，部分可超出Onyx平常02:00–06:00的头像班次。
