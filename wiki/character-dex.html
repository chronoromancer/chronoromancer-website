Overview

Dex is a Future Fringe Zone contact who provides ambient dialogue and appears in supporting story events. His current role does not include a direct relationship-action list.

Where to find Dex

Once Future travel is available, go to Future → Fringe Zone → Unregistered Housing Complex. His scheduled visit runs across midnight, from 16:00 until 01:00 the next morning.

LocationPortrait scheduleCondition
Unregistered Housing Complex16:00–01:00

Windows refer to the time you start an action; the ending time is excluded.

Route and relationship milestones

Dex’s involvement is reached through the relevant Jinx progression and current Objectives. There is no separate Dex affection grind or personal heart-to-heart chain to unlock from his portrait.

Activities and rewards

You can meet his normal portrait and ambient remarks during the schedule. No direct Talk button, gift, skill lesson, date or recruitment service is currently provided. His presence in a story event does not imply an additional shop or paid information action.

Missing actions and useful checks

An empty action list is expected here. Check the overnight schedule if you cannot find his portrait; after 01:00, return later in the afternoon. For his supporting events, follow Jinx’s current objective rather than repeatedly visiting Dex.

概览

Dex是未来边缘区的一位联系人，会提供日常台词并参与辅助剧情。目前没有独立的角色关系动作列表。

出现地点与时间

能前往未来后，走未来 → 边缘区 → 未注册住宅区。他的出勤跨越午夜，从16:00持续到次日01:00。

地点头像出现时间条件
未注册住宅区16:00–01:00

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

与Dex有关的事件随Jinx的相应进展及当前目标推进，不需要另外刷Dex关系，也没有可从头像解锁的个人谈心链。

行动与奖励

在出勤时段可看见普通头像与日常回应。当前没有独立交谈按钮、赠礼、技能课程、约会或招募服务；在剧情中出现，也不代表另有商店或付费买情报的动作。

动作缺失时的检查

这里的动作列表为空是当前正常状态。找不到头像时，检查跨午夜的时间：01:00后要等到下午再来。辅助事件请按Jinx当前目标推进，不必反复拜访Dex。
