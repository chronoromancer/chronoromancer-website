概览

Kent 是欢迎的,观察的客栈管理员 中世纪村落的旅店他目前的友谊路线包括每日谈话、一次性魅力奖、讲故事课和三次共同的性格相遇,他也出现在可选的妓院-客户轮换中。

当前内容和解决问题

  • 讨论似乎给予爱, 它的当前处理器给出 1个基点每天多达20次。
  • 故事智慧是没有的 : 检查 已知,通讯 38,以及是否已经要求一次性奖励。
  • 讲故事课没有可用: 检查 知识、创意、18、300美元,以及你是否已经学会了技能。
  • 旅店没有安排: 结束 Muramasa's 空环覆盖 两个角色都知道返回时 下午8时至11时.
  • "老治病者誓言"失传: 输入 村疗养院,上午8时至7时,随着前场景,Amelia的介绍和所有三种关系要求都达到了.

  • Kent不是选中的客户端 : 旋转是随机和共享的。检查 名誉 3,NTR,妓院经营和符合条件的工人.

Overview

Kent is the welcoming, observant innkeeper at Traveler's Inn in the Medieval Village Outskirts. His current friendship route consists of daily conversation, a one-time Charm reward, a Storytelling lesson and three shared character encounters. He also appears in the optional brothel-client rotation.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit Traveler's Inn at any hour and open Kent's portrait. Use Talk to Kent, up to 20 times per game day, to build friendship.
  2. At Known and Communication 38, choose Storytelling Wisdom for Charm +1 and 8 direct affection, taking 30 minutes, once.
  3. At Acquaintance and Creativity 18, learn Storytelling for 150 minutes, $300 and 50 Energy. This teaches the skill and gives 10 base Kent affection. The newly learned skill already applies to that lesson's shared relationship calculation.
  4. After Muramasa's The Empty Ring, reach Known with both Kent and Muramasa, then revisit the inn 8–11 PM for An Arrangement at the Inn.
  5. After that arrangement and Amelia's introduction, visit Village Healer's Hut, 8 AM–7 PM, while Muramasa Friend, Kent Acknowledged and Amelia Acknowledged, for The Old Healer's Oath.
  6. After recruiting Ryia as active worker 30, with her not on vacation, return to the inn for A Room Without Questions, at any hour. It has no extra Kent or Ryia affection-stage prerequisite.
  7. Optionally, at brothel reputation level 3, Kent can enter the separate Receive a Client rotation with NTR enabled. This does not replace his personal conversation, reward or lesson progression.
There is no required Muramasa or Ryia scene before either Storytelling Wisdom or the Storytelling lesson. His normal all-hours schedule does not remove the separate evening window on the inn arrangement.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Kent enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level3. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Kent. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Kent has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is lively conversation, with Gem listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Brandy, Gem, Ryia.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Kent affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Kent.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • Talk appears to give affection despite zero in older notes: its current handler gives 1 base point, up to 20 times daily.
  • Storytelling Wisdom is unavailable: check Known, Communication 38, and whether its one-time reward was already claimed.
  • The Storytelling lesson is unavailable: check Acquaintance, Creativity 18, $300, and whether you already learned the skill.
  • An Arrangement at the Inn is missing: finish Muramasa's The Empty Ring, reach Known with both characters, and return 8–11 PM.
  • The Old Healer's Oath is missing: enter Village Healer's Hut, 8 AM–7 PM, with the prior scene, Amelia's introduction and all three relationship requirements met.
  • A Room Without Questions is missing: Ryia must be active as worker 30 and not on vacation. Her being previously found is not enough.
  • Kent is not the selected client: the rotation is random and shared daily. Check reputation 3, NTR, brothel operation and eligible workers.
  • Looking for a romance route: Kent has no current personal H2H, date, ordinary gift, apartment visit, worker recruitment, resort, Kupie, pregnancy or Present venue-invitation route.
