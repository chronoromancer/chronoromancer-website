概览

Dawn 是个优秀的大学生游泳运动员 现为上城大学池她目前的路线包括三个个人的H2H,四个独立的日期,花儿礼物和一次性的能力奖赏,她还在偶数日访问上城海滩,一旦到达Acquaintance.

Dawn — Complete Character Guide

Overview

Dawn is an elite university swimmer who trains at the Present Uptown University Pool. Her current route contains three personal H2Hs, four separate dates, Flowers gifts and a one-time Ability reward. She also visits the Uptown beach on even-numbered days once you reach Acquaintance.

Unlocking the Pool

  1. Find Diane at the Town Hall Office and use Commission Pool. The commission has no affection requirement beyond Stranger, costs $2,000,000 and 180 minutes, and is available while the pool is not built.
  2. Complete its construction scene, which opens University Pool travel from the University Hall through the pool-built flag.
  3. Enter from the Hall using University Pool, a zero-minute travel action, and visit Dawn 9 AM–5 PM.
Building the pool opens the normal travel route; Dawn's own pool schedule does not add a separate story or relationship prerequisite. The Boardwalk Sunset Date needs Diane's separate boardwalk restoration later.

Step-by-Step Personal Route

  1. Use Pool Talk from Stranger, up to four times per day.
  2. At Known, give Flowers, use Race Pacing if you have Communication 35, and complete Lane Rhythm at the pool. Lane Rhythm does not require a prior date or automatic introduction.
  3. At Acquaintance, take Pool Training Date, starting from the pool 9 AM–3 PM. Her 5–8 PM beach visits on even days and separate Beach Talk also become available.
  4. At Friend, take the Zoo Date, starting 9 AM–4 PM, and the separate Win Her a Prize Voucher date, starting noon–5 PM. Both start from her pool menu.
  5. At Close Friend, complete False Start at the pool after Lane Rhythm. If Diane's boardwalk restoration is completed, take Boardwalk Sunset Date from Dawn's beach menu on an even day, 5–8 PM.
  6. At Crush, complete Quiet Water at the pool, 3–5 PM, after False Start.
The three H2Hs form their own chain: Lane Rhythm → False Start → Quiet Water. Each has its stated stage requirement. The four dates are independent, one-time activities; none is a prerequisite for that H2H chain. Dawn has no separate automatic personal plot chain to wait for.

If Progress Stalls

  • Missing pool travel means Diane's Commission Pool construction has not completed. Enter the University Pool from the University Hall.
  • Dawn's pool portrait appears 9 AM–5 PM. Her beach portrait needs Acquaintance, an even-numbered day, and 5–8 PM.
  • Give Flowers starts at Known. Use Pool Talk beforehand; there is no required automatic introduction.
  • False Start needs both Close Friend and Lane Rhythm. Quiet Water needs Crush, False Start and the 3–5 PM pool window.
  • Dates do not replace H2H prerequisites. Each of the four dates has its own completion flag.
  • For Boardwalk Sunset, complete Diane's boardwalk restoration, then start from Dawn's beach menu on an even evening.
  • Dawn's currently implemented route has no apartment encounter, worker recruitment or Endurance Training lesson. Her personal content consists of the actions, three H2Hs and four dates above.
