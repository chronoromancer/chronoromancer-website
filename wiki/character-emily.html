Overview

Emily is the waitress at the Uptown Cafe in the Present era. Her current route includes cafe interactions, evening park meetings, Beach Day and Future Dreams heart-to-hearts, four separate outings, shared scenes, a branching apartment visit, repeatable venue invitations, seasonal activities and an optional pregnancy continuation. This guide gives the actual implemented requirements and rewards without retelling major story outcomes.

Route Order

  1. Visit the Uptown Cafe, 7 AM–3 PM, and use Tip Emily to begin. Each tip costs 10 minutes and $10, with up to four daily uses. At Stranger and Acknowledged it gives 1 base affection.
  2. At Acknowledged, add Talk to Emily: 20 minutes, 1 base affection, three times per day. Its reward does not increase to 5 at later stages. At Known, prefer Talk and Service & Emotion Tips; the tip handler gives 0 base points at Known despite leaving the Tip action available.
  3. At Known, with Communication 20, use Service & Emotion Tips once: 30 minutes, +1 Charm and 8 direct affection. At Acquaintance, add daily Chocolate for 5 base points; tipping again gives 1 at this stage and disappears above Acquaintance.
  4. At 160 affection, choose Invite to Beach at the Cafe for Beach Day. This H2H takes 180 minutes and 40 energy and completes once.
  5. At Friend, the Zoo, Fair and separate Voucher prize date become available from the Cafe. They are not prerequisites for each other or for the next H2H. Also complete An Old Friend Walks In with Joey at Known to open later invitations.
  6. At Close Friend, give daily Flowers for 20 base points. Emily also appears at the Uptown Park, 7–9 PM on even-numbered days. Talk and Flowers share their daily allowances across the Cafe and Park.
  7. At 300 affection, start Heart to Heart / Future Dreams from the evening Park. It takes 120 minutes and 20 energy, restores 10 calm, and completes once.
  8. At Crush, use the Park's Romantic Walk and the Housing Block's Knock on Emily's Door. At Romantic, the Cafe's one-time Extra Cream action becomes available. At 700 affection, the Park's repeatable private video action opens.
  9. At Crush, after An Old Friend Walks In, Emily can also join repeatable KTV and Night Club Invite Dates. These are separate from the one-time Cafe/Boardwalk outings and Katie's shared KTV scene.
  10. Continue her shared scenes and apartment branches. Her family continuation has its own late-route requirements and an explicit choice; ordinary intimacy does not automatically start it.
