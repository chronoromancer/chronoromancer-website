概览

Bayne 是个30岁的保镖 混乱辛迪加她目前的路线包括Syndicate介绍和支持场景、四个个人关系场景加上Downtime、可重复的对话和人群控制课。她的普通对话开始时间比大多数NPC晚: 谈话需要知识.

Overview

Bayne is a 30-year-old bodyguard in the Chaos Syndicate. Her current route includes Syndicate introduction and supporting scenes, four personal relationship scenes plus Downtime, repeatable conversation and a Crowd Control lesson. Her ordinary conversation starts later than most NPCs: Talk requires Acquaintance.

Meeting Bayne and Unlocking Talk

  1. In the Future Prime Sector, use the map's Syndicate Gates destination. The Syndicate is part of the current built-in game locations.
  2. Your first eligible gate arrival triggers The Gates of Chaos, giving 3 base affection to Bayne as well as rewards to Poison and Motley.
  3. Return to the gates after that prologue for The Gatekeeper. It gives 5 base affection, and Caring → Caring supplies the best two Bayne choice bonuses, +3 → +4.
  4. Continue the Syndicate route and its supporting scenes until Bayne reaches Acquaintance. Her initial scenes do not guarantee that stage on their own, because final gains depend on your bonuses and the shared random calculation.
  5. At Known, visit The Den between 9 AM and noon for the one-time Downtime scene: 1 base affection, with Flirty +4 as its highest choice bonus. This is a plot appearance outside her ordinary gates schedule.
  6. At Acquaintance, use Talk at the gates: 15 minutes, 1 base affection, five times per day. There is no configured gold or energy charge.
Do not wait at Stranger looking for an unavailable Talk button. Use the introduction and broader Syndicate events first. Meeting her favorite-stat thresholds and already knowing Intimidating Presence improve gains, but they are bonuses rather than prerequisites for the introduction.

Personal Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Meet Kitty and Zodiac, completing their introduction scenes.
  2. Reach Friend with Bayne and return to the gates for The Post after both introductions. It gives 10 base affection plus choices.
  3. At Friend and Charm 18, you can also learn Crowd Control at the gates. The one-time lesson costs 180 minutes, $350, 40 energy and 35 calm, gives 12 base affection and teaches the skill. It disappears once Crowd Control is learned.
  4. At Close Friend, return to the gates after The Post for Standing Watch, worth 15 base affection plus choices.
  5. At Crush, return after Standing Watch for The Vigil, worth 10 base affection plus choices.
  6. Build Bayne to Intimate, Motley to Romantic, and complete the other Syndicate milestones below. Then visit Motley's Lair for The Order, worth 20 base affection to Bayne and 5 to Motley, plus choices.
The Order requires all five completed scenes: The Vigil, Motley's The Truth, Kitty's Tamed, Poison's The Test, and Chamel's The Real Thing. The current other-character routes require Romantic with Motley, Intimate with Kitty, Crush with Poison, and Intimate with Chamel to reach those final prerequisite scenes. Their earlier route flags also need to be complete; affection alone does not bypass them.
There is no separately authored personal date, home H2H, apartment visit or gift action in Bayne's current menu. Intimidating Presence is her relationship bonus skill; her lesson teaches Crowd Control, not Intimidating Presence.

If Progress Stalls

  • Talk starts at Acquaintance, not Stranger. Continue the Syndicate plot and use the available automatic rewards beforehand.
  • Downtime occurs in The Den, 9 AM–noon, rather than at the gates.
  • The Post needs both Kitty and Zodiac introductions, as well as Friend with Bayne.
  • Crowd Control requires Charm 18 and Friend; Strength 18 does not meet its stat gate.
  • Standing Watch and The Vigil each need their preceding personal scene completed.
  • The Order needs Intimate with Bayne, Romantic with Motley and all five named scene flags. Check Kitty, Poison and Chamel's routes too.
  • The Beat 85 alliance continuation also needs After the Line; finishing the numbered story beat alone does not satisfy its Syndicate prerequisite.
  • Higher affection does not create a separate date or H2H that is absent from her current authored action list.
