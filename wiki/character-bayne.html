Overview

Bayne’s route is part of Chaos Syndicate progression. Shared introductions supply early affection before his ordinary conversation becomes available; his personal scenes then require both affection and earlier group progress.

Where to find them

Use the Future Prime Sector map’s Syndicate Gates destination, subject to its content-access prompt. Bayne’s ordinary portrait is there all day. The separate Downtime scene is at The Den09:00–12:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Complete the gates introduction and gatekeeper event, then the available group scenes. Ordinary Talk requires Acquaintance 50, so early shared-scene rewards matter; there is no earlier personal gift action to bypass this gate.

The personal milestones below progress through Known 25, Friend 100, Close Friend 200, Crush 400 and Intimate 1600. The final lair scene also needs Motley at Romantic 800 and the completed personal routes listed; it is a group-route gate rather than an additional repeatable service.

Bayne also participates in the later shared Syndicate storyline. Follow the Syndicate guide for the full group chain and its Army connection, keeping personal and group prerequisites separate.

After Story85 and After the Line, the shared alliance thread begins at Operations Center, then visits The Parlor → Syndicate Gates → The Funhouse → The Parlor → Motley's Lair → The Parlor. Each stop needs the previous scene; no further numbered-story gate is added within that chain. The shared guide covers the choices and Army functions.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If Talk is unavailable below 50, continue the shared Syndicate scenes rather than waiting for a missing gift menu.
  • For the final personal scene, check every required earlier route and Motley’s relationship; Bayne’s affection alone is insufficient.

概览

Bayne的路线与混沌辛迪加进度相连。早期共同介绍提供好感，之后才开放普通交谈；个人事件还需要前面的团体进展。

去哪里找

从未来核心区地图选择辛迪加大门，并满足入口的内容权限提示。Bayne的普通头像全天在这里；独立的休息时间事件则在巢穴09:00–12:00触发。

路线与关系进展

先完成大门介绍、守门人事件及当前可进行的共同事件。普通交谈要求相识50，因此早期共同事件奖励很有用；没有更早的个人送礼行动能绕过门槛。

下表个人进展依次使用已知25、朋友100、亲密朋友200、迷恋400、亲密1600。最后的巢穴事件还要求Motley浪漫感800，以及列出的相关个人路线；这是共同路线门槛，不是另一项重复服务。

Bayne也参与较后的辛迪加共同剧情。完整团体事件及军团联系见辛迪加指南，个人前置与团体前置分开检查。

完成主线85及“防线之后”后，共同联盟路线从作战中心开始，随后依次前往会客厅→辛迪加大门→欢乐屋→会客厅→Motley的巢穴→会客厅。每处均要求上一事件完成，这条链内部不再追加主线编号门槛；选项与军团功能见共同指南。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 好感不足50无法交谈时，继续共同辛迪加事件，不必寻找不存在的个人送礼菜单。
  • 最后个人事件需要相关路线全部完成及Motley关系达标，不能只提高Bayne好感。
