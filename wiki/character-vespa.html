概览

Vespa是中世纪时期King Graham的女儿,她的路线包括城堡友谊,三次谈话和日期,20事件法庭招聘链,诺贝尔转介函,工人#22培训,精心设计的升级和锡雷纳岛内容,该指南涵盖了先决条件和奖励,而没有重述路线的个人启示.

普通亲情和转介信件

与 Vespa 交谈 需要15分钟,并给予1个实际基本感情,最多4天,其限度由城堡和所有招募后地点分担。

Gallery and Troubleshooting

Vespa has three current NPC intimacy gallery images, unlocking at100,250 and500 NPC Intimacy Experience. Sources include Closer's kiss, worker training, daily Sunset Walk and Private Beach Time. NPC intimacy experience, player Erotic Experience, worker Experience and affection are separate.
  • Missing castle portrait: check whether worker #22 is active; recruitment moves Vespa permanently to her brothel schedule.
  • Court event stalled: check the preceding numbered event, exact reputation/affection gates, location and time. Steps18/19 also require Festival Date/Night Bazaar respectively.
  • Missing garden, lounge or bar portrait after recruitment: check the day parity and Audrey, Vixen or Brandy's active worker status.
  • Missing gift upgrade: bring its crafted gift, reach In Love, finish the named conversation and return Vespa home.
  • Missing vacation group event: all listed workers must be active and vacationing simultaneously.
