Overview

Valerie is the secretary at the Downtown Office Building reception desk in the Present Era. Her current personal content consists of an automatic introduction and daily Talk. She also appears in two later office scenes in Miki's company-ownership route.

Her portrait has no required quest flag, weekday or affection stage. Visit the reception during the listed hours and select her portrait.

Welcome to the Offices

Welcome to the Offices triggers automatically once when you enter the Downtown Office Building. It has no relationship, company-ownership or previous-scene gate and no event-specific time restriction. The introduction's automatic trigger is distinct from her normal 9 AM–5 PM portrait availability.
Completion gives 5 base affection. Its three replies add:

Caring 3 8

Choose Funny for the highest total. No separate action time, money, Energy, Calm, player experience or NPC intimacy experience is configured for the introduction.

Daily Talk and Relationship Progress

Talk to Valerie takes 15 minutes, gives 1 actual base affection, and permits four positive uses per day. All four take 60 minutes. Stranger is sufficient, and no additional money, Energy or Calm cost is configured.
After the daily allowance is exhausted, the handler submits a negative relationship result. Return on the next game day rather than continuing exhausted Talks.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.
Market Analysis is Valerie's relationship bonus if already learned; her current portrait menu does not teach it. Higher affection changes her ordinary stage-based dialogue but does not unlock an additional personal date or heart-to-heart.

Shared Office Appearances

These automatic events belong to Miki's company route. Valerie's own affection is not an additional gate. Each occurs once, and both must happen before Evangeline's Controlling Interest is completed.

Recommended Order and Troubleshooting

  1. Enter the Downtown Office Building for the one-time introduction; choose Funny for9 base affection.
  2. Visit her portrait during 9 AM–5 PM and use up to four positive daily Talks.
  3. For her shared office appearances, progress Miki's company route and visit the specific Boardroom or Offices locations during 9 AM–3 PM before Controlling Interest is completed.
  • Her portrait is missing: check the Office Building reception and its 9 AM–5 PM schedule; the Boardroom and Offices are different scene locations.
  • The introduction did not repeat: it is one-time.
  • A shared scene is missing: check its Miki completion flags, company ownership and the Controlling Interest blocker; Valerie affection alone cannot unlock it.
  • No lesson or romance menu appears: Valerie currently has no personal gift, skill lesson, one-time stat reward, date, heart-to-heart, apartment intimacy or worker recruitment. No NPC intimacy gallery assets exist for her.
