Overview

Valerie is found at the Present Office Building. Her current personal interaction is Talk, with an introductory office encounter and appearances in the shared company-ownership route.

Where to find Valerie

Go to Present → Downtown → Office Building, daily 09:00–17:00. Entering the building can trigger Welcome to the Offices once.

LocationPortrait scheduleCondition
Office Building09:00–17:00

Windows refer to the time you start an action; the ending time is excluded.

Route and relationship milestones

For the shared corporate appearances, follow Miki’s ownership route. Still Not an Appointment requires 25 owned companies after Projected Household Growth; Requested by Name requires all 50, the preceding bedroom milestone and Miki’s two later corporate heart-to-hearts.

These encounters do not require a separate Valerie relationship stage. They also do not create a new personal romance action on her portrait.

Automatic encounters: complete start conditions

Enter the indicated location during its scene window. These encounters complete once; fixed base relationship rewards below are separate from choice bonuses. A missing hour restriction means the scene itself has no extra clock gate.

Activities and rewards

Talk costs 15 minutes and gives 1 actual base relationship point, with four safe uses daily. There is no ordinary gift, skill lesson, personal date or heart-to-heart in her current menu.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

概览

Valerie出现在现代办公楼。现有个人互动是交谈，另有办公楼初见，以及公司持有路线中的共同事件。

出现地点与时间

前往现代 → 市中心 → 办公楼，每天09:00—17:00。进入时可一次性触发欢迎来到办公楼。

地点头像出现时间条件
办公楼09:00–17:00

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

共同企业事件请沿Miki的公司持有路线推进。仍然没有预约接在家庭增长预测之后，要求拥有25家公司；点名邀请则要求全部50家公司、前一项卧室里程碑及Miki后两段企业谈心。

这些事件没有额外的Valerie关系阶段要求，也不会因此在她的头像上新增个人恋爱动作。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，实际给予1点基础关系，每日安全额度4次。现有菜单没有普通礼物、技能教学、个人约会或谈心。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。
