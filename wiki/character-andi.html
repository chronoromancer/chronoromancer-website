Overview

Andi is a nineteen-year-old, unmodified human living in the Future. Her self-contained romance explores conversation and dating in a world that normally schedules both. Her current route includes six automatic scenes, three illustrated heart-to-hearts and four optional dates.

How to Meet Andi

Visit the Human Interaction Optimization Center in the Future Fringe Zone. Learn System Hacking to reveal Hack Social Algorithms. The action costs 30 minutes and 15 energy and has difficulty 20; AI Programming and Digital Forensics are its listed bonus skills.
The hack must succeed. An attempted or failed hack does not unlock Andi. After success, visit the center between 11 AM and 9 PM to trigger One Hundred Percent. Finish that scene to make her ordinary character interactions available. Her later schedule and route continue to require the successful hack state.

Talk to Andi gives 2 base points in 15 minutes, up to three times per day. At Known, Give Chocolate costs 10 minutes and one Chocolate, gives 5 base points, and can be used twice per day. Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

If Progress Stalls

  • No Andi at the center: successfully complete Hack Social Algorithms and finish One Hundred Percent during her appearance hours.
  • No next automatic scene: check its affection stage and earlier H2H, then return to the center before 9 PM.
  • Missing H2H: come back after 6 PM and finish its preceding plot. These are one-time conversations and disappear after completion.
  • Missing date: check affection and the outing's time window. Her dates do not require completing the romance first.
  • After No Percentage Needed, you have completed the currently authored romance; the optional dates can be finished separately.
