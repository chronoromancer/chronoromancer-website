概览

Jinx 是个街头贩子 VOID 未来初级部门的消费者成就中心她目前的路线包括:十景主要的个人连锁、可选的住房相遇、三个心对心、两个日期、一个谈判课、军队招募和三个装备交易。这一走法提供了机械要求和有用的答案,同时将故事的启示和结果留给游戏。

解决问题

  • 无法在早晨找到 Jinx : 她的消费者中心的肖像开始于 中午12时招募后,上午只能到 从早上8点起的偶数天用品.
  • 消费者中心的肖像在招聘后失踪: 使用一个 奇数当前游戏日。午夜后窗口检查新一天的均衡。
  • 在第二篇文章中, 联合国 两个地点共用三用途津贴.
  • 谈判缺失: 检查 已知、情报16和有偿访问如果你已经知道技能,它就消失了。
  • 天园约会8点失踪: 从消费者中心开始 中午4时而不是供应。
  • 用品短缺H2H: 所有三个H2H是 消费者中心的行动被征召后,在奇日返回那里。
  • Ghost 午夜后失踪的列车: 它自己的自动窗口是 下午6点半;以下目录错误场景也允许 午夜2点.
  • 一个变量在她出现前就不见了: 在她招募的肖像画存在之前,自动相遇会一直运行。 Close Friend, 库存错误, Story第34和8话 AM-10 PM.
  • 选择风险后仍锁定的变量 : 军队的招募取决于 库存错误和Story而不是头吻H2H。
  • 丢失或残废的设备交易: 使用 用品肖像,携带足够多的 所有四种垦湾资源类型,然后检查军队的仓库。

Overview

Jinx is the street hustler at the VOID Consumer Fulfillment Center in the Future Prime Sector. Her current route includes a ten-scene main personal chain, an optional housing encounter, three Heart-to-Hearts, two dates, a Negotiation lesson, Army recruitment and three equipment trades. This walkthrough gives the mechanical requirements and useful answers while leaving the story revelations and outcomes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit VOID Consumer Fulfillment Center, starting noon–midnight or midnight–2 AM, for The Hustle. Choose Kind for 6 total base Jinx affection, then use Talk to Jinx, up to three times per game day.
  2. At Known, return for House Advantage after The Hustle. At Acquaintance, return for Honest Thief after House Advantage. Keep using Talk on subsequent days to meet the next stage.
  3. At Friend, continue the automatic chain through The Perfect Miss → Harmless Rebellion. After Honest Thief, you can also choose Rules of the Hustle and Neon Bazaar Date. The date starts 4–10 PM.
  4. At Friend, make an optional visit to Unregistered Housing Complex in the Fringe Zone for Honest Walls. It is separate from the main chain and is not an extra prerequisite to the later scenes, dates or Army recruitment.
  5. At Close Friend, continue No Free Variables, starting 6 PM–midnight or midnight–2 AM, then The Wrong Kind of Quiet, starting noon–midnight or midnight–2 AM. Complete The House Always Knows after No Free Variables and Rules of the Hustle. Take Skygarden Date after The Wrong Kind of Quiet, using the actual noon–4 PM portrait window.
  6. Complete Story Beat 34, then return to the Consumer Center 6 PM–midnight after The Wrong Kind of Quiet for Ghost in the Ledger. After that, return 6 PM–midnight or midnight–2 AM for Inventory Error.
  7. At Crush, after Ghost in the Ledger and The House Always Knows, choose Choose the Risk. This H2H is separate from Army recruitment and is not required to unlock The One Variable.
  8. After Inventory Error and Story Beat 34, with Jinx Close Friend, visit Supplies in the resistance Operations Center during 8 AM–10 PM for The One Variable. This automatic scene completes once and recruits Jinx into the Army.
  9. After recruitment, find her Consumer Center portrait on odd-numbered days, in its original hours, and her Supplies portrait on even-numbered days, 8 AM–8 PM. H2Hs and dates remain at the Consumer Center. Supplies offers Talk, Negotiation and the three Army equipment trades.

Story Beat 34 additionally gates Ghost in the Ledger and The One Variable. The H2H chain and optional Honest Walls scene have their own requirements.

Talk and Negotiation Lesson

Talk to Jinx takes 15 minutes and gives 1 base point, up to three times per game day. The allowance is shared between the Consumer Center and Supplies. Her ordinary Talk handler uses −1 base points with shared over-limit handling after the three-use allowance; costs still apply.
At Known, with Intelligence 16, choose Negotiation at either available portrait. It costs 180 minutes, $400, 40 Energy and 30 Calm, requires paid-version access, teaches the Negotiation skill, and awards 10 base Jinx affection through the shared lesson handler.
The lesson disappears once you already know Negotiation, including if learned elsewhere. It is effectively a one-time skill acquisition, rather than repeatable affection training. Sleight of Hand is her special relationship skill; it is not the lesson taught here.
Jinx has no ordinary Flowers, Book, Wine or other gift actions. Her main progression uses Talk, scenes, H2Hs and dates.

Army Recruitment and Supplies Trades

Complete The One Variable through its automatic Supplies encounter to unlock Jinx in the Army. It requires Close Friend, Inventory Error and Story Beat 34, starting 8 AM–10 PM. It does not add Crush, Choose the Risk, a fee, or any ordinary gift prerequisite.
Replaying the scene in EXTRAS does not perform Army recruitment. Her three H2Hs and two dates remain available at the Consumer Center according to their own completion and odd-day portrait schedule.
After recruitment, visit her Supplies portrait on an even-numbered day, 8 AM–8 PM, for the three trades below. Troubleshooting

  • Cannot find Jinx in the morning: her Consumer Center portrait starts at noon. After recruitment, mornings are available only at Supplies on even days, from 8 AM.
  • Consumer Center portrait missing after recruitment: use an odd-numbered current game day. The after-midnight window checks the new day's parity.
  • Talk stopped rewarding affection at the second post: the three-use allowance is shared across both locations.
  • Negotiation missing: check Known, Intelligence 16 and paid access. It disappears if you already know the skill.
  • Skygarden Date missing at 8 AM: start from the Consumer Center noon–4 PM, not Supplies.
  • H2H missing at Supplies: all three personal H2Hs are Consumer Center actions. After recruitment, return there on an odd day.
  • Ghost in the Ledger missing after midnight: its own automatic window is 6 PM–midnight; the following Inventory Error scene also permits midnight–2 AM.
  • The One Variable missing before she appears at Supplies: the automatic encounter runs before her recruited portrait exists. Check Close Friend, Inventory Error, Story Beat 34 and 8 AM–10 PM.
  • The One Variable still locked after Choose the Risk: Army recruitment depends on Inventory Error and Story Beat 34, not the first-kiss H2H.
  • Equipment trade missing or disabled: use the even-day Supplies portrait, carry enough of all four Reclamation Bay resource types, and check Army storage afterward.
