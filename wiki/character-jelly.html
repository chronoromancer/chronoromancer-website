概览

Jelly 这是一种在中世纪地牢中遇到的愉快的成人黏液女孩。她的路线包括被招募进入指定的Rose& Thorn细胞、日常交谈、三部个人动画、五次共享走廊相遇以及怪物护理和培训系统。该指南涵盖可玩性要求和奖励,但没有重复遭遇结果。

Overview

Jelly is a cheerful adult slime girl encountered in the Medieval dungeon. Her route includes recruitment into an assigned Rose & Thorn cell, daily conversations, three personal animations, five shared corridor encounters and the monster care and training system. This guide covers the playable requirements and rewards without retelling encounter outcomes.

Recruiting Jelly

  1. Own the Rose & Thorn and build its Cell Corridor, plus a vacant cell among cells 2–8. Cell 1 is reserved for Bobo and does not count as an available recruitment cell.
  2. Fight the named Jelly enemy in the normal dungeon on floors 6–12. Ordinary Slime enemies are a different entry and do not increase Jelly's defeat counter.
  3. For a reliable named battle, investigate the hidden wall at floor 10, tile (2, 10) after the corridor is built. This encounter remains available while Jelly is not already active.
  4. Win five named Jelly battles. Recruitment rolls begin on the sixth victory. The chance is 10 + 10 × (total victories − 5) + player Taming, capped at 100%: the first roll is 20% + Taming, and each further victory adds ten percentage points.
  5. When the recruitment offer appears with a vacant built cell, choose “Welcome her to the [operation name].” Accepting assigns her to that cell, activates her monster record and gives 10 direct ordinary affection, enough for Acknowledged.
  6. Visit her assigned cell for ordinary Talk and the later personal animations. Use the separate monster-management screen for care and training.
Infinity-mode victories do not increment the counter or offer recruitment. Once she is active, further capture processing stops. If no cell is available, Slime Girl – No Cell does not recruit her; build a vacant cell and win another eligible battle. Choosing Set her free also leaves her unrecruited.
The named enemy is weak to Ice, strong against Light and absorbs Light at 90. Her current skills are Acid Spit, Toxic Cloud and Strike, with 20 magic resistance, 10% damage reduction, 10% dodge chance and 1.5× damage. Use the named encounter rather than counting generic slime fights.

Assigned Cell and Daily Talk

After recruitment, her portrait is eligible 24 hours a day, on every game day, in whichever cell was allocated. She is not permanently assigned to a particular numbered cell, and the dungeon wall is not her home after recruitment.
Talk to Jelly takes 15 minutes, gives 1 base affection, and is allowed five times per game day. Its action advertises Stranger, but the portrait's availability requires Acknowledged; accepting recruitment normally supplies that stage through its direct ten-point reward.
Talk has no configured money, item or Energy cost. Beyond its allowance, the handler records −1 base points with shared over-limit handling. There is no ordinary gift, skill lesson or personal H2H button on this portrait.

Daily Monster Care

Use the monster-management screen to select active Jelly for a care session. Care is unavailable while she is resting or after she has already received that day's care visit. Her initial monster record starts at 70 tamed, 90 Health, 0 Stress and 0 monster experience.
  • Feed consumes 2 Food and adds 10 tamed, normally capped at 100. A failed feeding attempt without enough Food can be retried after obtaining Food.
  • A care session allows one use of each available category: Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Several categories can be used during the same session; their allowances are separate.
  • Teach and Training need at least 30 tamed. Feed first if her tamed value falls below this threshold. Ordinary affection stage does not replace it.
  • Teach selects a dialogue topic; it does not teach the player Engulf, Empathy or a skill lesson.
  • Tool effects differ in tamed, stress, health and experience. Inspect the selected tool's effects; Claw uses the shared higher tamed cap.
  • Ordinary daily decay is 3 tamed per day after the first care visit. The personal animations can also increase tamed by five without replacing daily care.
  • Smelling Salts reduce Stress by 10 for all active monsters through the shared care interface.
The care meter is separate from the portrait's ordinary affection. Feeding and monster training do not have an additional direct Jelly affection reward configured. A high Friend or Crush stage does not prevent tamed decay.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Jelly uses the monster system, separate from the numbered Rose & Thorn worker roster. She has no worker recruitment H2H, worker wage/clothing route, Sirena Isle worker vacation, Kupie selection, apartment invitation, personal date or pregnancy route configured.
  • No recruitment offer after five wins: the first roll is victory six, against named Jelly, in the normal dungeon.
  • Hidden-wall battle missing: build Cell Corridor, then check floor 10, tile (2, 10) while Jelly remains unrecruited.
  • Successful roll but she does not move in: build a vacant cell 2–8 and accept the next recruitment offer. Cell 1 is not counted.
  • Cannot find her after recruitment: check her assigned cell, not the dungeon or worker bedroom. Her portrait needs active recruitment and Acknowledged.
  • Later trick missing: check Known / Close Friend / Crush, paid access and the selected animation's daily allowance; the prior trick is not an extra prerequisite.
  • Training locked despite Friend affection: restore 30 tamed, then check resting and daily care status.
  • Corridor event missing: reach Friend with both participants and visit Cell Corridor. Each runs once; Arachne's encounter also requires both active.
