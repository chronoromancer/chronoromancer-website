Overview

Jelly uses both the ordinary relationship menu and the monster-care system. Affection, monster Tamed level, monster experience and player Erotic Experience are separate counters.

Where to find them

Find Jelly encounters on normal Dungeon floors 6–12. After building the cell corridor, the hidden wall at floor 10 tile(2,10) can force her encounter while she is not recruited.

Prepare an already built vacant cell after reserved cell 1. A successful recruitment assigns her to that actual cell; her portrait is available there all day once active.

Route and relationship milestones

Recruitment rolls start on the sixth victory against Jelly at 20% plus base Taming. Each later victory adds 10 percentage points, capped at 100%. Infinity Dungeon battles do not advance recruitment. A successful roll without a vacant built cell does not recruit her; prepare space and win again.

After recruitment, Talk builds ordinary affection. The three personal activities unlock at Known 25, Close Friend 200 and Crush 400. Each has its own once/day allowance,60 min/100 Energy and its indicated content access.

The three activities give 10/25/40 base affection and 50/65/80 player Erotic Experience respectively. Each also adds 5 Tamed without using the daily taming visit, and has a 1%chance to increase an already learned Taming stat by 1.

Corridor scenes connect Bobo, Myriss, Melanthe, Bessie, Daphne and Arachne when both residents are active and both relationships reach Friend 100.

The separate monster-care menu has its own daily visit and rest rules. Teaching/training require Tamed 30; training progressively opens eight types and gives monster stats/experience. Use the Brothel guide for feeding, tools, work and rest.

Favorite stats are Taming, Charm, Stamina. Learning Empathy improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Fight Jelly herself, not an ordinary slime enemy, for this recruitment counter.
  • The room must already be constructed and vacant; a no-room scene does not complete recruitment.
  • Ordinary affection does not replace Tamed 30, and player Erotic Experience is not monster experience.

概览

Jelly同时使用普通关系菜单与魔物照料系统。好感、魔物驯服度、魔物经验、玩家情趣经验分别计算。

去哪里找

在普通地下城第6–12层寻找Jelly。建好牢房走廊且她尚未加入时，第10层坐标(2,10)的隐藏墙可固定触发她的遭遇。

准备保留牢房1之后的一间已建成空牢房。成功招募后，她全天出现在实际分配的牢房，不固定在某个编号。

路线与关系进展

第六次击败Jelly开始判定招募，概率为20%加玩家基础驯服；之后每胜一次再增加10个百分点，上限100%。无限地下城战斗不累计招募进度。判定成功但没有已建成空牢房仍不会招募，先准备空间，再次获胜。

三种活动分别增加10／25／40基础好感及50／65／80玩家情趣经验，每次还增加5驯服度，不占每日驯服访问；若已有驯服属性，另有1%概率增加1点。

走廊事件连接Bobo、Myriss、Melanthe、Bessie、Daphne及Arachne，要求双方已入住，且两人的关系均达到朋友100。

独立魔物照料菜单另有每日访问及休息规则。教导、训练要求驯服度30，训练依次开放八种类型，增加魔物属性与经验。喂食、工具、工作与休息见青楼指南。

偏好的属性为驯服、魅力、耐力。学会共情可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 要击败Jelly本身，普通史莱姆敌人不能累计她的招募计数。
  • 房间必须已经建好且无人入住，没有空房的事件不算招募完成。
  • 普通好感不能替代30驯服度，玩家情趣经验也不是魔物经验。
