Overview

Mindy is met at the Present Uptown Metro Station. Her route includes a reunion, three heart-to-hearts, four dates and remote evening visits. Her three heart-to-hearts do not form a single mandatory chain.

Where to find Mindy

Her ordinary Metro portrait is available on even days 09:00–12:00. At Acknowledged (10), she also appears at Uptown Park daily 13:00–16:00 and Residential Block 17:00–19:00. The automatic reunion itself has a wider 09:00–15:00 window.

Route and relationship milestones

Finish A Familiar Face and return for Another Familiar Face. Vending Machine Trouble follows the second Metro encounter. Walk in the Park needs Known (25); Getting Closer needs 300 relationship points. These are independent unlocks rather than prerequisites for each other.

At Friend (100), visit the Uptown Kitchen 16:00–21:00 for the recipe encounter. Crush (400), that recipe and Getting Closer qualify her for KTV/Night Club invitations. Her apartment door is a separate location action at the Residential Block, 19:00–23:00, available at Crush without waiting for the ordinary 17:00–19:00 portrait.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 15 minutes and gives 1 actual base point, with seven safe uses shared across locations. Flowers allow two daily gifts; Teddy and Jewelry each allow one. High School Stories adds Charm +1 once at Known (25) and Communication 24.

Knock on Mindy’s Door costs 180 minutes and 40 Energy, with three safe uses daily. Its optional trust-focused conversation teaches Trust Building once; the other branches use different stat and relationship checks. You do not need to choose the same branch each visit.

KTV and Night Club invitations start 20:00–23:00. KTV costs 120 minutes, $150 and 40 Energy; Night Club costs 120 minutes, $100 and 30 Energy. Each venue has one daily invitation shared across its guest list. Optional pregnancy care is tracked separately; the Pregnancy guide explains readiness and check-ins.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

The park’s Voucher Fair invitation starts 13:00–16:00, while Fair Date starts 13:00–15:00. Stargazing and Boardwalk invitations are on her Residential Block portrait, 17:00–19:00. Remote apartment visits begin at 19:00 instead.

概览

Mindy的初次重逢在现代上城区地铁站。路线包括三段谈心、四项约会与远程晚间拜访；三段谈心并不是必须逐一接续的同一条链。

出现地点与时间

普通地铁站头像在双数日09:00—12:00出现。达到已遇见（10）后，每天13:00—16:00可在上城公园、17:00—19:00可在住宅街区见她。自动重逢事件本身采用更宽的09:00—15:00时段。

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

先完成熟悉的面孔，再返回触发又一张熟悉的面孔。自动售货机麻烦接在第二次地铁事件后；公园散步要求已知（25），逐渐靠近要求300点关系。三者独立开放，并不互为前置。

达到朋友（100）后，16:00—21:00到上城区厨房推进食谱事件。迷恋（400）、食谱事件及逐渐靠近都完成后，她会加入KTV与夜总会邀请名单。公寓敲门则是住宅街区独立的地点动作，迷恋阶段每天19:00—23:00开放，不依赖17:00—19:00的普通头像。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，实际获得1点基础关系，各地点共用每日7次安全额度。鲜花每天可送2次，泰迪熊与珠宝各1次。高中故事要求已知（25）、沟通24，一次性增加魅力1点。

敲Mindy的门消耗180分钟、40精力，每日安全额度3次。可选的信任对话会一次性教会建立信任；其他分支检查不同的属性与关系条件。每次拜访可以选择不同分支。

KTV和夜总会邀请在20:00—23:00开始。KTV消耗120分钟、150财富、40精力；夜总会消耗120分钟、100财富、30精力。各场所的全部来宾共用每日一次邀请额度。可选孕期照料另行记录，准备与探望方法见怀孕系统指南。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

公园的兑换券约会实际开始于13:00—16:00，普通游园会约会则为13:00—15:00。观星与木板路邀约绑定住宅街区头像，17:00—19:00可开始；远程公寓拜访则从19:00才开放。
