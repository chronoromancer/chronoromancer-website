Overview

Mindy is a Present-era friend from high school, found around the Metro Station, Uptown Park and Housing Block. Her route includes the reunion, shared daily conversations and gifts, three heart-to-hearts, four dates, a one-time Charm reward, a private animation, branching apartment visits, KTV/Night Club invitations, Kupie and an optional family continuation.
This guide explains playable requirements and rewards while leaving the scenes' personal revelations and story outcomes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter Present Metro Station, 9 AM–3 PM, for A Familiar Face, once. This automatic introduction is not limited to her even-day portrait schedule.
  2. Return to the metro 9 AM–5 PM after the introduction for Another Familiar Face. Flirty gives its best choice reward. It unlocks Vending Machine Trouble, which you start from her 9 AM–noon even-day metro portrait.
  3. Use Talk at any of her scheduled portraits to reach Known. At the park, add Flowers, claim High School Stories with Communication 24 and complete Walk in the Park.
  4. At Friend, add Teddy at the Housing Block, complete Mindy's Secret Recipe in your mansion kitchen 4–9 PM, and use the one-time Fair, Stargazing and Voucher prize dates as optional affection sources.
  5. At 300 affection, complete Getting Closer at the park. Its final kiss gives 5 Mindy NPC intimacy XP. Its Intelligence response scores less than the ordinary best reply.
  6. At Close Friend, add Jewelry at the Housing Block. The Boardwalk Sunset Date additionally needs Diane's boardwalk, paid/Higher Power access and a 5–7 PM start.
  7. At Crush, use Knock on Mindy's Door from the Housing Block 7–11 PM. Visit the short trust branch once to learn Trust Building, then explore the apartment branches below.
  8. At 700 affection, her ordinary Housing Block portrait offers Intimate with Mindy, 5–7 PM, once daily. The apartment is a separate remote activity.
  9. At Crush, after Secret Recipe and Getting Closer, use repeatable KTV/Night Club invitations, 8–11 PM. Each venue has its own shared daily allowance.
  10. For Kupie, reach Friend in the ordinary game and 5,000 total minigame clicks. For the optional family route, follow the separate readiness and timing requirements below.
There is no required chain connecting the three H2Hs: Vending needs the second metro encounter, Walk needs Known, and Getting Closer needs 300 affection. Apartment access also does not independently require Getting Closer.

Troubleshooting

  • Mindy is absent from the metro: her ordinary portrait is 9 AM–noon on even days; the automatic reunion scenes use wider hours and every day.
  • Vending is missing after the first reunion: finish Another Familiar Face first, then use her even-day morning portrait.
  • Getting Closer is missing: its gate is 300 ordinary affection, rather than the 200 Close Friend threshold or a prior Walk completion.
  • Apartment is missing when her street portrait disappears: use the remote Knock on Mindy's Door action, 7–11 PM, at Crush.
  • Apartment ends after a kiss: it checks existing affection + the current path's accumulated base points against 600. Higher affection or an applicable direct-position branch reaches later content.
  • A special reply is weaker: Getting Closer's Intelligence reply gives 10, below the ordinary best 14. Apartment paths likewise have different scores and requirements.
  • Invitations are missing: check Crush, Secret Recipe and Getting Closer, the 8–11 PM window, venue allowance and Night Club supporter access.
  • Kupie is locked: check Friend and 5,000 cumulative clicks, rather than five ordinary dates.
  • Family opening is missing: check 2,000 ordinary affection / 500 Mindy NPC XP, feature access, Akira's protocol and the two required encounters.
Mindy has no configured Christmas scene, numbered brothel-worker training, maid cleaning progression or resort vacation assignment. Her Kupie viewing rewards are separate album scenes.
