Overview

Guard is the throne-room sentry. His current appearance is a portrait and greeting; the court’s conversations and quests belong to their named participants.

Where to find them

From the Royal Castle, enter Throne Room for 0 min. Guard is present daily 00:00–10:00 and 17:00–24:00. King Graham occupies the room 10:00–17:00.

Route and relationship milestones

For a royal audience, return 10:00–17:00 and choose King Graham. For the guard captain’s friendship, follow Gruff’s own schedule.

There is no Guard relationship threshold, personal quest or heart-to-heart to complete. His greeting does not begin a hidden recruitment or dating route.

Activities and rewards

The room’s travel and court-story options remain separate from Guard’s portrait. Use the court character guides for the actual scene requirements.

What usually blocks the next step

  • An empty personal action menu is expected here. Waiting for another day or raising unrelated relationships will not add Talk or gifts.
  • If you arrive 10:00–17:00, look for King Graham instead of Guard.

概览

Guard负责王座厅值守，目前提供头像与招呼；宫廷交谈和任务由各自的参与者提供。

去哪里找

从皇家城堡进入王座厅，0分钟。Guard每天00:00–10:00、17:00–24:00在场；10:00–17:00则由King Graham在场。

路线与关系进展

觐见国王请在10:00–17:00返回并选择King Graham。若要推进卫队长的友谊，请按Gruff的行程寻找他。

Guard没有需要完成的关系门槛、个人任务或交心；招呼不会开启隐藏的招募或约会路线。

行动与奖励

房间中的移动与宫廷剧情选项和Guard头像分开。具体事件条件见相关宫廷角色指南。

卡住时检查什么

  • 个人行动菜单为空是正常情况，换一天或提高其他角色关系不会追加交谈、送礼。
  • 10:00–17:00到访时，请选择King Graham。
