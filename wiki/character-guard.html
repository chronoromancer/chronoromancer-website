Overview

The Guard watches the Throne Room while King Graham is absent. He is a supporting character with a portrait and greeting, but no playable interaction menu or personal walkthrough in the current game. He is separate from Gruff, the Captain of the Royal Guard.

Where and When to Find Him

Travel to the Royal Castle in the King's Demesne and choose Enter Castle to reach the Throne Room. Entering the castle itself takes 0 minutes.
Time of day Throne Room character
Midnight–10 AM Guard
10 AM–5 PM King Graham
5 PM–midnight Guard
These schedules apply every game day. If you came for a royal audience, return during 10 AM–5 PM and use King Graham's menu. The Guard cannot accept royal quests or provide the King's conversation actions.

Current Content

The Guard's character data defines his scheduled appearance and introductory dialogue, but its action list is empty. There is therefore no Talk, gift, skill lesson, date, Heart-to-Heart, apartment, recruitment, worker, resort or pregnancy activity to unlock for this NPC. Waiting for a different relationship stage will not add a Guard menu.
The room's travel choices and royal quest activities belong to the location or other characters. See King Graham, Barnard, Annette, Elara and Vespa for the court and castle routes. For daily conversations with the royal guard captain, use Gruff's separate schedule.

Troubleshooting

  • Guard portrait absent: during 10 AM–5 PM, King Graham occupies the throne instead.
  • No interaction buttons: this is the Guard's current defined behavior; he has no personal actions.
  • Trying to find Gruff: Gruff visits the Merchants Quarter, Grand Bazaar and Noble Quarter. He does not use the Guard's Throne Room schedule.
