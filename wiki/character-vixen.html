Overview

Vixen is a27-year-old confidence artist in the Medieval era, with Evil alignment. Her favorite stats are Rogue45, Communication55 and Charm60, and her special relationship skill is Silver Tongue. Her route covers six lessons, two one-time dates, recruitment as worker #19, the VIP Lounge, crafted training upgrades and island content. Personal revelations are left to the game.

Gallery and Troubleshooting

Vixen's three current gallery images unlock at100,250 and500 NPC Intimacy Experience. NPC intimacy is separate from affection, player Erotic Experience and worker Experience.
  • Start with the odd-day Alehouse portrait if you have not reached Acknowledged.
  • Check Known before searching the Noble Quarter, then its even-day3–6 PM restriction after recruitment.
  • Check Romantic and brothel Reputation10 for recruitment; it triggers on even evenings at the Bards Guild.
