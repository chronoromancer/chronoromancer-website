Overview

Saber is Synergy Corp's 29-year-old security chief in the Future. Her personal route becomes available at the Temporal Defense Barracks after the relevant story progression. It contains daily Talk, three heart-to-hearts, three independent dates, and three shared evening encounters with Kitty.
This guide explains access and mechanical requirements without narrating the story events behind her change of location.

Recommended Route Order

  1. Complete the story access above, then use Talk to Saber at the Barracks to reach Friend. Four successful daily talks cost 80 minutes for 4 base points, before relationship bonuses.
  2. After Story Beat 77, complete No Uniform to Hide Behind at Saber Friend.
  3. Raise Kitty to Acquaintance, then visit Barracks, 6–11 PM, for Off Duty Means Off Duty. It requires that first Saber H2H completed.
  4. At Saber Close Friend, complete Rules of Engagement after Off Duty.
  5. Raise Kitty to Friend, then visit Training Hall, 6–11 PM, for Crossing the Line. It requires Rules of Engagement completed.
  6. At Saber Crush, complete Permission to Want at Barracks, 7–11 PM, after Crossing the Line.
  7. With both Saber and Kitty at Crush, return to Barracks, 6–11 PM, for No One Stands Outside, after Permission to Want.
  8. Take the independent dates as their affection stages become available: Friend — An Unplanned Afternoon; Crush — One Credit Each; Romantic — Under Blue Glass. The dates do not replace any shared-route prerequisite.
The route deliberately alternates Saber H2H → shared automatic scene → next Saber H2H. Grinding Saber alone cannot supply Kitty's required Acquaintance, Friend and Crush stages. Consult Kitty's walkthrough for her own progression.

Army Recruitment and Other Systems

Completing Story Beat 76 acquires Saber's army unit automatically. There is no additional portrait Recruit Saber purchase, no ordinary affection threshold for that story recruitment, and no brothel-worker invitation.
Her army unit's battles, army equipment and progression belong to the separate Temporal Defense systems. Training army equipment does not complete a Saber H2H or supply Kitty's required relationship stage.
Saber's current content has no ordinary gifts, skill lessons, apartment conversation, venue Invite Date, Kupie entry, Christmas gift action, worker training or resort route. If Progress Stalls

  • Tower portrait has no options: this is her initial configuration. Complete Story Beat 76, then visit the Barracks.
  • Story Beat 76 does not start: complete Story Beat 75 and The Bug, then visit Operations Center, 10 PM–4 AM. The Bug requires No Plan Survives first.
  • No Uniform missing: check Saber Friend, Story Beat 77 completed, and that the H2H is still uncompleted.
  • Off Duty missing: complete No Uniform, reach Kitty Acquaintance, and visit Barracks, 6–11 PM.
  • Rules of Engagement missing: reach Saber Close Friend and finish Off Duty. Kitty's own personal route does not substitute for this shared flag.
  • Crossing the Line missing: finish Rules, reach Kitty Friend, and visit the Training Hall, 6–11 PM.
  • Permission to Want missing: reach Saber Crush, finish Crossing, and start at Barracks, 7–11 PM.
  • No One Stands Outside missing: finish Permission, reach both Crush, and return to Barracks, 6–11 PM.
  • Date missing: check its separate stage, starting window, uncompleted flag, funds and energy. The other two dates are not prerequisites.
  • Daily Talk turns negative: stop after four positive uses and return on the next game day.
  • Dating status differs from affection: Permission to Want sets the status; ordinary stage and NPC intimacy experience are separate meters.
  • Favorite-stat bonuses missing: the thresholds are 80 each, and Tactical Awareness must actually be learned for its special multiplier.
