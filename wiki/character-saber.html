Overview

Saber’s personal route becomes available late in the main story. It has three H2Hs, three independent dates and three shared evening scenes with Kitty. Keep both relationships moving: Kitty’s affection is an actual requirement for the shared scenes.

Where to find them

Before Story 76, Saber is at Synergy Corp Corporate Tower as a story contact. After completing Story 76, look in the stronghold’s Barracks, all day, every day. Her personal portrait actions are here. Complete Story 77 before starting the first H2H; ordinary Barracks Talk can be used after her move.

Route and relationship milestones

Follow this alternating order. Each H2H and shared scene completes once; the shared scenes trigger on entering the location, not from Kitty’s Big Top menu.

All three dates begin from Saber’s Barracks portrait, each once. They do not require the H2H/shared-scene sequence and do not replace it. Her favorite stats are Strength, Ability and Perception; Tactical Awareness improves her relationship bonus.

Story 76 also grants her Army unit; there is no separate affection requirement or portrait recruitment purchase. Army equipment, unit progression and deployment are handled in the Army and Tower Defense guides. Her H2H intimacy record is separate from ordinary affection; Permission to Want records 5 kissing experience. Her personal menu currently centers on Talk, H2Hs and these dates.

Time windows below are start times; 22:59 is the last minute of a window ending at 23:00. Base affection is adjusted by the relationship bonuses; direct affection is added as listed.

What usually blocks the next step

  • At the tower but no personal actions: progress through Story 76, then use Barracks.
  • First H2H missing: Story 77 and Friend (100) are both needed.
  • Shared scene missing: check Kitty’s separate affection threshold and enter the correct location before 23:00. Training Hall is used for the middle scene.
  • The third H2H starts at 19:00, even though the neighboring shared scenes start at 18:00.
  • A date or army upgrade does not count as a required H2H. Completed dates disappear after use.

概览

Saber的个人路线在主线后期开放，包含三次谈心、三场独立约会，以及三场与Kitty共同参与的夜间事件。需要同时培养两人的好感，Kitty的阶段是共同事件的实际条件。

去哪里找

主线76之前，Saber作为剧情联系人出现在新能集公司企业塔。完成主线76后，请到要塞的兵营，全天可见；个人行动也从这里开始。第一次谈心还要完成主线77，搬到兵营后即可先交谈。

路线与关系进展

按下表交替推进。每次谈心与共同事件都只完成一次。共同事件在进入地点时触发，不从Kitty的大帐篷菜单开始。

三场约会都从兵营的Saber头像开始，各一次。它们不要求先完成谈心与共同事件，也不能代替那些步骤。她偏好的属性为力量、能力与感知；战术意识可提高关系加成。

完成主线76还会获得她的军队单位，不另要求好感，也不需要从头像菜单付费招募。装备、部队成长与部署见军队及塔防指南。谈心中的亲密记录与普通好感分开计算；“想要的许可”会记录5点接吻经验。她的个人菜单目前以交谈、谈心和上述约会为主。

下表时间均指开始行动的时间；例如，结束于23:00的时段，最晚可在22:59开始。基础好感会受关系加成影响；直接好感按所列数值增加。

卡住时检查什么

  • 塔楼里没有个人行动：先完成主线76，再去兵营。
  • 第一次谈心没出现：必须同时完成主线77并达到朋友（100）。
  • 共同事件没触发：检查Kitty自己的好感阶段，并在23:00前进入正确地点；中间一场在训练厅。
  • 第三次谈心从19:00才开放，前后的共同事件则从18:00开始。
  • 约会或军队升级不算完成前置谈心；已完成的约会会从菜单消失。
