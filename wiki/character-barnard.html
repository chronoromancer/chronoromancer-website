Optional Private-Client Bookings

Prince Barnard enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level15. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Prince Barnard. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Vespa #22 is excluded from this client's booking choices. His authored preference is an appreciative audience, with Ryia listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Ryia, Sophie, Vixen.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Prince Barnard affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Prince Barnard.

If Progress Stalls

  • Start his introduction at the Courtyard, 11 AM–3 PM.
  • Use even-numbered in-game days for ordinary Castle Garden conversations after 8 PM.
  • The first court event requires selecting Discuss the Rose & Thorn Accounts, after both foundation flags, both Acknowledged stages and reputation 4.
  • Check the immediately preceding court scene, current brothel reputation and every listed NPC stage.
  • Keep Elara home for the court scenes that require her not to be vacationing.
  • Fernando's route needs both characters' affection and its preceding scenes; purchasing outfits does not replace those gates.
  • Isolde's bedroom event needs her Not His Bride H2H in addition to its plot-stage conditions.
  • There is no separate personal romance action to unlock by grinding Barnard to In Love. His authored progress is in these supporting scene threads.
