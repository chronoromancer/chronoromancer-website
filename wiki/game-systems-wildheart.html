Explore Wildheart: Wild Woods

Wildheart: Wild Woods is a separately installed optional world. It is not part of every base-game package. Its current cast is Hime, Huli, Nanami, Natsumi, Rin and Taiga, with personal routes and shared community events.

Install, enter and leave

  1. Install the official optional package for your game version. Open Mod Manager from the main menu and enable Wildheart: Wild Woods.
  2. Restart after changing its enabled state, and meet the access-redemption requirement supplied with that package. Enabling the package and access redemption are separate steps.
  3. In the Deep Woods, reach coordinate (3,2) and choose Enter Wild Woods. The entrance and Gateway’s Return to Deep Woods action take no time in this version.
  4. Use the world map for destinations. Riverglass Café is entered from Riverside Promenade. The Gateway provides the return route to the Deep Woods.

A practical order

Begin the Natsumi, Hime and Taiga introductions, then reach Known with each and finish their second scenes. Visit Canopy Commons between 09:00 and 17:00 for A Welcome Worth Walking. After that, advance each personal route and take its first Heart-to-Heart before the Friend-stage follow-up.

Huli’s early library route, Nanami’s café route and Rin’s market route can be started independently. Their later shared events have the exact prerequisites above. Do not assume a similar name or a visit to the river completes someone’s invitation.

If an event does not start

Confirm the world is actually installed and enabled, then check access, location, the starting hour, named previous events and ordinary affection. A completed scene will not automatically run again; EXTRAS replays are separate from current-timeline progress.

探索Wildheart：野林

「Wildheart：野林」是需要另行安装的可选世界，并非每个游戏本体安装包都包含。此版人物有Hime、Huli、Nanami、Natsumi、Rin与Taiga，各自有个人路线，也有共同社区事件。

安装、进入与离开

  1. 安装适合当前游戏版本的官方可选包，在主菜单「模组管理器」中启用「Wildheart：野林」。
  2. 改变启用状态后重启，并完成可选包提供的访问兑换要求。启用模组与兑换访问权是两步。
  3. 在深林到达坐标（3,2），选择进入野林。此版入口与野林入口处返回深林的行动都不耗时。
  4. 用世界地图前往各处；河镜咖啡馆从滨河步道进入。要离开世界，从野林入口返回深林。

实用推进顺序

先完成Natsumi、Hime与Taiga的初见，各自达到已知（25好感）并完成第二事件；随后在09:00–17:00拜访树冠广场，推进共同散步。之后分别推进个人路线，先完成第一段交心，再继续朋友阶段的后续事件。

Huli的图书馆路线、Nanami的咖啡馆路线和Rin的集市路线，可以分别开始。后续共同事件按上表核对；不要因事件名称相近，或自己走到河边，就当作完成了人物邀约。

事件没有开始时

先确认世界确实已安装并启用，再依次检查访问权、地点、开始时段、具名前置事件与普通好感。已经完成的事件不会自动重复；EXTRAS回放与当前时间线的任务进度分别记录。
