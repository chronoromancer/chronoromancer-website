Overview

Foxy is an adult fox woman first found at the Royal Hunting Grounds Edge. Her route includes discovery, outdoor relationship activities, Sashina's teaching scenes, recruitment, a Menagerie room, forest encounters, worker training, crafted upgrades and Sirena Isle vacation scenes. This guide gives the progression requirements and rewards without retelling major story outcomes.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Royal Hunting Grounds Edge, 6 AM–6 PM, with Perception 35. Select Follow Footprints, costing 30 minutes and 10 energy. It makes Foxy visible and starts First Contact; the best final reply is “Yes, I can understand you.” for 5 base points. No separate implant requirement is configured for this action.
  2. Build affection at the Hunting Grounds Edge during 6 AM–3 PM. Share Food, communicate, then add Play Fetch at Acknowledged, Pet Foxy at Known and Teach Foxy at Acquaintance. Animal Care improves her relationship rewards.
  3. At Known, complete Moonlight Teaching at the Fishing Village Coast, 10 PM–midnight on odd game days. Hunters' Pursuit is independently available at the Hunting Grounds Edge; prepare for its additional health and energy penalty if you lack one of its special skills.
  4. Raise Foxy and Sashina to Friend and visit the Ancient Earth Shrine, 7 AM–8 PM, after Moonlight Teaching for A Wild Education. This automatic scene can trigger on any game day, independently of Foxy's ordinary shrine portrait schedule.
  5. Complete Sashina's The Sorceress's Confession at the Shrine Altar, 8 AM–8 PM, with Sashina Known. Reach Foxy Romantic and return to the Ancient Earth Shrine for Wild Education Continues, which recruits worker 9 on every response. Caring “I want you to be happy, Foxy.” adds 5 base points to the recruitment's 20 base-point reward.
  6. Build the Menagerie, then use Courtyard → Menagerie → Foxy's Room for her new home interactions. Recruitment keeps her outdoor schedule and forest encounters available.
  7. At In Love, bring the two crafted gifts listed below for matching worker-training upgrades. Send Foxy to Sirena Isle to use the resort activities and vacation group scenes.
The recruitment scene specifically checks Foxy Romantic and The Sorceress's Confession completed; it does not separately require A Wild Education. The teaching scene is still part of the recommended route and is required for her Looking Glass upgrade.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

If Progress Stalls

  • Use Follow Footprints with Perception 35 before expecting Foxy's outdoor portrait. Its discovery window extends to 6 PM; her ordinary Hunting Grounds portrait ends at 3 PM.
  • Check the game-day number for Forest Clearing, Coast and Shrine schedules. The automatic teaching and recruitment scenes have their own windows.
  • Complete Moonlight Teaching, then raise Sashina and Foxy to Friend for A Wild Education. Recruitment instead checks Foxy Romantic and Sashina's confession.
  • Prepare for Hunters' Pursuit's 50 health / 50 energy completion penalty, or use one of its three round-2 special skills.
  • At the Hunting Grounds Edge, Try to Communicate stops after Known. Use teaching, petting, fetching, food and her other locations afterward.
  • Build the Menagerie and recruit worker 9 for room access. Foxy continues going outdoors, so an unlocked room can be temporarily empty.
  • Distinguish the 700-affection, 90-minute video from the Romantic, 180-minute clearing visit and their different rewards.
  • Worker visits require her assigned shift, enough energy/calm and no earlier visit that day. Training options unlock through completed training sessions.
  • At In Love, bring the actual crafted item and check her presence. The Looking Glass additionally requires A Wild Education.
  • Bring all participants to Sirena Isle together for group scenes, and bring Foxy home before trying ordinary home and crafted-gift interactions.
