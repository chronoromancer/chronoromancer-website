Overview

Foxy must first be discovered through a location action. Her outdoor social route and later worker recruitment are different milestones; discovering her does not automatically recruit her.

Where to find Foxy

At Medieval → Village Outskirts → Royal Hunting Grounds(Edge), use Follow Footprints 06:00–18:00 with Perception 35. It takes 30 minutes and 10 Energy. After discovery, use the schedule below.

After recruitment, her room door in the Menagerie opens even while she is off duty. She rests there whenever none of her outdoor schedules applies. The room has its own Talk action, while Give Food shares the same food-giving allowance used outside.

Route and relationship milestones

Known(25) opens the Hunters’ Pursuit and Moonlight Teaching conversations at their respective outdoor locations. The later Milk conversation uses 300 relationship points. Private clearing interactions use 700 points, while the separate remote Meet Foxy in the Clearing needs Romantic(800),even days 21:00–24:00.

For recruitment, complete Sashina’s brothel-confession milestone, reach Romantic(800) with Foxy and visit the Ancient Earth Shrine for the recruitment event. The separate meeting between Foxy and Sashina needs Friend with both and Moonlight Teaching. Follow Objectives for the preceding Sashina steps; the two shrine events are not the same requirement.

Activities and rewards

Wild Instinct gives Perception+1 once at Known(25) with Communication 52 and 30 minutes. Give Food consumes 1 Food and 10 minutes, with a shared 4-per-day allowance across locations. Its base relationship is 1 before Friend and 2 from Friend upward.

Try to Communicate is the early action; Teach Foxy replaces it from Acquaintance(50) at the hunting grounds. Recruited Foxy also participates in normal worker training, equipment and resort activities, including the resort’s separate sunset walk.

With Christmas ON, Holly’s Teach Foxy about Christmas task is a separate location action in Forest Clearing at Friend(100),21:00–24:00:30 minutes,+1 Token and+10 base relationship, once. Its task window is separate from Foxy’s ordinary even-day clearing schedule.

Missing actions and useful checks

No ordinary portrait before discovery is expected: use the footprint location action. After discovery, check day parity, hour and the shrine’s every-third-day condition before travelling.

If the room is empty, Foxy may be outside according to that schedule. If the door itself is absent, check recruitment rather than affection alone. Giving food at another location does not reset its daily allowance.

概览

Foxy要先通过地点动作发现。户外关系路线和后来的员工招募是两项不同进展，发现她不会自动完成招募。

出现地点与时间

在中世纪 → 村庄郊外 → 皇家狩猎场（边缘），感知35时于06:00—18:00使用「追踪足迹」，耗30分钟、精力10。发现后按下表寻找。

招募后，珍兽苑内的房门会开放，即使她当前未上班也能进入。没有适用的户外行程时，她会在房间休息。房内交谈有独立额度，但送食物与户外使用同一份送食物额度。

路线与关系进展

已知（25）时，可在对应户外地点进行「猎人的追捕」与「月光教导」；后续的「Foxy想要奶」要求关系点数300。森林空地的私下互动要求700点，另一个「在林间空地见Foxy」远程动作则要求浪漫感（800），只在双数日21:00—24:00开放。

招募需要先完成Sashina与青楼有关的告白里程碑，与Foxy达到浪漫感（800），再到远古大地神殿触发招募。另一个Foxy与Sashina相见的事件要求两人都为朋友，并已完成月光教学；两段神殿事件不是同一个条件。Sashina的更早步骤按当前目标推进即可。

行动与奖励

已知（25）且沟通52时，可花30分钟接受一次野性本能，永久增加感知1。送食物消耗食物1、10分钟，各地点共用每天4次额度；朋友之前基础关系+1，朋友起+2。

尝试沟通是早期动作，狩猎场中到相识（50）后改为教导Foxy。招募后也能参加普通员工培训、装备与度假村活动，包括度假村独立的日落散步。

圣诞开启后，Holly清单的「教Foxy认识圣诞节」任务是森林空地的独立地点动作，要求朋友（100），21:00—24:00开放；耗30分钟，奖励代币1与基础关系10，只能完成一次。任务时段与Foxy普通的双数日空地行程分别计算。

动作缺失时的检查

发现前没有普通头像是正常的，要先用追踪足迹地点动作。发现后，出发前检查单双数日、时段，以及神殿要求日期为3的倍数。

房间没人时，她可能按时间表外出了；连房门都没有时，则检查招募是否完成，而不是只刷关系。换地点送食物不会重置每日额度。
