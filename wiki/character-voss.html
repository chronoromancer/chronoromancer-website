概览和地点

Councilor Voss是未来时代52岁的政治家,在协同公司监督Fringe Zone的资源分配,他的配音是Evil.
他的注册地点是 议员在协同公司内的私人办公室时刻表没有额外的爱 或角色图门,但进入房间是单独控制的。

与办事处联系

从协同公司大堂选择 到议员办公室. Saber一旦获得明确的进入许可或已完成进入许可 Story 贝特76。进入和返回大厅行动每个时间成本为零。
如果Saber 封锁大门, 另一时间返回不能满足此大门。见 Saber的走过 或继续发布主故事,直到相关访问获准.

Overview and Location

Councilor Voss is a52-year-old politician in the Future era, overseeing Fringe Zone resource allocation at Synergy Corp. His alignment is Evil.
His registered location is the Councilor's Private Office inside Synergy Corp, every day, at all hours. The schedule has no additional affection or character-plot gate, but access to the room is controlled separately.

Reaching the Office

From the Synergy Corp lobby, choose To the Councilor's Office. Saber permits entry once you have her explicit access permission or have completed Story Beat76. The entry and Return to Lobby actions each have zero configured time cost.
If Saber blocks the door, returning at another time does not satisfy this gate. See Saber's walkthrough or continue the released main story until the relevant access is granted.

Current Character Interactions

Voss is an ambient story NPC. His current action list is empty: there is no Talk, gift, lesson, date, heart-to-heart or apartment conversation to unlock through affection. No favorite-stat thresholds or special relationship skill are configured. A visible portrait without interaction buttons is therefore expected.
