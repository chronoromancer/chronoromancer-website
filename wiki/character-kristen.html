Overview

Kristen is the aquatic mobility test participant with a teal cybernetic arm and leg at the Aquatic Modification Testing Zone, Future Fringe Zone. Her current route includes nine automatic story encounters, everyday conversation and Chocolate, a Swim lesson, a Stamina reward, three illustrated Heart-to-Hearts and two one-time dates. This walkthrough gives the practical sequence without revealing the investigation's answers or her later decisions.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit Aquatic Modification Testing Zone, 8 AM–8 PM, for The Honest Water. Talk works from Stranger, up to five times per day.
  2. At Acknowledged, return for A Copy of Her Own. With Stamina 10, optionally take Aquatic Basics to learn Swim; the skill also improves Kristen affection gains.
  3. At Known, return for Perfect Formation. Chocolate opens at this stage, up to three times daily. With Ability 30, optionally claim Waterline Breathing.
  4. At Acquaintance, return after Perfect Formation for The Moving Baseline.
  5. At Friend, return for An Unscheduled Current. Choose Where I End from her portrait after The Moving Baseline. Glass Reef Date is separately available after Perfect Formation, 8 AM–6 PM.
  6. At Close Friend, return after An Unscheduled Current for Consent by Environment.
  7. At Crush, choose No Monitor Light after Consent by Environment and Where I End. Also return for the next automatic encounter after Consent by Environment.
  8. At Romantic, the Neon Bazaar Date opens 4–8 PM after that encounter. To continue the later automatic story route, also complete Story Beat 35.
  9. After No Cooperative Subject, reach Intimate for Her Own Current. Complete No Monitor Light, then choose Still Mine at Intimate. These two final activities have separate completion requirements.
Her personal H2Hs all start from her regular 8 AM–8 PM portrait. No Monitor Light mentions evening in its story, but its action has no extra evening start gate.

Aquatic Basics: Swim Lesson

At Acknowledged and Stamina 10, choose Aquatic Basics while Kristen is present. The lesson costs 180 minutes, $150, 55 Energy and 8 Calm and gives Swim plus 10 base Kristen affection. It is available only while you do not already know Swim.
Swim is learned before the lesson's relationship calculation, so its relationship bonus already applies to that lesson's affection. It is a skill lesson, separate from the Waterline Breathing stat reward. No date or H2H completion is needed.

Waterline Breathing

At Known and Ability 30, choose Waterline Breathing from her portrait. It completes once, takes 30 minutes, and gives Stamina +1 and 8 direct Kristen affection. No money, Energy or Calm cost is configured.
This reward does not teach Swim and has no automatic-scene or H2H prerequisite beyond its stated stage and Ability threshold.
