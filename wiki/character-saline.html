概览

Saline 这是 27岁的人类护士 现场 未来弗林格区的生物增生医疗中心。她是一个有环境对话框的注册位置字符。当前游戏确实如此 没有 给她个人的爱意,追求或浪漫的动作链;这里有用的进展是通过诊所的位置菜单.

查找 Saline

打开 未来弗林格区地图 输入 医疗中心。房间命名 生物增生医疗中心 并且一旦到达这个区,就无需额外的具体诊所故事或感情解锁。
访问 下午6时至10时 查看 Saline 的肖像和环境护士对话框。她的时间表没有奇数/每天的限制。诊所的定位动作有 没有单独的小时限制; Saline 的肖像窗口并不限制它们的可用性.
打开她的肖像并没有透露隐藏的Talk或招聘菜单:她目前的个人和远程动作列表是空的. 重复的诊疗不会推进私人路线计数器.

Overview

Saline is the 27-year-old human nurse at the Bio-Optimization Medical Center in the Future Fringe Zone. She is a registered location character with ambient dialogue. The current game does not give her a personal affection, quest or romance action chain; the useful progression here is through the clinic's location menu.

Find Saline

Open the Future Fringe Zone map and enter Medical Center. The room is named Bio-Optimization Medical Center and is available without an additional clinic-specific story or affection unlock once you can reach this district.
Visit 6 AM–10 PM to see Saline's portrait and ambient nurse dialogue. Her schedule has no odd/even-day restriction. The clinic's location actions have no separate hour restriction; Saline's portrait window does not limit their availability.
Opening her portrait does not reveal a hidden Talk or recruitment menu: her current personal and remote action lists are empty. Repeated clinic visits do not advance a private route counter.

Clinic Actions and Rewards

Use the location action menu, rather than looking for these in Saline's portrait.
Action Requirement Cost Mechanical result
Receive AI Diagnosis No additional gate 45 minutes; 15 calm Clinic flavour text; no Health recovery, learned skill or Saline affection

The three general clinic actions have no daily use limit or money charge configured. Only Follow AI Medical Advice restores Health or energy. The two social actions spend calm and time; they do not generate Saline relationship points.
Pain Tolerance's 50 Health is a cost, not healing. Arrive with sufficient Health, energy, calm and $250 for the lesson. The learning action pays its configured costs once and teaches the skill; it does not create a separate Saline affection reward or require a relationship stage.

Suggested Clinic Visit

  1. Enter Medical Center from the Fringe Zone map. For Saline's ambient dialogue, arrive 6 AM–10 PM.
  2. If you want the skill, reach effective Stamina 18, carry $250, and prepare for 50 Health, 30 energy and 20 calm to be spent.
  3. Choose Pain Tolerance from the location menu. After learning, its action is hidden; it is not a daily repeatable stat-training activity.
  4. Use Follow AI Medical Advice when its +5 Health / +20 energy restoration is useful. It takes two game hours and does not improve affection.
There is no response quiz or best-answer sequence to solve at this clinic. The routine social action descriptions are flavour text with the listed costs.

Current Personal Content

Saline currently has no personal Talk action, gifts, automatic personal plot, heart-to-heart, date, apartment conversation, venue Invite Date, worker recruitment, resort route, Kupie entry, Christmas gift scene or NPC intimacy reward images.
Her portrait and ambient dialogue represent the nurse already present in the game. The clinic's skill and restoration actions are the available gameplay content; completing them does not unlock an unconfigured romance route.

If an Option Is Missing

  • Saline absent: visit the Future Fringe Zone Medical Center, 6 AM–10 PM, rather than the Present hospital or another Future clinic.
  • No Talk in her portrait: her current personal action list is empty; use the clinic location menu for services.
  • Pain Tolerance missing: check effective Stamina 18 and whether the skill is already learned.
  • Lesson unavailable: prepare $250, 30 energy, 50 Health and 20 calm. Health is spent during conditioning.
  • Diagnosis does not heal: Receive AI Diagnosis and Request Human Doctor are social actions that spend calm. Follow AI Medical Advice is the listed restoration action.
  • No affection gained from a visit: none of the four clinic actions currently awards Saline affection.
