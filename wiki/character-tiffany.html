概览

Tiffany是一个 普通公民和夜总会 她现在的路线 11场自动个人场景, 三颗心对心, 四个日期,普通的Talk and Perfume,一个一次性的魅力奖赏和与Chloe共享的场景.
指南列出了实际要求和答复,同时将路线的发现和结果留给游戏.

Overview

Tiffany is an organic baseline citizen and nightclub regular in the Future Prime Sector. Her current route has eleven automatic personal scenes, three heart-to-hearts, four dates, ordinary Talk and Perfume, a one-time Charm reward and a shared scene with Chloe.
The guide lists practical requirements and replies while leaving the route's discoveries and outcomes to the game.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the nightclub overnight for Average Encounter and use daily Talk to reach Known.
  2. Add Perfume and claim Read the Room when Communication reaches 50.
  3. Follow the automatic route through One Profile, Two Women, then visit the Underground Modification Clinic during 8 AM–8 PM for Baseline Duplicate.
  4. At Friend, return to the nightclub for A Daylight Address, then visit the Tower during noon–5 PM for Standard-Issue Home and Off the Clock.
  5. At Close Friend, complete The Other Tiffany and Recommended Merge at the Tower. Not the Same Woman also requires the first heart-to-heart.
  6. At Crush, return to the nightclub for Matched as One. At Romantic, visit the Archive Facility for Content Cohort T-IF-44, then complete Choose Me Twice at the Tower.
  7. At Intimate, return to the nightclub for Her Own Rhythm. Fit the optional dates and Chloe scene into their separate windows.

Troubleshooting and Current Scope

  • I cannot find her during daylight: finish A Daylight Address and visit the Tower during noon–5 PM.
  • The route stopped before the address: Baseline Duplicate occurs at the Underground Modification Clinic, not the nightclub.
  • The later route stopped after Matched as One: visit the Modified Historical Archive Facility at Romantic for Content Cohort T-IF-44.
  • A heart-to-heart is missing: check both its named personal-scene flag and earlier heart-to-heart requirement, then use the Tower portrait.
  • A date is missing: check its stage, specific earlier scene, start window and whether it is already completed.
  • Moving locations did not restore gifts or Talk: the daily counters are shared.
  • There is no worker or apartment route: neither brothel-worker recruitment nor apartment intimacy is registered for Tiffany, and no current NPC intimacy gallery assets exist for her.
