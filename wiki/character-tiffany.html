Overview

Tiffany’s route moves between the Neural Club, daytime tower visits and several off-site Objectives. Use current Objectives first; the navigation below numbers personal milestones to keep their explanations and revelations in the game.

Where to find them

At first, find her at the Neural Club 18:00–24:00 and 00:00–04:00. After the daytime-address milestone, she is also at Optimal Living Tower Alpha 12:00–17:00. This is a different place from the Entertainment Tower. Clinic and Archive Facility steps use their own locations, rather than either ordinary portrait.

Route and relationship milestones

Continue the eleven ordered personal milestones and insert each newly available H2H and date. The route moves through Known (25), Acquaintance (50), Friend (100), Close Friend (200), Crush (400), Romantic (800) and Intimate (1600); particular prior events remain required at each step.

Her four one-time dates are Neon Bazaar, Skygarden, Glass Reef and Memory Arcade, each with its own earlier milestone and start window. Private Time requires Intimate (1600), the completed late route and Choose Me Twice; start from her actual tower portrait 12:00–17:00.

Optional reference: NPC intimacy gallery

This character has three current gallery rewards at 100, 250 and 500 total NPC intimacy experience. Open the character details, then Intimacy details, and select an unlocked thumbnail. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience, worker Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate; viewing a reward adds no experience.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Charm 70; Stamina 75; Luck 70

Favorite skill: Cultural Adaptation

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

For an off-site milestone, go to its exact Clinic or Archive destination during the listed hours. For the private invitation, finish the late personal route and all three H2Hs, then visit the tower before 17:00. Starting near the end of that window remains valid once the action has begun.

概览

Tiffany 的路线在神经俱乐部、白天高塔探访与数项外出目标之间切换。请先查看当前目标；下方将个人节点编号，只提供导航，相关解释与揭示留在游戏中。

在哪里找到

前期可在神经俱乐部 18:00–24:00 及 00:00–04:00 找到她。完成白天地址节点后，还会在最优生活塔Alpha 12:00–17:00 出现。这里与娱乐塔是不同地点。诊所及档案设施节点使用自己的地点，不能由两个普通肖像代替。

路线与关系节点

按顺序推进十一项个人节点，并穿插新出现的谈心与约会。路线会经过已知（25）、相识（50）、朋友（100）、亲密朋友（200）、迷恋（400）、浪漫感（800）与亲密（1600）；各节点仍要求对应前置，不能只靠好感度跳过。

四项一次性约会分别为霓虹集市、空中花园、玻璃礁与记忆游戏厅，各有自己的前置节点及开始时段。私人相聚要求亲密（1600）、后期路线完成及选择我两次谈心完成，并从她实际在场的高塔肖像 12:00–17:00 开始。

可选查阅：NPC 亲密画廊

该角色目前有三项画廊奖励，分别要求累计 NPC 亲密经验 100、250 与 500。打开角色详情，再进入亲密详情，点击已解锁缩略图查看。普通好感度、玩家情欲经验、员工经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；查看奖励不会增加经验。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

魅力 70; 耐力 75; 幸运 70

偏好技能：文化适应

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

外出节点请在指定时段前往准确的诊所或档案地点。私人邀请则要完成后期个人路线及三段谈心，再于 17:00 前到高塔。只要在窗口内开始，行动经过时间后不会因超过整点而取消。
