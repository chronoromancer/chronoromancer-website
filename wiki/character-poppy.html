概览

Poppy 是一个快乐的半人半人厨师 红发和雀斑谁运行 玫瑰和索恩厨房她目前的路线包括招募, 三次个人自动相遇, 双H2H, 厨房秘诀,则 大师课每日的配料礼物 五次共同的厨房聚会 和一次可重复的酒吧表演反应
厨房还提供每日批量烹饪、原料升级和 做个心肝节.这些管理服务与普通的Poppy感情有不同的奖励和计数器。

Overview

Poppy is the cheerful halfling cook with red hair and freckles who runs the Rose & Thorn Kitchen. Her current route includes recruitment, three personal automatic encounters, two H2Hs, Kitchen Secrets, the Master Chef lesson, daily ingredient gifts, five shared kitchen encounters and a repeatable bar-performance reaction.
The Kitchen also offers daily batch cooking, ingredient upgrades and Cook a Hearty Feast. These management services have different rewards and counters from ordinary Poppy affection.

Recruiting Poppy and Opening the Kitchen

  1. Raise the Rose & Thorn's reputation to 4.
  2. Return to the establishment entrance in King's Demesne for A Cheerful Cook Arrives. The recruitment scene occurs once and has no configured time-of-day restriction.
  3. Completion recruits Poppy, opens the Kitchen at level 1, gives 10 base affection to Poppy and 5 base to Elara, and hands over all of your carried cooking ingredients. Your Common, Rare and Epic Ingredient stocks are all cleared; this is not a one-Rare-Ingredient recruitment fee. Materials, Fabrics and other inventory categories are not cleared by that handler.
  4. From West Hall, use To Kitchen, a zero-minute travel action requiring establishment reputation 4.
  5. Your first eligible Kitchen visit after arrival triggers First Taste. Choose Caring or Flirty for 7 fixed + 4 choice = 11 best base affection.
The arrival's ordinary affection reward is applied by its completion handler, although the plot registry itself has no fixed relationship-reward entry. Kitchen level, recruitment and ordinary affection are separate values.

Recommended Personal Route

  1. Recruit Poppy and complete First Taste. Use Talk, available from Stranger, to raise ordinary affection.
  2. At Known, return for Private Recipe, with Flirty giving 13 best base. Rare/Epic ingredient gifts now become available.
  3. At Known and Communication 45, claim Kitchen Secrets for Cooking +1 and 8 direct ordinary affection.
  4. At Acquaintance, after Private Recipe, revisit for Feeding Everyone. Choose Caring for 13 best base.
  5. At Acquaintance, with First Taste complete, use The Warmest Kitchen H2H at any hour. Feeding Everyone is not an added requirement for this H2H.
  6. At Friend and Cooking 50, complete the Master Chef lesson. The best scene reward is 35 base, and completion teaches Master Chef.
  7. At Close Friend, with the lesson and The Warmest Kitchen complete, start After the Last Plate, 6 PM–midnight.
  8. Continue the independent shared Kitchen scenes, optional bar-performance reaction and daily Kitchen management services.
The first three personal automatic encounters total 37 best base affection. They form First Taste → Private Recipe → Feeding Everyone, but the lesson and first H2H use their own prerequisites. There is no additional personal date or movie action to search for after the second H2H.

Kitchen Secrets and Master Chef

Kitchen Secrets

At Known and Communication 45, select Kitchen Secrets from Poppy's portrait. It takes 30 minutes, completes once, grants Cooking +1, and adds 8 direct ordinary affection. There is no configured cash, Energy, Calm or ingredient charge.
Its eight affection points are direct, rather than another base reward multiplied by her favorite stats and Fry Fish. It does not teach Master Chef or write the Master Chef lesson's plot flag.

Master Chef lesson

Select Master Chef at Friend and Cooking 50. The lesson costs 200 minutes, 70 Energy and 40 Calm, with no money or ingredient cost.
It plays The Master Chef's Lesson once. Caring or Flirty adds 5 choice base to 30 fixed, giving 35 best base affection. Completion teaches Master Chef and records the lesson as complete. No player Erotic XP or NPC intimacy XP is configured.
The lesson's availability is blocked by its completed plot, rather than by simply already knowing the skill. If you learned Master Chef through another route, Poppy's authored lesson can still be completed for its scene and prerequisite flag; learning an already-owned skill does not stack another copy.
For After the Last Plate, you need Poppy's actual lesson completed. Owning Master Chef from another NPC does not satisfy that plot requirement by itself. The separate Hearty Feast service requires the learned skill and Cooking 50, with no added Poppy romance-stage or lesson-plot condition.

Daily Kitchen Services

These are Kitchen location actions, with their own requirements and counters. They do not consume one of Poppy's Talk or gift allowances.

Cook for Rose & Thorn

Use Cook for Rose & Thorn once per game day. It takes 60 minutes and 20 Energy, consuming five Common Ingredients and one Rare Ingredient.
It adds stored Food using:
Food = 5 + floor(raw Cooking / 10) + 3 × Kitchen apprentice count.
For example, Cooking 50 and two Kitchen apprentices give 16 Food. The calculation uses the player's raw Cooking value, rather than a temporary modified Cooking value. It produces management Food stock, with no extra ordinary Poppy-affection, player Erotic XP or NPC intimacy XP award.

Cook a Hearty Feast

At the Kitchen, with learned Master Chef, modified Cooking 50+, at least one eligible recruited worker, and this service unused today, select Cook a Hearty Feast.
It takes 60 minutes and consumes one Epic Ingredient per eligible recruited worker. It has no configured separate money, Energy or Calm charge. The required ingredient total is based on the current roster, not a fixed recipe of one Epic Ingredient for the entire household.
The feast serves all recruited living workers, including inactive, resting and vacationing workers. Each served worker gains up to 1 Loyalty while below 100, capped at 100, and loses up to 5 Stress, down to 0. Ordinary worker loyalty decay is processed before the feast's gain. It does not give a bonus to unrecruited worker slots.
This is a separate daily use from batch cooking. There is no extra Poppy affection-stage condition, and it does not add Food stock or ordinary affection. Carry the full current Epic Ingredient cost before starting; having Master Chef alone is not enough.

At the Rose & Thorn Bar, use Perform Song with at least one composed song, 60 minutes and 30 Energy, once per game day for that venue.
A successful performance has a 25% base chance of a reaction scene, with +5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity and Storytelling, plus +5 for owning a Lute, capped at 50%. This is the chance of any bar reaction, not the chance of Poppy specifically; the bar then picks from seven registered reactions.
If Performance Reaction – Poppy is chosen, Funny adds 4 choice base to 8 fixed, for 12 best base affection. Her reaction is marked repeatable and does not use a completed personal-scene flag to prevent later appearances. You do not need to find her regular portrait in the Bar to get that reaction.
Performance gold, Valuables and Bards Guild clout are separate shared performance rewards. A performance does not guarantee Poppy's reaction on every visit.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Arrival missing: reach establishment reputation 4, then revisit the entrance. Recruitment clears all carried ingredient tiers and opens the Kitchen.
  • Kitchen entrance missing: use West Hall → To Kitchen and check the reputation gate.
  • Gift missing: reach Known and carry the exact Rare / Epic cooking-ingredient stock.
  • Kitchen Secrets missing: check Known, Communication 45 and the one-time reward completion. It does not need Cooking 45.
  • First H2H missing: finish First Taste, reach Acquaintance and use Poppy's Kitchen portrait.
  • Lesson missing: check Friend, Cooking 50, Energy/Calm and the lesson not already complete.
  • Second H2H missing: complete Poppy's Master Chef lesson and The Warmest Kitchen, reach Close Friend, then start 6 PM–midnight.
  • Hearty Feast missing: check learned Master Chef, modified Cooking 50, a recruited eligible roster and today's separate feast use. Carry one Epic Ingredient for every current recipient.
  • Shared scene missing: check every participant's individual stage, recruitment/vacation state and the scene's own hours.
  • Bar reaction missing: the reaction is random after a performance; the shared roll can also choose another NPC.
Poppy's current route does not add an ordinary apartment invitation, separate personal date or movie, pregnancy continuation, Kupie album, venue-invite minigame, numbered worker training, resort vacation or personal Christmas scene. The kitchen relationship route and management services above are the authored content covered here.
