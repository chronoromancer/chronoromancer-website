Overview

Poppy runs the Rose & Thorn kitchen. Her route combines staff and community scenes, ingredient gifts, the Master Chef skill and two personal conversations.

Where to find them

Reach business Reputation level 4 and return to the property for A Cheerful Cook Arrives. This opens the kitchen, where Poppy is available all day. At her arrival, your carried Common, Rare and Epic Ingredients are all handed over to the kitchen, leaving those three inventory stocks at zero.

Route and relationship milestones

Enter the kitchen for First Taste, then return at Known (25) for Private Recipe and Acquaintance (50) for Feeding Everyone.

The Warmest Kitchen opens at Acquaintance (50) after the first kitchen scene: 60 minutes, 10 Energy and 15 Calm restored. At Friend (100) and Cooking 50, Master Chef takes 200 minutes, 70 Energy and 40 Calm, with no money cost, and teaches Master Chef.

Complete that lesson and the first conversation, then reach Close Friend (200) for After the Last Plate, 18:00–24:00:90 minutes, 20 Energy and 20 Calm restored.

Rare and Epic Ingredient gifts unlock at Known (25), each taking 10 minutes and one ingredient of the exact rarity. Their 10-per-day allowances are separate; they give 5 and 10 base affection respectively.

Kitchen Secrets needs Known (25) and Communication 45:30 minutes, +1 Cooking and 8 direct affection, once. Ordinary Talk gives 1 base affection, 15 minutes, eight positive uses per day.

She can appear in the Bar performance reaction pool once that performance activity and its participant conditions are met. Ryia’s kitchen recollection needs Ryia active and home, and Poppy currently present. The relevant shared activity guides cover their wider requirements.

Favorite stats are Cooking, Perception, Charm. Learning Fry Fish improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Reputation level 4 is the business’s level, not Poppy’s affection or a faction Clout threshold.
  • After her arrival clears your carried ingredients, obtain fresh Rare or Epic stock before choosing those gifts.
  • The second conversation needs both the Master Chef lesson and the first conversation; Cooking 50 alone does not complete either.

概览

Poppy负责玫瑰与荆棘的厨房。她的内容包括员工与社区事件、食材礼物、厨艺大师技能，以及两次个人谈心。

去哪里找

青楼声誉达到4级后回产业，触发开朗厨娘到来并开放厨房；此后Poppy全天在厨房。她到来时，会将你随身的普通、稀有、史诗食材全部接收至厨房，这3档库存归零。

路线与关系进展

进入厨房完成第一次试吃，已知（25好感）时回访私人食谱，相识（50好感）时回访养活每个人。

相识（50好感）且完成第一次厨房事件后，可进行最温暖的厨房：60分钟、10精力，恢复15镇定。朋友（100好感）且烹饪50后，厨艺大师消耗200分钟、70精力、40镇定，不收金钱，教授厨艺大师。

完成技能课程与第一次谈心后，达到亲密朋友（200好感），即可在18:00–24:00进行最后一盘之后：90分钟、20精力，恢复20镇定。

卡住时检查什么

  • 声誉4级指青楼声誉等级，与Poppy的好感及阵营声望不同。
  • 她到来时会清空随身食材；送礼前需重新取得稀有或史诗库存。
  • 第二次谈心同时要求厨艺大师课程与第一次谈心，烹饪50本身不会代替这两项。
