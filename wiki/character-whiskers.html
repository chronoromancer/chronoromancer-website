Overview

Whiskers’s route begins with the ordinary dungeon floor-5 encounter. Her room, worker controls and personal relationship route have separate access checks. The personal chain includes three heart-to-hearts and two dates.

Where to find Whiskers

Winning the floor-5 encounter recruits her, regardless of the optional response afterward. Build the Menagerie to visit Whiskers’s Room: daily 00:00–10:00, 16:00–18:00 and 20:00–24:00. Her Worker Rooms hallway portrait is separate, 18:00–20:00, and does not itself require Menagerie construction.

Route and relationship milestones

Visit her room for The Door That Stays, then start Pieces That Fit at Acquaintance (50), 05:00–10:00. At Friend (100), Three Alleys and Home starts 06:00–10:00 after that conversation.

Return 20:00–24:00 for the next automatic event, then Blameless Footprints at Close Friend (200). Its completion opens A Sky With No Ceiling at Crush (400), 20:00–22:00. The later room event also requires Lindia to be an active worker; Home Is Who Returns follows at Crush.

Activities and rewards

Talk costs 15 minutes for 1 base relationship point, four safe uses shared between room and hallway. Teddy costs 10 minutes and one Teddy for 8 base points, with two daily gifts shared between those locations.

The two dates cost 120 minutes each: the morning date costs $75 and 25 Energy, restoring 10 Calm; the evening date costs $60 and 20 Energy, restoring 15 Calm. Worker training, the three crafted upgrades and resort activities use their own requirements.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

Missing actions and useful checks

An evening hallway portrait does not provide the room-only conversations or dates. For a missing late room scene, check Lindia’s active status and whether either participant is away on vacation.

概览

Whiskers的路线从普通地下城第5层遭遇开始。房间、员工控制和个人关系路线分别有各自的入口条件，个人主链包括三段谈心与两项约会。

出现地点与时间

赢下第5层遭遇即可招募，不需要指定的可选战后回应。建好珍兽苑后可进入Whiskers 的房间，时间为每天00:00—10:00、16:00—18:00及20:00—24:00。员工宿舍另有18:00—20:00头像，这处头像本身不要求珍兽苑建造。

路线与关系进展

先到房间完成留在原处的门，再于相识（50）时，在05:00—10:00开始能拼上的碎片。朋友（100）时，完成谈心后可在06:00—10:00开始三条巷子与家。

20:00—24:00返回触发下一项自动事件，再于亲密朋友（200）推进无罪的足迹。完成后，迷恋（400）时20:00—22:00开放无顶之空。后续房间事件还要求Lindia员工启用，之后才在迷恋阶段开放家是那个会回来的人。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，给予1点基础关系，房间与走廊共用每日4次安全额度。泰迪熊消耗10分钟和一只泰迪熊，给予8点基础关系，两处共用每日2次送礼额度。

两项约会各消耗120分钟；早晨约会消耗75财富、25精力，恢复10镇定，晚间约会消耗60财富、20精力，恢复15镇定。员工训练、三项制作升级与度假活动各有独立要求。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。

动作缺失时的检查

晚间走廊头像不提供房间专属谈心或约会。后期房间事件缺失时，请检查Lindia是否启用，以及参与者是否正在度假。
