概览和时间表

Whiskers是中世纪时代一个19岁的猫女,与中立对齐。 能力40、Percepion40和Charm35她特殊的关系技巧是 动物护理她走过的地方包括第5层营救、家居室出入、3次个人活动和谈话、2次约会、24号工人培训和3次人工升级。
本指南给出了机械要求和选项顺序,而没有重述路线的个人启示.

她的工人轮班是单独设置的,休假可以消除她一般的家庭存在.
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

Overview and Schedule

Whiskers is a19-year-old cat girl in the Medieval era, with Neutral alignment. Her favorite stats are Ability40,Perception40 and Charm35; her special relationship skill is Animal Care. Her walkthrough includes the floor5 rescue, menagerie room access, three personal events and conversations, two dates, worker #24 training and three crafted upgrades.
This guide gives mechanical requirements and option order without retelling the route's personal revelations.

Floor5 Rescue and Menagerie Access

Whiskers is the floor5,level2 dungeon encounter. Winning it activates worker #24 automatically, with no affection, brothel-ownership or separate recruitment-payment prerequisite. Later brothel access is still needed to visit her there.
The encounter has five rounds,185 enemy Energy and37 attack power targeting your Calm. Preserve Calm as well as dealing damage. Its strongest choices depend on your modified stats and learned skills:
Round Useful options
1 Animal Care uses Perception with excellent effectiveness; without it, Charm or Perception are good. Standing still uses Stamina and reduces the counter multiplier to0.45.
2 Acrobatics uses Ability with excellent effectiveness. Combat Reflexes uses Ability with a0.5 counter multiplier; learning the platform layout uses Intelligence and also has0.5 counter multiplier.
3 Talking her down with Charm is excellent, as is Silver Tongue. Yielding with Ability is good with0.55 counter multiplier. Avoid taking the platform: poor effectiveness and double counter damage.
4 Asking with Charm or Negotiation with Intelligence is excellent. Outlasting with Stamina is good with0.5 counter multiplier.

It requires Courtyard built,brothel Reputation9+,$2,000,000,50 epic materials,50 epic ingredients and50 epic fabrics, and takes 200 minutes. Completion builds the menagerie at level1 and raises brothel Decor by one. See Gastoff's guide for related construction.

Talk and Teddy Bears

Talk to Whiskers takes15 minutes and gives1 base affection, four/day, shared between her room and hallway. Give Teddy Bear is available in her room at Known+, takes10 minutes, consumes one Teddy and gives8 base affection, twice/day.
