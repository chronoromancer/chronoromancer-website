概览

有个 没有单独的原料,钱,能量,热巧克力或糖果甘蔗成本 为此动作配置,没有额外的时钟窗口 。它的直接 20 亲切 是不乘数的 。 任务与 最甜的甜点,这是Ginger的自动浪漫舞台场景.

圣诞精神技能

那个 圣诞精神技巧 是一个可能的 克劳机器奖 。胜利会增加技能,并取消未来卷中的奖品。它会加倍Ginger的关系乘数,但乡村的乘数 切换圣诞精神 动作只允许或禁用事件活动。
Ginger在目前的代码中没有单独的技能培训菜单、公寓H2H、Rose& Thorn招聘或度假村工人路线。她的亲密内容是通过注册的里程碑地块和甜点任务交付的; 建立爱心和重新审视房子,看看这些场景。

解决问题

* 没有热巧克力 : 到达 已知 并携带至少一个 圣诞热巧克力。普通巧克力不能满足它的事件项目成本。
* 普通巧克力无法使用 : 这需要 知识晚于热巧克力礼物。
* 糖类进展停滞: 检查寿命 给定 收集的手杖不会推进; 甘蔗贸易 在十杖捆。
* 圣诞甜点没有 : 到达 浪漫主义,启用圣诞活动,并检查一次性任务是否尚未完成。
* 缺少一个里程碑: 访问 姜饼店 里程碑是自动遇到,而不是额外的肖像按钮。

Overview

Ginger is the candy-cane-loving flirt in Christmas Village's Gingerbread House. Her complete current route consists of eight affection milestones, five candy-cane milestones, repeatable conversation and gifts, candy-cane trades, and a one-time Christmas Dessert task.

Getting There and Starting the Route

  1. In the Future Fringe Zone, enter the Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station and choose Christmas Village. From the village, enter the Gingerbread House. These Christmas travel actions have zero time cost.
  2. Use Toggle Christmas Spirit in the village to enable the Christmas activities when needed. This event toggle is separate from owning the Christmas Spirit skill; enabling the activities does not teach that skill.
  3. Complete the automatic Welcome to Christmas Village scene once. It gives 10 base affection each to Ginger, Holly and Noella, plus one Token. Choose “Blue, definitely blue.” for another 5 Ginger base points. Green favors Holly and red favors Noella instead.
  4. Visit Ginger's portrait in the Gingerbread House at any hour. Talk to build early trust, then use Hot Chocolate at Known and ordinary Chocolate at Acquaintance. Return to the house as each new affection milestone becomes eligible.
The village and Ginger's schedule do not have a calendar-date gate configured. Her introductory and affection-milestone scenes do not require completing Holly's or Noella's routes.

Candy-Canes and Milestones

Choose the Gingerbread House's location action Trade Candy Canes while holding at least 10. Each trade consumes 10 Candy Canes, gives one Token and 10 base Ginger affection, and increases the lifetime number of Candy Canes given by ten.
The milestone counter tracks Candy Canes actually traded to Ginger, not Candy Canes held, collected, won, or spent elsewhere. Spending the Token afterward does not reduce the lifetime trade total.

The 25 and 75 thresholds are reached at 30 and 80 with the current ten-cane trade action. Each milestone is a one-time automatic Gingerbread House encounter, with no extra affection-stage gate. Candy Queen's Champion includes its registered animated reward sequence.
To reach every candy milestone from zero, trade 150 Candy Canes, in 15 trades. Those trades give 15 Tokens and 150 base affection before bonuses. The five milestone scenes add six Tokens and 75 base affection. Revisit the house to trigger eligible milestone scenes; the trade action itself does not replace the automatic encounter.
With Christmas activities enabled, collectible Candy Canes appear at the configured locations across the three eras, including Christmas Village and Mistletoe Lodge. A collected location's cane returns after two game days. The Claw Machine can also award Candy Canes, but its random prize does not itself count as a cane given to Ginger.

Christmas Dessert Task

At Romantic, with the Christmas activities enabled, use Christmas Dessert in the Gingerbread House's location menu. It takes 15 minutes, completes “Make Ginger a Christmas dessert” on Holly's Christmas task list, and gives one Token and 20 affection directly, once.
There is no separate ingredient, money, energy, Hot Chocolate or Candy Cane cost configured for this action, and no additional clock window. Its direct 20 affection is not multiplied. The task is separate from The Sweetest Treat, which is Ginger's automatic Romantic-stage scene.

Christmas Spirit Skill

The Christmas Spirit skill is a possible Claw Machine prize until won or already learned. Winning it adds the skill and removes that prize from future rolls. It doubles Ginger's relationship multiplier, but the village's Toggle Christmas Spirit action only enables or disables event activities.
Ginger does not have a separate skill-teaching menu, apartment H2H, Rose & Thorn recruitment or resort-worker route in the current code. Her intimate content is delivered through the registered milestone plots and the Dessert task; build affection and revisit the house to see those scenes.

Troubleshooting

* Hot Chocolate is unavailable: reach Known and carry at least one Christmas Hot Chocolate. Ordinary Chocolate does not satisfy its event-item cost.
* Ordinary Chocolate is unavailable: it requires Acquaintance, a later stage than the Hot Chocolate gift.
* Candy progress has stalled: inspect the lifetime given total. Collecting canes does not advance it; use Trade Candy Canes in ten-cane bundles.
* Christmas Dessert is unavailable: reach Romantic, enable the Christmas activities, and check that the one-time task is not already completed.
* A milestone is missing: visit the Gingerbread House again after meeting its stage or trade threshold. Milestones are automatic encounters, not extra portrait buttons.
