Ginger

Candy Cane trades, milestone Tokens and Ginger’s Christmas interactions.

Overview

Ginger exchanges Candy Canes for Christmas Tokens and has her own relationship milestones. Her ordinary conversations and trade are separate from the one-time Holly checklist task.

Where to find Ginger

Go to Future → Fringe Zone → Weather Control → Christmas Village → Gingerbread House. Village travel takes no time, and Ginger is present all day. Christmas ON enables the scattered cane pickups and seasonal tasks; ordinary resident interactions and the cane trade remain usable when the switch is off.

Weather Control is the map button; entering it opens Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station, where the onward routes are listed.

Route and relationship milestones

Her relationship conversations trigger at Known(25),Acquaintance(50),Friend(100),Close Friend(200),Crush(400),Romantic(800),Intimate(1,600) and In Love(3,000). Visit Gingerbread House after meeting each threshold. The first seven grant 1 Token each; In Love grants 2.

At Romantic(800), Christmas Dessert is Holly’s task 2:15 minutes,+1 Token and+20 base Ginger relationship. It is a separate once-only task, not a repeatable alternative to trading.

Activities and rewards

Trade Candy Canes takes no time:10 Candy Canes become 1 Token and+10 base relationship, with no daily trade cap. It also raises Ginger’s lifetime-canes-given count by 10.

The cumulative milestones are listed at 25,50,75,100 and 150 canes. Since trades are in tens, the first reachable totals are 30,50,80,100 and 150. Their additional Token rewards are 1,1,1,1 and 2. Christmas Spirit improves the applicable ordinary relationship calculation, not every fixed Token reward.

Chocolate uses a normal Chocolate item; Hot Chocolate uses the separate Christmas HUD stock. The full Christmas guide covers all 23 collecting locations and the claw-machine prize pool.

Missing actions and useful checks

A missing trade requires checking the 10-cane balance, not the relationship stage. If no new collecting cane is visible, enable Christmas and remember that each location respawns two game days after collection.

For a milestone conversation, return to Gingerbread House after crossing its threshold. Replaying a completed fixed milestone does not pay its Tokens again.

概览

Ginger负责用拐杖糖兑换圣诞代币，也有自己的关系里程碑。普通互动、拐杖糖交易，以及Holly清单的一次性任务分别计算。

出现地点与时间

未来 → 边缘区 → 天气控制 → 圣诞村 → 姜饼屋。村内移动不耗时，Ginger全天都在。圣诞开启后才能进行地图拐杖糖拾取和节庆任务；关闭开关时，普通居民互动与拐杖糖交易仍可使用。

路线与关系进展

已知（25）、相识（50）、朋友（100）、亲密朋友（200）、迷恋（400）、浪漫感（800）、亲密（1,600）、恋爱中（3,000）分别有关系对话，达到门槛后回姜饼屋查看。前七段各给1枚代币，恋爱中给2枚。

浪漫感（800）的圣诞甜点是Holly第2项任务，耗15分钟，奖励代币1与Ginger基础关系20；它单独计算且只能完成一次，不能当作反复交易的替代方案。

行动与奖励

交易拐杖糖不耗时，每10根换1枚代币和基础关系+10，没有每日交易次数上限，同时把累计交出数量增加10。

累计里程碑标为25、50、75、100、150根，因为每次交10根，实际最早在30、50、80、100、150根达成，额外代币分别为1、1、1、1、2枚。圣诞精神会改善适用的普通关系计算，不会让每项固定代币奖励都翻倍。

巧克力消耗普通巧克力；热巧克力消耗圣诞资源栏里单独记录的库存。完整23处收集点与抓娃娃奖池见圣诞村指南。

动作缺失时的检查

不能交易时，先检查是否有10根糖，不用先刷关系。地图糖没有出现时，检查圣诞开关，并记得每个地点在拾取后的第2个游戏日才重生。

达到里程碑后要回姜饼屋查看对话；重看已完成的固定里程碑不会再次发代币。
