Overview

Miki is a Present-era gyaru fashionista found at the mall, park and mansion. Her current route includes shared daily conversations and gifts, four dates, two bedroom animations, KTV/Night Club invitations, a Kupie album and a longer company-ownership route with Maggie, Arnold and Evangeline. This walkthrough covers the actual unlocks and rewards while leaving the scenes' story outcomes to the game.
Timing matters: finish Miki's optional live content before the late Empire milestone Controlling Interest. In the currently released route, that completion blocks her normal portraits, bedroom access, interactions and earlier Empire scenes. This guide identifies the progression boundary without explaining its narrative cause. Do not assume raising affection bypasses that completion flag.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Meet Miki at Food Court, 9 AM–2 PM, or Park, 2–6 PM. Use Talk, then Teddy at Known, Perfume at Acquaintance and Jewelry at Friend. Daily allowances are shared across her locations.
  2. At Acknowledged, collect The Food Court Gauntlet with $50, and The GuGuDolly Emergency at the park with $80. The latter is a later story prerequisite.
  3. At Acquaintance, take the Zoo Date from the park. At Friend, complete Comic Con after the zoo date; optionally use the Voucher fair date. At Close Friend, the paid Boardwalk Sunset Date needs Diane's boardwalk built.
  4. Follow the Story Beats 47–55 requirements below. Comic Con, GuGuDolly Emergency, Dance Studio clout, Abby's affection and Christy/Mary Ann's shared introduction are important parts of this chain.
  5. After Story Beat 54, her bedroom animations and venue invitations become eligible at their stated affection thresholds. They are independent of her company-route H2Hs.
  6. After Story Beat 55, start Some Guy With Miki in her bedroom 8–11 PM, then follow The Wrong Office → The Eye for Acquisition. Maggie's The Eye for Value must also be complete.
  7. Buy out one company for His Name on the Door. At Friend with five acquired companies, complete Terms of Ownership in Miki's bedroom.
  8. Reach ten acquired companies for Ten Little Kingdoms, follow Projected Household Growth, then reach 25 acquired companies for Still Not an Appointment and Hostile Mentorship.
  9. Complete Arnold's Household Risk, reach 40 acquired companies for On the Radar, then return to Miki's bedroom for My Monkey's Skyline.
  10. Finish What Are You Building? at Close Friend, then No Audience at Crush. With all 50 companies acquired, What Are You Building? also unlocks Requested by Name and the later shared company encounters.
  11. Complete any remaining Miki live scenes before Controlling Interest. Her Kupie album uses its separate minigame eligibility checks.

Other Shared Affection and Troubleshooting

Nova's separate Data That Wasn't Ours encounter gives Miki +2 base affection, in addition to Nova's and Artemis's rewards. It triggers at Future Player Lab, 8 AM–10 PM, with Nova Close Friend, Access Revoked complete and Blind Calibration H2H complete. This is an optional shared reward, not a prerequisite for Miki's personal or Empire route.
  • Talk/gifts stop helping after changing locations: their daily counters are shared. Use the normal allowance and other authored affection sources.
  • Comic Con or Story Beat 47 is missing: finish Zoo Date first, then Comic Con at Friend; also finish GuGuDolly Emergency and Story Beat 46 for the story encounter.
  • Boardwalk action is missing: build Diane's boardwalk, reach Close Friend, meet the paid/Higher Power requirement and start from the park 5–6 PM.
  • Bedroom animations or invitations are missing: complete Story Beat 54, then check the exact 1,000 / 1,500 affection thresholds or Crush invitation gate.
  • Ownership scene is missing despite maxed investments: perform actual Buyouts. Check the owned-company count, prior scenes and the required Miki/Arnold H2H.
  • Miki live content is unavailable late in the Empire route: check the Controlling Interest completion boundary. Affection and previously earned achievements do not remove that gate in the current released route.
  • Kupie is locked: check ordinary Friend, Lucy Kupie level 5 and paid access, rather than personal-date count or Empire company ownership.
Miki has no configured personal apartment visit, pregnancy route, Christmas scene, numbered brothel-worker training, maid cleaning progression or resort vacation. Her Kupie beach viewing reward is an album scene, rather than an assigned resort vacation.
