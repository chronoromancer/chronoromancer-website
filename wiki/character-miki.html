Overview

Miki’s route starts around the Present mall and park, then connects to company ownership. Daily friendship actions, dates and the corporate sequence have separate conditions. This guide lists the mechanical steps without describing the corporate scenes’ outcomes.

Where to find Miki

Find her at the mall Food Court 09:00–14:00, Downtown Park 14:00–18:00, and Miki’s Bedroom at the mansion 18:00–23:00. Corporate bedroom scenes and heart-to-hearts use the narrower 20:00–23:00 start window.

A late corporate milestone temporarily pauses ordinary meetings; Story 91 restores live access. Complete optional meetings before advancing that branch if you want to avoid a waiting period.

Route and relationship milestones

Start with The Food Court Gauntlet at Acknowledged (10) and $50, and complete Katie’s shared mall encounter with both characters at Friend (100). After Story 55, follow the bedroom → Offices → Investment Planning sequence; Maggie’s The Eye for Value is required at the investment step.

Ownership milestones require 1, 5, 10, 25, 40 and finally all 50 companies. The five-company step opens Terms of Ownership. The ten-company scene requires that conversation. After the 40-company office stage and My Monkey’s Skyline, complete What Are You Building? and No Audience. The final corporate sequence also uses Arnold and Evangeline’s personal milestones; the table gives the named steps and places.

Activities and rewards

Talk has four safe uses daily, shared across her three portraits. Teddy, Perfume and Jewelry have separate once-daily limits. Fashion Makeover requires Known (25) and Communication 28; it costs 30 minutes and permanently adds Attractiveness +1 with 10 direct relationship points.

Zoo, Comic Con, Voucher Fair and Boardwalk are separate one-time dates. At Crush (400), after Story 54, KTV and Night Club invitations are available 20:00–23:00. Each venue permits one invitation daily across its full guest list. Kupie’s album is a separate minigame unlock; consult its guide rather than treating date completion as album completion.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

The effective Boardwalk invitation is 17:00–18:00 at Downtown Park; it does not remain on that portrait until 20:00. The corporate heart-to-hearts need their ownership and prior-scene checks as well as relationship. If meetings are paused, follow current Objectives and the main story.

概览

Miki的路线从现代商场与公园开始，之后延伸到公司持有进展。日常关系、约会和企业事件各自有独立条件；下文只说明推进方法，不展开企业事件的结果。

出现地点与时间

她每天09:00—14:00在商场美食广场、14:00—18:00在市中心公园、18:00—23:00在豪宅的Miki卧室。企业路线的卧室事件与谈心采用更窄的20:00—23:00开始时段。

企业路线后期的一个里程碑会暂时停止普通会面，主线91后恢复。若想避免中途等待，可以先完成可选互动，再继续该分支。

路线与关系进展

从已遇见（10）、持有50财富时的美食广场考验开始，并将Miki和Katie都提升到朋友（100），完成她们的商场共同事件。主线55后，依次推进卧室 → 办公室 → 投资规划的事件，其中投资阶段要求完成Maggie的识价的眼光。

公司持有里程碑依次检查1、5、10、25、40，最后是全部50家公司。持有5家后开放所有权条款；10家公司的事件还要求完成这段谈心。40家公司阶段及我家猴子的天际线完成后，继续你在建造什么？与谢绝观众。最后的企业序列还涉及Arnold和Evangeline的个人里程碑，下表列出相关名称与地点。

行动与奖励

交谈的每日4次安全额度由三处头像共用。泰迪熊、香水和珠宝各自每日一次。时尚改造要求已知（25）、沟通28，消耗30分钟，永久增加吸引力1点，并给予10点直接关系。

动物园、漫展、兑换券游园会与木板路是四项独立的一次性约会。达到迷恋（400）、主线54完成后，20:00—23:00可在KTV和夜总会邀约；每个场所的全部来宾共用每日一次额度。Kupie相册属于另一个小游戏解锁，完成约会不等于完成相册。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

木板路邀约实际只能在市中心公园17:00—18:00开始，不会因为动作写到20:00就延长头像。企业谈心除了关系之外，也检查公司持有数量及前置事件。会面暂停时，请继续当前目标与主线。
