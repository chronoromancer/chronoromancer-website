Rain——完全走过

概览

Rain 是一个 25岁的分析女仆 输入 中世纪时代,作为 五号女佣 通过随机 后行会合招募后,她的普通肖像出现在 女佣,上午10时至2时.
她现在的行走 包括两个招聘答复 一夜情 系统分析,可重复 女佣服务,所有6次注册的共享碰面,清洁水平,以及 Rain的分析镜头在给予要求和奖励时,不重述女仆后来的发现。

招募Rain

  1. 获取 Rose& Thorn 所有权 结束 国王 必要时预约路线。
  2. 学习技能 研究高智商本身不能取代拥有学到的技能。
  3. 访问 中世纪后列 和使用情况 探索后列成本计算 45分钟15节能源 每次尝试。 Rain 是一个 随机接触如果发生另一事件,请重复探索。
  4. Rain 出现时,请选择其中之一 “询问她的研究方法” 或 “讨论系统的数据收集。” 都成功招募了五号女仆.
  5. 招募后进入 女佣,上午10时至2时为了她的普通肖像和 Rain的到达,与 Elara 度假回家.
没有研究,相遇提供 “问她在做什么” 和 “让她去工作”既不招募她,也不招募她,带着技能离开也不招募她;你可以在她还不知道的情况下再试一次。
相遇时才进入随机池 妓院属所有,不购置Rain。其内容 在选择了 Rain 的相遇后, 85% 配置成功卷应用,而不是保证每一次探索都有机会获得85%的Rain。其他的相遇和优先追求可以首先发生。

Rain的分析镜头

到达 Ghost在Kupie的爱情训练中的第10级,则 Ghost在 Kupie 的成绩屏幕中最大 将接收 Rain的分析镜头. Rain没有她自己的Kupie NPC-日期条目. 完成Ghost的普通故事或仅查看一个日期并不能取代第10级并声称成就.
装备这个 通过女佣管理在Rain上的史诗项目专责 五号女佣 并给予:
  • 20%以上Rain清洁逐个计算
  • 减少15%的建筑杂乱,应用在日常乱编计算中。
  • 合格学徒每日经验计算30%的额外经验,四舍五入;这项福利指定了学徒,包括配置的舞厅学员计算。
没有其他设备,个人清洁奖金就会改变Rain's 10至12级捐款,或 MAX从40到48学徒经验奖金是一种工人经验效应,而不是直接+30%的女佣清洁经验奖、爱心奖或即刻增加。

解决问题

  • Rain从未出现在巷子里: 拥有 玫瑰色( T)并使用 探索后列它是一种随机的遭遇,而不是普通的预定肖像.
  • 研究答复缺失: 学习 研究仅靠情报和单独的材料科学技能不能提供这一大门。
  • Rain不加入: 选择 研究方法 或 系统数据收集而不是简单的询问或离开。
  • 招募完成但缺少肖像: 访问 女佣,上午10时至2时.
  • 抵达失踪: 在她过夜的窗口进入 Elara不休假.
  • Talk 给出的分数少于菜单字段 : 当前处理者奖项 1个基地在分享奖金之前
  • 缺少系统分析 : 检查 已知、第45号来文以及是否已经索赔.
  • 女佣服务缺失 : 到达 亲密的 / 1600 个普通的感情 并使用她的一夜肖像。
  • 缺少共享场景 : 满足每个女仆的招募/感情,早期的场景和共享场景的自己时间;有几起发生在Rain的肖像表之外.
  • 清理级别不会从对话框中上升 : 清洁经验来自 每日清洁服务.
  • 在 Ghost 级别 10 时缺少 : 索赔Ghost,然后在 Rain 上装备该物项。
她目前的个人内容没有单独的普通日期、公寓路线、正规的工人培训路线、度假假、个人Kupie-日期名册或继续怀孕,招聘、个人行动、所有注册的Rain共享场景和女佣设备都在上面。

Rain — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Rain is a 25-year-old analytical maid in the Medieval era, recruited as maid #5 through a random Back Alleys encounter. After recruitment her ordinary portrait appears in Maids Quarters, 10 PM–2 AM.
Her current walkthrough covers both recruitment replies, overnight Talk, Systematic Analysis, repeatable Maid Service, all six registered shared encounters, cleaning levels, and Rain's Analytical Lens. Requirements and rewards are given without retelling the maid story's later discoveries.

Recruit Rain

  1. Obtain Rose & Thorn ownership through The King's appointment route if needed.
  2. Learn the skill Researching. A high Intelligence stat by itself does not replace owning the learned skill.
  3. Visit Medieval Back Alleys and use Explore Back Alleys, costing 45 minutes and 15 Energy per attempt. Rain is a random encounter, so repeat exploration if another event occurs.
  4. When Rain appears, choose either “Ask about her research methodology” or “Discuss systematic data collection.” Both successfully recruit maid #5.
  5. After recruitment, enter Maids Quarters, 10 PM–2 AM, for her ordinary portrait and Rain's Arrival, with Elara home from vacation.
Without Researching, the encounter offers “Ask what she's doing” and “Leave her to her work”. Neither recruits her. Leaving with the skill also does not recruit her; you can try again while she remains unacquired.
The encounter enters the random pool only while the brothel is owned and Rain is not acquired. Its 85% configured success roll applies after Rain's encounter is selected, rather than guaranteeing an 85% chance of Rain on every exploration. Other encounters and priority quests can occur first.
Recruitment adds no separate fee, gift, affection threshold or private H2H requirement. It sets maid #5 acquired and updates the Maids Quarters room level to the recruited-maid count, capped at six. That room level is different from Rain's personal cleaning level. She has no ordinary pre-recruitment portrait route or Invite to Brothel action.

Systematic Analysis

At Known, with Communication 45, use Systematic Analysis at her 10 PM–2 AM recruited portrait. It takes 30 minutes and completes once, awarding Intelligence +1 and 10 direct Rain affection.
It has no additional configured cash, Energy, Calm, gift, other-maid or scene-completion gate. The ten affection points are direct rather than multiplied base. It is an Intelligence-stat lesson and does not teach Researching or Material Science.

Maid Service

At Intimate, 1,600 ordinary affection, choose Maid Service during Rain's 10 PM–2 AM portrait. It takes 30 minutes, with no configured money, Energy, Calm or consumable cost.
This is a repeatable illustrated, one-round H2H with three presentation variants. Choose “I appreciate your scientific approach, Rain.” for 15 base affection. The action's nominal 15-point field is not an additional second reward.
Learned Romantic adds 25 final affection at completion, separately from the ordinary Romantic relationship stage. Starting the service marks its EXTRAS entry; Extras replay does not award ordinary affection.
The menu advertises three services daily, but the current custom handler launches the repeatable H2H without recording or checking a service-use counter. The nominal cap is not enforced there; the portrait schedule and time cost still apply.
The service configures no player Erotic XP, NPC intimacy XP or separate movie reward. Shared-scene completion, Researching and Material Science are not extra service gates. Current loading removes a retired no-op service callback from older saves, so that callback adds no further reward or prerequisite.

Rain's Analytical Lens

Reach Ghost level 10 in Kupie's Love Training, then claim Ghost Maxed in Kupie's achievement screen to receive Rain's Analytical Lens. Rain does not have her own Kupie NPC-date entry. Completing Ghost's ordinary story or viewing a date alone does not replace level ten and claiming the achievement.
Equip this epic item on Rain through maid management. It is exclusive to maid #5 and gives:
  • 20% more Rain cleaning, rounded down in her individual calculation.
  • 15% less combined building mess, applied in the daily mess calculation.
  • 30% extra experience on the eligible daily apprentice experience calculation, rounded down; this benefits assigned apprentices, including the configured ballroom trainee calculation.
With no other equipment, the personal cleaning bonus changes Rain's level-1 contribution from 10 to 12, or MAX from 40 to 48, before combined daily modifiers. The apprentice experience bonus is a worker-experience effect, rather than a direct +30% maid-cleaning experience award, affection award or immediate stat increase.

Troubleshooting

  • Rain never appears in the alleys: own Rose & Thorn, confirm she is not already recruited, and use Explore Back Alleys. It is a random encounter rather than an ordinary scheduled portrait.
  • Research replies missing: learn Researching; Intelligence alone and the separate Material Science skill do not supply that gate.
  • Rain does not join: choose research methodology or systematic data collection, rather than simple inquiry or leaving.
  • Recruitment complete but portrait missing: visit Maids Quarters, 10 PM–2 AM.
  • Arrival missing: enter during her overnight window with Elara not on vacation.
  • Talk gives fewer points than the menu field: the current handler awards 1 base, before shared bonuses.
  • Systematic Analysis missing: check Known, Communication 45 and whether it was already claimed.
  • Maid Service missing: reach Intimate / 1,600 ordinary affection and use her overnight portrait.
  • Shared scene missing: meet every maid's recruitment/affection, the earlier scene and that shared scene's own hours; several occur outside Rain's portrait schedule.
  • Cleaning level does not rise from dialogue: cleaning experience comes from daily cleaning.
  • Lens missing at Ghost level ten: claim Ghost Maxed, then equip the item on Rain.
Her current personal content has no separate ordinary date, apartment route, regular worker-training route, resort vacation, personal Kupie-date roster or pregnancy continuation. Recruitment, personal actions, all registered Rain shared scenes and maid equipment are covered above.
