Overview

Rain is a recruitable maid whose late-night conversations complement her cleaning and equipment progression. Recruitment needs the learned Researching skill, not a high Intelligence stat alone.

Where to find them

Own Rose & Thorn, learn Researching, then use Explore Back Alleys in the Medieval Back Alleys: 45 minutes and 15 Energy per attempt. Rain is a random encounter. Choose the research-methodology or systematic-data-collection topic when she appears; either recruits her without a separate hiring fee.

After recruitment, her portrait is at Maids Quarters, daily 22:00–02:00. Return then with Elara home from vacation for Rain's Arrival.

Route and relationship milestones

Ordinary Talk takes 15 minutes, gives 1 base affection and has 30 positive uses per game day. The portrait window crosses midnight; the new day resets the counter, but moving rooms does not.

At Known (25) and Communication 45, Systematic Analysis takes 30 minutes and gives +1 Intelligence and 10 direct affection, once. This lesson does not teach Researching or Material Science.

At Intimate (1,600), Maid Service is a repeatable 30-minute conversation with a 15-base-affection reply.

Rain's Analytical Lens is her personal maid-equipment item. Claim the Ghost level-10 achievement reward in Kupie's Love Training, then equip it through Maid Management: +20% maid cleaning, −15% mess and +30% apprentice experience. It is not learned through the Systematic Analysis conversation.

Use the Maid System guide for cleaning levels, equipment slots and apprentices. Ordinary affection does not directly set Rain’s cleaning level, and her portrait has no ordinary gift button.

Favorite stats are Intelligence, Perception, Technology. Learning Material Science improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Without Researching, the simpler encounter replies do not recruit her. Learn the skill and search again.
  • The 85% encounter roll applies after Rain is selected from the random pool; it is not an 85% chance on every exploration.
  • For a missing shared scene, check all recruited maids and the scene’s own time window rather than only Rain’s overnight portrait.
  • After reaching Ghost level 10 in Love Training, claim the associated achievement reward to receive the Lens.

概览

Rain是可招募的女仆，深夜谈话与清洁、装备进度分别推进。招募需要已学会研究技能，单纯智力高不能代替。

去哪里找

拥有玫瑰与荆棘，学会研究后，在中世纪后巷使用探索行动，每次45分钟、15精力。Rain属于随机遭遇；遇到后选择研究方法或系统化数据收集话题，任一都可招募，不另收雇佣费。

招募后每天22:00–02:00可在女仆宿舍找到她。Elara结束度假后，于该时段回访触发Rain的到来。

路线与关系进展

日常交谈消耗15分钟，获得1基础好感，每个游戏日30次。排班跨过午夜；新的一天会重置次数，仅切换房间不会重置。

已知（25好感）且沟通45后，系统化分析消耗30分钟，一次性获得智力+1与10直接好感；这不是研究或材料科学教学。

亲密（1,600好感）开放女仆服务，每次30分钟，对应答复给予15基础好感，可重复。

Rain的分析镜片是她的专属女仆装备。在Kupie 的爱之训练将Ghost升至10级后，领取对应成就奖励，再从女仆管理装备：女仆清洁+20%、脏乱−15%、学徒经验+30%。这件装备不来自系统分析谈话。

清洁等级、装备槽与学徒请看女仆系统指南。普通好感不会直接设定Rain的清洁等级，头像菜单也没有普通送礼按钮。

偏好的属性为智力、感知、科技。学会材料科学可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 未学会研究时，简单问答不会招募她；先学习技能，再继续探索。
  • 85%判定发生在随机池已选中Rain之后，并不代表每次探索都有85%概率遇到她。
  • 共同事件未出现时，核对参与女仆是否都已招募及事件时段，不只看Rain的深夜头像。
  • 爱之训练中Ghost达到10级后，还需领取对应成就奖励才会获得镜片。
