Esperanza — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Esperanza is Kings Demesne's dance instructor. Her current route includes dance training, daily conversation and gifts, two personal heart-to-hearts, two dates, a refinement-training room and community scenes. Her private evening is authored, but its action hours currently conflict with her ordinary NPC schedule; see Private Evening and Current Access Limits before trying to unlock it.

Recommended Progression

  1. Complete the brothel operation choice, then enter the Grand Ballroom, noon–5 PM, for A Graceful Introduction. It awards 8 fixed base affection; Funny adds +4, Kind or Flirty +3.
  2. Use daily Talk, Dance Training and, from Acquaintance, Perfume and Jewelry. Their daily allowances are shared across her two locations.
  3. At Known, visit the Noble Quarter, 6–9 PM on an odd day, for A Different Kind of Lesson: 6 fixed base affection, with Flirty +4, Kind +3 or Funny +2. With Communication 65, take the one-time Grace and Elegance reward at the Ballroom.
  4. At Acquaintance, complete Dancing Toward. At Friend, take Bazaar Date, starting 2–5 PM at the Ballroom.
  5. At Friend with Esperanza, Known with Elara and Rose & Thorn reputation 7, meet them at the Ballroom, noon–5 PM, while Elara is home, for The Art of Nobility. This unlocks Esperanza's Ballroom in brothel management.
  6. Return to the main Rose & Thorn location while Elara is home for An Unexpected Visit, then explore the six room scenes below. These room visits do not have to be completed in a particular order.
  7. At Close Friend, complete Last Dance at the Ballroom and Sunset Walk, starting 6–9 PM on odd days in the Noble Quarter.
  8. At Crush and Charm 20, learn the Romantic skill from her Ballroom lesson. The skill adds a flat +25 final affection to completed H2H and apartment conversations. It is separate from the Romantic relationship stage.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Dance Together and the Skill-Name Mismatch

Dance Together is authored for the Ballroom at Acquaintance, costing 60 minutes and 20 energy, with 8 base affection, once per day. Its visibility condition specifically asks for Dance Master.
The available dance skill is named Dance Mastery. That name does not satisfy the literal Dance Master condition. Esperanza's daily Dance Training also does not award Dance Master. Accordingly, this action is not unlocked through the ordinary dance lessons in the current code. The same mismatch affects the optional Round 2 answer in Last Dance, but does not prevent completing that H2H with its standard answers.

Troubleshooting

  • For A Graceful Introduction, finish the brothel operation choice and visit the Ballroom during its noon–5 PM window.
  • Known comes before Acquaintance. Her one-time Communication 65 reward can be taken before the Acquaintance gifts.
  • Share the daily gift/Talk allowance across both locations; changing location does not reset it.
  • For The Art of Nobility, meet both relationship requirements and reputation 7, and bring Elara home from vacation.
  • For room scenes, recruit and activate the required worker, finish An Unexpected Visit, and enter that exact room.
  • For Sunset Walk, start between 6 and 9 PM on an odd-numbered day. This is a Noble Quarter date, not a Sirena Isle sunset H2H.
  • The Romantic skill and Romantic relationship stage are separate requirements. Learning the skill does not make the night actions overlap her schedule.
  • Missing Dance Together or Visit Esperanza can result from the current skill-name and schedule conflicts above, rather than insufficient affection.
