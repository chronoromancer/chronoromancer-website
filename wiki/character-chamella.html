Overview

Chamella has personal actions at the Syndicate Funhouse. Use the Syndicate Objectives for the surrounding quest order; this guide keeps the plot’s explanations and revelations inside the game.

Where to find them

The Funhouse: 09:00–15:00. These separate time windows matter when checking a portrait.

Route and relationship milestones

The normal Talk action requires Acquaintance (50). Earlier introductions and story events supply the route context; repeated waiting at the wrong hour will not replace their prerequisites.

Stay a While requires 1000 affection, not the Intimate stage at 1600. Continue the available relationship activities until the action appears; an exact point display may depend on your learned skills.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Optional reference: relationship bonuses

Meeting each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before the remaining relationship bonuses. The favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct one-time affection rewards are added as listed.

Charm 80; Creativity 75; Perception 75

Favorite skill: Deception

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Listed time and resources are base values; applicable skills, difficulty and active bonuses can change the shown cost or affection. Base affection, direct affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Check the Funhouse clock and Acquaintance (50) before expecting Talk.
  • Follow the prior Syndicate Objective when an automatic scene is missing. Affection alone does not bypass mission order.

概览

Chamella 的个人行动位于辛迪加的欢乐屋。相关任务顺序请先看辛迪加目标；这里不解释剧情揭示。

在哪里找到

欢乐屋：09:00–15:00。寻找肖像时请注意这一独立时段。

路线与关系节点

常规交谈要求相识（50）。较早的介绍与剧情事件负责开启路线，在错误时段反复等待无法代替前置。

「多待一会儿」要求 1000 好感度，并非亲密阶段的 1600。若界面未显示精确分数，可继续可用的关系行动，直到该按钮出现。
