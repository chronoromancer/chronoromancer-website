Momosagi——完全走过

概览

Momosagi 是一个 中世纪粉红兔女郎 通过隐藏招募 两层地牢通道她变得 26岁 她的个人肖像出现在电影里 偶数晚上的卧室主人.
她目前的路线包括Talk、Pet和Feed、每日鼓励、三次可重复的有偿图像活动、工人参观和8个培训选择、与Pepper的共享会面、两次精心设计的训练升级、度假村的心对心、私人海滩内容和 增加20次共享度假村聚会.本指南给出了实际要求,同时将场景的个人发现留给游戏.

Momosagi — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Momosagi is a Medieval pink bunny girl recruited through a hidden floor-two dungeon passage. She becomes worker 26 at the Rose & Thorn. Her personal portrait appears in the Master Bedroom on even-numbered evenings.
Her current route includes Talk, Pet and Feed, daily encouragement, three repeatable paid image activities, worker visits and eight training options, a shared encounter with Pepper, two crafted training upgrades, a resort Heart-to-Heart, private beach content and 20 additional shared resort encounters. This guide gives the actual requirements while leaving the scenes' personal discoveries to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Own the Rose & Thorn brothel and reach Beast 20. On ordinary dungeon floor 2, interact with the hidden wall at x=2, y=10, the third column, eleventh row in zero-based map coordinates.
  2. Complete The Scared Bunny. It activates worker 26 and awards 10 base Momosagi affection. This is an authored rescue, with no preceding random monster-capture roll or battle required by its trigger.
  3. Visit Master Bedroom, 9–11 PM on even-numbered game days, with Momosagi active and home. Use Talk, Pet and Feed; Pet/Feed give two base points each, while Talk gives one.
  4. Use Cuddle Momosagi once daily when her home portrait is present for Loyalty +1 up to 100 and 1 base affection. At the respective Acknowledged/Friend/Crush gates, the three paid image sessions offer separate direct affection and experience rewards.
  5. Use Visit Worker during her assigned shift, initially 8 AM–10 PM, for the separate daily worker session. Lingerie is her best ordinary session gift. Train her through levels 1–8 while managing Stress.
  6. With Momosagi and Pepper both active, home and Known, enter Master Bedroom 6–10 AM for Bunny Bonding once. This automatic encounter does not require Momosagi's evening portrait.
  7. Send Momosagi on a Sirena Isle vacation. Complete Sunset Walk at the Beach, 5–8 PM, and explore her personal and shared resort activities.
  8. At In Love, return her home. Give a crafted Golden Pulse Bracelet for One Heart, Two Voices, after Sunset Walk, and an Abyssal Looking Glass for A Name She Gives You, after her rescue. Use her 9–11 PM even-day Master Bedroom portrait.
The two crafted upgrades are independent. The cowgirl upgrade needs the resort H2H; the oral upgrade needs the rescue, without additionally requiring the cowgirl upgrade or sunset walk.

Dungeon Rescue and Home Availability

The hidden passage checks brothel ownership, Momosagi not already active, and player Beast at least 20. It is on floor 2 at (2, 10) in the ordinary dungeon; an arbitrary wall or a random encounter elsewhere does not replace that tile. Interact with the wall as a dungeon obstacle, rather than walking into an ordinary room tile.
Completing the rescue activates worker 26, records the canonical rescue completion and gives 10 base affection, once through the live scene. Her worker-management initial shift is 8 AM–10 PM, but her personal Master Bedroom portrait is 9–11 PM on even days. Changing the worker's assigned shift does not change that personal portrait schedule.
Vacation removes her home presence. Keep her active and return her home before looking for home actions or crafted upgrades. She is a numbered brothel worker, rather than a tamed monster assigned to a Dungeon cell.

Troubleshooting

  • Rescue does not start: check brothel ownership, Beast 20, ordinary floor 2 and exact hidden wall (2, 10). Worker 26 must not already be active.
  • Momosagi is absent at home: keep worker 26 active and home, then visit Master Bedroom, 9–11 PM on an even day. Her assigned shift is a separate schedule.
  • Feed gives nothing: carry positive player Food; a no-food response gives no affection.
  • Image session is absent: check her live evening portrait, paid access and the separate Acknowledged / Friend / Crush gate.
  • Worker visit is unavailable: check the assigned shift, active/resting/vacation state, daily visit and 60 Energy / 40 Calm before buffs.
  • Bunny Bonding is absent: both workers must be active, home and Known; enter Master Bedroom 6–10 AM, rather than waiting for Momosagi's evening portrait.
  • Cowgirl gift is absent: complete Sunset Walk on vacation, return her home, reach In Love and carry Golden Pulse Bracelet.
  • Oral gift is absent: check canonical rescue completion, In Love, Abyssal Looking Glass and the even-day home window.
  • Resort activity is absent: confirm worker 26 is vacationing and actually present at that room, then check its separate hour and daily allowance.
Momosagi has no configured ordinary personal apartment, pregnancy route, KTV/Night Club invitation, Kupie album, Christmas scene or maid cleaning progression. Her worker visits, evening image activities and resort content are the distinct playable systems above.
