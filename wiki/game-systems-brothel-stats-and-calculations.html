这是工人统计、每日收入、压力、经验、清洁、声誉、学徒和报告收集的数字参考。 导线 买下大楼,招聘工人, 并推进各个房间和故事。
以下公式描述当前每日计算. 工人的工资是每日一卷,而不是每个客户的薪酬乘以每卷。 金钱奖金分阶段适用;将其全部百分比加在一起不会复制一份报告。

阅读每日报告

报告涵盖 游戏中已完成的天数 从您获得该业务的那天起。当前未完成的一天没有计算。打开报告会赶上缺失的完成日并存储每个结果,所以重读一份已计算的报告不会再次判给收入。
报告采集者提供储存的每日报告。收入输入 待定; 使用募集行动将正的待收收入转化为财富。 查看一份更旧的报告不会再次收集当天的钱。
今天的薪水用的是名声等级和清洁度 开始计算清理以后就解决了 今日的名声奖励用的是清洁 打扫完之后今天获得的声誉会影响随后的日薪,而不是已经定价的一天。
补习计算使用当前工人名册、任务、设备,并按每份缺失报告运行,而不是重放一份历史名册的快照。它们仍然处理每份完成的天数。如果希望及时看到管理变化的效果,定期访问报告。

更高的外观或Charisma可以提高上限;提高较小的静态可以提高下限。 保持两者的平衡可以使最低支付更强。

压力和工作EXP

工作压力

每个工人,从 10,或 7如果 Willow 活动:
  1. 成功 Stamina 卷: -2.
  2. 添加 最大( 0, 客户 - 2).
  3. 减掉工人设备的压力,然后在零下铺.
  4. 应用主寝室 / Brothel Dungeon 保护或医务预防.
  5. 将剩余工作压力加到现有的压力中,将压力控制在0-100。
睡房主徒弟赚了钱 零工作与积极事件压力 Brothel Dungeon学徒不能通过工人更新器获得压力,也不能失去忠心;他们保留现有的压力,仍然可能受益于压力缓解。两个任务都只是将旧压力重置于零。

医务和医学

让 (单位:千美元) 医务学徒人数和 页:1 该建筑的医学水平:
每个工作压力点的概率=分钟(100米,×a)%。
在医学0,学徒防止 分钟( 出现压力, a) 点数不随机滚动。在0以上,每工作压力点一次滚动机会,然后防止 大于 成功计数和学徒保证。保证是一层楼,而不是每卷成功单上增加额外的预防。
10个工作压力、3个学徒和20个医学:卷a 60% 每一点的机会;至少防止3点,最多10点; 新工作压力;它不会抹去旧的应激或自动取消事件应激.

忠诚、访问和培训

所有30名工人都参加目前对忠诚的计算,按特定工人的费率计算。 Raven被排除在普通访问选择者之外,但她的忠诚对于日常工作仍然很重要。
  • 每过一天,都记录工人的衰败机会。 成功将失去1个忠诚;成功级联将失去1个附加点。
  • 30岁以下工人拒绝的机会可能使他们拒绝当天的工作。
  • 80岁以上,工人的奖金机会可以产生三个同样选定的奖金之一: 1至2个额外客户和每个额外薪级表, 1.25 每日工资,或 +2 平日声誉完全80没有资格。
  • 客户以外的付款不会独立地重复先前的双薪或设备奖金阶段;后来的培训、VIP和有针对性的活动乘数适用于工人综合工资。
  • 度假,Brothel Dungeon学徒, 和忠心锁设备暂停腐烂 而不积累积压 以后。
  • 普通的忠心更新使用0-100范围;度假胜地和特定设备可以将其提升到100以上,甚至达到自己的上限。 度假归来不会立即丢掉更高的价值。
对于完成的玩家训练,重点收益是 亲吻 —— 魅力, 口头-情报, 传教士-外观, 牛女 →, Doggie 能力, 特殊 1 + 数据交换. Special 2/3 每人将两个随机的初级统计数提高1. 快乐艺术 增加一个随机主点。
培训通常增加 20 压力,或 10 种术超过100种原因的压力 2每个溢出点的健康损害Brothel Dungeon学徒没有受到培训压力,因此,受保护的培训费用不会造成溢出损害。
训练的工人EXP基地 玩家Erotic Experience 1 -10级是 2, 13, 24, 35, 46, 57, 68, 79, 90, 100。为 Tantric Arts 添加 10, 乘以 1.5 , 加上感官大师和短音, 保留 80% 和短音, 完成匹配的训练升级后再加双倍。这是不同于普通日常工作的 EXP 。

QQ回游戏系统

This is the numerical reference for worker stats, daily earnings, stress, experience, cleaning, reputation, apprentices, and report collection. See Brothel guide for buying the building, recruiting workers, and progressing its individual rooms and stories.
The formulas below describe the current daily calculation. Worker pay is one daily roll, not a payment multiplied by every client. Money bonuses apply in stages; adding all their percentages together will not reproduce a report.

Reading the Daily Report

Reports cover completed in-game days from the day you acquired the business onward. The current unfinished day is not calculated. Opening the report catches up missing completed days and stores each result, so rereading a calculated report does not award its earnings again.
The report picker offers stored daily reports. Earnings enter pending collection; use the collection action to move positive pending earnings into Wealth. Looking at an older report does not collect that day's money a second time.
Today's pay uses the reputation rank and cleanliness at the start of the calculation. Cleaning resolves later, and today's reputation reward uses cleanliness after that cleaning. A reputation rank earned today affects subsequent daily pay, not the already priced day.
Catch-up calculations use the current worker roster, assignments, equipment, and state as each missing report runs, rather than replaying a historical roster snapshot. They still process those completed days once each. Visit the reports regularly if you want to see the effect of management changes promptly.

Buying Stats with EXP

Raising a primary stat from N to N + 1 costs N + 1 worker EXP, provided the stored stat is below 999. Increasing Stamina through the management stat button also restores the worker to the recalculated maximum Health.
For example, Ability 30 → 31 costs 31 EXP; 31 → 32 costs 32 EXP. The cost follows the stat being raised, not a universal worker level.

Client Count and Daily Pay

Client Count

For raw worker Ability A:
Minimum clients = max(1, floor(A ÷ 10) − 2).
Maximum clients = floor(A ÷ 10) + 2.
Roll an inclusive integer between them, then add applicable room, equipment, and distributed client bonuses. Ability 50 starts at 3–7 clients.
  • Bedroom Level 3: +1; Level 4: +2; Level 5: +3 clients.
  • Bar apprentices: +2 clients to themselves.
  • VIP beneficiaries: +1 client to themselves.
  • Kitchen: one randomly assigned building-wide client per apprentice.
  • Effective Promotion above Level 1: roll 0 through the level extra clients for the building, then distribute them randomly.
  • Maid advertising items: distribute their summed extra-client pool among workers actually available and not refusing. One worker can receive several.
  • Worker equipment: add that worker's client bonus.
Kitchen and Promotion distributions are drawn before the event and refusal checks, so a later refusal can waste their allocation. Maid advertising resolves refusals first and only assigns its pool to workers who will work. These are building-wide pools, not the listed extra count for every worker.

Pay Range

After adding equipped Appearance/Charisma bonuses, let P be Appearance and C be Charisma:
Base minimum pay = floor(min(P, C) ÷ 2).
Base maximum pay = max(P, C) × 2.
Modify that range using starting cleanliness, clothing, and the worker's bedroom, truncating at each stage. Then roll one daily payment between the resulting bounds.
Higher Appearance or Charisma can raise the upper bound; raising the smaller stat raises the lower bound. Keeping the two balanced makes the minimum payment stronger.

Daily Double Pay

Bonus chance = floor(Ability ÷ 2) + today's clients at this stage.
A 1–100 roll at or below that value doubles the daily roll. A threshold of 100 or more guarantees the double-pay result. Ability 50 and 5 clients gives a 30% chance.
This is one satisfaction-style pay bonus per worker per day. It is not a separate roll for every client, nor a direct payment of daily pay × client count.

Pay-Range Example

Appearance 40, Charisma 30 gives $15–$80. Starting cleanliness 90 raises the maximum to $128. Clothing Level 6 changes the bounds to $21–$204; Bedroom Level 5 changes the maximum to $326. The daily roll is therefore $21–$326, before the double-pay check and later bonuses.

Worker Pay Bonus Order

After the daily roll:
  1. Apply the double-pay result if successful.
  2. Apply summed worker equipment income percentage, then flat income.
  3. Apply a multiplicative item effect such as Pearl Necklace ×2.
  4. Apply a successful high-Loyalty bonus: extra-client payments, ×1.25 pay, or +2 daily reputation.
  5. Multiply by 1 + 0.5 × completed training upgrades for this worker.
  6. Double pay for a VIP beneficiary.
  7. Apply that worker's targeted daily-event gold multiplier last.
Completed gift training upgrades stack additively for income: one gives ×1.5, two ×2, three ×2.5, and so on for the upgrades available to that worker. They do not compound as ×1.5 ×1.5. An upgrade also doubles EXP from its matching player training action, separately from daily work EXP.
An event that sets targeted pay to ×0 keeps it at zero even with training upgrades, items, and VIP doubling. Global building-event income is handled later, before tax.

Stress and Work EXP

Work Stress

For each worker, begin with 10, or 7 if Willow is active:
  1. Successful Stamina roll: −2.
  2. Add max(0, clients − 2).
  3. Subtract worker equipment's stress reduction, then floor at zero.
  4. Apply Master Bedroom / Brothel Dungeon protection or Infirmary prevention.
  5. Add the remaining work stress to existing Stress, clamped to 0–100.
Master Bedroom apprentices gain zero work and positive event stress in the daily system. Brothel Dungeon apprentices cannot gain Stress through the worker updater and cannot lose Loyalty; they retain existing stress and may still benefit from stress relief. Neither assignment simply resets old Stress to zero.

Infirmary and Medicine

Let a be the number of Infirmary apprentices and m the building's Medicine level:
Chance per incoming work-stress point = min(100, m × a)%.
At Medicine 0, apprentices prevent min(incoming stress, a) points without random rolls. Above zero, roll that chance once per incoming work-stress point, then prevent the greater of the success count and the apprentice guarantee. The guarantee is a floor, not extra prevention added on top of every successful roll.
For 10 incoming work stress, 3 apprentices, and Medicine 20: roll a 60% chance for each point; prevent at least 3 and at most 10. This prevents new work stress; it does not erase old Stress or automatically cancel event stress.

Work Experience

  1. Base work EXP equals the client's count before high-Loyalty extra clients.
  2. Successful raw Intelligence roll doubles that base.
  3. Add +1 EXP per Secret Room apprentice, then worker equipment flat EXP.
  4. Apply worker equipment EXP percentage, truncating.
  5. Master Bedroom apprentices receive ×1.5, truncating.
  6. If the worker has an apprentice assignment, add the maid Mentorship percentage of that result, truncating the bonus.
Event EXP is awarded separately during event processing and appears in the report total. It is not run through the work-EXP multipliers a second time. High-Loyalty extra clients are added later, so they produce extra pay and mess but do not add work stress or work EXP in this calculation.
Ballroom training instead rolls 60–100 + 1–max(1, raw Intelligence) EXP, then adds applicable Mentorship. It does not use the ordinary Intelligence double-work-EXP roll.

Loyalty, Visits, and Training

All 30 workers participate in the current loyalty calculation, with worker-specific rates. Raven is excluded from the ordinary visit selector, but her Loyalty can still matter to daily work.
  • For each elapsed day, roll that worker's decay chance. A success loses 1 Loyalty; a successful cascade roll loses one additional point.
  • Below 30, the worker's refusal chance may make them refuse that day's work. Exactly 30 does not trigger this condition.
  • Above 80, the worker's bonus chance may produce one of three equally selected bonuses: 1–2 extra clients and an extra pay-range roll for each, ×1.25 daily pay, or +2 flat daily reputation. Exactly 80 does not qualify.
  • The extra-client payments do not independently repeat the earlier double-pay or equipment bonus stages; the later training, VIP, and targeted-event multipliers apply to the combined worker pay.
  • Resort vacations, Brothel Dungeon apprentices, and loyalty-lock equipment pause decay without accumulating a backlog for later.
  • Ordinary Loyalty updates use a 0–100 range; resort vacations and specific equipment can raise it above 100, up to their own caps. Returning from vacation does not instantly discard the higher value.
For completed player training, the focused gains are Kissing → Charisma, Oral → Intelligence, Missionary → Appearance, Cowgirl → Stamina, Doggie → Ability, Special 1 → Dexterity. Special 2/3 each raise two random primary stats by 1. Pleasure Arts adds one random primary-stat point.
Training normally adds 20 Stress, or 10 with Tantric Arts. Stress beyond 100 causes 2 Health damage per overflow point. Brothel Dungeon apprentices incur no training stress, so that protected training cost cannot create overflow damage.
Training's worker EXP base by player Erotic Experience level 1–10 is 2, 13, 24, 35, 46, 57, 68, 79, 90, 100. Add 10 for Tantric Arts, multiply by 1.5 with Sensual Mastery and truncate, keep 80% and truncate, then double for a completed matching training upgrade. This is a different formula from ordinary daily work EXP.

Fame and Reputation

Each working day, average Ability, Appearance, Charisma, Stamina, Intelligence, and Dexterity. Appearance/Charisma include the item bonuses used by the daily pay calculation. A 1–100 roll at or below the average adds 1 permanent Fame. Events can separately change Fame.
The initial daily reputation pool is the sum, over working workers, of:
current Fame + clothing flat bonus + bedroom flat bonus + worker item flat reputation + successful Loyalty reputation bonus.
Apply these stages in order:
  1. Multiply by post-cleaning cleanliness and the global event reputation multiplier; truncate. A reputation-blocking event sets this base to zero.
  2. With effective Promotion above 1, roll an integer percentage from 0 through 10 × the level and add that percentage.
  3. With effective Decor above 0, roll an integer percentage from 0 through 5 × the level and add that percentage.
  4. Owned Christmas Wreath: +5%.
  5. Verified Master Tier: +5%.
  6. Office: +5% per apprentice.
  7. Owned Bar: +5%, plus +1% per Bar apprentice.
  8. Apply summed maid equipment reputation percentage.
  9. An assigned Ballroom trainee adds +10%.
Each percentage bonus uses the current running value and truncates its added amount. These stages compound. The result adds to accumulated reputation points and updates the rank.

Building Income, Tax, and Upkeep

Start with the sum of all working workers' final daily pay. Apply, in this order:
  1. Starting reputation rank: +5% × (rank − 1).
  2. Owned Christmas Wreath: +5%.
  3. Verified Master Tier: +5%.
  4. Office: +5% per apprentice.
  5. Owned Bar: +5%, plus +1% per Bar apprentice.
  6. Effective Decor: a random integer percentage from 0 through 5 × the level.
  7. Summed maid equipment wealth percentage.
  8. Global daily-event income multiplier; truncate to obtain gross income.
The current Christmas Wreath check is ownership; it does not require the Christmas event to be active. Maid wealth bonuses affect the actual earnings, rather than being display-only.
Tax = floor(gross income × 15%).
Normal net = max(0, gross − tax − daily upkeep).
If there is a Ballroom trainee, Esperanza's fee is floor(normal net × 15%), deducted from that positive normal net. Monster income is then added separately. The normal negative result is covered by the business rather than deducted from your Wealth in this daily-profit calculation.

Daily Upkeep

$60 + $20 × (effective Master Bedroom level + sum of all defined worker-bedroom levels + effective levels of Bar, Maids' Quarters, Infirmary, Security, Exterior, and Brothel Dungeon).
The calculation includes bedrooms even when their workers are resting or not earning that day. Special-room effective levels are derived from the room's base and apprentice count; Maids' Quarters uses acquired maid count plus apprentices. Assigning staff can therefore increase upkeep. This function does not add an independent maid-wage charge or a $20 charge for every other specialty room.

Building-Income Example

Suppose worker pay totals $1,000, starting rank is 10, the Wreath is owned, there is one Office apprentice, the Bar has two apprentices, Decor's roll gives +10%, and maid equipment gives +5%. With no Master Tier or event change:
1,000 → 1,450 → 1,522 → 1,598 → 1,709 → 1,879 → 1,972 gross.
Tax is $295. With upkeep $200, normal net is $1,477. A Ballroom trainee would cost $221, leaving $1,256 before separate monster income. This example follows integer truncation at every bonus stage; a single summed percentage gives a different answer.

Mess and Maid Cleaning

Worker Mess

Base mess is the worker's final client count, including high-Loyalty extra clients. Roll 1–100 once:
Roll Worker mess
At most 10 + clients floor(clients × 2)
Otherwise, at most 20 + clients floor(clients × 1.5)
Otherwise, at most 30 + clients floor(clients × 1.25)
Otherwise clients
With 8 clients, the probabilities are 18%, 10%, 10%, and 62%, giving 16, 12, 10, or 8 mess. The thresholds are cumulative; they are not separate 18%, 28%, and 38% chances. VIP beneficiaries double the final worker mess.
An owned Bar adds 10 + Bar apprentice count mess. Working monster clients add 2 mess each. Apply maid equipment's summed mess reduction percentage and truncate, then subtract its flat reduction, with a minimum of zero.

Then add 10 cleaning per Maids' Quarters apprentice. Roll an integer percentage from 50 + variance item bonus through 150 + variance item bonus and apply it to that total, truncating.

Personal flat bonuses are added after the maid's percentage bonus, rather than multiplied by it. For example, base 20, +25%, and +5 flat gives 30, not 31.

Daily Events and Customer Appreciation

Daily events are selected before the main worker calculations from workers who are present, not resting, and not Ballroom trainees. Their stat, Health, Loyalty, Fame, and EXP changes are applied then. Report columns describe those changes; they do not apply them again afterward.
Events can target a single worker's pay, modify the whole building's income, multiply reputation, or block reputation gain. Worker-targeted pay multipliers apply after VIP and training bonuses. Building-wide income events apply before tax and collection. The report's displayed global event bonus is descriptive and is not a second payment.
Different events test different stats and can use their own minimum/maximum chance, skill adjustment, Security effect, or failure consequences. Dexterity is valuable to many events even though it is absent from the direct client and pay formulas. A skill's descriptive text alone is not evidence that it modifies every daily roll.
Customer Appreciation is a separate once-per-business-day opportunity, with a 1 in 19 opportunity roll when there is no pending reward. Eligible workers must actually have worked with clients and have Clothing Level 1–4. The system also needs an eligible customer at the current reputation rank.
Its quality chance is 20 + average(Charisma, Appearance) for Brothel mode, or 20 + average(Charisma, Intelligence) for Hotel mode. Add 8 + 5 per Security apprentice if Security is built, then the worker item event-success bonus; clamp to 5–95%. A successful opportunity creates the follow-up clothing reward. It is not one independent chance per client or per worker, and clothing Level 5 ends that worker's eligibility for this particular reward path. Level 6 clothing uses its separate upgrade path.

Practical Checks

  • Balance Appearance and Charisma for a stronger pay floor, then improve Ability for clients and the double-pay threshold. More clients also mean more mess and work stress.
  • Raise Intelligence for worker EXP and use that EXP to improve the stats you need. Fame grows from the average of the six primaries, rather than Ability alone.
  • Keep cleaning capacity ahead of the roster. Cleanliness helps today's reputation after cleaning and later days' pay range.
  • Check Loyalty and existing Stress before blaming room upgrades for a missing worker income row.
  • Use apprentices deliberately: VIP increases mess, Ballroom removes one earner and charges a fee, while staffing can raise upkeep.
