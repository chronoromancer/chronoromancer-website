Overview

Azariel offers relationship interactions, two lessons, a small business-observation route and Balance Stone bonding. These services have separate requirements.

Where to find them

She is in Secret Room, all day once its door is unlocked. Use the strange-door interaction in the house and its in-game hint to obtain access; the entrance itself takes 0 min.

Route and relationship milestones

After the introduction, Known 25/Perception 15 unlocks Empathy. Close Friend 200 unlocks Soul Sight, which costs 1 Crystal as well as the listed time and resources.

The business route starts at Friend 100 with The Missing Column, then Close Friend 200 with An Interested Witness. Both require ownership of Rose & Thorn; the second awards 1 Perception once.

Business Observations costs $10,000/20 min, at most once/day, after the first business heart-to-heart. Bring a completed daily report she has not already discussed, with at least one non-vacation staff entry in that report and at least one active employee currently home. It gives observations, not automatic collection or a recalculated business day.

In Love 3000 plus Neutral alignment tier 9 Harmonized unlocks Shield of Balance. Carrying Balance Stone separately triggers its recognition scene; no affection threshold is added to that recognition.

Intimate Bonding requires the Balance Stone and In Love 3000:60 min, one stone level, once/day. Other crystal services are explained in the shared system guides.

After Story 84 and Crush 400, one additional optional question can appear in this room. It takes 0 min and no money, once. Its wording and conversation are left for in-game discovery; it is not a paid material-exchange service.

Favorite stats are Charm, Mystic, Rogue. Learning Mystic Seduction improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • For Business Observations, a new calendar day alone is insufficient if the latest completed report was already discussed.
  • A report with everyone on vacation, or no active staff currently at home, blocks consultation before payment.
  • Shield of Balance needs the Neutral alignment track; a high Good or Evil rank does not replace it.

概览

Azariel提供关系互动、两门课程、简短的经营观察路线及平衡之石羁绊。各项服务的条件分开计算。

去哪里找

房门解锁后，她全天位于密室。从青楼的奇怪房门互动进入，依游戏内提示取得权限；进入房间本身为0分钟。

路线与关系进展

初次见面后，已知25、感知15可学习共情；亲密朋友200可学习灵魂视界，后者除表中时间和属性消耗外，还需水晶×1。

经营路线从朋友100的缺失的那一栏开始，再于亲密朋友200进行动了心的观察者。两者都要求拥有玫瑰与荆棘；第二次交心一次性增加1感知。

经营观察要求完成第一次经营交心，消耗$10,000、20分钟，每天最多一次。需有她尚未讨论过的已完成日报，日报中至少一位员工不处于度假状态，且当前至少一位在职员工留在青楼。她提供观察意见，不会自动收款或重算经营日。

恋爱中3000、中立阵营第9阶“和谐”可触发平衡之盾。持有平衡之石则独立触发灵魂石认可事件，不额外要求好感。

亲密羁绊要求持有平衡之石、恋爱中3000；60分钟，灵魂石等级+1，每天一次。其他水晶服务见相关系统指南。

完成主线84、达到迷恋400后，房间中还可出现一个额外问题，0分钟、不收费，仅一次。具体措辞与谈话留待游戏中发现；这不是付费材料兑换服务。

偏好的属性为魅力、神秘、盗贼。学会神秘诱惑可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 经营观察要求一份尚未讨论过的新日报；仅跨过午夜不一定够。
  • 日报中所有人都在度假，或当前没有在职员工留在青楼时，咨询会在收费前被挡住。
  • 平衡之盾要求中立阵营；善良或邪恶的高阶不能替代。
