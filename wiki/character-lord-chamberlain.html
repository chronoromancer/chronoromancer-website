Overview

Lord Chamberlain is a court-route contact. His personal action is a short conversation; the larger account review and inspections belong to the court quest chain.

Where to find them

Throne Room07:00–10:00 / 17:00–19:00—

Noble Quarter20:00–22:00 even days—

Route and relationship milestones

Meet him at the Throne Room from 07:00–10:00 or 17:00–19:00 for his introduction. After the Royal Appointment, reach Acknowledged (10) with him and the King and reputation 4, then use Discuss the Rose & Thorn Accounts in the Throne Room from 17:00–19:00. It takes 0 minutes and starts the court route.

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The account discussion is a Throne Room location action, not a missing extra portrait skill.
  • Even-day Noble Quarter visits are only 20:00–22:00. Talk uses the same normal daily count across his destinations.

路线与关系节点

在王座厅 07:00–10:00 或 17:00–19:00 可完成他的介绍。获得王室任命后，使他与 The King 都达到已遇见（10），并让声誉达到 4，再于王座厅 17:00–19:00 使用账目讨论地点行动，耗时 0 分钟，开启宫廷路线。
