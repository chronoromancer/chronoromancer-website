解决问题

  • Monk 缺席: 使用匹配当前时间的房间。 脚本室和涂鸦室是不同的地点他的大厅画像就在晚上
  • 精神纪律缺失: 到达 已知,已经 来文 40参观大厅 下午6时至10时,并检查其一次性奖励是否已经提出。
  • 大厅上午不开会: 检查 Grace 众所周知,度假回家,以及 下午7时至2时; Monk的普通肖像不需要.
  • 手写相遇没有: 检查 Grace 知识,一个 偶数日 和 下午2时至6时.
  • 培训厅没有碰面: 检查 Sabbath 亲密的朋友和家,然后进入大厅 下午2时至6时.
  • Monk 从未作为客户访问: 到达 7级妓院声誉,满足可选预订要求,并允许随机选择访客。

Overview

Monk is a 68-year-old Medieval monastery elder whose daily routine moves through the Scriptoria, Illuminated Manuscript Room, training hall and Scriptorium Lobby. His current content includes ordinary Talk, a one-time Perception reward, three shared encounters with Grace or Sabbath, and an optional private-client appearance.
This guide covers the actual unlocks and choices without revealing the shared scenes' story outcomes.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Medieval monastery and follow his room schedule below. Talk gives one actual base point per use, including at Stranger.
  2. Reach Known, 25 ordinary affection, and Communication 40. Visit Scriptorium Lobby, 6–10 PM, to claim Spiritual Discipline for Perception +1 and 8 direct affection, once.
  3. With Grace Known and home from vacation, enter the Scriptorium Lobby, 7 AM–2 PM, for A Gentle Reminder. Its Understanding reply maximizes Monk's affection.
  4. With Grace Acquaintance and home, enter the Illuminated Manuscript Room, 2–6 PM on an even-numbered day, for Lost in Translation. Again, choose Understanding for Monk's highest reward.
  5. With Sabbath Close Friend and home, enter the Monks' Temple Training Hall, 2–6 PM, for Discipline Under Siege. Rescue Monk gives Monk's highest bonus.
  6. If using the optional Receive a Client system, reach brothel reputation level 7 to add Monk to its random visitor rotation. This is separate from his friendship and monastery encounters.
The shared encounters do not require Monk to reach a particular relationship stage and do not form a sequential chain. Their automatic time windows can differ from his ordinary portrait schedule.

Spiritual Discipline — One-Time Perception Reward

At Known, 25 ordinary affection, with Communication 40, start Spiritual Discipline from Monk's Scriptorium Lobby portrait, 6–10 PM. It takes 30 minutes, costs no configured money, Energy, Calm or inventory item, and gives Perception +1 and 8 direct Monk affection.
It completes once through the shared one-time reward guard. The reward is a Perception stat increase, rather than a taught player skill named Spiritual Discipline. Raising affection further does not unlock a repeat of the stat reward, and his morning Lobby story scene does not make the ordinary reward action available outside the evening portrait window.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Monk enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level7. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Monk. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Monk has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is gentleness and discretion, with Grace listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Bellavia, Grace, Willow.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Monk affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Monk.

Troubleshooting

  • Monk is absent: use the room matching the current hour. Scriptoria and Illuminated Manuscript Room are different locations, and his Lobby portrait is in the evening.
  • Spiritual Discipline is missing: reach Known, have Communication 40, visit Lobby 6–10 PM, and check whether its one-time reward is already claimed.
  • Morning Lobby encounter is absent: check Grace Known, home from vacation, and 7 AM–2 PM; Monk's ordinary portrait is not required.
  • Manuscript encounter is absent: check Grace Acquaintance, an even-numbered day and 2–6 PM.
  • Training Hall encounter is absent: check Sabbath Close Friend and home, then enter the hall 2–6 PM.
  • Monk never visits as a client: reach brothel reputation level 7, satisfy the optional booking requirements, and allow for random visitor selection.
Monk has no configured ordinary gifts, personal dates, Heart-to-Hearts, apartment visit, pregnancy route, Kupie album, KTV/Night Club invitation, Christmas scene, numbered worker training, maid cleaning or resort vacation. The training hall is his scheduled monastery location, rather than his own numbered worker-training system.
