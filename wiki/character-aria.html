A.R.I.A.

Overview

A.R.I.A. is a 28-year-old android with an analytical personality. Her route includes dungeon recruitment, three personal heart-to-hearts, a garden date, ordinary worker visits and training, two special-gift upgrades and repeatable resort activities. This guide gives the access requirements and choices without retelling her origin or major scene outcomes.

Finding and Recruiting A.R.I.A.

  1. Reach floor 45 of the normal dungeon.
  2. Investigate the hidden wall at tile (1, 6) and defeat the ARIA Proxy Warden. This is a prerequisite encounter; it is not A.R.I.A.'s personal seven-round battle. The warden victory unlocks the floor's sealed threshold and grants 5 Components.
  3. Enter the floor's narrative miniboss encounter. A.R.I.A. has seven rounds, 2,700 enemy energy before difficulty adjustments and targets player energy. Replenish energy before starting.
  4. Win and complete the ensuing recruitment scene. Her worker record becomes active, you gain 25 affection directly and +1 Technology. An optional additional encounter is offered afterward; you do not need to accept it to activate her worker record.
  5. At the Rose & Thorn, find her in the Garden from 8–11 AM if the courtyard is built, or the Calibration Room from 11 AM–6 PM. The Calibration Room entrance is on the upstairs route and appears with her active worker record.

Personal Route Walkthrough

  1. Use Talk to reach Known. Talk takes 15 minutes, awards 1 base point, and allows five uses per day. The garden and room versions use the same action tracking.
  2. At Known, start Calibration in the Calibration Room, 11 AM–6 PM. It costs 75 minutes, 15 energy and 5 calm and completes once.
  3. At Friend, return to the Garden, 8–11 AM, for First Garden after Calibration. Build the courtyard first. This conversation costs 90 minutes and 15 energy and restores 5 calm.
  4. At Close Friend, start Closer in the Calibration Room, 11 AM–6 PM, after First Garden. It costs 90 minutes, 15 energy and 5 calm, once.
  5. The separate Garden Walk date needs Friend and the built courtyard. Start it from the Calibration Room between 11 AM and 4 PM. It costs 120 minutes and 15 energy, with no configured money cost, and completes once.
  6. At In Love, the two special-gift upgrades below become available once their respective conversation prerequisites and inventory requirements are met.
There is no separate permanent first-kiss action to perform after Closer; completing that conversation records its progress. The resort Sunset Walk is a different, repeatable conversation.
The shared Reassure A.R.I.A. action also appears when she is present at a home location and available rather than vacationing. It takes 20 minutes, gives +1 loyalty up to 100 and 1 base affection, and is available once per day. It follows her NPC schedule, rather than requiring her nighttime work shift.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

Use the brothel's worker visit menu with worker 21. Her initial shift is 8 PM–4 AM; later scheduling changes can change that window. She must be active, not resting, not at the resort and not already visited that day.
A visit normally costs 60 minutes, 60 energy and 40 calm. The Time, Power and Focus buffs halve their corresponding cost to 30 minutes, 30 energy and 20 calm. Within the visit, each category—Talk, Tip, Gift and Training—has its own once-per-session allowance. Training adds no separate entry charge.
Training begins at level 1 and advances one level per completed session, up to 8. Earlier options remain available. The menu only offers a type when its corresponding images exist.

Clothing

A.R.I.A. participates in Fernando's clothing fittings while active. The tailor selects randomly from eligible workers; buying a fitting does not guarantee she is selected. Ordinary fittings raise clothing by one level, with up to two Fernando upgrades per worker while below level 5. A separate MAX fitting applies at level 5. Gold and fabric costs follow the current quote, which rises across the roster.

If Progress Stalls

  • On floor 45, defeat the Proxy Warden at (1, 6) before attempting A.R.I.A.'s sealed encounter.
  • For her home interactions, check that worker 21 is active and use the correct daytime location.
  • First Garden needs both the built courtyard and completed Calibration. Her morning garden schedule ends at 11 AM.
  • Closer needs Close Friend and completed First Garden.
  • The date starts in the Calibration Room, not from her morning garden menu, and only 11 AM–4 PM.
  • Worker visits use her shift hours, initially 8 PM–4 AM, rather than her conversation schedule.
  • Special upgrades need the actual crafted gift in inventory, In Love, the listed H2H and no vacation.
  • Resort Sunset Walk can repeat daily; it does not replace the three home conversations.
