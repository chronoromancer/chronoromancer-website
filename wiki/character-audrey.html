Audrey — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Audrey is a 22-year-old Mandragora plant-woman who tends the Rose & Thorn garden. Her current content includes dungeon recruitment, ordinary garden conversation and encouragement, a garden date, Nature's Embrace training, a living medicinal gift, worker visits, three crafted-gift training upgrades and resort activities.

Finding and Recruiting Audrey

  1. Reach floor 35 of the normal dungeon and enter Audrey's seven-round narrative miniboss encounter. Her encounter targets health, with 1,800 enemy energy before difficulty adjustments. Restore health and prepare useful skills before starting.
  2. Win and complete the recruitment scene. It activates worker 23, after which she can appear in the Brothel Garden, 8 AM–6 PM.
  3. The optional Spend some time with her before you go branch needs 150 intimacy experience. Recruitment is already complete before that choice; accepting the branch is optional.
  4. Inside that optional branch, There is more additionally needs Beast 35 and gives +1 Beast after its scene. You do not need that branch for her later garden relationship.

Garden Relationship Walkthrough

  1. After recruitment, visit Audrey in the Garden, 8 AM–6 PM. She needs an active worker record and cannot use her home route while vacationing.
  2. Talk takes 20 minutes, gives 1 base affection and allows four uses per day.
  3. The separate shared Admire Audrey's Garden action takes 20 minutes, adds 1 loyalty up to 100 and 1 base affection, and can be used once per day while she is present. It follows her NPC schedule rather than requiring an additional worker-visit session.
  4. At Friend, start Garden Walk between 9 AM and 4 PM. This one-time date costs 120 minutes and 25 energy, with no configured money cost. It gives 8 base affection plus choice bonuses: Caring +5 → Caring/Funny +5 → Caring/Flirty +5.
  5. At Romantic and Mystic 60, the paid version offers Nature's Embrace. It costs 350 minutes, 180 energy and 100 calm, gives 30 base affection through its scene and teaches the skill. It disappears once learned.
  6. At In Love, collect her living medicinal gift and complete whichever crafted-gift upgrades you want. One upgrade requires the garden date; another requires her resort Sunset Walk.
There is no separately authored home H2H chain or apartment visit. Sunset Walk with Audrey is the repeatable resort conversation below.

A Living Gift and Rainheart Cuttings

At In Love, visit the Garden from 8 AM–6 PM and select A Living Gift. Audrey must be active and not vacationing, the gift scene must be unfinished, and Rainheart Cuttings must still be collectible: do not already hold the specimen or have it recorded as delivered.
The action takes 20 minutes and awards Rainheart Cuttings on completion, once. Its scene What I Choose to Share favors Caring or Flirty (+3 affection) over Funny (+2). The gift itself does not raise brothel Medicine.
Take the Cuttings to Willow in the Nurse's Room and use her specimen hand-in action while Willow is active and not on vacation. That one-time delivery consumes the specimen, gives +1 brothel Medicine and 10 base affection to Willow, and triggers After the Noise. An already delivered specimen cannot be collected again from Audrey.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

Use the brothel's worker visit menu with worker 23. Her initial shift is 8 AM–6 PM; later scheduling changes can change that window. She must be active, not resting, not at the resort and not already visited that day.
A visit normally costs 60 minutes, 60 energy and 40 calm. The Time, Power and Focus buffs halve their corresponding cost to 30 minutes, 30 energy and 20 calm. Within the visit, each category—Talk, Tip, Gift and Training—has its own once-per-session allowance. Training adds no separate entry charge.
Training begins at level 1 and advances one level per completed session, up to 8. Earlier options remain available. The menu only offers a type when its corresponding images exist.

Clothing

Audrey participates in Fernando's clothing fittings while active. The tailor selects randomly from eligible workers; buying a fitting does not guarantee she is selected. Ordinary fittings raise clothing by one level, with up to two Fernando upgrades per worker while below level 5. A separate MAX fitting applies at level 5. Gold and fabric costs follow the current quote, which rises across the roster.


If Progress Stalls

  • Recruitment is on floor 35; it does not require accepting the optional post-recruitment branch.
  • The garden menu requires active Audrey and her 8 AM–6 PM schedule.
  • Garden Walk has the narrower 9 AM–4 PM start window and needs Friend.
  • Nature's Embrace requires Romantic, Mystic 60, paid access and an unlearned skill, plus its substantial resource costs.
  • A Living Gift needs In Love and uncollected, undelivered Rainheart Cuttings. Collecting them is separate from Willow's Medicine reward.
  • The Prismatic Spell Brooch upgrade needs the resort Sunset Walk, not the garden date. Send Audrey on vacation, complete the walk, then return to her home garden for the gift action.
  • Worker training has a once-daily visit allowance; moving between home interactions does not reset it.
