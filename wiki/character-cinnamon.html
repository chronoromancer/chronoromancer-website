概览

Cinnamon 是猫女杂技演员 中世纪德米斯内国王的杂技训练厅她目前的路线包括个人故事链、两个大厅H2Hs、一个Bazaar日期、一个统计奖励、建造自己的表演室、第三个家庭H2H、工人培训和升级、服装和度假村活动。

Before Recruitment

  1. Enter the Acrobat Training Hall, 6 AM–9 PM, for The Cat Girl Acrobat, her introduction. It gives 1 base affection.
  2. Use Talk, available from Stranger, to reach Known. Chocolate gifts and Safe Landing become available there.
  3. At Known, revisit after the introduction for Landing Practice.
  4. At Friend, revisit for The Old Poster after Landing Practice. Complete Balance Notes with Ability 35 for Ability +1 and 8 direct affection. Take the Bazaar Date from 9 AM–2 PM.
  5. At Close Friend, complete Rafter Quiet after Safe Landing. Revisit for A New Routine after The Old Poster.
  6. At Crush, revisit for The First Bow after A New Routine.
  7. Complete every prerequisite in the construction checklist below before commissioning her room. Neither the H2Hs nor Balance Notes can be postponed until after the move: the room menu copies Talk and Chocolate, but does not copy these hall progression actions.
The hall scenes have their own completed flags. Completing an H2H does not replace the corresponding personal story scene, and raising affection does not skip the earlier scenes.

Building Her Performance Room

Choose Build Cinnamon's Performance Room at the Acrobat Training Hall after meeting all of these requirements:
  • Own the Rose & Thorn and have built the Menagerie.
  • Complete The Cat Girl Acrobat, Landing Practice, The Old Poster, A New Routine and The First Bow.
  • Complete the Bazaar Date.
  • Complete Safe Landing and Rafter Quiet through their H2H buttons.
  • Complete the one-time Balance Notes reward. This is a separate required completion, even though it is not a story scene.
  • Bring $250,000, 100 common materials, 50 rare materials and 10 epic materials.
Construction takes 240 minutes and consumes those resources through the action. It gives 10 base affection, with +3 from any of its three responses. The room and recruitment become available when the construction scene finishes.
Completion activates worker 6, changes Cinnamon's home and portrait schedule, and opens Enter Cinnamon's Room from the Menagerie. Entering or returning to the Menagerie takes zero travel time. Use the dedicated room rather than looking for her old hall portrait.
There is no additional room-level purchase in this commission action. The required built Menagerie and completed story/reward/H2H checklist are the readiness gates; money and the three material tiers are construction costs.

After the Move

  1. Visit Cinnamon's Room, 6 AM–11 PM, while she is home. Let Room for Ideas trigger after construction.
  2. At Crush, start Your Seat, Saved in her room from 6 AM–10 PM, after Room for Ideas and Rafter Quiet. It requires worker 6 to be found and completes once.
  3. Use her room's Talk and Chocolate actions, daily encouragement and the worker-visit menu for further progress.
  4. At In Love, after Your Seat, Saved and Room for Ideas, give the crafted upgrade gifts below through their dedicated actions.
  5. Once recruited and active, Cinnamon can use Fernando's fittings and the resort vacation system. Vacation replaces her home availability during that period.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

Clothing

Cinnamon participates in Fernando's clothing fittings while active. The tailor selects randomly from eligible workers; buying a fitting does not guarantee she is selected. Ordinary fittings raise clothing by one level, with up to two Fernando upgrades per worker while below level 5. A separate MAX fitting applies at level 5. Gold and fabric costs follow the current quote, which rises across the roster.


If Progress Stalls

  • Before recruitment, use the Acrobat Training Hall, 6 AM–9 PM. After construction, use Cinnamon's Room, 6 AM–11 PM.
  • Missing the commission action means a prerequisite is incomplete: check Balance Notes, the Bazaar date, both hall H2Hs, all five personal hall scenes, and the built Menagerie.
  • The commission costs $250,000 plus 100 common / 50 rare / 10 epic materials. Fabric and ingredients do not pay the material requirement.
  • Finish the construction scene before expecting worker 6 or the room entrance. Her hall portrait disappears after completion.
  • For Your Seat, Saved, finish Room for Ideas, reach Crush, and arrive before 10 PM. The portrait remains until 11 PM, but this H2H's window ends earlier.
  • For the two crafted upgrades, check In Love, Your Seat, Saved, Room for Ideas, the actual gift and home availability.
  • Worker visits use the assigned shift, initially 3–11 PM, and their separate once-per-day allowance.
  • Resort pair/trio scenes require every participant to be active and vacationing, not only Cinnamon.
  • Cinnamon has no Present-house apartment visit or separate personal repeatable intimate-video action in her current character menu.
