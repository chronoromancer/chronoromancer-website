概览和时间表

Zodiac是混沌星系未来时代25岁的算命者,与中立对齐。 福特勒的Booth, 整天,每一天从辛迪加门或大顶端到达展位。
她最喜欢的数据是 神秘90、佩西翁85和情报85她特殊的关系技巧是 占卜她的路线结合了辛迪加的追求链条,个人事件,两个独立对话以及两个可重复的私人场景,这个指南给出了要求而不透露追求结果或个人秘密.
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

对话与精神交流

说话 要求 知识+,需要15分钟和奖项 1个真爱, 5/天。 动作卡列出0, 但其操作者授予1。 完成她的早期自动会议和Syndicate工作, 到达Talk大门。
精神交流 要求 朋友+, 神秘18+ 和尚未掌握的技能。 180分钟,300美元,能源45和Calm40,教授精神交流和赠款 12个基调 通过学习动作。它没有额外配置的付费版本门。这是与Divination不同的技能,它提供Zodiac的特殊关系奖金,如果已经学到。
不为Zodiac配置普通礼物或一次性玩家-stat奖励.

Overview and Schedule

Zodiac is the Chaos Syndicate's25-year-old fortune teller in the Future era, with Neutral alignment. Her ordinary portrait is at the Fortune Teller's Booth,all day,every day. Reach the booth from the Syndicate Gates or Big Top.
Her favorite stats are Mystic90,Perception85 and Intelligence85; her special relationship skill is Divination. Her route combines the Syndicate quest chain, personal events, two independent conversations and two repeatable private scenes. This guide gives requirements without revealing quest outcomes or her personal secrets.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Talk and Spirit Communication

Talk requires Acquaintance+, takes15 minutes and awards 1 actual base affection, five/day. The action card lists zero, but its handler awards one. Complete her early automatic meetings and Syndicate jobs to reach the Talk gate.
Spirit Communication requires Friend+,Mystic18+ and the skill not already learned. It costs 180 minutes,$300,Energy45 andCalm40, teaches Spirit Communication and grants 12 base affection through the learning action. It has no extra configured paid-version gate. This is a different skill from Divination, which supplies Zodiac's special relationship bonus if already learned.
No ordinary gift or one-time player-stat reward is configured for Zodiac.

Troubleshooting

  • Talk missing: reach Acquaintance through the automatic route;her portrait alone is not enough.
  • Spirit Communication missing: reach Friend,Mystic18 and check whether already learned.
  • A Fair Game orCloser missing: check its independent Friend/Crush gate and whether already completed.
  • Quest stalls: visit the exact registered location and complete every stated prior event,including other characters' branches.
  • Open Doors missing: complete Lines on the Board and reach Rogue50;the next council step requires Rogue55.
  • Later reinforcement scenes missing: finish story beat85 and the preceding Syndicate events.
  • Private scene missing: reach its raw affection threshold and wait for that scene's next daily allowance.
