Jade — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Jade is Silas's adult daughter, a skilled accountant who enjoys the bazaar's performances. Her route includes gifts, sanctuary conversations, a festival date, the Emerald Dragon trade, recruitment at the Rose & Thorn, three crafted training upgrades, shared character encounters and Sirena Isle activities. This guide explains the playable requirements without retelling the family's story or the other characters' revelations.

Recommended Recruitment Walkthrough

  1. Meet Jade at the Central Plaza, 4–6 PM, or the Performance Stage, 9 AM–3 PM on odd days. Talk to build her relationship. At Known, give Flowers and visit the Secluded Sanctuary after 9 PM on even days.
  2. Complete Starlight Reflections in the sanctuary. It is useful early affection and unlocks a later crafted upgrade. At Communication 52, also take Accounting Wisdom once for Intelligence +1 and 10 direct affection.
  3. Build Silas to Known and Jade to Acquaintance, own the Rose & Thorn, and obtain the Emerald Dragon key item using one of the paths below.
  4. Visit Silas's Exotic Goods shop during 6 AM–9 PM and choose Trade Emerald Dragon from the location menu. This takes 120 minutes, completes once, delivers the statue, gives 30 base Jade affection and 20 base Silas affection, and opens her Master Bedroom portrait.
  5. Use Discuss Finances and Discuss Workers, each 15 minutes / 2 base points / three per day with separate counters. At Friend through Close Friend, use the ordinary Jewelry button for 12 base points, once per day. That portrait gift disappears at Crush.
  6. Reach Crush and choose A Special Connection in the Master Bedroom, 9 AM–2 PM. It takes 60 minutes, 30 Energy and gives 10 Calm. Starting it activates worker 15; completing it records the H2H and gives its rewards. It is offered while she is not yet an active worker.
  7. At Crush and Commerce 17, learn Market Analysis in the same portrait window for 90 minutes, $300 and 40 Energy, with 10 base affection. The lesson disappears once learned.
  8. At 800 ordinary affection, use Private Moment in the Master Bedroom. At In Love, use the three crafted upgrades and Queenstone bond described below. Active worker visits and Sirena Isle vacation content expand her route further.
The trade moves her daytime portrait; A Special Connection activates the worker. Do not treat the trade alone as recruitment. Starlight Reflections, Accounting Wisdom, Festival Evening and the earlier shared encounters are not extra trade prerequisites.

Christmas Gift from Jade and Sophie

Activate Christmas Spirit through the Winter Wonderland toggle, then return to the Rose & Thorn Master Bedroom during 6–11 PM or midnight–3 AM. Gift from Jade & Sophie requires both Crush, active workers 2 and 15, and the paid version. It takes 30 minutes, completes once, gives 15 direct affection each to Jade and Sophie, and completes Holly's task 12 for one Christmas Token.
This is a Master Bedroom location action, separate from Jade's daytime portrait. It does not require a real-world Christmas date. See Holly for the full holiday task route.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • Jade missing from the bazaar after the trade: her daytime portrait moves to the Master Bedroom, 9 AM–2 PM. Her even-day Sanctuary, 9 PM–midnight remains available at Known.
  • The trade is missing: own the Rose & Thorn, carry the Emerald Dragon key item, reach Silas Known / Jade Acquaintance, and enter his Exotic Goods shop in its 6 AM–9 PM window.
  • Jade is home but not an active worker: complete A Special Connection at Crush. The trade alone does not activate worker 15.
  • Festival Evening missing: it is a shared one-time date. Before the trade, use an odd day, 9 AM–2 PM Stage; afterward use 9 AM–1 PM Master Bedroom.
  • Ordinary Jewelry disappeared: that button is only for Friend through Close Friend. Worker Jewelry gifts use a different menu and preference.
  • Market Analysis missing: reach Crush and Commerce 17; the button hides once the skill is learned.
  • Crafted gift action missing: reach In Love, carry the exact consumable gift, complete its prerequisite and use the 9 AM–2 PM Master Bedroom portrait, with Jade home.
  • Christmas gift missing at night: use the Master Bedroom location menu, both workers active and both Crush, with Christmas Spirit enabled and paid access.
  • Kupie selection or apartment invite missing: Jade has no current Kupie or apartment route configured. She is also outside the current pregnancy roster.
  • Several NPCs overlap: use the available character-cycle control to select Jade's portrait.
