Overview

Jade starts around the plaza and performance stage, then moves into Rose & Thorn. The Emerald Dragon exchange opens her daytime home; a later heart-to-heart is the worker-recruitment step.

Where to find them

Before the trade: Central Plaza daily 16:00–17:59; Performance Stage on odd days 09:00–14:59. At Known (25), Secluded Sanctuary on even days 21:00–23:59.

After The Trade, her daytime portrait moves to Master Bedroom, daily 09:00–13:59. Her even-day sanctuary visit remains separate.

Route and relationship milestones

Own Rose & Thorn, reach Known (25) with Silas and Acquaintance (50) with Jade, then bring Emerald Dragon to Silas’s shop at 06:00–20:59. The trade takes 120 minutes and gives Jade 30 base affection and Silas 20.

Emerald Dragon is a key item. The alley merchant sells it for $1,500, or $500 with Negotiation; a dungeon encounter offers a fight or a 10 Dragon Coin exchange. Held or delivered ownership prevents another acquisition.

At Crush (400), start A Special Connection in the master bedroom to recruit her. Starting activates her as a worker; finish the conversation for its completion milestone. The earlier trade alone does not recruit her.

Festival Evening has daytime starts despite its title: before the trade, performance stage on odd days 09:00–13:59; afterward, master bedroom 09:00–12:59. These are two entrances to the same date.

At In Love (3,000), three crafted gifts upgrade her training at home in the master bedroom: Golden Pulse Bracelet after Starlight Reflections for An Unbalanced Equation; Abyssal Looking Glass after the trade for Beyond Appraisal; Moonfang Leather Choker after the trade and recruitment conversation for The Terms Are Mine. Each consumes its item at 0 minutes, doubles matching training experience and adds 0.5 of base income, up to 2.5× with all three.

An owned Queenstone and In Love enable 60-minute daily bonding for one stone level.

What usually blocks the next step

  • After the trade, look in the master bedroom rather than the stage.
  • Festival Evening starts during the listed daytime window, not at night.
  • Recruitment needs A Special Connection at Crush; the statue exchange only opens the earlier move.
  • For crafted gifts check the exact item, In Love, preceding conversation and her return home.
  • Evening Revelations needs Friend with Jade and Acquaintance with Ben Arthur; let the scene reveal its subject during play.

概览

Jade最初在广场与表演舞台附近活动，之后可搬到玫瑰与荆棘。翡翠巨龙交换会开放白天的新住处，正式招募则需要后续交心对话。

去哪里找

交换前：每天16:00—17:59在中央广场，单数日09:00—14:59在表演舞台；达到已知（25）后，双数日21:00—23:59还会在隐秘圣所出现。

完成交易后，白天头像改到主卧，每天09:00—13:59出现；双数日圣所会面另算。

路线与关系进展

交换要求拥有玫瑰与荆棘、Silas为已知（25）、Jade为相识（50），并持有翡翠巨龙。06:00—20:59到Silas商店，花费120分钟，给予Jade30基础好感、Silas20基础好感。

翡翠巨龙是关键物品。后巷商人售价$1,500，学会谈判后为$500；地下城对应遭遇也可战斗或交付10龙币取得。持有或已经交付后，不会再次取得。

达到迷恋（400）后，在主卧开始特别的联系才会招募她。开始即启用员工，仍应完成对话以推进对应记录；先前交换不会直接招募。

节庆之夜实际从白天开始：交换前为单数日表演舞台09:00—13:59，交换后为主卧09:00—12:59。这是同一次约会的两个入口。

恋爱中（3,000）后，在她回家后的主卧可交付三种礼物：完成星光沉思后，用金脉手镯触发失衡的方程；交换后，用深渊魔镜触发估价之外；交换及招募对话后，用月牙皮颈圈触发条件由我来定。每项消耗礼物，不消耗时间，使对应训练经验翻倍，并增加基础收入的0.5倍，全部完成为2.5倍。

持有女王石并达到恋爱中后，可每天花60分钟建立一次羁绊，使石头提升1级。

送珠宝仅在100—399好感可用，迷恋后消失。讨论财务和讨论员工各有独立次数。市场分析还要求商业17，仅能学习一次。

她在塞壬岛可交谈、用餐、放松、水疗和看日落，没有单人海滩私密时光；共同活动见度假村指南。

她偏好的属性为创造力、魅力、能力。学会舞台魅力可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 交换后找不到她时，改到主卧查看。
  • 节庆之夜应按表中白天时段开始，不必等晚上。
  • 招募需在迷恋后进行特别的联系，雕像交换只负责前期搬迁。
  • 礼物升级需指定物品、恋爱中、前置对话，以及她已回家。
  • 夜晚的领悟要求Jade为朋友、Ben Arthur为相识；事件内容留待游玩时了解。
