Overview

Rei is the Arcade’s gaming partner. Her route includes Invading Space, optional dates, a separate private action and apartment visits that can teach System Hacking.

Where to find them

Find her at Retro Arcade, daily 15:00–22:00; entering during those hours gives her introduction. Apartment visits use a remote option at Apartment Complex,22:00–24:00, after Crush (400) and a win on the game’s highest difficulty, Impossible.

Route and relationship milestones

Talk gives 1 base affection in 15 minutes, three positive uses daily. From Acknowledged (10), Invading Space takes 30 minutes and$10, three plays per day. Winning on the four difficulties gives 8/13/20/35 base affection; losing still gives 1/2/3/4, while quitting early gives a negative reward.

At Known (25), Gaming Luck Theory needs Communication 28 and 30 minutes for+1 Luck and 10 direct affection, once. Teddy gifts give 10 base affection, 10 minutes, one Teddy, two per day.

To open her apartment, win Impossible, then reach Crush (400). The apartment’s opening conversation has a separate System Hacking teaching topic while the skill is unlearned. Follow that topic to its conclusion; it does not need the Technology 35 opening used for the different hardware topic.

The apartment opening costs 180 minutes and 40 Energy. The ordinary 700-affection Play with Rei is a separate Arcade action, 20:00–22:00,60 minutes and 25 Energy, 25 base affection, once daily; it does not require the highest-difficulty win.

Her optional family continuation uses the apartment’s dedicated choice after the shared Pregnancy requirements are met. Follow the Pregnancy guide for conditions and stage timing; later automatic scenes appear while she is at the Arcade.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The highest difficulty is labeled Impossible in the current game menu. A win on a lower difficulty does not open apartment access.
  • For an apartment visit, use the Apartment Complex after 22:00; the Arcade portrait has already ended.
  • The 700-affection movie and apartment entry have separate gates.
  • If System Hacking is already learned, its teaching topic disappears.

概览

Rei是在街机厅陪你玩游戏的伙伴。她的内容包括入侵太空、可选约会、独立私人行动，以及可学习系统黑客的公寓来访。

去哪里找

她每天15:00–22:00在复古游戏厅，于该时段进入可触发介绍。公寓来访从公寓楼远程发起，时间22:00–24:00，需迷恋（400好感）并在最高难度“不可能”获胜。

路线与关系进展

交谈消耗15分钟，获得1基础好感，每天3次。已遇见（10好感）后，入侵太空每次消耗30分钟与$10，每天3局。4档难度获胜分别给予8／13／20／35基础好感，落败仍有1／2／3／4，提前退出则有负收益。

已知（25好感）且沟通28后，游戏幸运理论消耗30分钟，一次性获得幸运+1和10直接好感。赠送泰迪熊消耗10分钟与泰迪熊1只，获得10基础好感，每天2次。

公寓入口需在“不可能”难度获胜，再达到迷恋（400好感）。尚未学会系统黑客时，公寓开场有独立的教学话题，听到结尾即可学习；不需要另一条硬件话题所用的科技35。

可选家庭后续从公寓的专用选项开始，需先满足共享生育条件。具体条件与各阶段时间见生育指南，之后的自动事件在她街机厅在场时出现。
