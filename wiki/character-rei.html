  • 扩张到弱中性行星以增加产量。最接近的中性开始于 5名士兵下一个 10; 中心开始 50避免发动能量不足的进攻
  • 使用 + 在一个可负担的拥有的星球下升级: 一级 − 2 费用,以及 2— 3项费用 100。这些是匹配的信用, 与主游戏10美元入场费分开。
  • 导弹 费用 100个贷项,将每个非蓝色星球上的兵力减少 25%,最低损失1人,并有一个 15秒冷却. 速度 费用 150,将你拥有的行星的成长翻一番 7秒钟,并有一个 15秒冷却.

Overview

Rei is a 20-year-old gaming enthusiast in the Present. Her route includes arcade interaction, Invading Space, Gaming Luck Theory, three optional dates, a repeatable apartment visit that teaches System Hacking, KTV and Night Club invitations, and a separate private movie action.
The apartment requires a NOPE difficulty victory and Crush affection. The separate Play with Rei action requires 700 ordinary affection, without the apartment's NOPE flag. This guide gives requirements, replies and rewards without recounting personal story revelations.

Controls and practical approach

  • You control blue planets; Rei controls red. Select a blue planet with at least two troops, then select another planet to send half its troops, rounded. Sending to a friendly planet reinforces it; sending elsewhere attacks it.
  • Expand into weak neutral planets to increase production. The closest neutral starts with 5 troops, the next with 10; the center starts with 50, so avoid committing an underpowered opening attack there.
  • Use the + beneath an affordable owned planet to upgrade it: level 1 → 2 costs 50 minigame credits, and 2 → 3 costs 100. These are match credits, separate from your main-game $10 entry fee.
  • Missile costs 100 credits, reduces troops on every non-blue planet by 25% with a minimum loss of one, and has a 15-second cooldown. Speed costs 150, doubles your owned planets' growth for seven seconds, and has a 15-second cooldown.
  • Keep enough troops to hold your production planets while coordinating attacks. To win, capture all red planets; you do not need every neutral. Losing all blue planets ends the match in defeat.
NOPE uses the fastest enemy decision setting and shows collectible coins for only two seconds, versus five / four / three on Novice / Intermediate / Hard. Practice the controls at a lower difficulty, then select NOPE and finish an actual win to open the home route. No main-game Luck/Technology threshold is configured for selecting NOPE.

  • Rei absent: visit Downtown Arcade, 3–10 PM.
  • Apartment missing: check a recorded NOPE victory, Crush, Downtown Apartment Complex, 10 PM–midnight, and enough Energy.
  • Winning did not open the apartment: only NOPE wins set its flag; winning Hard or losing NOPE does not.
  • Visit stops after kissing: reach 600 existing affection plus accumulated visit base, or use an eligible branch that bypasses the check.
  • System Hacking missing: choose its round-1 teaching reply if the skill is not already learned. Gaming Luck Theory is a different action.
  • Voucher date missing before 3 PM: Rei's arcade portrait is not available until 3 PM.
  • Play with Rei missing: check 700 affection, arcade 8–10 PM, daily use, Energy and pregnancy restrictions.
  • Invite Date missing: check Crush, Gaming Luck Theory complete, venue 8–11 PM and its shared daily use; Night Club needs the latest supporter code.
  • Perfect invitation missing: choose five accepted answers and a +1 gift. All three listed gifts at each venue score one.
  • Family opening missing: check both Fair and Boardwalk dates, Akira's protocol, feature access, 2,000 affection and 500 Rei NPC XP.
Rei has no separate registered Kupie character entry or worker recruitment route in the current code.
