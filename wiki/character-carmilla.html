Overview

Lady Carmilla offers a Vampire progression conversation chain and the Vampirism skill lesson. The player’s Vampire stat, the learned skill and ordinary affection are separate requirements.

Where to find them

After Story 61 opens the route, go from the Library through Library Basement → Dark Corridor → Vampire Lair → Carmilla's Chamber. Her portrait is available all day in her chamber.

Route and relationship milestones

Meet her first, then revisit as Vampire reaches 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50. These five conversations require the preceding one completed; raising the stat does not skip their order. Their explanations stay in the game.

Vampirism needs Known (25), Vampire 25 and 30 Blood Orbs. The lesson consumes those Orbs once and teaches the skill; carrying 30 is not merely a threshold to keep afterward.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Optional reference: relationship bonuses

Meeting each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before the remaining relationship bonuses. The favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct one-time affection rewards are added as listed.

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Listed time and resources are base values; applicable skills, difficulty and active bonuses can change the shown cost or affection. Base affection, direct affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the lesson is absent, check Vampire 25, Known (25), 30 Blood Orbs and whether the skill lesson is already completed.
  • Mystic or affection cannot substitute for the Vampire stat.
  • The underground entrance is story-gated; her all-day schedule does not open the corridor early.

概览

Lady Carmilla 提供随吸血鬼属性推进的谈话链，以及吸血鬼化技能课程。玩家的吸血鬼属性、已学技能与普通好感度是不同条件。

在哪里找到

主线 61 开通路线后，从图书馆经图书馆地下室 → 黑暗走廊 → 吸血鬼巢穴 → Carmilla的寝室。她的肖像在寝室全天可用。

路线与关系节点

初次见面后，在吸血鬼属性达到 10、20、30、40、50 时回访。这五段谈话要求依次完成，单纯提高属性不能跳过前置；内容请在游戏中体验。

吸血鬼化课程要求已知（25）、吸血鬼 25 以及 30 鲜血宝珠。课程会一次性消耗这些宝珠，并授予技能，并非只需持有而不花费。
