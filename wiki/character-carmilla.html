概览

Carmilla女士 是个古老的吸血鬼和血魔法老师 吸血鬼巢穴她执行的路线包括对话、介绍、 五个连续的吸血鬼-洛尔场景 吸血鬼主义 与 Torea 一起进行训练和分享场景。这段路由可以解释大门,而不会透露神迹。

抵达Carmilla的会议厅

  1. 敦煌路线 : 到达 23楼 中世纪的地牢 城堡入口在 瓷砖(10,5)穿过血液排出能量,可以引发战斗,所以准备就绪。 吸血鬼巢穴,然后选择 Carmilla的房间。该门户路线没有Beat 61的前提条件。
  2. 库路由 : 完整主楼 击60,然后访问 8点到10点之间的图书馆 (单位:千美元) 击球61。一旦第61集完成,请跟随 图书馆地下室 黑暗走廊 吸血鬼巢穴 Carmilla的房间. 走廊的出入门为Beat 61;较早进入地下室不会打开更深的通道.

Overview

Lady Carmilla is an ancient vampire and blood-magic teacher in the Vampire Lair. Her implemented route includes conversation, an introduction, five sequential vampire-lore scenes, Vampirism training and shared scenes with Torea. This walkthrough explains the gates without disclosing the lore revelations.

Reaching Carmilla's Chamber

There are two access routes:
  1. Dungeon route: reach floor 23 of the Medieval dungeon. The castle entrance is at tile (10, 5), surrounded by blood tiles. Crossing blood drains energy and can trigger battles, so arrive prepared. Enter the castle to reach the Vampire Lair, then select Carmilla's Room. This portal route has no Beat 61 prerequisite.
  2. Library route: complete main-story Beat 60, then visit the Present Library between 8 and 10 PM for Beat 61. Once Beat 61 is complete, follow Library Basement → Dark Corridor → Vampire Lair → Carmilla's Room. The corridor's access gate is Beat 61; entering the basement earlier does not open the deeper route.
The lair's internal travel actions take zero minutes. Carmilla is present in her chamber at any hour. On the first visit, her Lady Carmilla introduction triggers automatically, grants 5 base affection, and favors Caring or Flirty +3 over Funny +2.

Conversation and Vampirism Training

Action Requirements Cost Reward Limit
Talk to Lady Carmilla Stranger or higher 20 minutes 1 base affection Four per day
Learn Vampirism Known; Vampire 25 or higher; carry 30 Blood Orbs; training not completed 180 minutes, 70 energy, 30 Blood Orbs Learns Vampirism; 15 base affection plus choice Once
Talk is the repeatable route to Known. The five lore scenes below provide additional one-time affection. Tantric Arts is her relationship bonus skill, while Vampirism is the skill she teaches; they are separate.
For the lesson, obtain 30 Blood Orb consumables, reach Vampire 25 and Known, then use Learn Vampirism in her chamber. The action consumes the orbs and its listed time and energy, plays the training scene and adds the skill. Its rewarded answer favors Caring or Flirty +3 over Funny +2. Completing the training hides that lesson thereafter.
The Vampire action gate uses the player's modified Vampire value. Knowing a similarly named skill, having high Mystic or carrying Blood Orbs alone does not replace the required Vampire threshold or affection stage.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Five Vampire-Lore Scenes

Complete her introduction, then revisit Carmilla's Chamber as your Vampire value increases. The registry checks the player's Vampire value for these automatic triggers. Every lore scene requires the previous one completed, so reaching a later threshold does not skip the earlier scenes.

If Progress Stalls

  • Use Carmilla's Room from the lair rather than looking for a separate surface-map icon.
  • The Library Basement's Dark Corridor opens after Beat 61; the floor 23 castle portal provides the other route.
  • Learn Vampirism needs Known, Vampire 25 and 30 Blood Orbs, plus sufficient time and energy. Completed training does not repeat.
  • Lore scenes use Vampire 10/20/30/40/50 in order and need each preceding scene completed. Mystic and Carmilla affection cannot replace those thresholds.
  • The Shape of Hunger needs Acquaintance with Torea, not a higher Carmilla stage.
  • Her current character data has no ordinary gift button, date, illustrated H2H, apartment conversation or worker recruitment. Her role is conversation and vampire instruction.
