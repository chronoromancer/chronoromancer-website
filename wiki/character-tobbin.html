Overview

Tobbin offers fishing conversations and a one-time Perception reward. The Fishing Hut’s equipment and boat services are location actions and remain available regardless of which staff member is present.

Where to find them

Medieval → Village Outskirts → Fishing Village (Coast) → Fishing Hut; local entrance and return take 0 minutes. Normally Tobbin is outside 04:00–17:00 while Marina staffs the hut, and inside 17:00–04:00. If Marina vacations, he covers the hut all day. The hut services are open 24 hours.

Route and relationship milestones

Reach Friend (100) and Communication 30, then take An Eye for the Water. It takes 30 minutes and gives Perception +1 and 8 direct affection once. No Energy or Calm cost is declared. His special-skill affinity does not grant a separate fishing-skill lesson.

Talk, Ask about Fishing and Hear a Fish Story each give 2 base affection. They take 15, 15 and 20 minutes, with positive daily limits of 4, 3 and 2 respectively. All four unique actions bind both locations and share their counters; moving indoors does not reset them.

The hut sells Wood Rod ($100, 10 Common materials, 10 minutes), Iron Rod ($500, 10 Rare materials, 20 minutes, after Wood Rod), Gold Rod ($1000, 5 Epic materials, 20 minutes, after Iron Rod), and ten Bait for $20/10 minutes. Rent Fishing Boat costs $60 and advances 300 minutes. See Fishing for the full session rules and ledger.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Stamina 30; Perception 35; Communication 25

Favorite skill: Master Baiter

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

Look inside when Marina is away, rather than waiting for Tobbin outside. Reaching the safe daily conversation limit means further attempts can lose affection. His current route has no personal H2H, romance, ordinary gift or worker recruitment.

概览

Tobbin 提供钓鱼谈话与一次性感知奖励。钓鱼小屋的装备及船只服务属于地点行动，不论当前由谁值班都可以使用。

在哪里找到

路线为中世纪 → 村庄郊外 → 渔村（海岸）→ 钓鱼小屋，进入与返回均为 0 分钟。通常 Marina 在小屋值班时，Tobbin 在外面 04:00–17:00 出现，17:00–04:00 则在屋内；Marina 度假时他会全天留在小屋。小屋服务全天 24 小时开放。

路线与关系节点

达到朋友（100）及沟通 30 后，可进行察水识鱼，耗时 30 分钟，一次性给予感知 +1 与 8 直接好感度，没有额外精力或镇定消耗。偏好技能不代表这里另有一项钓鱼技能课程。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

耐力 30; 感知 35; 沟通 25

偏好技能：钓饵大师

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

Marina 不在时请进小屋找 Tobbin，不要一直在海岸等待。超过每日正面谈话次数后，继续尝试可能降低好感度。他目前没有个人谈心、恋爱、普通送礼或员工招募路线。
