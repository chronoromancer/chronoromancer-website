Robin — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Robin is a 96-year-old elven archer and huntress in the Medieval era. She teaches Archery Focus and Animal Care, offers Tracking Wisdom, and has The Hunt heart-to-heart, personal and shared encounters, a Stargazing Date, a repeatable Intimate Evening at her lodge, and a separate Forest Walk movie action.
The lodge travel option unlocks through The Elusive Quarry, after The Hunt and Foxy's discovery. This walkthrough covers route requirements and rewards while leaving major story identities and outcomes to the game.

Suggested Progression

  1. Meet Robin at Archery Range, 9 AM–6 PM, and Talk up to three positive times daily.
  2. From Acknowledged and Perception 15, learn Archery Focus. From Known and Perception 20, learn Animal Care; Known and Communication 50 also unlocks one-time Tracking Wisdom.
  3. At Acquaintance, select Join the Hunt from the range. Complete its four-round H2H.
  4. Discover Foxy, then visit Forest Clearing after The Hunt for The Elusive Quarry. Completion opens Visit Robin's Lodge from the Royal Hunting Grounds edge.
  5. Visit the lodge evenings for Teddy gifts. At Friend, its paid Stargazing Date starts 9 PM–midnight. At Close Friend, the lodge's The Bow on the Wall encounter becomes available.
  6. After The Elusive Quarry, visit Forest Clearing with Robin Close Friend and Foxy Friend for A Proper Introduction.
  7. At Romantic, use Intimate Evening at the lodge, 6 PM–midnight. At 1,000 affection, the paid Forest Walk movie is a separate range action.
  8. At In Love while owning Aether Stone, use Intimate Bonding once daily to raise the stone's level.

Bards Guild performance reaction

After a Perform Song at Bards Guild, Robin's reaction can be randomly selected. It is repeatable and gives 8 fixed base + Flirty 4 = 12, or Caring 10 / Funny 9 total.
Performing requires a composed song and costs 60 minutes and 30 Energy, with one performance per venue daily. The reaction roll is 25%, plus 5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling, and owning a Lute, capped at 50%. If successful, one of four Bards Guild reactions is selected. Robin's reaction is therefore not guaranteed by a performance, and its affection is separate from the performance's ordinary currency/clout rewards.

Forest Walk Movie

At 1,000 ordinary Robin affection, select Forest Walk from Archery Range, 9 AM–6 PM, with paid access. It takes 30 minutes and 20 Energy, once daily, and gives 50 base affection, 60 player Erotic XP and 25 Robin NPC oral intimacy XP.
It has no added lodge-H2H/date completion, cash or Calm cost. Active Time / Power buffs halve movie time / Energy. This is separate from the lodge's 17-round visit and its rewards.

Aether Stone Bonding

At In Love while owning Aether Stone, select Intimate Bonding at an available range, lodge or Training Room portrait. It takes 60 minutes, raises the owned Aether Stone by one level, and is available once per day across all three locations. It gives no configured affection, player Erotic XP or NPC intimacy XP reward and consumes no stone, cash or additional Energy/Calm.
Owning the stone and reaching In Love are separate conditions; this action upgrades an owned stone rather than creating it.

Completion and Troubleshooting

NPC intimacy gallery

Robin has four current reward images, unlocking at 100, 250, 500 and 800 total Robin NPC intimacy XP. Open Intimacy details from her character details, then select an unlocked reward thumbnail. This uses total NPC experience, separately from ordinary affection and player Erotic XP. An event with multiple category tags adds its XP once to the total. Slots 5–8 have no current Robin reward file.
  • Robin absent: use range 9 AM–6 PM, lodge 6 PM–3 AM, or accessible Training Room 6–9 AM.
  • Talk becomes annoyed at another location: all portraits share her three positive talks daily.
  • Lodge travel missing: complete The Hunt, discover Foxy, then complete The Elusive Quarry at Forest Clearing. Use Visit Robin's Lodge from the hunting grounds edge.
  • The Hunt missing: reach Acquaintance and use Join the Hunt at the range; it disappears after completion.
  • Tracking Wisdom missing: check Known and Communication 50, and whether already claimed.
  • Skill lesson missing: check its relationship and Perception gates; learned lessons disappear.
  • A Proper Introduction missing: finish Elusive Quarry, reach Robin Close Friend / Foxy Friend, then visit Forest Clearing.
  • Intimate Evening missing at 5 PM: her lodge portrait begins at 6 PM. Check Romantic, entry hours and 60 Energy.
  • Stargazing missing: check Friend, paid access, lodge 9 PM–midnight, and completion state.
  • Forest Walk missing: check 1,000 affection, paid access, range hours and its daily use.
  • Aether bonding missing: check In Love, owning Aether Stone and today's shared use.
Robin has no current Kupie selection, venue invitation, pregnancy route or brothel-worker recruitment action. Her Training Room appearance does not make her a worker.
