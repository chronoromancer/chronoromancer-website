概览

Amelia 是善良的乡村治疗者 中世纪乡村疗养院她的内容包括介绍、两个心对心、社区相遇、两个外出、圣餐训练以及后来的动画关系相遇。

列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

Overview

Amelia is the kind village healer at the Medieval Village Healer's Hut. Her content includes an introduction, two heart-to-hearts, community encounters, two outings, Sacred Mending training and a later animated relationship encounter.

Where to Find Her

Visit the Village Healer's Hut in the Medieval Village Outskirts, from 6 AM to before 9 PM. Her introduction, The Healer's Hut, triggers automatically on the first eligible visit and awards 5 base points. The Respectful answer gives a 4-base-point bonus; Practical and Curious each give 3.

Talk gives 1 base point, takes 20 minutes, has no configured energy cost, and can be used five times per day. Amelia has no gift action, so progress comes from conversation, her one-time reward, heart-to-hearts and available scenes.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Walkthrough

  1. Visit the hut for The Healer's Hut introduction.
  2. Build affection with Talk. At Known and Communication 55, choose Healing Philosophy: 30 minutes, +1 Intelligence, and 11 direct affection, once.
  3. At Known, start The Healer's Craft between 9 AM and 5 PM. It costs 90 minutes and 20 energy and completes once.
  4. To open Nature Walk Date, complete A Fair Supply. This requires Acknowledged with Amelia, Friend with Willow, Willow active as a brothel worker and not away on vacation, and Amelia's introduction completed. Visit the hut between 8 AM and 7 PM after satisfying those conditions.
  5. At Acquaintance with Amelia, choose Nature Walk Date between 9 AM and 3 PM. It costs 120 minutes and 40 energy, with no money cost.
  6. At Friend, after Nature Walk, the paid-version Sunset Walk appears between 6 PM and 8 PM. It costs 120 minutes and 30 energy and also restores 10 calm.
  7. At Close Friend, after The Healer's Craft, start The Quiet Pillar between 9 AM and 5 PM: 90 minutes and 20 energy, once.
  8. At Romantic and Mystic 60, the paid-version Sacred Mending lesson becomes available. Prepare 350 minutes, 180 energy and 100 calm. The action launches its training scene and teaches Sacred Mending. Its scene gives 30 base points; the action disappears after the skill is learned.
  9. At 1,000 affection, the paid-version A Healer's Rest becomes available between 9 AM and 6 PM. It costs 90 minutes and 30 energy, awards 50 base points, and is available once per day.
The heart-to-hearts do not require completion of the dates. The Nature Walk's extra prerequisite is Willow's supply scene, not Amelia's first heart-to-heart. Sacred Mending and A Healer's Rest have their own affection and access conditions rather than a shared mandatory quest chain.

If Progress Stalls

  • Missing Nature Walk: check A Fair Supply, Willow's Friend stage, active-worker status, and vacation availability.
  • Missing The Quiet Pillar: finish The Healer's Craft and reach Close Friend, then return during its daytime window.
  • Missing Sacred Mending: check paid access, Romantic, Mystic 60, and whether the skill is already learned.
  • Missing A Healer's Rest: the threshold is 1,000 affection, not merely reaching Romantic at 800.
