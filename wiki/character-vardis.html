Meet Vardis

Vardis is the panther-beastkin warden you can meet at the Deep Woods entrance and three landmarks. She provides information about the woods and her watch. A short visit is enough to see her available interactions.

Reach the Deep Woods

Visit Medieval → Village Outskirts → Forest Clearing. With Perception 40, choose Enter the Deep Woods. Entering costs 10 minutes, 10 Energy and 10 Calm.

Vardis is present all day, every day at the four places below. You can meet her immediately at the entrance.

Meeting placeFrom the Deep Woods entrance
Deep Woods (Entrance)She is here when you enter
The Ancient TreeEast 3, then South 3
Huntress LairWest 2, then South 5
Ancient RuinsEast 4, then North 2

These directions start at the entrance each time. The woods wrap around at their edges. Each walking step costs another 10 minutes, 10 Energy and 10 Calm, so check your reserves before taking a long walk.

What a visit involves

Open Vardis’ portrait and choose the information you want. Her current visit has no personal quest or relationship route to complete. The Ancient Tree and Ancient Ruins are sightseeing landmarks, and her lair is another place to speak with her.

Her relationship stays at Stranger. Repeating questions will not unlock affection stages, dates, recruitment or a hidden reward.

Her three information options

OptionWhat you learnCost
Ask about Deep WoodsThe area and its current activities0
Ask about this placeThe landmark where you are standing0
Ask about her watchVardis’ role as warden0

You can ask again whenever you want. These questions spend no time, Wealth, Energy or Calm and award no affection. Walking between meeting places has its own costs.

Find your way back

Enter the Deep Woods is missing.
Go to the Forest Clearing and check Perception 40. The entrance is a location action there.
Vardis is absent from this part of the woods.
She appears at the entrance, Ancient Tree, Huntress Lair and Ancient Ruins. Ordinary woodland tiles have no Vardis portrait.
There is no map button.
Deep Woods uses directional walking. Reverse your steps to return to the entrance, where Leave the Deep Woods returns you to the Forest Clearing at no time cost.
I cannot start a landmark quest or raise her relationship.
Her current options provide information only. You can finish the visit without searching for an additional requirement.

认识 Vardis

Vardis 是豹族守林人，在深林入口及三处地标都能见到她。她会介绍森林、所在地点与自己的守望职责，简单拜访即可看完当前的交流选项。

怎么进入深林

前往中世纪 → 村庄郊外 → 林中空地。感知达到 40后，选择「进入深林」，消耗10 分钟、10 精力、10 镇定。

Vardis 在下列四处每天全天都会出现，刚进深林就能在入口找到她。

见面地点从深林入口出发
深林（入口）进入后即可见到她
古树向东 3 次，再向南 3 次
女猎手巢穴向西 2 次，再向南 5 次
古代遗迹向东 4 次，再向北 2 次

每条路线都从入口重新计算，森林边缘会绕回另一侧。每走一步另消耗10 分钟、10 精力、10 镇定；打算走远一点时，可以先检查剩余状态。

这次拜访可以做什么

点开 Vardis 的头像，选择你想询问的内容。她当前没有需要完成的个人任务或关系路线。古树与古代遗迹可供游览，女猎手巢穴则是另一处能和她交谈的地点。

她的关系会保持在陌生人，反复询问不会开放好感阶段、约会、招募或隐藏奖励。

三个询问选项

选项可以了解什么消耗

这些问题可以重复询问，不消耗时间、财富、精力或镇定，也不会增加好感。前往其他见面地点的步行消耗另计。

找路与常见疑问

没有「进入深林」。
先到林中空地，确认感知已达到 40，入口就在该地点的行动列表中。
走到这里却看不到 Vardis。
她只在入口、古树、女猎手巢穴与古代遗迹出现，普通林间格子没有她的头像。
为什么没有地图按钮？
深林使用方向步行。沿来路反向走回入口，再选择离开深林即可回到林中空地，离开本身不消耗时间。
无法开始地标任务，也无法提高关系。
她当前的选项只提供信息，不必继续寻找额外的开放条件。
