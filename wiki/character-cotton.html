Overview

Cotton is the local band's lead singer at the Present Downtown Music Studio. Her current route contains Band-clout introductions, conversation and gifts in her room, three personal H2Hs, three separate dates, Stage Presence instruction, shared band scenes and a repeatable private evening.

Cotton's room is inside the recording studio, reached through To Cotton's Room. It is not an apartment-complex destination. Ordinary Talk and gifts are in her room; her studio portrait supplies the first H2H rather than a second Talk menu.

Starting Her Route

  1. Raise Band clout to 16 and visit the Music Studio for Star Treatment, her introductory automatic scene. It grants 10 base Cotton affection, opening the Acknowledged Talk threshold.
  2. Build Band clout through scored Practice Drums sessions at the studio. The location action costs $30, 25 minutes and 20 energy and is offered from noon–11 PM. Clout depends on accuracy and difficulty; 80% or better earns a reward in scored mode. Practice mode earns no clout. Chill mode gives one clout at 80%+, while One Button mode halves earned clout, rounded up.
  3. Visit Cotton's Room from 2–11 PM. Hanging Out triggers after Star Treatment. Its best response is Playful +4, in addition to 5 base affection.
  4. Use Talk at Acknowledged, then Teddy, Perfume or Jewelry gifts at Known. At Known, Stage Lights can trigger in her room.
  5. At Friend, let Fan Service trigger in her room. With both Stage Lights and Fan Service completed, return to the Studio, 9 AM–2 PM, for The Unfinished Take.
  6. Complete Unfinished Chorus at the studio, after The Unfinished Take. At Close Friend, visit her room, 2–7 PM, for Off the Feed, after the first H2H and The Unfinished Take.
  7. Complete Phone Down in her room, 2–6 PM, after Off the Feed and Unfinished Chorus.
  8. At Crush, visit the room, 6–8 PM, for A Private Invitation, after Off the Feed and Phone Down.
  9. Complete On Her Terms in the room, 8–11 PM, after A Private Invitation and Phone Down. This opens Intimate Evening, also from 8–11 PM at Crush.
Her three dates and Stage Presence lesson are additional activities alongside that personal chain. Fair Date and Stargazing require Star Treatment, while the separate Voucher date does not have that scene gate.

Complete On Her Terms, reach Crush, and visit Cotton's Room from 8–11 PM for Intimate Evening. Its listed cost is 120 minutes and 50 energy, with no money payment. The action has no additional Intimate-stage, stat, player-experience, paid-version or supporter gate configured.
The encounter is repeatable. Its menu metadata specifies one per day, but its current handler does not record a daily use, so that configured limit does not itself prevent another start while the time and resource requirements remain satisfied. The personal H2Hs still complete only once.

If Progress Stalls

  • Missing ordinary Talk means Cotton is below Acknowledged or you are at the Studio instead of her room. Star Treatment gives the initial 10-base affection; it requires Band clout 16.
  • Use scored drum sessions for clout. Practice mode gives no clout, and scores below 80% do not meet the reward tier.
  • For The Unfinished Take, complete both Stage Lights and Fan Service and visit the Studio, 9 AM–2 PM.
  • For Phone Down, complete Off the Feed and Unfinished Chorus, reach Close Friend, and use the 2–6 PM room window.
  • For A Private Invitation, complete Phone Down and Off the Feed, reach Crush, and arrive 6–8 PM.
  • For On Her Terms or the private evening, use 8–11 PM. The evening needs the H2H completed; a date cannot substitute for it.
  • Fair Date and the Voucher outing have different costs and completion flags. Bring the right money or Voucher and start from her room.
  • Cotton has no worker recruitment or apartment-complex visit. Her repeatable evening takes place in her studio room.
