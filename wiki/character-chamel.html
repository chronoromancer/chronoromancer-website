Overview

Chamel is the Chaos Syndicate's playful infiltrator, found in The Funhouse. Her current route includes Syndicate introductions and missions, conversation, two illustrated H2Hs and a sequence of personal automatic scenes. This guide gives the progression requirements without explaining her story's identity revelations.

Meeting Chamel and Unlocking Talk

  1. In the Future Prime Sector, travel to Syndicate Gates and use To The Parlor. Complete The New Variable, which grants 7 base affection to Chamel. The Funny assessment answer adds 2 base points to her.
  2. Use To The Funhouse from the Syndicate hub or Big Top. After The New Variable, her Send in the Clown introduction can trigger there: 1 base affection, with Funny +4 as the highest choice reward.
  3. Continue the Syndicate missions and supporting scenes in the table below. Her ordinary Talk is locked until Acquaintance, so do not expect a repeatable Talk button at Stranger.
  4. At Known, visit The Den, 6 PM–midnight, for Reflections: 1 base affection, with Funny +4 as the best rewarded response. This is a scene appearance outside her usual Funhouse portrait location.
  5. At Acquaintance, use Talk in the Funhouse: 15 minutes, 1 base affection, five times per day. Its handler awards 1 even though its action data displays zero.
Her normal portrait is absent 9 AM–3 PM. Automatic quests have their own trigger conditions and can use different locations or hours; her normal Talk and H2H actions require her portrait schedule.
Early scene rewards do not guarantee Acquaintance by themselves. Favorite-stat bonuses, Deception and the shared reward roll affect the final gains. Progress through the other Syndicate routes for more opportunities rather than repeatedly revisiting a completed introduction.

If Progress Stalls

  • Her ordinary Funhouse portrait is available before 9 AM or from 3 PM onward.
  • Talk needs Acquaintance; its displayed zero-point value differs from the actual 1-base-point handler reward.
  • For early affection, complete The New Variable, her introduction and broader Syndicate scenes. Introduction scenes are one-time.
  • Clown Time needs Friend; Broken Carousel needs Close Friend, with no separate sunset-hour restriction.
  • The Mirror needs Curtain Call. The Real Thing needs both The Mirror and The Punchline, plus Intimate, and starts at the Big Top.
  • The Punchline depends on Chamella's The Performance, not merely high Chamel affection.
  • Her current menu has no ordinary gift, separate date button, skill lesson, apartment conversation or personal video action. Broken Carousel is the illustrated H2H despite its outing description.
