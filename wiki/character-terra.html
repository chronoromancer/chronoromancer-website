概览

Terra是 未来第一区变异植物保护的植物学家她目前的路线包括普通的Talk and Flowers、材料科学指导、10个自动个人场景、3个心对心、2个枣、一个药剂标本礼物和可选 军队的要塞招募.
本指南给出了实用的大门和最佳的回复,同时将更广泛的档案发现和故事结果留给游戏.

Overview

Terra is the botanist at the Future Prime Sector's Mutated Botanical Preserve. Her current route includes ordinary Talk and Flowers, Material Science instruction, ten automatic personal scenes, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, a medicinal specimen gift and optional Fortress army recruitment.
This guide gives the practical gates and best replies while leaving the broader archive discoveries and story outcomes to the game.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the Preserve during 6 AM–6 PM for Unnatural Selection, then use daily Talk to build the early stages.
  2. At Known, use Flowers and learn Material Science if needed. Claim Read Plant Stress at Friend with Intelligence35.
  3. Follow the automatic route table below. The Seed She Named opens after One Unapproved Seed; Beauty Without a Metric follows One Living Thing and the first heart-to-heart.
  4. At Crush, visit the Processing Zone for Beneath the Processing Zone, then return to Terra for After the Survey. This third heart-to-heart unlocks both optional dates.
  5. Continue at the Archive Facility after completing recovered archive entry 6, then finish the later story/relationship gates for The First Unmeasured Plot and What We Keep.
  6. After The First Unmeasured Plot, select Help Defend the Fortress if you want her army unit. At In Love after What We Keep, accept A Familiar Scent for Willow's medicine research.

Help Defend the Fortress

After The First Unmeasured Plot completed, choose Help Defend the Fortress at Terra's home portrait. This opens Something Worth Defending and occurs once. It has zero configured time, no recruitment fee, no extra item or affection-stage requirement beyond that earlier milestone, and no extra ordinary affection or NPC intimacy reward.
The completed plot acquires Terra as a Fortress army hero. Its replies converge on the recruitment completion. This is separate from a brothel-worker recruitment route and separate from What We Keep; the In Love finale is not an additional requirement.
Army deployment uses the Fortress system's own resources; her current base deployment cost is 40 Command and 10 Ammo. Those are deployment costs, not a fee charged by the recruitment conversation.

A Familiar Scent and Willow's Medicine Research

At In Love, after What We Keep, choose A Familiar Scent at the Preserve 6 AM–6 PM, while Terra is home. It takes 20 minutes, once, with no extra money, Energy or gift consumption.
You must not already hold or have delivered the Memorymint Runners specimen. Completing the scene grants the specimen key item, rather than directly increasing Medicine. Caring and Flirty give 3 choice base; Funny gives2. No fixed completion affection or NPC intimacy/player experience reward is configured.
