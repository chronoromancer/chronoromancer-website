概览

Gastoff 他是中世纪商人区中保留下来的铁匠。他拜访他是为了改进武器和装甲,使用采矿资源伪造,并委托主要妓院设施。他的关系内容包括介绍、两次交流、分享场景和布罗瑟尔酒吧的表演反应。他没有个人心跳、约会、公寓、工人或度假村路线。

Overview

Gastoff is the reserved blacksmith in the Medieval merchant quarter. Visit him to improve weapons and armor, use the mining-resource forge, and commission major brothel facilities. His relationship content includes an introduction, two one-time exchanges, shared scenes and a Brothel Bar performance reaction. He has no personal heart-to-heart, date, apartment, worker or resort route.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Blacksmith Shop between 6 AM and 9 PM for The Blacksmith Shop introduction. It gives 5 base points. Choose “A man of few words but serious skill. I respect that, Gastoff.” for 4 more base points; the funny answer gives 2, and the flirty answer −1.
  2. Use Talk to Gastoff up to six times per game day, at 10 minutes and 1 actual base point each. The action description lists 2, but its current handler records 1. Further attempts use −1 base points and the shared over-limit handling.
  3. Improve Strength, Creativity and armor to his favorite thresholds; Iron Will doubles the relationship multiplier. These and general relationship bonuses modify base rewards. The two one-time exchange rewards below are fixed additions.
  4. At Acknowledged, a Body Builder can take Musclecraft Exchange, 40 minutes, for 5 fixed Gastoff affection. At Known and Communication 48, take Forge Philosophy, 30 minutes, for 10 fixed affection and Strength +1.
  5. Purchase or upgrade sword and armor as needed, and bring mined ingots to Use the Forge. These shop services have no Gastoff affection prerequisite.
  6. Once the brothel reaches the required reputation, commission its facilities using the tables below. Every construction action requires only Stranger with Gastoff, the displayed money and resources, and its specific facility prerequisites.
Musclecraft Exchange's advertised Attractiveness +1 is not currently applied. Its reward goes through the core-stat handler, while Attractiveness is stored separately. The one-time completion and fixed 5 affection still work. Forge Philosophy's Strength +1 is a valid core-stat reward. Both exchanges disappear after completion.

Brothel Bar Performance Reaction

His Three Words reaction can follow Perform Song at the Brothel Bar. Have a composed song and access to the bar. Performing takes 60 minutes and 30 energy, with one performance per venue per game day.
An NPC reaction has a 25% base chance, increased by 5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling and Lute, up to 50%. Gastoff is one of seven possible bar reactions, alongside Sherry, Elara, Poppy, Kasumi, Gandon and Blinky; the outcome is not guaranteed to select him.
Three Words gives 8 base Gastoff points. Choose the funny response for +4; caring gives +2 and buddy +1. The maximum is 12 base points. This is separate from Talk's six-use allowance.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Gastoff enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level6. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Gastoff. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Gastoff has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is confidence and composure, with Bellavia listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Bellavia, Grace, Valkyra.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Gastoff affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Gastoff.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong Talk gain: the current handler uses 1, not the action metadata's 2. Favorite and general bonuses can change the final amount.
  • Musclecraft missing: it requires Acknowledged and Body Builder and is one-time. Its advertised Attractiveness bonus is not currently applied.
  • Forge Philosophy missing: reach Known and Communication 48; it is also one-time.
  • Construction missing: check reputation, the facility sequence, paid access for cells, and Raven's active recruitment for the corridor. Construction does not require a higher Gastoff friendship stage.
  • Insufficient building resources: costs apply separately to Materials, Ingredients and Fabrics, rather than a single combined quantity.
  • No Cell 1 button: the corridor commission includes Cell 1. Cell 8 is not currently commissionable.
  • Forge upgrade missing: each piece advances separately and cannot skip tiers; check mining inventory, Gems and the matching monster drop.
