Gastoff at a glance

Gastoff is the blacksmith and major construction contractor in the Medieval merchant quarter. Visit for equipment, the Forge, building commissions and two one-time stat exchanges. Ordinary conversation builds your acquaintance with him.

Find the Blacksmith Shop

Visit Medieval → Kings Demesne → Merchant Quarter → Blacksmith Shop. Gastoff is there every day, 06:00–20:59. The first visit during these hours starts The Blacksmith Shop introduction.

Personal exchanges and shared scenes

ActionRequirementsCost / allowance
Musclecraft ExchangeAcknowledged (10); Body Builder40 min; +1 Attractiveness; once
Forge PhilosophyKnown (25); Communication 4830 min; +1 Strength; once

Two shared scenes give more opportunities to meet him: The Fashion Intervention at Fernando’s Tailor Shop when both men are Known, and A Respectable Front at the Blacksmith Shop after Ryia joins as worker 30 and is home from vacation. He also has a reaction to a performance at the business bar.

These visits do not form a personal romance or Heart-to-Heart sequence.

Talk, equipment and commissions

Talk to Gastoff: 10 minutes, 1 base affection, up to six times per day. His preferred conditions are Strength 35, Creativity 45 and Armor level 2; Iron Will improves affection gains. Admire Armor, on the shop’s location menu, takes 5 minutes and restores 1 Calm.

For equipment purchases, sword/armor improvements and the four-piece Forge, follow the Blacksmith and Forge guide. Construction commissions cover the Courtyard, Pool, Menagerie, Training Room, Cell Corridor and Cells 2–7. They have separate reputation, resource and preceding-building requirements; the maintained guide lists exact costs.

Gastoff can also appear in the optional business client-booking system from reputation 6 in brothel mode with NTS enabled. A client booking has separate rules and does not raise his ordinary affection.

If an option is missing

  • Visit before 21:00 for his portrait and personal actions.
  • Musclecraft Exchange needs the Body Builder proficiency; Forge Philosophy needs Known and Communication 48. Both disappear after their one-time reward.
  • For a construction commission, check the Forge guide’s reputation, resources and prerequisite building. Current commissions stop at Cell 7.
  • A locked Forge or equipment upgrade has its own requirement; more Talk does not replace missing materials or skills.

先认识 Gastoff

Gastoff 是中世纪商人区的铁匠，也是玫瑰与荆棘主要设施的承建人。可以找他购买和强化装备、使用熔炉、委托建造，或完成两次属性交流，平时交谈也能慢慢熟悉起来。

去哪里找他

前往中世纪 → 国王领地 → 商人区 → 铁匠铺。Gastoff 每天06:00–20:59在店里，第一次在这个时段进店会开始《铁匠铺》。

个人交流与共同事件

行动条件消耗／次数
肌肉技艺交流已遇见（Acknowledged，10）; 健美者40 分钟；吸引力 +1；一次
锻炉哲学已知（Known，25）; 沟通 4830 分钟；力量 +1；一次

两段共同事件也可以留意：Fernando 与 Gastoff 都达到已知后，到 Fernando 的裁缝店遇见《时尚干预》；Ryia 以 30 号员工身份加入、且未度假时，在铁匠店可遇到《体面的门面》。他还会对青楼酒吧的表演作出回应。

这些相处没有另外的个人恋爱或谈心顺序。

交谈、装备与建造

和 Gastoff 交谈：10 分钟、1 点基础好感，每天六次。力量 35、创造力 45、护甲等级 2，以及钢铁意志技能都有助于好感收益。店铺地点菜单中的「欣赏护甲」消耗 5 分钟，恢复 1 镇定。

装备购买、剑与护甲强化，以及四件熔炉护甲，请查阅铁匠与熔炉指南。建造项目包括庭院、水池、珍兽苑、训练室、牢房走廊与2–7 号牢房；声誉、材料和前置建筑各有要求，准确费用以系统指南为准。

青楼声誉达到 6、以青楼模式经营且开启 NTS（寝取）内容时，他也可能出现在可选的接待客户系统中。客户接待使用独立规则，不增加 Gastoff 的普通好感。

找不到选项时

  • 在 21:00 前到店里，才能见到头像与个人行动。
  • 肌肉技艺交流需要 健美者开局专长；锻炉哲学需要已知与沟通 48。领取过的一次性奖励不会再次出现。
  • 建造委托请核对声誉、各类资源与前置建筑；当前可委托的牢房到 7 号为止。
  • 熔炉或装备升级另有条件，缺少材料或技能时，继续交谈不能代替这些要求。
