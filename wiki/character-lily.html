概览

Lily 是一个银发的流浪者,他成为常客, 中世纪酒吧协会 之后 6次成功发现她目前的路线包括: 7个自动故事场景,3个插图"心对心",2个日期,每日酒和一首歌曲的表演进步.
这个指南在将她的个人历史,歌曲歌词和后来的情感场景留给游戏的同时,解释了解锁和柜台.

Overview

Lily is a silver-haired wandering bard who becomes a regular at the Medieval Bards Guild after six successful discovery encounters. Her current route includes seven automatic story scenes, three illustrated Heart-to-Hearts, two dates, daily Wine and a song-performance progression.
This guide explains the unlocks and counters while leaving her personal history, song lyrics and later emotional scenes to the game.

Unlocking Lily at the Guild

You need six successful discovery encounters, rather than six sightings. Both routes below advance the same saved counter.
Discovery route What counts Other result
Dungeon wandering bard — Listen to her play Always adds one discovery success in live gameplay Fully restores Health, Energy and Calm to their current maximums
Dungeon wandering bard — Play music with her Adds one only on a successful music roll +1 Bards Guild clout and 30 Lily NPC intimacy experience, missionary classification
Medieval Back Alleys — Play music with her Adds one only on a successful music roll; needs at least one composed song +1 Bards Guild clout
Dungeon listening is the reliable route once you encounter her: it does not require a composed song or a successful roll. Dungeon music success compares 10 times your composed-song count against a 1–120 roll: one song gives about 8.3%, six give 50%, and twelve or more guarantee the music check.
For the Back Alleys, use Explore Back Alleys until the random bard encounter appears. Its music chance is 10% per composed song, capped at 100%. A successful duet advances discovery. Walking away, failed music, pickpocketing and the illusion option do not advance her discovery counter.
These are encounter chances, not guarantees that each dungeon step or alley search finds Lily. EXTRAS replays do not increase discovery or award gameplay rewards.
After the sixth success, she leaves the random wandering-bard encounter pools and becomes available at the Bards Guild, 1 PM–midnight. Visit for The Echo Finds a Room. You do not need a particular relationship stage to trigger that introduction.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Complete six successful discoveries, then visit the Bards Guild, 1 PM–midnight, for The Echo Finds a Room. Caring or Funny gives 20 base affection total.
  2. Reach Acknowledged and 10 Bards Guild clout, then return after the introduction for Songs Without Endings. At Known, Wine opens.
  3. At Known, after Songs Without Endings, start A Note She Won't Resolve, 9 PM–midnight.
  4. At Friend, complete Lantern Rhythms, 5–10 PM, after the first H2H. Raise Bards Guild clout to 15 and visit the Rose & Thorn Bar, 9 AM–midnight, for A House With Two Names.
  5. Raise Guild clout to 20, then return to the Guild at Friend for The Rose and the Thorn. This unlocks the route song and records its first Guild performance.
  6. At Close Friend, after that scene and the first H2H, start After the Applause, 9 PM–midnight. Complete A Rest Between Notes, 6–10 PM, afterward.
  7. Perform The Rose and the Thorn at at least three distinct official venues, reach 25 Guild clout, and return to the Guild at Close Friend after the second date for Both Names on the Page.
  8. Reach 30 Guild clout, then return at Close Friend for Where the Echo Returns. Start it at the Guild; its scene location does not mean you must find another random dungeon encounter.
  9. Perform the route song at all four official venues and reach 40 Guild clout. At Crush, after Where the Echo Returns, visit the Rose & Thorn Bar, 9 AM–midnight, for A Place in the Refrain.
  10. At Crush, after A Place in the Refrain and After the Applause, start Without the Song, 9 PM–midnight, at the Guild.
Both dates are required for later automatic scenes in this route. High affection alone does not replace the H2H completions, clout thresholds or distinct-venue requirements.

Performing the Route Song and Building Clout

For the venue gates, choose The Rose and the Thorn in Perform Song at these four locations. Repeating it at one place does not increase the number of distinct venues.

Each ordinary Perform Song action takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy. You can perform once per game day at each venue. That venue's allowance is shared across songs; performing another song there uses the same daily allowance.
Because the Guild debut already counts, perform the route song at any two of the remaining three venues to reach the three-venue gate. Perform at the last remaining venue for the four-venue gate. A personal performance for Kuro, listening to music or performing a different song does not count toward these specific gates.
For Guild clout, an ordinary performance gives +1 clout the first time a particular song is performed at a particular venue. Repeat performances have a 10% chance of +1, or 20% with Master Performer. Completing eligible Bards Guild Quest Board tasks is another clout source. Ordinary performance payouts do not directly award Lily affection unless a separately triggered scene does so.
You can use additional composed songs to obtain more first-performance clout. The standard composing route requires Song Writing and Music Production, Creativity 50, and Compose Song at the Present Downtown Music Studio, noon–11 PM. Song Writing is learned at the Guild with Creativity 25, effectively 1–9 PM, for 220 minutes, $300, 60 Energy and 60 Calm. Music Production is learned at the Studio with Technology 25, noon–11 PM, for 180 minutes, $500, 50 Energy and 50 Calm. A committed composing attempt costs 30 minutes, $200 and 30 Energy; successfully finish it to add a song.
Lily's route song is awarded by her story handler: once you have reached that scene, performing it does not add a Song Writing or Music Production skill requirement of its own. Those skills are useful for making additional songs and for the optional pre-unlock alley duet strategy.

Shared Encounter with Ryia

The Unruly Chorus triggers at the Guild while Lily is present, with Ryia active as a Rose & Thorn worker and not on vacation. It has no separate minimum Lily affection gate and runs once.
Completion grants 8 base Lily affection and 5 Ryia affection. For the best Lily reward, choose Caring at the first choice (+3 Lily, +2 Ryia) and Funny at the second (+3 Lily, +1 Ryia), giving 14 base Lily affection and 8 Ryia affection total.
The best Ryia choices are instead Funny first (+3 Ryia) and Flirty second (+3 Ryia), for 11 Ryia affection total. All choices converge. This recollection is optional and does not replace Lily's personal route prerequisites.

Current Route and Troubleshooting

Without the Song is the end of Lily's currently implemented personal route. Her current character definition has no apartment encounter, personal intimate animation, pregnancy route, Kupie selection or Rose & Thorn worker recruitment.
  • Lily absent from the Guild: complete six successful discovery encounters. Finding her, walking away or failing a music roll does not count. Dungeon listening reliably adds one once you find the encounter.
  • Songs Without Endings missing: complete the Guild introduction, reach Acknowledged and 10 Bards Guild clout, and revisit 1 PM–midnight.
  • H2H missing during the afternoon: all three start 9 PM–midnight, with their own story and completion gates.
  • First Bar scene missing: complete Lantern Rhythms, reach Friend and 15 Guild clout, build/access the Bar, and visit 9 AM–midnight.
  • Both Names on the Page missing: complete the second date, reach Close Friend and 25 Guild clout, and perform the route song at three distinct official venues.
  • Later Bar scene missing: finish Where the Echo Returns, reach Crush and 40 Guild clout, and perform the route song at all four official venues.
  • Perform Song unavailable: check the venue's opening hours, your song list and that venue's daily allowance. The story's Guild debut uses today's Guild allowance.
  • Wine no longer helps today: give it only once per day, independently of the four daily talks.
