投资系统指南
投资规划办公室 现时代 下城 办公楼 投资规划 以被动收入购买投资水平,然后买断一个完全有资金的公司成为其所有者。 经纪人 在同一地点工作是赚钱和筹集商业的另外一种方式。
本指南涵盖了当期价格,公司收购,风险,游戏中的日常收入,以及收藏. 字符故事结果留给游戏.

开始和使用组合

  1. 打开 投资规划 并选择负担得起的企业。 下一个 投资水平。
  2. -第5级的价格是额外的购买,而不是达到第5级的总成本。
  3. 预付 a 游戏中的日子,然后重新启动投资规划,以计算新收益。
  4. 新闻 汇编 将预留收入转入你的财富。待留收入不会再投资。
  5. 5级的瓦片更改为 嘘。如果您负担得起,请选择瓷砖并确认获取。然后显示 空穴 增加额 公司:.../50.
当前的投资组合中没有销售或降级按钮。 计划花在您可以留下的投资资金上。 投资购买本身不消耗一小时或工作班次。

投资水平和公司所有权

每个企业都有 5个投资层次加上一个单独的公司所有权国家5级投资不是被收购的公司。
让 页:1 作为该企业的五种上市价格的总和:
  • 名义上的全部投资股份: 师曰.
  • 公司买断价值: 15 × S. (英语).
  • 买断现金价格 : 该值,或 85%与投资Savvy,四舍五入到最接近的美元, 一半的圆形。
  • 获得公司收入价值: S + 15 × S = 16 × S.
投资 收入计算和公司估值仍然使用 未贴现 价格。少花钱不会降低你投资的收入价值。
公司估价 增加被收购公司的未贴现买断价值. 持有量 持有权不仅仅是在贴现后实际支付的金额。
那个 拥有每家公司 所有50家公司的实际收购情况。 50/50.

咖啡店实例

咖啡店的五个价格是 50美元、75美元、110美元、165美元和250美元:
  • 第5级名义股权总额: $650.
  • 每日基地5级: $13.
  • 购出值 : $9,750.
  • 与投资Savvy买断: $8,288.
  • 购置后的每日基地: $208.
  • 收购后捐助的控股: $10,400.
那208块还用咖啡店的 低风险 推出投资组合奖金。买断大大提升了收入基础;它不会消除风险。

每日收入公式

该系统使用您当前购买水平的累计名义价格:
日基=圆半(名义股权+收购-公司买断价值)__50.
收购前,第二个期限为零,没有购买投资水平的公司贡献为零.
每过一天的比赛:
  1. 计算每个获资助的企业的日基数.
  2. 将风险乘数独立滚动 并绕过整个生意的回报。
  3. 加上生意报酬
  4. 组合总额乘以 1+ 原始商业 + 1000,然后绕半圈。
  5. 有经过核实 师级 惠益乘以 1.05 再来一次
  6. 将结果加入待收金额。
商业 50 +5% 投资组合; 商业100 +10%。这一计算使用储存的商务,而不是设备修改后的商务。中间商工资使用修改后的统计数据,并分别计算。

时间和收集

收入紧随其后 游戏中日更改在现实世界中结束游戏本身不会积累金钱。
Open Investment Planning 计算当前游戏日与最后一个计算日的差额。每个错过的一天都会收到自己的商业风险卷。在同一天重新开放无法再次产生相同的收益,COLLECT在转账后会结清待付款项。
追赶计算使用您 当前 当投资水平、所有权、商业、技能和权益运行时,它不会重建历史的日常建筑。正常的游戏会计算进入屏幕的等待日数,然后在屏幕上进行新的购买。定期重新打开投资组合,以结算您打算使用的建筑的收入。
在此计算中,待付收入不会产生利息、到期或存储上限的影响。只有你收集并花在额外水平或收购上的钱才能增加你的持有量。

规划和解决问题

  • 购买负担得起的水平,同时又不耗尽旅行、学习和其他目标所需的资金。 收入来自累积投资价值;5个小投资不会自动超过一个更大的利益。
  • 在实际可行的情况下,在大量购买之前学习投资。 其好处也适用于大得多的买断付款。
  • 市场分析特别有助于投资组合集中于中低风险。如果投资风险高,它仍然使经纪公司的报酬翻一番。

Investment System Guide
The Investment Planning office in Present Era → Downtown → Office Building → Investment Planning offers 50 businesses. Buy investment levels for passive income, then buy out a fully funded company to become its owner. The Broker job at the same location is a separate way to earn money and raise Commerce.
This guide covers the current prices, company acquisitions, risk, in-game daily earnings, and collection. Character story outcomes are left to the game.

Starting and Using the Portfolio

  1. Open Investment Planning and select an affordable business. Its tile shows the price of the next investment level.
  2. Purchase levels in order, from 1 through 5. Each purchase costs the displayed amount; Level 5's price is an additional purchase, not the total cost of reaching Level 5.
  3. Advance an in-game day, then reopen Investment Planning to calculate new earnings.
  4. Press COLLECT to transfer pending earnings into your Wealth. Leaving earnings pending does not reinvest them.
  5. At Level 5, the tile changes to BUYOUT. If you can afford it, select the tile and confirm the acquisition. It then displays ACQUIRED and increases Companies: …/50.
There is no sell or downgrade button in the current portfolio. Plan spending around money you can leave invested. Investment purchases themselves do not consume an hour or a work shift.

Investment Levels and Company Ownership

Each business has five investment levels plus a separate company ownership state. A Level 5 investment is not an acquired company.
Let S be the sum of that business's five listed level prices:
  • Nominal full investment stake: S.
  • Company buyout value: 15 × S.
  • Buyout cash price: that value, or 85% of it with Investment Savvy, rounded to the nearest dollar with halves rounded up.
  • Acquired-company income value: S + 15 × S = 16 × S.
Investment Savvy also discounts each level purchase by 15%. The income calculation and corporate valuation still use the undiscounted prices. Paying less does not reduce the income value of your investment.
Corporate valuation adds the undiscounted buyout values of acquired companies. Holdings adds both your nominal investment stakes and that corporate valuation. Neither figure is cash available for spending, and Holdings is not simply the sum you actually paid after discounts.
The Own Every Company objective checks actual acquisitions of all 50 companies. Funding all 50 investments to Level 5 does not complete it; the company counter must reach 50/50.

Coffee Shop Example

Coffee Shop's five prices are $50, $75, $110, $165, and $250:
  • Total nominal stake at Level 5: $650.
  • Daily base at Level 5: $13.
  • Buyout value: $9,750.
  • Buyout with Investment Savvy: $8,288.
  • Daily base after acquisition: $208.
  • Holdings contributed after acquisition: $10,400.
The $208 still goes through Coffee Shop's Low risk roll and then your portfolio bonuses. A buyout greatly raises the income base; it does not remove risk.

Daily Income Formula

The system uses the cumulative nominal prices of your currently purchased levels:
Daily base = round-half-up((nominal stake + acquired-company buyout value) ÷ 50).
Before acquisition, the second term is zero. A company with no purchased investment level contributes zero.
For each elapsed in-game day:
  1. Calculate each funded business's daily base.
  2. Roll its risk multiplier independently and round that business's payout to a whole dollar.
  3. Add the business payouts together.
  4. Multiply the portfolio total by 1 + raw Commerce ÷ 1,000, then round half up.
  5. With the verified Master Tier benefit, multiply that result by 1.05 and round half up again.
  6. Add the result to the pending collection amount.
Commerce 50 gives +5% to the portfolio; Commerce 100 gives +10%. This calculation uses stored Commerce, rather than equipment-modified Commerce. Broker wages use modified stats and are calculated separately.

Market Analysis increases the lower bound by 20%, not every payout by 20%. High risk's lower bound is zero, and zero × 1.2 remains zero. The skill therefore does not improve High risk investment rolls.
Low risk has the smallest spread, but without Market Analysis its average multiplier is 0.95 rather than 1.00. Medium and High share a 1.00 average multiplier without the skill, with very different volatility. A High risk business can pay zero on a day; losses do not subtract from your stake.

Timing and Collection

Earnings follow in-game day changes. Leaving the game closed for real-world days does not accrue money by itself.
Opening Investment Planning calculates the difference between the current in-game day and the last calculated day. Each missed day receives its own business risk rolls. Reopening on the same day cannot generate the same earnings again, and COLLECT clears pending money after transferring it.
The catch-up calculation uses your current investment levels, ownership, Commerce, skills, and entitlement when it runs; it does not reconstruct historical daily builds. Normal play calculates pending days when you enter the screen, before you make new purchases there. Reopen the portfolio regularly to settle earnings at the build you intend to use.
Pending earnings have no interest-on-interest effect, expiry, or storage cap in this calculation. Only money you collect and spend on additional levels or acquisitions increases your holdings.

Useful Skills and the Broker Job

Skill Portfolio effect Other effect here
Investment Savvy 15% discount on level purchases and company buyouts; nominal income values unchanged Helps cash go further when acquiring companies
Market Analysis Improves Low and Medium risk lower bounds as shown above Doubles Broker's base wage
Investment Savvy can be learned through Portia's apartment conversations or Arestes's skill lesson. Market Analysis is taught by Jade. Their character guides give the relationship gates and lesson costs. Skills need their own stat prerequisites: Intelligence 19 for Investment Savvy and Commerce 17 for Market Analysis.
Broker appears at Investment Planning with Commerce 30+, during 09:00–16:59. Each shift takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy, and has an 8% chance to raise Commerce by 1. It is not a proficiency-specific job.
Its base wage is an inclusive random integer between Charm + Communication and Commerce + Intelligence, with the endpoints swapped if needed, then Luck is added. Market Analysis doubles this amount. These are equipment-modified stats.
With all five stats at 50, both endpoints are 100: the wage is $150, or $300 with Market Analysis, before the separate job bonuses. The current job system then applies its Easy-mode wage bonus where applicable and adds its working-experience reward. These job benefits do not modify the investment risk formula above.

Planning and Troubleshooting

  • Buy affordable levels without exhausting the money needed for travel, lessons, and other objectives. Income derives from cumulative invested value; five tiny investments do not automatically outperform one larger stake.
  • Learn Investment Savvy before substantial purchases when practical. Its benefit also applies to the much larger buyout payments.
  • Market Analysis particularly helps a portfolio concentrated in Low and Medium risk. It still doubles Broker pay if your investments are High risk.
  • Compare a buyout's additional price and additional daily base. The nominal ratio is approximately 50 in-game days of base income, before risk, skill discounts, Commerce, Master Tier, and rounding; this is not a guaranteed payback date.
