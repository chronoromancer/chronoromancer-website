概览

Diedra 是个狂野的吸血鬼猫女 现时代吸血鬼休息室。她的当前内容包括一个介绍性场景,可重复的对话和通过Torea和Helsa的路线到达的两个共享场景,她在本游戏中没有独立的H2H或日期链.

到达吸血鬼休息室

有两条路通往 吸血鬼巢穴:
  1. 打开 中世纪地牢楼层 23进入吸血鬼城堡 瓷砖(10,5)。周围的血缘地形会消耗能量,并可能导致战斗,所以在穿越之前做好准备。这个入口不需要Story Beat 61.
  2. 在主要故事中,完整 Story 击60,然后访问 现图书馆,下午8时至10时,用于 Story 击球61。跳过61后,跟随 图书馆地下室 黑暗走廊 吸血鬼巢穴更早进入地下室不会打开更深的走廊。

Diedra — Complete Character Guide

Overview

Diedra is a feral vampire catgirl in the Present Era Vampire Lounge. Her current content consists of an introductory scene, repeatable conversation and two shared scenes reached through Torea and Helsa's routes. She has no standalone H2H or date chain in the current game.

Reaching the Vampire Lounge

There are two routes to the Vampire Lair:
  1. On Medieval dungeon floor 23, enter the vampire castle at tile (10, 5). The surrounding blood terrain drains energy and can lead to battles, so prepare before crossing. This entrance does not require Story Beat 61.
  2. In the main story, complete Story Beat 60, then visit the Present Library, 8–10 PM, for Story Beat 61. After Beat 61, follow Library Basement → Dark Corridor → Vampire Lair. Entering the basement earlier does not open the deeper corridor.
From the Lair, select Vampire Lounge, a zero-minute travel action. Diedra is available there at any hour, with no additional Diedra-affection, Vampire-value or skill requirement in her schedule.

Introduction and Daily Conversation

On your first eligible Lounge visit, her automatic Diedra introduction grants 5 base affection. Its best response is Caring or Flirty +3; Funny gives +2. The introduction completes once and has no extra hour gate.
Use Talk to Diedra from Stranger to build affection. Each positive use takes 20 minutes, awards 1 base affection, and shares a four-per-day allowance. No money, gift or energy payment is configured for Talk.
Her ambient responses can draw from relationship, hunger and clan-dialogue pools. Having a positive Vampire value adds a further possible dialogue pool; this flavor variation does not create another quest or personal action.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

If Progress Stalls

  • To find Diedra, use Vampire Lounge from the Vampire Lair. Her ordinary portrait has no evening-only schedule.
  • If the Library Basement's Dark Corridor is unavailable, complete Story Beat 61. The dungeon castle provides the separate route described above.
  • For Loose the Cat, complete both characters' required H2Hs, Torea's Fair Date and Helsa's The Elf Behind the Hat, with both at Friend. Visit Torea's Room, 7–10 PM.
  • For Teeth With Ribbons, finish Loose the Cat, then visit the Lounge, 6–10 PM. Raising Diedra's affection cannot replace that scene flag.
  • Additional Diedra affection does not open a missing personal date, gift, H2H or apartment button in the current implementation.
