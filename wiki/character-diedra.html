Overview

Diedra is found in the Vampire Lounge. Her current direct interaction is a repeatable Talk action; she does not offer a skill lesson or personal dating chain.

Where to find Diedra

After the passage opens through Story 61, take Present → Uptown → City Library → Library Basement → Dark Corridor → Vampire Lair → Vampire Lounge. These interior travel actions take no time. The lounge is available all day once reached.

Vampire Loungeall day

Windows refer to the time you start an action; the ending time is excluded.

Route and relationship milestones

Her introduction occurs at the lounge on the first eligible visit. Continue with ordinary conversation if you want to improve the relationship; there is no separate item-delivery or recruitment step on her action list.

Activities and rewards

Talk to Diedra takes 20 minutes, starts from Stranger and has 4 safe uses per day, with+1 base relationship before modifiers. Vampirism is a skill she favors in relationship calculations; it is not a skill she teaches here.

Missing actions and useful checks

If the Dark Corridor route is missing, advance the main-story access milestone rather than raising Diedra’s affection. Once inside, changing lounge visits does not provide a different daily Talk allowance. No additional date or training button is expected at higher affection in the current action list.

概览

Diedra在吸血鬼休息室出现，当前直接互动主要是可重复交谈，没有提供技能课程或个人约会链。

出现地点与时间

主线61完成并开放通道后，走现代 → 上城区 → 城市图书馆 → 图书馆地下室 → 黑暗走廊 → 吸血鬼巢穴 → 吸血鬼休息室。室内这些移动不耗时，抵达后可全天见到她。

地点头像出现时间条件
吸血鬼休息室全天

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

首次符合条件地进入休息室时，会触发初次见面。之后可用普通交谈培养关系，动作列表没有额外送任务物品或招募步骤。

行动与奖励

「与Diedra交谈」从陌生人起可用，耗20分钟，每天安全额度4次，未计修正时基础关系+1。吸血鬼化是她在关系计算中偏好的技能，不代表能在这里向她学习。

动作缺失时的检查

找不到黑暗走廊入口时，应推进主线的通道开放条件，而不是刷Diedra关系。进入后反复离开再回来，也不会另给一份交谈额度。当前动作列表不会因高关系就新增约会或训练按钮。
