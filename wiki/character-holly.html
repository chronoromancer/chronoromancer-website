Overview

Holly’s route combines eight affection milestones and five cumulative Christmas-task milestones. Her 25-task list spans the three eras; those tasks and ordinary affection are separate progress tracks.

Where to find them

Travel Future → Fringe Zone → Weather Control on the map, which opens Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station; continue to Christmas Village → Winter Wonderland. Village travel costs 0 min and works year-round. Holly is present all day; no redemption code or calendar holiday is needed.

Route and relationship milestones

Talk and gifts raise the relationship through Known 25, Acquaintance 50, Friend 100, Close Friend 200, Crush 400, Romantic 800, Intimate 1600 and In Love 3000. Visit her at each threshold for its one-time scene.

The task milestone scenes require 5/10/15/20/25 distinct completed Christmas tasks. Their completion affection is 5/10/15/20/25 base points respectively. The first four give 1 Token each; the 25-task scene gives 2. Affection milestones also give 1 Token each except the In Love scene, which gives 2.

With Christmas activities enabled, Stargaze with Holly needs Friend 100 and a 20:00–04:00 start:15 min, once,1 Token and 10 direct affection. It is task 3, separate from the automatic milestones.

Enable Christmas activities for the scattered Candy Canes and holiday tasks; ordinary village travel and resident interactions remain available when the activity switch is off. The shared Christmas guide lists all 25 tasks and their exact items, hours and route prerequisites.

Favorite stats are Perception, Stamina, Mystic. Learning Christmas Spirit improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • A Hot Chocolate gift requires the Christmas prize stock, not an ordinary Chocolate item.
  • High affection does not substitute for completing distinct tasks.
  • The Christmas icon-display setting and activity switch are different; use the activity switch for tasks.

概览

Holly的路线包含八段好感里程碑与五段累计圣诞任务里程碑。25项任务分布于三个时代，任务完成数与普通好感分开计算。

去哪里找

从未来边缘区地图选择天气控制，进入天空级大气管理站，再前往圣诞村→冬日仙境。村内移动为0分钟，全年可用，不要求兑换码或现实节日；Holly全天在场。

通过交谈和送礼依次达到已知25、相识50、朋友100、亲密朋友200、迷恋400、浪漫感800、亲密1600、恋爱中3000，再到访领取各阶段的一次性事件。

送热巧克力消耗圣诞奖品库存中的热巧克力×1，20分钟、10基础好感，要求已知25，每天最多80次。普通巧克力是另一种消耗品：相识50、20分钟、3基础好感，每天七次。

开启圣诞活动后才可拾取散落拐杖糖并进行节日任务；关闭活动开关仍能移动和进行居民普通互动。25项任务的物品、时间与路线前置见圣诞村指南。

偏好的属性为感知、耐力、神秘。学会圣诞精神可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 热巧克力礼物需要圣诞奖品库存，普通巧克力不能替代。
  • 好感再高也不能代替不同任务的完成数。
  • 圣诞图标显示选项与活动开关不同，进行任务请使用活动开关。
