概览

Grace 这是修道院的新修道院,其个人路线是从Scriptorium到Sashina的Grove和在Rose& Thorn的招聘。她目前的内容包括三个个人心脏对心脏、一个自然行走、五个修道院/招聘碰面、一个较晚的场景Jade、八个工人培训选项、两个精心设计的升级、度假活动以及Kupie奖励。

Grace — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Grace is the monastery novice whose personal route leads from the Scriptorium to Sashina's Grove and recruitment at the Rose & Thorn. Her current content includes three personal heart-to-hearts, a Nature Walk, five monastery/recruitment encounters, a later scene with Jade, eight worker-training options, two crafted upgrades, resort activities and Kupie rewards.

Building Affection Before Recruitment

  1. Meet her at the Scriptorium Lobby during one of its two portrait windows. Talk to Grace takes 15 minutes and awards 1 actual base point, despite its zero-point action metadata, up to five uses per game day.
  2. At Known, Give Flowers consumes one Flowers for 15 minutes and 5 actual base points, up to twice per day. Flowers also advertises zero, but its handler uses five.
  3. At Known and Communication 40, use Spiritual Awareness at the Lobby: 30 minutes, direct +8 affection and Perception +1, once. The actual action uses the shared one-time reward path.
  4. At Acknowledged, take Life Beyond the Walls from the Lobby. At Acquaintance, take the Nature Walk Date there if desired. Complete these before recruitment, while her Lobby portrait is still available.
  5. Raise Grace to Friend and the Rose & Thorn to reputation 5, then follow the recruitment scenes below.
Ordinary Talks, Reflect with Grace at the Grove, and her brothel Talk with Grace all use the same five-per-day Talk allowance. Flowers uses its own shared two-per-day counter. Beyond either allowance, its handler records −1 base points with the shared over-limit handling. At the brothel portraits, her personal menu currently offers Talk; Flowers remains available at the Grove.

Nature Walk Date

At Acquaintance, choose Nature Walk Date from her Scriptorium Lobby portrait. Its advertised start window is 8 AM–2 PM, but her actual portrait is present only 8–10 AM and 11 AM–2 PM within that window. It costs 120 minutes and 40 energy, with no money payment, and completes once.
Nature Walk with Grace gives 5 base points. Best responses are:

Troubleshooting

* No Grace at the monastery: worker 5 activation removes her monastery portraits. Look at the Grove or the brothel schedule instead.
* Flowers or Talk gives less than expected: their daily allowances are shared across ordinary locations; moving rooms does not reset them.
* The final recruitment button is missing: finish Whispers Among the Dead, reach 500 affection, and visit the Grove 8 PM–midnight on an even-numbered day.
* The Sashina preparation scene did not recruit her: recruitment starts through Where My Light Belongs, after the Catacombs step.
* Nature Walk is missing: use her Lobby portrait 8–10 AM or 11 AM–2 PM before recruitment, at least Acquaintance, and check its one-time completion state.
* Grace's New Routine is missing: recruit Jade, keep both home from vacation, and enter Worker Rooms 2–10 PM.
* An upgrade is missing: check its prior plot, In Love, crafted item and actual portrait window. The Garden gift also needs Audrey active.
* An older save uses the retired recruitment-seen flag: the current load migration reconciles it with A Different Kind of Light completed. It does not turn that preparation plot into the worker-activation step.
