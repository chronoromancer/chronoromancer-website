The Blessed Ones

Overview

The Blessed Ones are the group of temple worshippers found at the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple in the Future era. They appear as a single group portrait, with varied appearances and temple robes. Their current role is environmental dialogue and a shared encounter in Pixel's route. Their origins and the larger story behind the temple are left for you to discover.

The existing wiki page address uses Karens. The current in-game name of this temple group is The Blessed Ones.

How to Meet Them

  1. Travel to the Future era's Prime Sector.
  2. Select Cult Temple on the district map. The location opens as Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple.
  3. View the group portrait and their arrival dialogue. Their schedule covers midnight to midnight, every day, with no additional required or blocked flags on the group itself.
The location is registered as unlocked and visible and has no opening-hour restriction. Their greeting always uses the Stranger dialogue group. The changing portraits represent members of the group; they do not mark a friendship stage or a collectible challenge.

Current Interaction Limits

The character's temple action list and action handlers are empty. There is currently no selectable Talk, gift, lesson, Heart-to-Heart, date, apartment visit, Kupie training, worker recruitment or resort invitation for this group. The temple's ordinary location action list is also empty.
Their data lists Mystic, Charm and Intelligence 70 as favorite-stat values, but there is no implemented ordinary affection action for them. Raising those stats does not create a conversation menu or a new relationship route. No special relationship skill or gift is configured.
This entry therefore documents their current appearance and shared encounter rather than asking you to grind an unavailable personal walkthrough.

Shared Temple Encounter with Pixel

Pixel has a one-time automatic scene here, Not Divine Enough. Its requirements are separate from the temple group's all-day presence:
Requirement or result Detail
Location Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple
Time 9 AM–6 PM
Pixel relationship Close Friend or higher
Previous scene Pixel's Missing Hour completed
Repeatability Once
Base reward 7 Pixel points
Best reply Caring, adding 3 for 10 total Pixel base points
Other replies Flirty: +2, total 9; Funny: +1, total 8
This scene awards Pixel affection, with ordinary modifiers. It does not establish a Blessed Ones relationship route. See Pixel's guide for her earlier prerequisites and later steps. The outcome and the temple's wider significance are omitted here to avoid revealing the story.

Troubleshooting

  • No Talk button: the group currently has no ordinary actions; this is how its character definition is authored.
  • Portrait changes without friendship progress: the group draws from multiple portraits while keeping Stranger greetings.
  • Pixel's scene will not start: check Close Friend, Missing Hour completed, the exact temple location and 9 AM–6 PM. The group's all-day schedule does not remove Pixel's scene window.
  • Searching the wiki: use this existing Character:Karens page for the temple group named The Blessed Ones in game.
