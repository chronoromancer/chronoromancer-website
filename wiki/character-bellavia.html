Bellavia — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Bellavia is a vampire and Rose & Thorn worker who makes her home in the cellar beneath the infirmary. Her current route includes a main-story recruitment gate, personal conversation, Cellar Feeding, shared worker training, two crafted-gift upgrades and resort activities. Her origin and later story developments are left undisclosed here.

Meeting Bellavia and Opening the Cellar

  1. Continue the main story through Beat 58.
  2. Visit the Rose & Thorn's Nurse's Room for Story Beat 59. There is no additional configured hour or affection restriction on that story trigger.
  3. Complete the scene. It recruits worker 10, unlocks the cellar, advances the story and grants 10 base affection to Bellavia plus choices. For her rewarded choice, Caring or Flirty gives 5, compared with Funny's 4. The completed scene also records 30 NPC intimacy experience and advances time by 90 minutes.
  4. In the Nurse's Room, use Down to the Cellar. This travel action costs no time. Back to Nurse's Room returns you upstairs.
Her personal presence in the unlocked cellar is 24 hours, separate from the worker-menu shift of 9 PM–4 AM. Sending her to the resort replaces her home presence during the vacation.

Personal Relationship Walkthrough

  • Talk to Bellavia is available from Stranger: 20 minutes, 1 base affection, three times per day. The actual handler awards 1, despite a higher configured display value.
  • Keep Bellavia Company is the separate shared worker encouragement: 20 minutes, 1 base affection and loyalty +1 up to 100, once per day while she is present at home. Its allowance is separate from Talk and worker visits.
  • Build to Crush for Cellar Feeding, a paid-version video encounter taking 60 minutes and 80 energy, once per day. It gives 30 base affection, 70 player Erotic Experience and 25 NPC intimacy experience.
  • Use worker-menu Book gifts and training for additional growth. The worker's relationship meter and Bellavia's NPC affection are separate.
  • At In Love, carry the crafted gifts below to unlock her two one-time training upgrades in the cellar.
  • Send her on vacation to meet her at the resort for repeatable activities and Sunset Walk.
Her current cellar menu has no separate ordinary gift button, skill lesson, apartment visit or home H2H. Sunset Walk is a resort heart-to-heart. Seduction is her relationship bonus skill; her worker starts with Sucking.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

Use the brothel's worker visit menu with worker 10. Her initial shift is 9 PM–4 AM; later scheduling changes can change that window. She must be active, not resting, not at the resort and not already visited that day.
A visit normally costs 60 minutes, 60 energy and 40 calm. The Time, Power and Focus buffs halve their corresponding cost to 30 minutes, 30 energy and 20 calm. Within the visit, each category—Talk, Tip, Gift and Training—has its own once-per-session allowance. Training adds no separate entry charge.
Training begins at level 1 and advances one level per completed session, up to 8. Earlier options remain available. The menu only offers a type when its corresponding images exist.

Later Main-Story Requirements

Beat 63 starts at the Brothel Demesne, 7 PM–10 PM, after Beat 62. Its completion grants Bellavia 10 base points, and Flirty gives her 5 base choice points.
Beat 80 starts at the Brothel Demesne after Beat 79. It requires all twelve workers Elara, Sophie, Sandra, Raven, Bellavia, Valkyra, Lindia, Ava and Eva, Gem, Pixie, Willow and Ember active and home rather than on vacation. There is no additional hour restriction. At the volunteer-response choice, Flirty gives Bellavia 2 base points. This guide gives the gates without describing the scene's developments.

Clothing

Bellavia participates in Fernando's clothing fittings while active. The tailor selects randomly from eligible workers; buying a fitting does not guarantee she is selected. Ordinary fittings raise clothing by one level, with up to two Fernando upgrades per worker while below level 5. A separate MAX fitting applies at level 5. Gold and fabric costs follow the current quote, which rises across the roster.


If Progress Stalls

  • Finish Beat 59 in the Nurse's Room to recruit her and open the cellar. The travel action is Down to the Cellar in that room.
  • Her personal cellar menu is available 24 hours, but the worker visit uses her assigned shift, initially 9 PM–4 AM.
  • Cellar Feeding needs Crush, the paid version and 80 energy, and has its own once-per-day limit.
  • The shared worker Book preference does not create a separate gift button in her cellar menu.
  • Both crafted-gift upgrades need In Love, Beat 59 and the correct gift, with Bellavia home. Neither needs a resort H2H.
  • Sunset Walk needs her active vacation presence at the resort beach, 5 PM–8 PM, and is separate from the Shade Treaty automatic scene.
  • Shared resort scenes require all named participants vacationing together; otherwise their automatic trigger cannot fire.
  • For Beat 80, check all twelve active workers and bring vacationing participants home.
