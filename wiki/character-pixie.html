概览

Pixie 好玩吗? 二十岁的仙女 绿色的翅膀,她的招募始于 八次有秩序的地牢会合,然后是单独的 征聘深度然后她变成了 劳动者 16 现场 玫瑰色( T)她自己的房间 与主卧室相邻
她目前的家用路线包括: 7次自动碰面, 3个人H2H, 两个日期页:1 培训室座谈会每日谈话和赠与、工人访问和八种培训选择, 3个设计升级页:1 心跳承诺 报酬和归宿, 十三次共同会面.
本页解释可玩的步骤和奖励,同时将地牢场景的结果和后来的主场启示留给游戏. 探索她的个人路线和可选活动,在它们可用时;依赖故事的可用性仍然适用.

Overview

Pixie is the playful 20-year-old fairy with luminous green wings. Her recruitment starts with eight ordered dungeon encounters, followed by a separate Epic Depths recruitment encounter. She then becomes worker 16 at the Rose & Thorn, with her own room adjoining the Master Bedroom.
Her current home route includes seven automatic encounters, three personal H2Hs, two dates, a Training Room encounter, daily Talk and gifts, worker visits and eight training options, three crafted upgrades, a Heartbeat Promise reward, and resort activities including thirteen shared encounters.
This page explains the playable steps and rewards while leaving the dungeon scenes' outcomes and later main-story revelations to the game. Explore her personal route and optional activities while they are available; story-dependent availability still applies.

The progression system makes a 25% priority roll when an eligible encounter check runs. That is not a guaranteed Pixie scene on one quarter of all movement steps; the general encounter selection, terrain and other higher-priority encounters still matter. The ordinary scenario pool also has the sequential stage requirements.
For stages 1–3, the ordinary stat replies roll 1–100 against the named stat. A failed stat reply spends 30 minutes and does not advance the encounter counter. Stage 7 is a boss encounter. Its Greater Demon is weak to Light, has 30 magic resistance, and absorbs 70% Fire / 50% Dark damage. Prepare suitable combat resources before seeking it. The guide deliberately does not describe what happens during or after that fight.

Do not read this as +1 to every other stat: Communication, Rogue and Beast are not in that reward list.

Epic Depths Recruitment and Her Room

After completing all eight earlier encounters, explore Epic Depths, floors 46–50, for Pixie's separate recruitment encounter. It is selected from the ordinary weighted Epic scenario pool, rather than receiving the earlier 25% progressive-stage roll.
Its eligibility requires the eight-stage counter complete, its own encounter not yet resolved, and worker 16 not already active. The invitation replies all converge on recruitment; no additional stat-test answer, gifted item or cash cost is needed for that invitation.
Completion activates worker 16, opens her room at level 1, and records 5 Pixie NPC kissing XP. Owning and accessing the Rose & Thorn lets you use that room and its worker systems. The initial eight encounters do not by themselves activate worker 16.
From Master Bedroom, select Enter Pixie's Room, a zero-minute travel action. Her room portrait is available all hours while she is home. Assigned worker hours, initially 7 AM–11 PM, are separate from the personal portrait.
The next room encounter, One Door Away, records a dating status and another 5 NPC kissing XP, in addition to its ordinary affection. That status is a distinct relationship-history value; it does not automatically mean the ordinary affection stage is Friend.

Recommended Home Route

  1. Enter Pixie's Room for One Door Away. Its best reward is 103 base affection. Use daily Talk and, at Known, Wine and Chocolate to keep raising ordinary affection.
  2. At Friend, complete Fairy Terms, then A Thousand Flowers Shouting, both in her room at any hour.
  3. Complete After the Heroics, 6–11 PM, then return 6 PM–midnight for Mortal Giggle Potion. Her first picnic date then opens 2–4 PM.
  4. At Close Friend, enter her room 6 AM–midnight for Not Waiting by the Door, then complete A Door Is Not a Chain, 8 AM–5 PM.
  5. At Crush, enter 6 AM–midnight for A Window Both Ways. Complete A Glade Between Worlds, 2–4 PM, and No More Vanishing, 6–11 PM.
  6. At Romantic, with the glade date and No More Vanishing complete, enter 6 AM–midnight for Chosen Trouble.
  7. At In Love, 3,200 ordinary affection, finish Heartbeat Promise once and obtain the three crafted training upgrades as their separate prerequisites allow.
  8. Use worker visits and training, the independent Training Room scene, and a Sirena Isle vacation for the remaining personal content.
The first picnic date is optional for the later automatic chain. A Glade Between Worlds is required for Chosen Trouble. The two dates therefore have different roles in progression.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Early dungeon stage repeats: finish its progressing reply. Failed stat rolls at encounters 1–3 and refusal at encounter 4 leave the same stage unfinished.
  • Worker 16 is not recruited after encounter eight: continue to the separate Epic Depths encounter, floors 46–50; finishing the eight-stage counter is not the worker activation.
  • Room entrance missing: check worker 16's recruitment and current availability, then use Master Bedroom → Enter Pixie's Room.
  • Next home scene missing: check the previous scene/H2H, ordinary affection stage, vacation state and that scene's own hours.
  • Chosen Trouble missing: finish A Window Both Ways, A Glade Between Worlds and No More Vanishing, then reach Romantic and enter 6 AM–midnight.
  • Date missing: both start 2–4 PM, with different personal-scene/H2H prerequisites. Their entry costs do not include inventory Wine or Chocolate.
  • Promise missing: check In Love, Chosen Trouble, home room presence and the one-time completion. It is a live action, not an EXTRAS reward.
  • Crafted upgrade missing: carry the exact gift, meet its own listed scene/H2H requirements and reach In Love. Worker training level alone does not unlock the gift action.
  • Personal portrait absent during vacation: use resort presence for vacation content, then return her home for room actions and upgrades. The broader main story can also determine live availability; its plot outcomes are intentionally omitted here.
Her current route does not add a separate ordinary apartment invitation, pregnancy continuation, Kupie album, venue-invite minigame, ordinary room intimacy movie or personal Christmas scene. The dungeon encounters, home route, earned combat promise, worker training and resort activities above cover the authored optional progression.
