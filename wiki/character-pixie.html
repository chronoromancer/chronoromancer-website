Pixie’s dungeon and home route

Pixie’s route starts with eight ordered dungeon encounters. A separate Epic Depths encounter then recruits her to the Rose & Thorn and opens her room. At home, follow seven visits, three Heart-to-Hearts and two afternoon dates, with worker training and island activities alongside them.

From the dungeon to her room

Find the early encounters in the ordinary dungeon, following the minimum floors below. Once all eight are complete, explore Epic Depths, floors 46–50, for her separate recruitment encounter. It is a random weighted scenario, so it may take more than one exploration attempt. The invitation has no extra stat-test, item or cash cost.

Recruitment activates worker 16 and opens her room at level 1. With access to the establishment, go to Master Bedroom → Enter Pixie’s Room; the travel action costs no time. Her room portrait is normally available all hours while she is home. The assigned worker shift, initially 07:00–22:59, is separate; a vacation puts her on Sirena Isle.

These are minimum floors, not exact-floor requirements. Only the next unfinished encounter can advance. The early selection includes a 25% priority roll on eligible encounter checks, not on every movement step. A failed stat reply in encounters 1–3 costs 30 minutes without advancing; retry or use the learned-skill reply. Raising ordinary affection does not skip a dungeon encounter.

Encounter 7 contains the Greater Demon fight: it is weak to Light, has 30 magic resistance, and absorbs 70% Fire and 50% Dark damage. Encounter 8 awards +1 each Strength, Intelligence, Charm, Stamina, Ability, Creativity, Perception, Mystic, Technology, Luck, Cooking and Commerce, plus 100 player Erotic XP,30 Pixie intimacy XP and five Dragon Coins. These rewards do not recruit her by themselves.

Talk, Wine, Chocolate and Head Pat have separate daily counters. Five talks plus two of each gift take 115 minutes and give 37 base affection before bonuses. Preferred stats are Mystic 70, Luck 65 and Charm 60; learned Mystic Seduction improves gains. Her worker-session favorites are Wine and Chocolate, and she dislikes Perfume.

At In Love (3000), after Chosen Trouble, choose Heartbeat Promise from her room portrait. It takes 45 minutes, completes once, and has no extra Energy, Calm, Wealth or item cost. It teaches Pixie’s Promise: while active, it automatically restores you to maximum Health after one otherwise-fatal ordinary-combat hit per game day. It does not restore Energy or Calm; the daily use is shared across battles and runs before the Helmet of Immortality or Phoenix Feathers.

At Friend (100), after One Door Away, enter the Training Room,07:00–22:59, with worker 16 active and home for a separate one-time encounter. This is independent of the regular worker visit and the crafted upgrades.

For Visit Worker, keep worker 16 active, healthy, home and not resting, and start during her assigned shift. Base entry is 60 minutes,60 Energy and 40 Calm, once daily. Eight training options unlock in sequence; her Fast Learner worker skill is separate from ordinary affection bonuses. Use the Brothel guides for training rewards, stress, clothing and room upgrades.

The following three upgrades start from her home room portrait, at In Love (3000), with worker 16 active and home. Each costs 0 additional minutes, consumes one exact crafted gift and completes once. Each doubles its matching training’s worker experience and adds 50% to her income multiplier, for 2.5× with all three.

Checks that help without spoiling scenes

  • An early encounter repeats: your selected reply may not have advanced it. Recheck the skill or stat route for that stage.
  • Eight encounters completed but no room: seek the separate Epic Depths encounter on floors 46–50. It is not a guaranteed early priority roll.
  • A home visit is missing: check the earlier room visit and required H 2 H, then its time window and vacation status.
  • Chosen Trouble needs the glade date and No More Vanishing; the picnic does not substitute.
  • Heartbeat Promise needs In Love plus Chosen Trouble and a live room visit; a replay does not award the skill.
  • Check current story objectives and Communications when normal availability changes.
  • The assigned worker shift and worker visit allowance are separate from the all-day home portrait and gift counters.

Pixie 的地下城与家中路线

Pixie 的路线先从八次按顺序出现的地下城遭遇开始。之后还有一次独立的史诗深渊遭遇，完成才会招募她到玫瑰与荆棘并开放房间。家中有七次剧情拜访、三次谈心和两场午后约会，也可以进行员工训练和海岛活动。

从地下城走到她的房间

先在普通地下城按下表最低楼层寻找早期遭遇。八次全完成后，到史诗深渊 46–50 层探索独立的招募遭遇；它属于随机抽取，可能需要多次探索。邀请不另要求属性检定、物品或费用。

招募会激活员工 16，开放 1 级房间。能进入店铺后，从主卧 → 进入 Pixie 的房间前往，不消耗移动时间。她在家时，房间头像通常全天可用；员工工作班次另算，初始为07:00–22:59。度假期间则到塞壬岛找她。

表中是最低楼层，不必停留在某一层。每次只能推进尚未完成的下一次遭遇；符合条件的遭遇检查有25% 优先抽取机会，不代表每走一步都有 25% 概率。第 1–3 次属性选项失败会花掉 30 分钟而不推进，可重试或改用已学技能选项；普通好感再高也不能跳过遭遇。

第 7 次含大恶魔首领战：弱点为光属性，魔法抗性 30，并吸收 70% 火伤与 50% 暗伤。第 8 次奖励力量、智力、魅力、耐力、能力、创造力、感知、神秘、科技、幸运、烹饪、商业各 +1，另有 100 玩家情趣经验、30 Pixie 亲密经验及五枚龙币；这些奖励本身不会招募她。

交谈、葡萄酒、巧克力与摸头各自计数。五次交谈、两次葡萄酒、两次巧克力合计 115 分钟，提供加成前的 37 点基础好感。她偏好的属性是神秘 70、幸运 65、魅力 60，学会神秘诱惑也能提高收益。员工拜访中的最爱礼物是葡萄酒与巧克力，不喜欢香水。

达到恋爱中（In Love，3000）并完成「自选的麻烦」后，从房间头像选择「心跳之约」。消耗45 分钟，仅一次，没有额外精力、镇定、财富或物品消耗。会学到「Pixie 的承诺」：生效时，普通战斗中每天第一次本应致命的伤害会自动使你恢复到生命上限。不恢复精力或镇定；每天的次数在各场战斗间共用，并优先于不朽头盔或凤凰羽毛处理。

达到朋友（Friend，100）、完成「一门之隔」后，在员工 16 在职且在家时，于07:00–22:59进入训练室，还有一次独立剧情。它与普通员工拜访、制作礼物升级分别计算。

「拜访员工」需要员工 16 在职、健康、在家且未休息，并在分配的班次内开始。基础消耗60 分钟、60 精力、40 镇定，每天一次，八项训练依序开放。她的快速学习是员工技能，与普通好感加成分别计算。训练收益、压力、服装及房间升级见青楼系统指南。

下列三项升级都从家中房间头像发起，需要恋爱中（In Love，3000）、员工 16 在职且在家。每项只完成一次，不另花时间，消耗一个指定制作礼物；使对应训练的员工经验翻倍，收入倍率各增加 50%，三项合计为 2.5 倍。

卡住时的实用检查

  • 早期遭遇重复出现：上次所选答复可能没有推进，检查这一阶段的属性或技能选项。
  • 八次已完成却没有房间：还要在史诗深渊 46–50 层找到独立招募遭遇，它不保证使用早期的优先抽取。
  • 家中剧情不出现：核对前一次房间剧情、必需谈心、时段及度假状态。
  • 「自选的麻烦」要求林间空地约会与「不再凭空消失」，野餐不能代替。
  • 「心跳之约」需要恋爱中、「自选的麻烦」及实际房间拜访；重播不会发放技能。
  • 正常出现情况变化时，检查当前剧情目标与通信提示。
  • 工作班次与员工拜访次数独立于全天房间头像、送礼次数。
