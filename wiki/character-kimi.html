概览

Kimi 是个滑冰运动员 现为市中心.在市中心早期的救援中遇见她,然后通过日间练习,俱乐部花样滑冰,两次心对心,两次一次性约会和可重复的会场邀请,跟踪她的友情.本指南在将个人场景留给游戏时给出了当前的要求和有益的选择.

解决问题

  • Kimi的肖像不见了: 结束 Story覆盖 确认,并检查位置的具体时间表。
  • 没有俱乐部肖像 : 她的夜晚开始 2,5,8天... 晚上9点,持续到 第二天凌晨2点.
  • 第一次点名失踪: 返回时 下午10点,它是一个每天可以使用的自动场景,而不是肖像动作.
  • 日光轮丢失: 到达 知识,结束 第一次点名访问 下城公园,下午2时-6时.
  • 在Alley失踪之后: 到达 朋友们 结束 日光轮然后打开她的公园肖像。
  • 缺少地板灯: 到达 关闭朋友,补全 {\fn黑体\fs22\bord1\shad0\3aHBE\4aH00\fscx67\fscy66\2cHFFFFFF\3cH808080}在阿莱之后,然后访问 夜总会,晚上10点.
  • 午夜后失去地板: 它的起始窗口是 午夜9点尽管Kimi的肖像画在凌晨2点之前仍然可用.
  • 板行日期锁定 : 检查 密友,构建,日光轮,付费版访问和最近的支持者代码.
  • 中午缺票日期: 实际园区- 图片起始窗口为 下午2时至6时.

Overview

Kimi is a roller skater in Present Downtown. Meet her during the early downtown rescue, then follow her friendship through daytime practice, club skating, two Heart-to-Hearts, two one-time dates and repeatable venue invitations. This guide gives the current requirements and useful choices while leaving the personal scenes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Complete Story Beat 5, then visit Downtown Back Alley, 10 PM–midnight, for Story Beat 6: Alley Rescue. Finish the two enemy encounters; this makes Kimi available at Acknowledged.
  2. Return to Night Club, 10 PM–midnight, for First Roll Call. Its automatic scene is available every day once the rescue is complete, even when Kimi's regular portrait is absent.
  3. Build affection with Park Talk, 2–6 PM, daily, and Chocolate at Known. Boardwalk Talk becomes available after Diane's construction. Club Talk follows Kimi's separate club-night schedule.
  4. At Acquaintance, after First Roll Call, visit Downtown Park, 2–6 PM, for Daylight Wheels.
  5. At Friend, complete After the Alley from her park portrait, 2–6 PM. Optionally claim Skate Balance Drill with Stamina 35, and take Win Her a Prize with one Voucher.
  6. At Close Friend, after After the Alley, visit Night Club, 10 PM–midnight, for Floor Lights. The separate Boardwalk Sunset Date also becomes available after construction, with its paid and supporter access requirements.
  7. Reach Crush, then choose Own the Floor from her club portrait 9 PM–midnight on club nights, after Floor Lights and After the Alley.
  8. After Own the Floor, use Invite Date at KTV or Night Club, 8–11 PM. Invitations are separate from her portrait schedule and do not require either one-time date.

First Meeting: Alley Rescue

Visit Downtown Back Alley, 10 PM–midnight, after Story Beat 5. The rescue is automatic and completes once. Its three opening responses converge on the same combat sequence; choosing the magic-themed response does not bypass the fights.
There are two floor-6 Hoodlum encounters. They are weak to Light and strong against Dark, with combat statistics scaled by the shared enemy system. Prepare for both encounters rather than relying on the opening dialogue choice to avoid combat.
The registry awards 5 base Kimi affection and 10 base Becky affection. On ordinary first completion, the rescue handler also gives 10 direct Kimi affection and ensures she has reached Acknowledged, making her portrait schedules available. The rescue does not require prior Kimi friendship.

Troubleshooting

  • Kimi's portrait missing: finish Story Beat 6, reach Acknowledged, and check the location's specific schedule. Boardwalk also needs construction.
  • Club portrait absent: her nights start on days 2, 5, 8… at 9 PM, continuing until 2 AM the following day.
  • First Roll Call missing: return 10 PM–midnight after the rescue. It is an automatic scene available every day, rather than a portrait action.
  • Daylight Wheels missing: reach Acquaintance, finish First Roll Call, and visit Downtown Park, 2–6 PM.
  • After the Alley missing: reach Friend and finish Daylight Wheels, then open her park portrait.
  • Floor Lights missing: reach Close Friend, complete After the Alley, then visit Night Club, 10 PM–midnight.
  • Own the Floor missing after midnight: its starting window is 9 PM–midnight, even though Kimi's portrait remains available until 2 AM.
  • Boardwalk date locked: check Close Friend, construction, Daylight Wheels, paid-version access and the recent supporter code.
  • Voucher date absent at noon: the actual park-portrait start window is 2–6 PM.
  • Invitation roster missing Kimi: reach Crush, finish Own the Floor, and use Invite Date, 8–11 PM. Night Club needs the latest supporter code.
  • Perfect invitation ending missing: Chocolate or Flowers supplies the required gift point; Wine does not.
  • Another Chocolate gives a penalty: all locations use the same once-daily gift allowance.
Her current personal route has no configured apartment visit, ordinary private-time animation, worker recruitment, resort vacation, Kupie roster entry or pregnancy route.
