Overview

Kimi’s route alternates between daytime skating, club nights and the Boardwalk. Check the day number as well as the clock before looking for a night portrait.

Where to find them

After Story 6, her daytime Park schedule is 14:00–18:00. The Boardwalk opens after Diane’s construction and hosts her 17:00–20:00. Night Club evenings are days 2, 5, 8… from 21:00–24:00, continuing on days 3, 6, 9… from 00:00–02:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Work through the daytime and club events in order, with After the Alley, Own the Floor and On Purpose between them. The Boardwalk date precedes One More Lap; the later private invitation needs Romantic (800), Where I Want to Be and On Purpose.

Private Time starts from any of her three actual locations while she is present, costs 60 minutes and 30 Energy, and shares one daily use across them.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Strength 50; Stamina 50; Ability 60

Favorite skill: Martial Arts

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The 00:00–02:00 part belongs to the next calendar day.
  • Her Club, Park and Boardwalk Talk actions have separate normal limits of 5, 4 and 3 per day.
  • A shared invitation can pause during Abby’s pregnancy. Her ordinary club schedule is not an additional attendance requirement.

概览

Kimi 的路线在白天滑行、夜店与木板路之间切换。寻找夜间肖像时，除了时钟，也要检查日期编号。

在哪里找到

主线 6 后，她每天在公园 14:00–18:00 出现。Diane 建成木板路后，她也会在那里 17:00–20:00 出现。夜店时段为第 2、5、8……日的 21:00–24:00，并延续到第 3、6、9……日的 00:00–02:00。

路线与关系节点

白天与夜店事件需按顺序推进，并穿插相应谈心。木板路约会要先于动物园约会完成；后续私人邀请要求浪漫感（800）、完成她的后期个人目标，以及对应的亲吻谈心。

私人相聚可从她实际在场的三个地点开始，消耗 60 分钟与 30 精力，三个地点共用每天一次的次数。

常见卡点

  • 00:00–02:00 这一段属于下一日。
  • 夜店、公园与木板路交谈的常规次数分别为每天 5、4、3 次。
  • 共同邀请可能因 Abby 的孕期暂停，但不额外要求 Abby 的普通夜店日程。
