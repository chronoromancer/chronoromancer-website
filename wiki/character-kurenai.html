概览

Kurenai 是爱和机器人在 网络部门,未来初级部门合成配套匹配设施她的红白底盘,发光的红眼睛和干燥的幽默很容易认出来,她的路线结合了 介绍、十幕肖像画、三幕《心对心》、三期约会、珠宝礼物、技能课和黑客无限制的私人互动.
这个指南给出了完整的活动序列和奖励,同时将后来的调查和她的最后决定留给故事本身.

Overview

Kurenai is the love android at Cyber-Sects, the Synthetic Companion Matching Facility in Future Prime Sector. Her red-and-white chassis, glowing red eyes and dry humor are easy to recognize. Her route combines an introduction, ten portrait-selected story scenes, three illustrated Heart-to-Hearts, three dates, Jewelry gifts, skill lessons and a hacking-unlocked private interaction.
This guide gives the complete activity sequence and rewards while leaving the later investigation and her final decisions for the story itself.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Synthetic Companion Matching Facility for Welcome to Cyber-Sects. Talk works from Stranger, three times per day.
  2. At Known, choose Conversion Audit from her portrait after the introduction.
  3. At Acquaintance, choose Unpaid Attention, then begin giving Jewelry, up to three times daily. Paid-version players can also learn Emotional Bonding if they meet its stat requirement.
  4. At Friend, choose Premium Profile, then complete Service Mask. Reading the Script, her other two lessons, and Recharge Station Date are optional activities with separate requirements.
  5. At Close Friend, choose Retention Loop after Service Mask, then Warranty Clause. Skygarden Date opens separately after Retention Loop, 8 AM–4 PM.
  6. At Crush, choose Private Partition, then complete the distinct H2H Kept Partition.
  7. At Romantic, choose Unprofitable Refusal after Kept Partition.
  8. At Intimate, choose First Unscripted Choice, then complete Counterfeit Love. Memory Arcade Date is separately available after First Unscripted Choice, noon–10 PM.
  9. At In Love, choose The Pattern Beneath after Counterfeit Love, then Choose the Signal.
Most route scenes are selected manually from Kurenai's portrait. Re-entering the room alone does not start Conversion Audit or its later scenes. H2H completions and story-scene completions are separate requirements; higher affection does not replace either.

Heart-to-heart choice points accumulate and use the shared relationship calculation at completion. The listed points are base values, not guaranteed final affection gains.
For Service Mask, the Technology 35 answer in round 2 is the observation about the markers ranking your reactions. For Kept Partition, the Technology 45 answer identifies a protected local archive. Reading the Script

At Friend and Communication 50, choose Reading the Script from her portrait. It takes 30 minutes, completes once, and awards Communication +1 and 8 direct Kurenai affection.
The action definition lists Energy and Calm effects, but the current one-time-reward handler does not apply those effects. The implemented reward therefore does not deduct the listed 10 Energy or 5 Calm. It does not require a route H2H or teach a skill.

Hack SECTS Protocol and Private Access

Private Access is a separate facility activity unlocked by a successful Hack SECTS Protocol. It has no ordinary Kurenai affection-stage prerequisite and is not a completion prerequisite for her personal story route.
  1. Learn System Hacking. One lesson is at the Present University's Computer Lab, requiring Technology 30, 220 minutes, $350, 60 Energy and 60 Calm. Other game sources of the skill also work.
  2. At the Synthetic Companion Matching Facility, choose the location activity Hack SECTS Protocol. Each attempt costs 30 minutes and 15 Energy.
  3. The success chance is Technology −25 +5 for AI Programming +5 for Cybernetics, clamped to 5%–95%. Those two bonus skills are optional; System Hacking is required to attempt it.
  4. Success permanently unlocks the private facility action for that save. Failure ejects you to the Future player lab in Fringe Zone; return to try again. The current failure handler relocates you rather than starting a combat encounter.
  5. Choose Private Access from Kurenai's portrait. It normally costs 60 minutes and 40 Energy, gives 60 base Kurenai affection, and has a once-per-day animation allowance.
Private Access's animation has no additional NPC intimacy XP or player Erotic Art XP award configured. The action does not have a separate paid-version or high-affection requirement in its current definition. Its reward handler checks the animation's registration and daily allowance before granting affection.
