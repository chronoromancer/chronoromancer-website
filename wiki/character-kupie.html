  1. 开始 Emily,而无需主游戏朋友要求。单击她的图像开始当前关卡的亲情定时器并添加亲情。

Overview

Kupie is an adult Sylph, centuries old, training to become a Love Guardian. She stays in the Attic of your Uptown home, Present era, and helps with Love Training, an idle and clicker minigame. Her personal relationship has its own conversations, Chocolate and Wine, a sunset date, a special skill interaction, the Celestial Trumpet, and Twilight Stone bonding.
This walkthrough gives the requirements and reward choices without explaining the later conversations' revelations. Kupie's normal affection and Love Training's NPC affection, Hearts, Charms and levels are separate progress.

Recommended Personal Walkthrough

  1. Enable Kupie access through the game's redeem-code system, then visit your Attic. Her portrait is available at any hour. The Attic Guest introduces her, and Talk works from Stranger.
  2. At Known, return for Love Training 101 and start giving Chocolate, up to four times per day. Love Training itself is the Attic's location activity; the tutorial is not a required completion flag for entering it.
  3. At Acquaintance, return for No Misunderstandings.
  4. At Friend, return for The Bigger Picture and add Wine, up to three times per day.
  5. At Close Friend, return for Borrowed Time. If Diane's boardwalk construction is complete and you have paid-version and the required Higher Power access, choose Boardwalk Sunset Date, 5–8 PM.
  6. At Crush, paid-version players who have learned Mystic Seduction can use its Kupie interaction once per day.
  7. Keep building ordinary affection to In Love, then return for The Celestial Trumpet. If you also own the Twilight Stone, Intimate Bonding becomes available once per game day.
The six automatic encounters each have their own stage and access requirement. Their registry does not require the previous encounter's completion flag; the stage order is a practical way to see them naturally. There are no personal Heart-to-Heart or apartment-conversation chains to complete between these milestones.

Mystic Seduction Interaction

At Crush, paid-version players with Mystic Seduction already learned can choose Mystic Seduction from Kupie's portrait. Its normal cost is 120 minutes and 70 Energy, and its reward is 50 base Kupie affection. The animation has a once-per-day allowance.
Kupie does not teach the skill. Sashina's Mystic Seduction conversation is the teaching route: it requires 300 Sashina affection and Mystic 65 and takes 180 minutes. Learning it also improves Kupie's ordinary relationship gains before you reach her own Crush interaction.
Kupie's configured animation does not grant a separate NPC intimacy XP or player Erotic Art XP reward. Its normal time and Energy costs can be reduced by applicable time or access benefits.

Celestial Trumpet and Twilight Stone

At In Love, the automatic Celestial Trumpet encounter awards the Celestial Trumpet key item. All its choices converge on the reward; the higher-affection answers do not gate the item.
In Love Training, the Trumpet gives one phantom Sylph to each Heart job that already has at least one real Sylph assigned. It improves those jobs' effective worker counts. It does not add a freely assignable Sylph, start an empty job, or provide this phantom bonus to Charm crafts.
If you already own the Twilight Stone and Kupie is In Love, choose Intimate Bonding from her Attic portrait. It takes 60 minutes and adds one Twilight Stone level, once per game day. It has no configured money, Energy, ordinary affection or intimacy XP reward. The action returns the next game day after use; owning the Trumpet is not an extra requirement.

Love Training: Getting Started

See the complete Love Training guide for all 31 NPC gift sequences, production synergies, the 140-achievement checklist, TD equipment rewards, and current Rebirth rules.
Choose Kupie's Love Training from the Attic's location activities. Entering costs zero game minutes. Its production uses real elapsed time, rather than minutes advanced by normal game actions, and the entry routine settles accumulated production from time away.
  1. Start with Emily, who is available without a main-game Friend requirement. Click her image to begin her current level's affection timer and add click affection.
  2. Assign available Sylphs to Love Letter Delivery in the Hearts tab and Feather Weaving in the Charms tab. These are the initial unlocked jobs. Split or move workers as your current requirements demand.
  3. Meet the selected NPC's four separate requirements: affection, Hearts, Charms and a gift. Use each row's confirmation button when ready; merely having enough resources does not check off all the requirements automatically.
  4. When all four are confirmed, the NPC advances one level. Their minigame affection and four checkmarks reset for the next level. Click their image again to start the next level's timer.
  5. Claim available achievements, buy useful upgrades, and develop jobs and NPCs to open the remaining roster and crafts.
Hearts and Charms are spent when confirmed. Gifts are consumed from your actual player inventory, including the displayed quantity. Minigame affection does not spend or increase the selected NPC's normal main-game relationship points. Each NPC can reach level 10.
The affection timer continues for NPCs whose timer has been started; changing the selected NPC does not itself erase earlier progress. Clicking is an additional source of that NPC's minigame affection, rather than a source of Hearts or Charms.

The preview calculates Stars from lifetime Hearts + lifetime Charms + lifetime affection credited at NPC level-ups, minus 10,000,000, with a minimum of zero.
