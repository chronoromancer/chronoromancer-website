Overview

Seraph offers guidance at Seraph's Sanctuary in the Future Fringe Zone. Her current route centers on ordinary conversations, alignment, crystal services and Soul Stones. She has no authored heart-to-heart, date, apartment scene or NPC intimacy gallery in the current game files.

Finding Seraph

Use Cloudy Path at the Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station in the Future Fringe Zone to enter her sanctuary. This travel takes no time. Climb Down Mountain returns you to the processor, also with no travel time. The sanctuary is initially visible and unlocked and has no opening hours.
Several tutorials also feature Seraph in other locations. Those appearances are automatic scenes; her repeatable personal actions remain at the sanctuary.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the sanctuary and use Talk to Seraph up to five positive times each day.
  2. Complete the early condition, caterpillar and crystal tutorials as they appear. Their completion rewards also help her relationship.
  3. At Known and Communication 75, claim Divine Glow once. Its current reward is explained below.
  4. At Friend, revisit the sanctuary for The Eternal War. Choose the alignment you want to pursue.
  5. Bring your Queenstone, Sword Stone and Aether Stone to the sanctuary for their individual explanations and one-time relationship rewards.
  6. At In Love and Divine Good alignment or higher, return for her special item event.
  7. At In Love, bring a Twilight Stone for daily Intimate Bonding upgrades.

Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Helmet of Immortality

Complete the sanctuary event at In Love with at least Divine Good alignment to receive the Helmet of Immortality key item. Any reply receives the item; the reply above gives the highest affection.
In ordinary combat, its protection catches a final hit when all of these conditions are met:
  • Your current Health is above 10.
  • The remaining hit would be lethal and deals at least 250 damage.
  • At least 10 battle turns have passed since the helmet last activated.
  • There is no remaining active force field.
It reduces that hit to leave 1 Health. It is conditional protection, so continue to heal and defend normally. Some scripted story combat can override ordinary survival effects.

Proficiency Upgrade is a separate personal action. It costs 10 crystals, takes no time and requires Patreon Adventurer Tier+ entitlement and the game's normal save checks. It sets Patreon Master proficiency, updates the proficiency equipment name and upgrades your bedroom to the enhanced bedroom. It grants no affection. The action hides when that enhanced-bedroom state is already recorded.

Twilight Stone Bonding

Intimate Bonding appears at the sanctuary when Seraph is In Love, you own Twilight Stone, and you have not used her bonding action today.
It takes 60 minutes and adds 1 Twilight Stone level. It does not consume the stone and has no configured money, Energy or Calm cost, affection reward, player experience or NPC intimacy experience. Her stone levels have no configured cap. The daily allowance belongs to Seraph's bonding action.

Troubleshooting

  • I cannot find Seraph on the map: enter through Cloudy Path at the Future Fringe Zone Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station.
  • Divine Glow did not raise Attractiveness: the current active reward grants affection and completion without the advertised stat boost.
  • Her item event will not start: check both In Love and your actual Good alignment tier. Friend and a single Good reply are insufficient.
  • The helmet failed to save me: check current Health, the final damage amount, its turn cooldown and force field state. It does not catch every lethal hit.
  • Bonding disappeared: it returns on the next game day. Keep the Twilight Stone in your key items.
  • There is no date or romance scene menu: Seraph's current authored content is the conversations, events and services listed above.
