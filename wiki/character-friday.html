概览

Friday 她目前走过的地方结合了豪宅的灵魂收集路线、五个自动场景、对话、三个可重复的视频动作、一个静悄悄的隐蔽遭遇和一个圣诞礼物。豪宅的线索位置和谜题控制在下面;谋杀谜题的罪魁祸首和故事启示留给玩家去发现。

Overview

Friday is the Haunted Mansion's deadpan soul collector in Present Uptown. Her current walkthrough combines the mansion's soul-collection route, five automatic scenes, conversation, three repeatable video actions, a quiet crypt encounter and a Christmas gift. The mansion's clue locations and puzzle controls are covered below; the murder mystery's culprit and story revelation are left for the player to discover.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enable the Haunted Manor through the game's Halloween unlock; the game advertises HALLOWEEN in its Redeem Codes notice. The destination appears on the Present Uptown map when enabled. Friday's route then has no time-of-day or odd/even-day restriction.
  2. Arrive at the Exterior for Meeting Friday, then use Enter Mansion to reach the Foyer for The Soul Jar. Both automatic scenes complete once and give 5 base affection each. The empty Soul Jar is granted on the first Foyer visit.
  3. Select the Foyer's Harvest Soul, then click the revealed soul to put it in the jar. Merely revealing a soul does not collect it. Each actual soul added gives 10 fixed Friday affection.
  4. Explore the ground floor, gardens and basement, harvesting ordinary souls and completing the Library, Piano and Nursery puzzles below. Use Talk to Friday as needed; the allowance is shared wherever she appears.
  5. At 100 affection, use her three location-specific video actions when their rooms are accessible. Friday's Resolve in the Master Bedroom enables that room's Harvest Soul action.
  6. Reach the required soul totals for the Bedroom (9), Master Bedroom (11), Attic (15) and Crypt (16). Complete Ghostly Barriers at the South Stairs and, at Known after The Soul Jar, Depths of Appreciation in the Dungeon.
  7. At Crush, select Friday Fantasy in the Crypt for her five-round quiet encounter. Solve the mansion's separate mystery using the clue and review actions if pursuing its Crypt soul.
  8. With at least 21 souls in the Soul Jar, return to the Foyer for Soul Collection Complete. It awards 15 base affection, an Ornate Locket key item and 1 Crystal, once.
The currently registered collection has 21 clickable soul locations totaling 22 souls: the Attic Twins count as two. The completion scene and collection display use 21 as their target; you do not need to wait for 22 to trigger the Foyer reward.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Where Friday Appears

Friday's ordinary portraits are scheduled all day, every day in the Foyer, Master Bedroom, Bedroom, South Stairs / Attic Stairwell, Attic, Conservatory Passage, Rooftop Terrace and Crypt. Talk is available at each of these eight locations, with the same shared daily counter.
The current portrait and Talk entries use the actual South Stairs / Attic Stairwell travel location. Ghostly Barriers also triggers there automatically after The Soul Jar.

Talk and Repeatable Video Actions

Talk to Friday takes 20 minutes, gives 1 base affection and allows 50 uses per day shared across all rooms. Further attempts record −1 base affection. She has no ordinary Food, Perfume, Jewelry or other standard relationship gift action.
All three video actions below require 100 affection, are available all day once their rooms are accessible, and each plays once per day with its own counter. The handlers grant 50 player Erotic experience for each action; their current video records do not configure separate NPC intimacy-experience rewards.

Friday Fantasy in the Crypt

At Crush, enter the Crypt with its 16-soul access requirement and select Friday Fantasy. It costs 120 minutes and 15 energy, all day. This is a repeatable five-round quiet encounter, with one available answer at each step and no additional skill/stat branch.

Christmas Gift

During the active Christmas event, reach Close Friend and meet Friday in the Foyer. Give Christmas Gift consumes one Special Christmas Gift, takes 30 minutes and awards 100 fixed affection, once per character. Her themed gift is the Nightmare Vessel; you do not need to craft an ordinary item with that name. The gift's dialogue choices do not add an extra affection reward.

If Progress Stalls

  • Enable the Haunted Manor unlock and use the Present Uptown map if the destination is missing. Once enabled, it is not limited to night hours or the real-world Halloween date.
  • Click each revealed soul. Harvesting or solving a puzzle alone does not raise the collected count or jar quantity.
  • Use the actual room thresholds: Bedroom 9, Master Bedroom 11, Attic 15, Crypt 16.
  • Build 100 affection and complete Friday's Resolve before looking for Master Bedroom Harvest Soul.
  • Use puzzle actions in the Library, Music Room, Nursery and Crypt. Those current room menus do not offer an ordinary Harvest button.
  • The Terrace requires 15,000 Monster Dance points, then its separate Harvest action.
  • The twins count as two; the completion ceremony needs 21 in the jar, despite there being 22 souls in the current collection.
  • Return to the Foyer, not the Crypt, for Soul Collection Complete. Secret Passage provides a quick return.
  • Friday's ordinary portrait, Talk and the automatic Ghostly Barriers scene use South Stairs / Attic Stairwell. The automatic scene requires The Soul Jar first.
  • Friday has no separate worker, resort, date or personal H2H menu beyond the crypt encounter and mansion content documented here.
