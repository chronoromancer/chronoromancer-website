Overview

Friday appears throughout the Haunted Mansion. Her personal interactions sit alongside the mansion’s soul collecting and puzzle progression; raising affection does not complete those exploration requirements.

Where to find Friday

Enter the Haunted Estate from Present → Uptown after enabling its released event access. The Haunted Mansion guide gives the current entry instructions. Friday’s original room appearances are all-day, once their rooms are accessible.

The second-story conversation rooms open through their respective exploration challenges. The Coffin Chamber is a late second-story destination after its final objective. Follow the mansion guide for those routes; high affection does not open their doorways.

Route and relationship milestones

For the original collection reward, actually collect 21 souls and return to the foyer for the ritual. A reveal animation alone does not collect a soul: click its blue orb. The reward includes an Ornate Locket and 1 Crystal; this page leaves the ceremony’s story results in the game.

After the original ceremony and the second-story introduction, three room-specific heart-to-hearts become available as their routes open. Excellent Company is in the Mourning Parlour, Things That Take Root in the Thorn Conservatory and A Brief Eclipse in the Eclipse Chamber. Each is once-only and has no additional affection gate or fixed action-time charge.

Stay with Friday is a separate Coffin Chamber visit,120 minutes and 40 Energy, available under that room’s access rules. Its dialogue choices are explored inside the visit; it is not an additional soul pickup or a promised resource payout.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 20 minutes,+1 base relationship and 50 safe uses per game day. This allowance is shared across all rooms, including the Coffin Chamber. Moving between rooms does not reset it.

The original bedroom, master-bedroom and attic special interactions require Friend(100); the crypt visit requires Crush(400). At Close Friend(200), Friday is also a one-time Christmas Gift recipient at the original foyer, costing 1 Gift and 30 minutes for+100 base relationship. The full mansion guide covers rooms, puzzles, rewards and access without requiring outcome screenshots.

Missing actions and useful checks

If a soul seems missing, check whether its orb was clicked and whether Friday’s current position covers it. Leave and return or use her normal Talk response to change the portrait placement.

For a second-story heart-to-heart, finish the original ceremony, enter the second-story introduction and reach the correct room. High affection cannot replace room access. The Coffin Chamber visit follows its own room rule rather than a newly invented affection requirement.

概览

Friday会出现在闹鬼豪宅的多个房间。个人互动与收魂、解谜并行，刷高关系不会自动完成探索条件。

出现地点与时间

开放相应活动内容后，从现代 → 上城区地图的闹鬼庄园进入，当前入口说明见闹鬼豪宅指南。原篇房间能进入后，Friday全天都可出现。

第二篇谈心房间随各自的探索挑战开放，棺室则在第二篇最终目标完成后抵达。具体路线见豪宅指南，高关系不会直接打开这些入口。

路线与关系进展

原篇收藏奖励需要实际收齐21个灵魂，再回门厅完成仪式。揭示动画本身不算收集，必须点击蓝色灵魂光球。奖励包含华丽吊坠盒与1枚水晶，本页不展开仪式的剧情结果。

原篇仪式与第二篇开场完成后，随着房间路线开放，可进行三项专属谈心：「绝佳的同伴」在悼念会客室，「生根之物」在荆棘温室，「片刻月蚀」在月蚀室。各一次，没有额外关系门槛，也不另收固定动作时间。

棺室的「留下陪Friday」是独立拜访，耗120分钟、精力40，按该房间的进入规则开放。内部选择在拜访中探索，它不是另一颗灵魂，也不保证额外资源奖励。

行动与奖励

交谈耗20分钟，基础关系+1，每个游戏日安全额度50次，所有房间共用，包含棺室；换房间不能重置。

原篇卧室、主卧室与阁楼的特殊互动要求朋友（100），地穴拜访要求迷恋（400）。亲密朋友（200）时，也可在原篇门厅向Friday送出一次圣诞礼物，消耗1份礼物和30分钟，基础关系+100。房间、谜题、奖励与入口详情见豪宅指南，无需查看结果截图。

动作缺失时的检查

灵魂像是漏收时，检查是否点过光球，以及Friday当前站位是否挡住它。离开再进入，或使用普通交谈回应改变头像位置，可能便于查找。

第二篇谈心要检查原篇仪式、第二篇开场和正确房间；高关系不能代替进入条件。棺室拜访按自己的房间规则开放，不要额外假设一个关系门槛。
