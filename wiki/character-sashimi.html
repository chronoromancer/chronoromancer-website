  1. 完成 Sashimi's 在Dungeon楼13号古老的Catacombs接连发生3次水面.
  2. 构建 玫瑰和索恩池.
  3. 完成 Marina 捕鱼会话 至少有25条鱼在那次比赛中捕获,而Sashimi尚未移动到池。

Overview

Sashimi is a 20-year-old mermaid princess whose route connects the Ancient Catacombs Dungeon's Water Level, Marina's fishing trips, the Rose & Thorn Pool, and Sirena Isle. Discover her through three dungeon encounters and a fishing session, then follow her pool conversations, three dates, four personal heart-to-hearts, and voluntary recruitment as worker #27.
Her current route also includes the island's Water Rock, two crafted training upgrades, worker visits, a daily resort Sunset Walk, and 14 shared island encounters. The guide gives the required actions and flags without retelling the characters' personal discoveries or the scene outcomes.

Discover Sashimi

The current fishing discovery requires all three conditions together:
  1. Complete Sashimi's three sequential water encounters on Ancient Catacombs Dungeon Floor 13.
  2. Build the Rose & Thorn Pool.
  3. Finish a Marina fishing session with at least 25 fish caught in that one session, while Sashimi has not already moved to the Pool.
The 25-fish check uses the most recent fishing session, not a cumulative lifetime catch total. Discovery is checked while Marina processes the session results; it is not a random chance once these conditions are satisfied.

Three Water Encounters

Explore Floor 13, the Water Level for Sashimi's encounters. They are random selections from the available water-scenario pool and become eligible in sequence, so repeated water exploration may be needed between them.

Troubleshooting

  • Fishing will not discover her: complete all three Floor 13 water encounters, build Gastoff's Rose & Thorn Pool, and catch at least 25 fish in one session before processing Marina's results. A lifetime total or Diane's university pool does not satisfy the check.
  • She lives at the Pool but is not a worker: residency is the fishing discovery reward. Complete the later personal route through Her Own Current at Elara's Office to recruit worker #27.
  • First Steps is missing: reach Close Friend, finish The Shore Beyond the Rock → The Pool That Remembers → Two Tides, One Form, with Sashina at Acquaintance and home for her required scene. Return to the Pool during 9 AM–4 PM.
  • The morning form scene will not trigger: revisit the Pool, 6–11 AM, after The Pool That Remembers, with Sashina home. Its automatic hours begin before Sashimi's ordinary portrait.
  • Recruitment is missing at the Pool: Her Own Current starts automatically in Elara's Office, 9 AM–6 PM, at Crush, after More Than a Guest and the two required H2Hs, with Elara home.
  • The Shore at Last is missing: reach Romantic, complete After the First Tide, and finish Marina's $1,000,000 Sirena Isle boat project; start from the Pool 3–5 PM.
  • Both Are Me is missing: send Sashimi herself on vacation and visit the Water Rock, 3–8 PM, after The Shore at Last and Before the Tide Returns, at Romantic. The generic Beach Sunset Walk is a different H2H.
  • Crafted gifts will not appear: complete Both Are Me, reach In Love, return Sashimi home, carry the right item, and use the Pool during 3–5 PM. The rest of her portrait window does not offer these gift actions.
  • A shared island scene is missing: keep all named workers active and vacationing, then visit its exact room and hours. Uhura / Shelly remain island residents; they are not vacation worker requirements.
