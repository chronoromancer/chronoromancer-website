Overview

Sashimi’s access begins in the ordinary Dungeon’s water encounters and a fishing-session result. Living at the Pool and becoming a worker are separate milestones.

Where to find them

Complete her three sequential water encounters on ordinary Dungeon floor 13, build the house Pool, then catch at least 25 fish in a single fishing session and process the results. This opens her Pool residency. Her portrait is 09:00–19:00; later automatic scenes sometimes use earlier or later hours.

PlacePortrait hoursCondition
Pool09:00–19:00after fishing discovery; home rather than vacationing
Sirena Isle Water Rock15:00–20:00after fishing discovery; her own vacation required at the Water Rock

Route and relationship milestones

Continue Pool events, Quiet Beneath Questions and Boat Between Worlds. At Close Friend (200), the next Pool steps also need Sashina at Acquaintance (50) and home, including a return at 06:00–11:00. First Steps follows these events and starts from the Pool portrait 09:00–16:00.

Next visit Elara’s Office 09:00–18:00 with Elara home. At Crush (400), finish the two required H2Hs and the office event to recruit Sashimi. Return to the Pool 17:00–20:00 for its follow-up. The Shore at Last needs Romantic (800), that follow-up and Marina’s completed island boat project, and starts 15:00–17:00.

For Both Are Me, send Sashimi herself on vacation and meet her at the Water Rock 15:00–20:00 after the island date and Before the Tide Returns. Merely visiting Sirena Isle without her vacation assignment does not supply this portrait.

Optional reference: resort activities

After recruitment, assign her a vacation through Worker Rooms. Ordinary appearances pause while she is away. Resort portraits are selected on arrival; travel out and return for another presence roll. Talk, meals, beach relaxation, spa evening and the repeatable Sunset Walk use the resort menu. Shared scenes require their named workers active and vacationing, independently of random portrait selection.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Mystic 70; Charm 70; Communication 70

Favorite skill: Transmutation

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

The 25 fish requirement is for one session, not a lifetime total. Use the house Pool, not the university pool. Recruitment happens at Elara’s Office, not through a capture roll or a vacant cell. The morning automatic event can start before the ordinary Pool portrait appears.

概览

Sashimi 的入口始于普通地下城水域遭遇与一次钓鱼结算。入住水池和正式成为员工是两个不同节点。

在哪里找到

先完成普通地下城第 13 层的三次连续水域遭遇，建成宅邸水池，再在同一局钓到至少 25 条鱼并结算，才能开放她入住水池。普通肖像时段为 09:00–19:00；后续自动事件有时使用更早或更晚的时间。

地点肖像在场时段条件
水池09:00–19:00完成钓鱼发现；本人未在度假
塞壬岛水中岩15:00–20:00完成钓鱼发现；塞壬岛水中岩还需她本人正在度假

路线与关系节点

继续水池事件，并穿插指定谈心与乘船约会。亲密朋友（200）的水池后续还要求 Sashina 达到相识（50）且未在度假，其中一次需要在 06:00–11:00 回访。之后从水池肖像进行首次步行约会，可开始时段为 09:00–16:00。

接着在 Elara 未度假时，于 09:00–18:00 到她的办公室推进。迷恋（400）后完成两段指定谈心与办公室事件，才会正式招募 Sashimi。之后于 17:00–20:00 回水池推进后续。海岛约会要求浪漫感（800）、该后续及 Marina 已完成的海岛船只项目，只能在 15:00–17:00 开始。

后期塞壬岛水中岩谈心要求 Sashimi 本人正在度假，并已完成海岛约会与对应的先前谈心，再于 15:00–20:00 拜访塞壬岛水中岩。只有玩家抵达塞壬岛，而没有安排她度假，不能满足此项条件。

员工探访与训练

可选查阅：度假村活动

招募后，可通过员工房间安排她度假；离开期间暂停普通地点出现。抵达时才抽选度假肖像，可离开再返回重新判定。交谈、用餐、海滩放松、夜间水疗及可重复日落散步使用度假菜单。共同事件要求指定员工在职且正在度假，不依赖随机肖像抽选。

度假活动时段、奖励与海贝壳判定

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

神秘 70; 魅力 70; 沟通 70

偏好技能：转化术

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

25 条鱼指同一局的数量，不是累计总数。要求的是宅邸水池，不是大学水池。正式招募发生在 Elara 的办公室，不使用捕获概率或空牢房条件。清晨自动事件可以早于普通水池肖像出现。
