概览

Portia 是一个 22岁的社会主义者 有昂贵的品味, 发现周围 现为市中心她现在的个人路线 跑车 – 购物 – 设置顶楼,然后打开可重复 访问别墅还包括日常互动和馈赠, 约会标准两个可选的商场日期, KTV和夜总会邀请函,以及Kupie的爱情训练.
参观别墅也是玩家可以学习的地方 投资这种走过方式给出了实际的菜单要求,成本和分支奖赏,而没有重述场景的个人启示.

Overview

Portia is a 22-year-old socialite with expensive tastes, found around Present Downtown. Her current personal route follows Sports Car Ride → Shopping Trip → Set Up Penthouse, then opens repeatable villa visits. It also includes daily interaction and gifts, Dating Standards, two optional mall dates, KTV and Night Club invitations, and Kupie's Love Training.
The villa visit is also where the player can learn Investment Savvy. This walkthrough gives the actual menu requirements, costs and branch rewards without retelling the scenes' personal revelations.

Start Her Route

  1. Obtain the Sports Car key item. The Sports Car Dealer sells it for $250,000, with a 30-minute purchase action, 9 AM–6 PM.
  2. Find Portia on City Street, 10 AM–2 PM, or at another listed portrait location, and use Compliment Portia. It costs 10 minutes, gives 1 base affection, and has three positive uses daily.
  3. At Acknowledged, Talk to Portia becomes available. At Known, use Jewelry and, on the street or at the club, Flirt with Charm 50.
  4. At Known, select Sports Car Ride on City Street. It needs paid-version access, takes 45 minutes and 20 Energy, and completes once.
  5. Reach Friend, wear Dating Outfit level 3+, carry $50,000, and visit the mall on an even day, 2–6 PM, for Shopping Trip, after the car-ride H2H.
  6. At 300 ordinary affection, after Shopping Trip, use Set Up Penthouse at her Night Club portrait. It takes 120 minutes, $300,000 and 40 Energy.
  7. Reach 600 ordinary affection, then use Visit Portia at the Villa from Apartment Complex, 7–11 PM, on any game day.
The three H2Hs are ordered and one-time. The menu calls the third action Set Up Penthouse, but its conversation is Villa with Portia. It is the prerequisite for the villa visit; no separate mansion purchase or rent listing is added to that visit's definition.
The car purchase plus the shopping and setup action costs total $600,000, before ordinary gifts and optional activities. Sports Car Ride adds no further cash fee. Its own action definition has no additional Sports Car check, although the ordinary Stranger compliment route requires owning that key item.

Villa Visit Entry and Investment Savvy

At 600 ordinary affection, after Villa with Portia, open the Apartment Complex location menu and select Visit Portia at the Villa, 7–11 PM on any game day. It is a remote action; you do not need her ordinary street, mall or club portrait there.
The current entry cost is 180 minutes and 40 Energy, with no configured cash, Calm or ordinary gift cost. The dialogue description says variable length, but the action and shared handler charge that fixed entry time; choosing the shorter skill branch does not reduce it. Gifts selected inside a branch consume their own inventory items.
The visit is repeatable. Its action metadata lists three visits per day, but the current villa handler does not record a visit counter that enforces that limit. The guide therefore does not treat three as a guaranteed hard cap; each actual entry still charges its full time and Energy. No gameplay change is needed to follow the branches below.

Learn Investment Savvy

On round 1, choose Discuss Investment Savvy while you do not already know that skill. Continue the two investment-dialogue rounds 47 → 48 to learn it. This branch gives 50 base affection, has no player Erotic XP or NPC intimacy XP award, and ends the visit.
It does not require Commerce 35/50, Charm, Silver Tongue or a gifted item. The Commerce 35 investment-portfolio reply is a different branch. Once learned, the teaching reply disappears; ordinary repeatable villa visits remain available.
Learned Investment Savvy doubles Portia's adjusted ordinary-affection base in the shared relationship calculation. Learned Romantic adds 25 final affection at villa completion, separately from her bonus skill and from the base branch totals.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Compliment missing: carry the Sports Car key item and find her during the actual portrait schedule.
  • Talk missing: it starts at Acknowledged; compliments are the ordinary starting action.
  • Flirt missing at the mall: use street or Night Club, with Known and Charm 50.
  • Shopping Trip missing or rejected: complete Sports Car Ride, reach Friend, wear Dating Outfit level 3+, and use an even-day mall portrait with the full $50,000 cost.
  • Set Up Penthouse missing: complete Shopping Trip, reach 300 affection, and find her current Night Club portrait with $300,000 available.
  • Villa visit missing: complete Villa with Portia, reach 600 affection, and use the Apartment Complex remote action, 7–11 PM.
  • Investment Savvy reply missing: the teaching reply disappears once the skill is learned. The Commerce portfolio reply is different.
  • Footjob branch missing: choose the Commerce 35 portfolio entry, then its third reply, rather than the best ordinary affection answer or Teddy gift.
  • Extended villa branch missing: check Stamina 45, Beast 35 or 1,500 player Erotic XP at the particular branch where it is offered.
  • Mall date missing: actual starts are 5–6 PM for Boardwalk / 2–6 PM for Voucher, on even game days.
  • Invite Date missing: check Crush, Villa with Portia complete, venue hours and shared daily use; Night Club also needs the latest supporter code.
  • Perfect invitation ending missing: give a +1 gift and answer all five questions correctly. Night Club Flowers give zero score.
  • Kupie Portia missing: check ordinary Friend, Jessie Kupie level 5 and paid selection, rather than the villa's completion alone.
Her current route has no separate numbered brothel-worker training, resort vacation or personal Christmas scene. The ordinary route, villa branches, optional mall dates, venue invitations and Kupie progression above cover the authored personal content.
