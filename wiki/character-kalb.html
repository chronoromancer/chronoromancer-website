概览

Kalb 是个勤奋的狐狸人造食品商 国王的德梅斯他目前的内容包括每日谈话、建立他关系的食品采购、厨师奖、社区场景、表演舞台反应、妓院促销和早期的盗贼协会探险连锁店,他还加入了可选的私人客户轮换。

Overview

Kalb is a hardworking fox humanoid food vendor in the King's Demesne. His current content includes daily Talk, food purchases that build his relationship, a Cooking stat reward, community scenes, a Performance Stage reaction, brothel promotion and an early Thieves Guild quest chain. He also joins the optional private-client rotation.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Grand Bazaar, then Food Vendors, between 6 AM and 9 PM. Use up to six Talks per game day. Food purchases provide another relationship source while restoring player resources.
  2. With 80 gold available, select Cooking Tips for +3 Cooking and 5 direct Kalb affection, once. It is available from Stranger and does not require a friendship stage or teach Creative Cooking.
  3. At Acknowledged, visit Food Vendors for The Kind Fox and The Recipe Exchange when their other-character conditions below are met. Visit the General Goods Store from 6–9 PM for The Missing Ingredients Mystery.
  4. Prepare for two successive battles when first entering the Caravan Rest Stop. Completing Caravan Under Attack awards 5 base Kalb points; it is separate from the later guild battles.
  5. Follow Shadow's Thieves Guild trail, then complete Shadow's Rules. Return to Food Vendors for The Fox's Ears, go to the Caravan Rest Stop 8 PM–midnight for Clearing the Road, and return to Food Vendors for Good News Travels Fast.
  6. At Friend, with Sophie working, consider Ask Kalb to Spread the Word for a one-time promotion level increase. Optional performances and private-client bookings have their own requirements below.
These activities are separate branches. Cooking Tips, the community scenes, promotion and the performance reaction are not prerequisites for Kalb's guild chain. The story outcomes are left for you to discover.

The following location actions are available at Food Vendors 6 AM–9 PM. They do not use the six-Talk allowance, and have no daily limit configured.

Caravan Under Attack

On your first eligible visit to the Caravan Rest Stop in Village Outskirts, Caravan Under Attack starts automatically. It has no Kalb relationship, guild or hour prerequisite. Prepare your health and combat equipment before entering.
The scene contains two battles in sequence: a Roadside Thug at floor level 3, then a Raider Brute at floor level 4. Their authored base HP values are 15 and 22, before combat scaling. Both are strong against Dark and weak against Light. Complete both battles to reach the scene's ending and its 5 base Kalb points.
This is a separate one-time tutorial from Clearing the Road. Completing one does not substitute for the other.

With a composed song, use Perform Song at the Performance Stage, normally accessible 6 AM–9 PM. It costs 60 minutes and 30 Energy, with one performance per venue per game day.
After performing, a guest reaction has a 25% base chance. Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling and owning a Lute each add 5 percentage points, up to 50%. A successful reaction roll selects one of six guests: Kalb, Silas, Gruff, Rose Arthur, Jade or Esperanza. Kalb is not guaranteed, and his portrait need not be at the stage.

Ask Kalb to Spread the Word

At Food Vendors, 6 AM–9 PM, this location action requires Kalb Friend and Sophie active as a brothel worker. It costs 500 gold, has no configured time or energy cost, and adds +1 brothel promotion level, once per save. It does not award Kalb affection.
Unlike Cooking Tips, this action uses the ordinary one-time-action handler and does deduct its 500 gold. Its promotion effect is added to the current level. Talking about workers is separate from paying for this promotion.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Kalb enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level3. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Kalb. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Kalb has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is a friendly welcome, with Foxy listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Foxy, Sandra, Sophie.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Kalb affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Kalb.

Troubleshooting

  • Cannot find Kalb: enter Food Vendors inside the Grand Bazaar, not just its outer entrance or Central Plaza. Check 6 AM–9 PM.
  • Talk reward differs from the old guide: his current handler uses 1 base point; the six-use allowance resets each game day. Food rewards use a separate path.
  • Cooking Tips greyed out: have 80 gold available, and check whether you already claimed it. It is not an Accounting or Creative Cooking lesson.
  • Community scene missing: check every named NPC's stage, the exact location and hour, and Sophie's or Honey's vacation status where required.
  • Guild step missing: follow the completion flags in order. Clearing the Road requires 8 PM–midnight and its own three battles.
  • No promotion: reach Friend, activate Sophie as a worker, have 500 gold, and check the one-time claim.
  • Looking for personal romance content: no personal date, Heart-to-Heart, ordinary gift, apartment, worker, resort, Kupie or invitation-date route is currently configured for Kalb.
