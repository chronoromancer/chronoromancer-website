概览

Arestes 是26岁的小偷大师 领导着 小偷协会她的内容包括:盾牌会员和军衔场景、两条心对心的个人路线和星辰日期、可重复的屋顶参观、刺客战斗道路和幽灵石。

Overview

Arestes is the 26-year-old Master Thief who leads the Thieves Guild. Her content includes guild membership and rank scenes, a personal route with two heart-to-hearts and a stargazing date, repeatable rooftop visits, the Assassin combat path and the Phantom Stone.

Finding Arestes and Joining the Guild

  1. At the Thieves Guild, reveal the entrance to the Hidden Chamber by learning Lockpicking and reaching Perception 60. These entrance requirements are separate from the initiation requirements.
  2. Reach Known with Shadow and Rogue 35, then select Thieves Guild Initiation with Arestes in the Hidden Chamber. It takes 30 minutes and is available once. The scene gives 3 base affection to each of Arestes and Shadow.
  3. Follow Shadow's trail in order: Whispering Woods → Tournament Grounds → Arcane Shop → Mountain Foothills → Archery Range → Castle Garden → Back Alleys. The final scene gives 5 base affection to Arestes, 10 to Shadow and 1 guild clout.
  4. Continue at the Thieves Guild for Shadow's Rules, then Food Vendors for The Fox's Ears, Caravan Rest Stop from 8 PM to midnight for Clearing the Road, and Food Vendors again for Good News Travels Fast. This enables the fencing service and the early heist route.
  5. Raise guild reputation/clout through guild activities and fencing. Return to Arestes for the rank scenes listed below.
Use Talk to Arestes after reaching Acknowledged to continue building affection: it takes 20 minutes, awards 1 base point and permits three uses per day. Her affection, guild reputation and completed story scenes are separate progression measures.

Personal Route Walkthrough

  1. Reach Known with Arestes and Thieves Guild reputation 120.
  2. Visit the Hidden Chamber for Rank Up: Shadowblade, then the Thieves Guild for The Arrangement. Complete both scenes.
  3. Return to the Hidden Chamber for The Guild Pretends Not to Know.
  4. Select the first heart-to-heart, The Private Ledger.
  5. Visit the Hidden Chamber again for A Seat Beside the Master.
  6. Start Stargazing with Arestes between 7 PM and 11 PM. This one-time date costs 150 minutes, $30 and 30 energy, restores 10 calm and gives 12 base affection before choice bonuses.
  7. At Friend, complete No Mask Between Us after the date and first heart-to-heart.
  8. At Romantic, start Rooftop Appointment between 7 PM and midnight. It costs 120 minutes and 50 energy, once per day. It is a repeatable apartment encounter with a choice branch; follow the offered branch through its final response for the completion reward.
  9. The separate animated Rooftop Arrangement requires Romantic plus at least 1,300 affection, the second heart-to-heart and the paid version. It uses the same evening window and 120 minutes/50 energy, once per day, with 80 base affection and 75 intimacy experience on a successful completed action.
The Early Watch is an independent shared scene at the Hidden Chamber when both Arestes and Keira are Friend. It is not a prerequisite for the rooftop route.

Phantom Stone Walkthrough

  1. Reach Romantic with Arestes, Keira and Shadow. Keep Shadow active as a brothel worker.
  2. Obtain Thieves Gloves through Keira's route and Dark Consolador through Arestes's 500-reputation action.
  3. Visit the Thieves Guild while you do not already own the Phantom Stone to trigger The Phantom Stone Raid. Completing it awards the stone at level 1, 3 guild clout, and 8 base affection to each participant.
  4. With the stone in your key items, return to the Hidden Chamber for The Phantom Stone Awakens, worth 10 base affection before choices.
  5. At In Love with Arestes, use Intimate Bonding in the Hidden Chamber while holding the Phantom Stone. Each use takes 60 minutes, raises its level by 1, and can be used once per day. It has no configured money or energy cost and no direct affection award.

Fencing and Guild Protection

Fence Valuables appears in the Hidden Chamber after Good News Travels Fast. Have at least 10 Valuables. The trade takes no configured time and sells your entire current stack, not just ten. It grants gold, guild clout and 1–3 affection directly. It can be repeated when you obtain another eligible stack.
For a stack of N Valuables, guild clout is a random integer from roughly 70–130% of N, divided by 10, with a minimum of 1. Gold is twice a random integer from roughly 50–150% of N, plus a random 1 to effective Luck bonus. Integer rounding applies. You do not need to choose a dialogue category to obtain the fencing rewards.
Request Guild Protection is a separate location action. Own the Rose & Thorn, reach 180 Thieves Guild reputation, and pay $10,000. It is available once ever and adds 1 to the brothel's current promotion level. Its actual reputation threshold is 180; completing Rank Up: Phantom at 170 is not enough by itself.

If Progress Stalls

  • For the Hidden Chamber entrance, check Lockpicking and Perception 60; Rogue 35 is the initiation requirement.
  • For initiation, also check Known with Shadow.
  • Private Ledger needs the completed Shadowblade rank scene, The Arrangement and The Guild Pretends Not to Know. Reputation 120 without those scene flags is insufficient.
  • Stargazing starts only 7–11 PM, after A Seat Beside the Master.
  • Rooftop Appointment needs Romantic and No Mask Between Us. Rooftop Arrangement additionally needs 1,300 affection and paid access.
  • The Phantom Stone raid needs all three Romantic relationships, active Shadow and both key items.
  • Fencing needs the Kalb report scene and at least ten Valuables; guild protection instead needs 180 reputation, brothel ownership and $10,000.
