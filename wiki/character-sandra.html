Overview

Sandra’s recruitment route is built around her Guild conversations and a successful duet. Her later Bar activities require both her recruitment and access to the house Bar.

Where to find them

Find her at the Bards Guild daily 13:00–18:00, at the Performance Stage on even days 18:00–21:00, and at the house Bar after recruitment 21:00–24:00. The Bar entrance itself requires Brandy recruited and active. Vacation temporarily replaces ordinary appearances.

Route and relationship milestones

At Known (25), complete Lost Passion. Reach Friend (100), then return to the Guild 13:00–18:00 for the duet proposal. Use Duet with Sandra and finish with a score strictly above 20,000. This opens A New Beginning at Crush (400), provided you own the house.

A New Beginning recruits her once and adds Promotion +1 and player Erotic Experience +25. Its relationship reward comes from the conversation. The Tavern Evening date and crafted worker upgrades have their own later conditions.

Follow the duet’s timing prompts. A completed attempt gives 10 base affection; passing adds another 15. Each completed attempt also has a 25% chance of Bards Guild Clout +1. The performance can be repeated while time and Energy allow; cancellation does not count as a completed attempt.

Artistic Vision and Dramatic Arts are separate paid skill lessons. Performance Wisdom is the once-only Creativity +1 lesson at Known (25) and Communication 48, with 10 direct affection. Their thresholds, costs and rewards are in the action table.

Optional reference: resort activities

After recruitment, assign her a vacation through Worker Rooms. Ordinary appearances pause while she is away. Resort portraits are selected on arrival; travel out and return for another presence roll. Talk, meals, beach relaxation, spa evening and the repeatable Sunset Walk use the resort menu. Shared scenes require their named workers active and vacationing, independently of random portrait selection.

Optional reference: NPC intimacy gallery

This character has three current gallery rewards at 100, 250 and 500 total NPC intimacy experience. Open the character details, then Intimacy details, and select an unlocked thumbnail. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience, worker Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate; viewing a reward adds no experience.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 before ordinary affection bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount. An affinity is not itself a lesson offered by this character.

Creativity 50; Ability 40; Charm 35

Favorite skill: Music Production

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection, player experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate. Conversation choices can add rewards beyond event-completion affection.

What usually blocks the next step

Exactly 20,000 is not enough for the duet pass. If A New Beginning is missing, check Crush, house ownership and the recorded pass, rather than repeating the date. After recruitment, Brandy’s Bar-entry requirement still applies. Her normal Talk allowance is shared across all three locations.

概览

Sandra 的招募路线围绕公会谈话与一次成功二重唱展开。后期酒吧活动既需要招募她，也需要宅邸酒吧入口已经开放。

在哪里找到

每天 13:00–18:00 可在吟游诗人公会找到她，偶数日 18:00–21:00 在表演舞台；招募后还会在宅邸酒吧 21:00–24:00 出现。酒吧入口本身需要 Brandy 已招募且在职。度假期间会暂停普通地点出现。

路线与关系节点

已知（25）时先完成失落的热情，达到朋友（100）后于 13:00–18:00 返回公会触发二重唱提议。进行与 Sandra 二重唱，结束分数必须严格超过 20,000。之后达到迷恋（400）且拥有宅邸，即可进行新的开始。

新的开始会一次性招募她，使宣传等级 +1，并给予玩家情欲经验 +25；好感度由谈话选择决定。酒馆晚间约会及制作礼物员工升级各有独立的后续条件。

员工探访与训练

可选查阅：度假村活动

招募后，可通过员工房间安排她度假；离开期间暂停普通地点出现。抵达时才抽选度假肖像，可离开再返回重新判定。交谈、用餐、海滩放松、夜间水疗及可重复日落散步使用度假菜单。共同事件要求指定员工在职且正在度假，不依赖随机肖像抽选。

度假活动时段、奖励与海贝壳判定

可选查阅：NPC 亲密画廊

该角色目前有三项画廊奖励，分别要求累计 NPC 亲密经验 100、250 与 500。打开角色详情，再进入亲密详情，点击已解锁缩略图查看。普通好感度、玩家情欲经验、员工经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；查看奖励不会增加经验。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。偏好技能本身不代表该角色会教授它。

创造力 50; 能力 40; 魅力 35

偏好技能：音乐制作

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度、玩家经验与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择可能在事件完成好感度之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

二重唱恰好 20,000 分还不算通过。新的开始未出现时，请检查迷恋、宅邸所有权与已记录的通过结果，不必反复约会。招募后仍需满足 Brandy 的酒吧入口条件。三个地点共用普通交谈次数。
