Overview

Sandra is the 26-year-old bard at the Medieval Bards Guild. Her route begins with everyday conversation and gifts, then continues through Lost Passion, a duet rhythm minigame, and A New Beginning, which recruits her to the Rose & Thorn as worker #4.
Her current content also includes Tavern Evening, performance reactions, shared encounters with Sherry, Brandy and Vixen, two late-route training upgrades, worker visits and Sirena Isle vacation activities, including 22 shared vacation scenes. This guide gives the requirements and rewards without retelling the personal revelations or scene outcomes.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the Bards Guild, 1–6 PM and build affection with Talk. At Known, add Flowers and complete the paid Lost Passion H2H.
  2. At Friend, return to the Guild during 1–6 PM. A Different Stage triggers automatically after Lost Passion and unlocks Duet with Sandra.
  3. Play the duet until the final score is greater than 20,000. A score of exactly 20,000 does not pass. Passing sets the lasting recruitment requirement.
  4. Own the Rose & Thorn, reach Crush, and select the paid A New Beginning H2H at the Guild. It activates Sandra as worker #4 and gives the recruitment extras.
  5. Take Tavern Evening at Friend or higher, Guild 3–6 PM. This date is independent of recruitment and does not require paid access.
  6. After recruitment, visit the Brothel Bar with Sherry at Acquaintance for A Stage Reborn. At Friend with both Sandra and Brandy, visit 9–10 PM for Bar Duet.
  7. Build Friend with both Sandra and Vixen, then visit the Guild, 4–8 PM on even days, for The Art of Performance. After Vixen's VIP Lounge Introduction, return 6–10 PM on an even day, with both workers active, for The Shape of Things.
  8. At In Love, give the two crafted upgrade gifts after their separate scene prerequisites. They improve matching worker-training Experience and worker income.
  9. Send Sandra on Sirena Isle vacation for her daily activities, repeatable Sunset Walk and shared vacation encounters.
This order is convenient rather than an extra set of requirements. Dates, lessons, shared scenes and training gifts use their own gates described below.

Troubleshooting

  • Sandra is missing from the Guild: her portrait is 1–6 PM. On even days she moves to the Performance Stage 6–9 PM; after recruitment, her Bar portrait is 9 PM–midnight. Also check whether she is vacationing.
  • Duet is unavailable: reach Friend, complete the paid Lost Passion H2H, and revisit the Guild 1–6 PM for A Different Stage; the duet itself also requires paid access.
  • A New Beginning stays locked after a performance: pass Duet with Sandra with score above 20,000, reach Crush, own the Rose & Thorn and use her Guild portrait. Ordinary Perform Song reactions do not set the duet-passed flag.
  • A late Vixen scene seems unreachable: enter or remain in the Bards Guild during its own even-day plot window. The room is still open after Sandra's ordinary portrait moves.
  • A training gift is missing: check In Love, its exact required completed scenes, one crafted gift in inventory, her home portrait hours and vacation status. The two gifts unlock independently.
  • No Encore does not appear at the Guild: give the Choker from the Brothel Bar portrait, 9 PM–midnight, after A Stage Reborn.
  • Training or gallery progress looks too low: ordinary affection, worker relationship, worker Experience, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate counters. Only the NPC sources listed above unlock Sandra's gallery.
  • A resort scene will not trigger: all companions must be active and currently vacationing. The two Cinnamon scenes additionally require A Room to Perform. Manual activities also need Sandra to be the selected portrait.
