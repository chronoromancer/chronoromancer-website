概览

Riki 是一个 27岁合同狙击手 输入 未来二. 结 论 Story 击打 033 招募她 并打开她的 关系行动 TD 兵营她的个人路线包括: 未来几个地点的10次自动碰头, 三颗心对心, 两个可选日期, 重点培训,以及 范围估计.
本指南给出了路由顺序,地点,要求和最高点的回复,但没有重述个人启示或结论结果.

Overview

Riki is a 27-year-old contract sniper in the Future. Complete Story Beat 033 to recruit her and open her relationship actions at the TD Barracks. Her personal route includes ten automatic encounters across several Future locations, three heart-to-hearts, two optional dates, Archery Focus training, and Range Estimation.
This guide gives the route order, locations, requirements and highest-point replies without retelling the personal revelations or concluding outcomes.

Suggested Progression

  1. Recruit Riki in Story Beat 033, then visit TD Barracks for ordinary Talk. Complete 034 at Operations Center.
  2. At Acknowledged, visit Barracks for Twenty Empty Beds. At Known, go to TD Armory for The Armory Clause.
  3. Learn Archery Focus from Known with Perception 15. At Acquaintance, claim Range Estimation after Armory Clause, and visit Transit Nexus Fringe for Shared Sightline.
  4. At Friend, follow A Clean Target at Parking Complex → Bad Coordinates at Barracks → Contract Maintenance at Operations Center. After Bad Coordinates, complete The Last Report H2H from the Barracks action menu.
  5. At Close Friend, complete The Counteroffer at Barracks; then A Clean Exit H2H becomes available after The Last Report. Continue No Invoice at Barracks → The Renewal Window at Operations Center.
  6. At Crush, complete No Contract for This after A Clean Exit and The Renewal Window. Return to Barracks for Chosen Position, the final personal encounter.
  7. The two optional dates can be completed independently from their Friend / Close Friend unlocks. They do not replace the personal encounters or H2H prerequisites.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Riki visible but no actions: her Recharge Station appearance before recruitment has no relationship actions. Complete 033 and use TD Barracks.
  • Recruitment will not trigger: finish 032, carry $20,000, and visit the Recharge Station, 6 PM–midnight.
  • First personal encounter missing: finish 034, reach Acknowledged, and visit Barracks.
  • Later encounter missing: check its actual location, minimum stage and preceding encounter in the route table; they are not all at Barracks.
  • Range Estimation missing: check Acquaintance, Armory Clause complete, and whether its one-time reward is already claimed.
  • Archery Focus missing: check Known and Perception 15; it disappears once learned.
  • The Last Report missing: finish Bad Coordinates and reach Friend.
  • A Clean Exit missing: finish The Last Report H2H and The Counteroffer, then reach Close Friend.
  • No Contract for This missing: finish A Clean Exit H2H and The Renewal Window, then reach Crush.
  • Chosen Position missing: finish No Contract for This, then return to Barracks at Crush.
  • Date missing: select it from Barracks within its own window; it disappears after completion.
