概览

Poison 这是 24岁的混沌集团首席执行官,通常发现 花园 她的路线包括: 7个个人自动场景支持辛迪加事件, 两个独立的H2Hs,每日谈话和 两个电影动作任选联盟继续招募她加入抵抗军。
这种穿行给游戏留下了位置,先决条件和奖励,同时将故事启示和相应的情节结果留给游戏.

Overview

Poison is the 24-year-old chief enforcer of the Chaos Syndicate, usually found tending The Garden in the Future Prime Sector. Her route includes seven personal automatic scenes, supporting Syndicate events, two independent H2Hs, daily Talk and two movie actions. The optional alliance continuation also recruits her for the Resistance army.
This walkthrough gives locations, prerequisites and rewards while leaving story revelations and consequential plot outcomes to the game.

Meeting Poison and Unlocking Talk

  1. In Future Prime Sector, visit Syndicate Gates. Your first eligible arrival triggers The Gates of Chaos, giving 3 base affection to Poison, alongside Bayne and Motley rewards.
  2. From the gates, use To The Garden. After the prologue, Street Knowledge triggers here once and gives 3 base affection to Poison and Motley.
  3. Continue the Syndicate's shared events. Talk requires Acquaintance, normally 50 affection, so it does not provide a repeatable affection source immediately at Stranger.
  4. At Known, visit The Den, noon–6 PM, for Something Green once. Choose Flirty for 4 choice base + 1 fixed = 5 best base affection.
  5. At Acquaintance, use Talk from Poison's Garden portrait. Each positive use gives 1 base affection and takes 15 minutes; the daily positive allowance is five, totaling 75 minutes and 5 base before bonuses.
Early plot rewards do not guarantee Acquaintance on their own; favorite stats, learned Martial Arts and the shared affection calculation affect your actual gains. Continue the shared route instead of looking for a gift or Talk action that is absent at Stranger. Talk has no configured cash, Energy or gifted-item cost. Over-limit Talk can incur the shared escalating annoyance penalty, up to −5 affection.
