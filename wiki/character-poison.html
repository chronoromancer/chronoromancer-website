Overview

Poison can be found in The Garden. Her personal route runs alongside the wider Syndicate story, with two independently unlocked conversations and two repeatable private actions.

Where to find them

Reach the Syndicate through the Future Prime Sector route, then enter The Garden from Syndicate Gates. Her normal portrait is available all day. Something Green is a separate event at The Den, 12:00–18:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Complete The Gates of Chaos, then visit the garden for Street Knowledge. Supporting Syndicate scenes and the Known-stage Den encounter help build affection before ordinary Talk opens at Acquaintance (50).

The personal chain is Green Thumb at Friend (100) → Roots at Close Friend (200) → The Test at Crush (400), also after Kitty’s and Chamel’s introductions → Proving Ground at Romantic (800) → The Real Poison at Intimate (1,600).

Patch Job and No Armor open independently at Friend (100) and Crush (400); the latter does not require completing the former.

Her supporting group events are part of the Syndicate guide, including the collection route at Robot Bar, reconnaissance, the shared meetings and the later alliance continuation. Group events outside the garden do not require moving Poison’s normal portrait there.

The optional Army continuation starts after Story 85 and the Syndicate’s After the Line, then follows Room for Reinforcements → Not for Free → The Third Knock → The Long Way Out → Terms of Command → What She Built → Our War. Recruitment comes from completing that live continuation, not from affection or movie viewing.

Talk gives 1 base affection in 15 minutes, five positive uses per day. Prove Yourself needs 1,000 affection and gives 80 base affection; The Garden After Dark needs 1,500 and gives 120. Each costs 30 minutes and 20 Energy and has its own once-daily allowance. Their titles do not impose a night-time start requirement.

She does not teach Martial Arts in this portrait menu. Army stats and upgrades are separate from personal affection; use the Army guide for deployment.

Favorite stats are Strength, Stamina, Ability. Learning Martial Arts improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Before Acquaintance (50), continue shared Syndicate scenes and the Den encounter rather than looking for an earlier ordinary Talk button.
  • For The Test, check both Kitty’s and Chamel’s introductions as well as Poison’s earlier scene.
  • The independent H 2 Hs do not replace the automatic plot chain, and an Extras replay does not recruit an Army member.

概览

Poison通常在花园。她的个人路线与辛迪加主线并行，包含两次独立开放的谈心和两项可重复的私人行动。

去哪里找

沿未来核心区的路线进入辛迪加，再从辛迪加大门前往花园；她的日常头像全天可用。一片绿色则是巢穴12:00–18:00的独立事件。

路线与关系进展

完成混沌之门后，到花园触发街头情报。普通交谈需相识（50好感），之前可通过辛迪加共同事件和已知阶段的巢穴事件提高好感。

个人事件依次为：朋友（100好感）的园艺技巧 → 亲密朋友（200好感）的根源 → 迷恋（400好感）的测试，同时需完成Kitty与Chamel的介绍 → 浪漫感（800好感）的训练场 → 亲密（1,600好感）的真正的 Poison。

应急包扎和无护甲分别在朋友（100好感）、迷恋（400好感）独立开放；后者不要求先完成前者。

交谈消耗15分钟、获得1基础好感，每天5次。证明你的价值。需1,000好感，给予80基础好感；暗夜花园需1,500好感，给予120基础好感。两项均消耗30分钟、20精力，分别每天一次；行动名称不会额外限制为夜间开始。

她的头像菜单不教授武术。军团属性、升级与个人好感分开计算，部署请看军团指南。

偏好的属性为力量、耐力、能力。学会武术可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 相识（50好感）前，先推进辛迪加共同事件和巢穴事件；普通交谈尚未开放。
  • 测试还需Kitty与Chamel的介绍，以及Poison的前置事件。
  • 两次独立谈心不会替代自动事件链；在附加内容中重播也不会完成军团招募。
