Artemis — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Artemis is a 96-year-old elven Resistance marksman. Her current route has ten personal story scenes, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, a Tactical Awareness lesson and a one-time Perception reward. Progress depends on completed scenes and main-story milestones as well as affection.

Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Use Talk to Artemis to reach Acknowledged. Talk takes 15 minutes, gives 1 base affection, and permits three uses per day across her locations.
  2. After Story Beat 32, visit the Barracks, 6 PM–midnight, for The Name She Chose at Acknowledged.
  3. At Known, return during that evening window for No Easy Forgiveness.
  4. At Acquaintance, visit the Armory, 8 AM–6 PM, for Two Hands, One Weapon. This unlocks the one-time Listen Before the Shot lesson, worth +1 Perception and 8 direct affection for 45 minutes, 15 energy and 10 calm.
  5. Complete Story Beat 34, then visit the Armory, 8 AM–6 PM, for Two Sets of Eyes after the previous scene.
  6. At Friend, visit the Barracks, 6 PM–midnight, for A Memory Without Orders, which unlocks Ash and Cedar. Complete that H2H. The Last Is Not a Relic is a separate automatic evening scene after A Memory Without Orders; it does not itself require the first H2H.
  7. At Close Friend, visit the Armory, 8 AM–6 PM, for The Biobot Line after The Last Is Not a Relic. It unlocks The Last Witness, provided Ash and Cedar is complete, and the separate Life After Ruin date.
  8. Return to the Barracks, 6 PM–midnight, for What the Ribbon Kept at Close Friend.
  9. At Crush, visit the Armory, 8 AM–6 PM, for A Future, Not a Replacement, then complete What Survived after The Last Witness.
  10. With What Survived complete, visit the Barracks, 6 PM–midnight, for Both Names, One Choice at Crush. This is the final currently authored scene in her personal chain.
The optional dates can help build affection, but they are not prerequisites for the ten-scene personal chain. The first date needs Beat 34 and Two Sets of Eyes; the second needs The Biobot Line.

  • Her automatic Barracks scenes need 6 PM–midnight; merely finding her there before 8 AM does not trigger them.
  • Two Sets of Eyes and the first date need completed Beat 34.
  • Affection alone does not unlock H2Hs: each also needs its listed story scene and previous conversation.
  • The second date can start 4–6 PM in the Armory or 6–10 PM in the Barracks.
  • Complete What Survived before expecting Both Names, One Choice.
  • There is no separate gift, apartment or animated personal action to unlock after that final scene.
