Overview

Artemis’s personal route begins after the early Resistance chapters and moves between the Armory and Barracks. It includes ten personal scenes, three conversations, two optional dates and one-time perception training.

Where to find them

After Story 28 and before Story 32, her portrait is at Entertainment Tower all day. After Story 32, find her at Armory, 08:00–17:59, or Barracks, 18:00–07:59.

Her evening automatic personal scenes specifically start at 18:00–23:59. The overnight Barracks portrait does not extend those events past midnight.

Route and relationship milestones

After Story 32, reach Acknowledged (10) and visit the Barracks in the evening for The Name She Chose. Follow the location changes in the automatic-scene table. Two Sets of Eyes also needs Story 34.

The conversations run Ash and Cedar → The Last Witness → What Survived. Complete the third before returning for the last personal scene. Both dates are optional for the automatic chain.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Check whether the next automatic scene belongs at the Armory or Barracks.
  • For evening personal scenes, start before midnight even though her Barracks portrait stays overnight.
  • For the last scene, finish What Survived as well as the preceding personal scene and Crush stage.
  • Two Sets of Eyes requires Story 34; repeatedly waiting before that chapter will not trigger it.

概览

Artemis的个人路线在反抗军前期主线后展开，往返军械库与兵营，包含十段个人事件、三次交心对话、两次可选约会及一次性感知训练。

去哪里找

主线28后、主线32前，头像全天在娱乐塔；主线32后，08:00—17:59到军械库，18:00—07:59到兵营。

晚间自动个人事件的开始时间为18:00—23:59，兵营头像通宵出现不代表这些事件也能午夜后触发。

路线与关系进展

主线32后达到已遇见（10），傍晚到兵营进行她选择的名字，之后按表中地点切换推进。两双眼睛还要求主线34。

交心对话顺序为灰烬与雪松 → 最后的见证者 → 幸存下来的是什么。第三次完成后再返回进行最后的个人事件；两次约会都不是自动事件链的必经步骤。

卡住时检查什么

  • 检查下一自动事件是在军械库还是兵营。
  • 晚间个人事件要午夜前开始，头像通宵出现也不延长事件窗口。
  • 最后事件除前置个人事件和迷恋外，还要求完成幸存下来的是什么。
  • 两双眼睛需主线34，提前反复等待不会触发。
