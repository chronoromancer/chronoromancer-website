概览

Rose Arthur 是一个 60岁的工匠 输入 中世纪大集市跟她在酒吧见面 手工艺品和珠宝店在那里她提供普通的谈话, 阿尔蒂桑智慧,两个 精心制作的礼品她的摊位也通过地点行动出售珠宝和妓院设备。
她目前的路线以社区和工艺为中心,该指南涵盖已实施的服务和遭遇,但没有重述个人故事结果。

Overview

Rose Arthur is a 60-year-old artisan in the Medieval Grand Bazaar. Meet her at the Crafts and Jewelry Stall, where she offers ordinary Talk, Artisan Wisdom, two crafted gifts, and several community encounters. Her stall also sells Jewelry and brothel equipment through its location actions.
Her current route centers on community and crafting. This guide covers the implemented services and encounters without retelling personal story outcomes.

Where to Find Her and Suggested Progression

Visit the Crafts and Jewelry Stall from the Grand Bazaar, 6 AM–9 PM, every game day.
  1. Use Talk to Rose Arthur, up to five positive talks daily. The handler awards 1 base each, despite the action metadata's nominal 2.
  2. At Acknowledged, build the same stage with Robin, Valkyra or Nerida to unlock their shared encounters at the stall, 9 AM–5 PM.
  3. At Known and Communication 45, use the one-time Artisan Wisdom reward.
  4. When you have the required Infinity Dungeon drops, use her two Craft actions to create gifts for supported worker training upgrades. Crafting does not require a later Rose affection stage.
  5. After recruiting Ryia, visit Rose during her schedule with Ryia available for What They Carried Home. This encounter has no additional Rose/Ryia affection prerequisite.
  6. Perform a composed song at Performance Stage for a chance of Rose's repeatable performance reaction.
Purchases and crafting are separate from giving Rose an ordinary gift; they do not grant configured Rose affection.

Performance Reaction

After Perform Song at the bazaar Performance Stage, Rose's reaction can be randomly selected. It is repeatable, giving 8 fixed base + Caring 4 = 12. Funny gives 10 total, and Buddy 9.
Performing requires a composed song, takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy, and is available once per venue daily. The reaction roll is 25%, plus 5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling, and owning a Lute, capped at 50%. A successful roll then randomly selects one of six Performance Stage reactions. Rose's reaction is not guaranteed and its affection is separate from the song's normal performance rewards.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Rose absent: visit the Crafts and Jewelry Stall, 6 AM–9 PM.
  • Talk gives less than expected: its actual positive handler records 1 base, despite nominal metadata 2. Five positive talks are 5 base, before shared bonuses.
  • Artisan Wisdom missing: check Known and Communication 45, and whether its one-time reward is already claimed.
  • Community encounter missing: check the other NPC's stage and the listed 9 AM–5 PM window; vacation restrictions apply where specified.
  • Ryia encounter missing: recruit Ryia, ensure she is active and not on vacation, and visit while Rose is present. The other history scenes are independent.
  • Crafting unavailable: carry the exact quantities of all three required dungeon drops for that recipe.
  • Crafted gift did not unlock a scene immediately: it stays in inventory; use the eligible worker's named gift-upgrade action.
  • Equipment gives no benefit: equip it through the appropriate management screen after purchasing.
  • Performance did not show Rose: the reaction roll and selected audience NPC are random; try another eligible day.
Rose has no current heart-to-heart, apartment visit, ordinary date, private movie, Kupie selection, venue invitation, pregnancy route or NPC intimacy reward-image files. Her current completion goals are Artisan Wisdom, the five community encounters, her crafting services and the repeatable performance reaction.
