Overview

Rose Arthur is the Grand Bazaar’s artisan. Her useful content centers on a Creativity lesson, community encounters and crafting two specific gifts from Infinity Dungeon trophies.

Where to find them

Find her at Crafts and Jewelry Stall, daily 06:00–21:00, from Grand Bazaar. Ordinary Talk gives 1 base affection in 15 minutes, five positive uses per day.

Route and relationship milestones

At Known (25) and Communication 45,Artisan Wisdom takes 30 minutes and gives+1 Creativity and 8 direct affection, once. Anthropology improves her relationship bonus, but she does not teach it here.

At Acknowledged (10), her shared encounters with Robin, Valkyra or Nerida require that other character also be Acknowledged (10),09:00–17:00. Valkyra must be home from vacation. The separate Sophie gift encounter needs Rose Arthur Acknowledged (10), Sophie available and home, 06:00–21:00.

Ryia’s stall recollection needs Ryia active and home, and Rose Arthur currently present. Her Performance Stage reaction is a separate possible result of the shared music activity.

Gift crafting is available from Stranger (0), without an affection lesson or private relationship route. Each trade takes 0 minutes and consumes the exact trophies in the table; the finished gift goes into consumables. Bring a fresh set for each additional copy.

These gifts are used by particular staff members’ late relationship/training scenes; crafting a gift does not itself complete the recipient’s route. Use the Brothel training reference and each recipient’s guide for their exact In Love requirement, earlier scenes and delivery location.

The stall’s location menu also sells Jewelry and staff equipment. These purchases have their own prices and do not automatically raise Rose Arthur’s affection.

Favorite stats are Charm, Creativity, Perception. Learning Anthropology improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • For crafting, check the exact Infinity Dungeon trophy names and quantities. Ordinary ingredients, materials or a similarly named gift cannot replace them.
  • Crafting produces a gift; you must still deliver it through the intended recipient’s own action.
  • She has no ordinary gift-receiving button or personal apartment route in this menu. Community events and crafting remain useful without inventing one.

概览

Rose Arthur是大集市的工匠。她的实用内容围绕创造力奖励、社区事件，以及用无限地下城战利品制作两种指定礼物展开。

去哪里找

每天06:00–21:00，从大集市前往工艺品与珠宝摊找到她。日常交谈消耗15分钟，获得1基础好感，每天5次。

路线与关系进展

已知（25好感）且沟通45后，匠人智慧消耗30分钟，一次性获得创造力+1与8直接好感。人类学可提高她的关系加成，但她不在这里教授该技能。

与Robin、Valkyra或Nerida的共同事件需Rose Arthur及对方均达到已遇见（各10好感），时间09:00–17:00；Valkyra还需结束度假。Sophie的独立礼物事件需Rose Arthur达到已遇见（10好感），Sophie可用且已结束度假，时间06:00–21:00。

Ryia的摊位回忆需Ryia在职、已结束度假，且Rose Arthur当前在场；表演舞台反应则属于共同音乐活动中的另一种可能结果。

卡住时检查什么

  • 制作时核对无限地下城战利品的准确名称与数量，普通食材、材料或近似名称的礼物不能代替。
  • 制作只获得成品，之后仍需到目标角色的指定行动交付。
  • 她的菜单没有普通收礼按钮或个人公寓路线；社区事件与制作服务仍可照常推进。
