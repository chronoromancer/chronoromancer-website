概览和时间表

Willow是一位24岁的中世纪治愈者,有着良好的配乐。她的路线始于巷道相遇,通过蘑菇送货和两次交谈继续,并招募她为工人#18。这个指南给出了要求和选择顺序,而没有揭示个人的背影或后来的故事结果。

招聘可以取消避难所的肖像,假期可以取消她一般的家庭存在,她的工人轮班是一个单独的时间表.
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

Overview and Schedule

Willow is a24-year-old Medieval healer with Good alignment. Her route starts with an alley encounter, continues through mushroom delivery and two conversations, and recruits her as worker #18. This guide gives requirements and choice order without revealing personal backstory or later story outcomes.
Favorite stats: Creativity40,Mystic40 and Charm35. Her special relationship skill is Material Science.

Continue exploring the alleys for Glowcap Mushrooms. After the meeting, an eligible priority encounter has a30% roll, after earlier priority encounters; the ordinary encounter pool can also select the mushroom encounter. Choose Carefully collect all the mushrooms for Willow to receive the key item. Leaving them does not collect it.
Deliver the key item through Glowcap Mushrooms at her Sanctuary portrait,6 AM–6 PM. Delivery consumes and permanently records the key item; do not expect another collectible set afterwards. It starts the first conversation below.
