Overview

Officer Becky works at the downtown police station in the Present. Her current route includes early main-story meetings, a seven-scene case investigation, three heart-to-hearts, four optional date outings, book gifts, a stat reward and later intimacy. This guide gives the progression requirements while leaving the case outcome undisclosed.

At Story Beat 7, give a straightforward police statement: the first answer in each of the six choices gives the highest Becky bonus, 3 base points each. At Beat 10's final question, “For you.” gives 5 base points, compared with 3 for the duty answer or 2 for the backup joke. Building Affection

  • Talk is available from Stranger: 15 minutes, 1 base point, three times per day.
  • At Known, Give Book consumes one Book, takes 10 minutes, gives 8 base points and can be used twice per day.
  • At Known and Communication 42, Authority & Boundaries is a one-time 30-minute action giving Strength +1 and 9 direct affection.
  • Her favorite-stat thresholds and Lockpicking improve relationship gains. Lockpicking is her relationship bonus skill; Authority & Boundaries gives Strength rather than teaching that skill.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

After Case Resolution

Use Mystic Seduction becomes available once the case is resolved, with Mystic Seduction learned, Mystic 60 and the paid version. It costs 30 minutes and 30 energy, gives 10 base affection and can be used three times per day. One of three scenes is selected randomly, so repeats can unlock different Extras entries. The selected scene also records 25 or 30 intimacy experience. Becky knowingly accepts the player's aura; the current action leaves her clear-headed.

Supporting Scene and Christmas

An optional shared scene, Blood Owed, Blood Chosen, occurs in Torea's Room, 7 PM–10 PM. It needs Close Friend with Torea, Torea's Boardwalk date, Helsa's A Hunter Bleeds, Torea's Life After Midnight heart-to-heart and Becky currently available. Completion gives Becky 4 base points; Funny supplies her highest choice bonus, 7 base points. See Torea and Helsa for their preceding routes.
With Christmas content enabled, Holiday Cheer for Becky is a one-time location action at the Police Station, 8 AM–6 PM. It requires Acquaintance with Becky and the paid version, takes 30 minutes, gives 10 direct affection and one Christmas Token, and consumes no Christmas Gift. This location action has its own hours, separate from her ordinary NPC shift.

If Progress Stalls

  • Before the investigation, finish Story Beat 35 and reach Known; The Follow-Up needs a visit after 8 PM.
  • Return to the Back Alley for An Empty Warehouse, The Burner Phone and the final case scene, rather than searching the station menu for them.
  • The Blue Thread needs Why the Badge. The Missing Report needs Comiccon Date as well as its prior case scene. The Burner Phone needs After the Shift.
  • Fair Patrol starts 3 PM–5 PM, and After the Shift starts 10 PM–11 PM; these short windows are easy to miss.
  • Closing the Net requires No More Alone, and No More Alone requires Fair Patrol. The Voucher outing does not satisfy this requirement.
  • During the temporary absence, continue at the Back Alley, 8 PM–midnight. Her return follows that case scene.
  • The later case chain needs paid heart-to-hearts and dates. More affection cannot remove those access requirements.
  • Mystic Seduction requires completed case resolution, the skill and Mystic 60; Romantic affection alone does not unlock it.
