怀孕制度

概览

怀孕是一种 选项后期游戏续作 通常的亲密关系不会随意导致怀孕。
此指南涵盖当前系统, 并与游戏对照 2026年10月4日 (中文(简体) ).它列出了要求和机械,但没有说明人物的个人故事结局或家庭场景的内容。

快速启动

  1. 重新授权 怀孕内容代码 在游戏的密码输入屏幕中。关系屏幕然后显示 怀孕时间:; 使用该设置来管理怀孕前的偏好。
  2. 完成 Akira的怀孕议定书说明如下。
  3. 具有符合条件的字符,覆盖 2 000个关系点 和 国家计生委关于这一特性的亲密经验共计500次然后完成她需要的个人进步。
  4. 进入正常的私人/地方接触,选择专注的谈话来尝试婴儿,然后完成这个分支。单满足要求并不能开始怀孕。
  5. 返回字符的里程碑位置 11天时间,完成披露,然后在时间允许时返回最后准备和交付里程碑。
  6. 使用 输入 分娩后,允许 8天的恢复时间;进行中的行动变成 家庭检查.
NPC亲密体验是每个特征的总和. 玩家一般的Erotic Experience是一个不同的值,不能取代500分的要求.请检查她的亲密性信息/淫秽行为,继续现场接触以获得相关的体验. Replaying a Extras scene不能开始怀孕.

解决问题

怀孕选择从未出现

检查设置已解锁并打开, 完成 Akira 的协议,然后检查 她的真爱, 她自己的NPC亲密性总数,以及表格中的所有个人先决条件。Ghost 和Nerida 还要求最后列出的H2H。请输入个人现场接触,而不是额外接触。现有的怀孕、产后康复或已经记录的儿童可以阻止重新开始。

几天过去了,但下一阶段或交付没有发生

在她的时间表中返回正确的里程碑位置。在11岁时,揭发就已就绪;最后进入还需要完成和一个新的日历日。在实际最后阶段需要30天、最后准备完成和准备完成后一天。单靠等待无法替代任何一幕。

今天无法进入

报到或里程碑已经使用过该角色的日常照顾记录。在下一天的比赛中尝试一下。她的常规谈话有自己的正常津贴, 并且仍然分开 。

一个熟悉的日期、教训或分享的场景消失了

检查一下她的目前阶段和动作障碍解释。 早孕和产后恢复可以推迟活动。 康复后返回;已经完成的一次性场景仍将继续完成。

怀孕切换已锁定

至少有一个支持的NPC仍然怀孕,包括隐性怀孕.所有主动怀孕必须到达分娩后才能关闭共同的偏好. 访问不同的时代或非怀孕的NPC无法解锁.

Pregnancy System

Overview

Pregnancy is an optional late-game continuation for twelve named relationships. It begins through a deliberate branch in an eligible private encounter, then follows three milestone scenes, recovery, and ongoing family check-ins. Ordinary intimacy does not randomly cause pregnancy.
This guide covers the current system, checked against the game on 4 October 2026. It lists requirements and mechanics without describing the characters’ personal story endings or the contents of their family scenes.

Quick Start

  1. Redeem the authorized pregnancy-content code in the game’s code-entry screen. The Relationships screen then shows Pregnancy: ON; use that setting to manage the preference before starting a pregnancy.
  2. Complete Akira’s Pregnancy Protocol, described below.
  3. With an eligible character, reach 2,000 relationship points and 500 total NPC intimacy experience for that character, then finish her required personal progression.
  4. Enter her normal private/apartment encounter, choose the dedicated conversation about trying for a baby, and finish that branch. Meeting the requirements alone does not start pregnancy.
  5. Return to the character’s milestone location after 11 days, complete the reveal, then return for the final-preparation and delivery milestones as their timing permits.
  6. Use Check In for daily care and affection. After delivery, allow eight days of recovery; the ongoing action becomes Family Check In.
NPC intimacy experience is a per-character total. The player’s general Erotic Experience is a different value and cannot substitute for the 500-point requirement. Check her intimacy information/gallery and continue her live encounters to earn the relevant experience. Replaying an Extras scene cannot start a pregnancy.

Unlock and Preference

The Pregnancy setting is on the Relationships screen, near its other content toggles. Its four states are:
Setting Meaning
LOCKED Pregnancy entitlement has not been redeemed; changing ordinary relationship progress does not unlock it
OFF Entitlement is available, but new pregnancy content is disabled
ON Entitlement and the preference are both active
ON (LOCKED) At least one supported NPC is pregnant; the preference cannot be switched off until no pregnancies remain
Redeeming the pregnancy-content code initially enables the preference. Akira’s protocol and the relationship requirements remain necessary afterward. A pregnant NPC locks the shared preference, including during the hidden stage. The lock checks pregnancy itself; postpartum recovery is a separate state. Turning the preference off after delivery does not erase the existing child or undo the recorded route.
Pregnancy does not automatically add a route to every romanceable character. Akira provides the protocol but is not one of the twelve candidates. The candidate list below is the implemented roster.

Starting the Deliberate Branch

At the opening of the eligible private/apartment encounter, choose the special branch proposing a baby or family together. Its entry choice is hidden until the shared requirements and her personal readiness checks pass. Continue the authored conversation to its terminal commitment choice; that final choice starts the pregnancy.
The ordinary encounter’s intimacy outcomes do not invoke conception. There is no random conception roll, contraceptive item requirement, or fertility-calendar minigame in this implemented system. Existing private encounters remain usable according to their normal requirements until the pregnancy restrictions below apply.
Each character can currently have one child with the player. Birth does not reset eligibility for a second pregnancy. Separate eligible characters maintain separate records; the system does not impose a single pregnant-character limit across the whole roster.

Stages and Milestone Timing

The conception date is age 0, so “11 days” means eleven complete in-game calendar days after conception. All eras use the game’s shared day progression. Real-world waiting and changing locations do not advance the pregnancy by themselves.
Stage When it applies What to do
Hidden Ages 0–10 Use Check In if desired; normal visible-pregnancy restrictions have not begun, but wine gifts are hidden
Early Age 11 onward, until the final-stage conditions are met Visit the listed location while she is present and complete the reveal scene
Final At least age 31, and at least the day after the reveal Complete the final-preparation scene and allow the final phase to run for 30 days

The reveal is required to enter the final stage. Its start is the later of conception date + 31 and reveal date + 1. The final phase’s 30-day clock starts on that actual entry date, rather than automatically on age 31 if the reveal was delayed.
Delivery requires both clocks and the final-preparation scene. It becomes eligible after 30 days in the final stage, with final preparation completed, and at least one calendar day after that preparation scene. Return to the registered location while the NPC is present to trigger delivery. The game does not deliver the baby just because a countdown has elapsed.

Check In and Family Check In

The character’s ordinary Talk action stays in place. Check In is an additional action, available in hidden, early, final, and postpartum stages. In the family stage its name becomes Family Check In.
  • 30 minutes per successful check-in, with one shared check-in allowance per character per day.
  • 5 direct affection points from the care action; these are not an additional normal Talk multiplier calculation.
  • No configured money, energy, or calm cost.
  • Stage-specific dialogue and presentation, without changing the character’s usual schedule.
  • When a milestone is ready and matches the current location, the check-in can launch that lifecycle scene before charging the time cost.
A reveal, final-preparation, or delivery completion also records a visit. It shares the day’s check-in record, so completing a milestone and then trying Check In on that same day does not provide another care visit. A check-in that crosses midnight is recorded against the day on which it started.
There is no required daily-care streak or minimum prenatal-visit count for delivery. Check-ins provide care scenes and affection; skipping them does not cancel the pregnancy. The required reveal and final-preparation milestones still must be completed.

Shared scenes also check their participants. A pregnancy can postpone a multi-character plot involving her; more affection with another participant will not bypass that block. A missing wine gift is intentional, rather than evidence that the gift inventory disappeared.

Birth, Recovery, and Family

Completing the delivery scene records the child and starts the eight-day postpartum period. No additional care-visit quota is required to end recovery; the stage changes after the shared calendar reaches birth date + 8.
Family status is persistent in the current save, with one child recorded per supported character. Family Check In provides continuing daily care dialogue and affection. It does not reopen the conception branch or reset one-time dates and story scenes.
Delivery also unlocks pregnancy video Extras for Emily, Ghost, and Mindy, subject to the normal Extras/content access. The other nine candidates do not currently have an equivalent video unlock defined; their lifecycle and family content still works.

Troubleshooting

The pregnancy option never appears

Check the setting is unlocked and ON, finish Akira’s protocol, then check her exact affection, her own NPC intimacy total, and every personal prerequisite in the table. Ghost and Nerida also require their final listed H2Hs. Enter the live private encounter rather than Extras replay. Existing pregnancy, postpartum recovery, or an already-recorded child prevents a new start.

Days passed, but the next stage or delivery did not happen

Return to the correct milestone location during her schedule. At age 11 the reveal becomes ready; final entry additionally requires its completion and a new calendar day. Delivery requires 30 days in the actual final stage, final preparation completed, and a day after that preparation. Waiting alone cannot substitute for either scene.

Check In is unavailable today

A check-in or milestone already used that character’s daily care record. Try on the next game day. Her regular Talk has its own normal allowance and remains separate.

A familiar date, lesson, or shared scene disappeared

Check her current stage and the action’s blocker explanation. Early pregnancy and postpartum recovery can postpone the activity. Return after recovery; a one-time scene that was already completed will still remain completed.

The Pregnancy toggle is locked

At least one supported NPC is still pregnant, including hidden pregnancies. All active pregnancies must reach delivery before the shared preference can be switched off. The lock cannot be removed by visiting a different era or a nonpregnant NPC.
