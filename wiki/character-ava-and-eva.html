Ava & Eva — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Ava and Eva are the Rose & Thorn's two masseuses. They share one NPC relationship and one worker record, so the relationship stages and rewards in this guide apply to the pair. Their current route includes recruitment, five personal automatic scenes, two home heart-to-hearts, two dates, supporting scenes with other workers, ordinary worker training, two crafted-gift upgrades and resort activities.

Finding and Recruiting Ava and Eva

  1. Recruit Vixen (worker 19) and keep her active.
  2. Raise brothel reputation to at least 14.
  3. Visit the Brothel Demesne outside the Rose & Thorn. An Impossible Coincidence triggers automatically once its requirements are met; there is no additional configured hour restriction.
  4. Finish the arrival scene. It activates worker 13 and gives 10 base affection plus its choice rewards. Recruitment does not require winning a dungeon encounter.
  5. Enter the Massage Room from the upstairs floor. Its entrance appears with their worker record. Their ordinary interaction schedule is noon–midnight.

Personal Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Use Talk to build the pair's shared affection: 20 minutes, 1 base affection, three times per day.
  2. At Friend, return to the Massage Room between noon and midnight after recruitment to trigger Separate Methods automatically.
  3. At Close Friend, visit the room 6 PM–midnight after Separate Methods for The Favor Nobody Asked For.
  4. After that scene, select A Quiet Interval in the same room at Close Friend, 6 PM–midnight. This one-time H2H costs 60 minutes.
  5. Return noon–midnight, still at Close Friend or higher, after A Quiet Interval for Still Sharing.
  6. Reach Crush and visit 6 PM–midnight after Still Sharing to trigger No Borrowed Answers.
  7. Select Three Honest Answers at Crush, 6 PM–midnight, after both No Borrowed Answers and A Quiet Interval. This one-time H2H costs 60 minutes.
  8. Three Honest Answers also unlocks their first resort Sunset Walk. Complete that walk while they are vacationing, then return them home to qualify for both crafted-gift upgrades at In Love.
The two dates below are additional relationship opportunities; neither is a prerequisite for this home scene chain. Start dates from their Massage Room menu, not by traveling directly to the bazaar or tavern. Their route has no separately authored apartment visit or personal skill lesson.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

If Progress Stalls

  • Their arrival needs active Vixen and brothel reputation 14, outside the brothel; affection alone does not recruit them.
  • The Massage Room entrance needs their worker record. Use noon–midnight for ordinary interactions and the narrower windows for dates/H2Hs.
  • Still Sharing needs the completed A Quiet Interval H2H as well as the preceding scene.
  • Three Honest Answers needs Crush, No Borrowed Answers and A Quiet Interval.
  • Their first resort Sunset Walk needs Three Honest Answers first. Sending them on vacation before that conversation will not unlock it.
  • Known Apart needs both H2Hs; Ours on Purpose needs only Three Honest Answers. Carry the correct crafted gift and return them home before trying either action.
  • Dates and automatic scenes disappear after completion. Talk, encouragement, worker visits and resort actions have separate daily allowances.
