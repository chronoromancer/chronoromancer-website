Overview

Ava and Eva use one relationship entry and shared progress. Their route alternates automatic massage-room scenes and two evening heart-to-hearts; the optional dates and resort conversation are additional activities.

Where to find them

Their arrival at the Rose & Thorn entrance requires active Vixen staff and business Reputation 14. Once they join, their portrait is in Massage Room daily 12:00–24:00, except during resort vacations.

Route and relationship milestones

Follow Separate Methods → The Favor Nobody Asked For → A Quiet Interval → Still Sharing → No Borrowed Answers → Three Honest Answers. The two heart-to-hearts start 18:00–24:00 and take 60 min each.

What usually blocks the next step

  • For the arrival, use business Reputation 14, not a guild’s Clout, and check Vixen is active staff.
  • The second heart-to-heart needs both the first one and the preceding room scene.
  • The cowgirl gift needs both Three Honest Answers and the resort sunset walk; the oral gift needs Three Honest Answers only.

概览

Ava和Eva共用一个关系条目和进度。个人路线在按摩室自动事件与两段晚间交心之间推进；可选约会及度假交心是额外内容。

去哪里找

她们的到访在玫瑰与荆棘入口触发，要求Vixen已启用为员工、青楼声誉14。加入后，每天12:00–24:00在按摩室，度假期间除外。

路线与关系进展

依次完成各有章法→没人开口的帮忙→片刻宁静→依然同住→各自的真心话→三份真心答复。两次交心均在18:00–24:00开始，各60分钟。

偏好的属性为魅力、能力、吸引力。学会密宗技巧可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 到访要求的是青楼声誉14，不是公会声望；还要检查Vixen是否在职。
  • 第二次交心需要第一次交心及前一段按摩室事件。
  • 女上位礼物升级需要真心答复交心及度假日落散步；口交升级只需真心答复交心。
