概览

Muramasa 是一个 27岁的中世纪卡塔纳大师她训练在 骑士训练场晚上在医院过夜 旅行客栈,以后可以与 妓院培训室她的个人进步包括: 5次早期自动相遇, 三颗心对心, 两个日期, 三个后来的故事行动, 作战训练,以及 两次单独的可重复的私人活动.
穿行会给出要求和奖励选择,同时将故事场景的启示留给游戏.

Muramasa Walkthrough and Blademaster Guide
Spoiler warning: This guide covers Muramasa's character plots, permanent training, Blademaster combat path, cross-character events, and Sword Stone bond.
This guide expands on Sh1n7's community-created [Muramasa wiki page](https://chronoromancer.miraheze.org/wiki/Character:Muramasa), used under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). All requirements and mechanical effects below were rechecked against the current game source.

Overview

Muramasa is a 27-year-old Medieval katana master. She trains at the Knights' Training Grounds, spends her evenings at the Travelers' Inn, and can later work with the brothel Training Room. Her personal progression includes five early automatic encounters, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, three later story actions, combat training, and two separate repeatable private activities.
The walkthrough gives the requirements and reward choices while leaving the story scenes' revelations for the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Knights' Training Grounds, 8 AM–6 PM, for The Blind Swordswoman. Choose Kind for the largest introduction reward, then use Talk to build affection.
  2. At Acknowledged, return to the grounds for The Empty Ring. Choose Caring or Flirty. Katana Training is also available at this stage.
  3. Get Muramasa and Kent to Known, then enter the Travelers' Inn, 8–11 PM, for An Arrangement at the Inn. Flirty maximizes Muramasa's bonus. At Known, Share Wine is a useful daily affection action.
  4. At Acquaintance, complete Listen Before Moving at the grounds. Perception 40 improves its second-round answer. At Friend, this unlocks Dinner Without Casualties, starting at the Inn 8–9 PM.
  5. Get Amelia and Kent to Acknowledged, complete Amelia's introduction, and have Muramasa at Friend. With Muramasa and Kent home from vacation, enter Amelia's Healer's Hut, 8 AM–7 PM, for The Old Healer's Oath. Return to the Inn for The Measure of Mercy, after Listen Before Moving.
  6. At Close Friend, start A Blade at Rest from her Inn portrait, after The Measure of Mercy. This opens The Road With No Job at the grounds 8 AM–3 PM, once the dinner date is also complete.
  7. At Crush, complete Between Heartbeats at the grounds, after the road date. Charm 45 improves its second-round answer. At Romantic, start A Place in the Silence at the Inn to unlock her private activities.
  8. For her brothel content, enter the Training Room, 6–8 PM, after The Empty Ring, with Muramasa Acknowledged. Later, at Close Friend and after A Blade at Rest, choose A Wider Ring there to unlock the room's apprentice progression.
  9. For combat, learn Stormslash at Crush and Ability 22. To acquire Blademaster, reach Close Friend and Fighters Guild reputation 200, and prepare 150 Energy and 100 Calm for the five-hour lesson.
The dinner and healer/H2H branches can be completed in either order when their own requirements are met. A Blade at Rest is the bridge into the road date. The Training Room branch is separate from the romantic private-room unlock.

Sense Mastery and Combat Training

Sense Mastery

At Known, with Communication 45, request Sense Mastery at the Knights' Training Grounds, 8 AM–6 PM. It costs 30 minutes, grants Perception +1 and 8 direct affection, and completes once. It has no configured money, Energy, Calm or gift cost. It is a one-time stat reward rather than a repeatable lesson or a skill named Sense Mastery.

Katana Training

At Acknowledged, train at the grounds for 75 minutes, $150, 40 Energy and 10 Calm. Each successful session grants Perception +1, Ability +1 and 1 base affection, up to three sessions daily.
All three sessions cost 225 minutes, $450, 120 Energy and 30 Calm in total and grant Perception +3, Ability +3 and 3 base affection. Prepare recovery between sessions if needed. The fourth attempt gives −1 base affection and no training stats.

Stormslash

At Crush, with Ability 22 and Stormslash not already learned, the grounds offer Stormslash for 240 minutes, $600, 80 Energy and 40 Calm. The lesson teaches Stormslash and gives 15 base affection. It disappears once the skill is learned. There is no configured paid-version requirement on this lesson.

Blademaster

At Close Friend, with Fighters Guild reputation 200, request Blademaster at the grounds while the path is not already acquired. It costs 300 minutes, 150 Energy and 100 Calm, with no configured money cost.
The resulting Blademaster Path scene gives 30 fixed base affection, plus Caring 5, Flirty 4 or Funny 3. Caring makes 35 base. Completion acquires the Blademaster path at zero path points, which is rank 1, Novice Blade. It becomes unavailable after acquisition.
The path advances through combat rather than repeating Muramasa's acquisition scene: basic Attack and Power Strike award Blademaster progress after it is acquired. Combat-path rank determines its multiplier; rank 1 is 1.15× and the early rank thresholds are 0, 100, 300 and 700 points. The path is separate from the Stormslash lesson.

Follow Her Upstairs — Daily Animated Scene

At Romantic, after A Place in the Silence, select Follow Her Upstairs from Muramasa's Inn portrait, 8–11 PM. It requires the paid version, costs 60 minutes and 40 Energy, and is available once daily.
The encounter gives 50 base affection, 60 player Erotic experience, and 30 NPC intimacy experience for missionary. Its animated scene is present in the game assets. These rewards belong to this scene's active handler and intimacy metadata; the longer room visit has different rewards.
This video's daily allowance is separate from Visit Muramasa's Room. Completing one does not spend the other's use.

Visit Muramasa's Room — Branching Private Evening

At Romantic, after A Place in the Silence, the paid version offers Visit Muramasa's Room remotely from the Travelers' Inn, 8 PM–midnight. It costs 120 minutes and 40 Energy and is once daily, with a recorded daily use. This remote window lasts later than her ordinary 8–11 PM portrait.
The visit contains 39 possible rounds across several branches, rather than 39 rounds on every route. Every completed branch awards 150 base affection at its ending. The earlier choices grant zero affection and choose the route. Romantic adds 25 final affection at completion. The configured end-of-visit intimacy event records the encounter without adding another experience reward.
There are no additional stat, skill or gift gates within these choices. These private-room branches have no configured player Erotic experience award; NPC intimacy experience depends on the selected branch.

Sword Stone Bonding

If you already own the Sword Stone and have Muramasa In Love, Intimate Bonding appears at her grounds portrait, 8 AM–6 PM, and her unlocked Training Room portrait, 6–8 PM.
It costs 60 minutes, increases the Sword Stone level by 1, gives zero ordinary affection, and is once daily shared across those locations. It has no configured money, Energy, Calm or consumable cost. The stone remains in the key-item inventory.
The stone adds one damage per level to basic Attack and Quick Attack. Its acquisition belongs to the wider story; the bonding action requires possession and does not itself grant the stone.

Completion Checklist

For Muramasa's configured content, complete the five early automatic encounters, Sense Mastery, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, Blademaster, Stormslash, and the three later story actions. Katana Training, Talk and Wine remain daily activities. Her two private activities each have their own daily allowance, and the branching visit offers eight route/ending variants.
Sword Stone bonding is optional once its key item is available. Muramasa has no configured Kupie album, invite minigame, pregnancy route, personal Christmas scene, maid progression or personal resort vacation sequence.
