Vera——完全走过

概览

Vera是现时代下城酒吧的竞技赌徒,她的走法结合了爱情,积累的网赌赢家,两段对话,一个棋盘行走日期和酒店内容,她的路线的个人启示留给游戏.

净赌博赢家

Vera的净胜负要求是指在Darts,Lail's Dice,Hold'em和Hlack这四场比赛中获得的合并履带钱,减去合并履带钱在这四场比赛中的损失. 它是一个累积的跟踪器,而不是当前的财富,毛胜率,需要的赢数或爱意。 损失可以再次将先前达到的要求推向无法达到的境地。
使用 玩飞镖 从她的肖像或 玩表格游戏 。表格游戏在酒吧的夜间窗口中提供;其菜单本身没有时间成本,而单个回合/手提前时间。

Vera — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Vera is the Downtown Bar's competitive gambler in the Present era. Her walkthrough combines affection, accumulated net gambling winnings, two conversations, a boardwalk date and hotel content. Her route's personal revelations are left to the game.

Net Gambling Winnings

Vera's net winnings requirement means the combined tracked money won in Darts, Liar's Dice, Hold'em and Blackjack, minus the combined tracked money lost in those four games. It is a cumulative tracker, not current wealth, gross winnings, a required number of wins or affection. Losses can push a previously reached requirement out of reach again.
Use Play Darts from her portrait or Play Table Games from the bar's location menu. Table Games is available during the bar's nighttime window; its menu itself has no time cost, while individual rounds/hands advance time.
Darts costs 30 minutes and $50 per match. The game starts at369, gives three darts per turn and requires a double to finish at exactly zero. Going below zero, reaching one, or finishing without a double busts the turn. A win awards 10 base affection, pays $100 and adds $100 to tracked money won. A loss awards 1 base affection and tracks $50 lost. Thus a win's wallet gain after the entry fee is $50, although the route tracker rises by $100. Its configured limit is20 matches per day; exceeding an action's limit can incur a negative response.
The table games also update Vera's gambling trackers. In Blackjack, the win tracker records the payout minus the bet rather than the whole payout; a push changes neither wins nor losses. Choose bets you can afford and check the net requirement again after a losing session.

Hotel Content

Back to the Hotel

This is the reachable repeatable video action on Vera's bar portrait. It requires Romantic+, net winnings3000+ and the paid version. It costs 180 minutes, $500, Energy50 and Calm20, with a one-per-day limit. The handler awards 40 base affection before modifiers; the scene records 25 NPC Intimacy Experience in Missionary. No player Erotic Experience award is configured for it. There is no separate Higher Power prerequisite.

Night with Vera: current availability limitation

A separate Night with Vera apartment-style action is defined for the Love Hotel, 8 PM–midnight, with Romantic+, net5000+ and paid-version access. Its listed cost is 180 minutes, $500 and Energy65, once per day.
The current character schedule places Vera only at the Downtown Bar. Ordinary NPC actions are collected from characters present at the location, and this hotel action is not a remote action. Moving to the hotel through When the Tables Turn does not add Vera to the hotel's schedule. Consequently, meeting the listed requirements does not currently make this separate action appear through the normal location interface. Use Back to the Hotel for her reachable repeatable hotel content.
For completeness, the authored conversation has13 numbered nodes with a choice hub: arrival, anticipation and the initial kiss give10 points each; Oral, Missionary, Cowgirl and Doggy paths return to the hub and give15 points per completed path. Choosing to finish gives15 points before the final150-point ending. Its written reward is therefore 195 base points plus15 per completed position path, rather than a fixed60-point action award. Each completed position path records25 NPC Intimacy Experience; the first kiss records5. The ending counts one encounter after tracked intimacy has occurred and gives no additional NPC experience. No player Erotic Experience award is defined for this conversation.

Intimacy Gallery

Vera currently has three gallery images in the game's NPC intimacy rewards folder. They unlock at 100, 250 and500 NPC Intimacy Experience. This experience is separate from player Erotic Experience and ordinary relationship affection.
