Jessie — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Jessie is the tenant in your Present Uptown basement apartment. Her route begins with a rental listing and a café encounter, then develops through gym recognition, listening, a personal Heart-to-Heart, gifts and dates. This guide covers her current requirements, rewards and repeatable activities while leaving the personal story outcomes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Go through your Present Uptown home → Living Room → Basement and choose Rent Out Apartment, once, for 30 minutes. Then visit Café Exterior for A Girl in Need. Completing the scene gives 10 base Jessie affection and $300, and opens Visit Jessie in the Basement.
  2. Visit her room during 2–11 PM. Talk, Chocolate, Flowers and Teddy require Known. If these are still locked, build 10 Gym clout, then return for Jessie Notices Your Gains, which has no separate affection requirement. Its best reply is Flirty, for 18 total base points.
  3. After gym recognition, choose Listen to Jessie, for 60 minutes and 30 Energy. It does not add a Friend requirement. The best sequence is Caring → Flirty → Caring, for 22 total base points.
  4. After Listen to Jessie, complete Closer, for 60 minutes and 30 Energy. Its best answers total 84 base points, with 5 Jessie NPC intimacy experience for the final kissing round. There is no separate minimum affection stage on this H2H.
  5. At Friend, revisit her room for What Jessie Really Needs, and take her Zoo, Fair and separate Voucher date in their starting windows. Each date completes once and has its own completion.
  6. For the following evening encounter, carry one Wine, learn Alcohol Tolerance, finish What Jessie Really Needs, and visit 7–10 PM. Jessie's Rebound consumes the Wine and advances four hours after completion. Listen to Jessie and Closer are not additional gates to this evening encounter.
  7. At Close Friend, use Give Wine and, after building Diane's Boardwalk and unlocking the supporter content, take Boardwalk Sunset Date, starting 5–8 PM from her room.
  8. At Crush, after Listen to Jessie and Closer, use Invite Date at the Downtown KTV entrance or Night Club, starting 8–11 PM. These dates repeat with venue-specific daily limits.
  9. At 400 ordinary affection, use Intimate Time in her room with paid-version access. For her separate Kupie progression, reach Friend in the main game and Vera level 5 in Kupie, then meet the costs below.
The gym/listening branch and the evening encounter branch share gym recognition, but Closer does not require Jessie's Rebound, and the rebound encounter does not require Closer. The room dates use their own stage requirements. KTV and Night Club invitations specifically need both Listen to Jessie and Closer.

Meeting Jessie and Visiting Her Room

Rent Out Apartment appears in the Basement of your Present home. It takes 30 minutes, once, and has no money or Energy charge configured. After listing, visit Café Exterior for the automatic first meeting. There is no extra day-wait requirement in the encounter's gate.
Completing the café scene opens Visit Jessie in the Basement. Travel into and out of her room takes zero minutes. Both the visit action and her portrait use 2–11 PM daily. The visit's explanatory message mentions 10 PM, but the actual configured closing boundary is 11 PM.
The $300 café payment is a one-time completion reward. Her rented basement room is her authored home route; it does not use the separate apartment invitation conversations available for some other NPCs.

Intimate Time

At 400 ordinary Jessie affection, use Intimate Time from her room portrait during 2–11 PM. It requires paid-version access, costs 45 minutes, 40 Energy and 30 Calm, and repeats once per game day.
It gives 50 base affection, 60 player Erotic experience, and 25 Jessie NPC intimacy experience, missionary classification. The action does not add Wine, money, Closer, or rebound-night completion as separate requirements. Those other activities keep their own requirements and counters.

Christmas Wine Activity

During the active Christmas Village task event, Wine with Jessie is Task 4, in Jessie's Room. Reach Close Friend, carry one Wine, and choose it as a location activity. It costs 30 minutes, consumes the Wine, and completes once for one holiday Token and 10 direct Jessie affection, plus 25 Jessie NPC intimacy experience, special classification.
There is no extra hour restriction on the holiday action itself; normal entry into her room still uses Visit Jessie, 2–11 PM. This is separate from Give Wine and Jessie's Rebound, and does not add a paid-version gate of its own.

Troubleshooting

  • No Visit Jessie: choose Rent Out Apartment in the Basement, then complete A Girl in Need at Café Exterior. Return during 2–11 PM.
  • Talk and gifts locked: they start at Known. Her 10 Gym clout recognition, then Listen to Jessie and Closer, provide progression without adding a Known gate to those scenes.
  • Listen to Jessie missing: complete Jessie Notices Your Gains, then open her room portrait. The scene is manual.
  • Closer missing: complete Listen to Jessie; the separate evening rebound scene is not its prerequisite.
  • Evening encounter missing: finish What Jessie Really Needs, learn Alcohol Tolerance, carry Wine, and visit 7–10 PM.
  • Fair Date missing late afternoon: its starting window is only 2–3 PM. The separate Voucher date runs 2–6 PM.
  • Boardwalk date locked: check Close Friend, Diane's construction, paid access and the recent supporter code.
  • Jessie absent from Invite Date: reach Crush, complete Listen to Jessie and Closer, then use KTV or Night Club, 8–11 PM. Night Club additionally needs the latest supporter code.
  • Perfect invitation ending missing: give a +1 gift and answer all five questions correctly. Night Club Flowers do not add score.
  • Invite Date already used: its allowance is shared across all NPCs at that venue, not five uses or one per NPC.
  • Intimate Time locked: check 400 ordinary affection, paid access and that animation's daily allowance.
  • Kupie Jessie missing: reach main-game Friend and Vera Kupie level 5, with paid selection enabled.
