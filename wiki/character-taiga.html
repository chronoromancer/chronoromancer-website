概览

Taiga是 赫斯伍德住宅的银色虎女,在可选 野心:野林 她目前的个人路线包括: 两个自动场景和每日谈话。本指南涵盖所有目前注册的个人内容,但不设定以后的路径。

地点和可用性

  1. 启用 野心:野林 主菜单中 Mod 管理器。在更改其启用状态后重新启动。
  2. 执行 野心赎回要求。启用和赎回的访问是单独的检查。
  3. 在那个 深层森林编辑 进入野生森林 到达野生森林网关。
  4. 用世界地图访问 赫斯伍德住宅区,然后选择 Taiga 的肖像。
网关 回到深林区 动作离开世界 。这两个出入口动作目前配置了零时间成本,尽管它们有更古老的60分钟描述。

基础奖励在成为最终爱人之前使用共享关系计算. Taiga没有配置自己最喜爱的-stat或特殊技能乘数. 设备和共享修饰器仍然可以影响结果.
每日津贴之后,管理者记录 负面奖励 而非另一个正用 。在下个游戏日返回。 高级关系阶段会改变她的普通对话框,但不会添加另一个注册动作 。

Overview

Taiga is the silver tiger woman at Hearthwood Residences, in the optional Wildheart: Wild Woods content. Her current personal route consists of two automatic scenes and daily Talk. This guide covers all currently registered personal content without inventing a later route.

Location and Availability

  1. Enable Wildheart: Wild Woods in the main menu's Mod Manager. Restart after changing its enabled state.
  2. Fulfill the Wildheart redeem requirement. Enabled and redeemed access are separate checks.
  3. In the Deep Woods, use Enter Wild Woods to reach the Wild Woods Gateway.
  4. Use the world's map to visit Hearthwood Residences, then select Taiga's portrait.
The Gateway's Return to Deep Woods action leaves the world. These two entrance/exit actions currently configure zero time cost, despite their older 60-minute descriptions.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Enter Hearthwood Residences for A Door and a Straight Answer, which triggers automatically once at any hour without an affection prerequisite.
  2. Choose Caring → Funny for 3 +3 choice base and 5 completion base =11 total base affection.
  3. Use daily Talk to reach Known, 25 ordinary affection. The introduction plus Talk are sufficient; no gift or another resident's scene is required.
  4. Revisit Hearthwood Residences with the introduction complete for Room for Another Pair of Hands.
  5. Choose Caring → Caring or Flirty for 3 +3 choice base and 8 completion base =14 total base affection.
Both scenes run once and have no additional configured entry fee, action time, Energy cost or gift consumption. Choices converge on completion; lower-point replies do not lock out the follow-up scene.

Daily Talk

Select Talk from Taiga's Hearthwood Residences portrait. It takes 20 minutes, and the active handler records 1 actual base affection, even though the action definition contains a zero-point placeholder. There are five positive uses daily: 100 minutes for all five.
Base rewards use the shared relationship calculation before becoming final affection. Taiga has no configured favorite-stat or special-skill multiplier of her own. Equipment and shared modifiers can still affect results.
After the daily allowance, the handler records a negative reward rather than another positive use. Return on the next game day. Higher relationship stages change her ordinary dialogue but do not add another registered action.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Room for Another Pair of Hands is the end of Taiga's current registered personal story. Daily Talk remains available afterward. Her current definition has no personal gift action, lesson, date, heart-to-heart, apartment route, worker recruitment/training, resort vacation or NPC intimacy gallery assets.
  • Wild Woods entrance is missing: check Mod Manager enabled status and redeemed access; restart after changing the enabled state.
  • Taiga is missing: visit Hearthwood Residences in Wild Woods, rather than another world's housing area.
  • Follow-up scene is missing: complete A Door and a Straight Answer, reach Known and revisit the residences.
  • No further romance activity appears: the current route is the two scenes and Talk above; affection alone does not create an additional authored activity.
