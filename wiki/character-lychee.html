  • 在另一地点的礼物将受到惩罚: 每个礼物类型的计数器都是在所有站点共享的;移动不会重置.
  • 缺少美容演示文稿( P) : 打开她招募的下午肖像 检查 来文 45 和之前的奖励完成。

  • 对话后清理级别不会上升 : 女佣的经验来自 每日清洁服务与爱无关
  • Frost Crystal在 Honey 级别 10 之后丢失 : 索赔Honey,然后在Lychee上装备奖励。

Lychee — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Lychee is a fashionable maid in the Medieval era. Before recruitment she follows a daily route through six locations, carrying the same Talk, gift and invitation actions with her. At Crush and Rose & Thorn ownership, she accepts an invitation as maid #3, then appears in Maids Quarters, 2–6 PM.
Her current walkthrough includes recruitment, daily conversation, a one-time Charm lesson, repeatable Maid Service, eight shared automatic scenes and cleaning progression. The instructions give practical requirements and rewards without retelling the sisterhood's later discoveries.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Find her on the daily route below, between 7 AM and 1 PM. Her action menu is the same at all six stops.
  2. Build 400 ordinary affection / Crush with Talk and gifts. Jewelry gives the highest gift base reward, 10, followed by Perfume 8, Flowers 6 and Chocolate 5. All four are available from Stranger before recruitment.
  3. Obtain Rose & Thorn ownership, through The King's appointment route if you have not done so. At Crush, choose Invite to Brothel from any pre-recruitment stop. It takes 10 minutes, with no extra gold or Energy cost.
  4. Once she accepts, visit Maids Quarters, 2–6 PM for her new portrait and Lychee's Arrival, while Elara is not vacationing.
  5. At Known and Communication 45, claim Beauty & Presentation for Charm +1 and 10 direct affection, once. Recruitment already requires a higher stage, so check Communication after inviting her.
  6. Follow the shared-scene table with the other maids recruited. These scenes use their own times, including early morning or late evening while Lychee's ordinary portrait is absent.
  7. At Intimate, 1,600 ordinary affection, use Maid Service during her 2–6 PM portrait window. Keep maid cleaning levels and equipment separate from this affection route.

Recruiting Lychee

Choose Invite to Brothel during any of her six pre-recruitment windows. The action appears while the brothel is owned, even at Stranger, but she accepts only at Crush or at least 400 ordinary affection. Inviting earlier produces a polite refusal; it does not substitute for the affection requirement.
The invitation costs 10 minutes and awards no extra affection. It does not charge a recruitment fee, consume a gift, require a personal date, H2H, Fashion or a particular other maid. Her action lists three invitations daily, but the current invitation handler does not record invitation uses; repeating an invitation still cannot bypass the acceptance threshold.
Acceptance recruits maid #3, updates the Maids Quarters room level to the number of recruited maids, capped at six, and moves her ordinary schedule to the brothel. It does not put her into the regular worker-training roster or require a prebuilt third-level maid room.

Beauty & Presentation

At the Maids Quarters portrait, 2–6 PM, reach Known and Communication 45, then choose Beauty & Presentation. It completes once, takes 30 minutes, and gives Charm +1 and 10 direct Lychee affection.
There is no money, Energy, Calm, gift or extra quest requirement configured. The direct affection is not multiplied. This is a Charm-stat lesson and does not teach Fashion.

Maid Service

At Intimate, 1,600 ordinary affection, choose Maid Service from her recruited Maids Quarters portrait, 2–6 PM. It costs 30 minutes, with no configured money, Energy, Calm or consumable cost.
This is a repeatable illustrated H2H with one round. Three presentation variants all lead to the same answer, “I'd love your special attention, Lychee.”, for 15 base affection. The action's 15-point field is not a second reward.
The shared completion adds 25 final affection if you know Romantic, separately from the ordinary relationship calculation and independently of the relationship stage. The service marks its EXTRAS entry; Extras replay does not award affection.
The menu advertises three services per day, but the current service handler launches the repeatable H2H without recording uses or checking a daily service counter. That advertised limit is not enforced by this handler. Her normal portrait hours and time cost still apply.
No NPC intimacy experience, player Erotic experience, separate looping video, apartment visit, pregnancy route or shared-scene completion prerequisite is configured for this service.

Maid Cleaning Progression

After recruitment Lychee contributes to daily cleaning. Her maid level grows through cleaning experience, independently of ordinary affection. Lychee's Frost Crystal

This epic equipment is exclusive to maid #3. Obtain it by reaching Honey level 10 in Kupie's minigame, then claiming Honey Maxed in the achievement panel. Lychee does not have a separate Kupie date roster entry.
While equipped it gives +30% Lychee cleaning, +3 to the combined maid cleaning total, and +20 percentage points to the cleaning-variance range. The current daily random modifier is normally 50–150%; with this item's variance bonus alone it becomes 70–170%. Its +3 is a single addition to combined cleaning, rather than +3 separately for each recruited maid.
This item does not award ordinary affection, maid experience directly, or unlock Maid Service. Equip it through the maid-management equipment controls.

Troubleshooting

  • Lychee missing before recruitment: follow the one-hour stops from 7 AM to 1 PM; she moves each hour.
  • She refuses the invitation despite the button appearing: own the brothel and reach Crush / 400 ordinary affection. The action is visible earlier than its acceptance threshold.
  • Her portrait vanished after accepting: recruitment moves her to Maids Quarters, 2–6 PM, and removes the six earlier schedules.
  • Gifts missing after recruitment: her recruited portrait has Talk, Maid Service and Beauty & Presentation, with no ordinary gift actions.
  • Talking gives less than the action's point field suggests: the current handler records 1 base affection, before modifiers.
  • A gift in another location causes a penalty: every gift type's counter is shared across all stops; moving does not reset it.
  • Beauty & Presentation missing: open her recruited afternoon portrait and check Communication 45 and prior reward completion.
  • Shared scene missing: check every named maid's recruitment and affection, the room, the scene's own time and its earlier-scene prerequisites. Elara must be at home for Arrival.
  • Maid Service missing: reach Intimate / 1,600, then use her 2–6 PM portrait. Fashion or the shared scenes are not extra gates.
  • Cleaning level does not rise after conversations: maid experience comes from daily cleaning, independently of affection.
  • Frost Crystal missing after Honey level 10: claim Honey Maxed, then equip the reward on Lychee.
Her current personal route has no separate personal date, apartment route, regular worker-training route, resort vacation, Kupie date entry or pregnancy continuation. This guide covers recruitment, personal actions and all eight registered shared encounters.
