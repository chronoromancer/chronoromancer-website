当前路线边界

Motley在目前的角色定义中没有普通的礼物菜单、个人付费日期、公寓探访、妓院-工人培训或怀孕路线。 皇后奖 Capstone 已被移除; 增加亲情不会恢复它们.目前的材料包括上面的四个心对心,个人自动场景和联盟续作.

  • 无聊天按钮 : 到达 知识 通过早期和支持辛迪加的场景 奖励第一。
  • 对话收益与元数据不同: 实际操作者奖 1个基点,尽管动作元数据列出为零。每日使用5次后停止;进一步尝试记录-1并使用共享的超限处理。

  • 缺少一分钟: 到达密友并完成无观众。
  • 失踪的苏门斯: 完成Tell和Zodiac的幕后,然后到达Crush.

Motley

Overview

Motley leads the Chaos Syndicate in the Future Prime Sector. Her current route includes the Syndicate's introductory missions, a personal relationship story, a Comedy Timing lesson, three sequenced personal Heart-to-Hearts, and an independent alliance follow-up. Older wiki links and some game identifiers use Harlequin; the current displayed name is Motley.
Her background revelations and later story outcomes are left for you to discover. This walkthrough concentrates on access, relationship stages, locations, prerequisites and rewards.

Meeting Motley and Unlocking Talk

  1. In the Future Prime Sector, travel to Syndicate Gates. The Gates of Chaos starts automatically once and awards 3 base Motley points, alongside Bayne and Poison rewards.
  2. Visit The Garden after the prologue for Street Knowledge, worth another 3 base Motley points.
  3. Travel through The Den or The Big Top → The Parlor → Motley's Lair. These internal travel actions take 0 minutes. The Lair is available at every hour; her ordinary schedule has no additional story or relationship flag gate.
  4. Welcome to the Circus starts on your first eligible Lair visit. It awards 1 base Motley point. Choose “I don't plan on being suspicious. I plan on being impressive.” for +4, giving 5 base points in total.
  5. Continue Zodiac's collection thread and Kitty's early missions: Collecting Dues - The Bar → New Blood → Taming the Rookie. The Rookie scene starts at the Prime Sector Transportation Hub, 7 AM–5 PM. Return to the Lair after both Taming the Rookie and Welcome to the Circus for The Boss Takes Notice, worth 3 base Motley points.
  6. Complete Cat's Eyes at The Big Top and Street Knowledge, then visit The Parlor after The Boss Takes Notice for Lines on the Board. Its 3 base completion points, plus Flirty +3 → Flirty +3, give a maximum of 9 base Motley points.
  7. At Known, check The Den, 6–9 AM, for The Interview: 1 base completion point, with Flirty +4 as its best reply.
  8. Continue the supporting Syndicate scenes below until Motley reaches Acquaintance. Then use Talk at the Lair: 15 minutes, 1 actual base point, five times per game day.
The initial rewards do not guarantee Acquaintance without bonuses. There is no Stranger-level Talk or ordinary gift action; early progress comes from automatic Syndicate scenes. Silver Tongue and her favorite stats improve applicable gains but are not entry requirements for these introductions.

Personal Relationship Walkthrough

The personal automatic scenes and personal Heart-to-Hearts are two related tracks. No Audience does not require The Observation, and One Honest Minute does not require The Tell. Both still need their own affection stages.
  1. At Friend, visit the Lair for The Observation, worth 10 base completion points. The highest two bonuses are Cunning +3 → Skeptic +3, for 16 base points total.
  2. At Friend and Creativity 16, learn Comedy Timing if you do not already know it. The lesson costs 150 minutes, $250, 30 energy and 20 calm, teaches the skill and awards 12 base Motley points. It disappears once learned.
  3. At Friend, choose No Audience for the first personal Heart-to-Heart. It costs 60 minutes and 10 energy, with no money payment.
  4. At Close Friend, return after The Observation for The Tell, worth 15 base points, with Funny +3 → Funny +3 for a best total of 21. Complete One Honest Minute after No Audience; this second Heart-to-Heart costs 60 minutes and 10 energy.
  5. Progress the broader Syndicate plot through Invisible, then The Honeypot, which requires Rogue 55 at the Lair. Progress Zodiac's route through The Necessary Evil and The Confession; the latter requires Zodiac Crush at her booth.
  6. After The Honeypot and The Confession, visit the Lair for The Game. It gives 3 base points plus four scored replies. Clever → Strategic → Cunning → Patient gives +4 each, for 19 base points total.
  7. At Motley Friend, return after The Game and The Confession for The Reckoning: 15 base Motley points and 5 base Zodiac points. Loyal +5 → Cocky +4 gives the highest Motley total, 24 base points.
  8. Visit Fortune Teller's Booth after The Reckoning for Behind the Curtain. It supplies the flag needed for Motley's next personal scene.
  9. At Crush, visit the Lair after The Tell and Behind the Curtain for The Summons. Then visit The Den for The Fascination. These are one-time scenes with no additional hour restriction.
  10. At Romantic, visit the Lair after The Fascination for The Dissection, then return after it for The Truth. Each requires its preceding scene completed.
  11. After The Truth and One Honest Minute, while Motley is Romantic, choose The Last Inch at the Lair. It costs 90 minutes and 20 energy, and restores 10 calm.
No Audience and One Honest Minute do not substitute for the automatic plot flags. Conversely, completing The Truth does not skip the preceding Heart-to-Hearts needed for The Last Inch. The current personal route ends with these authored scenes; older explicit encounter actions and their former capstone are retired.

Current Route Boundaries

Motley has no ordinary gift menu, personal paid date, apartment visit, brothel-worker training or pregnancy route in the current character definition. Troubleshooting

  • No Talk button: reach Acquaintance through early and supporting Syndicate scene rewards first.
  • Talk gain differs from metadata: the actual handler awards 1 base point, despite the action metadata listing zero. Stop after five daily uses; further attempts record −1 and use shared over-limit handling.
  • Comedy Timing missing: reach Friend and Creativity 16, and check whether you already learned the skill.
  • No Audience missing: it requires Friend, but does not require The Observation.
  • One Honest Minute missing: reach Close Friend and finish No Audience.
  • The Summons missing: finish The Tell and Zodiac's Behind the Curtain, then reach Crush.
  • The Last Inch missing: reach Romantic and complete both The Truth and One Honest Minute.
  • A Place to Return missing: complete Our War and reach Friend. The romance chain is not an additional gate.
  • Alliance missing: complete both Beat 85 and After the Line, then start at Operations Center. The six follow-up locations must be visited in order.
