概览

Kuro 运行 未来总理区音乐广播中心。她有长长的黑色头发,上面有紫罗兰的亮点、操作耳机和她的作品的干线。她现在的关系路线包括: 11次自动相遇,1次首演场景,3次插图"心对心",2次约会,图书礼物和感官奖.
行走过程解释了实际路线,同时将后来的广播调查以及她的最终决定留给故事.

Overview

Kuro operates the Mood Regulation Audio Broadcasting Center in Future Prime Sector. She has long black hair with violet highlights, an operator headset and a dry view of her work. Her current relationship route includes eleven automatic encounters, one first-performance scene, three illustrated Heart-to-Hearts, two dates, Book gifts and a Perception reward.
The walkthrough explains the practical route while leaving the later broadcast investigation and her final decisions to the story.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Mood Regulation Audio Broadcasting Center, 10 AM–10 PM, for Optimal Misery. Talk works from Stranger, up to three times per day.
  2. Reach Acknowledged and return for Baseline Error. At Known, return for Noise Between Notes and begin giving Books, up to three times daily.
  3. Add at least one composed song to your song list. At Acquaintance, return after Noise Between Notes for Authored Signal.
  4. Choose Perform for Kuro from her portrait and select a composed song. The first successful performance starts Private Performance. At Friend, return afterward for The Afterimage. Frequency Analysis and Neon Bazaar Date are optional branches.
  5. At Close Friend, return after The Afterimage for Control Group. Choose Silence After the Song, then return for The Compliance Chord.
  6. At Crush, return after The Compliance Chord for Open Channel. Glass Reef Date opens separately after it, with an effective portrait start window of 10 AM–6 PM.
  7. At Romantic, choose The Hurt Problem after Open Channel and the first H2H. Return afterward for The Cost of Feeling.
  8. At Intimate, return after The Cost of Feeling for Counter-Frequency. Complete Constant Frequency after Counter-Frequency and The Hurt Problem.
  9. At In Love, return after Counter-Frequency and Constant Frequency for Constant Signal.
Authored Signal cannot appear without a composed song. Learning music skills or listening to music alone does not fulfill that requirement. The three H2Hs also have their own completion records; affection alone does not replace them.

Composing a Song for the Route

If you already have a song in your composed song list, it qualifies. Otherwise, a straightforward preparation path is:

Song Writing's action has a wider nominal window, but the Bards Guild opens at 1 PM, so 1–9 PM is the usable lesson window.
In Compose Song, select an available topic and finish its quiz. Same Song, Different Day is an initial topic without an extra character or quest unlock. The quiz normally allows one wrong answer, or two if you know Storytelling. Answering all questions within that allowance adds the song to your composed list.
Simply opening and closing the composing menu before selecting a topic does not commit the activity's costs. Selecting a topic commits the activity, including when its quiz is failed. Listening to an existing song does not itself compose one.
Kuro's Authored Signal check requires the stored song, rather than a specific title, song quality, performance history or Bard clout threshold. Other legitimate game sources of a composed song also satisfy it.

Perform for Kuro and Private Performance

At Acquaintance, after Authored Signal, choose Perform for Kuro while she is present. You must have at least one composed song. Select a song in the performance menu; a successful session costs 60 minutes and 25 Energy and awards 6 base affection, once per game day.
The first successful session also plays Private Performance. Its completion gives 10 base affection, with Caring +8, Funny +6 or Flirty +7 from its choice. A Caring answer therefore gives 24 total base affection across the first session: 6 performance +10 scene completion +8 choice. The rewards pass through their usual relationship calculations separately.
The selected song's prior history changes the first scene's presentation:
Prior history before this performance Presentation
Never performed anywhere First audience
Performed before, at fewer than three different venues Performed
Performed at at least three different venues Seasoned
These are presentation branches, not extra affection tiers or route prerequisites. All lead to the same story choice and completion. There is no requirement to perform publicly first.
After the first scene, later successful performances give their normal 6 base affection and do not replay the one-time 10-point completion or choice bonus. The route retains the song anchored by the first performance for later story references, even if you perform other songs afterward.
Kuro's private performance records the song's venue history. Its handler does not apply the normal public-venue money, valuables or clout reward calculation.

Frequency Analysis

At Friend and Perception 50, after Authored Signal, choose Frequency Analysis while Kuro is present. It completes once, takes 30 minutes, and gives Perception +1 and 8 direct affection.
The definition lists 10 Energy and 5 Calm, but the current one-time-reward handler does not apply those effects, so this reward does not deduct them. It has no separate first-performance or H2H prerequisite, and does not teach Music Production.
