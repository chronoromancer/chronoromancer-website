Overview

Kuro’s route needs an actual composed song and a performance for her. Skill ownership or performances for other audiences do not replace that private performance step.

Where to find them

PlacePortrait hoursCondition
Mood Center10:00–22:00—

Route and relationship milestones

Compose at least one song, reach Acquaintance (50), finish Authored Signal and use Perform for Kuro. A completed performance gives 6 base affection and opens the next personal event. Continue the event/H2H order shown below.

At In Love (3000), finish Constant Signal and Unmeasured Time for the daily private visit. Completing one visit opens a later gift event for the Resonance Amplifier, which doubles final grenade damage in Neural Targeting after other bonuses.

The Carrier Window also needs Story 94. The one-time Propaganda lesson requires her full listed personal chain, the amplifier event, that Story-94 event and Constant Frequency, at In Love (3000). It takes 0 minutes and adds 1 Propaganda.

Optional reference: personal and shared event navigation

Each listed event happens once. Return to the named place after completing its prior steps. Completion affection is a base reward; dialogue choices can add further affection. The scenes and choice answers remain in the game.

Her Glass Reef date starts at 10:00–18:00 in practice because her portrait is unavailable before 10:00. The ordinary performance action and private visit each have their own daily use.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Technology 80; Creativity 70; Perception 75

Favorite skill: Music Production

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If Authored Signal or the performance is missing, create a song first; a music skill alone is insufficient.
  • For the amplifier, complete the first private visit and return for its event.
  • For the Propaganda lesson, check the Story-94 event and all personal prerequisites; no Clout is spent.

概览

Kuro 的路线要求你真正创作一首歌曲，并为她演奏。只拥有相关技能，或只在其他场所演出，不能代替这项私人演奏。

地点肖像在场时段条件
情绪调控中心10:00–22:00—

路线与关系节点

至少创作一首歌曲、达到相识（50），并完成对应个人事件后，使用为 Kuro 演奏。演奏完成会增加 6 基础好感度，并开放下一段个人事件；之后依下表穿插事件与谈心。

恋爱中（3000）后，完成对应的两项后期个人目标，即可进行每天一次的私人相聚。完成一次相聚后可推进后续礼物事件，获得共振放大器，使神经瞄准中计算完其他加成后的手雷最终伤害翻倍。

另一个后期节点还要求主线 94。一次性宣传课程要求恋爱中（3000），完成指定个人链、放大器事件、该主线 94 节点及最终谈心，耗时 0 分钟，增加 1 宣传。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。

科技 80; 创造力 70; 感知 75

偏好技能：音乐制作

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择还可能在事件完成基础奖励之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

  • 创作相关事件或演奏未出现时，请先真正创作歌曲，只有音乐技能还不够。
  • 若要获得放大器，请先完成一次私人相聚，再回访触发礼物事件。
  • 宣传课程需检查主线 94 节点与所有个人前置，不消耗声望。
