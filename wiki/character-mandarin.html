Overview

Mandarin is one of the brothel’s maids. Recruiting her opens daily cleaning contributions, her relationship actions and shared maid encounters. Her portrait and cleaning work have different schedules.

Where to find Mandarin

Own the brothel, then investigate the hidden passage on ordinary dungeon floor 21. Counting from the upper-left corner with the first column and row as 1, the entrance is column 3, row 11. Complete the encounter’s demon battle to acquire Mandarin.

She appears in Maids Quarters daily, 10:00–14:00. Cleaning still contributes outside those visiting hours.

Route and relationship milestones

At Known (25) and Communication 45, Strength in Service grants Strength +1 and 10 direct relationship points once, for 30 minutes. At Intimate (1600), Maid Service opens as a repeatable 30-minute conversation.

Shared maid scenes depend on the other maids being acquired and, where listed, on their own relationship stages. Their automatic trigger windows can extend beyond Mandarin’s ordinary portrait hours.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 15 minutes and gives 1 base relationship point, with 30 safe uses daily. Maid Service awards the points earned by its choices; its best ordinary choices total 15 base points. It remains repeatable after completion.

Mandarin contributes the maid system’s cleaning and equipment bonuses. Her exclusive forge equipment, cleaning level and Cleansing progression are covered in the Maid System guide; meeting a relationship threshold does not automatically craft her equipment.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

概览

Mandarin是青楼女仆之一。招募后，她会参与日常清洁，并开放个人关系动作与女仆共同事件。头像出现时间和清洁工作的结算时间并不相同。

出现地点与时间

先拥有青楼，再探索普通地下城第21层的隐藏通道。以上左角为起点、第一列和第一行为1计，入口在第3列、第11行。完成事件中的恶魔战斗后即可取得Mandarin。

她每天10:00—14:00出现在女仆宿舍。清洁贡献不会因为不在这个拜访时段就停止。

地点头像出现时间条件

时段按开始动作时判定，结束时间不包含在内。

路线与关系进展

达到已知（25）及沟通45后，选择服务中的力量可花30分钟，一次性获得力量+1和10点直接关系。达到亲密（1600）后，女仆服务成为可重复的30分钟对话。

女仆共同事件还要求取得相应的其他女仆，部分事件也检查她们各自的关系阶段。自动事件的触发时段可能超出Mandarin普通头像的出现时间。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，实际获得1点基础关系，每日安全额度30次。女仆服务按对话选项奖励关系点，最佳普通选择合计15点基础关系，完成后仍可再次进行。

Mandarin的清洁贡献、装备加成、清洁等级与净化进展详见女仆系统指南。关系达到门槛不会自动制作她的专属装备。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

第21层入口要求拥有青楼，取得她之后就不再用于重复招募。服务中的力量除了关系之外也检查沟通。交谈超过安全额度会降低关系，即使她的回复语气仍很平静。
