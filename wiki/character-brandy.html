Meeting and Recruiting Brandy

  1. Visit the Rowdy Alehouse in Kings Demesne between 10 PM and 3 AM. Start with conversation and tips; she has no initial recruitment prerequisite.
  2. At Known, complete Life Philosophy at the alehouse: 60 minutes, 15 energy, restoring 10 calm.
  3. Reach Friend, own the Rose & Thorn, and complete About the Rose & Thorn after Life Philosophy: 75 minutes, 18 energy, restoring 12 calm.
  4. Raise brothel reputation to 8, use the paid version, and choose Brandy Joins at Friend after the previous H2H: 90 minutes, 20 energy, restoring 15 calm.

Worker Visits and Eight Training Levels

Use the brothel's worker visit menu with worker 17. Her initial shift is 6 PM–2 AM; later scheduling changes can change that window. She must be active, not resting, not at the resort and not already visited that day.

Training begins at level 1 and advances one level per completed session, up to 8. Earlier options remain available. The menu only offers a type when its corresponding images exist.

Clothing

Brandy participates in Fernando's clothing fittings while active. The tailor selects randomly from eligible workers; buying a fitting does not guarantee she is selected. Ordinary fittings raise clothing by one level, with up to two Fernando upgrades per worker while below level 5. A separate MAX fitting applies at level 5. Gold and fabric costs follow the current quote, which rises across the roster.


If Progress Stalls

  • Find the alehouse portrait 10 PM–3 AM for the home H2Hs. The brothel-bar portrait does not contain them.
  • Complete Life Philosophy, then About the Rose & Thorn, then meet reputation 8 and the paid-version gate for joining.
  • Visit the Brothel Bar 5–10 PM for ordinary home interactions after recruitment. Worker visits use the separate assigned shift, initially 6 PM–2 AM.
  • Talk, Drink and Flirt limits are shared between the two locations.
  • Tavern Evening is one-time across both locations and ends its brothel-bar start window at 9 PM, before her portrait leaves at 10 PM.
  • The Cowgirl upgrade needs Tavern Evening; the Oral upgrade needs Life Philosophy. Both need In Love and their actual crafted gift.
  • Bring workers home for community scenes, or send all listed participants to the resort together for vacation group scenes.
