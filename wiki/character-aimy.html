AIMy——完全走过

概览

AIMy 是Nova的热情AI研究助理。她的个人路线发展了她对倾听,先问,以及享受玩家陪伴的理解。她有一个成熟的机器人身体;不需要单独的造身探索。

查找 AIMy

到达 Cid诊所下面的TD要塞 通过主故事,然后访问 AIMy的控制室她24小时都在现场她的开场表演需要 Story第32话.
虽然她在故事里已经认识你,但她的爱度表开始于 0 并使用通常的关系阈值。 Story 熟悉不会自动给您带来已知的舞台。

AIMy — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

AIMy is Nova's enthusiastic AI research assistant. Her personal route develops her understanding of listening, asking first, and enjoying the player's company. She has an established adult android body; a separate body-building quest is not required.

Finding AIMy

Reach the TD Fortress beneath Cid's clinic through the main story, then visit AIMy's Control Room. She is available there around the clock. Her opening personal scene requires Story Beat 32.
Although she already knows you in the story, her affection meter starts at 0 and uses the usual relationship thresholds. Story familiarity does not automatically give you the Known stage.

Current Walkthrough

All of these steps take place in AIMy's Control Room. Affection and completed scenes are separate requirements: raising affection alone does not bypass an earlier scene.
Order Scene How to start Requirements
1 For Your Amusement Return to her control room; automatic scene Friend and Story Beat 32 completed
2 When You Aren't Here Choose the heart-to-heart action Close Friend and For Your Amusement completed; 60 minutes and 20 energy
3 Too Much Help Return to her control room; automatic scene Close Friend, For Your Amusement and When You Aren't Here completed
For Your Amusement awards 6 base points. Its Flirty choice adds 5 base points, Caring adds 4, and Funny adds 3. Too Much Help awards 8 base points, with the same 5/4/3 choice bonuses. These rewards use the shared relationship calculation.
Return to the room after completing the heart-to-heart so that the next automatic scene can start. The heart-to-heart completes once and is not a repeatable affection action.

When You Aren't Here

This five-round conversation costs 60 minutes and 20 energy. These are the highest base-point choices in each round:
Round Choice Base points
1 You can miss my company even when you know I am safe. 12
2 The cat demands a live audience. Very unreasonable animal. 12
3 Ask me. I will tell you when I can. 12
4 I like hearing you too. We can leave room for both of us. 12
5 I would like that. 18
Choice points accumulate during the conversation and are converted into the final relationship reward when it finishes. They are not five separate guaranteed fixed awards.

Supporting Syndicate Scene

After Story Beat 85 and After the Line in the Syndicate route, return to the Operations Center for Room for Reinforcements. This automatic one-time supporting scene gives 3 base affection to AIMy, alongside rewards for Ghost and Kitty. The Funny response adds another 3 base points to AIMy; Caring and Flirty instead favor Ghost. Complete the Syndicate route through The Second Knock → After the Line to satisfy its separate quest flag. It is available within the current release boundary even though AIMy's next personal scene is still story-gated.

Current Release Boundary

The current game configuration releases numbered story beats through Beat 85. AIMy's next personal step requires Beat 94, so her presently reachable route stops after Too Much Help. Her later heart-to-hearts, two personal outings and concluding relationship scene cannot yet be reached through ordinary story progression.
If you have reached Close Friend, completed When You Aren't Here and seen Too Much Help, you are caught up with her currently playable route. Additional affection will not unlock the unreleased story prerequisite. There is no missing gift, bodybuilding task, or extra android-construction step to complete.
