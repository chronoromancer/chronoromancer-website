Inventory and Supplies do different jobs

Open Inventory to check your personal resources, equipment, gifts, potions, mystery slots and Key Items. The round button at the upper right switches to Supplies; the same button returns to Inventory. Owning an item for a worker, maid or army hero does not equip it on your own character.

Hover over an icon for its current name and effect. A zero count, an undiscovered mystery slot and an unavailable action have different meanings. When a recovery item cannot be used, check both the stock and the relevant resource maximum.

Currencies, stock and recovery

Wealth, Gems, Crystals, Dragon Coins and Sea Shells are distinct currencies. A large cash balance cannot pay a Crystal or Dragon Coin price. Materials, Fabrics and Ingredients each have Common, Rare and Epic quantities; tiers are not automatically interchangeable.

MedicineHealth +25, or +50 with Willow’s enhanced healing
Energy ItemsEnergy +25, or +50 with enhanced healing

Food and the three recovery stocks are used from the left side of Inventory. Recovery stops at the current maximum. A full relevant resource prevents ordinary use instead of consuming stock for no benefit. Willow’s enhanced healing changes recovery amounts; it is separate from the brothel Infirmary’s Medicine level. See Willow’s guide for her progression.

For Crystal purchases and repeatable sources, use Crystals and Doomsday. Several Crystal services are repeatable; they are not all once-per-timeline purchases.

Cooking and Ingredients

At your modern Uptown home, enter the Kitchen. Prepare Simple Food needs no learned cooking skill. Other options appear after learning their named skills. Cooking is also a core stat: the learned skill Cook Food and the numeric Cooking stat are different requirements.

Eight personal equipment slots

EquipmentWhat its level means
Melee Weapon / ArmorBase levels 0–10; physical combat equipment. Extra effective levels can add to the base level.
Ranged WeaponBase levels 0–10. Neural Targeting prizes can grant late upgrades; TD reads the weapon’s base level.
GrimoireLevels 1–10; improves magic and certain arcane actions. High levels include character-linked upgrades.
Casual / Formal / Dating OutfitSeparate clothing levels, normally up to 5; meeting or route requirements can ask for one particular outfit.
Proficiency ItemNormally up to 5; the purchased Proficiency Upgrade can permit 10. Its specialty-work income and story-miniboss benefits differ from ordinary weapon damage.

The three clothing slots plus the other five equipment types make eight slots. Permanent upgrades replace the previous tier; they are not separate spare weapons waiting to be equipped. The cyan effective-level bonus is different from the item’s paid level. An exact-level purchase requirement still checks its own stated level.

Forged gauntlets, helmets, shields and pauldrons are Key Items with their own effects. They do not replace the main Armor slot. See Blacksmith and Forge and Combat. The player’s armor, TD hero armor and a TD hero’s Shield stat also remain separate.

Give gifts through the relevant activity

The first middle row contains relationship gifts. Use a character’s gift action or the appropriate date/training choice; clicking a gift icon alone does not choose a recipient. A gift’s presence in Inventory does not remove relationship, time, quantity or content requirements.

Ordinary gifts, crafted special gifts, permanent Key Items and worker-training presents serve different purposes. If an action asks for a named gift, carry that exact item rather than a similarly shaped accessory. See Relationships and Dating and worker-training gifts.

Potions and temporary effects

The second middle row contains eight enhancement consumables. Ordinary active effects expire after 24 in-game hours. An already active potion cannot be consumed again simply to extend the timer. Inventory’s red tint marks active effects; it is not a universal “bad item” warning.

Supported action-cost reductions

For Full Body Workout and supported intimate video activities, Time halves the action’s game-clock cost, Power halves its Energy cost, and Focus halves its Calm cost. Each positive cost rounds up and stays at least 1. The current action tooltip shows applicable reductions; other activities have their own rules.

Six Supplies categories, five visible rows

Supplies uses two snapped row positions. The first view shows five categories; scroll or use the on-screen arrow to reveal the bottom Army Equipment row. Each category can also contain its own horizontal item row. It is easy to own an army item without seeing that row in the first view.

CategoryUse
Worker ItemsEquip workers through worker management; some owned-copy displays also count equipped items.
Maid ItemsChoose equipment in maid management; the Supplies row is not the selected maid’s equipment screen.
Monster ItemsResident-care consumables and tools use their own management and visits.
Mining ItemsPickaxes, ingots, mineral clusters and Chronium; a held pickaxe is not a personal combat weapon upgrade.
CollectionSea Shells, Leather, Components, Phoenix Feathers and Infinity drops have separate sources and uses.
Army EquipmentAcquire and equip items for a specific recruited TD hero in Barracks.

Phoenix Feathers have an automatic-revival toggle. When enabled and a supported combat defeat triggers revival, one Feather is consumed and Health, Energy and Calm fill; Satiation is not part of that refill. Disabling the toggle preserves your stock. Its red tint indicates disabled automatic revival, unlike a potion’s active-effect tint.

Key Items and permanent collections

Key Items appear in the bottom horizontal bar. Most passive effects work while owned; do not look for a player equipment slot for every collected object. A numeric badge may be an upgrade level rather than a stack quantity, especially for Soul Stones and upgradeable Key Items.

库存与补给分别存放什么

打开「库存」可查看个人资源、装备、礼物、药剂、神秘物品栏与关键物品。右上角的圆形按钮可「切换到补给」，在补给页再点同一按钮可返回。员工、女仆或军队英雄的物品，不会自动装备到玩家身上。

将指针停在图标上可看当前名称与效果。数量为零、神秘栏位尚未发现、行动不可用，是不同情况。恢复物品不能使用时，既要看库存数量，也要看对应状态是否已经补满。

货币、库存与恢复

财富、宝石、水晶、龙币与海贝壳是不同货币。现金再多，也不能直接支付要求水晶或龙币的价格。材料、布料与食材各分普通、稀有、史诗三级，不能默认用低级资源抵扣高级资源。

食物与三类恢复库存从左侧使用，最多恢复到当前上限；对应状态已满时，普通恢复物品不会继续消耗。Willow的强化治疗提高玩家恢复量，与青楼医务室的药品等级分别计算。相关步骤见Willow指南。

水晶的用途与可重复来源见水晶与末日倒计时。多项水晶服务可以重复购买，不能一概当作每周目限购一次。

八个个人装备栏位

装备等级代表什么
近战武器／护甲基础等级0–10；用于个人物理战斗。部分加成会提高有效等级。
远程武器基础等级0–10；神经瞄准奖品可提供高阶升级。塔防读取武器本身的基础等级。
魔导书等级1–10；改善魔法及部分奥术行动。高阶升级与人物服务和路线相连。
休闲装／正装／约会装三项衣装分别升级，常规上限为5。人物事件可能只检查其中一项，不能互相代替。
专长物品常规上限5；购买熟练度升级后可到10。专长工作收入与剧情小首领加成，不等于普通武器伤害。

三套衣装加上另外五类装备，共有八个栏位。永久升级会替换前一级，不会变成一把等着手动换上的备用武器。青色显示的有效等级加成，与已经购买的基础等级不同；要求特定基础等级的升级，仍按其自身条件检查。

锻造的护手、头盔、盾牌与肩甲属于各有作用的关键物品，不会取代主要「护甲」栏位。详见铁匠与锻造与战斗系统。玩家护甲、塔防英雄护甲、塔防英雄的护盾属性，也要分别查看。

Aziza另有连续的魔导书购买：3级需要朋友、5,000财富与3颗水晶；4级需要亲密朋友、10,000财富与4颗水晶；5级需要迷恋、50,000财富与5颗水晶。每项购买本身耗时0分钟，要求前一级魔导书，并且Aziza当时在奥术商店。这些购买与最初的1→2级商店行动分别提供。

6–10级魔导书依次与Aziza、Blinky、Sashina、Sabbath和Nyxara的升级行动相连。请按对应人物页面与当场提示，核对关系、装备和访问条件。远程武器的后期升级见神经瞄准奖品；幽谷的高阶服务见Sylwithe的幽谷。

从人物行动或活动中赠礼

中间第一排是关系礼物。应从人物的赠礼行动，或约会、训练中的对应选择送出；单独点击库存礼物图标，并不会自动选定收礼对象。拥有礼物，也不能代替好感、时段、数量或内容访问条件。

普通礼物、制作的特殊礼物、永久关键物品与员工训练赠礼，用途不同。行动要求某个具名礼物时，要准备该物品，不能用外观相似的饰品替代。详见关系、约会与亲密互动及员工训练赠礼。

药剂与临时效果

中间第二排是八类强化消耗品。常规持续效果在24个游戏内小时后结束；同一种效果仍生效时，不能再喝一瓶来延长计时。库存中的红色效果标记，不能一概理解为物品有问题。

适用活动的消耗减免

对「全身锻炼」及适用的亲密视频活动，时间效果使游戏内耗时减半，力量药水使精力消耗减半，专注使镇定消耗减半。每项正消耗向上取整，最低为1；当场行动提示会显示适用减免，其他活动各有自己的规则。
