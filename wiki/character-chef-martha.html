概览

Chef Martha 是当代 下城餐厅 她的路线包括对话、烹饪场景、两个H2Hs、两个日期、一个主厨课、一个有多个分支的公寓访问、一个私人录像和一个可选的家庭续集。

Overview

Chef Martha is the Present-era Downtown Restaurant's head chef. Her route includes conversation, culinary scenes, two H2Hs, two dates, a Master Chef lesson, an apartment visit with several branches, a private video and an optional family continuation.

Relationship Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Downtown Restaurant, 10 AM–10 PM, for The Downtown Restaurant introduction: 5 base affection. Funny +4 is its highest choice reward.
  2. Use Talk to Chef Martha, available from Stranger, for 15 minutes, 1 base affection, five times per day.
  3. At Known, revisit for The Special and Tip Money. With Communication 50, use Culinary Creativity: 30 minutes, Cooking +1 and 8 direct affection, once.
  4. At Acquaintance, start Beach Day from her restaurant portrait 2–5 PM: 60 minutes, 15 energy, restoring 10 calm.
  5. At Friend, revisit for Kitchen Confessions, and start the Farmers Market Date during its effective 10 AM–2 PM window.
  6. At Close Friend, revisit for The Competition, start Starlight Walk 7–10 PM, or take the Boardwalk Sunset Date 5–8 PM if its extra gates are met.
  7. At Romantic, use Master Chef with Cooking 60, or visit her apartment from the Downtown Apartment Complex, 10 PM–midnight.
  8. At 1,000 affection, her paid Private Evening video becomes available from the restaurant portrait 8–10 PM, separately from the apartment conversation.
The H2Hs, dates, automatic scenes and skill lesson use their own gates. Neither H2H requires the other completed, and the apartment action does not require finishing either date first.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Optional Family Continuation

To unlock Martha's deliberate family branch, enable unlocked pregnancy content, complete Akira's Pregnancy Protocol, reach 2,000 affection and 500 NPC intimacy experience with Martha, and finish both The Competition and Master Chef. She cannot already be pregnant, recovering from delivery or have a child with the player.
At the opening of an eligible apartment visit, choose Ask Martha if she wants to have a child with you. Complete that dedicated branch. Ordinary intimate endings do not substitute for this choice. The branch records 40 NPC intimacy experience, awards 50 player Erotic Experience, and has two final 25-base-point choices.

If Progress Stalls

  • Her restaurant portrait and the restaurant itself use 10 AM–10 PM. The market date cannot actually begin at its configured 9 AM opening.
  • H2Hs start at the restaurant, while the apartment action starts at the Downtown Apartment Complex, 10 PM–midnight.
  • Master Chef needs Romantic and Cooking 60, plus its substantial resource costs. Cook Food is a different lesson.
  • Private Evening needs 1,000 affection, the paid version and 8–10 PM, not Intimate or an apartment-visit flag.
  • Apartment special answers need their named stats, skills or Perfume; missing one still leaves ordinary paths available.
  • The optional family branch requires both The Competition and Master Chef, plus 2,000 affection, 500 NPC intimacy experience, the enabled content and Akira's protocol. Player Erotic Experience does not replace NPC intimacy experience.
