Overview

Aaliyah is the friendly employee at the entrance to Downtown KTV in the Present Era. She welcomes guests and helps keep the private rooms organized during the evening rush.

Where to Find Her

Visit the KTV Entrance in Present Downtown between 8 PM and 2 AM. Her schedule crosses midnight: she is present from 8 PM to midnight and again from midnight to before 2 AM. There is no relationship-stage prerequisite for her appearance.

Current Content

Aaliyah currently serves as the KTV entrance hostess. She has introductory dialogue and a character profile, but no dedicated quest chain, heart-to-heart, date, gift action, or skill-teaching route is currently available. You do not need to build a relationship with her to use the venue's existing activities.
The Invite Date action at the entrance is for eligible date guests; it does not make Aaliyah a date candidate. Venue dates and access to the private room are separate from her personal content.
