Troubleshooting

  • Invite Ryia to Join missing: complete the three huts scenes, A Page of Her Own, and the house operation choice; return to Peasant Huts 8 AM–8 PM before recruitment.
  • She vanished from Peasant Huts: recruitment moves her permanently; enter her room through Worker Quarters.
  • Room access works while inactive: that follows her recruited/found state. Worker Visits and nine shared history scenes separately require active duty.
  • Tutorial stalled: follow room → Elara's Office → Worker Quarters → room and bring Elara/Ryia home from vacation where required; no week-long waiting timer is needed.
  • Heart-to-heart missing: check its exact ordinary affection stage and earlier H2H/plot, plus her room hours. Worker relationship hearts and Loyalty do not substitute.
  • Date missing: use the room action, within its narrow starting window. Moonlit Courtyard starts only 5–6 PM, while her room portrait is still present.
  • The Next Account Book missing: reach Intimate, finish Morning Without Proof, and ensure Elara is not on vacation.
  • History encounter missing: make Ryia active and home, then visit while the host's own portrait is present. The nine encounters are independent.
  • Worker Visit missing: check active duty, no rest/vacation, the configured work shift and today's visit use.
  • Training option missing: advance the shared training level through any completed lesson; each visit permits one lesson.
  • Gift upgrade missing: reach In Love, complete The Next Account Book and Leave a Light for Me, and carry the exact gift at her room 8 AM–6 PM.
  • Resort portrait missing: travel out and back for a fresh presence roll; check her vacation assignment and location hours.
  • Gallery not unlocked by worker Experience: earn Ryia NPC intimacy XP, then view the separate Intimacy details screen.
