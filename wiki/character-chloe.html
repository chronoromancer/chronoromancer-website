Overview

Chloe is an adult advanced-track Academy student in the Future's Prime Sector. Her current route contains a long personal scene sequence, conversation and gifts, Researching instruction, a Perception reward, three H2Hs, two dates and a shared lunch scene with Tiffany. This walkthrough describes the playable progression without retelling the later Academy revelations.

Meeting Chloe and Following Her Schedule

  1. Visit the Skill Enhancement Facility from 8–11 AM or 3–8 PM for Quantum Achievements, her automatic introduction. It gives 3 base affection, with Caring or Flirty +3 as the best choice bonus.
  2. Use Talk from the initial stage to build Acknowledged, then revisit the facility for The Correct Answer after the introduction. This opens her 11 AM–3 PM Food Court presence.
  3. At Known, visit the food court for The Unstructured Question, and begin Book or Chocolate gifts. You can also learn Researching at the facility with Intelligence 35.
  4. At Acquaintance, return to the facility for The Useful Mistake after The Unstructured Question.
  5. At Friend, revisit for Cohort Review and take the first H2H, Why I Qualified. With Intelligence 45 and The Useful Mistake completed, use Question Framing for its one-time Perception reward.
  6. At Close Friend, after Cohort Review, visit the Prime Transportation Hub, 9 AM–7 PM, for Provisional Placement. This opens her 8 PM–midnight Residential Tower presence.
  7. Continue the automatic scene sequence and H2H chain below. The two optional dates have separate quest flags and start locations.
The transportation-hub scene is an automatic quest appearance outside her normal portrait schedule. Her routine conversation and gifts use the three locations listed above, with their respective unlocks and hours.

If Progress Stalls

  • Use the facility, 8–11 AM or 3–8 PM, until The Correct Answer opens lunch at the Food Court.
  • Provisional Placement starts at the Transportation Hub, 9 AM–7 PM, after Cohort Review and Close Friend. It opens her evening Tower presence.
  • Question Framing needs The Useful Mistake and Intelligence 45, not Friend alone.
  • Idle Time needs Why I Qualified; No Score for This needs Idle Time. A date completion cannot replace those H2Hs.
  • Recreation Credit starts only at the facility, 8–11 AM / 3–6 PM, after Cohort Review. Unscheduled Night starts at the Tower, 8–10 PM, after Attachment Variance.
  • Daily conversation and gifts share their limits between all three portrait locations.
  • Her current character has no registered apartment conversation, personal intimate video or worker recruitment. The evening Tower interactions are Talk, gifts, Idle Time and the night date.
