TD Fortress Workshops

Overview

Battle Guide • Army and Training • Fortress Workshops • Reclamation Bay • Waves and Enemies
Workshop purchases are permanent in the current game save and take effect in the next battle. They use Reclamation specialist materials, Pristine Cores, and shared Army EXP. The Salvage/Ammo/Command displayed inside a battle are separate deployment currencies.
Menu Provider Purpose
Turret Workshop EPOM Purchase seven additional designs and improve all eight turrets
Fortress Control AIMy Starting Lives, barrier supply, barrier Health, total turret capacity
Fortress Hardware Tinker The same four shared control tracks
Barrier Workshop Orchid Eight independent regular-barrier Health and supply tracks
Army Armor Tinker Separate armor level for each recruited hero
Use the Operations Center's room links to reach the providers. Purchasing at AIMy and Tinker changes the same control levels, not two separate copies. Select a card to inspect current/next values and exact owned/required resources. Each research track stops at Level 10.

Turret Improvements

Every research step adds 20% of base Power and 10 pixels of range. Accuracy progresses toward 95% by Level 10 for attacking turrets. Ordinary attack rate and turret Health stay constant. Nanite Beacon repairs instead of attacking.

Regular Barriers

All eight regular designs start at Level 1, without an initial design purchase. Orchid upgrades each design independently: +20% base HP and +1 whole-battle deployment per level. AIMy's shared bonuses multiply their HP and add to their supply.

Final HP = base HP × (1 + 0.2 × Orchid steps) × Structural Reinforcement percentage, rounded once at the end. At both Level-10 upgrades, a design has 6.58 times its base HP. For example, a Wall rises from 320 to 2,106 HP.
Supply for each design = 6 + 2 × shared Barrier Deployment steps + that design's Orchid steps. At all relevant Level-1 tracks it is 6; at all Level-10 tracks it is 33 deployments of each design per attempt.
Destroyed barriers do not refund supply or currency. New waves do not replenish supply. Moving a surviving barrier is free and does not consume another use. Arcane Scrap Ward and Razor Scrap Barricade are regular obstacles; their names do not add an undocumented damage or shielding ability.

The required hero must be recruited, alive, and deployed to place a new copy. Their destruction frees an active slot, so replacements are possible while the hero remains eligible. These designs gain Structural Reinforcement HP but have no Orchid design-research track and do not spend regular-barrier supply.
Terra's automatic Living Network conjures a separate set of free Trees. Those use her Special Power for HP, her Special Range for their active limit, and do not receive shared reinforcement. See Army and Training.
