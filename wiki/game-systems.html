⚙️ Game Systems
A reference for all major game systems in Chronoromancer — how they work, how to use them, and how to master them.

Guilds / Factions / Combat Class

🎵
Bards Guild
Compose songs, perform at venues, climb the quest board, and recruit Violet as your 1st maid.
⚔️
Fighters Guild
25 progressive contracts, bounty boss hunts, skill training, unique key items, and boss challenges up to the Succubus Tier.
🗡️
Thieves Guild
Join Arestes and Shadow's underground network. Heists, fencing, clout ranks, and Shadow's recruitment arc.
🔮
Wizard Faction
Build magical clout through skills and actions, pass the Initiation Test, and recruit Lime as your 4th maid.
Specialist Class
Combat Class advancement through Specialist Path. Each path is unlocked through a specific character, then grows as you fight using its style.

Minigames

∞
Infinity Dungeons
Endless dungeon progression, enemy modifiers and tiers, hazards, special drops, and farming.
🛡
Tower Defense
Godot battles, Army training and equipment, turret and barrier workshops, fortress upgrades, Reclamation, and all three maps.
🎯
Neural Targeting
Future Era shooting gallery: grenades, enemy fire, three bosses, eight upgrade tracks, and twelve high-score prizes.
🃏
Bar Games
Darts, Liar's Dice, Christmas Hold'em and Blackjack against Vera. Strategy guides for all four.
🏚
Brothel
Complete guide to running the brothel — workers, maids, monster girls, rooms, upgrades, daily operations, reputation, and income systems.
🐛
Caterpillar
Raise silkworms, harvest cocoons, and process silk thread for profit. A passive income system tied to the brothel.
🎣
Fishing
Two-stage minigame, 30-fish ledger, upgrades, and a full quest chain with Marina.
Kupie Love Training
An incremental idle/clicker management system hosted by Kupie in the Attic.
⛏
Mining
Match-and-cascade puzzle. Three pickaxes, 33 tile types, key item stones, Soul Stone enhancements, and power-up chains.
👻
Haunted Mansion
37 rooms, 21 ghost souls, puzzles, and Friday the Soul Collector. Unlocked with the HALLOWEEN code.

Buildings / Items / Objectives

🎪
Chaos Syndicate Story
Shared missions, character-route requirements, and the Syndicate Alliance side quest with its six army recruits and Gates defense.
⚙
Body Modifications
Tinker’s eight permanent upgrade tracks, Lab and Fortress access, level costs, and installed bonuses.
🧪
Ruxandra's Alchemy Shop
Trade Blood Orbs for six vampire artifacts and repeatable Energy and Elemental Stone upgrades. Unlocks at effective Vampire 25.
🔨
Blacksmith & Forge
Gastoff's shop for weapons, armor, the four forge pieces, and all brothel construction commissions.
🧹
Maids
Five Elemental Maidens. Recruit, level, equip, and keep the Rose & Thorn at Spotless cleanliness.
🏖️
Sirena Isle Resort
A tropical getaway where your brothel workers can finally rest
💎
Soul Stones
Eight rare key items that grow stronger through intimate bonds and Theneas's Infinity-drop upgrades. No level cap.
🌳
Sylwithe's Hollow
Hidden crystal shop in the Whispering Woods. Buy equipment upgrades, skill training, and rare items from an ancient weather spirit.
Full Story Beats
The full list of story beat quests so far.
💀
Dungeon & Dragon Coins
45-floor catacombs, fixed guardians, secret walls, Floor 13 coin farming, and the endless Infinity Dungeons.
Morrie — Dungeon Encounters
Fifteen one-time encounters with the wandering soul collector. Floor ranges, item requirements and a completion route.


🎄
Christmas Village
Gingerbread House, Winter Wonderland, and Mistletoe Lodge — the year-round Christmas event cluster.
