Overview

Orchid is the cheerful scavenger first found at the Surplus Distribution Center in the Future era. Her current personal route opens the Reclamation Bay, with seven automatic encounters, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, daily Talk and Flowers, salvage processing and a separate Kupie progression.
This walkthrough gives the requirements and useful choices without describing the main story's revelations or the personal scenes' outcomes.

Personal Route in Order

  1. Progress the main story through Story Beat 34, then reach Acquaintance with Orchid.
  2. Visit Surplus Distribution Center, noon–6 PM, for The Overflow.
  3. Visit Engineering, 8 AM–10 PM, for Room to Reclaim. This opens the Bay and moves her portrait there.
  4. Visit Reclamation Bay, 8 AM–8 PM, for First Intake. This opens the intake and processing actions.
  5. At Friend, complete What Survives from her Bay portrait. Then visit the Bay, 8 AM–8 PM, for Keep One.
  6. At Close Friend, complete Named for Something Beautiful. Return to the Bay, 6–8 PM, for Useful Until Empty, then 8 AM–8 PM for No Assigned Value.
  7. At Crush, complete No Practical Purpose. At Romantic, return to the Bay, 6–8 PM, for Something New.
The two dates are additional route content. Borrowed Yesterdays becomes available after What Survives; Under Glass after Named for Something Beautiful. Neither is an extra prerequisite for the seven personal automatic encounters.

Daily Talk and Flowers

Talk / Talk to Orchid costs 20 minutes. The menu's zero-point field is not the actual reward: her handler awards one base point for each of four positive uses per game day. That allowance is shared across the surplus centre and Bay.
At the Bay, Give Flowers costs 10 minutes and one Flowers for three base points, up to three positive uses daily. It has no additional affection-stage prerequisite. Over-limit Talk or gift attempts can lose affection; a spent Flowers is still a consumed gift.
Four talks plus three Flowers take 110 minutes and give 13 base affection before shared bonuses. Add one Exceptional processing run for another 60 minutes and 10 base, making 170 minutes and 23 base for that daily combination. Preparing and depositing suitable items is a separate inventory task.
Orchid's ordinary menu does not add Chocolate, Book, Teddy, Perfume or Jewelry gift actions. Those gifts are used in Kupie, separately.

Reclamation Intake and Daily Processing

After First Intake, select the location action Use Reclamation Intake at the Bay. Deposit items shown in its inventory categories into the queue. Depositing immediately removes those quantities from their original inventory, but does not process them or give Orchid affection yet.
The available catalogue includes eligible stock, crafting supplies, consumables, mining items, spare worker/maid equipment and dungeon drops. It protects special worker equipment and maid-specific items, and keeps one copy of each monster tool. A protected or unrecognised item is not a valid deposit.
Before starting a batch, Undo Queue returns the whole unprocessed queue to the original inventories. It can fail if a safe return is impossible, such as insufficient pickaxe storage; then the queue stays intact. A batch already being processed cannot be withdrawn.
When Orchid is present, use Process Reclamation Queue from her portrait. It needs First Intake complete and at least one queued item, takes 60 minutes, and is enforced once per game day. It has no additional affection-stage, cash, Energy or Calm cost. The completed batch consumes the queue, adds the recovered materials and awards affection according to its grade score:
Queue score Response tier Orchid base affection
1–9 Light Sorting 1
10–39 Useful Haul 3
40–99 Substantial 6
100 or more Exceptional 10
The score is the sum of each item grade's score multiplied by its quantity, rather than its sale price or the raw number of items. 100 reaches the highest affection tier; larger batches can still recover more materials but do not increase that batch's base affection above ten.

The current grades use the following per-item values. Specialist output means the combined Alloy, Circuitry, Reagents and Flux pool, not that amount of each material:

Residue 1 1 0 0 0

Useful 4 2 0.5 0 0

For example, Components are Useful, Gems Epic, and Crystals / Phoenix Feathers Exotic. Fifty Common-grade items reach score 100; twenty-five Useful-grade items also reach 100. Check the intake's grade display for other items before committing valuable supplies.
Fractional specialist output uses a random final unit: an expected total 2.25 means two guaranteed units and a 25% chance of a third. Allocation among the four specialist materials favours the stocks you have less of, but does not guarantee an exact even split.
Each 100 accumulated Core progress produces one guaranteed Pristine Core, with the remainder carried forward. Lucky-Core probabilities combine across the batch, but that roll awards at most one extra lucky Core per batch. Guaranteed Cores can still exceed one. The lucky roll supports the game's Luck Potion retry when available.
The console allows controlled refinement of 20 Scraps into one chosen Alloy, Circuitry, Reagents or Flux. During a processing run, if the resulting Scrap reserve is greater than 500, Orchid automatically refines the whole reserve instead. Complete groups retain the 20:1 value; leftover Scraps give a proportional chance of one final specialist unit. The Scrap reserve then becomes zero. Exactly 500 does not trigger that automatic refinement.

Rebalance Salvage

After First Intake, Orchid's Rebalance Salvage action needs at least two total units across Alloy, Circuitry, Reagents and Flux. It costs zero minutes, with no cash, Energy, Calm or affection award.
The service charges a random fee of 2–10 specialist units, capped by the total you have. The remaining combined specialist stock is divided as evenly as whole units allow across the four materials; any remainder is assigned randomly without losing extra units. Scraps and Pristine Cores are unchanged.
This does not create materials: with only two available units, a two-unit fee leaves zero. Repeating the service incurs a fresh fee each time. The current handler does not enforce a daily use counter.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Orchid disappeared from Surplus: check Room to Reclaim / First Intake progress and the game day number; her later home is the Bay.
  • Bay travel action missing: complete Room to Reclaim in Engineering. The intake then needs First Intake.
  • Personal route will not start: complete Story Beat 34, reach Acquaintance, and visit Surplus noon–6 PM for The Overflow.
  • Next scene missing: check the earlier scene, required H2H, affection stage and that scene's own hours. The two evening scenes require 6–8 PM.
  • Date missing on every third day: start from the Bay 10 AM–noon, while her portrait and the date's time window overlap.
  • Processing unavailable: First Intake must be complete, the queue nonempty, Orchid present, and today's processing allowance unused.
  • Rebalance missing: the four specialist materials must total at least two. Scraps and Pristine Cores do not count toward that requirement.
  • Kupie locked: check ordinary Friend and Emily's Kupie level 3.
Her current route has no separate apartment invitation, ordinary intimacy animation, pregnancy continuation, numbered brothel-worker training, resort vacation or personal Christmas scene. The three H2Hs, two dates, seven automatic encounters, reclamation actions and Kupie cover the authored personal content here.
