Overview

Valkyra is a professional knight in the Medieval era. Her route includes daytime friendship and combat lessons, a festival date, floor30 recruitment as worker #11, the Security Office's Off-Duty conversation and Quiet Hour, Side-Step training, Sword Stone bonding, two crafted training upgrades and Sirena Isle vacation content.
This guide gives the mechanical requirements without retelling the encounter's personal revelations or later story outcomes.

Floor30 Recruitment

Reach Valkyra's floor30 miniboss encounter in the Infinity Dungeon and defeat her. The encounter uses six rounds, 980 enemy Energy, 185 attack power, and targets player Health. Recruitment activates worker #11 and unlocks the Security Office. There is no affection-stage, festival-date or recruitment-fee prerequisite on this victory reward.

Side-Step's30 is the plot completion award, not a second extra30 from the action. In combat, a Quick Attack against a surviving enemy rolls a base20% proc chance, modified by the shared dodge-chance helper. NPC Intimacy Gallery and Troubleshooting

Her three current gallery assets unlock at 100,250 and500 NPC intimacy experience. Current experience sources include worker training, Off-Duty's5 kissing, Quiet Hour's25 blowjob, daily Sunset Walk's5 kissing and Private Beach Time's25 doggie style. Ordinary affection, worker Experience and player Erotic Experience are separate counters.
  • Daytime portrait missing: check even Tournament / odd Guild, 10 AM–6 PM, and vacation status.
  • Security Office portrait missing: activate worker #11 through the floor30 victory and visit during6 PM–4 AM.
  • Off-Duty is missing: reach Close Friend; daytime portraits do not offer it.
  • Quiet Hour is missing: check1,200 affection, paid access and Higher Power.
  • Side-Step is missing: check Romantic, Ability60, paid access and whether already learned.
  • A gift upgrade is missing: reach In Love, finish its exact prerequisite, return her home and carry the named crafted consumable.
  • A shared vacation scene is missing: every named worker must be active and vacationing, with the exact room/time and any Cinnamon prerequisite met.
