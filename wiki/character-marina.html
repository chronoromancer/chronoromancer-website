Marina — Complete Walkthrough

Overview

Marina is a fishing merchant in the Medieval era, based in the Fishing Hut. Her route combines daytime friendship, evening conversations, fishing performance, three boat upgrades, a sunset date and the Void Leviathan challenge. She also arranges the boat that unlocks Sirena Isle. This guide covers the requirements and rewards without retelling her personal scenes.

  3. At Known, collect The Fishing Hut follow-up and Feeding the Villages. With Communication 35, claim Fishing Wisdom for Commerce +1 and 8 direct affection.
  4. On an even-numbered day, meet Marina on Fishing Village Coast, 6–8 PM. Use the separate Personal Talk allowance and complete Sunset at the Dock.
  5. At Friend, complete The Impossible Catch in the hut, 10 AM–2 PM. Starting this H2H activates the Leviathan fishing quest; complete it for the later reward requirement.
  6. At Close Friend, purchase Upgrade Boats, optionally take the paid Sunset Walk, and continue fishing for the Void Leviathan.
  7. With the Leviathan caught and The Impossible Catch complete, request The Impossible Wager at Close Friend. Its reward does not require Romantic or Intimate affection.
  8. At Crush, buy Enhance Boat Speed after Upgrade Boats. At Romantic, buy Ultimate Speed Mastery after the enhancement, and request A Length of Rope once.
  9. For Sirena Isle, follow Sashimi's separate island proposal, then purchase Boat to Sirena Isle. This is independent of Marina's affection and fishing-boat speed chain.

Fishing and Building Affection

Starting a Fishing Session

Use Rent Fishing Boat in the hut. The action is configured for 300 game minutes and $60 and requires a fishing rod. It adds 10 free Fishing Bait, capped at 99 held bait. Each ring attempt normally consumes one bait, whether the subsequent reel challenge succeeds or fails.
Hut purchase Cost Requirement / effect
Buy Wood Rod 10 minutes; $100; 10 common materials Basic rod
Buy Bait x10 10 minutes; $20 Ten Fishing Bait
Upgrade to Iron Rod 20 minutes; $500; 10 rare materials Own Wood Rod; reel zone +5 degrees, one fewer required hit, one extra allowed miss
Upgrade to Gold Rod 20 minutes; $1,000; 5 epic materials Own Iron Rod; reel zone +10 degrees, two fewer required hits, two extra allowed misses, compared with Wood
The best owned rod is used automatically. Required hits never fall below one. Click the water to steer the boat, reach a ring to start a reel challenge, then click while the moving marker lies within the target zone. The normal navigation timer is 45 seconds, upgraded to 60 seconds by the first boat upgrade; the reel challenge pauses navigation.
These public boat, rod and bait services are available at all hours, independently of Marina's portrait. Tobbin handles the hut when Marina is off duty. The attendant for fishing results is chosen when you return, after the clock has advanced. Tobbin uses the same payout, Marina-affection rewards and Leviathan catch recording, so fishing outside Marina's own 4 AM–5 PM schedule still advances her route.

The Void Leviathan Challenge

Start The Impossible Catch at Friend, in the hut 10 AM–2 PM, then finish the H2H. Its handler activates the fishing quest immediately on entry, independently of any chosen reply. Completion is still necessary to claim The Impossible Wager.
The Void Leviathan is legendary fish #26, difficulty level 10. It enters the eligible legendary pool once the quest is active. It is not a guaranteed catch from an epic ring: first a legendary rarity must be selected, then the fish is randomly selected from that rarity's eligible species.
An epic ring has a 5% legendary chance with Wood Rod, 10% with Iron/Gold, or 15% with Master Baiter. A rare ring has 1%, 2% or 4%, respectively. Master Baiter also permits 2% legendary chance from a common ring. Luck Potion's better-of-two rarity rolls can help; successful reel timing is still required to land the selected fish.
The fish database lists a 3–4 AM time label, but the current reel selection routine does not filter species by those time labels. The active quest is the actual Leviathan-specific selection gate; you do not need to wait for that hour to qualify.
After a successful catch, complete the results sequence to record the catch flag. Then visit Marina in the hut at Close Friend, with The Impossible Catch complete, and choose The Impossible Wager. It has no configured time or money cost, no paid-version requirement, and is once-only.
The reward scene gives 22 fixed base affection, with Funny +4 at each of three choices, for 34 best base affection. All three reply sets continue the scene. Its illustrated intimate sequence has no configured NPC intimacy or player Erotic experience award. The route is gated by the catch and H2H, rather than the generic 1,600-point Intimate threshold.

Sunset Walk and Rope

Sunset Walk

The date requires Close Friend, the paid version, and Marina's coast portrait, 6–8 PM on even days. It costs 120 minutes, $50 and 30 Energy, and is once-only. The date does not require either H2H or the Leviathan catch.
Its best sequence is Flirty +5 → Sweet +5 → Sweet or Flirty +5, plus 5 fixed base affection, making 20 best base. There is no configured NPC intimacy or player Erotic experience reward.

A Length of Rope

At Romantic, request A Length of Rope in the hut. It costs 15 minutes, grants one rope in the monster-tools inventory, and completes once. The scene gives 10 fixed base affection, with Funny +4 as its best choice, making 14 base. It has no extra catch, H2H, boat-upgrade, money or material requirement.

Unlocking Sirena Isle

Marina's Boat to Sirena Isle is a separate action from her fishing-boat upgrades. It requires the paid version, completion of Sashimi's An Island in Mind proposal, and $1,000,000. Its configured time cost is 120 minutes. There is no Marina-affection threshold and no required fishing-boat speed upgrade.
The proposal automatically plays at the brothel pool, 9 AM–7 PM, after Sashimi's Void Leviathan quest scene is complete. Follow Sashimi's route to that prerequisite; Marina's personal Impossible Catch H2H does not itself count as Sashimi's quest scene.
Complete the boat arrangement to unlock the vacation system. The scene's choice rewards Uhura, rather than Marina: Caring +4, Flirty +3 or Funny +2 base affection. After unlocking, Travel to Sirena Isle appears at both the hut interior and brothel pool, costing 45 minutes and requiring the paid version.
This unlock gives access to the resort and its staff vacations; Marina is not assigned a personal resort vacation route. There is no configured Marina apartment visit, pregnancy route, Kupie album, Christmas encounter, worker training or maid cleaning progression.
