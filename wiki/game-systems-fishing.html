捕鱼——完整系统指南
钓鱼结合了船只导航,计时盘,设备升级和长期捕获分类账,在推进Marina关系的同时提供黄金,食物和配料,本指南涵盖了当前力学和可选Marina/Sashimi路线,场景结果留待游戏使用.

快速启动和开放时间

  1. 旅费 渔村海岸 在中世纪脱衣舞中进入 钓鱼船.
  2. 买个 木头罗德 (单位:千美元) 100金+10件普通材料允许10分钟。
  3. 选择 租赁渔船 在小屋里: 60金,300分钟 每件租赁用品 10个诱饵,在您的库存中添加到99个限值。需要一根棒;您可以从空饵量开始,因为租赁供应。
  4. 点击附近的环来引导船。 到达环会启动螺旋。 成功的螺旋鱼数 向会议支付。
  5. 返回后进行自动处理,然后再投资于下面的课,棒和其他升级.
那个小屋 24小时提供公共捕鱼服务. Marina 平时在那里工作 04:00–17:00; Tobbin 当她下班或离开时,盖住小屋,当Marina在小屋执勤时,Tobbin在海岸,Rod通过公共服务机构购买、诱饵、租赁和分类账。
Marina的个人交谈和升级需要 Marina本人出席. Tobbin 的报导没有让她的恋情动作一整夜都可用.在Knownd+,她还访问了海岸. 平日下午6:00-2:00. 缺勤会影响时间表。
下面的所有成本均为基本动作成本,租赁时间不同于迷你游戏的实时倒计时.

点击水来设定船的目的地;点击环向该位置方向移动。 船一定在50像素的环内 6个岩石/日志阻碍移动,因此选择无障碍环或绕过障碍物。

Sashimi和可选池路

这条路线提供了永久的钓鱼祝福,并开启了进一步的人物内容. 不需要通过Marina的H2H激活利维坦.
  1. 探索 普通邓肯楼13号 用于 Sashimi 的 三次相继相遇。这些是随机碰头,所以根据需要重新审视。第三个包括战斗。完成战斗就可以了。 Sashimi的吻改善环的产卵, 稀有和终生, 甚至在她到泳池之前。
  2. 盖妓院 庭院 和 池头 穿过Gastoff的建筑路线. Courtyard需要Republication 5, 3万黄金和10个史诗材料、原料和织物集合需要名声 6和法院, 20万黄金和25个史诗资源180分钟
  3. 三次相遇之后 和"池子"一起 完成一个钓鱼场 至少25条成功捕获的鱼。这触发了Sashimi的发现,如果她还没有移动到游泳池。无限的诱饵特别有用:仅出租诱饵就只能提供十次尝试。
  4. 访问池中 09:00–19:00 她的到来和关系通过她的谈话和天赋来建立她的关系 翻译 是她的性格特殊技能,是适用关系点总和的两倍。
  5. 时 朋友+ 时间轴她的自动 世界之间的野兽 剧情提供了利维坦探索激活 心目中的岛屿 解锁 Marina 的 前往锡雷纳岛的船只 行动。
对于她完整的性格走过,看 Sashimi。关于滑雪艇打开的目的地,见 西雷纳岛度假村. 路线的对话框和故事披露留给游戏使用.

Void 利维坦:解锁和捕捉路线

  1. 启动探索 无论是 完成 Marina 的 无法捕捉 H2H在 Friend+, 或 Sashimi 的 世界之间的野兽 池图在Friend+.

进步和解决问题

* 廉价开始 : Wood Rod和所提供的出租诱饵足够开始. Marina的课和Iron/Gold Rod直接减轻渔获量;Fisherman的升级需要地牢接入和Dragon Coins.
* 建立永久覆盖范围: 卡特彼勒·巴伊特移除了诱饵瓶颈. 艇艇的首次升级增加了一个四分之一速度的改进和15个更活跃的秒;更高的等级进一步提高了速度.
* 租金不见了: 进入小屋,检查你是否拥有一根棍子,并有60金.
* Marina的行动在晚上失踪: Tobbin提供公共服务;在Marina的茅屋值班期间因个人行为和升级而返回.
* 戒指在胶卷开始前消失: 在船的寿命到期前到达它。单独点击它不会立即钩上鱼。
* 彩色的戒指可以让鱼更便宜: 环色和鱼的稀有性是独立的卷。使用与当前棒或贝特大师技能相匹配的桌子。
* 捕鱼活动提前结束: 检查诱饵和正在运行的船定时器。失败的胶卷会消耗诱饵。无限诱饵会删除这个限制,而不是定时器。
* 分类账显示的金子比钱包多: 它跟踪总捕获值,在40%的加工份额和你的租金。
* 利维坦不会出现: 首先完成一个搜索激活路径。没有实际的每日时间捕获过滤器;重复的合格尝试仍然是随机的。
* Sashimi 尚未出现在池中 : 完成所有三个正常的13楼相遇,建造宝池,然后在一次外出中捕获25+鱼,累计的25个分类账都不够.
* 后面的船层不见了: 检查它的前身和当前 粉碎/浪漫主义 后两层都需要完整版本。

Fishing — Complete System Guide
Fishing combines boat navigation, a timing reel, equipment upgrades and a long-term catch ledger. It supplies gold, food and ingredients while progressing Marina's relationship. This guide covers the current mechanics and the optional Marina/Sashimi routes, with scene outcomes left for the game.

Quick Start and Opening Hours

  1. Travel to Fishing Village Coast in the Medieval Outskirts and enter the Fishing Hut.
  2. Buy a Wood Rod for 100 gold + 10 common materials; allow 10 minutes.
  3. Select Rent Fishing Boat inside the hut: 60 gold, 300 minutes of in-game time. Each rental supplies 10 bait, added to your stock up to its 99 limit. A rod is required; you can start with an empty bait stock because the rental supplies it.
  4. Click toward nearby rings to steer the boat. Reaching a ring starts the reel. Successfully reeled fish count toward that session's payout.
  5. Return for automatic processing, then reinvest in the lesson, rods and other upgrades below.
The hut's public fishing services are available around the clock. Marina normally works there 04:00–17:00; Tobbin covers the hut when she is off duty or away. When Marina is on hut duty, Tobbin is at the coast. Rod purchases, bait, rentals and the ledger remain available through the public service setup.
Marina's personal conversations and upgrades require Marina herself to be present. Tobbin's coverage does not make her relationship actions available all night. At Known+, she also visits the coast 18:00–20:00 on even days. Vacation absence can affect that schedule.
All costs below are base action costs. Rental time is distinct from the minigame's real-time countdown.

Rod upgrades replace the previous rod. Required hits cannot fall below one. Iron and Gold also improve the fish rarity tables relative to Wood, unless Master Baiter supplies its own stronger table.
Marina's Fishing Lesson: Acknowledged+, 500 gold and 120 minutes, one time. Permanently adds another 5° to the reel hit zone, stacking with the rod.
Old Fisherman's Lesson: find his random encounter on normal Dungeon Floor 13, pay 5 Dragon Coins. Permanently multiplies spinner speed by 0.85, making it 15% slower.
Master Baiter: learned from that encounter for another 5 Dragon Coins. Speeds up ring spawning and supplies the best fish-rarity table. Meeting the fisherman is random; simply reaching Floor 13 does not guarantee him.
Caterpillar Bait: Marina Acknowledged+ and 1,000,000 combined living caterpillars/adults. The action costs 0 gold and 0 minutes, does not consume the ecosystem population, and unlocks permanent unlimited fishing bait. It also improves ring frequency and rarity. See Caterpillar Rearing.
Before unlimited bait, reaching a ring consumes one bait even if you fail the reel. The next attempt with no bait ends the outing. Extra stock can therefore matter even though rentals include ten.

Boat Navigation

Clicking water sets the boat's destination; clicking a ring steers toward that position. The boat must come within 50 pixels of the ring to start a catch. Six rocks/logs obstruct movement, so choose accessible rings or steer around obstacles.
The base outing has 45 seconds of active boat time. The first boat upgrade raises it to 60 seconds; later upgrades keep that duration and improve movement. The countdown pauses during the reel, so an outing can take considerably longer than 45–60 seconds to play.
Base rings last three active seconds and spawn every 20 frames at 20 updates per second. Ring rarity begins at 60% common / 30% rare / 10% epic.
Permanent upgrade Ring interval change Ring rarity change Ring lifetime
Master Baiter −4 frames No change to ring colors; improves fish rarity instead Unchanged
Caterpillar Bait −4 frames Common −12 points, rare +4, epic +8 Unchanged
Sashimi's Kiss −3 frames Common −9 points, rare +4, epic +5 +0.5 sec
With all three, rings spawn every 9 frames (0.45 seconds), last 3.5 seconds, and use 39% common / 38% rare / 23% epic. These are ring chances. The fish inside a ring receives a separate rarity roll.
Prioritize reachable epic rings when looking for valuable or legendary fish. Chasing a distant ring behind an obstacle can waste more boat time than taking a nearby one.

The Reel Minigame

The target is a clock position shown as a Roman numeral. Click while the rotating marker crosses its highlighted arc. You must score the required hits before reaching the miss limit. A failed reel awards no fish, then returns you to the boat if the outing can continue.

The fish catalog's ledger level does not always equal the reel's difficulty. Crystal Bass and Shadowfin use difficulty 9; Godly Goldfish and Chrono Carp use 10, even where their catalog levels are lower. The catalog below lists the actual reel difficulty.

An active Luck Potion rolls the fish rarity twice and keeps the better tier. It does not select the species twice or favor a particular legendary species. For example, the Master Baiter epic-ring legendary chance rises from 15% to 27.75%: 1 − (1 − 0.15)². Reeling success is still required.
Fish descriptions include preferred hours, but the current catch selection does not filter by those hours. You do not need a dawn-only rental for the Void Leviathan. Its actual restriction is the quest unlock described below.

Processing, Payouts and the Ledger

After a session with at least one successful catch, the fish are processed automatically. Tobbin uses the same rewards as Marina and still awards Marina relationship progress, including the Leviathan bonus. A no-catch outing gives no processing rewards.
Player gold = gross session value − floor(40% of gross session value). That is roughly 60%, rounded upward where necessary. The gross total determines the reward tier; fish count and displayed weight do not determine it.

A session containing the Void Leviathan adds another 50 Marina points to that tier reward. These processing points are flat awards.
Example: a gross catch of 1,200 pays 720 gold, 10 food, 5 common + 3 rare + 1 epic ingredient and 25 Marina points. After the 60-gold rental, the outing's net gold gain is 660 before optional bait purchases.
View Fishing Ledger is free and instant at the hut. It records lifetime counts per species, largest caught size, total fish and gross catch value. Its “Total Gold Earned” is not your net wallet profit: it includes the processor's share and does not subtract rentals or equipment.

Sashimi and the Optional Pool Route

This route supplies a permanent fishing blessing and opens further character content. It is not required to activate the Leviathan through Marina's H2H.
  1. Explore normal Dungeon Floor 13 for Sashimi's three sequential water encounters. They are random encounters, so revisit as needed. The third includes a fight. Completing it grants Sashimi's Kiss, improving ring spawning, rarity and lifetime permanently, even before she is at the pool.
  2. Build the brothel Courtyard and Pool through Gastoff's building route. Courtyard requires Reputation 5, 30,000 gold and 10 epic materials, ingredients and fabrics each. Pool requires Reputation 6 and the Courtyard, 200,000 gold and 25 of each epic resource, taking 180 minutes.
  3. After all three encounters and with the Pool built, finish a fishing outing containing at least 25 successfully caught fish. This triggers Sashimi's discovery if she has not already moved to the Pool. Unlimited bait is especially useful: rental bait alone supplies only ten attempts.
  4. Visit the Pool during 09:00–19:00 for her arrival and relationship plots. Build her relationship through her available conversations and gifts. Transmutation is her character special skill; it doubles applicable relationship point totals.
  5. At Friend+, her automatic Beast Between Worlds plot offers the alternate Leviathan quest activation. Afterward, An Island in Mind unlocks Marina's Boat to Sirena Isle action.
For her full character walkthrough, see Sashimi. For the destination opened by the skiff, see Sirena Isle Resort. The route's dialogue and story reveals are left for the game.

Void Leviathan: Unlock and Catch Route

  1. Activate the quest by either completing Marina's The Impossible Catch H2H at Friend+, or Sashimi's Beast Between Worlds pool plot at Friend+.
  2. Fish normally. The Void Leviathan is now one of three eligible legendary species, uniformly selected after a legendary rarity roll. An epic ring plus Master Baiter gives a 15% legendary chance, and thus 5% Void chance per attempt while the quest is active, before reeling. With Luck Potion's rarity reroll, those become 27.75% legendary / 9.25% Void. These are probabilities, not guaranteed session rewards.
  3. Win its difficulty-10 reel. Gold Rod, both lessons and unlimited bait make repeated attempts easier. Boat tiers and Sashimi's Kiss help you reach more rings, but the quest does not require every upgrade.
  4. For Marina's The Impossible Wager, also complete her own H2H and reach Close Friend+, then use that action at her hut. Sashimi's quest activation alone does not fulfill the H2H requirement.
The catch flag is recorded through processing with either Marina or Tobbin. The follow-up scene is one time; its narrative payoff is left for the game.

Progression and Troubleshooting

* Start cheaply: Wood Rod and the supplied rental bait are enough to begin. Marina's lesson and Iron/Gold Rod directly ease catches; the Fisherman's upgrades require dungeon access and Dragon Coins.
* Build permanent coverage: Caterpillar Bait removes the bait bottleneck. The boat's first upgrade adds a quarter-speed improvement and fifteen more active seconds; higher tiers improve speed further.
* The rental is missing: enter the hut, check that you own a rod and have 60 gold. Bait stock is not an entry gate.
* Marina's action is missing at night: Tobbin supplies public services; return during Marina's hut duty for her personal actions and upgrades.
* A ring disappears before the reel starts: reach it with the boat before its lifetime expires. Clicking it alone does not instantly hook a fish.
* Epic-colored rings give cheaper fish: ring color and fish rarity are separate rolls. Use the table matching your current rod or Master Baiter skill.
* Fishing ended early: check bait and the active boat timer. Failed reels consume bait. Unlimited bait removes that limit, not the timer.
* The ledger shows more gold than the wallet gained: it tracks gross catch value, before the 40% processing share and your rental costs.
* The Leviathan will not appear: first complete one quest activation route. There is no actual time-of-day catch filter; repeated eligible attempts are still random.
* Sashimi has not appeared at the Pool: finish all three normal Floor 13 encounters, build the Pool, then catch 25+ fish in one outing. A cumulative ledger total of 25 is insufficient.
* A later boat tier is missing: check its predecessor and the current Crush / Romantic requirements. Both later tiers require the full version.
