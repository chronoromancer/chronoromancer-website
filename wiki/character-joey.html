Overview

Joey is the clerk at Corner Convenience Store in Present Uptown. Her current route includes Talk, Perfume, a Reading People lesson, a Perception reward, six personal automatic encounters, three evening Heart-to-Hearts and four dates. A shared café scene with Emily connects the later conversation chain. This guide keeps the personal story outcomes out of the walkthrough and flags the current unlock limitation below.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit Corner Convenience Store, 7 AM–11 PM, and use Talk, up to four times per day. No tutorial scene or affection stage is required to start talking.
  2. At Known, give Perfume, up to twice per day, and return 10 AM–8 PM for Inventory Rush. With Communication 35, choose Learn Reading People, costing 150 minutes, 35 Energy and 20 Calm, with no money fee.
  3. At Acquaintance, return 8 AM–6 PM for The Regular. It does not additionally require Inventory Rush.
  4. At Friend, choose Late Shift Quiet during 8–11 PM. With Communication 45, choose Counter Instinct once for Perception +1 and 8 direct affection. Take the Fair Date, starting 11 AM–3 PM, and the separate Voucher prize date, starting noon–6 PM.
  5. Reach Emily Friend and Joey Known, then visit Café Interior, noon–5 PM, for An Old Friend Walks In. Afterward, with Joey Friend, return to the store 10 AM–9 PM for Emily's Missed Call. Both encounters complete once.
  6. At Close Friend, after Inventory Rush, return 4–10 PM for Double Shift. After building Diane's Boardwalk, take Boardwalk Sunset Date, starting 5–9 PM.
  7. The authored next H2H is The Missing Years, at Close Friend, 7–11 PM, after Late Shift Quiet and Emily's Missed Call. Check the current-code limitation below: completing the call scene can fail to satisfy this action's unlock check. More affection or replaying dates does not repair that mismatch.
  8. For saves where The Missing Years is available or already completed, at Crush choose No Counter Between Us, starting 8–11 PM, and return 6–11 PM for Old Number. Take Stargazing Date, starting 7–11 PM; Stargazing is independent of the blocked H2H chain.
  9. For saves with the later chain completed, reach Romantic and return 9–11 PM after Old Number, Stargazing Date and No Counter Between Us for Late-Shift Light.
Her four dates have their own stage and location requirements. They do not require completing every earlier personal encounter. The Missing Years unlock limitation affects the later H2H chain, Old Number and Late-Shift Light, while her first H2H, ordinary interactions, lesson, stat reward and four dates remain independent of that gate.

Current Unlock Limitation

The intended prerequisite for The Missing Years is Emily's Missed Call completed, together with Close Friend and Late Shift Quiet. In the current game code, the call scene records completion in the plot system, while this H2H's unlock check looks for a separate completion value that the ordinary scene completion does not set.
As a result, The Missing Years can remain unavailable after you meet the visible story prerequisites. Existing saves that already carry the required unlock value or completed later conversations can differ. The later content is documented here with its authored requirements, rather than presented as guaranteed progression from a fresh save.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Joey's current route has no private relationship animation, ordinary apartment invitation, brothel recruitment, resort portrait, Kupie selection, or KTV/Night Club invitation configured. Her authored current content is covered above; avoid applying another NPC's route requirements to her.
  • Portrait missing: visit Corner Convenience Store in Uptown, 7 AM–11 PM, on any game day.
  • Perfume missing: reach Known and carry the inventory item Perfume.
  • Reading People missing: reach Known and Communication 35; the lesson disappears if already learned.
  • Counter Instinct missing: reach Friend and Communication 45. It is a one-time reward.
  • Emily's Missed Call missing: first complete An Old Friend Walks In at Café Interior, noon–5 PM, with Emily Friend and Joey Known. Then return to the store 10 AM–9 PM with Joey Friend.
  • The Missing Years still missing after the call: check Close Friend, Late Shift Quiet and 7–11 PM. If these are met, the current completion/unlock mismatch can block it; more affection or other dates do not resolve that gate.
  • No Counter Between Us or Old Number missing: both depend on The Missing Years, so the same route limitation can affect them.
  • Late-Shift Light missing: for a save with the later H2Hs completed, reach Romantic, finish Old Number and Stargazing Date, and visit 9–11 PM.
  • Voucher date did not complete Fair Date: they are separate events and completion states.
  • Stargazing missing despite completed earlier scenes: it starts at Crush, 7–11 PM; those earlier scenes are not extra date requirements.
  • Stock Shelves missing: check Stamina 10 and store opening hours, rather than Joey affection.
