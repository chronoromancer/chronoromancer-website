概览和时间表

Yrsa是一个19岁的中世纪勇士,有着良好的配角。 土匪藏身处,下午6点她的路线包括友谊活动、两次早期的战斗课、一次强力奖、Skull Crusher和Brute Stone 结对。个人历史和事件结果留给游戏。
最喜欢的数据 : 力量80、Stamina80和能力70她特殊的关系技巧是 贝塞尔克Rage目前没有安排普通的礼物、公寓、专注的心脏或工人招聘行动。
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.

Overview and Schedule

Yrsa is a19-year-old Medieval warrior with Good alignment. Find her at the Bandit Hideout,6 AM–6 PM, every day. Her route covers friendship events, two early combat lessons, a Strength reward, Skull Crusher and Brute Stone bonding. Personal history and event outcomes are left to the game.
Favorite stats: Strength80,Stamina80 and Ability70. Her special relationship skill is Berserker Rage. No ordinary gift, apartment, dedicated heart-to-heart or worker-recruitment action is currently configured.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Talk and Combat Lessons

Talk to Yrsa takes15 minutes and gives 1 actual base affection, five/day. Its action card lists zero points, but the handler awards one. Exhausted Talk submits a negative relationship result.

The two early lessons have no extra paid-version gate. Berserker Rage also activates Yrsa's special relationship bonus for subsequent base affection rewards. Barbarian Strength is a stat reward rather than a learned skill. Skull Crusher's30 is its plot completion award, not another30 added from the action.
In combat, Berserker Rage checks Power Strike while at least90% of maximum Health: a35% proc delivers its maximum damage and costs a random3–10% of maximum Health, rounded down with a minimum cost of one. Skull Crusher raises Power Strike damage by10% with truncation, then independently rolls30% to multiply that result by four. Intimidating Presence unlocks named encounter choices and other matching skill checks; it does not configure a universal fear roll on every attack.

Shared Healer Event and Stone Bonding

Not Again triggers once at Amelia's Healer's Hut,8 AM–7 PM, after Amelia's introduction, with Amelia Acknowledged+,Yrsa Friend+ and Yrsa home. It gives3 completion each. The second response, asking for context, addsYrsa3/Amelia1, for totals6/4. First gives5/5;third gives4/5. This does not recruit Yrsa or unlock an unlisted follow-up route.
At In Love, use Intimate Bonding at her Bandit Hideout portrait while owning Brute Stone. It takes60 minutes and raises the stone's level by one, once/day, with no configured extra money,Energy,Calm,affection or intimacy-experience award. It keeps the stone and does not acquire a missing one. Brute Stone adds one damage per level to Power Strike and Stormslash through their matching combat rule.

Troubleshooting

  • Portrait/actions missing: visit the Bandit Hideout between6 AM and6 PM.
  • Early skill lesson missing: reach Acquaintance,meet its Strength threshold and check whether already learned.
  • Strength reward missing: reach Known,Communication45 and check whether already claimed.
  • Skull Crusher missing: reach Romantic,Strength60 and paid access;ensure the skill is not already learned and enough resources remain.
  • Not Again missing: finish Amelia's introduction,meet both affection gates and visit her hut during its event window.
  • Bonding missing: reach In Love and already own Brute Stone.
