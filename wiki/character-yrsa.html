Overview

Yrsa offers combat instruction and a longer friendship route at the Bandit Hideout. Her current personal route includes a later heart-to-heart and follow-up meeting; it does not stop at the early confession.

Where to find Yrsa

Go to Medieval → Village Outskirts → Bandit Hideout, daily 06:00–18:00. A late-story allied deployment can temporarily move her to Allied Quarters; use current Objectives to follow that change. Only Talk and stone bonding follow her there, while location-specific lessons remain at home.

Route and relationship milestones

The early personal encounters appear at Known (25), Acquaintance (50), Friend (100), Close Friend (200), Crush (400) and Romantic (800). Complete all of them before Chosen Company, 14:00–17:00 at Romantic.

Close Enough follows Chosen Company, also at Romantic and 14:00–17:00. Room for Two then requires the early encounter set, Chosen Company and that heart-to-heart, 10:00–17:00. Her joint Amelia encounter is a separate Friend-stage visit at the healer’s hut.

Enter the indicated location during its scene window. These encounters complete once; fixed base relationship rewards below are separate from choice bonuses. A missing hour restriction means the scene itself has no extra clock gate.

Activities and rewards

Talk takes 15 minutes for 1 actual base point, five safe uses daily. Barbarian Strength costs 30 minutes at Known (25) and Communication 45, granting Strength +1 and 8 direct relationship points once.

Berserker Rage and Intimidating Presence each cost 120 minutes, $300, 50 Energy and 30 Calm, requiring Acquaintance (50) and their stat minimum. Skull Crusher requires Romantic (800), Strength 60 and content access; it costs 350 minutes, 180 Energy and 100 Calm and teaches the skill once.

An owned Brute Stone and In Love (3000) unlock Intimate Bonding for 60 minutes, stone level +1, once daily. Its allowance follows her between the hideout and the temporary allied quarters.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

概览

Yrsa在强盗藏身处提供战斗教学与较长的个人关系路线。当前内容在早期告白之后还包括后续谈心与会面，并非到告白就结束。

出现地点与时间

前往中世纪 → 村庄外围 → 强盗藏身处，每天06:00—18:00。后期盟友部署可暂时让她转往盟友居所，请按当前目标继续。只有交谈与灵魂石羁绊会随行，地点专属教学仍留在原处。

路线与关系进展

早期个人事件依次在已知（25）、相识（50）、朋友（100）、亲密朋友（200）、迷恋（400）、浪漫感（800）开放。全部完成后，浪漫感阶段14:00—17:00可推进只想和你作伴。

再近一点接在只想和你作伴之后，同样要求浪漫感和14:00—17:00。两个人的位置随后要求完成早期全套事件、只想和你作伴及该谈心，在10:00—17:00触发。Amelia共同事件则是朋友阶段独立的诊疗所拜访。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，实际获得1点基础关系，每日安全额度5次。野蛮人力量要求已知（25）、沟通45，花30分钟，一次性给予力量+1和8点直接关系。

狂战士之怒和威慑气场各消耗120分钟、300财富、50精力、30镇定，要求相识（50）及各自属性门槛。碎颅击要求浪漫感（800）、力量60及内容访问权限，消耗350分钟、180精力、100镇定，一次性学会对应技能。

持有蛮力之石并达到恋爱中（3000）后，可花60分钟进行亲密羁绊，增加一级灵魂石，每日一次。藏身处与临时盟友居所共用这份额度。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。

动作缺失时的检查

高好感不会自动补齐再近一点或两个人的位置所需的早期事件。盟友部署期间，藏身处头像会暂停，居所的交谈与羁绊仍使用原本的每日计数。
