Overview

Isolde’s current route includes an initial castle conversation, eight personal scenes, four dates and three heart-to-hearts. Several steps also require Prince Barnard or court events; outcomes are left for discovery.

Where to find them

Search the medieval Back Alleys for the Lost Noble encounter and choose to escort her five times. Each completed escort adds 60 min and gives 1 Epic Fabric; refusing does not count. After the fifth return, the search encounter stops and her courtyard portrait opens.

After unlock, courtyard 08:00–12:00 and 15:00–22:00. After reaching Acknowledged 10, garden 07:00–10:00 and Royal Bedroom 12:00–15:00 also appear.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Only completed escorts count toward five; this is a random Search encounter, not a guaranteed result every search.
  • After unlock, start the courtyard conversation before looking for her garden or bedroom portrait.
  • For a stalled castle step, check Barnard, the specified court event and dates as well as Isolde’s affection.

概览

Isolde当前有初次城堡谈话、八段个人事件、四次约会及三段交心。部分步骤还需要Prince Barnard关系或宫廷事件，具体结果留待发现。

去哪里找

在中世纪后巷搜索迷路贵族遭遇，选择护送她返回城堡，共完成五次。每次成功护送另加60分钟，并获得史诗布料×1；拒绝不计数。第五次返回后，该搜索遭遇停止，她的庭院头像开放。

解锁后，庭院行程为08:00–12:00及15:00–22:00；达到已遇见10后，花园07:00–10:00与皇家卧室12:00–15:00也开放。

卡住时检查什么

  • 只计成功完成的护送；这是随机搜索遭遇，不是每次搜索必出。
  • 解锁后先进行庭院谈话，再寻找花园或卧室头像。
  • 城堡路线卡住时，除Isolde好感外，还要检查Barnard、指定宫廷事件与约会。
