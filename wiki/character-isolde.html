Overview

Isolde is the foreign noblewoman associated with Prince Barnard's castle route. Find and escort the lost noble before meeting her as a castle NPC. Her current content includes a courtyard introduction, eight personal story scenes, four dates and three Heart-to-Hearts. This walkthrough gives their requirements and order while leaving the court and romance outcomes for you to discover.

Unlocking Isolde

  1. Go to the Medieval Back Alleys in the King's Demesne and use Explore Back Alleys. Each search costs 45 minutes and 15 Energy.
  2. When the lost noble encounter appears, choose “Agree to escort her to the castle (60 minutes)”. Escorting takes another 60 minutes, gives one Epic Fabric, increases the successful-return counter by one, and transports you to the Castle Courtyard. It also has a 25% Good-alignment roll.
  3. Repeat until you have completed five successful escorts. The fifth unlocks Isolde as a castle NPC and removes that lost-noble encounter from later alley searches.
  4. In the Castle Courtyard, choose A Decision to Stay during her courtyard portrait window. It takes 45 minutes, completes once and gives 25 base Isolde affection. There is no prior affection-stage requirement for this introductory action.
The lost noble has a 60% encounter roll after selection from the shared search pool, so a search does not guarantee her appearance. Refusing, or pickpocketing while giving directions, does not count as a successful escort. The optional pickpocket response requires Sleight of Hand and Thieves Guild clout 10; it is not a shortcut to the five returns.
If all five encounters happen on your first five searches, the search and escort time totals 525 minutes and 75 Energy, with five Epic Fabrics. Random searches can make it take longer. There is no separate money cost for the escorts.

Recommended Story and Romance Order

  1. After A Decision to Stay, visit the Castle Garden, 7–10 AM, at Acknowledged, for A Civil Prison.
  2. Meet Barnard through A Prince In The Room, an automatic Castle Courtyard, 11 AM–3 PM scene. Raise Isolde to Known and Barnard to Acknowledged, then visit the Courtyard, 3–10 PM, for The Prince in Profile after A Civil Prison and Barnard's introduction.
  3. At Isolde Acquaintance, take Garden Date from her Garden portrait, 7–10 AM, after The Prince in Profile. The date's completed flag is required for the next story encounter.
  4. At Isolde Friend and Barnard Known, visit the Throne Room, 9 AM–5 PM, for Terms of Endurance, after The Prince in Profile and Garden Date.
  5. At Isolde Known, take No More Alleys in the Garden, 7–10 AM, after A Civil Prison. At Friend, take The Treaty Seal in the Royal Bedroom, noon–3 PM, after Terms of Endurance and No More Alleys. These conversations form the H2H chain for the later manual romance scene.
  6. Continue Barnard's court route through The Prince Demands Respect. Reach Isolde Close Friend and Barnard Known, then visit the Garden, 7–10 AM, for A Walk Without Escape, after Terms of Endurance and that court scene.
  7. At Isolde Close Friend with at least 350 affection, visit the Royal Bedroom, noon–3 PM, for The Shape of Want, after A Walk Without Escape. Both the stage and numerical affection requirement are checked.
  8. At Crush, take Moonlit Courtyard Date, starting 7–10 PM, after The Shape of Want. Also take Not His Bride in the Royal Bedroom, noon–3 PM, after The Shape of Want and The Treaty Seal.
  9. Continue Barnard's court route through A Rumor With Good Breeding. With Isolde Crush and Barnard Friend, visit the Courtyard, 3–10 PM, for A Betrothal Without Theater, after The Shape of Want, Moonlit Courtyard Date and that court scene.
  10. At Isolde Romantic and Barnard Close Friend, take The Door Left Unlatched from Isolde's Royal Bedroom portrait, noon–3 PM. It requires A Betrothal Without Theater and Not His Bride completed, and takes 120 minutes, 35 Energy and Calm +10, once.
  11. Return to the Garden, 7–10 AM, with the same Romantic / Close Friend stages, for An Arrangement of Their Own, after The Door Left Unlatched.
  12. Finish the optional dates as desired. Festival Date unlocks at Close Friend after Terms of Endurance and Garden Date; Night Bazaar Date requires Romantic, A Betrothal Without Theater, Festival Date and Moonlit Courtyard Date.
Festival Date and Night Bazaar Date are not prerequisites to The Door Left Unlatched or the final Garden story scene. Festival Date is needed for Night Bazaar Date itself. The eight-story chain and the three-H2H chain have separate completion flags; raising affection alone does not complete them.

Current Route Boundaries

Isolde is explicitly not a Rose & Thorn worker candidate. There is no worker recruitment, training, apartment invitation, Kupie selection, ordinary gift or skill lesson configured for her. Her intimate route uses the manual castle story action and its following Garden encounter.
The current castle introduction is A Decision to Stay, rather than an automatic event immediately after one rescue. Five successful escorts are the unlock; completing the introductory portrait action begins the personal castle story chain.

Troubleshooting

  • Isolde is still appearing as a lost noble: complete five successful escorts. Giving directions through the pickpocket response does not increment the counter.
  • Courtyard Talk missing after unlock: Talk needs Acknowledged. Use A Decision to Stay first; it has no prior affection requirement.
  • Garden or Bedroom portrait missing: reach Acknowledged, then use 7–10 AM Garden or noon–3 PM Royal Bedroom. Princess Bedroom is a different location.
  • Talk stopped rewarding affection: all castle locations share five Talks per game day.
  • The Prince in Profile missing: finish both A Civil Prison and Barnard's A Prince In The Room, reach the two required stages, and visit after 3 PM.
  • The Shape of Want missing: finish A Walk Without Escape and meet both Close Friend and 350 affection.
  • Late story stalled: check the named court scene and brothel reputation prerequisite in Barnard's guide.
  • The Door Left Unlatched never starts automatically: choose it from Isolde's Royal Bedroom portrait after the final H2H, at Isolde Romantic / Barnard Close Friend.
  • Night Bazaar Date missing: it needs both Festival and Moonlit Courtyard dates, A Betrothal Without Theater and Romantic, starting 7–10 PM.
  • Another NPC occupies the location's portrait: use the available character-cycle control to select Isolde when multiple eligible NPCs overlap.
