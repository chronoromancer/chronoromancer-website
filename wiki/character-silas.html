Overview

Silas is the Exotic Goods Merchant in the Medieval King's Demesne. His current walkthrough includes repeatable Talk, Appraisal training, two one-time Commerce boosts, special-gift crafting, shop purchases, the Emerald Dragon trade involving Jade, brothel promotion, performance reactions and optional private-client bookings.

His current character age field is 57, even though an older backstory string gives a different age. His shop and personal portrait share the 6 AM–9 PM window. He has no current authored personal H2H, date, apartment conversation or NPC intimacy gallery.

Recommended Progression

  1. Visit the shop and use Talk to Silas, up to four positive times each day.
  2. If your exact proficiency is Salesman, claim Money Talks for Commerce +2.
  3. At Known, learn Appraisal if you need it, and claim Merchant Wisdom once you have Communication 70.
  4. Build Jade to Acquaintance, obtain the Emerald Dragon key item, and own the Rose & Thorn to use Trade Emerald Dragon from the shop's location menu.
  5. Continue Jade's own route after the trade. Recruiting her is a separate step, described on Jade.
  6. Once Jade is active as worker #15, use Ask Silas to Spread the Word for a one-time Promotion increase.
  7. Use Silas's repeatable crafted-gift trade when you need an Abyssal Looking Glass for another character's training upgrade.

Business Discussion with Jade

The one-time Business Discussion triggers at the shop 6 AM–9 PM, with Silas and Jade both Acknowledged, while Jade is home rather than vacationing.
It gives 5 base affection to each at completion. Its two question groups have no additional configured choice rewards, so choose the responses you prefer. This encounter is not an extra prerequisite on the Emerald Dragon trade.

Steal Valuables

The optional shop location action requires Sleight of Hand and Thieves Guild reputation 10. It takes 20 minutes and can be used once daily, whether you succeed or fail.
Its success chance is your modified Rogue stat as a percentage, capped at 75%. Success gives 1–4 Valuables, with a separate 25% chance of Thieves Guild clout +1. There is also a 25% chance of Evil alignment +1 on the attempt regardless of success. No Silas affection reward is configured.

Song Performance Reaction

Use Perform Song at the Performance Stage with a composed song. It takes 60 minutes and 30 Energy, once daily for that venue. A scene is rolled at 25%, improved by 5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling and owning Lute, capped at 50%. Silas is one of six Stage reactions selected randomly after a successful scene roll.
His best reply is Caring: “Glad I could help business, Silas. Music and commerce make good partners.” It gives 4 choice +8 completion =12 total base affection. Buddy gives 10 and Funny 9. The reaction is repeatable; performing does not guarantee that Silas will be selected.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Silas joins the Receive a Client roster when your brothel reputation reaches level 9. It is available at the Rose & Thorn entrance, with the Netorare option enabled, while operating as a brothel rather than a hotel. The roster selection is random, so starting a booking does not guarantee Silas.
This system has a shared once-per-day booking allowance. Accepting a completed visit takes 30 minutes and pays its offered fee directly, with possible tip and service-quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response uses the booking allowance and takes 10 minutes, without the accepted-visit payment.
Choose an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift, home rather than vacationing and not resting or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need at least 30 Tamed. Silas's family exclusion prevents assigning Jade. His authored client preference is poise and attentive service, with Vixen listed as his favorite. Special pairing scenes are authored for Bobo, Foxy and Vixen.
The current shared booking logic checks the worker's Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for service quality. Appearance and Charisma affect the tip range; they are not a replacement for the quality check. Even a very strong worker retains the system's chance of an unsuccessful result.
Private bookings award their worker and business rewards separately. They do not award ordinary Silas affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful praise bonus can award the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection; that is the worker's relationship, rather than Silas's.

Troubleshooting

  • Silas is missing: visit the shop between 6 AM and 9 PM.
  • Money Talks will not unlock: its check is exact Salesman proficiency, not Commerce level alone.
  • Appraisal disappeared: the lesson hides once Appraisal is learned through any source.
  • Trade Emerald Dragon is missing: check the shop location menu, Silas Known, Jade Acquaintance, establishment ownership and the actual Emerald Dragon key item.
  • Jade moved but is not an active worker: continue her Master Bedroom route and use A Special Connection at Crush.
  • Promotion is unavailable: Jade must already be active as worker #15; the trade itself is insufficient.
  • The crafted gift is unavailable: bring the three exact kinds and quantities of Infinity Dungeon drops; ordinary materials are not substitutes.
  • Silas never appears in Receive a Client: reputation level 9 unlocks him into a random roster, with the feature's option and operation-mode gates still applying.
  • There is no personal romance or intimacy gallery menu: Silas's current authored route is the conversations, trade, services and shared content described above.
