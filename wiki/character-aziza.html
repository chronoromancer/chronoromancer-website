Overview

Aziza combines an arcane shop with a full personal route. Equipment purchases, lessons, outings and private continuation use different relationship and completion requirements.

Where to find them

Her portrait is at Arcane Shop daily 10:00–18:00. Start personal dates and lessons there; the two later outings have deliberately narrow one-hour start windows.

Route and relationship milestones

Meet her in the shop, then use the two early heart-to-hearts and automatic scenes below. At Friend 100, complete What the Wards Hold, then A Small School of Thought10:00–17:00.

Complete the bazaar date, then Light-Fish Walk at Close Friend 200,14:00–15:00. Afterwards The Useful Part needs Crush 400,10:00–17:00. Under the Stars follows 16:00–17:00, also requiring The Old Book.

Purchase Grimoire upgrades in order 2→3→4→5; the exact previous equipment level is required each time. Sixth Binding is separate:In Love 3000, exact level 5,180 min/80 Energy/40 Calm, subject to its content lock.

Sorcerer awakens Sorcerer once:In Love 3000, Lime recruited, floor 40 miniboss defeated and Sashina's Gift learned. It costs 300 min/150 Energy/150 Calm and needs the indicated content access.

Craft Prismatic Spell Brooch exchanges 2 Soul Thread,4 Shimmering Heartscale,4 Gilded Heart for one Prismatic Spell Brooch,0 min/no money. These are Infinity Dungeon trophies; see Forge for the shared recipes.

Carry Elemental Stone for its recognition scene. At In Love 3000, her stone bonding takes 60 min and adds 1 level, once/day.

Favorite stats are Charm, Mystic, Ability. Learning Arcane Knowledge improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

The Arcane Shop location menu also offers the Grimoire 1→2 upgrade for$1,000/30 min, while you still have level 1. Its separate Magical Artifact Tutorial costs$200/60 min once and gives 3 Wizard Clout plus 3 base Aziza affection. At Close Friend 200, owning Rose & Thorn also unlocks Ask Aziza to Spread the Word at 10:00–18:00:$500/0 min, once, adding 1 promotion level. Potion purchases and the wider faction progression are in the Wizard guide.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Light-Fish Walk starts 14:00–15:00; Under the Stars starts 16:00–17:00. Arriving later cannot start them even if the shop remains open.
  • For sixth Grimoire binding, check exact level 5 and In Love; the ordinary purchases stop at 5.
  • Showing pregnancy suspends the normal physical activities. The separate early/final pregnancy activity remains available during its own hours.

概览

Aziza同时经营奥术商店并拥有完整个人路线。装备购买、课程、出游与私密续篇各有关系及事件要求。

去哪里找

每天10:00–18:00在奥术商店。个人约会和课程从这里开始；后两次出游的开始窗口只有一小时。

路线与关系进展

先在商店见面，再进行早期两段交心及下表自动事件。朋友100完成结界之下后，可于10:00–17:00触发小小鱼群的大学问。

完成集市约会后，亲密朋友200可在14:00–15:00开始光鱼漫步。之后有用的那部分要求迷恋400、10:00–17:00；再于16:00–17:00开始星空之下，另需完成古老魔导书。

魔导书按2→3→4→5逐级购买，每次要求恰好拥有前一级。第六重缚印是独立升级，要求恋爱中3000、恰好5级，180分钟、80精力、40镇定，并满足内容权限。

术士仅能觉醒一次，要求恋爱中3000、已招募Lime、击败第40层小头目并学会Sashina的恩赐；300分钟、150精力、150镇定，另需内容权限。

制作棱彩法术胸针用灵魂之线×2、微光心鳞×4、鎏金之心×4兑换棱彩法术胸针×1，0分钟、不收费。这些材料来自无限地下城，通用配方见锻造指南。

持有元素之石可触发认可事件；恋爱中3000可进行羁绊，60分钟、等级+1，每天一次。

偏好的属性为魅力、神秘、能力。学会奥术知识可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

奥术商店地点菜单另提供魔导书1→2升级，要求仍为1级，消耗$1,000、30分钟。独立的“魔法神器教程”消耗$200、60分钟，仅一次，增加3巫师声望和3基础好感。亲密朋友200、拥有玫瑰与荆棘时，还可在10:00–18:00进行「请Aziza帮忙宣传」，$500、0分钟，仅一次，宣传等级+1。药剂购买及完整阵营进度见巫师指南。

卡住时检查什么

  • 光鱼漫步在14:00–15:00开始，星空之下在16:00–17:00开始。过了窗口，即使商店仍营业也不能开始。
  • 第六次魔导书绑定需要恰好5级及恋爱中；普通购买只到5级。
  • 显怀会暂停普通身体活动；独立的怀孕私密活动仍按前期／末期及自身时段开放。
