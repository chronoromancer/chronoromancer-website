Overview

Myriss is a 27-year-old Medieval dark elf warrior recruited through the ordinary dungeon's Dark Elf Warrior encounters. Her current content includes daily Talk, four personal animations, five shared corridor scenes, and the separate monster care and eight-level training system.
This guide explains recruitment, requirements and rewards without revealing the personal stories inside her scenes.

Recommended Walkthrough and Recruitment

  1. Own the Rose & Thorn, build Cell Corridor, and prepare a built, vacant cell among cells 2–8. Cell 1 is reserved for Bobo and does not count as an available recruitment cell.
  2. Defeat the named Dark Elf Warrior in the ordinary dungeon, floors 13–23. The separate enemy named Dark Elf does not count toward Myriss recruitment.
  3. For a reliable encounter, enter the hidden wall on floor 18 at x=1, y=6 while Cell Corridor is built and Myriss is not already active. In one-based grid counting, this is the second column, seventh row.
  4. Win five Dark Elf Warrior battles. Recruitment rolls begin on the sixth victory, at min(100, 10 + 10 × (total victories − 5) + player Taming)%. The first roll is 20% + Taming, with ten extra percentage points per later victory.
  5. On a successful roll with a cell available, choose recruit. The handler assigns her to that cell, activates her monster record and adds 10 direct ordinary affection.
  6. Visit the actual assigned cell for her Acknowledged+ portrait. Recruitment's direct affection normally supplies that initial stage. Use daily Talk to reach Known, unlocking her first personal animation.
  7. Build toward Friend, Crush, then Romantic for the remaining animations. None requires completing a previous animation.
  8. Raise the relevant corridor companions to Friend as well, then re-enter Cell Corridor for the five shared scenes.
  9. Use monster management for care and training. Myriss starts at the 30 tamed training threshold; feeding uses 5 Food and restores 10 tamed when she falls below it.
Infinity Dungeon does not increase recruitment counters or offer captures. Use the ordinary dungeon. A successful capture roll without a built vacant cell does not recruit her; prepare a cell and win another named battle. Choosing release also leaves her inactive.

Battle Preparation

The Dark Elf Warrior has 51 base HP, a 1.5 damage multiplier, 12% dodge, 15% damage reduction, and 20 magic resistance, before shared floor/difficulty scaling. Her attacks include Shadow Strike, Magic Missile and Magical Vortex.
She is strong against Dark, weak to Light, and absorbs Dark at 90%. Prepare recovery and appropriate equipment for floors 13–23. The floor-18 hidden wall starts the regular enemy battle and recruitment system; it does not grant automatic recruitment after one win.

Assigned Cell, Talk and Affection

Her portrait is available in her actual assigned numbered cell, all hours every day, while active and at Acknowledged or higher. The placeholder cell in her character definition is replaced by the recruitment assignment. The Cell Corridor is the location for shared scenes, rather than her ordinary Talk portrait.
Talk to Myriss costs 15 minutes and gives 1 base affection, up to three uses daily. All three cost 45 minutes for 3 base affection. She has no ordinary gift-giving action and no personal player-skill lesson.
Beyond the Talk allowance, her handler submits a negative affection reward. Stop after the three successful daily uses rather than spending more time on failed attempts. A monster-care visit uses its own daily system and does not replace the ordinary Talk counter.

Daily Monster Care

Select active Myriss through monster management. She starts with 30 tamed, 70 Health, 0 Stress and 0 monster experience. A care session is unavailable while she is resting or after its daily visit is spent.
  • Feed uses 5 Food and adds 10 tamed, normally capped at 100. Starting at 30, a successful initial feeding reaches 40. If you lack Food, obtain it and retry the feeding.
  • A session has separate allowances for Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Feeding and training can occur in the same visit.
  • Teach and Training require 30 tamed or higher. Her initial 30 meets the threshold, but daily decay can put her below it. High ordinary affection does not replace the tame requirement.
  • Discipline tool effects vary in tame, Stress, Health and monster experience. Inspect the chosen tool; Claw uses a higher tame cap of 150.
  • Teach selects dialogue topics; it does not grant the player Shadow Grip, Intimidation or Intimidating Presence.
  • After the first care visit, her stored tame value decays by 6 per game day. Personal animations can help restore tame, alongside feeding and other care.
  • One Smelling Salts reduces Stress by 10 for all active monsters through the care interface.
Feeding and monster training have no separate direct ordinary Myriss-affection reward configured. Their purpose is the monster-care progression described here.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • No recruitment offer: defeat Dark Elf Warrior, rather than Dark Elf, in the ordinary dungeon. Rolls start after five victories; recruitment also needs a built vacant cell 2–8.
  • The hidden wall is blocked: use floor 18, x=1, y=6, build Cell Corridor, and check whether Myriss is already active.
  • Her portrait is missing: visit the actual assigned numbered cell, check active recruitment, and reach Acknowledged.
  • A later animation is missing: check its Known / Friend / Crush / Romantic stage, paid access, resource cost and its individual daily use. Prior animations are not required.
  • Training is locked despite affection: restore 30 tamed, then check resting and daily care status.
