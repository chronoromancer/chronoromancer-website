概览

EPOM——强化精密整形大师. -是那个... TD 堡垒总工程师 他目前的内容侧重于工程服务:日常对话、战斗扫描仪植入、总工程师培训、物质分散器资源平衡、5个可重复的陆军装备行业、永久性Turret车间研究和主楼理事会外观。

物质迁移器

选择 物质迁移器 以重新分配玩家的普通 材料、织物和材料 均匀 每个稀有的:常见,稀有和Epic分别处理.

转换工作按此顺序进行:
  1. 旋转 2-10 随机选择的工艺资源 从所有三个资源类别和稀有层次的库存总量中选择。随机选择按持有的数量加权。
  2. 在每个稀土中添加剩余材料,织物和成分.
  3. 除此稀有总数三, 向下转,并将这一数额分配给这三类。
四舍五入弃物 0 - 2个追加资源例如,如果随机成本后一个稀有值的总和是 31,结果是 10种材料、10种织物和10种原料; 剩下的一个丢失。它不会升级为 rare/ Epic, 也不会创造更多的总资源 。
先用确认是确认,而不是改变后来转换成本的解锁. 回收合金、电路、试剂、奢侈品和纯油芯是单独的库存,而不能与这项服务保持平衡。

Overview

EPOM — Enhanced Precision Ordnance Master — is the TD Fortress Chief Engineer in the Future era. His current content focuses on engineering services: daily conversation, the Combat Scanner implant, Master Engineer training, Matter Displacer resource balancing, five repeatable Army equipment trades, permanent Turret Workshop research and a main-story council appearance. He currently has no personal H2H, date, apartment or romance-scene chain.

Starting His Route

  1. Reach the Future TD Fortress Operations Center and choose Engineering. This internal travel costs 0 minutes.
  2. Enter Engineering, 6 AM–10 PM, for the one-time introduction The Engineer. It grants 3 fixed base affection. The Funny response adds +3, Kind adds +2 and Cold adds +1.
  3. Use Talk to EPOM up to three times per day, at 15 minutes per conversation. His handler records 1 base point, despite the action metadata listing 0. The fourth attempted conversation produces a negative-point response.
  4. At Acquaintance, with Intelligence 20, install Combat Scanner. This is a one-time skill route, hidden after the skill is learned.
  5. At Friend, with Technology 60, take Master Engineer Training. This teaches Master Engineer once and grants 20 base affection.
  6. His Matter Displacer, Army trades and Turret Workshop have no separate affection-stage prerequisite. Use them when you have the relevant resources; they do not replace conversation for building affection.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Combat Scanner's energy and calm deductions are applied by its installation handler, so they are real costs even though its generic action stat-effect field is empty. The skill adds the enemy combat-data display during normal battles, including health, element and power information.
The Master Engineer lesson requires Technology 60 at EPOM; the skill's general Technology 50 definition does not lower this lesson's action gate. Neither lesson requires an additional personal H2H or a gift.

Matter Displacer

Choose Matter Displacer to redistribute the player's ordinary materials, fabrics and ingredients evenly within each rarity: Common, Rare and Epic are processed separately.
The action needs at least 10 total crafting resources in one rarity tier. It has 0 minutes, no configured money, energy or calm payment, and no affection prerequisite or affection award. It is repeatable while the resource condition remains satisfied.
The conversion works in this order:
  1. Consume 2–10 randomly chosen crafting resources from the total inventory across all three resource categories and rarity tiers. The random selection is weighted by the quantities held.
  2. Add the remaining materials, fabrics and ingredients within each rarity.
  3. Divide that rarity total by three, round down, and assign that amount to each of the three categories.
Rounding discards 0–2 additional resources per rarity, up to six across all three rarities. For example, if a rarity's total after the random cost is 31, the result is 10 materials, 10 fabrics and 10 ingredients; one remainder is lost. It does not upgrade Common into Rare/Epic, and it does not create more total resources.
The first-use confirmation is an acknowledgement, not an unlock that changes later conversion costs. Reclamation Alloy, Circuitry, Reagents, Flux and Pristine Cores are a separate inventory, and are not balanced by this service.

Turret Workshop

Open Turret Workshop from EPOM's Engineering menu. Opening the workshop takes 0 minutes, with no entry payment or affection requirement. Purchases use Reclamation resources and Army EXP, and do not award EPOM affection.
Select a turret design to compare its current specification with the next level, then Purchase design if unowned or Upgrade if already acquired. Steamshot starts at level 1. A new design purchase establishes level 1; each design has an independent permanent research level with a maximum of 10. Research unlocks the design for battle; battle deployment still uses that battle's own resources and limits.

Army Role and Main-Story Appearance

EPOM begins acquired in the TD Army roster. Army equipment and unit upgrades are managed through the Army/Barracks system, separate from his ordinary relationship stats and skill lessons. His five trades can provide equipment for eligible Army units, rather than being gifts to raise his affection.
For Story Beat 34, visit the Operations Center after Story Beat 33 completed, at any hour. It completes once and grants 5 fixed base EPOM affection. The Funny strategy response adds +3 EPOM and +2 AIMy; the later assignment choice does not have an EPOM reward. This story event has no EPOM affection or skill prerequisite. Its narrative outcome is left for the game.

If Progress Stalls

  • Find him in Engineering, 6 AM–10 PM. The Operations Center is a different location.
  • Talk gives 1 base point, three times per day. Resource trades and turret purchases give no affection.
  • Combat Scanner requires Acquaintance and Intelligence 20; account for its full 150 energy and 80 calm cost as well as time and money.
  • Master Engineer requires Friend and Technology 60, despite the skill definition's lower threshold.
  • Matter Displacer needs 10 resources in one rarity; having 10 spread across several rarities does not satisfy that check. It consumes resources and rounding leftovers.
  • Army trades and research need Reclamation resources, rather than the ordinary materials/fabrics/ingredients balanced by Matter Displacer.
  • Initial turret purchases and later research upgrades have different costs. At level 10, that design is fully upgraded.
  • There is currently no EPOM H2H, apartment visit, date or ordinary gift route to unlock by raising affection further.
