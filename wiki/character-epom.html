Overview

EPOM offers combat and engineering lessons, crafting-resource redistribution and Army equipment trades. These services are his current personal activities, with no separate romance chain.

Where to find them

After Story 31 opens the command center beneath Cid’s clinic, choose Engineering. EPOM is there daily 06:00–21:59. The navigation caption is Engineering; the Body Modifications screen uses its own engineering header.

Route and relationship milestones

Visit during his shift for The Engineer. At Acquaintance (50) and Intelligence 20, his Combat Scanner lesson takes 120 minutes, $500, 150 Energy and 80 Calm. The Energy and Calm costs apply even though some older menus omit them.

The EngineerEngineering; 06:00–21:59

At Friend (100) and Technology 60, Master Engineer training takes 300 minutes, $900, 150 Energy and 80 Calm. Both skills are learned once.

Professional Appreciation is a one-time Army gift at 80 Resistance Clout: EPOM must be acquired in the Army, which is his default roster state. It takes 0 minutes, does not spend Clout and grants Berserker Reactor.

Matter Displacer needs at least 10 crafting resources within one rarity. It removes 2–10 random resources, then divides each rarity’s remaining materials, fabrics and ingredients equally; division leftovers are lost. Rarities stay separate. It takes 0 minutes and can be repeated.

EPOM also makes five repeatable Army items from Reclamation Bay materials. Trades take 0 minutes and put equipment in Army storage. Use the Army guide for the five exact recipes and effects; Body Modifications remain a separate installation service.

Turret Workshop and Grenade Workshop open from his Engineering portrait at 0 minutes. Purchases inside spend Reclamation materials and Army EXP and advance independent research up to level 10; see Workshops for each track’s exact costs.

Favorite stats are Technology, Intelligence, Strength. The learned Engineering Genius skill improves her relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Combat Scanner also deducts 150 Energy and 80 Calm. Restore beforehand if you want to avoid finishing with depleted resources.
  • For Matter Displacer, 10 resources spread across different rarities do not meet the single-rarity threshold.
  • The redistributor spends resources and can remove small leftovers; inspect the balances before using it.
  • For the Army gift check Resistance Clout, not EPOM affection. For trades bring Reclamation materials.

概览

EPOM提供战斗与工程技能课、制作资源重分配和军团装备交换，目前没有另一条个人恋爱路线。

去哪里找

主线31开放Cid诊所下方的指挥设施后，进入工程室。他每天06:00—21:59在场。导航显示工程室，身体改造界面的标题另为要塞工程部。

路线与关系进展

在班次内到访进行工程师。相识（50）、智力20后，可学习战斗扫描器，花费120分钟、$500、150精力与80镇定；即使旧菜单漏写，精力和镇定仍会扣除。

自动事件地点／开始时段条件
工程师工程室；06:00–21:59

朋友（100）、科技60后，可进行工程大师训练，花费300分钟、$900、150精力和80镇定。两项技能都只学习一次。

专业人士的谢意是80反抗军声望的一次性军团礼物，要求EPOM已加入军团；他属于默认军团成员。事件不消耗时间或声望，给予狂战士反应堆。

物质置换器要求至少一个稀有度内共有10份制作资源，先随机扣除2—10份资源，再把每个稀有度剩余的材料、布料、食材平均分配，不能整除的余数会丢失，不会跨稀有度转换。不消耗时间，可重复使用。

他还可用再生工坊材料反复制作五种军团物品，不消耗时间，成品进入军团装备库。五项准确配方与效果见军团指南；身体改造是另一项独立安装服务。

从工程室头像可打开炮塔工坊和手榴弹工坊，打开不消耗时间。内部购买使用回收材料与军团经验，各研究独立提升，最高10级；每条路线的准确消耗见工坊指南。

偏好的属性为科技、智力、力量。学会工程天才可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 战斗扫描器课程还会扣除150精力和80镇定，建议先恢复，避免训练后资源耗尽。
  • 资源散落在多个稀有度、合计10份，并不等于单一稀有度达到门槛。
  • 置换会扣资源，也会损失少量余数，使用前先检查余额。
  • 军团礼物看反抗军声望，不看EPOM好感；交换装备则要带回收材料。
