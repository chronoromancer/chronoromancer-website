Overview

Diane handles public construction at the Town Hall office. Her commissions open useful venues, while the Responsibility lesson improves work-experience growth. These services do not require a high relationship stage.

Where to find Diane

Go to Present → Uptown → Town Hall → Town Hall Office. After the boardwalk is restored, she also walks there 17:00–20:00. The office and evening Talk actions have separate daily allowances.

Route and relationship milestones

Start with the office introduction. Complete Katie’s tutorial visit to open the one-time Responsibility lesson. Her boardwalk date requires Close Friend(200), the finished boardwalk and the corresponding additional-content access.

The Bulk Store commission appears after Joey’s The Bigger Picture. It is the fifth project, rather than a basic construction choice available immediately.

Activities and rewards

Every public commission takes 180 minutes. The prices are$1,000 for the patron statue,$1,000,000 for the butterfly pavilion,$2,000,000 for the public pool,$5,000,000 for the boardwalk and$10,000,000 for the Bulk Store. The venue opens on completion; there is no additional construction-day countdown.

The statue is reached through the park; the pavilion and public pool are in the university area; the boardwalk connects to the beach. Do not confuse these public projects with Gastoff’s brothel construction. Responsibility gives an independent 25% chance for+1 extra Working Experience per completed job.

Missing actions and useful checks

For the Bulk Store, finish Joey’s preceding personal objective and check the office menu again. For Responsibility, complete the Katie tutorial visit rather than increasing Diane’s affection.

If Diane is absent from the boardwalk, verify that restoration has completed and start before 20:00. Office Talk allows 5 safe uses per day; boardwalk Talk allows 3. Ordinary positive relationship gains use the shared modifiers.

概览

Diane在市政厅办公室办理公共建设。委托会开放实用地点，责任感课程则改善工作经验成长；这些服务不要求先刷到很高的关系阶段。

出现地点与时间

路线为现代 → 上城区 → 市政厅 → 市政厅办公室。木板路修复后，她还会在17:00—20:00前往木板路散步。办公室与傍晚木板路交谈使用不同的每日额度。

路线与关系进展

先完成办公室的初次见面，再完成Katie的教程拜访，即可开放一次性的责任感课程。木板路约会则要求亲密朋友（200）、木板路已建成，以及相应追加内容访问权限。

大包装商店委托需要先完成Joey的「更大的蓝图」。这是第五项建设，不是初到办公室就能直接购买的基础项目。

行动与奖励

每项公共委托均耗180分钟：赞助人雕像财富1,000，蝴蝶馆1,000,000，公共泳池2,000,000，木板路5,000,000，大包装商店10,000,000。完成后即可开放，不需要再等额外施工天数。

雕像从公园进入，蝴蝶馆和公共泳池在大学区域，木板路连接海滩。它们与Gastoff的青楼建设是不同项目。责任感会让每次完成工作时，独立有25%概率额外获得1点工作经验。

动作缺失时的检查

找不到大包装商店委托时，先完成Joey的前置个人目标，再回办公室查看。找不到责任感课程时，要检查Katie的教程拜访，不是继续刷Diane关系。

木板路没有Diane时，确认修复已完成，并在20:00前到达。办公室交谈每日安全额度5次，木板路交谈3次；普通正向关系奖励仍按共用加成计算。
