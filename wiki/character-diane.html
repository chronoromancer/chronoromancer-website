Overview

Diane is the Town Hall clerk in Present Downtown, handling civic commissions and a practical Responsibility lesson. Her current personal content includes an introduction, a shared Katie scene, four construction scenes and a Boardwalk Sunset Date. Her role also appears in Story Beat 55; this guide lists its access and costs without revealing the outcome.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Town Hall Office for the automatic, one-time Welcome to Town Hall introduction. Choose Professional for the best +3 choice points, in addition to 1 base affection.
  2. Talk to Diane during office hours, up to five times per day. Civic commissions have no affection prerequisite beyond Stranger; choose the project whose location you want to open and bring its payment.
  3. After Katie Explains What She Likes, use Diane's Responsibility lesson at the Office. It costs 60 minutes, 30 energy and 30 calm, grants the Responsibility skill, and completes once. The best response is Caring +3, in addition to 5 base affection.
  4. After Katie Explains Jobs and Gifts and Diane's introduction, visit the Office, 9 AM–5 PM, for Family Matters, a one-time shared scene. Favor Diane for her largest choice award.
  5. Use Restore the Boardwalk, then complete its construction scene. This opens the Boardwalk travel action from Uptown Beach, Diane's 5–8 PM portrait there and her separate evening Talk allowance.
  6. At Close Friend, use Boardwalk Sunset Date from her Boardwalk menu 5–8 PM, with the paid version and Higher Power/supporter access. The date completes once.
Her current character definition has no personal H2H chain, apartment encounter or worker recruitment. Responsibility, shared scenes, civic commissions and the Boardwalk date are the activities to complete.

Main-Story Appearance

Story Beat 55 — The Counter-Offer triggers automatically at the Town Hall Office, 9 AM–5 PM, after Story Beat 54, with $10,000,000 wealth. It has no minimum Diane-affection requirement. During ordinary main-story progression, completion spends $10,000,000 and advances 45 minutes.
Its fixed relationship reward is 20 base affection with Diane and 20 with Christy. The Good response gives the largest combined choice reward, +5 each; Neutral gives +3 each, Evil gives Diane +1 / Christy +4, and Unaligned gives +2 each. Good, Neutral and Evil choices also carry their matching alignment tags. The scene's result is left for you to discover in the story.

If Progress Stalls

  • Her ordinary Office menu is available 9 AM–5 PM. After restoration, find her at the Boardwalk, 5–8 PM.
  • The Responsibility lesson waits for Katie Explains What She Likes. Family Matters waits for Katie Explains Jobs and Gifts and Diane's introduction; these are different tutorial milestones.
  • A completed commission disappears once its building is flagged. Use the new destination's travel button, from the Park, University Hall or Beach as listed above.
  • For Boardwalk Sunset Date, reach Close Friend and meet both the paid-version and Higher Power/supporter checks. Restoring the boardwalk alone does not meet those access requirements.
  • Story Beat 55 needs Story Beat 54 and $10,000,000 wealth. More affection cannot replace those requirements.
  • There is no current gift, H2H, apartment or recruitment button to unlock for Diane. Her implemented personal content is covered above.
