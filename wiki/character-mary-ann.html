Overview

Mary Ann manages the Butterfly Pavilion. Her route links caterpillar discoveries, fieldwork at the Hidden Waterfall, three heart-to-hearts and four dates. A later specimen gift helps Willow’s medicine work.

Where to find Mary Ann

Commission the Butterfly Pavilion through Diane before visiting the university venue. Mary Ann is there on even days, 10:00–17:00. At Known (25), she also appears at the Hidden Waterfall: odd days 07:00–15:00 and every evening 18:00–22:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Follow the automatic fieldwork milestones below. Field Notes follows the species-count encounter at Friend (100). Chrysalis Window needs the waterfall microclimate encounter, Close Friend (200) and the first heart-to-heart. Lantern Moths follows the field report and the second heart-to-heart at Crush (400).

The later record requires Story 55. Complete the last survey after that record and Lantern Moths to unlock the remote home visit. The survey starts on odd days, 07:00–14:00, at the waterfall.

Activities and rewards

Talk gives 1 base point, five safe uses shared between both locations. At Known, Book gives 8 and Flowers gives 5 base points, each once daily. Review Caterpillars costs 30 minutes and 6 Energy, once daily: an empty collection gives 2 base points; a discovered species gives at least 3, rising by 1 for each five species, to a maximum of 10.

Learn Researching requires Intelligence 35 and Known (25). Field Observation grants Perception +1 once. At In Love (3000), Some Quiet to Grow at the pavilion gives Hushbell Seeds for Willow; it is once-only, requires the pavilion introduction and takes 20 minutes. The gift does not directly raise Medicine.

At Crush (400), after the field report, KTV and Night Club invitations are available 20:00–23:00. Each venue has one daily invitation shared across its guest list. The home visit is separate: Residential Block, 22:00–24:00, 120 minutes and 50 Energy, once daily, after the final survey.

Listed relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers can change the final gain. Daily allowances for the same action follow the character across locations; starting it elsewhere does not create a fresh allowance.

Missing actions and useful checks

Boardwalk, Fair and Voucher Fair invitations are bound to the pavilion portrait: plan for an even day. Chrysalis Window starts on an odd morning, 08:00–15:00; the daily evening waterfall portrait does not make that morning conversation available at dusk.

概览

Mary Ann管理蝴蝶馆。她的路线结合毛虫发现、隐藏瀑布的实地调查、三段谈心与四项约会；后期还能取得用于Willow药品研究的标本。

出现地点与时间

先委托Diane建造蝴蝶馆，再前往大学内的场馆。Mary Ann在双数日10:00—17:00出现。达到已知（25）后，也可在隐藏瀑布见她：单数日07:00—15:00，以及每天18:00—22:00。

路线与关系进展

按下表推进自动调查事件。达到朋友（100）并完成物种统计事件后开放田野笔记。蛹之窗要求完成瀑布小气候事件、第一段谈心，以及亲密朋友（200）关系。灯蛾则需要调查报告、第二段谈心和迷恋（400）。

后期记录事件要求主线55。记录与灯蛾都完成后，在单数日07:00—14:00到瀑布完成最后的调查，才会开放远程的居家拜访。

行动与奖励

交谈每次获得1点基础关系，两处地点共用每日5次安全额度。已知阶段可送书获得8点、送花获得5点，各自每日一次。查看毛虫消耗30分钟、6精力，每日一次：空收藏奖励2点；已有发现时至少3点，每发现5种再加1点，最高10点直接关系。

学习研究要求智力35及已知（25）；实地观察一次性给予感知+1。达到恋爱中（3000）后，可在蝴蝶馆进行让宁静萌芽，取得交给Willow的静铃花种子；需要完成蝴蝶馆初见，消耗20分钟，只能取得一次，不会直接增加医学。

达到迷恋（400）并完成调查报告后，20:00—23:00可在KTV或夜总会发出邀请，各场所的全部来宾共用每日一次额度。居家拜访是另一项活动：最后调查完成后，在住宅街区22:00—24:00开始，消耗120分钟、50精力，每日一次。

除注明直接增加外，所列关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量会受共通关系修正影响。同一动作的每日额度跟随角色计算，换地点不会获得新额度。

动作缺失时的检查

木板路、游园会和兑换券约会绑定蝴蝶馆头像，记得安排在双数日。蛹之窗实际在单数日08:00—15:00开始；每天傍晚出现的瀑布头像不会把这段上午谈心延长到晚上。
