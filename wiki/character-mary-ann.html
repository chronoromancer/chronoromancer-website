Overview

Mary Ann is the Present-era lepidopterist who manages the university's Butterfly Pavilion. Her current route includes caterpillar collection reviews, Researching training, seven automatic encounters, three linked heart-to-hearts, four dates, KTV/Night Club invitations and a one-time living-specimen gift. This guide explains progression while leaving her personal discoveries and shared story outcomes to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Visit Butterfly Pavilion, 10 AM–5 PM on an even-numbered day, for The Butterfly Pavilion introduction. Kind is its highest affection reply.
  2. Use Talk to reach Known. Add Book, Flowers and Review Caterpillars at the pavilion. The waterfall also offers Book and Flowers during her available hours; their allowances and Talk are shared across locations.
  3. Complete The Species Count at Known. With Intelligence 35, learn Researching once at the pavilion.
  4. At Friend, complete Field Notes, at the pavilion 11 AM–5 PM on even days. With Perception 35, claim Field Observation once.
  5. At Friend, visit the waterfall 7 AM–3 PM on an odd day for The Waterfall Microclimate. This automatic scene's hours extend beyond her regular daytime portrait.
  6. Complete Christy's Zoo Date and Explore Market, then visit Farmers Market, 7 AM–5 PM, for Meet Christy's Cousin Mary Ann. Mary Ann's pavilion introduction must also be complete.
  7. At Close Friend, complete The Field Report at the pavilion. Separately, finish Chrysalis Window after Field Notes and The Waterfall Microclimate, using her waterfall portrait 8 AM–2 PM on an odd day.
  8. At Crush, finish Lantern Moths at the waterfall 6–10 PM, after The Field Report and Chrysalis Window. KTV/Night Club invitations also require Crush and The Field Report, independently of the final H2H.
  9. After Story Beat 55 and The Field Report, revisit the pavilion for The Record. After that scene and Lantern Moths, visit the waterfall 7 AM–2 PM on an odd day for The Next Survey.
  10. At In Love, 3,200 affection, request Some Quiet to Grow at the pavilion to receive Hushbell Seeds once.
The four dates are optional affection sources with their own requirements below. There is no required personal apartment or pregnancy sequence connecting these encounters.

What kind of song helps me sing instead of hiding behind the catalog? Something gentle and warm enough that mistakes feel safe.

Some Quiet to Grow — Hushbell Seeds

At In Love, 3,200 affection, after The Butterfly Pavilion introduction, visit the pavilion 10 AM–5 PM on an even day and request Some Quiet to Grow. This once-only shared medicine-source action costs 20 minutes, with no configured money, Energy, Calm or material cost.
The gift requires that Hushbell Seeds remain eligible to be collected, and Mary Ann be present and not away. Caring or Funny gives 3 base affection; Flirty gives 2. There is no additional fixed affection reward. Completion grants the Hushbell Seeds key item once.
The seeds belong to Willow's medicine progression. Receiving them from Mary Ann does not itself raise Medicine; Willow's hand-in does. This gift does not require The Field Report, the later story scenes, a completed H2H, an invitation date or an apartment visit beyond the stated introduction and In Love gates.

Route Troubleshooting

If Mary Ann is missing, check the game day's parity, her location and Known for the waterfall. At the pavilion she is present only on even days. At the waterfall her evening visit is available every day, while morning visits are odd days only.
If The Field Report or venue invitations are missing, verify both The Waterfall Microclimate and the cousin introduction. The cousin introduction requires Christy's Zoo Date, Explore Market and Mary Ann's pavilion introduction. This is a shared completion chain, rather than a demand for a specific caterpillar count or review score.
If The Next Survey is missing, check Story Beat 55 → The Record, plus Lantern Moths completed, then visit the waterfall 7 AM–2 PM on an odd day. If Chrysalis Window is missing, verify Field Notes and the microclimate scene, and start before the morning portrait leaves at 2 PM.
Mary Ann has no configured apartment visit, pregnancy route, Kupie album, Christmas scene, brothel-worker training, maid cleaning progression or personal resort vacation. The route above covers her current authored content.
