Overview

Annette is a 27-year-old royal guard in the Medieval Era. She has an introduction, conversation actions and an active role in the court storyline. Her protective attitude shows through her professional manner.

Visit the Castle Courtyard in the morning for her introduction, The Guard Who Notices. It triggers automatically between 7 AM and 11 AM, awards 10 base points, and gives a 3-base-point choice bonus for any of its three response categories.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Annette currently has no personal gift, date, heart-to-heart, repeatable animated encounter, or skill lesson. Building affection supports the court storyline; it does not unlock a separate finished romance route.
  • For her introduction or court gatehouse scenes, visit the courtyard in the morning.
  • For her ordinary throne-room appearance, use an even-numbered in-game day after 8 PM.
  • If a court scene is missing, check the previous court milestone, brothel reputation and every listed NPC stage.
  • If a plot involves Elara while she is on vacation, return after she is available again.
  • Talk's five-use allowance is shared across Annette's four locations.
