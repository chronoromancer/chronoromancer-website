Overview

Annette is a royal-court contact. Her current personal interactions are the introductory scene and conversation; court events provide the longer connected progression.

Where to find them

Find her in Castle Courtyard07:00–11:00, Grand Bazaar12:00–15:00 and Noble Quarter16:00–19:00 daily. On even days she also appears in Throne Room20:00–23:00.

Route and relationship milestones

Start The Guard Who Notices in the courtyard 07:00–11:00. It gives 10 base affection before choice rewards. Conversation then supports the relationship gates of the wider court route.

Automatic sceneLocation / start windowRequirements
The Guard Who NoticesCastle Courtyard; 07:00–10:59

Her connected court stops use these gates: courtyard 07:00–11:00 after court event 4 with business Reputation 8; courtyard 07:00–11:00 after court event 13 with Reputation 17, Annette Acknowledged 10 and Vespa Close Friend 200.

The two later office stops start 18:00–23:00, Reputation 18, Elara Friend 100 and Vespa Intimate 1600, with Elara home from vacation. The first needs court event 18 plus Vespa’s night-bazaar date and Annette Acknowledged 10; the next needs that office scene completed and Annette Known 25. Follow the court guide for their scene order; outcomes are left to play.

Activities and rewards

ActionRequirements / start windowCost / recoveryReward / limit
Talk to AnnetteCastle Courtyard / Grand Bazaar / Noble Quarter / Throne Room; Stranger (0)15 min+1 base affection; 5/day

The same conversation allowance is shared across her four locations:15 min,1 base affection, five/day. There is no separate personal date or skill lesson in her current menu.

Favorite stats are Strength, Perception, Charm. Learning Combat Leadership improves the relationship bonus.

Start times are shown; the ending hour is excluded. Base affection receives the relationship bonuses, while direct affection is added as listed. Ordinary affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • The throne-room portrait is even days only.
  • For a missing court scene, check its previous court event, all named participants and business Reputation, then visit its event location during the listed hours.

概览

Annette是宫廷路线中的联系人。目前个人内容为初次事件与交谈，更长的进展来自共同的宫廷事件。

去哪里找

每天07:00–11:00在城堡庭院，12:00–15:00在大集市，16:00–19:00在贵族区。双数日20:00–23:00还会出现在王座厅。

路线与关系进展

先在庭院07:00–11:00触发察觉异样的卫兵，获得10基础好感及选项奖励。之后通过交谈满足共同宫廷事件的关系要求。

自动事件地点／开始时段条件
察觉异样的卫兵城堡庭院；07:00–10:59

共同宫廷环节的条件为：宫廷事件4之后，青楼声誉8，庭院07:00–11:00；宫廷事件13之后，青楼声誉17、Annette已遇见10、Vespa亲密朋友200，仍在庭院07:00–11:00。

后两段办公室事件均在18:00–23:00开始，要求声誉18、Elara朋友100、Vespa亲密1600，且Elara已结束度假。第一段需宫廷事件18、Vespa的夜市约会及Annette已遇见10；下一段需前一办公室事件完成、Annette已知25。完整顺序见宫廷路线指南，事件结果留待游玩。

行动与奖励

行动条件／开始时段消耗／恢复奖励／次数
与Annette交谈城堡庭院 / 大集市 / 贵族区 / 王座厅；陌生人（0好感）15分钟基础好感+1；每天5次

四处地点共用同一交谈额度：15分钟、1基础好感，每天五次。目前菜单没有独立的个人约会或技能课程。

偏好的属性为力量、感知、魅力。学会战斗领导力可提高关系加成。

表中均为开始时间，不包含结束整点。基础好感受关系加成影响，直接好感按所列数值增加。普通好感、玩家情趣经验和NPC亲密经验分别计算。

卡住时检查什么

  • 王座厅头像只在双数日出现。
  • 共同宫廷事件不触发时，检查上一事件、所有参与者的关系及青楼声誉，再于事件规定时段前往对应地点。
