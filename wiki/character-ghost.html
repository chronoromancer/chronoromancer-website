概览

Ghost 引导未来的抵抗。她的个人路线包括5个心对心,2个日期,8个场景的追求链,可重复的私人访问和可选的家庭路线。本指南给出了要求、地点和有用的选择,同时将主要的故事启示留给游戏。

私人时间和射箭焦点

私人时间 要求 浪漫主义中,从隐藏处开始 下午1时至11时和费用 120分钟70个能量它已经 没有单独的钱或葡萄酒成本。它所公布的25个关系点不另加:奖励来自选择的对话路径。
谈话是 可重复。虽然动作列出每天限值为1,但目前的私人访问处理者确实 不记录或强制执行每日津贴.
学习 射箭焦点选择 “教我如何有重点地瞄准” 。它只在您缺乏技能时才出现 。 任何三课的回答都会导致最后练习的响应; 完成它以学习技能 。 50个基本感情,与 没有额外的Stat阈值或课费,且没有配置玩家 Erotic 或 NPC 亲密体验。然后该技能会提高 Ghost 的关系乘数。

Starting and Building Trust

  1. Progress through Story Beat 14, then enter the Underground Resistance Hideout, 1–11 PM, for Story Beat 15, which gives 10 base Ghost points.
  2. Use her portrait at the Hideout during 1–11 PM. Talk gives 1 actual base point despite its zero-point metadata, up to four times per game day.
  3. At Known, give Wine, consuming one bottle for 7 base points, up to twice per day. Her Wine handler uses that fixed base value. Talk and Wine each use −1 base points after their respective allowances, with the shared over-limit handling.
  4. At Known and Communication 85, complete Focus Over Emotion: 30 minutes, direct +13 affection and Technology +1, once. Its affection is added directly rather than multiplied.

Ghost has a separate optional track in Kupie's Love Training, entered from the Present home's Attic for zero game-time cost. Select Ghost once she is Friend in the main game and Willow is level 5 in the minigame. Ghost is not in the minigame's paid-only NPC selection list.
At each level, complete all four requirements: accumulate and confirm Kupie affection, spend Hearts, spend Charms, and give the inventory gift. Troubleshooting

* An Operations Center scene is missing: check its own story flag and night window. Ghost's normal portrait remains at the Hideout.
* No Contingency is missing: complete Breaking Protocol, The Cost of Command and Off the Grid, then visit her Hideout portrait at 8–11 PM while at least Crush.
* After the Orders is missing: finish The Line That Holds and No Contingency, reach Romantic, then visit at 5–7 PM.
* A date disappeared: each date completes once; its completion flag hides it afterward.
* A pregnancy option is missing: check both Ghost intimacy XP and affection, her final quest and H2H, the feature setting, and Akira's protocol. Other completed H2Hs do not replace After the Orders.
* An older save has stale Ghost options: the current load repair restores the canonical five H2Hs, dates and windows while retaining completed progress; retired custom H2H flags are not separate new-route requirements.
