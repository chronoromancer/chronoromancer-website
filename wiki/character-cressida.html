概览

Cressida 谷 现任公民连续性司司长 弗林格区未来时代官僚自动化中心她目前的个人路线包含10个自动场景,3个5轮H2H,2个日期,2个技能课和1次情报奖励,下面的走法给出了进步要求,而没有重述她后来的发现或结局.

Cressida — Complete Character Guide

Overview

Cressida Vale is the Director of Civic Continuity at the Future Era Bureaucracy Automation Center in the Fringe Zone. Her current personal route contains ten automatic scenes, three five-round H2Hs, two dates, two skill lessons and a one-time Intelligence reward. The walkthrough below gives the progression requirements without retelling her later discoveries or ending.

The day number determines odd/even days, rather than the weekday. Her two locations share the same Talk allowance. Her even-day Operations Center menu offers Talk, Information Brokering and Economic Warfare.

Step-by-Step Route

  1. Visit the Bureaucracy Automation Center, 8 AM–10 PM, for Form 47-B. No personal relationship or story-beat prerequisite is needed to begin.
  2. Talk to Cressida up to four times per day. At Acknowledged, return for The Human Signature; at Known, for Proof of Residence; at Acquaintance, for Reason for Existing.
  3. At Friend, complete The Courtesy Denial, then visit 6–10 PM for Signed in Advance. After the Last Applicant also unlocks at Friend after Proof of Residence; its start window is 7–10 PM.
  4. Take Skygarden Date at Friend after Reason for Existing, starting 8 AM–4 PM. Read the Fine Print and Information Brokering are optional additional ways to gain affection and skills.
  5. At Close Friend, finish Unsigned, which requires Signed in Advance and After the Last Applicant. It has no extra hour restriction beyond her Center schedule.
  6. Complete Story Beat 34 in the main story. At Close Friend, visit the Center 6–10 PM for The Accountability Layer, after Signed in Advance.
  7. Return 8 AM–10 PM for The Appeal Window, then 6–10 PM for Authority to Refuse. Both require Close Friend and the preceding automatic scene. Authority to Refuse also checks Story Beat 34.
  8. Her schedule now alternates: Center on odd days, Operations Center on even days. Start Neon Bazaar Date at the Center 4–10 PM on an odd day, after The Appeal Window.
  9. At Crush, complete No Approval Required at the Center on an odd day, after Authority to Refuse and Unsigned. Its menu is available throughout her 8 AM–10 PM Center schedule, despite its after-hours story setting.
  10. Visit the TD Operations Center on an even day, 8 AM–10 PM, for The Exception Becomes Policy, after Authority to Refuse, Story Beat 34 and No Approval Required, at Crush.
The automatic scene chain and H2H chain have separate completion requirements. Dates are additional activities; neither date is required by the finale. All ten automatic scenes, both dates and all three H2Hs complete once.

If Progress Stalls

  • If she is missing after Authority to Refuse, check the day number: odd means Bureaucracy Center, even means Operations Center, 8 AM–10 PM.
  • After the Last Applicant starts only 7–10 PM, at Friend, after Proof of Residence. Its dialogue cannot substitute for Signed in Advance's automatic completion.
  • Unsigned needs both Signed in Advance and the first H2H. Its menu has no evening-only gate.
  • The later automatic route needs Story Beat 34. More affection alone cannot unlock The Accountability Layer or Authority to Refuse before that story milestone.
  • No Approval Required stays at the Bureaucracy Center, requiring an odd day after the schedule change, Crush, Authority to Refuse and Unsigned.
  • For the final scene, move to the Operations Center on an even day, after all its listed requirements. Neither date is a mandatory finale prerequisite.
  • Economic Warfare is an Operations Center lesson. Read the Fine Print and the dates remain Center actions.
  • Her present route has no apartment encounter, brothel recruitment or implemented gift menu. The three H2Hs and two dates are the authored personal activities to complete.
