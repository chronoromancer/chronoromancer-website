Overview

Bobo is a mischievous goblin woman recruited through three dungeon encounters. Once recruited, she lives in Brothel Cell 1 and has conversation, three personal video visits, six shared corridor scenes and monster care and training.

Recruitment Walkthrough

Build the Cell Corridor, bring 20 Dragon Coins for two visits, and explore the normal dungeon. If you know Negotiation, both visits offer a 7-coin alternative, reducing the total to 14 Dragon Coins. Coins are consumable Dragon Coins rather than ordinary money.
Step Where / prerequisite What to do Result
First encounter Floor 32 or deeper; Cell Corridor built; no completed Bobo encounter Pay 10 Dragon Coins, or choose Negotiation with the skill and at least 7 coins Completes encounter 1; 35 NPC intimacy experience
Second encounter Floor 33 or deeper; first encounter completed Pay another 10 Dragon Coins, or negotiate for 7 Completes encounter 2; another 35 NPC intimacy experience
Third encounter Floor 34 or deeper; first two encounters completed Choose Step in and stop this, then win the bodyguard battle Recruits Bobo; 10 direct affection; 30 NPC intimacy experience; Cell 1 built; Whip tool if not already owned
The first two visits do not recruit her. Choosing Not interested does not advance the encounter chain; a later eligible encounter lets you try again. Walking away from the third scene likewise leaves the recruitment unfinished.
On floors 32–34, the next eligible Bobo encounter has a special priority roll whenever the scenario selector reaches this check: 35% on its minimum floor, plus 15 percentage points per floor beyond that minimum. This is a scenario-selection roll, not a chance on every dungeon step. Other encounters may still appear.
After completing the first two encounters, investigate the hidden wall at floor 34, tile (1, 6) with the corridor built. It starts the third encounter directly, so you can avoid waiting for its random selection.
The Mutant Goblin Bodyguard is weak to Ice and strong against Light, regenerates and has substantial damage reduction. Its Ground Slam is telegraphed. This is a boss fight, so prepare healing and combat resources before intervening; fleeing and sleeping are blocked.
Successful recruitment automatically builds reserved Cell 1. Bobo does not need a vacant cell among cells 2–8 or six named-enemy victories. Those are other monsters' recruitment mechanics.

Monster Care and Training

Her affection meter and tamed percentage are separate. Recruitment begins her monster record at 50 tamed, with 60 health, 0 stress and 0 experience. Regular care helps maintain access to training.
  • Use the monster-management screen to select Bobo for a daily care session. The session is unavailable while she is resting or after she has already had that day's care visit.
  • Feed consumes 3 food and adds 10 tamed, subject to the normal cap. A failed feeding attempt without enough food can be retried after obtaining food.
  • A care session permits one use of each available category: Feed, tool-based Discipline, Teach and Training. Tool effects differ; inspect the selected tool's effects on tamed, stress, health and experience.
  • Teach and Training need at least 30 tamed. Feed first if her tamed value has fallen below that threshold.
  • Ordinary daily decay is 5 tamed per day after her first care visit. Tamed is normally capped at 100; the Claw tool uses the shared higher-cap behavior. Each of her three personal video visits can also add 5 tamed.


Eight Training Levels

Training unlocks sequentially from level 1 through level 8. Completing a training session advances the level by one, up to 8, and leaves earlier types available. It improves Bobo's monster stats, rather than teaching the player those named skills.

If Progress Stalls

  • Build the Cell Corridor before searching for the first encounter.
  • Complete both paid visits. Refusing a visit leaves the same step unfinished.
  • Bring Dragon Coins for each visit; ordinary money cannot pay these costs. Negotiation reduces each payment only if that skill is known.
  • After the second visit, use the floor 34 hidden wall at (1, 6) for the third encounter.
  • Recruitment follows the third scene's bodyguard battle. There is no repeated-victory capture roll for Bobo.
  • Visit Cell 1 after recruitment. Her NPC portrait needs active status and Acknowledged affection.
  • For monster Teach or Training, maintain 30 tamed and check her daily care/rest status.
  • Personal videos require the paid version, their affection stage and sufficient energy. Shared corridor scenes need Friend with both participants.
