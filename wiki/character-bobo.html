Overview

Bobo becomes a resident of the Rose & Thorn Cell Corridor after recruitment. This page covers the entrance route, cell visits and relationship activities without recounting the scenes.

Where to find them

After recruitment: Cell 1, all day.

Route and relationship milestones

Bobo uses the three-part Snaggle encounter route in the ordinary Dungeon, rather than the repeat-win capture system. Build the Cell Corridor first, then work through the encounters on floors 32–34 in order.

The first two stages each ask for 10 Dragon Coins, reduced to 7 with Negotiation. Declining does not advance that stage. After both, take the intervention option in the third encounter and complete its battle.

Completion recruits Bobo, adds 10 direct affection, builds Cell 1 automatically and gives a Whip if you do not own one. You do not need another vacant cell for this route.

Optional navigation: third encounter

After the first two stages, the hidden wall at floor 34, tile (1, 6), counting coordinates from 0 provides the repeatable entrance to the final encounter. If an earlier random stage has not appeared, revisit its eligible floor before expecting the finale.

Optional reference: relationship bonuses

Meeting each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before the remaining relationship bonuses. The favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct one-time affection rewards are added as listed.

Taming 40; Rogue 50; Ability 40

Favorite skill: Deception

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Listed time and resources are base values; applicable skills, difficulty and active bonuses can change the shown cost or affection. Base affection, direct affection, player Erotic Experience and NPC intimacy experience are separate.

What usually blocks the next step

  • Bobo’s third encounter requires both paid earlier stages. Winning unrelated goblin fights does not replace them.
  • A locked later activity needs the listed relationship stage and 100 Energy; each has its own once-daily use.
  • Shared corridor events require the other named residents too; ordinary affection with one character does not recruit the others.

概览

Bobo 招募后会居住在玫瑰与荆棘的牢房区域。这里列出加入路线、探访方式与关系行动，不复述剧情。

在哪里找到

招募后：牢房1，全天可探访。

路线与关系节点

Bobo 通过普通地下城中的三段 Snaggle 遭遇路线加入，不使用重复胜场捕捉。先建牢房走廊，再依次推进第 32–34 层的遭遇。

前两段各需支付 10 龙币；拥有谈判技能时，每段降为 7 龙币。拒绝支付不会推进该段。完成两段后，在第三次遭遇选择介入并完成战斗。

完成后招募 Bobo，直接增加 10 好感度，并自动建成牢房 1；尚无鞭子时还会获得一条。这条路线不需要另备空牢房。

可选路线提示：第三次遭遇

完成前两段后，第 34 层坐标（1，6）（坐标从 0 开始）的隐藏墙可重复进入最后一段。若较早的随机遭遇尚未出现，请先回到对应楼层推进前置。

常见卡点

  • Bobo 的第三段要求完成前两段付款；击败其他哥布林无法代替这些前置。
  • 后续行动要求对应关系阶段与 100 精力，各行动每天分别可用一次。
  • 共同牢房事件也要求其他指定住民已加入；只提升一人的好感度不会自动招募其他人。
