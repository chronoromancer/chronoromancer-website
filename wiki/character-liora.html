Overview

Liora’s personal Objective route becomes actionable after Story 71. It includes temporary location changes, return visits and next-day waits. Start with current Objectives; the optional numbered reference below preserves the route’s story revelations.

Where to find them

PlaceWhen to look
Anomaly Templenormal home, 06:00–24:00; individual route actions have narrower windows
Underground Modification Clinicstep 9 ready: 08:00–18:00
Archive Facilitystep 10 ready: 08:00–20:00
Operations Centerstep 18 ready: 08:00–18:00; after it, ordinary afternoons 12:00–18:00

An unfinished afternoon date keeps her Temple departure available from 12:00–16:00 instead of making that outing unreachable after the move. The active clinic/archive/Operations step takes priority over her usual Temple portrait.

Route and relationship milestones

The route’s first nineteen Objective conversations are numbered below in their normal order. Each needs the preceding step. Steps 3 onward also need Story 74; steps 4 onward need Story 77. Step 5 onward needs the Temple’s 25-wave story checkpoint or the Tower Defense story-requirement setting that permits continuing without it. Steps 10 onward also need Story 78 and archive entry 7.

A new visit means leave the location and enter again; waiting in the same action menu is insufficient. A next-day requirement means the following calendar day. The table marks both.

The first two H2Hs open after steps 12 and 14 at 100 and 200 affection; the third follows step 16 at 400. All begin at the Temple from 18:00–21:00. The two dates need step 16, the second H2H and 200 affection.

Optional reference: affection bonuses

Each favorite-stat threshold adds 1 to ordinary base affection before other bonuses; the favorite skill doubles the adjusted base. Direct rewards remain the listed direct amount.

Mystic 80; Communication 75; Charm 70

Start windows exclude their ending hour. Costs are base values; applicable skills and active bonuses may change them. Base affection, direct affection and NPC intimacy experience are distinct. Dialogue choices can add rewards beyond the listed event-completion base.

What usually blocks the next step

  • If the Temple portrait is missing, check the active clinic/archive/Operations step and its hours.
  • A previous day’s completion or a fresh visit can be required even when every listed story flag is already satisfied.
  • An earlier postponed decision does not permanently remove the current Operations milestone. Follow the remaining Objectives.

Liora 的个人目标路线在主线 71 后可推进，其中包含临时换地点、重新到访与等到次日的条件。请先查看当前目标；下方可选编号表只说明导航，保留剧情揭示。

路线与关系节点

下表将前十九段个人目标谈话按正常顺序编号，每步都要求上一段完成。第 3 步起还要求主线 74，第 4 步起要求主线 77；第 5 步起要求神殿 25 波主线关卡，或使用允许免除此要求的塔防主线设置。第 10 步起还要求主线 78 与第 7 份档案。

重新到访要求离开地点后再次进入，在同一行动菜单里等待不够。次日条件则要求进入下一日；表中分别注明。

前两段谈心分别在第 12、14 步后，于 100、200 好感度开放；第三段在第 16 步后，要求 400 好感度。三段都在神殿 18:00–21:00 开始。两项约会要求第 16 步、第二段谈心及 200 好感度。

可选查阅：好感度加成

每达到一项偏好属性门槛，普通好感度计算的基础值增加 1；偏好技能会将调整后的基础值翻倍。直接奖励仍按标明的直接数值加入。

神秘 80; 沟通 75; 魅力 70

开始时段不包含结束整点。消耗为基础值，适用技能与当前加成可能改变实际数值。基础好感度、直接好感度与 NPC 亲密经验分别计算；对话选择还可能在事件完成基础奖励之外追加奖励。

常见卡点

  • 神殿肖像缺失时，请检查当前是否在诊所、档案馆或作战中心推进，以及对应时段。
  • 即使所有主线前置都已满足，也可能还需要等到次日或重新到访。
  • 较早暂缓的决定不会永久移除现行作战中心节点，请继续跟随剩余目标。
