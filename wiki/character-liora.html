概览

Liora 是一个富有同情心的三臂芹菜 圣安乐寺 输入 未来的总理部门她以简短的环境问候欢迎来访者,并在主要故事中出现。
她目前的性格执行 没有个人关系行为或浪漫发展本指南涵盖与她见面的地点以及如何在不透露事件的情况下达到她执行的故事外观。

何处查找 Liora

一旦你可以旅行在 未来时代打开 总理区地图 选择 圣安乐寺。该位置可显示并解锁,没有单独的Liora招募或爱心门。
她的普通肖像出现 从早上6点到午夜画像的开张显示了她短暂的问候和早期接触的图像。 没有 使用赠与或授予关系分数。
没有Talk或礼品按钮在当前字符定义中是故意的,在另一天返回或提高列出的最爱数据不会解锁隐藏的个人动作.

当前行走

  1. 到达 未来的总理部门 输入 圣安乐寺.
  2. 访问 上午6点到午夜 来看看Liora的环境肖像和问候。
  3. 通过 Story 击败 70.
  4. 回神庙去 Story 击败71。其注册自动触发盖 任何时间与她普通的肖像计划无关
  5. 继续主讲 Story 击球73,然后输入 业务中心 (单位:千美元) Story 击打 74在那里她有另一个作者的外表。
这些外观不会激活个人浪漫路线. No Liora 情结阈值或学习技能是两个注册的章节触发器需要的.

Overview

Liora is a compassionate, three-armed celebrant at the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple in the Future Prime Sector. She welcomes visitors with short ambient greetings and also appears in the main story.
Her current character implementation has no personal relationship actions or romance progression. This guide covers where to meet her and how to reach her implemented story appearances without revealing their events.

Where to Find Liora

Once you can travel in the Future era, open the Prime Sector map and select the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple. The location is configured visible and unlocked, with no separate Liora recruitment or affection gate.
Her ordinary portrait appears from 6 AM until before midnight, every day. Opening the portrait displays one of her short greetings and early-contact images. It does not consume a gift or award relationship points.
The absence of a Talk or gift button is intentional in the current character definition. Returning on another day or raising the listed favorite stats does not unlock a hidden personal action.

Current Walkthrough

  1. Reach the Future Prime Sector and enter the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple.
  2. Visit 6 AM–midnight to see Liora's ambient portrait and greetings.
  3. Progress the main story through Story Beat 70.
  4. Return to the Temple for Story Beat 71. Its registered automatic trigger covers any hour, independently of her ordinary portrait schedule.
  5. Continue the main story through Story Beat 73, then enter the Operations Center for Story Beat 74, where she has another authored appearance.
These appearances do not activate a personal romance route. No Liora affection threshold or learned skill is required for the two registered chapter triggers.

Main Story Appearances

Appearance Start conditions Time on first story advancement Liora relationship reward
Story Beat 71 Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple, any hour; Beat 70 completed; Beat 71 not already completed 120 minutes None configured
Story Beat 74 Operations Center, any hour; Beat 73 completed; Beat 74 not already completed 90 minutes None configured
Both are one-time automatic main-story chapters. Their completion handlers advance the story to 72 and 75, respectively. Other participants have chapter rewards, but these are not Liora affection awards. The handlers do not enable new Liora actions.
This guide leaves her role, the Temple's larger story and the chapters' outcomes to the game.

Available Content and Troubleshooting

* Liora is missing: use the Sacred Anomaly Blessing Temple in Future Prime Sector, during 6 AM–midnight for her ordinary portrait.
* Her portrait has no actions: this is the current implementation. She has no Talk, gifts, lessons, H2Hs, dates, apartment route, training or personal affection rewards.
* Story Beat 71 does not trigger: complete Beat 70, visit the Temple and check that Beat 71 has not already completed. Its story trigger is available at any hour.
* Her later appearance does not occur at the Temple: Beat 74 starts in the Operations Center, after Beat 73.
* High Mystic does not unlock a route: favorite-stat metadata is not a story-activation condition. No personal relationship progression is currently configured.
Additional character images or future route plans do not establish playable unlocks. The present content is her ambient Temple presence and the implemented main-story appearances above.
