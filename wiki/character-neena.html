概览

Neena 是一个 成人未来特性 以网络网状的重建 和精确,有纪律的方式。 Story第69节 打开她在要塞的普通互动 弹药她的个人路线包括: 九次自动相遇、三次心跳、两次约会、一次战术意识课程、一次私人术语场景和军队招募.
这种走过让机械进步和奖励选择,同时将她的历史和主故事发展留给游戏.

Overview

Neena is an adult Future character with cybernetic reconstruction and a precise, disciplined manner. Story Beat 69 opens her ordinary interactions at the fortress Armory. Her personal route includes nine automatic encounters, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, a Tactical Awareness lesson, one Private Terms scene and army recruitment.
This walkthrough gives the mechanical progression and reward choices while leaving her history and the main-story developments to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Complete Story Beat 68, then visit Transit Nexus Fringe for Story Beat 69. After its completion, find Neena at the Armory. The main story supplies starting affection; its best Neena reply is Flirty.
  2. At Acknowledged, enter Armory, 6 AM–midnight, for Terms of Occupancy. At Known, return 8 AM–midnight for Adequate Repairs. Use daily Talk and Give Wine between scenes.
  3. At Known and Intelligence 15, learn Tactical Awareness if using the paid version and you do not already know it.
  4. At Acquaintance, enter Armory, 6 PM–midnight, for No True Believer. At Friend, complete Before the Metal, then start Same Quiet Corner Date, 6 PM–midnight.
  5. With that date and Before the Metal complete, return to Armory, 8 AM–midnight, at Friend for Twenty-Three, Eleven, None.
  6. At Close Friend, complete Doctrine Has Exits, then Permission to Proceed. Revisit Armory, 8 AM–midnight, for A Voice Without Faith.
  7. Start Open Sightlines Date, 5–11 PM, at Close Friend after A Voice Without Faith. At Crush, return to the Armory for The Patient Breach, then complete No False Interpretation.
  8. At Romantic, enter Armory, 8 AM–midnight, for No One Gave the Order. The separate Private Terms action is also eligible at Romantic after No False Interpretation, in the paid version; the action plays once.
  9. At Intimate, after No One Gave the Order, enter Operations Center, 8 AM–10 PM, for By Her Own Authority. Its completion recruits Neena to the army and changes her portrait schedule.
The personal automatic encounters remain at their registered locations. Moving her portrait after recruitment does not move those scene triggers. A higher affection stage alone does not replace a missing date, H2H or earlier scene completion.

Tactical Awareness Lesson

At Known, with Intelligence 15, the paid version offers Tactical Awareness if it is not already learned. Start at any available Neena portrait.
The lesson costs 160 minutes, $250, 30 Energy and 20 Calm, teaches Tactical Awareness, and awards 15 base affection. It is hidden once the skill is learned. It requires no personal H2H, date, army recruitment or Private Terms completion.
Tactical Awareness is both her registered relationship skill and a player combat skill described as predicting enemy moves. Learning it elsewhere also satisfies the relationship multiplier's skill check.

Private Terms — Once-Only Personal Scene

At Romantic, after No False Interpretation, select Private Terms from any available Neena portrait in the paid version. It costs 180 minutes and 25 Energy, restores 20 Calm, and has no configured money or consumable cost.
This is a once-only plot scene, not a repeatable apartment conversation or separate movie action. Completion hides the action, and the active handler also refuses a repeat. Current save-load repair preserves that completion rule for saved action data.
The scene gives 12 fixed base affection, plus Caring 5, Funny 3 or Flirty 6. Choose Flirty for 18 base total. The action's separate three-point field is not an additional award: the custom handler starts the plot, whose fixed and choice rewards supply the affection.

Army Recruitment

Complete By Her Own Authority, at Intimate, after No One Gave the Order, by visiting Operations Center, 8 AM–10 PM. Its completion adds Neena to the TD army roster. Merely learning Tactical Awareness, completing the main-story introduction or selecting Private Terms does not replace the final personal scene.
The roster also reconciles this completed-plot recruitment when loading. The completed final scene is the recruitment authority; there is no additional personal H2H or date after it. Her H2Hs and both dates already form the earlier chain.
Recruitment changes her ordinary schedule to the morning Armory / afternoon Operations Center / alternating evening locations shown above. Her personal story is complete at this point; Talk and Wine remain daily actions.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

  • No ordinary Neena portrait: finish Story Beat 69, then use the pre- or post-recruitment schedule above.
  • Next personal scene is absent: check its exact location, hours, relationship stage, earlier plot, H2H and date requirements. The dates are necessary bridges in the chain.
  • Final recruitment scene seems unavailable: enter Operations Center with Intimate and No One Gave the Order complete, during 8 AM–10 PM. The automatic scene can trigger there before her ordinary Operations portrait is unlocked.
  • An evening date is absent after recruitment: use Armory on odd evenings or Recharge Station on even evenings, within the date's start window. Open Sightlines also overlaps Operations Center, 5–6 PM.
  • Private Terms disappeared: its scene is once-only. It does not refresh the next day.
