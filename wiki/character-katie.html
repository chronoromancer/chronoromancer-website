Recommended Walkthrough

  1. In your Present bedroom, choose START TUTORIAL. Complete Katie Comes Over, then return to Present Uptown and visit Katie's Home on the map.
  2. Complete Katie Explains What She Likes, then visit Diane at Town Hall Office, 9 AM–5 PM, and take her Responsibility lesson. Return to Katie for Katie Explains Jobs and Gifts.
  3. Visit Convenience Store for Joey Puts You to Work. Build Working Experience to 3, then return to Katie for Katie Explains Growing Relationships. This opens her Chocolate gift, General Suggestions, personal H2Hs and six ordinary dates.
  4. At Known, give Chocolate, up to eight times daily. Talk works from the beginning, up to seven times daily. The ordinary dates do not add a Friend requirement, so you can also use them to build affection after the growth tutorial.
  5. Reach Friend, complete Zoo Date and Family Photo, then revisit Katie for Katie Explains People. Visit University Hall, 9 AM–5 PM, for Katie Meets Rachel.
  6. Reach Emily Friend, then visit Café Interior, 7 AM–3 PM, for Katie Meets Emily. Equip Dating Outfit level 2 or better, and return to Katie's Home 8–10 PM for the one-time tutorial KTV Evening with Katie and Emily.
  7. At Close Friend, after Family Photo, take Late Night Relaxing, starting 9 PM–midnight. It is separate from the tutorial requirements: it is not required for Katie's First Confession.
  8. After the tutorial KTV evening, reach Crush and visit her home for Katie's First Confession. This opens Private Time with Katie, Be With Katie, and her repeatable venue invitations.
  9. Use Invite Date at KTV Entrance or Night Club, 8–11 PM, after the confession. For Kupie, reach main-game Friend and Bella level 3 in that minigame. For the optional family route, meet the later readiness requirements below, including Something Sweeter.

Optional Family Route

This later continuation requires pregnancy content unlocked and enabled, plus Akira's Pregnancy Protocol completed. Katie needs 2,000 ordinary affection, 500 Katie NPC intimacy experience, Katie's First Confession and Something Sweeter completed. Player Erotic experience and Kupie affection do not replace these requirements.
Katie cannot already be pregnant, recovering from delivery or have a child from this route; the current system permits one child per NPC.
  1. Start Be With Katie and choose “Ask Katie if she wants to try for a baby with you.” Complete the consent and commitment responses. This path records conception and gives 50 base affection, 50 player Erotic experience and 40 Katie NPC intimacy experience, using the apartment visit's 120-minute / 45-Energy cost and daily allowance.
  2. Revisit Katie's Home, any hour, for No Rehearsal, available from pregnancy age 11 days, for 5 base affection. Conception is age zero; the hidden stage lasts through age 10.
  3. Complete Ready Enough from age 31, also at least one day after the reveal, for 7 base affection. Delaying the reveal delays this stage.
  4. The First Minute becomes ready 30 days after the final stage starts, and at least one day after the final-stage visit, for 10 base affection. Prompt completion of every milestone allows delivery from age 61 days; it is not a fixed due date when milestones are delayed.
  5. Recovery lasts eight game days, followed by family check-ins.
Check In / Family Check In takes 30 minutes, once per game day, and adds 5 direct affection. A check-in can start a ready milestone when Katie is present at its registered location. The current configuration does not require an extra numerical count of prenatal visits before delivery.
Talk, Chocolate, General Suggestions, tutorial scenes and the shared character encounters remain pregnancy-safe. Ordinary dates, both personal H2Hs, Private Time, Be With Katie and venue invitations are blocked while she is showing in the early/final stages and during postpartum recovery. The Voucher prize date is blocked throughout an active pregnancy, including its hidden stage. Katie has no separate dedicated pregnancy-intimacy animation registered in the current system.

Troubleshooting

  • Katie's map icon is missing: begin START TUTORIAL in your Present bedroom.
  • Responsibility is missing: first finish Katie Explains What She Likes, then open Diane's portrait at Town Hall 9 AM–5 PM.
  • The growth tutorial does not trigger: complete Joey Puts You to Work and build Working Experience to 3, then revisit Katie.
  • Chocolate or the ordinary dates are missing: complete Katie Explains Growing Relationships; Chocolate also needs Known.
  • Katie Explains People is missing: finish Zoo Date and Family Photo and reach Friend. Other dates or Late Night Relaxing do not replace those requirements.
  • Katie Meets Emily is missing: finish Katie Meets Rachel, reach Emily Friend, then enter the Café 7 AM–3 PM.
  • Tutorial KTV evening is missing: equip Dating Outfit level 2+, complete Katie Meets Emily, then visit Katie 8–10 PM.
  • Fair Date is missing: its starting window is 1–3 PM. The separate Voucher date runs noon–6 PM.
  • Late Night Relaxing is missing: reach Close Friend, finish Family Photo, and start 9 PM–midnight.
  • Private activities or venue invitations are missing: complete the tutorial KTV evening, reach Crush, and trigger Katie's First Confession at her home.
  • An apartment opening is absent: Oral needs 900 ordinary affection; Have Fun needs 1,100. Their optional extensions also need Beast 5.
  • Invite Date is already used: the daily allowance is shared with all NPCs at that venue; KTV and Night Club have separate allowances.
  • Kupie Katie is absent: reach main-game Friend and Bella Kupie level 3.
  • Family option is absent: check feature access, Akira's protocol, 2,000 ordinary affection / 500 Katie intimacy XP, First Confession and Something Sweeter after Story Beat 61.
  • An older tutorial save has confusing flags: the current load migration reconciles retired Katie tutorial aliases without replaying tutorial rewards; the requirements above use the current canonical milestones.
