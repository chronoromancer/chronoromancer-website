概览

Tinker是一个 遗传增强中心哥特机器人增强工匠 她目前走过的路包括11个自动个人场景、3个心跳、2个约会、2个带薪技能课程和2个一次性奖励。 堡垒工程.
这个指南给出了路线的机械门,而没有揭示它们背后的发现.

  1. 在实验室见Tinker,使用每日谈话,然后在Known添加珠宝.

  4. 完成StoryBeat15,并在晚上返回"协议墓地"前成功黑入增强中心终端. Claim Reversible Retrofit when Technology covery update 45.
  5. 在碎纸上完成祝福,然后中立有牙齿,没有工厂 重置在浪漫。
  6. 在StoryBeat35之后,在上午8时至6时回到实验室“我有手 ” 。在临终前,在下午6时至午夜参观工程。
  7. 在窗口中使用可选日期;它们不是此个人链的前提条件 。

Overview

Tinker is a gothic robot enhancement artisan at the Genetic Enhancement Center in the Future Fringe Zone. Her current walkthrough includes eleven automatic personal scenes, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, two paid skill lessons and two one-time rewards. Later, her evening portrait moves to Fortress Engineering.
This guide gives the route's mechanical gates without revealing the discoveries behind them.

Recommended Progression

  1. Meet Tinker at the lab, use daily Talk, then add Jewelry at Known.
  2. Follow the personal scenes below, using their exact time windows. Cybernetics is available at Known and Modification Aesthetics at Acquaintance if their stat checks are met.
  3. After The Body Argues Back, complete Visible Repairs at Friend. At Close Friend, finish Warranty Fraud and Open Chassis.
  4. Complete Story Beat15 and successfully hack the enhancement-center terminal before returning at night for The Protocol Graveyard. Claim Reversible Retrofit when Technology reaches 45.
  5. Finish Blessed on Paper at Crush, then Neutrality Has Teeth and No Factory Reset at Romantic.
  6. After Story Beat35, return to the lab during 8 AM–6 PM for I Have Hands. At Intimate, visit Engineering during 6 PM–midnight for Chosen Architecture.
  7. Use the optional dates in their windows; they are not prerequisites for this personal chain.

Later Shared Story Appearances

Released Story Beat68 requires Story Beat67, Tinker's I Have Hands and Jinx's One Variable completed, at the Fortress Operations Center. Its completion gives 10 base Tinker affection; the Flirty reply gives her the highest extra 5, for 15 base.
Story Beats73 and74 also occur at the Operations Center after their preceding story beats. Completion gives Tinker 4 and 3 base affection respectively, with no additional Tinker-specific choice bonus. These are shared story events, separate from her personal eleven-scene route; their outcomes are left to the game.

Troubleshooting and Current Scope

  • She vanished from the lab at night: after I Have Hands, find her at Fortress Engineering from 6 PM to midnight.
  • The Protocol Graveyard does not trigger: finish Story Beat15, Warranty Fraud, reach Close Friend, and successfully hack the enhancement-center terminal; visit the lab after 6 PM.
  • I Have Hands is missing: complete Story Beat35, Neutrality Has Teeth and No Factory Reset at Romantic, then enter the lab during 8 AM–6 PM.
  • A lesson is missing: check paid access, the stat and relationship gate, and whether the skill is already learned.
  • Recharge Station Date disappeared at the lab: after the schedule change, its evening action is on Engineering's portrait.
  • There is no apartment or worker route: Tinker has no configured apartment intimacy or brothel-worker recruitment, and no current NPC intimacy gallery assets.
