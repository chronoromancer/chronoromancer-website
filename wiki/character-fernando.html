概览

Fernando 是 Kings Demesne 中的主裁。他的当前内容包括普通对话、时尚建议、时尚技巧课、玩家服装升级、工人服装配件、社区场景和与Prince Barnard 的三幕线。他没有个人H2H、日期、公寓或度假村路线。

Overview

Fernando is the master tailor in Kings Demesne. His current content includes ordinary conversation, Fashion Advice, the Fashion skill lesson, player outfit upgrades, worker clothing fittings, community scenes and a three-scene thread with Prince Barnard. He has no personal H2H, date, apartment or resort route.

Starting His Route

  1. Enter the Tailor Shop, 6 AM–9 PM, for the one-time The Tailor Shop introduction. It awards 5 fixed base affection, with Funny +4, Caring +3 or Flirty +3.
  2. Use Talk to Fernando, up to eight times per day, at 15 minutes each. The handler records 1 base affection, despite the action metadata listing 2. Extra attempts record −1 base affection.
  3. At Known, take Fashion Advice, a one-time 30-minute interaction with 8 fixed affection. See the current stat-reward limitation below.
  4. Raise Gastoff to Known and return to the Tailor Shop while Fernando is Known for The Fashion Intervention. It awards Fernando +6 and Gastoff +5.
  5. Owning the Rose & Thorn opens worker fittings. Visit with Elara home for Professional Attire, then use Worker Clothing or, for level 5 workers, MAX Worker Clothing as appropriate.
  6. Build the required affection with Fernando and Prince Barnard to complete their three fitting scenes. Worker clothing purchases are not prerequisites for those scenes.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.

Worker Clothing Walkthrough

Both worker actions require Rose & Thorn ownership, an eligible active worker, sufficient gold and fabrics, and a starting time during 6 AM–9 PM. They share one fitting per game day across the entire workforce, not one per worker. Each takes 30 minutes and randomly chooses an eligible worker; there is no worker-selection menu.

Ordinary Worker Clothing

Worker Clothing raises the selected worker's clothing by one level. An ordinary candidate must:
  • Be active and among the 30 supported workers below.
  • Have clothing below level 5.
  • Have received fewer than two Fernando fittings.
You can therefore give each worker up to two ordinary Fernando upgrades while they remain below level 5. It does not automatically raise a new level 1 worker to level 5. There is no additional affection-stage or completed-Professional-Attire requirement in this fitting action.

MAX Worker Clothing

MAX Worker Clothing takes an active worker whose clothing is exactly level 5 to level 6, displayed as MAX. It shares the same daily fitting allowance and increases the same global cost tier. It can still apply after the worker has already received two ordinary Fernando upgrades; the MAX candidate check uses clothing level 5 rather than the ordinary two-fitting limit. A worker already at MAX is no longer eligible.
Each resource is charged at twice the current ordinary tier, with these minimum MAX costs:
Gold Common fabrics Rare fabrics Epic fabrics
$5,000 100 50 25
The minimum applies separately to each resource. For example, at ordinary tier 50, MAX costs $1,000,000; 500 Common, 300 Rare and 198 Epic fabrics, rather than the minimum quote.

Reaching Level 5

The Christmas Claw Machine can award another +1 clothing level to an active worker below level 5. Its clothing-prize allowance is one per worker in the free version, or two with the version access that unlocks the larger prize allowance. Prizes are random; a play does not guarantee a clothing upgrade or a particular worker. A play costs 1 Token and 5 minutes, and View Prize Odds shows the current pool.
For a worker starting at level 1, two ordinary Fernando fittings and two Claw Machine clothing prizes reach level 5, after which the separate MAX fitting reaches level 6. The free version's single clothing prize plus the two ordinary fittings reaches level 4 from level 1. Do not expect Fernando's ordinary action to supply additional fittings after its two-upgrade allowance is exhausted. Existing saves may already have higher clothing levels.

Performance Reaction

Compose a song and use Perform Song at the Alehouse, costing 60 minutes and 30 energy, once per day at that venue. The reaction chance is 25%, plus 5 percentage points each for Master Performer, Stage Presence, Musical Virtuosity, Storytelling and carrying a Lute, capped at 50%. Fernando is one of five possible Alehouse reactions.
When his reaction is selected, it grants 8 fixed base affection, with Caring +4, Funny +2 or Buddy +1. His scene is repeatable on later performances; the random selection does not require his normal shop portrait to be present at the Alehouse.

Optional Private-Client Bookings

Fernando enters the Receive a Client roster at brothel reputation level8. Start this action at the Rose & Thorn entrance with Netorare enabled, while operating a brothel. The hotel branch does not provide this system.
The roster selection is random among eligible clients, so starting a booking does not guarantee Fernando. The system has a shared once-per-game-day booking allowance, separate from his ordinary Talk counters. A completed accepted visit takes 30 minutes and pays the offered fee directly, with possible tip and quality bonus. Declining the booking or an unwilling worker's response takes 10 minutes and still uses the booking allowance, without the accepted-visit payment.
Use an eligible worker who is active, healthy, on shift and available, rather than resting, away at the resort or assigned as the Ballroom apprentice. Active monster workers need 30 Tamed. Fernando has no configured family-exclusion entry. His authored preference is presence and confidence, with Raven listed as his favorite; special pairing scenes exist for Bellavia, Raven, Sophie.
The actual shared service logic uses Loyalty/Tamed for willingness and the average of Dexterity and Ability for quality. For monsters, Agility supplies the Dexterity slot. Appearance and Charisma affect tip calculation (Allure and Aura for monsters). The client's legacy favorite, stat and weight fields do not replace these implemented calculations or the uniform client selection.
Even a worker strong enough to exceed the difficulty retains a 10% upset chance. Client visits award business and selected-worker rewards separately; they do not grant ordinary Fernando affection or NPC intimacy experience. A successful worker praise bonus can give the selected worker's NPC 5 direct affection, rather than Fernando.

Troubleshooting

  • Visit the shop before 9 PM, not at its closing hour.
  • Talk gives 1, not the metadata's 2, and allows eight daily conversations.
  • Fashion Advice does not currently increase Attractiveness; use the separate Fashion lesson when Attractiveness 18 is met.
  • Worker Clothing disappears after the shared daily fitting, or when no active worker qualifies under its below-level-5 / fewer-than-two-fittings rule.
  • MAX Worker Clothing requires an active worker at exactly level 5; it cannot take a level 4 worker directly to MAX.
  • Fittings choose randomly among eligible workers, and both fitting types raise the shared cost tier. Check the displayed quote before paying.
  • Use fabrics, not crafting materials, for outfit and worker clothing payments.
  • Prince Barnard's fitting scenes have their own 8 AM–6 PM window and affection prerequisites.
  • The Time Capsule Discovery uses Ben Arthur's Cobbler Shop, not Fernando's shop.
