女佣制度——完整指南

概览

那个 六位家庭完美少女 它们是Rose& Thorn的清洁队——六位受过训练的专家,他们的综合工作保持了妓院的清洁程度,直接限制了日常收入和声誉收益。 没有工人 ——他们不挣钱,不收客户,从工人管理轨道上完全分开的进化系统运行.
最初的5个女神中,每个女神都与一个神圣的元素(空气,火,地球,精神,水)捆绑在一起,并在她自己的学校"Elemental Homeperfection"——一个传奇的, 完全模糊的预言 她的主人告诉每个女仆总有一天她会发现 法师一个宇宙命运的人,他把所有五个元素都团结在一个屋檐下。他们各自长大后都相信这会发生。然后他们都出现在你的妓院里,意识到你把他们全部聚集起来,开始怀疑预言可能是真的。
现实是预言不是关于"通过服务拯救世界大约五个孤独的超专业专家终于找到了理解他们的奉献精神的同伴,挑战他们的姐妹,以及一个同等重视他们的主人.完美的和谐"他们被告知,只有五个例外的人与尊重他们的人一起工作。
后经揭示,实际上可能存在 第六女佣,而前5将热衷于等待她加入球队.
本指南涵盖 机械 对于每个女佣的个人个性、招聘和对话,请参看她专注的角色指南。

如何获得每个女仆

前5名女佣有自己的招募途径,没有一条是自动的;没有一条是其他的前提条件,你可以按任何顺序招募她们。 Onyx只能最后招募(详情见下文)。

Violet——"长期排卵".

  • 在下列地点: 酒吧协会
  • 要求: 酒吧协会 派别声誉 50+
  • Violet是一个挣扎的戏剧表演者,无法完全赶上她的休息。 "坚固的排华" 剧情大火——你参加她最新的表演并站出来鼓掌。她泪流满面,她加入了妓院,成为第1号女佣(Air).

Mandarin——敦煌救援.

  • 在下列地点: Dungeon 情景(大恶魔相遇)
  • 要求: Dungeon勘探+作战能力
  • 在地牢里,你会遇到Mandarin清洁。 大恶魔 将出现,声称他拥有她。

Lychee——浪漫主义招聘

  • 在下列地点: 城镇周围(全国人大)
  • 要求: 粉碎阶段 在她的关系弧
  • Lychee是你们在城镇周围见面的NPC,通过定期访问建立关系,她美丽,时尚迷恋,并迅速识别你值得她的注意. 粉碎阶段 与她对话的选项似乎邀请她去妓院,

Lime— 向导启动测试奖励

  • 在下列地点: 向导塔 (Gandon)
  • 要求: 填写 向导启动测试。为了Gandon能够选择启动测试,您需要他在Acquaintance+,Blinky(brothel rep 7+)在Known+,以及50+ 向导关节 棒极了,如果测试失败了,你可以第二天再试一次
  • Lime是在Arcane商店完成魔法师启动测试的招聘奖励. Pass(甚至只是完成)测试,她以Maid#4 (Spirit)的身份加入,她在Arcane商店等了一段时间,感觉"宇宙能量",测试结果就是标志.

Rain— 后列车搜索相遇

  • 在下列地点: 后行( 搜索相遇)
  • 要求: 研究技能 (接触期间的聘用选择)
  • Rain见于"后行道"(Back Alleys)——一位分析研究员,用笔记本和镜头记录了城市的底部。 研究 她接受妓院邀请继续她的实地工作。

Onyx——最后的女仆

  • 先决条件: 获得 其他的女仆们 并引发了阴谋 "六声" (要求所有5名女佣在"近友+","女佣"区自动触发(上午8点-下午8点)).
  • 在下列地点: 登革热( scenarii)
  • 要求: 5次在地牢的不同地方遇到她
  • Onyx 最早见于 图像深度 (第46-50层)然后,你需要再找到她4次,以便能招募她:
  1. 在那个 过度增长深度 (第31-36层)
  2. 在那个 机械深度 (第41-45层)
  3. 在那个 淹没的地面 (第13层)
  4. 在那个 Void 深度 (36-40楼),她最终会同意加入你们的建制,她以6号女仆的身份加入.
欲了解每个女佣的更多细节,请点击她们在上面的桌子上的名字进入她们的行走(只有Onyx还没有一个).
瓶颈: Bards Guild派系代表50(Violet), crushstage romancy(Lychee),和向导启动测试(Lime)是三个最慢的门. Mandarin(战斗)和Rain(研究技巧)通常更快解锁.
显然,由于她要求所有5名女仆解锁,Onyx将是最难得到的.

如何清洁(为什么女佣很重要)

女佣的存在是为了保持妓院的清洁性。清洁性是0-100分,分数为8级,直接 限制每日收入和每日声誉收益。这里的乘数表:

每日mess 生成

妓院每天都在制造混乱:
  • 在职工人人数(更多工人=更多混乱)
  • 工人活动(高客户日=更乱)
  • 其他因素(基于事件的杂乱,房间升级等)
女佣每天在轮班期间减少这种混乱。所有六位女佣(她们的级别+物品+奖金)的全部清洁能力从日常混乱中扣除。如果女佣产出超过混乱,清洁度就会上升。

女仆清理数学

女佣的日常清扫贡献:她的清扫基数乘以她的百分比奖金,然后在顶部加上她的平面奖金.
妓院总计:所有个别女佣产出均以总和乘以所有女佣百分比奖金,然后加上所有女佣定额奖金。
单人女佣物品效果只能提升他们的佩戴者;全人女佣效果能提升队伍的总和.

清理差异

每一天的实际清洁都有自然的差异——同一个女仆设置并不总是产生相同的日产出. 差额奖金 条目会减少随机性,使您的日常清洁产出更可预测。在低水平上,很少有项目,预计每天输出会吵闹;在高水平上,配备了不同的项目,输出会稳定。

学徒奖金

分配的工人 女佣的四分之一学徒 添加 +10 清洁每名学徒 这堆东西都是女佣的,一旦有多个工人,就用最快的速度将清洁工作推到乱七八糟的地方——向女佣区分配3名剩余工人每天免费增加+30清洁工作。

女佣如何积累经验

每个女佣每天获得以下经验:
  • 工作天数 (被动基 EXP).
  • 支付 EXP项目奖金 (单件女佣物品只乘她的EXP;全件女佣物品乘每个人的)
  • 所有女佣EXP奖金 (Lime 燃烧的激情史诗项目给予所有女佣+20%)
EXP奖金相乘:女仆基础EXP由她自己的EXP项目百分数提升,然后再次由所有女佣EXP百分数提升.
Stack Cleanning Manuary Vol. 2 (+20% 1), Mastery Guidebook (+35% 1),以及Lime的烧伤激情(+20%全部)在一个单身女佣身上,有效EXP率为%~85%.

The Maid System — Complete Guide

Overview

The Six Elemental Maidens of Domestic Perfection are the cleaning team of the Rose & Thorn — six trained specialists whose combined work keeps the brothel's cleanliness rating up, which directly caps daily income and reputation gains. They are not workers — they don't earn income, don't take clients, and run on an entirely separate progression system from the worker management track.
Each of the first 5 Maids is bound to one sacred element (Air, Fire, Earth, Spirit, Water) and trained in her own school of "Elemental Domestic Perfection" — a legendary, completely obscure prophecy that nobody else in the realm has heard of. Each maid was told by her master that one day she would find The Prismatic Master, a person of cosmic destiny who would unite all five elements under one roof. They each grew up convinced this would happen. Then they all show up at your brothel, realize you've gathered them all, and begin to suspect the prophecy might actually be real.
The reality is that the prophecy isn't about "saving the world through service." It's about five lonely, hyper-specialized experts finally finding peers who understand their dedication, sisters who challenge them, and a master who values them equally. The "Perfect Harmony" they were told about is just five exceptional people working together with someone who treats them with respect.
It is later revealed that there may actually be a 6th Maid, and the first 5 will be eagerly waiting for her to join the team.
This guide covers the mechanics of the maid system. For each maid's individual personality, recruitment, and dialogue, see her dedicated character guide.


The Six Maids

The six maids together cover the entire 24-hour cycle in 4-hour blocks. Each works alone during her shift, and no more than one maid staffs the brothel at a time. The order you acquire them doesn't matter (except for Onyx). The Maids' Quarters (Specialty Room) levels up automatically: its level equals the number of acquired maids + the number of apprentices, and adds to the daily upkeep.

How to Acquire Each Maid

The first 5 maids have their own recruitment path. None are automatic; none have prerequisites on the others. You can recruit them in any order. Onyx can only be recruited last (see details below).

Violet — "The Standing Ovation"

  • Where: Bards Guild
  • Requires: Bards Guild faction reputation 50+
  • Violet is a struggling theatrical performer who can't quite catch her break. After supporting her enough at the Bards Guild to earn 50 faction reputation, the "Standing Ovation" plot fires — you attend her latest performance and stand to applaud. Moved to tears, she joins the brothel as Maid #1 (Air).

Mandarin — Dungeon Rescue

  • Where: Dungeon scenario (Greater Demon encounter)
  • Requires: Dungeon exploration + combat capability
  • During a dungeon scenario, you encounter Mandarin cleaning. A Greater Demon will appear, claiming he owns her. Defeat the demon, and impressed by your strength, she offers her services as Maid #2 (Fire).

Lychee — Romantic Recruitment

  • Where: Around town (NPC)
  • Requires: Crush stage in her relationship arc
  • Lychee is an NPC you meet around town and build a relationship with through regular visits. She's beautiful, fashion-obsessed, and quickly identifies you as worth her attention. Once you reach Crush stage with her, a dialogue option appears to invite her to the brothel — accept, and she joins as Maid #3 (Earth).

Lime — Wizard Initiation Test Reward

  • Where: Wizard Tower (Gandon)
  • Requires: Complete the Wizard Initiation Test. For Gandon to have the option to initiate the test, you'll need him at Acquaintance+, Blinky (brothel rep 7+) at Known+, and 50+ Wizard Guild clout. If you fail the test, you can try it again the next day.
  • Lime is the recruitment reward for completing the Wizard Initiation Test at the Arcane Shop. Pass (or even just complete) the test, and she joins as Maid #4 (Spirit). She's been waiting at the Arcane Shop, sensing "cosmic energies" for some time, and the test outcome is the sign.

Rain — Back Alleys Search Encounter

  • Where: Back Alleys (search encounter)
  • Requires: Researching skill (engagement choice during the encounter)
  • Rain is found in the Back Alleys — an analytical researcher documenting the city's underbelly with a notebook and lens. The encounter offers multiple choices: the Researching skill choice resonates most with her, and she accepts a brothel invitation to continue her fieldwork. She joins as Maid #5 (Water).

Onyx — The Last Maid

  • Prerequisite: Having acquired all the other maids and triggered the plot "Whispers of Six" (requires all 5 maids at Close Friend+, auto-triggers in the Maids' Quarters (8 AM — 8 PM))
  • Where: Dungeon (scenarii)
  • Requires: Encountering her 5 times in different areas of the dungeon
  • Onyx is first found in the Epic Depths (floors 46-50). You will then need to find her 4 more times in a specific order to be able to recruit her:
  1. In the Overgrowth Depths (floors 31-36)
  2. In the Mechanical Depths (floors 41-45)
  3. In the Flooded floor (floor 13)
  4. In the Void Depths (floors 36-40), where she will finally agree to join your establishment. She joins as Maid #6 (Darkness)
To see more details about each maid, click on their name in the table above to access their walkthrough (only Onyx doesn't have one yet).
The bottlenecks: Bards Guild faction rep 50 (Violet), Crush stage romance (Lychee), and the Wizard Initiation Test (Lime) are the three slowest gates. Mandarin (combat) and Rain (Researching skill) are usually faster to unlock.
Obviously, since she requires all 5 maids to be unlocked, Onyx will be the hardest to get.

Maid Equipment & Items

The Maids Management Screen

The Maids Management screen is accessed from the Maids Quarters: "Manage Maids". The top section lists all your acquired maids, and selecting one of them lets you review their level, cleaning stat, and currently equipped items in the middle section. The bottom section of the screen contains your maid's item inventory. Maids can equip items in their slots (1 slot at level 3 or less, 2 slots at level 4+). Items grant a wide variety of bonuses, from simple flat cleaning boosts to brothel-wide income and reputation bonuses, mess reduction, EXP rate increases, and more. Each item is unique, meaning that once equipped, it's locked to that maid until unequipped. Most items can be acquired/purchased multiple times, while some are obtained only once. Maid-specific signature items (from the Epic tier) only fit their named maid. To equip an item, choose one in the inventory section, then select an empty maid's slot.

How Cleanliness Works (Why Maids Matter)

Maids exist to keep brothel cleanliness high. Cleanliness is a 0–100 score with 8 tiers, and it directly caps both daily income and daily reputation gain. Here's the multiplier table:
Cleanliness Tier Income Cap Reputation Multiplier
0–4 Revolting 0% (workers earn nothing) 0% (no rep at all)
5–14 Filthy 25% floor 25%
15–29 Dirty 50% floor 50%
30–44 Messy 75% floor 75%
45–59 Acceptable 100% baseline 100%
60–74 Clean 120% ceiling 120%
75–89 Very Clean 140% ceiling 140%
90–100 Spotless 160% ceiling 160%
A worker earning $100/day at Acceptable will earn $160/day at Spotless — and your reputation grows 60% faster. Cleanliness is one of the highest-leverage stats in the entire game.

Daily Mess Generation

Each day, the brothel generates mess based on:
  • Number of active workers (more workers = more mess)
  • Worker activity (high-client days = more mess)
  • Other factors (event-based mess, room upgrades, etc.)
Maids reduce that mess each day during their shifts. The total cleaning power of all six maids (their level + items + bonuses) is subtracted from daily mess. If maid output exceeds mess, cleanliness rises.

The Maid Cleaning Math

A maid's daily cleaning contribution: her base cleaning is multiplied by her percentage bonuses, then her flat bonuses are added on top.
For the brothel total: all individual maid outputs are summed, multiplied by any all-maids percentage bonuses, then all-maids flat bonuses are added.
Single-maid item effects only boost their wearer; all-maids effects boost the team total.

Cleaning Variance

Each day's actual cleaning has natural variance — the same maid setup doesn't always produce the same daily output. Cleaning Variance bonus items reduce that randomness, making your daily cleaning output more predictable. At low levels with few items, expect noisy daily output; at high levels with variance items equipped, output is steady.

Apprentice Bonus

Workers assigned as Maids' Quarters apprentices add +10 cleaning per apprentice to the team total. This stacks with maid items and is the single fastest way to push cleaning above mess once you have multiple workers — assigning 3 spare workers to the Maids' Quarters adds +30 cleaning daily for free.

How Maids Gain Experience

Each maid gains experience daily based on:
  • Days worked (passive base EXP)
  • Maid EXP item bonuses (single-maid items multiply only her EXP; all-maid items multiply everyone's)
  • All Maids EXP bonus (Lime's Burning Passion epic item gives +20% to all maids)
The EXP bonuses multiply together: the maid's base EXP is boosted by her own EXP-item percentage, and then again by any all-maids EXP percentage.
Stack Cleaning Manual Vol. 2 (+20% one), Mastery Guidebook (+35% one), and Lime's Burning Passion (+20% all) on a single maid for ~+85% effective EXP rate.

Power-Leveling One Maid

If you want to push a specific maid to Level 6 fast:
  1. Equip her with Mastery Guidebook (+35% EXP)
  2. Equip her second slot with Cleaning Manual Vol. 2 (+20% EXP) once she reaches Level 4
  3. If you have Lime's Burning Passion equipped on Lime, the +20% all-maids EXP stacks
  4. Total effective EXP rate: ~+85%

  3. United Sisterhood — late plots have them coordinating, supporting one another
The "Whispers of Six" plot at all-five Close Friend+ stage hints at a future expansion (a sixth Void Maid concept that exists in narrative documents).

The Prophecy of the Prismatic Master

Each maid believes in the same obscure prophecy — passed down only through the closed network of Elemental Service masters:
When chaos threatens all the realms,
And darkness seeks to overwhelm,
Five Maidens blessed with Elemental grace
Shall find their Master in a sacred place...
The prophecy claims that when all five Maidens serve The Prismatic Master together, they will achieve "Perfect Harmony of Service" — a mystical state where their combined elemental energies create absolute perfection, peace, and beauty.
The reality: Nobody else in the realm has ever heard of this prophecy. It's a private teaching from each of their five different masters. The player has NO idea this prophecy exists until the maids start acting strange about it.
The cosmic joke: The prophecy isn't actually about saving the world through service. It's about five lonely, hyper-specialized experts finally finding each other and a master who treats them well. The "Perfect Harmony" turns out to be just five exceptional people working together for someone who values them. That's the real miracle.
The player gradually realizes through dialogue:
  • Violet declares them The Master with theatrical flair
  • Mandarin accepts after seeing them defeat the demon
  • Lychee is convinced because they appreciate beauty and gave her gifts
  • Lime senses cosmic energy around them (and blushes a lot)
  • Rain documents the statistical anomaly that her predictions fail around the player
By the time all five are recruited, they're each privately convinced you're The Prismatic Master — and the competition for your favor begins.

Recommended Strategy

Phase 1: Get Your First Maid

  • Cleaning tanks fast in an unattended brothel — get a maid as soon as possible
  • Mandarin (Dungeon) and Rain (Back Alleys) are usually fastest
  • Violet if you've been building Bards Guild rep already
  • A single maid covers only 4 hours of the day; mess accumulates the rest

Phase 2: Build to Level 4 on Your First Maid

  • Level 4 unlocks her second equipment slot — the biggest power spike in the system
  • Equip her with Sturdy Broom ($300) and Cleaning Manual Vol. 1 ($400) early
  • Push her to Level 3 (600 EXP) for the Maid Mentor tutorial reward (Master Mop, Rare tier)

Phase 3: Acquire Maids 2 and 3

  • Aim for full daytime coverage first (Violet, Mandarin, Lychee — 6 AM to 6 PM)
  • Each new maid expands the dialogue and unlocks cross-maid plots
  • Build cleanliness multipliers to push past Acceptable (100%) toward Clean (120%)

Phase 4: Acquire Maids 4 and 5

  • Lime (Wizard Initiation Test) and Rain (Back Alleys) round out 24-hour coverage
  • "Three Maidens First Meeting" auto-triggers when Mandarin/Lychee/Rain all join
  • Whispers of Six plot at all-five Close Friend+

Phase 5: Equip the first 5 Maids Epic Signature Items

  • Pursue Kupie Love Training achievements for the first 5 maid-specific Epic items
  • Each maid's signature item is uniquely tuned to her strengths
  • Fully kitted out, the team should push cleanliness to Spotless (160% income/rep cap)

Phase 6: Apprentice Workers to the Maids' Quarters

  • Assign 3 workers to the Maids' Quarters → +30 cleaning daily for free
  • Combined with maxed maids and full epic items, you stay Spotless permanently

Phase 7: Build Relationships

  • Daily visits to maids unlock dialogue, plots, and the cross-maid chain
  • Push to Close Friend+ with all five for the "Whispers of Six" capstone
  • The maids' three-phase sisterhood arc plays out in their plots

Phase 8: Acquire Maid 6

  • Trigger Whispers of Six
  • Encounter her 5 times in the dungeon
  • Pursue her signature item: unlock Chrysalia, then complete "The Mourning Veil" (auto-trigger plot, requires Onyx recruited and Chrysalia at Friend+). You should get to Friend level with Chrysalia pretty fast, since her intro plot (the first time you actually visit her) gives a huge relationship boost. The real difficulty comes from actually unlocking her.

Tips & Optimal Play

  • The first maid is critical — without one, cleanliness collapses fast and your income/reputation cap drops to floor levels.
  • Level 4 is the power-spike level — second equipment slot dramatically increases what each maid contributes. Push past Level 3 ASAP.
  • Apprenticing workers to the Maids' Quarters is free cleaning (+10 per apprentice, max 3 = +30 daily). Use it.
  • Maid-specific Epic items only fit their named maid — Mandarin's Iron Will doesn't fit on Violet. The Perfection Pendant is the one Epic item any maid can wear.
  • Cleaning Variance items reduce daily randomness, especially valuable when you need predictable cleanliness levels for events (like Royal Delegation, which gates at Cleanliness 75+).
  • Mess Reduction is more efficient than raw cleaning at scale — every −1 mess per day is permanent; every +1 cleaning is a one-day output. Late game, mess reduction items dominate.
  • Lime's Burning Passion is the team multiplier — +20% EXP for all maids stacks on every maid simultaneously. Lime should be a Kupie achievement priority.
  • The Bards Guild reputation 50 gate is the slowest — start building Bards Guild rep early if you want Violet.
  • Don't ignore Lime's shy personality — she's the only maid with a major personality overhaul (heavy whispering, hesitant speech, lots of "fidgets"). Players sometimes overlook her quiet content.
  • Cross-character integration: Lime has a special plot meeting Blinky in the Arcane Room. Several maid plots intersect with brothel management plots (Esperanza tour, Elara recruitment).
  • The "Whispers of Six" plot teases a future addition: a Void Maid. She's actually a Darkness Maid and is now recruitable in the current build (9.7b).
  • Recruiting Onyx: Having triggered The "Whispers of Six" plot, pursue Onyx and her Maid-specific Epic item.
