Lindia Walkthrough
Spoiler warning: This guide covers Lindia's dungeon encounter, recruitment, relationship plots, heart-to-hearts, resort content, and late-game bond action.
This guide expands on Sh1n7's community-created [Lindia wiki page](https://chronoromancer.miraheze.org/wiki/Character:Lindia), used under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Requirements and mechanics below were rechecked against the current game source.

Overview

Lindia is a gifted, anxious wizard's apprentice in the Medieval era. Defeating her in the dungeon recruits her as worker 12. At the Rose & Thorn, her personal route focuses on rebuilding confidence through conversation and controlled magic.
Her implemented content includes four automatic personal scenes, two personal Heart-to-Hearts, a bazaar date, a permanent Mystic lesson, an Elemental Stone bond, two crafted training upgrades, a shared Whiskers scene and resort activities. This guide explains the requirements while leaving her history and later story discoveries to the game.

Finding and Recruiting Lindia

Lindia is the floor 7 miniboss. Her encounter has five rounds, 235 enemy Energy and 50 base attack power, and attacks the player's Energy. Easy difficulty adjusts enemy Energy and incoming damage through the shared miniboss system.
Defeating her activates worker 12 before the victory-scene choices. Recruitment does not require choosing an intimate response, a particular affection stage or already owning the Rose & Thorn. Reach the brothel and its relevant rooms afterward to use her home portrait route.

Victory Choices

The gentle response is always available and gives +1 Charm. The first optional intimate response requires Beast 25 and player Erotic Experience 300; its continuation requires Erotic Experience 600 with the same Beast threshold. Its ending gives +1 Beast under the game's normal access rules. These are alternative victory presentations; all recruit Lindia. Neither is required for her later relationship route.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Defeat Lindia on floor 7 and confirm she is an active worker.
  2. Visit Training Room, 9 AM–6 PM, for Lindia at the Training Room: 5 base affection.
  3. Build to Known using four daily Talks. Begin giving two Books per day once Known.
  4. Return to the Training Room for Margin Notes at Known. The warm response gives 13 base affection including completion.
  5. At Acquaintance, complete Controlled Magic. If Mystic 40 is available, use its stronger round-2 answer.
  6. At Friend, return for The Master Question, claim Spell Control Notes if Mystic 35, and take the Bazaar Date during 9 AM–2 PM.
  7. At Close Friend, after Controlled Magic, complete Close Enough. Its stronger round-2 answer needs learned Empathy.
  8. At Crush, after The Master Question, return for Apprentice No More.
  9. At In Love, after Apprentice No More and Close Enough, bring the two crafted gifts to unlock her training upgrades. If you own the Elemental Stone, use her daily stone bond.
  10. Send her on an available Sirena Isle vacation for repeatable resort activities and the fourteen one-time encounters below.
The bazaar date does not require either personal H2H. Her personal H2Hs do not require the bazaar date or all four automatic scenes. Those conditions are separate.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

* Home portrait missing: defeat her floor 7 encounter, keep worker 12 active, return her from vacation and use Bar 4–8 AM or Training Room 9 AM–6 PM.
* Automatic scene missing: check the preceding scene and its relationship stage, then return to the Training Room during 9 AM–6 PM.
* Book missing: reach Known and carry a Book. The two locations share the same daily Book counter.
* Close Enough missing: reach Close Friend and complete Controlled Magic.
* Bazaar date missing: reach Friend, use the Training Room before 2 PM, and check whether the one-time date is already completed.
* Crafted upgrade missing: reach In Love, complete Apprentice No More and Close Enough, bring its exact gift and use the home Training Room portrait.
* Resort scene missing: keep every listed participant active and vacationing, and visit the specified room in its window. Ordinary home schedules do not apply on vacation.
Lindia currently has no apartment conversation, Kupie date entry or personal pregnancy route. Her two personal H2Hs, bazaar date, worker progression, stone bond and resort content are the implemented paths covered here.
