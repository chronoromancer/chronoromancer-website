概览

Shelly 运行 西雷纳岛礼品店她现在的行走方式是对话,收集并支出海贝壳,以及11次与度假工人的一次性会面。她是一个永久的岛屿居民,所以你不需要招募或派她去度假。

去她的店铺

解锁到 西雷纳岛经过Marina的船道,然后使用 到礼品店 从度假村的入口,店里的旅行 没时间了 和要求 有偿使用启用 进入 回到岛中心。 Marina 对船的先决条件。
Shelly的肖像和 浏览礼品店 可用 上午8时至9时房间本身没有开放时间限制,因此在9点之后进入房间并不能保证任何一项行动都可以使用。

谈话和关系进展

说话 带 20分钟 并给出 1个真爱 它的配置是: 每天50次正面会谈但没有金钱、能源或平静的成本,也没有爱的前提条件。
除了正面的津贴, 处理者记录了负面的感情。 等待新的一天而不是继续。 Shelly 的普通 Talk 计数器与度假工人使用的通用 Talk 计数器是分开的 。
列出的关系奖励是基础值. 最喜欢的STAT在达到阈值时会增加奖金,特殊的关系技巧会在共享随机卷前将调整的基数增加一倍. 设备,buffs和对齐可以进一步改变最终的奖励. 提高爱心不会取代追求旗帜或更早的心跳.
Shelly目前没有赠送礼物的动作,技能课,心与心,日期,公寓对话,工人招聘或NPC亲密画廊资产. 提升她普通的感情不会在目前的建筑中打开额外的编剧浪漫场景. 评估 是她特殊的关系奖金 而不是她现在教的技能

收集海壳

海贝壳是商店的货币 完成符合条件的度假胜地活动 找到一枚炮弹的概率为25%活动 运气 重试一次失败的卷子; 有两个独立的卷子,总的机会是 43.75%,而成功的活动则会给出一个贝壳。
符合条件的活动包括: 放松,巴菲特,斯帕,日落步行和私人海滩时间 每个同伴的活动和日常限制在他们的性格页面上描述。普通谈话没有活动贝壳卷。
商店购买不会将普通的黄金换成贝壳。在浏览前收集货币 。

建议的进展

  2. 当您想要发展Shelly的普通关系时进行交谈,使用她最喜爱的高调阈值,并在有现成的评价奖金.

  4. 购买符合您当前作战或管理需要的物品,然后根据需要为工人/女佣购买设备。
  5. 带符合条件的在职工人度假,并重访店铺进行一次性Shelly碰面.
  6. 一旦你拥有皇后石,使用 蜗牛史莱姆 当你有十枚炮弹 想要另一个石层。

  • 我买不起东西,尽管有Gold: 店里用海贝壳
  • 蜗牛史莱姆缺席: 拥有Queenstone作为关键物品;它是一种升级服务,而不是获得石头的方法.
  • 海洋断裂并没有增加一个保证的亲情点: 它提高中立的NPC的上随机约束;它不是每个相互作用的固定奖金.
  • 我的工人购买似乎不活跃: 仅仅拥有并不影响设备的作用。
  • 共享店面不会触发: 时间必须是早上8点到9点,
  • 没有浪漫菜单出现 之后,爱增加: Shelly目前作者的内容是上面的个人谈话,商店和分享的遭遇.

Overview

Shelly runs the Sirena Isle Gift Shop. Her current walkthrough is about conversations, collecting and spending Sea Shells, and eleven one-time encounters with vacationing workers. She is a permanent island resident, so you do not need to recruit or send her on vacation.

Getting to Her Shop

Unlock travel to Sirena Isle through Marina's boat route, then use To Gift Shop from the resort entrance. The shop travel takes no time and requires paid access. Use To Entrance to return to the island hub. See Marina for the boat prerequisites.
Shelly's portrait and Browse the Gift Shop are available 8 AM–9 PM. The room itself has no opening-hours restriction, so being inside after 9 PM does not guarantee either action remains available.

Talk and Relationship Progress

Talk takes 20 minutes and gives 1 actual base affection at every relationship stage. Its configured allowance is 50 positive talks per day, though the opening window and available time limit how many fit into an ordinary day. It has no money, Energy or Calm cost and no affection prerequisite.
Beyond the positive allowance, the handler records negative affection. Wait for a new day instead of continuing. Shelly's ordinary Talk counter is separate from the generic Talk counter used by vacationing workers.
Listed relationship rewards are base values. Favorite stats add bonuses when their thresholds are met, and the special relationship skill doubles the adjusted base before the shared random roll. Equipment, buffs and alignment can further change the final reward. Raising affection does not replace quest flags or earlier heart-to-hearts.
Shelly has no current gift-giving action, skill lesson, heart-to-heart, date, apartment conversation, worker recruitment or NPC intimacy gallery assets. Raising her ordinary affection does not unlock an additional authored romance scene in the current build. Appraisal is her special relationship bonus, rather than a skill she currently teaches.

Collecting Sea Shells

Sea Shells are the shop's currency. Completing eligible resort activities gives a 25% chance of finding one shell. An active Luck Potion retries one failed roll; with two independent rolls the overall chance is 43.75%, and a successful activity gives one shell.
Eligible activities include the authored Relax, Buffet, Spa, Sunset Walk and Private Beach Time activities where a character has them, plus the shared island encounters. Each companion's available activities and daily limits are described on their character page. Ordinary Talk has no activity shell roll.
Shop purchases do not exchange ordinary Gold for shells. Collect the currency before browsing.

Recommended Progression

  1. Reach Sirena Isle and visit the shop 8 AM–9 PM.
  2. Talk when you want to develop Shelly's ordinary relationship, using her high favorite-stat thresholds and Appraisal bonus when available.
  3. Complete daily island activities to collect Sea Shells.
  4. Buy the item that serves your current combat or management needs, then equip worker/maid purchases where required.
  5. Bring eligible active workers on vacation and revisit the shop for their one-time Shelly encounters.
  6. Once you own Queenstone, use Snail Slime whenever you have 10 shells and want another stone level.

Troubleshooting

  • Shelly or Browse the Gift Shop is missing: check the current time. Their window ends at 9 PM even if you can remain in the room.
  • I cannot afford an item despite having Gold: the shop uses Sea Shells.
  • Snail Slime is absent: own Queenstone as a key item; it is an upgrade service, not a way to obtain the stone.
  • Ocean Fragrance did not add a guaranteed affection point: it raises the upper random bound for Neutral NPCs; it is not a fixed bonus on every interaction.
  • My worker purchase seems inactive: equip the item on that worker. Possession alone does not apply its equipment effect.
  • A shared shop scene will not trigger: the named worker must be active and currently vacationing, the time must be 8 AM–9 PM, and that scene must not already be completed.
  • No romance menu appears after increasing affection: Shelly's current authored content is the personal Talk, shop and shared encounters above.
