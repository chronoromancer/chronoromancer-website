Overview

Jarid is the enforcement officer stationed at Synergy Corp in the Future. He is a supporting NPC with a scheduled portrait and a shared Syndicate encounter. His character record has no Talk, gift or personal relationship menu. This guide covers his actual appearances and encounter requirements.

Visiting Enforcement Operations

Travel to Synergy Corp Corporate Tower in the Prime Sector and choose To Enforcement Operations from the lobby. This room-entry action takes zero minutes, but Saber checks access: she must already have granted it, or you must have completed Story Beat 76. Her blocked response leaves you in the lobby.
After entering the Enforcement Operations Center, Jarid's portrait can appear 24 hours a day, on both odd and even days. He has no additional relationship-stage or story-flag requirement in his own schedule. The room's Return to Lobby travel action also takes zero minutes.
His greeting describes his enforcement role. It is not a Talk action, affection award, housing-tier penalty or behavioral-score minigame. No such player-facing interaction is configured on his portrait.

Badge and Bluster Walkthrough

  1. Progress Chamel's Syndicate route through The Punchline.
  2. Visit the Syndicate Parlor for the one-time automatic scene The Weight of a Secret. Its entry requirement is The Punchline completed.
  3. After completing it, visit the Syndicate Gates for Badge and Bluster. This scene triggers automatically once and has no extra time-of-day, Jarid affection or NPC-stage gate configured.
  4. Choose the two responses below according to which Syndicate relationships you want to improve. Complete the encounter normally to record it and receive its base rewards.
The scene starts at the Syndicate Gates, separate from Jarid's Synergy Corp portrait. Its registration does not require entering Enforcement Operations or completing Story Beat 76. Conversely, gaining access to his office does not substitute for The Weight of a Secret.
The scene gives 5 base Kitty affection, 3 Poison, 3 Motley and 2 Bayne, plus the selected choice rewards. Jarid has no affection reward configured for this encounter. Listed values are base points before each recipient's shared relationship calculation.

These are different relationship goals; no one response pair maximizes every NPC. The code still uses Motley's older internal name, Harlequin, for his reward entry; the current character name is Motley. The encounter's story outcome is left for you to discover.

Current Content and Troubleshooting

Jarid has no personal quest chain, lesson, date, Heart-to-Heart, apartment invitation, worker recruitment, resort interaction, Kupie selection or pregnancy route configured. His office's empty action list is his current supporting-NPC behavior.
  • To Enforcement Operations does not enter the room: obtain Saber's explicit access or finish Story Beat 76. Jarid's own all-day schedule does not bypass that room gate.
  • Portrait visible but no conversation buttons: Jarid has no ordinary interaction menu; waiting or building an unrelated relationship will not add one.
  • Badge and Bluster missing: complete The Weight of a Secret, then visit Syndicate Gates, rather than the office. The encounter only runs once.
  • Trying to raise Jarid affection through the scene: the rewards go to Kitty, Poison, Motley and Bayne.
  • Another eligible NPC occupies the portrait slot: use the available character-cycle control to check the room's other NPC portraits.
