Overview

Jarid is a supporting enforcement contact. His current content is an office portrait and a shared Syndicate appearance, rather than a personal friendship or romance route.

Where to find them

Enter the Synergy Corp lobby and use its enforcement-office entrance,0 min. Saber must allow inner-office access, or Story 76 must be completed. Once inside, Jarid’s portrait is at Enforcement Operations Center all day. The shared scene instead occurs at Syndicate Gates.

Route and relationship milestones

Complete The Weight of a Secret, then visit the Syndicate Gates for Badge and Bluster, once. There is no Jarid affection requirement or special hour added to that event.

Automatic sceneLocation / start windowRequirements
Badge and BlusterSyndicate Gatesafter The Weight of a Secret

The scene supports the group’s next gate encounter. Continue through the Syndicate guide for the preceding missions and later alliance; Jarid’s office portrait does not replace the gate visit.

Activities and rewards

Jarid has no direct Talk, gift, lesson, date or heart-to-heart action. His dialogue about enforcement does not provide a player-operated housing, compliance or recalibration service.

What usually blocks the next step

  • An empty personal action menu is expected.
  • For Badge and Bluster, visit the gates after the required shared scene; waiting in the office will not trigger it.
  • If Saber blocks the office entrance, follow the main story or her access instructions; the office’s all-day portrait does not bypass that door.

概览

Jarid是执法方面的配角，目前提供办公室头像及一段共同辛迪加事件，没有独立友谊或恋爱路线。

去哪里找

从Synergy Corp大厅进入执法办公室，0分钟；需要Saber允许进入内部办公室，或完成主线76。进入后，Jarid全天位于执法行动中心，共同事件则发生在辛迪加大门。

路线与关系进展

完成秘密的重量后，到辛迪加大门触发徽章与虚张声势，仅一次；该事件不额外要求Jarid好感或特定时段。

自动事件地点／开始时段条件
徽章与虚张声势辛迪加大门完成：秘密的重量

这段事件衔接团体的下一次大门事件。前面的任务与后续联盟见辛迪加指南，不能用办公室头像替代大门到访。

行动与奖励

Jarid没有直接交谈、送礼、课程、约会或交心行动。执法话题的对白不会开放由玩家操作的住房、合规或重新校准服务。

卡住时检查什么

  • 个人行动菜单为空是正常情况。
  • 共同执法事件需要在前置完成后到访大门，待在办公室不会触发。
  • 若Saber挡住入口，请推进主线或按她的进入提示行动；办公室的全天头像不能绕过房门。
