Overview

Glitch is the hacker in Glitch's Den, reached from the Future Fringe Zone's Unregistered Housing. Her current route has seven personal story encounters, three heart-to-hearts, two dates, four skill lessons and an army recruitment milestone. She also has a separate progression track in Kupie's Love Training.

First Meeting and Early Progress

  1. Complete Story Beat 21. In Unregistered Housing, choose Visit Glitch's Den. The travel action takes zero minutes and is hidden until that previous story beat is complete.
  2. Enter the Den at any hour for Story Beat 22, which gives 10 base Glitch affection.
  3. Story Beat 22 completion teaches System Hacking if you do not already know it, adds Technology +5, advances 18 hours, then sets energy, calm and satiation to 10. Plan recovery supplies and rest around this first meeting. There is no separate lesson fee for that story reward.
  4. Use Talk to Glitch, up to five per game day, for 15 minutes and 1 base point each. At Known, use Give Chocolate, consuming one Chocolate for 4 base points, up to twice per game day. The handler uses that fixed four-point base value.
  5. Continue the main story through Story Beat 66 to open her later personal route. High affection alone does not replace this story prerequisite.
Talk and Chocolate use separate daily counters. Beyond their respective limits, the handlers record −1 base points with the shared over-limit handling. The Den has no ordinary closing time, but H2Hs and dates have their own start windows.

Current Route Boundaries and Troubleshooting

Glitch's current portrait menu has no apartment/private-time visit, ordinary repeatable intimate-video action, optional pregnancy branch or Rose & Thorn worker recruitment. Her H2Hs, dates, automatic story scenes and Kupie rewards cover her authored current interactions.
* The Den travel action is missing: complete Story Beat 21, then check Unregistered Housing.
* The later route will not begin: complete Story Beat 66 and reach at least Acknowledged.
* Permission to Disconnect is missing: complete The Trace Runs Both Ways and The Camera She Chose H2H, reach Friend, then re-enter the Den.
* The Person Behind the Alert is missing: finish When the Channel Is Quiet H2H and reach Close Friend.
* Mutual Authentication is missing: complete No Diagnostic for This H2H and reach Crush.
* A date button is missing: check its exact H2H, quest flag, paid access, clock window and one-time completion state.
* Glitch is locked in Kupie: check her main-game Friend stage, Ghost's minigame level 3, and paid roster access; main-story army recruitment is not an additional Kupie selection prerequisite.
