Overview

Noella is the warm hostess of Mistletoe Lodge in Christmas Village. Her route includes eight affection milestones, four automatic Extras milestones, a repeatable reward at 80% Extras completion, and a one-time mistletoe task.

Getting There

  1. In the Future Fringe Zone, visit the Sky-Level Atmospheric Management Station and choose Christmas Village.
  2. Enter Mistletoe Lodge from the village. Both Christmas travel actions take zero minutes. Noella's portrait is available all day, without a prior affection requirement.
  3. Complete the automatic Welcome to Christmas Village scene once. It awards 10 base affection each to Noella, Holly and Ginger, plus one Token. Choose “I'd have to say red.” for another 5 Noella base points. Green favors Holly; blue favors Ginger.
  4. Enable Christmas activities with Toggle Christmas Spirit for holiday location tasks and the Claw Machine. This setting is separate from learning the Christmas Spirit skill.
The village is available on game days throughout the year; Noella's schedule has no real-world December restriction.

Check the List and Extras Completion

At Acknowledged, use Check the List from Noella's portrait to see your current Extras completion percentage, shown to one decimal place. The check takes zero minutes, awards no affection or Tokens, and does not itself play the 80% reward.
Progress is measured by distinct earned entries in the current Extras catalogue, divided by the catalogue's total entries. Repeating an already earned scene does not repeatedly increase that percentage. Gifts, affection points, Tokens held and Christmas tasks completed are not interchangeable with the Extras percentage.
Explore and unlock new Extras across the game, then return to the lodge for Noella's milestone encounters. New catalogue content can lower the displayed percentage by increasing the total number of possible entries. Her already earned main reward access is preserved as described below.

Noella's Reward at 80%

At Acknowledged or higher, reach 80% Extras completion, then select Noella's Reward from her portrait. It plays The Complete List, including Noella's registered animated reward. Each live completion takes 30 minutes and 30 Energy, awards 25 base Noella affection, and gives two Tokens.
The scene is repeatable. It has no extra money, consumable, paid-version or higher romance-stage gate. The action lists a three-per-day allowance, but its current custom handler does not enforce or record that limit. Its practical repeat costs remain time and Energy. No additional player erotic-experience or NPC intimacy-experience reward is configured.
Once earned, access remains unlocked if the Extras catalogue grows and your percentage drops below 80%. Existing completion or earned reward records also preserve eligibility on older saves. You still need the action's Acknowledged stage. A lower percentage shown by Check the List therefore does not necessarily mean the reward should disappear.
Rewards described here apply to live gameplay. Replaying a collected scene from Extras is not a method for farming live affection or Tokens.

Mistletoe Kiss: Christmas Task 1

Enable Christmas activities, reach Known, and choose Mistletoe Kiss from the Mistletoe Lodge location menu. This is separate from Noella's portrait interactions. It takes five minutes, completes Christmas Task 1, and awards one Token plus five Noella affection directly.
The task is one-time, has no special clock window, and has no separately configured money, consumable, Energy or intimacy-experience reward. Its direct affection does not use the shared relationship multiplier.
Completing it advances the shared Christmas task checklist by one. It does not substitute for Noella's Extras-completion thresholds. See Holly's walkthrough for the full 25-task list and task-milestone route.

Christmas Spirit and Route Boundaries

The learned Christmas Spirit skill is a possible Claw Machine prize until acquired. Winning it teaches the skill and removes it from later rolls. Toggle Christmas Spirit enables activities; it does not teach that skill, and Noella has no separate paid lesson for it.
Her current personal content consists of the eight affection milestones, four automatic Extras milestones, repeatable 80% reward and mistletoe task. No separate Heart-to-Heart menu, apartment invitation, worker recruitment, resort-worker or pregnancy route is configured for her.

Troubleshooting

  • Noella's portrait is missing: enter Mistletoe Lodge, where she stays all day.
  • Give Chocolate is unavailable: reach Acquaintance and hold ordinary Chocolate.
  • Give Hot Chocolate is unavailable: reach Known and hold the separate Christmas Hot Chocolate resource.
  • Check the List is missing: reach Acknowledged.
  • An Extras milestone does not play: check the current completion percentage and re-enter the lodge. A previously completed milestone is one-time.
  • Noella's Reward is missing: reach Acknowledged and either attain 80% Extras completion or have previously earned its eligibility.
  • The reward remains available below 80%: the current route intentionally preserves earned access as the Extras catalogue grows.
  • Mistletoe Kiss is missing: enable Christmas activities, reach Known, use the location menu, and check that Task 1 is not already completed.
  • Repeating a scene does not increase Extras percentage: already earned entries count once; unlock different unearned entries.
