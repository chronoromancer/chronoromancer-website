Overview

Nerida is the hot-springs and wellness attendant found across the Present, Medieval and Future eras. Her route includes healing baths and daily wisdom, six personal story encounters, three heart-to-hearts, one Stargazing Date, an expanded branching private bath, a separate hot-springs animation, two shared encounters, Kupie, Aether Stone bonding, and an optional family continuation.
This guide covers the playable requirements and reward choices while leaving her deeper history and the scenes' revelations to the game.

Recommended Walkthrough

  1. Meet Nerida at any of her three wellness locations. Use Listen to Wisdom for affection and Take Bath when you need recovery. Both daily counters are shared across the eras.
  2. Enter Present Downtown's Tranquil Spa for The Tranquil Spa introduction. Caring gives its best reward, 9 base total.
  3. At Known, revisit the Spa 9 AM–6 PM for Still Water. At Acquaintance, complete Quiet Waters, from the Spa 7–11 PM.
  4. At Friend, visit Medieval Royal Hot Springs for The Same Water, after Still Water and Quiet Waters. At Close Friend, return there for The Third Answer, then complete The Unasked Question at the same springs.
  5. At Crush, visit Future Prime Sector's Virtual Wellness Immersion Center for A Room for Rivers, after The Third Answer and The Unasked Question. Return to the Present Spa, 7–11 PM, for No Single Shore, then complete One Breath there.
  6. At Friend, her paid Stargazing Date can be started from the Present Spa, 5–7 PM. It does not require a personal H2H or the completed cross-era story chain.
  7. At Romantic, use Private Bath with Nerida, Present Spa, 7–10 PM, in the paid version. Its ordinary routes can be explored independently of finishing her personal story chain.
  8. At 2,000 ordinary affection, try the separate Intimate with Nerida animation at Royal Hot Springs, 8 PM–midnight, in the paid version.
  9. For Kupie, reach Friend in the ordinary game and Potion Brewing level 7 inside Kupie. For family content, finish No Single Shore and One Breath, then satisfy the separate requirements below.
The personal story deliberately moves between eras. Raising affection without changing to the next scene's required location will not trigger that scene.

Listen to Wisdom

From Stranger, spend 20 minutes for 1 actual base affection, up to eight successful uses per day. All eight cost 160 minutes for 8 base affection before modifiers. Changing location or era does not reset the allowance. The action changes dialogue as affection grows; it does not teach a player skill.

Take Bath

From Stranger, spend 45 minutes and $100 for 3 base affection and Health +50, Energy +50, Calm +50, Satiation +20. Recovery respects the player's normal limits. The bath has ten successful uses daily, shared across all three locations, separately from wisdom listening.
All ten baths cost 450 minutes and $1,000, for 30 base affection plus their recovery. Using both full allowances totals 610 minutes, $1,000 and 38 base affection, before travel and modifiers. You can use fewer baths according to your recovery needs.
Beyond either allowance, the handler submits a negative affection reward, and an exhausted bath attempt does not provide its recovery. Stop at the successful daily limits. There is no ordinary Flowers, Book, Wine or other gift action for Nerida.

At Romantic, select Private Bath with Nerida from the Present Spa, 7–10 PM, in the paid version. Entry costs 60 minutes and 20 Energy, with no configured money or gift cost.
Its action declares a one-use daily allowance, but the current spa handler does not record or check a daily use. Optional Family Continuation

This continuation requires pregnancy content unlocked and enabled, Akira's Pregnancy Protocol complete, 2,000 ordinary Nerida affection, 500 Nerida NPC intimacy XP, and both No Single Shore and One Breath complete. It also requires that she is not already pregnant or recovering and that you do not already have a child together.
Use the Private Bath, Present Spa, 7–10 PM, and select its optional family opening when eligible. Complete the authored conversation and commitment path. It uses the normal 60-minute / 20-Energy entry cost and awards 50 base affection, 50 player Erotic XP and 40 Nerida NPC intimacy XP. Conception is recorded at the designated late choice; the ordinary hub's intimacy choices do not replace this family path.
The three automatic milestones occur at Medieval Royal Hot Springs while Nerida is present, with no hour restriction. They grant 5, 7 and 10 base affection, respectively, without additional configured intimacy/player Erotic XP.
  1. The first milestone becomes ready from pregnancy age 11 days. Conception is age zero, with the hidden stage through age ten.
  2. The final-stage milestone becomes ready 20 days after entering the early stage, after the first milestone is complete. Completing it starts the final stage.
  3. Delivery becomes ready 30 days after the final stage starts, and at least one day after the final-stage milestone. With prompt visits, the earliest age is 61 days; delayed visits move it later.
Check In / Family Check In costs 30 minutes, once per game day, and grants 5 direct affection. It can be used at an available portrait; a ready milestone launches through the check-in only at its registered Royal Hot Springs location. No numerical prenatal-visit count is required for delivery by the current configuration. Postpartum recovery lasts eight days, followed by the family stage.
Listen to Wisdom, Take Bath, personal story encounters and Aether Stone bonding remain pregnancy-safe. Her ordinary H2Hs, Stargazing Date, private bath and ordinary springs animation are blocked while she is showing in early/final stages and during postpartum recovery. Nerida has no separately configured pregnancy-intimacy movie action.

Aether Stone Bonding

If you already own the Aether Stone and Nerida is In Love, use Intimate Bonding from her portrait at any of the three wellness locations, all hours.
It costs 60 minutes, raises the Aether Stone level by 1, gives zero ordinary affection, and is once daily shared across the three locations. It has no configured money, Energy, Calm or consumable cost. The stone is retained as a key item.
While owned, each stone level contributes +1 maximum Energy and +1 maximum Calm. This bonding action upgrades the stone; its acquisition belongs to the wider story and is not granted by an ordinary bath.

Completion and Troubleshooting

  • Next story scene missing: use the required era and location, complete the listed earlier scene/H2H, and meet its stage and hours.
  • Private bath missing: reach Romantic, use the Present Spa, 7–10 PM, and check paid access and pregnancy-stage restrictions.
  • Springs animation missing: check 2,000 ordinary affection, Royal Hot Springs, 8 PM–midnight, paid access and its separate daily video use.
  • New private-bath options missing in an older save: the current version refreshes Nerida's saved spa conversation when loading. Use the current game build and reload the save; the older six-round ordinary route is not the complete current bath content.
  • Kupie locked: check Friend, paid access and Potion Brewing level 7.
  • Family opening missing: check No Single Shore, One Breath, Akira's protocol, feature access, 2,000 ordinary affection and 500 Nerida NPC XP.
  • A family milestone is ready elsewhere: visit Royal Hot Springs; ordinary availability in another era does not move the lifecycle plot.
Nerida's configured content does not include a venue invite minigame, personal Christmas scene, numbered brothel-worker training, maid progression or resort vacation. Her ordinary baths, private CYOA, springs movie, Kupie and family continuation use the distinct rules above.
