  6. 完成 Helsa 的猎人血迹。在目前可用的 Becky 中, 返回 Torea 的血迹室, 血液选择 。

Overview

Torea is a playful Sangrael vampire who spends time in both clan locations and the Present Era. Her current relationship route uses ordinary Talk, three dates and three heart-to-hearts, with several personal events depending on Helsa's route.
This walkthrough lists the requirements and rewards while leaving the clan's origins, discoveries and story outcomes to the game.

Talk to Torea takes 20 minutes, gives 1 base affection, and permits three positive uses per day. The allowance is shared across all four portraits. Exhausted Talk gives a negative relationship result; moving locations does not reset it.

Recommended Route Order

  1. Finish Story Beat61, then build affection through the shared daily Talks and eligible automatic events.
  2. At Acknowledged, visit Torea's Room during 7–10 PM for The Hunt in Neon. Follow it at the Back Alley midnight–3 AM for The Scent of Silver, then 1–4 AM for Helsa's The Hunt Arrives.
  3. At Friend, take the ordinary Fair Date from Torea's School portrait during 11 AM–2 PM, then complete The Shape of Appetite in her room.
  4. Progress Helsa to Friend, complete Helsa's post-convention library scene and The Hunter I Chose, then return to Torea's Room for Loose the Cat. Visit the Vampire Lounge for Teeth With Ribbons.
  5. At Torea Close Friend, complete A Larger Night in her room during 8–10 PM, then start Boardwalk After Sunset during 6–8 PM on a later visit.
  6. Finish Helsa's A Hunter Bleeds. With Becky currently available, return to Torea's Room for Blood Owed, Blood Chosen.
  7. Complete Helsa's The Long Pursuit and Stargazing Date, then The Arrow She Does Not Loose. With both women Close Friend, visit the Back Alley during 9 PM–midnight for Five Minutes of Night.
  8. At Torea Crush, complete A Claim Answered during 8–10 PM. At Romantic, return during 7–10 PM for The Vampire's Claim.
