Overview

VOID’s late-game relationship route at Master Control is required to finish Story 100. It includes daily conversations and gifts, four personal heart-to-hearts and four one-time dates. Use the requirements below to plan the meetings and their resource costs.

Where to find VOID

Travel to Future → Prime Sector → VOID Command. After Story 99, finish the associated late-story objective there; Enter VOID Master Control then opens the zero-minute entrance. Follow current Objectives for that prerequisite before seeking the character portrait.

The ordinary portrait opens after Story 99 and is available all day at VOID Master Control. Complete the required personal meetings before advancing Story 100: after that milestone, completed material can be revisited through Extras, while new live meetings are unavailable.

Route and relationship milestones

Return to Master Control as the relationship grows. At Known (25), the early automatic encounter opens Why You Keep Returning. Acquaintance (50) then opens the next personal milestone; Friend (100) continues the sequence. At Close Friend (200), complete Why Anything Matters and the following milestone.

At Crush (400), The Unsolved Variable follows. Romantic (800) and the next automatic milestone open the adventure-date sequence. At Intimate (1600), Between Two Skies opens The Distance She Chooses. In Love (3000), that conversation and the date unlock the final personal invitation. The optional reference table lists titles and prerequisites without scene outcomes.

Activities and rewards

Talk costs 15 minutes and gives 1 base relationship point, with three safe uses daily. Give Jewelry costs 10 minutes and one Jewelry for 5 base points; Gemstone Heels costs 10 minutes and one pair for 8. The two gifts each have their own once-daily allowance.

Each core heart-to-heart takes 60 minutes and 20 Energy, once. Each date takes 120 minutes and 25 Energy, with no money cost, once. The three adventure dates form their own sequence; Between Two Skies belongs to the main personal route.

High relationship points do not replace the preceding scenes and conversations. Her preferred-stat values in the optional reference are unusually high; they are bonus targets rather than requirements to start Talk or gifts.

Relationship rewards are base values unless marked direct. Shared relationship modifiers affect the final gain. Repeated actions keep their daily allowance when you meet the same character elsewhere.

Missing actions and useful checks

If the portrait is missing, check the Story 99/100 window and Master Control location before changing stats. If a later conversation is missing, revisit for the automatic milestone between the two heart-to-hearts. Raising points alone does not advance her dialogue tone past unfinished personal milestones.

概览

VOID在主控室有推进主线100所需的后期关系路线，包括日常交谈与礼物、四段个人谈心、四项一次性约会。可以按下列条件安排会面与资源。

出现地点与时间

前往未来 → 核心区 → VOID 指挥部。主线99后，先在这里完成接续的后期目标，才会开放进入 VOID 主控室这一零分钟入口。寻找角色头像前，请先按当前目标完成这一步。

主线99完成后，可全天在VOID主控室拜访她。推进主线100前，请完成本页所列必要会面；之后可在「附加内容」回看已完成的事件，但不能再开始新的会面。

路线与关系进展

关系提升后返回主控室。已知（25）阶段的早期自动事件会开放你为何一再回来；相识（50）继续下一项个人里程碑，朋友（100）再推进后续。亲密朋友（200）时，完成为何有事物值得在乎及接续事件。

迷恋（400）后继续未解的变量。浪漫感（800）及下一项自动里程碑会开放冒险约会流程。亲密（1600）时，两片天空之间开放她选择的距离；恋爱中（3000）并完成这段谈心和约会后，开放最后的个人邀约。可选参考表只列名称与条件，不写事件结果。

行动与奖励

交谈消耗15分钟，给予1点基础关系，每日安全额度3次。送珠宝消耗10分钟和一份珠宝，获得5点基础关系；宝石高跟鞋消耗10分钟和一双对应物品，获得8点。两种礼物各有独立的每日一次额度。

四段核心谈心各消耗60分钟、20精力，一次性完成；四项约会各消耗120分钟、25精力，不花财富，也只能完成一次。三项冒险约会有自己的前后顺序，两片天空之间则属于核心个人路线。

高关系点不能跳过前置事件或谈心。可选参考栏中的偏好属性数值很高，但那是加成目标，并非开始交谈或送礼的门槛。

除注明直接增加外，关系奖励均为基础值，最终增量受共通关系修正影响。同一角色换地点不会重置重复动作的每日额度。

动作缺失时的检查

头像没有出现时，先检查主线99／100之间的阶段和主控室地点，不必先调整属性。缺少后续谈心时，请返回触发两段谈心之间的自动里程碑。单纯提高点数，也不会让对话语气跨过尚未完成的个人事件。
