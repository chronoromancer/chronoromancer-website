当前发布可用性

VOID在目前发布的故事中还没有可访问的字符行走. 普通的人物时间表被锁定在超出当前发行边界的故事事件背后。 提高爱心、购买礼物或在不同时间返回不能绕过故事的大门。
目前发行的内容包括Story Beats1–85.本页面保留其现有的肖像和画廊条目,但后来的对话,路由要求和选择被省略,直到发布内容,以避免主要破坏者,指令播放器目前无法遵循.

相关进展

继续使用游戏当前寻寻追踪器发布的主要故事。在普通的 VOID 字符路径可用之前,其他角色可以有参考 VOID 的求求求;这些参考文献不会解锁此肖像的交互功能。 遵循这些角色自己发布的行走程序作为其先决条件 。

Current Release Availability

VOID does not yet have an accessible character walkthrough in the current released story. The ordinary character schedule is gated behind a story event beyond the current release boundary. Raising affection, buying gifts or returning at a different hour cannot bypass that story gate.
The current release includes Story Beats1–85. This page retains its existing portrait and gallery entry, but later dialogue, route requirements and choices are omitted until their content is released, to avoid major spoilers and instructions players cannot currently follow.

Related Progression

Continue the released main story using the game's current quest tracker. Other characters can have quests referring to VOID before an ordinary VOID character route becomes available; those references do not unlock this portrait's interactions. Follow those characters' own released walkthroughs for their prerequisites.
